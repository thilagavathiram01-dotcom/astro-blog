Google expanded Gemini Intelligence to the Pixel 10 series on November 10, 2026, as part of the November Pixel Drop. Features that first arrived on the Pixel 11 series, including Rambler voice typing and multi-step task automation, now work on Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold. The update also brings Create My Widget and Device Assistance to more devices.

This guide shows how to access and use the main new capabilities on a supported Pixel phone. Availability depends on your region, language, and account. Some tools require you to be 18 or older and may have usage limits.

## What is Gemini Intelligence?

Gemini Intelligence brings Gemini models deeper into Android on advanced devices. It handles proactive suggestions, cross-app tasks, and smarter input methods. On Pixel 10 phones the November drop adds Rambler for cleaner dictation, expanded task automation for ordering food or booking rides, and the ability to generate custom home screen widgets from a text description.

Motorola’s signature 27 also gains Gemini Intelligence support, with pre-orders starting November 19 in the U.S. For most users the practical change is that Pixel 10 owners no longer need to upgrade to Pixel 11 to get the core productivity tools.

## Set Up Rambler Voice Typing

Rambler cleans up spoken text in real time. It removes filler words such as “um” and “ah,” fixes mid-sentence corrections, and organizes your thoughts into clear sentences. It runs inside Gboard and works in any app that uses the keyboard.

To enable it:

1. Open any text field so Gboard appears.
2. Tap the gear icon or go to Settings > System > Languages & input > On-screen keyboard > Gboard.
3. Select Voice typing.
4. Choose Rambler instead of Standard.

Speak naturally. A waveform appears while you talk. When you finish, Rambler inserts polished text. Review it before sending, because results can vary. Google notes that Rambler is available on Gboard for select countries and languages and is subject to usage limits.

The feature is useful for messages, emails, or notes when you want to speak faster than you type. It keeps the meaning while dropping the pauses and restarts that normal dictation captures.

## Use Task Automation for Everyday Logistics

Task automation lets you ask Gemini to complete multi-step actions across apps. Examples include ordering groceries, hailing a ride, booking a restaurant reservation, or reordering takeout. You can watch the progress and stop the task at any time.

Open the Gemini app and describe the task in plain language. Gemini works with connected apps in the background. The November update expands this capability to the UK, India, and Japan in addition to earlier markets. It is available to users 18 and older in select countries and languages. Higher usage may require a subscription.

Proactive suggestions also improve. When someone texts asking for your ETA, Gemini can surface a one-tap option to share it. Grocery items mentioned in a conversation can move to Google Keep. These suggestions appear in context so you stay in the current app.

For more on connecting apps to Gemini, see our guide on [building connected apps with Gemini Search](/blog/build-connected-apps-gemini-search-guide/).

## Create Custom Widgets with a Prompt

Create My Widget generates home screen widgets from a short description. You can request a monthly spending tracker, a countdown to a trip that pulls flight details from Gmail, or a game-day widget in your team colors. The widget can draw information from Gmail, Calendar, and Search.

The tool is rolling out over the next few weeks on Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 series phones in select regions. It will also reach Pixel Watch 4 and Pixel Watch 5 later.

To try it:

1. Long-press the home screen and open the widget picker.
2. Look for Create My Widget or describe your widget in the available field.
3. Review the generated options.
4. Edit if needed and add the widget to your home screen.

No coding is required. Results vary, so test a few prompts. The feature is limited to users 18 and older and may require a subscription for heavier use.

## Device Assistance and Other Updates

Device Assistance lets you ask Gemini to manage your phone. You can request to free up storage, check which apps have location access, or mute notifications while still allowing important calls. It is available on select devices running Android 17 or later and varies by language and country.

The Pixel Drop also adds conversation summaries for long chats in additional languages, new custom icon styles, expanded Pixel Search on Pixel 6 and newer, and HiLight support for Google Messages on certain models. Pixel Tag, a Bluetooth tracker that works with the Find Hub network, launched on November 11.

## Tips for Best Results

- Update your Pixel to the latest software through Settings > System > System update. The November drop rolls out gradually.
- Confirm Gemini is set as your default assistant.
- Speak clearly for Rambler and review the output, especially for formal messages.
- Start with simple task automation requests and confirm permissions for connected apps.
- Check Google’s support pages for region-specific limits, because feature availability changes.

Gemini Intelligence features are designed to reduce busywork while keeping you in control. You can always intervene, edit, or cancel a task.

## Conclusion

The November 2026 Pixel Drop brings core Gemini Intelligence tools to the Pixel 10 series. Rambler improves voice typing, task automation handles logistics across apps, and Create My Widget personalizes your home screen. Combined with Device Assistance, these updates make a one-year-old Pixel feel more capable without requiring a hardware upgrade.

Try the features after your update arrives and adjust settings to match how you use your phone.
