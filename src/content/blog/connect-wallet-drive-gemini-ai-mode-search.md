Google Search AI Mode now draws from more of your own apps. On 23 November 2026, the company began rolling out an opt-in connection for Google Wallet to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode. Drive and the Gemini app follow in the coming weeks. These join Gmail, Calendar, and Photos.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="30" />

The result is responses that reference your spending patterns, documents, and prior chats instead of generic web results. Nothing activates until you choose to connect each app.

![Smartphone showing AI app icons including Gemini](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1757310998309-87a97e562ee5?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What Personal Intelligence Does in AI Mode

Personal Intelligence lets AI Mode reason across data from the Google apps you connect. It pulls specific details such as an email confirmation, a photo, or a calendar event and weaves them into answers. Earlier versions already supported Gmail, Calendar, and Photos. The 23 November update adds Wallet immediately and schedules Drive plus Gemini app chats for the next few weeks.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="30" />

Google states that Wallet access remains read-only. AI Mode can view spending and linked cards but cannot move, manage, or invest money. Drive connections will let the system reference Docs, Sheets, and PDFs. Linking the Gemini app will carry conversation context both ways between the app and Search.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="30" />

You also start seeing more responses shaped by recent Search activity. A trip you researched yesterday can continue in a new thread without you repeating the details.

## How to Connect Apps to AI Mode

Connections stay off until you turn them on. Follow these steps on a personal Google Account (Workspace and school accounts are excluded in many cases):

1. Open Google Search on mobile or desktop and go to Settings.
2. Select Search Personalization.
3. Choose Connected Apps.
4. Toggle on Google Wallet (and any others already available such as Gmail or Photos). Drive and Gemini app options appear as they roll out.
5. Confirm the choice. You can return to the same menu and turn any connection off later.

The direct link is [myactivity.google.com/search-services/apps](https://myactivity.google.com/search-services/apps). Google notes that you remain in control and can disconnect anytime.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="30" />

Soon a Personalization icon will appear under tailored responses so you can see which context was used. You can also correct details in the thread itself, for example by typing “I’m no longer a vegetarian.”

## Practical Uses After Connecting

With Wallet linked, AI Mode can outline financial milestones based on your linked cards and recent activity. It suggests ways to reduce everyday spending and points out which credit card maximizes cash-back or travel rewards for a given category. The same Wallet context also works inside the Gemini app once connected there.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="30" />

Drive connections (coming weeks) let AI Mode draft a tax-season checklist from documents already stored in your account or pull project details scattered across Sheets and PDFs. Gemini app linking means an idea you explored in a chat becomes available when you continue the topic in Search, and vice versa.

These features build on the existing ability to connect Search history to Personal Intelligence inside the Gemini app. For more on Gemini-side setup, see our guide to [enabling Personal Intelligence on Android](/blog/how-to-enable-gemini-personal-intelligence-on-android/).

## Privacy Controls and Limits

Every connection is opt-in and revocable. Wallet data is read-only; the system cannot initiate payments or alter accounts. Google emphasizes that you choose if and when to connect, and you can adjust personalization settings at any time in Search Settings.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="30" />

Availability starts with the Wallet rollout and expands over the following weeks. Early reporting indicates the feature is primarily for personal accounts in supported regions (English, often U.S.-focused at launch stages). Check your own account for the Connected Apps menu to confirm local availability.

## Tips for Better Results

- Connect only the apps you actively want referenced. Start with Wallet for spending questions and add Drive once it appears if you store project files there.
- Use clear follow-ups. After an answer, ask “Which of my cards has the best cash-back for groceries this month?” to refine the response.
- Review the upcoming Personalization icon to understand sources before acting on advice.
- Keep personalization off for sensitive research if you prefer generic results.
- Test in a new Search thread after connecting so the system has fresh context.

## Conclusion

Connecting Wallet, Drive, and the Gemini app turns AI Mode into a search surface that already knows parts of your financial picture, documents, and ongoing conversations. The process takes under a minute once the options appear, and you can reverse it immediately. Start with Wallet today if the toggle is present, then watch for Drive and Gemini app support in the coming weeks.
