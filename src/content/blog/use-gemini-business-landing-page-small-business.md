Small business owners already use Gemini to brainstorm products, handle customer feedback, and review performance. On October 20, 2026, Google added a dedicated business landing page plus specialized tools so those tasks happen in one place.

The new hub lets you set a brand style once, generate on-brand visuals with Nano Banana 2.1, turn receipts and spreadsheets into clear financial graphics, and connect Google Business Profile, Google Ads, Workspace, and apps such as Canva, Shopify, Xero, and Zoho. The features are rolling out globally to Gemini app users.

## Open the Business Landing Page

Open the Gemini app on your phone or go to gemini.google.com. Look for the new business landing page or hub. It appears as a central workspace for brand, finance, and customer tasks.

If you do not see it yet, check again in a day or two. Google stated the rollout is underway for all users.

Once inside, you can start a conversation that stays focused on business work instead of general chat.

## Set Your Brand Style Once

Upload your website, brand kit, fonts, or color palettes. Gemini stores this style and applies it to future image requests.

This pairs with Nano Banana 2.1, Google’s latest image generation model. You can request product photos, social posts, or campaign graphics and receive results that match your colors and typography without re-uploading guidelines each time.

Example prompt: “Using my saved brand style, create three Instagram post images for a new product launch. Leave space for a short headline and match my palette.”

Test with a low-stakes request first so you can refine the style before generating larger campaigns.

## Connect the Tools You Already Use

Link Google Business Profile, Google Ads, and Google Workspace. You can also connect Adobe, Canva, Dropbox, Shopify, Wix, Zoho, and Xero.

Start with one or two connections you will use this month. For a local shop, Google Business Profile is often the highest-value first link. Gemini can then pull recent performance data and help draft updates.

Prompt example: “Review my Google Business Profile performance for the last 30 days. Summarize search and Maps changes in plain language and suggest three weekend updates tied to a local event.”

For online sellers, connect Shopify or Xero so Gemini can reference orders or bookkeeping data directly.

## Turn Numbers into Clear Insights

Upload or describe messy spreadsheets, receipts, and purchase records. Gemini converts them into financial graphics and interactive models in seconds.

Instead of reading raw numbers, you receive plain-language summaries and visuals. Ask for month-over-month comparisons, top expense categories, or simple projections.

Prompt example: “Analyze the attached sales spreadsheet. Show revenue by week, flag the three highest-growth products, and list any unusual expenses.”

You can also request a short Monday briefing that covers cash in versus cash out, the highest-priority marketing task, and any customer feedback that needs a reply.

## Practical Workflows That Save Time

Ceramicist Morgane in Bordeaux uses Gemini for product descriptions, newsletters, and social hooks, saving two to three hours each day. Farmer Santosh Jadhav in Maharashtra treats it as a virtual agronomist for soil, seed, and budget questions. Restaurant owner Josh in New Jersey connects his Google Business Profile between shifts to track local search and plan weekend updates.

These examples show the same pattern: give Gemini the relevant context once, then ask for finished drafts or analysis you can edit quickly.

Try these three workflows this week:

1. Upload your brand assets, then generate a full set of social images for one offer.
2. Connect Google Business Profile and request a performance summary plus three post ideas.
3. Feed recent receipts or a sales export and ask for a one-page financial snapshot.

## Tips for Better Results

Be specific about length and tone. “Draft three review replies under 40 words each in a friendly voice” works better than “write some replies.”

Review every output before you publish or send it. Gemini accelerates drafting and analysis; you still own accuracy and brand voice.

Start narrow. Master brand style and one connected app before adding more tools.
