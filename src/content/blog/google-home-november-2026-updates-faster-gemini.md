Google rolled out a fresh set of Google Home updates in November 2026. Everyday voice requests now run up to 20% faster. Nest Hub displays can show relevant images with answers. Android users gain keyless entry through Google Wallet. Nest Thermostat owners in the US and Canada will soon see estimated savings in the app.

These changes arrive through software updates. No new hardware is required for most features. Gemini for Home remains in early access for some improvements, while others land more broadly.

If you have not enabled Gemini for Home yet, start with our earlier guide on [how to set up Gemini for Home on Nest speakers](/blog/how-to-set-up-gemini-for-home-nest-speakers/).

## What Changed in the November 2026 Google Home Update

Google published the details on November 17, 2026. The post from Group Product Manager Mark Alexander highlights four main areas.

- Everyday Gemini for Home requests (smart home controls, shopping lists, timers) respond up to 20% faster.
- Smart displays now include relevant images in on-screen answers when they help.
- Android users can unlock compatible front doors with a tap or hands-free approach using digital home keys stored in Google Wallet.
- Google Home Premium Advanced detects audio events such as barking dogs or breaking glass and adds them to camera and doorbell descriptions.

Additional notes from the November 11 release notes cover more reliable complex commands (Gemini asks “which bedroom?” instead of guessing), faster timers/alarms/media sleep timers, faster broadcasts, and a new Eco Mode Everywhere toggle for homes with multiple Nest Thermostats.

Gemini for Home is also expanding in December to India, Taiwan, Singapore, Colombia, South Korea, and Brazil. It works on Google Home and Nest speakers and displays sold over the last decade.

## How to Get the Updates

Updates roll out automatically through the Google Home app and device firmware. Follow these steps to check and enable the new features.

1. Open the Google Home app on your phone.
2. Tap your profile picture in the top right.
3. Select Settings, then check for software updates on each speaker, display, and thermostat.
4. For Gemini for Home early access features, go to Home settings > Gemini for Home voice assistant and confirm it is enabled.
5. Restart the device if the new behaviors do not appear immediately. Rollouts can take hours to a few days.

Compatible devices include the Google Home Speaker (2026), Nest Hub (2nd gen), Nest Audio, Nest Mini (2nd gen), Nest Hub Max, and earlier Nest Hub and Home models for most features. Gemini Live still requires Google Home Premium and newer hardware.

## Using Visual Answers on Smart Displays

On Nest Hub and other smart displays, ask a question that benefits from a picture. Examples include:

- “What’s the weather this weekend?”
- “Show me the front door camera.”
- “What does a [recipe ingredient] look like?”

The display now adds relevant images alongside the text answer when they are useful. This reduces the need to listen to long spoken replies. The feature is part of the November 11 Gemini for Home improvements and appears first for early access users.

## Keyless Entry with Google Wallet

Android users with a compatible Aliro or Matter smart lock can store a digital home key in Google Wallet.

1. Open the Google Home app and confirm the lock is connected.
2. Follow the prompt to add the digital key to Google Wallet (Public Preview).
3. On your Android phone, hold it near the lock or use the hands-free approach if supported.
4. Unlock even when the phone is locked.

This works for compatible front door locks. Check the lock’s compatibility list in the Google Home app or Nest Community.

## Nest Thermostat Improvements Coming in December

US and Canada Nest Thermostat owners will gain new options starting in December:

- View estimated cumulative savings directly in the Google Home app.
- Balance hot and cold spots by pairing compatible third-party room sensors with the 4th-gen Nest Learning Thermostat.
- New Dynamic Farsight faces with seasonal animations and a lunar phase tracker (4th-gen only).

Homes with multiple Nest Thermostats can already use the Eco Mode Everywhere toggle. Open the Google Home app, go to the energy or thermostat section, and enable it with one tap. This helps maximize savings across the home.

## Faster Timers, Broadcasts, and More Reliable Controls

Timers, alarms, and media sleep timers now respond faster. Setting, checking, or canceling them feels snappier. Broadcast messages also start playing more quickly across speakers and displays.

For complex smart home requests, Gemini is less likely to refuse and more likely to ask a clarifying question when multiple devices match. Voice feedback is more conversational, letting you add detail before sending.

Google Home Premium users will soon see a faster Gemini Live experience powered by an updated speech-to-speech architecture. Say “Hey Google, let’s chat” to brainstorm ideas, interrupt naturally, and control devices mid-conversation.

## Tips for Best Results

- Keep the Google Home app updated. Many controls and the Premium page live in the app.
- Enable Continued Conversation in Gemini for Home settings if you want multi-turn dialogue.
- For camera audio detection, confirm you have Google Home Premium Advanced and that event descriptions are turned on.
- Test keyless entry with a short distance first. Phone placement and lock model affect range.
- Check the Nest Community or Google Home release notes if a feature is missing after a few days.

## Conclusion

The November 2026 Google Home updates make everyday controls faster and more useful. Visual answers help on displays, keyless entry simplifies entry for Android users, and thermostat tools give clearer savings data. Most changes arrive as free software updates.

Update your devices, enable Gemini for Home if you have not already, and try the new commands. Expansion to more countries in December will bring the same experience to additional markets.
