Google Fitbit Edge brings a slim OLED display, built-in GPS, and the Gemini-powered Google Health Coach to a tracker that sits between the screenless Fitbit Air and the Pixel Watch. It launched for pre-order on October 12 and reached shelves on October 27 at $179.99. Buyers receive three months of Google Health Premium, which unlocks the coach and Health Guardian features.

This guide walks through unboxing, pairing with the Google Health app, and starting personalized coaching. You need a phone running Android 11 or higher or iOS 16.4 or higher, a Google Account, and an internet connection.

## What the Fitbit Edge Includes

The Edge measures 11.5 mm thick with a recycled aluminum case. Its Hybrid OLED display offers 46 percent more viewing area than earlier Fitbit trackers. Sensors include optical heart rate, ECG, SpO2, skin temperature, altimeter, and accelerometer. Built-in GPS improves route accuracy by up to 40 percent over previous models. Battery life reaches up to seven days with the always-on display off. A five-minute charge provides a full day when the battery is low. It is water-resistant to 50 meters (5 ATM) and IP68.

The device is Google’s first repairable Fitbit tracker with a user-replaceable battery. It supports more than 50 exercise modes and automatic recognition of up to 100 workout types using Gemini intelligence.

## Pair the Device with the Google Health App

Download the Google Health app from the Play Store or App Store if it is not already installed. Sign in with your Google Account.

Charge the Edge fully using the included magnetic USB-C puck. Press and hold the side button until the screen lights up.

Open the Google Health app. Tap the devices or add device option and follow the on-screen prompts to pair via Bluetooth. The app will confirm the connection and guide you through basic settings such as wrist preference and units.

Grant the necessary permissions for notifications, location (for GPS), and health data. The Edge can also show Google Maps turn-by-turn directions once that feature arrives, plus media controls and call/message notifications.

After pairing, the app syncs historical data if you previously used a Fitbit or Pixel Watch on the same account. You can switch between Air, Edge, and Pixel Watch devices on one account.

## Activate Google Health Premium and the Coach

Purchase of the Edge includes three months of Google Health Premium. The coach requires an active Premium subscription after the trial. Premium is also included with Google AI Pro and Ultra plans in supported countries.

In the Google Health app, look for the coach prompt on the Today screen or Fitness tab. Start the goal-setting conversation. Answer questions about your fitness level, goals, available equipment, schedule, and any health factors you choose to share. The conversation takes about five to ten minutes. You can use text or voice.

The coach builds a personalized weekly plan with specific exercise recommendations and targets. It adapts based on your tracked data, recovery metrics, and progress. Ask follow-up questions anytime via the Ask Coach button. Conversations appear in Coach Notes or history.

Health Guardian features, available with Premium, track blood pressure trends, insulin resistance trends, and high or low heart rate alerts in the background. These are for general wellness information only and are not medical advice.

## Use Workout Tracking and Real-Time Guidance

Start a workout from the Edge exercise app or the phone app. Choose from more than 50 modes. Real-time guidance shows pace, heart-rate zones, splits, or core metrics depending on the activity. The physical side button gives quick access to pause or controls during sessions.

Automatic activity detection recognizes walks, runs, rides, and other efforts even if you forget to start tracking. It uses contextual analysis powered by Gemini to categorize the activity and count it toward weekly goals.

After each session, review summaries in the Google Health app. The coach can adjust the next week’s plan based on your performance, sleep, and recovery scores.

## Tips for Best Results

Wear the tracker consistently, including overnight, for accurate sleep stages, HRV, and skin temperature data. These feed into recovery scores and coach recommendations.

Charge regularly. The replaceable battery extends device life, but follow local guidance for replacement availability.

Keep the Google Health app updated. Some advanced features, such as full Maps integration, roll out after launch.

The coach is an AI system and can make mistakes. Verify important guidance and consult a healthcare professional for medical questions. It is not a substitute for professional advice.

The Google Fitbit Edge delivers a focused fitness experience with a readable display, strong battery life, and direct access to the Gemini-powered Health Coach. Setup takes a few minutes once you have the app and Premium trial activated. Consistent wear and honest goal-setting conversations produce the most useful personalized plans.
