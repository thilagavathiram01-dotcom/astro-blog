Google made Tasks in the Gemini app free for adults in six countries on 17 November 2026. You can now hand a job to Gemini, let it work in the background, and pick it up only when it needs a decision.

This guide shows exactly how to start, what it can do, the limits, and the controls that keep you in charge. All details come from Google’s official announcement and help pages.

## What Gemini Tasks actually does

Chat mode answers a question and stops. Task mode takes a goal and keeps working. Gemini plans the steps, opens connected apps or websites, and reports back when it needs approval.

It runs on the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model. You can have up to 15 tasks running at the same time. You stay in the loop: Gemini pauses before buying anything or taking other critical actions.

If you already keep your email, calendar, files and photos inside Google, Tasks can use that context through Personal Intelligence without sending the data elsewhere.

## Who can use Tasks for free

Tasks is free (no Google AI subscription required) for people aged 18 or older with a personal Google Account in these countries:

- United States
- India
- Canada
- Australia
- Japan
- South Korea

You also need Gemini History turned on. Work and school accounts are not supported.

In other countries where Gemini works, you need a Google AI Pro or Ultra plan. Tasks is not available at all in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or Nigeria.

Advanced options such as calling businesses, checking out with Google Pay, texting Gemini or forwarding email are currently US-only. Calling is unavailable in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana and Nebraska.

## How to start a task

1. Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS, or go to gemini.google.com. Sign in with your personal Google Account.
2. Find the Chat / Task toggle. On a phone it sits above the text box. On the web and Mac app it sits below the box.
3. Switch it to **Task**.
4. Describe the finished result, not the individual clicks. Include dates, budget, stores or anything Gemini should avoid.
5. Submit. Gemini creates its own thread and works in the background.

You can add more instructions while a task is running. To stop one, use the Stop button in the text box.

Google’s own short instruction is: open Gemini, flip the toggle from Chat to Task, and tell Gemini what you need done.

## Practical examples

Google’s product team shared several real uses:

- Call nearby car dealerships to book a service appointment and add it to Google Calendar (US, with the calling feature).
- Analyse utility bills from email and the utility portal to find why a bill spiked.
- Compare product options and check out with Google Pay while staying inside a spending cap you set.
- Add items to an Instacart order by text, including a photo of an empty box.
- Forward school emails to gemini@google.com so every date lands on your calendar.

Gemini discloses that it is an AI when it calls a business. Businesses can opt out. Receipts from purchases land in Google Wallet.

It currently connects with more than 60 apps, including Gmail, Calendar, Maps and Instacart. Developers can apply to the Connected Apps Platform to add more.

## US-only ways to start a task

In the United States you can also:

- Text Gemini. Go to Settings > Channels > Set up under Text with Gemini and verify your phone number. You need a US number on an eligible carrier. Texts work through Google Messages or iMessage over RCS.
- Forward an email to gemini@google.com. Replies go only to you.

Both methods create a normal task thread inside the Gemini app.

## Tips for better results

Write the task the way you would brief a helpful colleague. State the goal, the limits and the timing. “Book an oil change at my usual dealer next week, any morning before 10, under $150” works better than “oil change”.

You can schedule recurring tasks. On free plans the timing is once a day in a rough window. Higher Google AI plans allow more precise hourly schedules.

Set a hard spending cap before any shopping task. Gemini is designed to ask before clicking Buy. For routine actions you trust, you can set “Always allow”.

If a task misses the mark, give the response a thumbs down so Google can improve it. You can also delete history or disconnect apps at any time.

Tasks uses Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine. Each task runs in a hardware-isolated virtual machine dedicated to your account.

## What Tasks cannot do yet

It will not move money or invest on your behalf. When it shops, it uses the Agent Payments Protocol with tokenised cards from supported networks. You still earn your card’s points and protections.

On some websites Gemini may complete most of the steps and then hand the screen back to you for the final click. The web is still adapting to personal assistants.

Usage limits are compute-based and rise with higher Google AI plans. Google warns you when you are close to the limit.
