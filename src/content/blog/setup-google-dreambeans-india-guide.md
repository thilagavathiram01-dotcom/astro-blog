Google Labs has brought Dreambeans to India. The experimental app turns data from apps you choose into a daily collection of illustrated stories. It is available now for users 18 and older on Android and iOS.

Unlike endless feeds, Dreambeans keeps the list finite. Stories cover places to explore, events, hobbies, or reminders drawn from your connected Google services. You control which apps it can use.



![Person holding a smartphone showing an app interface](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1507238691740-187a5b1d37b8?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)



## What Dreambeans does

Dreambeans uses Google’s Personal Intelligence to build stories overnight from the apps you enable. It then presents them as a short morning collection.

Supported sources include Calendar, Gemini, Gmail, Photos, Search, and YouTube. Connecting Photos can incorporate images of you and people in your library into the illustrations. Gemini adds another signal for personalization.

The app works best with more connections, but you must enable at least one. Choices in Dreambeans do not change settings in Gemini Apps or AI Mode. Stories stay private to your account.

India gets support for 14 languages: English plus Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Marathi, Tamil, Urdu, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Punjabi, Assamese, and Nepali. Select one language for the stories.

## Download and sign in

1. Open the Google Play Store on Android or the App Store on iOS.
2. Search for Dreambeans by Google Labs.
3. Install the official app.
4. Open it and sign in with a personal Google Account. Workspace accounts are not supported.
5. Confirm you are 18 or older.

The first set of stories can take up to a day to generate after setup. You can provide feedback on stories to improve future ones.

## Choose connected apps

After signing in:

1. Tap to connect apps. Select any combination of Gemini, Gmail, Calendar, Google Photos, YouTube, or Search.
2. Grant the required permissions for each.
3. Pick your preferred language for the stories.
4. Finish setup.

You can change connections later. Open the profile icon, review connected apps, and toggle them on or off. Deleting data from a source also removes it from Dreambeans personalization.

If you already connect apps to Gemini, the process is similar. See the guide on [connecting apps to Gemini](/blog/connect-apps-to-gemini/) for related permission steps.

## Use the daily stories

Open the app each morning. Browse the finite list of illustrated stories. Tap one to read more, bookmark it, or share it.

Provide feedback with likes or dislikes. Use the tuning options to request more of certain topics or exclude others. This updates future collections.

Some stories may include animated elements powered by Veo. The focus remains on inspiration rather than infinite scrolling.

## Privacy controls

You decide which apps connect. Review or delete data from the profile icon. Select “Delete my data” to clear history, or manage specific feedback history.

Dreambeans does not affect Gemini or AI Mode settings. Stories are visible only to you. When you remove data from a connected service, it leaves the Dreambeans system as well.

Start with one or two apps you trust, such as Calendar and Photos. Expand later if the stories prove useful.

## Tips for better results

- Connect Photos and Calendar for personal context around events and people.
- Give clear feedback on the first few days so the app learns your preferences.
- Check language settings if stories appear in the wrong language.
- Update the app if the Play Store or App Store shows a new version.
- Disconnect unused apps to limit data access.

Dreambeans remains an experiment from Google Labs. Features and availability can change.

## Conclusion

Setting up Dreambeans in India takes a few minutes: download the app, sign in with a personal account, pick at least one Google service, and choose a language. The result is a short daily set of personalized illustrated stories instead of another endless feed.

Use the privacy controls to keep only the connections you want. Pair it with existing Gemini workflows if you already connect apps there.
