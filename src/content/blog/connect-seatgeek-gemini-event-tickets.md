Google added SeatGeek to Gemini Connected Apps on 23 September 2026. Mai Lowe, group product manager for the Gemini app, listed it with apartments.com, Experian, and Peloton in the lifestyle group of that rollout. The point is simple: ask about events in chat instead of opening another tab first.

SeatGeek is a ticket marketplace. Gemini does not invent inventory. After you connect the app, it can call SeatGeek for listings that match your prompt. You still confirm price, fees, and the order on SeatGeek.

This guide follows Google’s Connected Apps help page and the 23 September product post. It does not claim a specific city, fee, or inventory count.

## What the September rollout included

The product post grouped new partners into three sets:

- **Productivity:** Airtable, Linear, monday.com, PandaDoc, Wispr AI, and Zoho.
- **Creativity:** Adobe, Picsart, Squarespace, and Webflow.
- **Lifestyle:** apartments.com, Experian, Peloton, and SeatGeek.

Google said the wave was beginning to roll out that day. Partner apps do not appear for every account at the same moment. If SeatGeek is missing from your list, the flag has not reached that login yet.

Connected Apps also vary by Gemini surface, device, country, and account type. Google’s help page says an app that exists only in the Android Gemini app will not show on the web or on iOS. Check the surface you actually use before you assume the partner is unavailable.

If you already linked Experian from the same wave, the setup pattern is the same. See our [Experian credit connection guide](/blog/connect-experian-gemini-credit/) for the authorization screens, then come back here for event prompts.

## Requirements before you turn SeatGeek on

Google requires a signed-in Gemini account before Connected Apps work. Work and school accounts follow a different admin path. Use a personal Google Account for this walkthrough.

Keep Activity has to be on for most Connected Apps. When it is off, Gemini on the web, iOS, and watches will not offer those apps. On Android, only Device assistance, Phone, Messages, and WhatsApp stay available with Keep Activity off.

You also need a SeatGeek account you are willing to authorize. Create or sign in to that account on SeatGeek’s own screens, not by pasting a password into the chat.

Gemini cannot use Connected Apps inside Google Messages. Start the request in the Gemini app or at gemini.google.com.

## Turn SeatGeek on from Settings

Google documents the same control on the web and in the mobile apps.

On the web:

1. Open [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) and sign in.
2. At the bottom, open **Settings and help**, then **Connected Apps**. If that label is missing, open **Personal Intelligence**, then **Connected Apps**.
3. Find **SeatGeek**. The list only shows apps available to your account, device, and country.
4. Turn the app on and finish the SeatGeek authorization screens.
5. Return to the list and confirm the toggle stayed on.

On Android, open the Gemini app, tap your profile, then Settings, then Connected Apps. The same toggle lives there. iOS uses the same menu when the partner is offered on that device.

Open **Learn more** under SeatGeek on the details page. Google puts supported actions, unsupported actions, and example prompts there. Use that page as the source of truth if a prompt fails.

## Call SeatGeek from the prompt box

You can connect from chat instead of Settings. Google says typing `@` and picking an app will connect it, or ask for permission, if it is still off.

1. In the prompt box, type `@` and select **SeatGeek**.
2. Add a specific request: city, date window, and event type.
3. Submit. Follow any permission card Gemini shows.
4. Read the reply, then open the SeatGeek link before you pay.

Useful prompts stay concrete:

- `@SeatGeek find NBA games in Chicago next weekend under $80 before fees if the listing shows that.`
- `@SeatGeek what concerts are in Austin in November?`
- `@SeatGeek compare afternoon and evening listings for this artist in Los Angeles.`

If Gemini answers from general web knowledge and never asks to connect SeatGeek, the app is not enabled for that account yet. Wait for the rollout rather than repeating the prompt.

## Check the listing before you buy

SeatGeek listings change. A chat summary can lag the live marketplace. Open the listing and confirm section, price, and fees on SeatGeek before checkout.

Google’s help page says Gemini can take actions in connected apps with your permission, including editing and managing content. For tickets, treat any purchase step as something you confirm yourself. Do not approve a payment prompt you did not request.

SeatGeek support says order help lives in the Tickets section of your SeatGeek account, via Contact Support on that order. Phone support is limited to the week before an event. Gemini is not SeatGeek customer service.

If you bought tickets somewhere else, SeatGeek cannot manage that order. Ask Gemini only about listings it can reach through the connection.

## Pair SeatGeek with other apps in the same chat

Connected Apps can sit next to Google services Gemini already uses, such as Maps, when those services are available. A practical sequence is to find an event with SeatGeek, then ask for transit or parking with Maps in a follow-up. Name the app with `@` when you want a specific source.

Keep credit checks and ticket searches apart. Experian answers bureau questions. SeatGeek answers event inventory. One prompt that mixes both makes it harder to see which app supplied the data.

The wider September list also covers project tools and design tools. Our [overview of connecting apps to Gemini](/blog/connect-apps-to-gemini/) covers the shared Settings path if you plan to add monday.com or Adobe later.

## Disconnect SeatGeek

You can turn any Connected App off from the same list.

1. Open **Settings and help**, then **Connected Apps**.
2. Find SeatGeek and turn it off.
3. On SeatGeek, review connected apps or authorized logins and revoke Gemini if that control exists there.

Turning the toggle off stops new requests from Gemini. It does not cancel tickets you already bought. Google’s privacy hub for Gemini Apps explains what happens to data exchanged with a connected app. Read that page if you linked a marketplace account.

## Tips that keep searches accurate

- Name the city and a date range. “Tickets near me” depends on location permission and can miss the venue you meant.
- Ask for a short list, then pick one event for a second prompt. Long multi-city asks tend to drop fees and sections.
- Recheck the toggle after a Gemini app update. Rollouts sometimes reset partner switches.
- Do not paste payment card numbers or one-time codes into the chat.
- If SeatGeek is absent on the web but present on Android, stay on the device where the app is listed.

## Conclusion

SeatGeek joined Gemini Connected Apps in the 23 September 2026 lifestyle wave, next to Experian, Peloton, and apartments.com. Sign in, turn Keep Activity on, enable SeatGeek from Connected Apps or with `@SeatGeek`, and confirm every listing on SeatGeek before you pay.

Leave the connection off when you only want general event ideas. When you need a refund or a transfer, use SeatGeek’s own order screen. Gemini is the search box, not the box office.
