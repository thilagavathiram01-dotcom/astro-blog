Google rolled out 66 new regional voices in the Gemini app on November 16, 2026. These options cover U.S. English, U.K. English, India English, Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian, and Korean. Each voice matches local cadence, pitch, and rhythm without exaggerated accents.

The selected voice works across the app. It applies during Gemini Live conversations, language practice, and when you tap Listen on a written reply. This update builds on plans shared at Google I/O earlier in the year.

If you use Gemini for daily questions or longer voice sessions, a local voice can make responses feel more natural. Rollout is gradual, so check after updating the app.

## What the new voices include

Google tuned the voices for everyday clarity. They capture familiar local speech patterns while staying easy to understand. Text replies stay in standard language. Only the spoken output changes.

U.K. English receives more than ten options. These include Geordie, Scouse, Brummie, Scottish, Welsh, Northern Irish, Modern Received Pronunciation, and Multicultural London English. Other languages receive tailored sets as well.

The voices are not labeled by city or region in the picker. Gemini will not identify the accent if you ask. You preview them by ear.

## How to preview and select a voice

Update the Gemini app first on Android or iOS. Then try one of two paths.

**From Gemini Live**

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Start a Gemini Live session.
3. Tap the three-dot menu.
4. Preview available voices and select one.

**From app settings**

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Tap your profile picture.
3. Go to Settings.
4. Choose Gemini’s voice.
5. Swipe through previews and pick your preferred option.

The change applies immediately across Live and Listen features. You can switch again at any time.

## Practical uses for regional voices

A matching voice helps during longer spoken sessions. You can brainstorm ideas, practice a language, or listen to summaries while driving or cooking. The familiar rhythm reduces the sense of talking to a generic assistant.

For language practice, pair the voice with the target language. Indian English or Hindi voices support learners in those varieties. U.K. dialects can help with regional listening practice.

The feature does not require a paid plan. It is available to eligible users on Android and iOS. Web and other platforms were not listed in the announcement.

## Tips for better results

Preview several options before settling. Ask Gemini about local topics such as food, sports, or daily routines. This often highlights the natural cadence of each voice.

Keep the app updated. Rollout continues, so missing options may appear later. If the menu is empty, wait and check again after a restart.

Combine the voice with other Gemini tools. For multi-step work you can hand off background tasks. See our guide on [how to use Tasks in Gemini](/blog/how-to-use-tasks-in-gemini/) for related steps.

Test Listen on a written reply after selecting a voice. Confirm it matches the Live setting.

## Limitations to know

Voices focus on spoken cadence only. Written answers do not adopt regional slang or spelling. The picker does not name dialects, so selection requires listening.

Availability depends on your account and device. Some users see the options later than others. The announcement covers Android and iOS apps specifically.

## Conclusion

The 66 regional voices make Gemini Live and read-aloud replies feel closer to everyday conversation. Open the app, update if needed, and preview options through the Live menu or settings. Pick one that matches how you speak, then use it for planning, practice, or quick listening.

Official details remain on the Google Keyword post from November 16, 2026. Check the Gemini app for the latest available voices on your account.
