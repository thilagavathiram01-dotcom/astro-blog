Amazon launched Bee Frontier and Bee Eclipse on October 15, 2026. These personal AI devices capture conversation details so you stay present while the hardware handles memory tasks. Frontier is a wearable. Eclipse attaches to your phone. Both turn spoken words into summaries, reminders, and action items.

This guide covers what each device does, how to set them up, practical ways to use them, and privacy controls. Facts come from Amazon’s official announcement.

## What Bee Frontier and Bee Eclipse Do

Bee Frontier costs $74.99 and is available now on Amazon.com in Onyx, Lavender, and Sage. You wear it on a soft silicone wristband or clip the module to clothing. It has dual microphones with noise filtering, IP67 water resistance, USB-C charging, and up to seven days of battery life.

Bee Eclipse starts at $99.99 and is coming soon in Silver, Midnight, Honey, and Blush. The credit-card-sized device snaps magnetically to the back of a phone. Amazon claims more than 21 days of battery life. It captures in-person talks and phone calls. The device prompts you to get permission from call participants each time recording starts.

Both run the same core software. When active, they process audio in real time and delete it after creating insights. No audio is stored. Transcripts stay encrypted with keys controlled by your device. You access summaries in the Bee mobile app or the desktop app.

Free features include conversation summaries, recaps, and 14-day memory. Bee Pro costs $11.99 per month or $99.99 per year and adds unlimited summaries, Alexa+ and Amazon.com integrations, third-party app connections, suggested to-dos, a personal chat assistant, conversation templates, and the desktop app. New buyers get 30 days of Bee Pro free.

## How to Set Up Bee Frontier or Bee Eclipse

Start by ordering the device from Amazon. Frontier ships immediately. Eclipse has no confirmed ship date yet.

1. Unbox the device and charge it fully with the included USB-C cable.
2. Download the Bee app from the App Store or Google Play.
3. Create or sign in to your Bee account.
4. Pair the device via Bluetooth. Follow the on-screen prompts.
5. Press the single button to turn processing on. The indicator light shows when the device is active.
6. Configure auto-off. Choose intervals such as 5 or 15 minutes of silence so the device stops when no one is talking.
7. Optional: Enable Bee Pro in the app for extra features. The first 30 days are free.

For phone calls with Eclipse, the app prompts you to confirm permission from others before processing starts. Keep the light visible so people know the device is on.

Once paired, test a short conversation. Open the app and check the summary within a few minutes.

## Use Bee for Daily Productivity

Bee helps capture details you would otherwise forget. Use it in meetings, planning sessions, or casual talks about tasks.

After a work call, open the Bee app and review the summary. It lists action items. With Bee Pro, the app can draft a follow-up email in Gmail or Outlook or create a calendar invite in Google Calendar.

Connect Bee to Alexa+ (Bee Pro required). Ask an Echo device for a meeting summary or your to-do list without opening your phone. Voice ID on the Echo ensures only your account sees the data.

Link to Amazon.com for shopping suggestions. Mention a product in conversation and Bee can surface related items on the Amazon homepage or inside the app.

Review the last few days of memories to spot recurring themes. Bee recommends goals based on patterns, such as tracking health notes if you connect Apple HealthKit.

For lectures or brainstorms, choose matching templates in the app so summaries match the context.

Compare this approach to other AI memory tools. For example, Gemini’s Daily Brief on Android organizes information from your apps. See our guide on [Gemini Daily Brief setup](/blog/gemini-daily-brief/) for a software-only alternative.

## Privacy Controls and Best Practices

Amazon built Bee around Bee Private Compute. Audio is processed in an isolated environment and deleted immediately. No one at Amazon can access the raw audio or your transcripts unless you share them.

- Press the button to turn the device off at any time. The light confirms status.
- Delete individual transcripts or summaries in the app whenever you want.
- Do not leave the device on in private spaces without consent from everyone present.
- For Eclipse calls, always get verbal permission first. The app reminds you each time.
- Review connected apps regularly. Disconnect ones you no longer need.
- Use the auto-off setting so the device does not stay active during long quiet periods.

These controls match the original Bee design principles. You decide what stays and what goes.

## Tips for Getting the Most Value

Start with the free tier to test accuracy on your typical conversations. Noise filtering on Frontier works best in moderate environments; very loud rooms may reduce clarity.

Charge Frontier weekly and Eclipse less often. Both use USB-C.

Combine Bee summaries with your existing task system. Copy action items into your preferred app after each session.

If you already use Alexa, the Bee Pro connection reduces the need to open a phone for quick recaps.

Monitor battery and connection status in the app. Re-pair if Bluetooth drops.

## Conclusion

Bee Frontier and Bee Eclipse give you a dedicated way to capture conversation details without constant note-taking. Frontier suits people who want a wearable option. Eclipse fits those who prefer a phone attachment with longer battery life. Setup takes minutes. Free features cover basic summaries. Bee Pro unlocks deeper integrations.

Test the free tier first. Confirm privacy settings match your needs. Then decide if the hardware and optional subscription fit your workflow.
