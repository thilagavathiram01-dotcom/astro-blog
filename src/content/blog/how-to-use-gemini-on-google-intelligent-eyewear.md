Google Intelligent Eyewear puts Gemini on frames you wear all day. The glasses connect to your phone and deliver hands-free help without pulling out a screen. You can ask about what you see, get directions, translate speech, and more.

Pre-orders opened on 12 November 2026 from Warby Parker and Gentle Monster. Retail availability starts 10 December 2026 in the United States and South Korea. Prices begin at $395 for Warby Parker frames and $449 for Gentle Monster styles. Prescription lenses are available on most frames.

You need a compatible phone and an 18+ Google Account. The glasses use Bluetooth to your phone for full Gemini features. Some functions work without a constant connection.

This guide covers setup and everyday use based on official Google details.

## Check compatibility and requirements

The glasses work with:

- Pixel 7 series and later running Android 17 (September 2026 or later update).
- Samsung Galaxy S, S FE, Z Fold, Flip, and Fold Ultra series with One UI 9.0 or later.
- iPhone 13 or later (minimum 6 GB RAM) running iOS 26.6 or later.

You must be 18 or older with a valid Google Account. Setup cannot finish without an eligible account. Agree to responsible-use terms that prohibit recording others without permission or covering the indicator light.

Battery life reaches up to 9 hours depending on use. The charging case holds additional charges. A physical switch turns off the camera, microphones, and Gemini so the frames work as ordinary glasses.

## Set up the glasses with your phone

Charge the glasses in the case first. Remove any protective paper from the case before the initial charge. A full charge takes time; 60 percent can arrive in about 30 minutes.

On Android 17 or later, Fast Pair handles connection:

1. Turn on Bluetooth on your phone.
2. Bring the glasses close to the phone.
3. Accept the Fast Pair prompt that appears.
4. Follow the on-screen steps to sign in and complete setup.

On iOS, download the Glasses app from the App Store when it becomes available and follow the pairing instructions.

After pairing, the glasses connect automatically when nearby. You can check battery status and adjust settings from the phone. Grant any requested permissions for camera or microphone access on a per-app basis.

If pairing fails, confirm your phone meets the OS requirements and that Bluetooth is enabled. Restart both devices and try again.

## Activate Gemini and ask about your surroundings

Say “Hey Gemini” or long-press the touchpad on the right stem to start a conversation. Swipe backward on the touchpad to stop Gemini.

For visual questions, tell Gemini to look. Saying “Hey Gemini, what is this?” may not trigger the camera. Instead use “Hey Gemini, look at this and tell me what it is.” The camera activates only on explicit request. Camera visuals from these queries are not saved in your Gemini History.

Useful examples:

- “Hey Gemini, look at this mural and tell me the story behind it.”
- “Hey Gemini, look at the menu and recommend something that matches my usual preferences.”
- “Hey Gemini, look at this poster and check if it fits my calendar.”

Gemini draws on your Google account data for personalised answers when relevant. Responses play through the open-ear speakers in the stems.

You can also ask Gemini to capture a photo or video with the 12-megapixel ultra-wide camera. Press the button on the top of the frame for a quick shot, or ask Gemini to take one. An outer indicator light shows others when the camera is active.

## Use navigation, translation, and accessibility tools

Google Maps delivers turn-by-turn directions through the speakers while your phone stays in your pocket. Ask Gemini for the route or start navigation from Maps on the phone.

Google Translate provides real-time speech translation with automatic language detection across more than 70 languages. Speak or ask Gemini to translate what you hear.

Guided Vision offers conversational audio descriptions. It helps people who are blind, have low vision, or want assistance reading small print or locating everyday items. On compatible Android phones you can enable it through Gemini settings or accessibility shortcuts. Note that Guided Vision and Aira Explorer currently require a compatible Android phone; they are not yet available when the glasses are paired only with an iPhone.

Say “Hey Gemini, open Aira Explorer” to stream the camera feed to a trained human visual interpreter for real-time support (Android pairing required).

These tools keep your hands free and your eyes up during walks, meetings, or travel.

## Manage privacy and everyday controls

A physical power switch on the left stem turns the glasses fully off. This disables the camera, microphones, and Gemini so you can wear them as regular eyewear.

You control which apps access the camera and microphones. Revoke access at any time from your phone settings. Gemini History settings let you review, delete, or control whether chat text is used for model training. Camera visuals from Look and Ask queries stay out of History.

Touch gestures on the right stem handle media and calls:

- Single tap: play or pause audio.
- Swipe forward or backward: skip tracks.
- Two-finger swipe: adjust volume.
- Tap to answer a call; swipe back to end it.

Battery notifications appear on your phone. Replace nothing yourself; the case handles charging. The glasses weigh about 41 grams before lenses.

Always respect the indicator light and local recording rules. Google’s terms require responsible use.

## Tips for better results

Speak clearly and include “look at this” when you want visual analysis. Short, specific requests work better than long compound questions.

Keep the glasses charged and paired before you leave home. Test translation and Maps in a quiet environment first.

If Gemini does not respond, confirm the glasses are powered on, Bluetooth is connected, and “Hey Gemini” detection is enabled on the paired phone. Force-stop and reopen the relevant apps if needed.

For accessibility users, enable Guided Vision in advance and confirm Aira access if you plan to use human interpreters.

Update your phone’s OS and the Gemini app regularly. New capabilities may arrive after the December 10 launch.

## Conclusion

Google Intelligent Eyewear brings Gemini to everyday frames from Warby Parker and Gentle Monster. Setup takes a few minutes with a compatible phone and Google Account. Once paired, you activate Gemini by voice or touch, ask about your surroundings with explicit Look requests, and receive navigation, translation, and accessibility help through the speakers.

Privacy controls include a physical off switch, per-app permissions, and History settings. Features expand over time and depend on your phone, region, and account status.

Check official Google pages and the retailer sites for the latest compatibility and availability notes before you buy.
