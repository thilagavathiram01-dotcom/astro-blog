Google began rolling out refreshed icons for Google Messages, Phone by Google, and Google Contacts on November 2, 2026. The update adds gradients, sharper edges, and updated curvature so the icons look clearer on home screens.

The apps themselves stay the same. No features, menus, or tools change. You only see a cleaner look in the app drawer and on your home screen over the coming weeks.

![Hand holding smartphone showing app icons](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1510557880182-3d4d3cba35a5?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What the New Icons Look Like

Google described the icons as sharper and clearer. Each one keeps its familiar shape while gaining a subtle gradient and more readable edges.

Phone by Google keeps the classic handset symbol but now uses a smooth blue gradient. Google Messages shows overlapping speech bubbles with a soft blend from lighter to deeper blue. Google Contacts uses a simple person silhouette that fades toward a lighter shade at the bottom.

The designs align with the rest of Google’s apps and the updated core Google logo. The company said user feedback guided the changes in curvature and coloring.

These icons also appear on the web versions at messages.google.com and contacts.google.com once the update reaches those surfaces.

## How the Rollout Works

The icons arrive through normal Play Store app updates. Google said the rollout started on November 2 and continues over the coming weeks. Not every phone sees the new icons on the same day.

You do not need a system update or a new Android version. The change travels with the individual app updates for Messages, Phone, and Contacts.

Check for updates in the Play Store:

1. Open the Play Store app.
2. Tap your profile picture.
3. Select Manage apps & device.
4. Look under Updates available for Google Messages, Phone by Google, and Contacts.
5. Tap Update if the option appears.

Some devices may already show the new icons if they received the update early. Beta testers of Google Messages saw the redesigned icon first.

If the icons still look old, wait a few days and check again. Play Store updates reach devices in stages.

## Tips While You Wait

Restart your phone after updating the apps. Some launchers cache the old icon and need a refresh.

If you use a custom launcher, clear its cache or re-add the app shortcuts. Most stock Android launchers pick up the new icons automatically.

The new look does not affect RCS messaging, call quality, or contact syncing. Those features continue to work exactly as before.

For related connectivity tools on Android, see our guide to [setting up Quick Share automatic photo backup to Windows](/blog/quick-share-automatic-photo-backup-windows/). That feature also arrived in the same November Play services update.

Samsung and other manufacturer skins sometimes apply their own theming. The core Google icons should still update once the Play Store versions land.

## What Does Not Change

Google confirmed that functionality stays identical. You still open the same apps, use the same settings, and reach the same features.

Pinned chats, search placement tests, and other Messages experiments continue separately. The icon update is purely visual.

Family management tools also moved into Settings with the same Play services version. Our [Android Family Hub settings guide](/blog/android-family-hub-settings-guide/) covers that change in detail.

## Conclusion

The new gradient icons for Google Messages, Phone by Google, and Contacts give the core communication apps a cleaner, more consistent look. The update rolls out gradually through the Play Store starting November 2, 2026, with no changes to how the apps work.

Check the Play Store for updates on your phone. Once the new versions install, the refreshed icons appear on your home screen and app drawer. The rest of your messaging and calling experience remains exactly the same.
