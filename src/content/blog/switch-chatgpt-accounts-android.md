OpenAI rolled out account switching for the ChatGPT Android app in late October 2026. You can now stay signed in to two accounts at once and switch between them without logging out each time. This guide shows the exact steps from OpenAI’s Help Center, what stays separate, and how to avoid common issues.

Many people use one ChatGPT account for work and another for personal tasks. Before this change, switching meant signing out and signing back in. The new switcher removes that friction while keeping each account’s data independent.

## Check if the feature is available

Account switching is rolling out gradually on Android and iOS. Open the ChatGPT app, go to Settings, and look for “Switch account.” If the option is missing, the rollout has not reached your account yet. Update the app from Google Play and check again later. Enterprise workspace owners can also disable the feature for managed accounts.

The official Help Center notes that the feature is available on the web for all plans and is expanding to the mobile apps. Desktop support is planned but not yet available.

## Add a second ChatGPT account on Android

You do not need to log out of your current account to add a second one.

1. Open the ChatGPT app.
2. Tap your profile icon or the menu to open **Settings**.
3. Select **Switch account**.
4. Tap **Add account**.
5. Sign in with email, Google, Microsoft, or Apple, or create a new account.

A new account starts on the Free plan. No payment is required to create it. Once added, both accounts appear in the Switch account menu. A checkmark marks the currently active account.

If two accounts are already signed in, the Add account option is hidden. Log out of one account first to free a slot.

## Switch between the two accounts

To change the active account:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Switch account**.
3. Tap the account you want to use.

Switching is immediate. The app reloads chats, memory, files, and settings for the selected account. Only one account is active at a time. Notifications from either account can arrive. Tapping a notification for the other account switches you to that account and opens the relevant content.

## What stays separate

Switching does not merge accounts. According to OpenAI:

- Chats do not move between accounts.
- Memory and conversation history stay with each account.
- Uploaded files and attachments remain separate.
- Billing and subscriptions are not shared or combined.
- Workspaces stay scoped to their own account.

A Plus or other paid plan on one account does not apply to the second account. If you want paid features on both, you need separate subscriptions. Always confirm which account is active before starting a conversation or making a purchase.

For more control over data on each account, see our guide to the [ChatGPT Privacy Center](/blog/chatgpt-privacy-center-setup/).

## Log out of an account

Logging out removes that account from the switcher.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Switch account**.
3. Tap the more options menu (•••) next to the account.
4. Choose **Log out** and confirm.

If you log out of the active account and another is signed in, the app switches to the remaining account. Logging out of the last account returns you to the sign-in screen.

## Subscriptions and purchases

Each account keeps its own plan. Switching does not transfer a subscription. Before purchasing a plan, check that you are on the correct account. If you already paid but cannot access features, verify the account email that owns the subscription. Manage mobile subscriptions through Google Play.

Creating a second account is free, but paid features require a separate subscription on that account. Check existing subscriptions to avoid duplicate charges.

## Enterprise and managed accounts

Workspace owners can allow or block account switching in the Admin Console under Workspace settings. The setting applies on the web and in the mobile apps where the feature is available. Data created in a managed account follows your organization’s policies. Switching accounts does not change which policies apply. Contact your administrator if the option is unavailable on a work account.

## Tips for using two accounts

- Check the active account before uploading sensitive files or starting long conversations.
- Use different email addresses so the accounts are easy to distinguish.
- Review notification settings for each account if you receive alerts from both.
- If Switch account does not appear, wait for the gradual rollout or update the app.
- Keep the app updated from the official Google Play listing published by OpenAI.

## Common questions

**Can I have more than two accounts?**  
Yes. You can own more than two ChatGPT accounts, but the switcher supports only two signed-in accounts at a time. Log out of one to add another.

**Does switching share my memory or chats?**  
No. Memory, chats, files, and settings stay with the selected account.

**Will a paid plan work on both accounts?**  
No. Subscriptions are not shared. Each account needs its own plan.

**What if Switch account is missing?**  
The feature is rolling out. Update the app and check again. Enterprise admins may have disabled it.

## Conclusion

Account switching on Android lets you keep work and personal ChatGPT use separate without repeated logouts. Stay signed in to two accounts, switch from Settings, and confirm the active account before important actions. Chats, memory, files, and subscriptions remain independent.

Check the official OpenAI Help Center article “Using multiple ChatGPT accounts on web and mobile” for the latest status, as the rollout continues.
