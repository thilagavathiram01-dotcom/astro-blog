Gemini Notebook now lets you shape Slide Decks and Video Overviews before it generates them. Instead of accepting a finished result from one prompt, you pick topics from your sources, choose style and length, and review the plan. Google announced the co-create flow on 19 October 2026. It is rolling out to all users over the following week.

This guide follows the official Gemini Notebook blog post and Help pages. You stay in control of what appears in the final slides or video.



![Person reviewing presentation slides on a laptop](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1516321318423-f06f85e504b3?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)



## What co-create adds

Previously, you asked for a Slide Deck or Video Overview and waited for the full output. If the focus or order was wrong, you regenerated the whole artifact.

The new flow walks you through decisions based on your sources:

- Suggested topics drawn from the notebook content
- Choices for format, length, language, and visual style
- Optional review of the outline (slides) or producer notes (video)
- Feedback on the plan before anything renders

You can still skip the review and generate quickly from the Studio panel. The review toggle is the main addition for tighter control.

Availability depends on your plan for some advanced options. Official Help notes that the full co-create review steps are available to Google AI Ultra subscribers in certain flows, while the broader co-create capabilities are rolling out to all Gemini Notebook users.

## Prepare your notebook

Open [notebook.google.com](https://notebook.google.com/) and create or open a notebook with sources. Add PDFs, Google Docs, web links, YouTube videos, or pasted text. The suggestions and outline come from these sources only.

If you use the Gemini app connector, you can start or update notebooks from chat and open the same library later. See [Connect Gemini Notebook to the Gemini App on Android](/blog/connect-gemini-notebook-gemini-app-android/) for the setup steps.

Keep sources focused. Broad or mixed material produces vaguer topic suggestions.

## Create a Slide Deck with review

Start from chat or the Studio panel.

1. In the chat box, type a request such as “Create a slide deck about the key findings in my sources.”
2. Gemini Notebook returns suggested topics based on the content. Select one or type your own focus.
3. Choose format, length, language, and visual style from the options presented.
4. Turn on the Review toggle if you want to check the plan first.
5. Click Review outline notes in the Studio panel. Edit the sequence, add an executive summary, or reorder sections through the feedback box.
6. Select Proceed when the outline matches what you need. The deck generates from the confirmed plan.

You can also start in the Studio panel: select Slide Deck, set the options, enable Review outline notes, and generate. If you already know the exact structure, include it in the initial prompt so the notebook goes straight to the review step.

Generation takes several minutes. You can continue working in the notebook while it runs.

## Create a Video Overview with review

The process mirrors slides but uses producer notes for scenes.

1. In chat, ask for a video overview of a specific topic from your sources, or select Video Overview in Studio.
2. Confirm suggested topics or enter your own.
3. Set length, language, and style. Options may include standard or cinematic depending on availability.
4. Enable the Review toggle.
5. Open Review producer notes. Adjust scene order, request emphasis on a data point, or move an introduction.
6. Select Proceed. The video renders from the approved notes.

Video generation can take longer than slides, sometimes more than 30 minutes. Return later; the artifact appears in the Studio panel when ready.

Official Help warns that Video Overviews are AI-generated and may contain inaccuracies or audio glitches. Review the output before sharing.

## Tips for better results

Specify audience and purpose in the prompt. “Create a 5-minute video for executives summarizing Q3 risks” produces clearer producer notes than a generic request.

Use the review step for any deck or video you will present. A short feedback note such as “Move the methodology section before results and add a one-slide summary” prevents full regenerations.

If the outline still drifts, add or remove sources and try again. The plan is grounded in what the notebook can read.

For quick internal drafts, skip the review toggle and generate from Studio. Save the review for external-facing work.

Export finished Slide Decks to Google Slides or download Video Overviews from the Studio menu when available.

## Common issues

The Review toggle does not appear. Confirm you are on the latest version and that the rollout has reached your account. Try both chat and Studio paths. Some advanced review options require Google AI Ultra.

Suggestions ignore a key source. Check that the source is selected in the Sources panel and that it is a supported type.

Generation stalls. Large notebooks or complex requests take more time. Refresh the page or check back later.

Output contains errors. Edit the final artifact or regenerate with a tighter prompt and reviewed outline. Always verify facts against the original sources.

## Conclusion

The co-create flow in Gemini Notebook turns Slide Decks and Video Overviews into guided steps instead of single-shot results. You select topics, set style, and review the plan before rendering. Use the Review toggle for important outputs and skip it when speed matters.

Start with a focused notebook, run one reviewed deck, and adjust the outline once. The same process works for Video Overviews. Official documentation and the blog post from 19 October 2026 describe the current behavior.
