Gemini Notebook now lets you shape Slide Decks and Video Overviews before they render. On 19 October 2026 Google added a co-creation flow that starts from your sources, suggests topics, and gives you a Review toggle so you can check outline notes or producer notes and give feedback first.

This replaces the older one-prompt jump straight to a finished artifact. You stay in control of focus, format, length, language, and visual style. The feature is rolling out globally to all Gemini Notebook users over the coming week, with earlier access for Pro and Ultra subscribers.

## Start from chat or the Studio panel

Open notebook.google.com and load a notebook that already contains sources. Those sources can be PDFs, Docs, slides, YouTube links, audio, or web pages. Everything the model produces stays grounded in them.

In the chat panel ask Gemini Notebook to create slides or a video overview. It suggests topics drawn from your sources. Confirm one, type your own, or combine them. Then choose format, length, language, and visual style.

You can also open the Studio panel, click the Slide Deck or Video Overview card, and start the same flow. If you already know exactly what you want, put the details in your prompt and the notebook skips straight to the review step.

## Turn on the Review toggle for full control

The new Review toggle is the main change. Leave it off and Gemini Notebook generates the artifact immediately, the same as before. Turn it on and the plan appears in the Studio panel before anything is rendered.

For slides click “Review outline notes”. You see the planned structure. For videos click “Review producer notes”. You see the planned scenes.

Give feedback in the box. Ask for an executive summary at the start, swap the order of two scenes, shorten a section, or change the visual style. When the plan matches what you need, select “Proceed” and the final deck or video is produced.

This step catches mismatches early. You spend less time regenerating full outputs and more time refining the direction.

## Build a slide deck step by step

1. Add or select sources that cover the material you need. Remove anything off-topic so the suggestions stay relevant.
2. In chat type something like “Create a slide deck on the key findings from these papers for a 10-minute team update.”
3. Pick the suggested focus or type your own. Choose Detailed Deck (more text) or Presenter Slides (more visual, with talking points). Set length and language.
4. Turn on Review if you want to check the outline. Edit notes or add feedback, then Proceed.
5. Review the generated slides in the Studio panel. Download as PDF or PowerPoint, or revise individual slides if needed.

The same sequence works for Video Overviews. Choose Explainer, Cinematic, or Short. Review producer notes, reorder scenes, then generate.

If you want a quick result and already trust the sources, skip the Review toggle and generate directly from the Studio card.

## Tips for better results

Curate sources first. Gemini Notebook only uses what you upload, so clean notebooks produce cleaner plans. Name sources clearly so you can spot which ones feed each suggestion.

Be specific in the first prompt. “10-slide Presenter deck, minimal style, one chart per slide, executive summary first” steers better than “make slides.”

Use the review step when the audience or length is fixed. It is faster to adjust producer notes than to regenerate a full video.

Combine with other Studio tools. Generate an Audio Overview or quiz from the same sources first, then pull key points into the slide or video plan. See our earlier guide on [short Video Overviews](/blog/gemini-notebook-short-video-overviews-guide/) for related options.

Check availability. The co-creation flow is rolling out. If the Review toggle is missing, wait a day or confirm you are on a supported plan and region. Pro and Ultra subscribers saw it first.

## Conclusion

Co-creation in Gemini Notebook gives you a clear middle step between prompt and finished artifact. You confirm topics, set style and format, review the outline or producer notes, give feedback, then proceed. The result stays grounded in your sources and matches the structure you need.

Try it on a notebook you already use for research or study. Start in chat, turn on Review, and adjust the plan before generating. The rollout continues over the next week, so check back if the toggle is not yet visible.
