Holiday photos pile up fast. Thanksgiving dinners, family gatherings and year-end trips create hundreds of images that sit unsorted. Google Photos added five updates on 23 November 2026 to reduce that work. You can now ask Gemini to handle multi-step requests, apply new Remix templates, filter shared albums by people or pets, pin items in Collections, and order gifts from the Fujifilm Print Store.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="478" />

These tools help you spend less time managing memories and more time with the people in them. This guide shows the practical steps for each feature.

## Ask Gemini for Multi-Step Holiday Tasks

Gemini in Google Photos now handles combined requests in one prompt. You no longer need separate actions for creating a highlight, sharing it and adding a summary.

Open the Gemini app or the Photos integration. Type a clear request such as: “Make a highlight album of our Thanksgiving celebration, email the link to my family, and include a quick recap of the day.” Gemini processes the steps and returns the results.

This works best when you specify the event, the recipients and the output format. For background multi-step work outside Photos, see our guide on [how to use free Gemini Tasks mode](/blog/how-to-use-free-gemini-tasks-mode-step-by-step/).

## Apply the 12 New Remix Templates

Remix turns existing photos into new styles. Google added 12 templates focused on holiday use, including scrapbook-style cards and pastel illustrations.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="478" />

1. Open Google Photos and tap the Create tab.
2. Select Photo Remix or browse the templates carousel.
3. Choose a holiday template such as scrapbook or pastel.
4. Pick the source photo from your library.
5. Tap generate and wait for the result. Save or share it directly.

Some templates require a backed-up photo and a personal Google account. Availability varies by region and device. Video Remix remains a separate paid feature for AI subscribers and works on short clips in the same Create tab.

## Filter Shared Albums by People or Pets

Shared albums now show a face carousel at the top. Tap any face to filter the album to photos of that person or pet. This removes the need to scroll through hundreds of images.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="478" />

Open the shared album. Look for the row of faces. Select one to see only matching photos. You can switch between people quickly. The filter works for yourself, family members and pets that Google Photos has grouped.

This is useful after a large gathering when multiple people upload to the same album.

## Customize the Collections Tab

The Collections tab lets you pin favorite albums and memories so they stay at the front. You can also hide items you do not use often.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="478" />

1. Open Collections in Google Photos.
2. Long-press or use the menu on an album or memory.
3. Choose pin to keep it visible.
4. Hide less-used items the same way.

On Android, the “On this device” option appears so you can reach local media without opening cloud backups. Pinning keeps holiday albums one tap away during the season.

## Order Personalized Gifts from the Print Store

The Fujifilm for Google Photos Print Store turns selected photos into physical items. Options include photo books, blankets, mugs and more.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="478" />

Select photos or an album, open the Print Store option, choose the product and complete the order. The process uses the same photo library you already manage in the app.

## Tips for Best Results

Keep Face Groups enabled so people and pet filters work reliably. Back up new photos so Remix templates can access them. Test a short multi-step Gemini prompt first before running a full family request. Check regional availability for Remix and Video Remix before planning large batches.

Update the Google Photos app to the latest version on Android or iOS. Features roll out gradually, so some users see them later than others.

## Conclusion

These Google Photos updates reduce the friction of holiday photo management. Multi-step Gemini requests combine creation and sharing. New Remix templates add festive styles quickly. Album filters surface the right faces. Collections pinning keeps important albums visible. The Print Store turns digital memories into gifts.

Start with one feature that matches your current library. A single filtered album or a Remix card often shows the value immediately. The tools are available now in supported regions and devices.
