Google DeepMind made Genie 4 available in Project Genie on November 18, 2026. Eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers can now generate interactive worlds, edit them with mid-session prompts, and hear native audio that matches the scene.

This guide shows how to access the new experience, create worlds, and use the Project Genie agent. It also covers Genie 3 upgrades that remain available in the model picker.

## What Genie 4 Adds to Project Genie

Genie 4 is Google DeepMind’s most realistic interactive world model yet. You can type a new prompt while navigating a world to change weather, add characters, or redirect action in real time. The model generates synchronized native audio, including ambient soundscapes and character dialogue.

Project Genie runs in the browser. It combines Genie world models with Gemini models so you can generate, enter, and shape worlds without extra software.

![Person exploring a virtual environment](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1535223289827-42f1e9919769?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Requirements and Access Steps

You need an active Google AI Ultra subscription. AI Pro members can join a waitlist on the same page.

1. Open the Project Genie page at labs.google/projectgenie.
2. Sign in with the Google account that holds the Ultra subscription.
3. Select Genie 4 from the model options if a picker appears.
4. Enter a text or image prompt to generate your first world.
5. Use keyboard controls to move once the world loads.

Eligible Ultra subscribers gain access starting the day of the announcement. The experience includes the Project Genie agent and a community gallery.

## Creating Worlds with the Project Genie Agent

The Project Genie agent turns a conversation into a complete interactive space. Describe the story or simulation you want. The agent orchestrates multimodal models to produce starting visuals and a matching background score.

You can bring Gemini into the session. It watches the screen, suggests next actions, or triggers events automatically.

Example prompts that work well:

- “A quiet hillside café at golden hour that becomes a glass pavilion in the desert.”
- “An ant’s-eye view inside a colony with photorealistic detail.”
- “A gothic manor mystery that I can explore room by room.”

Once generated, you can remix environments, rules, or audio before publishing to the gallery.

## Editing Worlds in Real Time

While you explore a Genie 4 world, type a new prompt to alter it on the fly. Shift the weather, introduce a character, or change the direction of events. Audio updates to match the new scene.

Genie 3 remains available for longer sessions. Updates include:

- Sessions up to three minutes (previously 60 seconds).
- World chaining for continuous journeys.
- 360-degree panorama image grounding.
- Real-time in-world events via natural language.

These features let you link forests, underwater ruins, and icy landscapes into one continuous experience.

## Exploring the Community Gallery

The gallery collects curated worlds and community creations. Examples include “Living Memory” (personal photos turned into explorable spaces), “Dino Field Guide” (prehistoric habitats from panoramas), and “Rock Paper Scissors” with Gemini as referee.

You can remix any public world, adjust its structure or soundtrack, then publish your version for others.

## Responsible Use Notes

All video and audio generated in Project Genie include SynthID watermarking. Inputs and outputs pass through safety screenings. Genie models are still research prototypes. Challenges remain around camera latency, complex physics, precise real-time audio, and long-term scene permanence.

For deeper technical details, see the Genie 4 model research overview on the DeepMind site.

## Tips for Better Results

Start with clear, specific prompts that describe lighting, scale, and mood. Use the agent for multi-step world building rather than single-shot prompts. Test mid-session edits early so you learn how the model responds to live changes. Check the gallery for remix ideas before starting from scratch.

If you work with other Gemini features, the same Ultra subscription that unlocks Genie 4 also provides access to models such as Gemini 4 Neon for coding and knowledge work. See our guide on [using Gemini 4 Hydrogen](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-4-hydrogen-guide/) for related agentic workflows.

## Conclusion

Genie 4 turns Project Genie into a more interactive browser experience for Ultra subscribers. Real-time prompting, native audio, the creation agent, and the gallery lower the barrier to building shareable worlds. Access is limited to Ultra plans today, with a waitlist for Pro members. Start at labs.google/projectgenie and experiment with mid-session edits to see what the model can sustain.
