Google rolled out Personal Intelligence for Gemini on Android Auto on October 20, 2026. This update lets the assistant pull details from your connected Google apps so you can ask natural questions without pulling over or providing extra context.

Personal Intelligence remains optional. You control which apps Gemini can access, and you can turn the feature off at any time. Cars with Google built-in will receive support in the coming months, and personal responses there only appear when your phone is connected.

![Driver using phone navigation mounted on car dashboard](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1600320254374-ce2d293c324e?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What Personal Intelligence Does in the Car

Gemini already works on Android Auto for navigation, media, and general questions. Personal Intelligence adds the ability to retrieve specific information from your apps and reason across them.

Google lists these connected sources: Workspace (Gmail and Google Calendar), Google Photos, Google Maps, YouTube, and Search. With permission granted, Gemini can answer requests like these:

- “What’s my flight gate and is my flight on time?” It checks the confirmation email and current status in one response.
- “Take me to my doctor’s appointment.” It finds the address from Calendar.
- “Find the car wash name from the photo I took last week.” It locates the place from Photos and starts navigation.
- “Find me an indoor restaurant along my route that matches my usual vibe.” It factors in preferences if available.
- “Take me to the coffee shop we were talking about yesterday.” It recalls prior context.

If you leave Personal Intelligence off, navigation, music, calls, and standard Gemini questions still work. Answers simply stay general instead of tailored.

## Who Can Use It and Where

The feature rolls out to eligible personal Google accounts for users aged 18 and older. It does not support Workspace business, enterprise, or education accounts at this time.

Availability excludes the European Economic Area, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and Nigeria for now. The rollout began on Android Auto on October 20, 2026, and extends to cars with Google built-in over the following months.

If you already enabled Personal Intelligence in the Gemini app on your phone, those preferences carry over to the car automatically. Otherwise, you set them in the app before or after connecting.

## How to Enable Personal Intelligence

Follow these steps on your Android phone:

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Tap your profile picture in the top right.
3. Select Settings.
4. Look for the Connected Apps section (sometimes labeled under Personal Intelligence).
5. Toggle on the apps you want Gemini to use: Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Drive), Google Photos, Google Maps, Search, and YouTube.
6. Confirm any permission prompts.

You can adjust or revoke access for individual apps at any time in the same menu. Google states that apps remain disconnected until you choose to turn the feature on.

![Smartphone mounted on car dashboard for navigation](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1539787200876-3c033a7bebcd?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

After enabling, connect your phone to the car via Android Auto (wired or wireless). The next time you ask Gemini a relevant question, it can draw from the connected sources.

For cars with Google built-in, the same settings apply once the feature arrives, but personal responses require your phone to be connected in the vehicle.

## Using It Safely While Driving

Keep your hands on the wheel and eyes on the road. Activate Gemini with “Hey Google,” the microphone button on the car screen, or the voice button on the steering wheel.

Speak naturally. Gemini handles the retrieval and reasoning. After it responds, you can continue the conversation for more details or navigation adjustments without restarting the request.

Double-check critical information such as flight gates or appointment addresses before relying on them fully. Google notes that results are examples and you should verify important details.

If you prefer limited access, enable only Calendar and Maps while leaving Photos and Gmail off. You can also delete stored memory items from past Gemini conversations in the app settings if you do not want older topics recalled.

## Privacy Controls

Google designed Personal Intelligence with privacy controls. Your data is not used to train Gemini models. Inputs and outputs during use may be reviewed under standard Gemini policies, but the connected app content itself stays under your control.

Turn the feature off completely in Gemini settings whenever you want. On Android Auto the change takes effect for the car session. On cars with Google built-in, personal answers stop when the phone disconnects or when you disable the feature.

## Tips for Better Results

- Enable only the apps you actively use for driving tasks.
- Keep your Google Calendar updated with appointment locations.
- Label Photos of places you may want to revisit later.
- Test a few queries in the Gemini app first to confirm connections work.
- Update the Gemini and Android Auto apps through the Play Store for the latest rollout.

## Conclusion

Personal Intelligence makes Gemini on Android Auto more useful for real-world driving tasks by pulling context from the apps you already use. The setup takes a few minutes in the Gemini app, and you retain full control over what gets shared.

The feature is rolling out now on Android Auto and coming to cars with Google built-in soon. If your account qualifies and you are outside the excluded regions, enable the apps you want and try a natural request on your next drive.
