Google is rolling out new privacy controls in the Gemini app that separate chat history from generative AI model training. You can now keep your past conversations available while independently deciding whether future chat content helps train models.

This change replaces the older Keep Activity setting. The update started in mid-November 2026 and is reaching more accounts globally. Check your settings today so the defaults match your preference.

## What the New Controls Do

The History setting controls whether chats appear in your Gemini History and how long they stay. A second setting controls whether chat content, including text and attachments, is used to train generative AI models.

Training on chat content is off by default. Audio and Gemini Live videos and screenshares have their own opt-in control. If you turn History off, future chats are still retained for about two months so Gemini can respond, process feedback, and support safety reviews.

You can still change the auto-delete period for saved history and manually delete individual chats or ranges at any time.

## How to Check and Adjust the Settings on the Web

Open gemini.google.com in a browser and sign in with the account you use for Gemini.

Click the settings gear icon, then select History near the bottom of the menu. This opens the dedicated Gemini History page on myactivity.google.com.

Review the On/Off status for History. If it is off and you want to keep conversations, turn it on and confirm the disclosure.

Under the training section, look for “Chat content including text & attachments” or similar wording. Leave it unchecked if you do not want future chats used for model training. Confirm the change if prompted.

Optionally review the audio and Live video improvement toggle. It remains off unless you enable it.

Set your preferred auto-delete interval if History is on. Options typically include 3 months, 18 months, 36 months, or never.

## How to Adjust the Controls in the Mobile App

Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS.

Tap the menu or your profile picture, then choose Settings. Select History under the data or privacy section.

Turn History on or off as needed. With History on, uncheck the chat content training option to opt out of model training while keeping chats available.

Confirm any prompts. The exact labels can vary slightly between platforms, but the purpose is the same: History for visibility and retention, separate toggle for training.

On some Android versions the entry may still reference older activity names until the rollout fully lands. Refresh the app if the new options do not appear.

## What Happens When You Change the Settings

If your previous Keep Activity was on when the new controls arrive, both History and training default to on. Turn training off if you want to keep history without contributing future content.

If Keep Activity was off, both new options start off. Turn History on separately if you want access to past chats.

Turning History off does not instantly delete existing chats. New chats after the change are held for roughly two months for service and safety purposes. Temporary chats follow similar retention.

Chats that have already been reviewed by humans may be retained longer, up to three years, for quality and safety work. Deleting history does not remove data already used in other Google services such as Wallet or YouTube.

## Tips for Everyday Use

Check the settings once the rollout reaches your account. Defaults can carry over from the old preference, so verify rather than assume.

Keep History on and training off if you want to refer back to conversations without adding them to model training.

Shorten the auto-delete period if you prefer less long-term storage. You can always delete specific chats manually.

Review Connected Apps separately if you use Personal Intelligence features. History status can affect some integrations.

## Conclusion

The updated Gemini controls give clearer choices between keeping your chat history and contributing to model training. Check the settings once the rollout reaches you, keep History on if you want access to past conversations, and turn off the training option for future content.

Official details are on the Google blog and Gemini Help Center. Adjust the toggles to match how you want your data handled.
