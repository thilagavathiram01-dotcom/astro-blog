Google opened certified refurbished Pixel sales on the Japan Google Store on 7 October 2026. Previous-generation phones, including the Pixel 8 and Pixel 9 series, are listed at up to 45% off the original Google Store price, and stock is limited to what the program has finished testing.

A certified unit is not a marketplace bargain with an unknown repair history. Google says these phones are inspected, repaired with genuine parts when a part fails the check, loaded with the latest Android build, and sold with the same one-year limited warranty as a new phone. That is the difference worth paying attention to before you click Buy.

## What Google means by certified refurbished

On the US Google Store, Google defines the label directly. A certified refurbished Pixel may have had a previous owner, or it may have sat in a warehouse longer than a typical new unit. Either way, technicians inspect it, repair it with genuine OEM parts where needed, and test it so it works like new. The Japan launch uses the same standard: technicians check the battery, exterior, display, and internal components, and only authorized Google service providers fit genuine parts.

Each phone ships in 100% plastic-free packaging with a compatible charging cable. Google also says the phone arrives on the latest Android software and keeps receiving regular software updates and Pixel Drops, the same feature drops other Pixel phones get.

The warranty is the other hard line. Japan listings include the same one-year limited warranty and customer support as a new purchase. The US store lists a one-year warranty and free shipping and returns on Google Store orders. Third-party resellers can use the word refurbished without that process, so the store page matters more than the adjective.

## Where you can buy one today

In Japan, the program is online-only on the Google Store. Google's 7 October post points shoppers to the refurbished devices page and names the Pixel 8 and Pixel 9 series among the previous-generation phones on offer, at up to 45% off the launch Google Store price. Free shipping and a straightforward return process apply to Google Store orders in Japan.

In the United States, the program has been running since October 2024 and expanded again in March 2026, when Google added the Pixel 8a and said savings could reach 45% off the original price. The US store also sells certified units through Amazon Renewed, where Amazon's Renewed Guarantee covers the purchase. Listed US starting prices change with stock. On the Google Store refurbished page, recent listings included a Pixel 9 Pro XL from $699 against an original price of $1,099, a Pixel 9 Pro from $649 against $999, and a Pixel 8 from $389 against $699. Treat those figures as examples of the current listing, not a fixed sale.

Google has said inventory is limited. Popular colors and storage sizes can sell out, and the company restocks as more phones clear testing. If a model is missing, that is a stock issue, not a sign the program dropped it.

## How to order without guessing

1. Open the refurbished page for your store, not a search ad. In Japan that is the Google Store magazine page for refurbished devices. In the US it is the Google Store refurbished devices page, or Amazon Renewed if you specifically want that channel.
2. Match the model to the work you need. A Pixel 9 series phone is the newer option in the Japan launch set. A Pixel 8 series phone is the lower price if you mainly want current Android, Pixel Drops, and a clean warranty.
3. Read the listed original price next to the refurbished price. The discount is measured against the launch Google Store price, not against whatever a third-party seller is asking this week.
4. Check color and storage before you assume a configuration exists. Google warned that popular combinations can go first.
5. Confirm the listing says certified refurbished and shows the one-year limited warranty. If a seller cannot state the warranty term, leave the listing.
6. Place the order on the Google Store so shipping and the return window are the store's, not a marketplace seller's.
7. On delivery, power the phone on, connect it to Wi-Fi, and finish setup with your own Google account. A certified unit should not be tied to a previous owner's account.

If setup asks for the previous owner's credentials, stop and use the store's return path. That lock is Factory Reset Protection, and a certified unit sold by Google should not arrive in that state.

## What to check in the first hour

The box should include the phone and a compatible charging cable in plastic-free packaging. Charge it on a known-good cable and confirm the battery percentage moves. Open Settings and check the Android version, then look for a system update so you are not judging the phone on a build that is one patch behind.

Test the display at low and high brightness, then run the front and rear cameras in daylight and indoors. Make a short call and play audio through the speaker and a wired or Bluetooth headset. Genuine-part repairs are supposed to keep touch, color, and battery behavior in line with a new phone, and these checks are how you confirm that on your unit.

Set a screen lock before you install banking or work apps. If you want a tighter baseline, turn on the theft and protection controls covered in our [Android theft protection setup guide](/blog/android-theft-protection-setup/). A refurbished phone is still a Pixel, so the same account recovery and device-finding steps apply.

## Warranty, returns, and software support

Keep the order confirmation. The one-year limited warranty is the same term Google attaches to a new phone in this program, and support runs through the same customer channels. Japan orders include free shipping and a smooth return process. US Google Store orders include free shipping and returns. Amazon Renewed purchases follow Amazon's Renewed Guarantee instead of the Google Store return flow, so do not mix the two policies if something arrives wrong.

Software support is not shortened by the refurbished label. Google says these phones get regular updates and Pixel Drops. That does not extend a model past its normal support window. A Pixel 8 still follows the Pixel 8 update schedule. You are buying remaining support on that generation, not a new support clock.

## When a new phone is the better buy

Buy certified refurbished if you want a previous-generation Pixel, a written one-year warranty, and genuine parts, and you can accept that color and storage come and go. Buy new if you need a current-generation feature that is not on the Pixel 8 or Pixel 9 series, or if the refurbished configuration you want is out of stock and the discount no longer beats a current sale on a new unit.

Skip listings that only say refurbished, renewed, or open-box without Google's certified process. Those phones can be fine, but they are a different product with a different repair standard and often a different warranty.

## Bottom line

Japan's 7 October 2026 launch puts certified refurbished Pixel 8 and Pixel 9 series phones on the Google Store at up to 45% off the original store price. The phone is tested, repaired only with genuine parts, covered for one year, and kept on the normal Pixel update track. Order from the Google Store page, confirm the warranty on the listing, and run a short hardware check on day one. If a color you want is gone, wait for the next tested batch instead of switching to an uncertified seller.
