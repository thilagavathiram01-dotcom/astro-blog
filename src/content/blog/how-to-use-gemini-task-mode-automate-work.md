Google folded Spark into the main Gemini chat. A Chat / Task toggle now sits above the prompt box on Android and in the web app. Task mode lets you hand multi-step work to Gemini and walk away.

Pro and Ultra subscribers get it. Free accounts do not. The rollout is server-side, so update the app and restart if the toggle is missing.

![Person using a laptop to manage tasks](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1516321318423-f06f85e504b3?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What Task mode actually does

Task mode is the new home for the personal agent formerly called Spark. You describe a goal. Gemini plans the steps, uses connected Google apps and third-party services, and keeps working in the background.

It can:
- Pull data from Gmail, Calendar, Drive, and Docs
- Call connected apps such as Instacart, OpenTable, Adobe, or Shopify
- Create files, update sheets, or send drafts
- Run on a schedule or after a trigger
- Report progress so you can jump in or stop it

Sensitive actions (orders, payments, external sends) still need your confirmation. You stay in control.

For longer, higher-quality reasoning on complex tasks, pair it with Gemini 4 Argon. See our guide on [how to use Gemini 4 Argon in the Gemini app](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-4-argon-gemini-app/).

## Requirements before you start

You need:
- A personal Google account (work or school accounts are not supported yet)
- Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription
- Age 18 or over
- Keep Activity turned on in Gemini settings
- The latest Gemini app or gemini.google.com

Some connected apps are limited by country and language. Instacart is US-only. OpenTable is available in the US and UK. Check the Connected Apps list in settings for what is active on your account.

## Switch to Task mode

1. Open the Gemini app (Android) or go to gemini.google.com.
2. Look just above or below the prompt box. You will see Chat and Task buttons.
3. Tap or click Task. The placeholder text changes to something like “Describe a task.”
4. Type your instruction. Be specific about the outcome and any timing.
5. Press Submit.

On Android the toggle appeared first. iOS and some web accounts may still be rolling out. If you only see Chat, force-close the app or clear cache and try again later.

Example prompts that work well:
- “Every weekday at 8 AM, check my unread emails and Calendar for the next 24 hours, then summarize the three highest-priority items in a short note in Drive.”
- “When I get an email about a photography inquiry, extract the client name, date, and service type, add a row to my Client Tracker sheet, and create a new Drive folder named after the client.”
- “Find ingredients for this recipe and add them to my Instacart cart.” (mention the app or use @Instacart)

## Monitor and manage tasks

Your chats and tasks now share one history list. Use the filter icon to show All, Ready, Needs attention, or In progress.

A new Scheduled page groups active, paused, completed, and suggested items. Open it from the sidebar or menu.

While a task runs you can:
- Watch the progress chip at the top of the thread
- Jump in if Gemini asks you to take over (for example, to complete a browser step)
- Stop the task at any time from the prompt box

Settings that used to say “Gemini Spark Settings” are now simply “Task settings.”

## Schedule recurring work

You can create schedules in two ways.

Conversational:
1. Switch to Task.
2. Describe the task and the timing in one sentence (“Every Monday at 9 AM…”).
3. Submit.

From the Scheduled page:
1. Open Scheduled.
2. Choose Create a scheduled task.
3. Name it, write the instructions, set the time or trigger, and save.

Schedules appear under Active until you pause or delete them.

## Connect apps for better results

Gemini already talks to more than 40 services. To add one:
1. Go to Settings → Connected Apps (or Personal Intelligence → Connected Apps).
2. Toggle the service on and complete the sign-in.
3. In a task, mention the app by name or type @ followed by the name.

Useful combinations:
- Gmail + Calendar + Drive for morning briefings
- Instacart for grocery lists from recipes
- OpenTable for reservations that check your calendar first
- Sheets + Drive for lead logging

Keep Activity must stay on. Without it, most third-party connections stop working.

## Tips that improve reliability

- Start small. One clear outcome beats a long chain of vague steps.
- Name the apps you want Gemini to use. “@Gmail @Sheets” reduces guesswork.
- Check the work panel or progress updates before you walk away for hours.
- If a task stalls on a browser step, switch to the remote browser option when offered so it can continue without your phone.
- Update the model selector to Gemini 4 Argon (if available on your plan) for multi-step research or coding-related tasks.
- Review the Connected Apps availability table in Gemini Help. Region and language restrictions are common.

## Limits to know

Task mode is not available to free users. It is still rolling out, so some accounts see the toggle later than others. Work and school accounts, and users in certain regions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, Nigeria for some Spark features), are excluded for now.

Gemini will pause and ask for confirmation on purchases, external messages, or other irreversible actions. It does not silently spend money or send emails on your behalf.

## Conclusion

The Chat / Task toggle removes the separate Spark silo. You can assign work, schedule it, and let Gemini continue across your Google apps and connected services while you do something else. Start with one daily briefing or one lead-logging flow, confirm the results, then expand.

Pro and Ultra subscribers can try it today. Update the app, flip the toggle, and give it a concrete task.
