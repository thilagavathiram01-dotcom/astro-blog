Your to-do list grows while you sit in meetings or drive kids to practice. Gemini Tasks lets you hand the busywork to Google’s assistant so it keeps going in the background. As of 17 November 2026 the feature is free for adults in the United States, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia and India. It runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="20" />

Chat answers one question and stops. A Task keeps working through steps, checks back when it needs you, and pauses before anything critical.

## What Tasks Can Do

Google describes Tasks as a way to make calls, pay bills, finish multi-step projects and handle recurring routines. In the U.S. it can text businesses, place orders with a spending cap you set, and add items to grocery carts through Connected Apps such as Target or Instacart. Receipts land in Google Wallet. You still earn card points and protections.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="20" />

You can forward emails to gemini@google.com or text Gemini once you set up Channels. The assistant works inside an isolated virtual machine on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine so the environment stays dedicated to your account.

It asks for confirmation before purchases or other high-stakes actions. You can mark routine steps as “Always allow.” You can also stop a task at any time.

## Requirements and Limits

You need a personal Google account and must be 18 or older. Work and school accounts are not supported. Gemini History must stay on. Up to 15 tasks can run at once. Free usage has rolling limits that reset every five hours and every week. Calling is unavailable in a few U.S. states. Texting requires a U.S. number on an eligible carrier.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="20" />

Advanced scheduling and some agent features remain stronger on paid Google AI plans, but the core free rollout covers the six countries listed above.

## Step-by-Step: Start a Task

1. Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS, or go to gemini.google.com. Sign in with your personal account.
2. Flip the toggle from Chat to Task near the prompt box. On mobile it sits above the text field.
3. Describe the full job in plain language. Include budget, deadline, preferred store, and anything it should not do. Example: “Compare three USB-C chargers under $40 that ship by Friday and add the cheapest one to my Target cart with a $50 spending cap.”
4. Grant access to any Connected Apps or Google services it requests (Calendar, Gmail, Wallet, Maps).
5. Submit. Gemini works in the background. You can switch to other apps or close the chat. It checks back for clarification or approval.
6. Review the result. Give feedback with a thumbs-down if something misses the mark so the system improves.

You can jump in mid-task to add instructions. For recurring work, ask it to run the same job every Monday morning or set it from the scheduled actions page.

## Set Up Text and Email Channels (U.S.)

Starting in the United States you can hand off tasks without opening the app:

1. Open Gemini and tap Settings.
2. Tap Channels.
3. Tap Set up under Text with Gemini and verify your phone number.
4. Enable Email with Gemini if offered.
5. Forward an email to gemini@google.com or text the number. Gemini replies with a link to the new task.

This is useful when you are away from the phone or want to act on a school or bill email immediately.

## Practical Examples

- Service appointment: “Our minivan needs an early Friday service next month. Call the two nearest dealerships, check my calendar, and book the first open slot.” Gemini discloses it is an AI, finds availability, and adds the event.
- Grocery run: Text a photo of an empty cereal box and say “Add this plus strawberries and ice cream to the Instacart order.” It updates the cart and hands off for final checkout.
- Bill review: Give it access to utility statements in email. Ask it to find why the last bill jumped and suggest one change. It works through the PDFs and reports back.
- School logistics: Forward a batch of parent emails. It extracts dates, adds them to Calendar, invites your partner, and flags a carpool conflict.

These match the real-world uses Google shared in its 17 November announcement.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="20" />

## Tips for Better Results

Write the outcome, not a question. “Build a comparison sheet of three flights to Tokyo under $900 in March” works better than “flights to Tokyo?”

State constraints up front: spending cap, preferred time window, apps to use, and steps that always need approval.

Keep Personal Intelligence on if you want it to remember your car model, address, or usual stores. You control what it can access in settings.

Check the task regularly the first few times. Sites are still adapting to personal assistants, so Gemini may finish most steps and hand the final click back to you.

## Safety and Privacy Notes

Each task runs in a sandboxed virtual machine dedicated to your account. Prompt-injection filters and Safe Browsing limit risky links. You can disconnect apps, delete history, or adjust task settings at any time. Google invites reports through its AI Vulnerability Reward Program.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="20" />

Never paste passwords or full card numbers into the prompt. Use the official Connected Apps and Google Password Manager integrations instead.

## Conclusion

Gemini Tasks turns a chatbot into a background helper that finishes multi-step jobs while you do other things. The free rollout in six countries on 17 November 2026 makes it available to hundreds of millions of people. Start with one clear, constrained task, watch how it checks back, and expand from there. The control stays with you.
