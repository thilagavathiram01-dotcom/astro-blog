Students capture lectures on paper, then spend time retyping everything into clean study material. On Android, Gemini now lets you skip that step for PDFs.

Google added in-chat file generation, preview, and editing to the Gemini app on 29 October 2026. PDF preview and editing works on both the web and the mobile app. Other formats such as Google Docs, Sheets, slide decks, Markdown, TXT, and code remain available on the web only. The feature is open to all users in every language where the Gemini app is offered.

This guide focuses on the Android workflow that turns photos of handwritten notes into a refined PDF study guide you can edit without leaving the app.

## What Works on Android

Open the Gemini app on your Android phone. Sign in with your Google account. PDF generation, preview, and editing are supported on mobile. You can expand the PDF to full screen, type changes, highlight text to ask questions or request refinements, and export the result.

Google Docs, Sheets, presentations, and code editing are not available in the mobile app at launch. For those formats, switch to gemini.google.com on a computer.

## Step 1: Photograph Your Notes Clearly

Place each page of handwritten notes on a flat surface under even lighting. Avoid shadows and glare. Take one photo per page so the text stays sharp. You can attach multiple photos in a single Gemini prompt.

Good photo quality improves transcription accuracy, especially for technical terms or dense handwriting.

## Step 2: Upload Photos and Request a PDF Study Guide

Start a new chat in the Gemini Android app. Tap the attachment icon and select your note photos. Then enter a specific prompt:

"Create a PDF study guide from these handwritten lecture notes. Organize into clear sections with headings, key points in bullets, and a short summary. Highlight important terms and add simple explanations where needed."

Gemini extracts the text from the images and builds a structured PDF. The preview appears in the chat once processing finishes.

## Step 3: Preview and Expand to Full Screen

Tap the PDF preview in the chat. On Android you can expand it to full screen. Review the headings, layout, and accuracy of the transcribed content. Note any misread words or sections that need tightening.

Because editing stays inside the app, you can correct issues immediately without downloading or opening another program.

![Person holding a smartphone showing an AI chat interface](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1682941664177-7920d0e59418?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Step 4: Edit Manually or Highlight for Gemini Refinement

Three ways to refine the PDF on Android:

- Expand to full screen and type corrections yourself.
- Highlight a passage and ask a question about that section.
- Highlight text and tell Gemini to revise it, for example “Make this section more concise and turn the key points into a numbered list.”

Gemini updates the file in place. You can repeat the process several times. An undo option is available in the editor for recent changes.

## Step 5: Export the Finished Study Guide

When the content is ready, use the export controls in the preview. Download the PDF to your device or save it to Google Drive. The file is then available for review on any device or for later upload into other tools.

## Mobile-Specific Tips

Take photos in good light and crop tightly around the text before uploading. Mention the desired structure in your first prompt so Gemini produces a usable layout from the start.

For multi-page lectures, process one or two pages at a time if the full set is large. Verify names, formulas, and dates against your original notes before relying on the guide for exams.

If you later want to turn the PDF into a Google Doc or add interactive elements, open the file on the web version of Gemini. For complementary study features such as flashcards or deeper notebook research, see our guide on [turning notes into PDF study guides](/blog/create-pdf-study-guide-gemini-handwritten-notes/).

## Limitations on Mobile

Only PDFs support full preview and editing on Android. Other file types require the web app. Processing time increases with more pages or complex handwriting. Always cross-check critical information with the original notes.

## Conclusion

On Android, Gemini turns photos of handwritten notes into editable PDF study guides inside the same chat. Upload clear images, review the preview, make manual or prompted edits, and export the result. The workflow removes the usual copy-and-paste cycle for students who prefer paper during lectures.

Try it with your next set of notes. Clear photos and a specific prompt produce the most useful guides.
