Google Health version 5.12 rolled out on November 6, 2026, for Android and iOS. The update lets you log blood pressure readings by hand or sync them from other apps. It also adds pregnancy tracking that pauses cycle predictions and displays pregnancy weeks.

The changes arrive after the app rebranded from Fitbit earlier this year. They expand clinical data options without requiring a Premium subscription for basic logging.

![Person wearing smartwatch displaying fitness metrics](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1722153105551-cfea928e80de?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Key Updates in Google Health 5.12

Blood pressure logging works through the Today tab. Tap the +Log button and select Blood pressure. Enter systolic and diastolic numbers, then save. Premium subscribers can also log readings by chatting with the Google Health Coach.

Synced readings arrive via Health Connect on Android or Apple Health on iOS. Graphs appear under the Health tab in the Heart section. Manual entry and syncing do not need Premium, though some trend analysis tools still do.

Pregnancy logging lives in Cycle Health settings. Activate it there or through the Health Coach chat if you subscribe to Premium. The app pauses period predictions and notifications. Your cycle page switches to show current pregnancy weeks. Premium users also get personalized coach insights and feed cards.

Glucose visualization improved. A new overlay shows logged exercise, sleep, and nutrition events on the graph in the Metabolic Health section. You can log events directly from the graph.

Additional fixes include better food item grouping for nutrition (items logged within 60 minutes group together), corrected Resilience scores that previously skewed low, and progress summaries for fitness plans in the Fitness tab (Premium).

Medical records now support syncing laboratory data from Function Health. Select it when searching for a provider during setup. Note that personal health records stored with Google may not be complete. Consult a doctor for clinical use.

## How to Log Blood Pressure Manually

Follow these steps after updating the app from the Play Store or App Store:

1. Open the Google Health app.
2. Go to the Today tab.
3. Tap the +Log button.
4. Choose Blood pressure.
5. Enter your reading and save.

To sync from another app, connect it through Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS). Readings then appear in the Heart graphs.

Check for the update if the option is missing. The rollout continues in phases over the next week.

## Setting Up Pregnancy Tracking

Open Cycle Health settings in the app. Look for the pregnancy logging option and enable it. Confirm the change. Predictions stop, and the view updates to pregnancy weeks.

Premium subscribers can start the same process by messaging the Health Coach. The coach then personalizes responses and insights based on the logged pregnancy.

Review the updated cycle page after enabling. It displays weeks instead of the usual period calendar. You can still access prior data, but active tracking focuses on the pregnancy stage.

## Tips for the New Features

Update the Google Health app before trying the tools. The Play Store or App Store shows version 5.12 once available for your device.

For blood pressure, use a validated cuff and log readings at consistent times. Compare graphs in the Health tab over days or weeks.

Pregnancy tracking works best when started early. Double-check the settings if notifications continue after enabling. Premium features enhance coach interactions but are not required for basic logging.

Nutrition grouping helps review meals more clearly. Log related items close together so they cluster in the 60-minute window.

Resilience scores should stabilize after the fix. Previous incorrect scores update soon. Continue normal activity while the correction propagates.

Related Android updates include the new gradient icons for Messages, Phone, and Contacts. See our [guide to the icon redesign](/blog/google-messages-phone-contacts-new-icons/) for details on that visual change.

## Other Improvements and Rollout Notes

Function Health sync adds laboratory data to medical records. Search for the provider during setup to connect it.

Fitness plan progress summaries appear in the Fitness tab for Premium users. They help track goals with personalized notes.

The update reaches both Android and iOS users. Availability depends on your region and account. Check the app store listing or in-app updates if features are not present yet.

Google notes that health features are not intended for medical purposes. Always verify data with a healthcare provider. Results may vary, and some AI elements require an internet connection.

## Conclusion

Google Health 5.12 brings practical tools for blood pressure and pregnancy tracking. Manual logging and syncing expand what the app can record without a subscription for core entry. Pregnancy mode adjusts the interface automatically and adds personalization for Premium users.

Update the app, try the +Log button for blood pressure, and enable pregnancy tracking in Cycle Health if it applies. Graphs and summaries make trends easier to follow. Pair the features with consistent data entry for the most useful results.
