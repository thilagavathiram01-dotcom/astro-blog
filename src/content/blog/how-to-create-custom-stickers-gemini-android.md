Google is preparing a dedicated sticker maker inside the Gemini app. An APK teardown of Google app version 17.78.13 by Android Authority revealed strings for copying, sharing, and downloading sticker packs in WebP format. The feature is not yet live, but you can already create usable stickers today with Gemini’s image generation tools and share them to WhatsApp or Telegram.

This guide shows the current practical method and what the upcoming pack feature will add. For broader Gemini model access, see our guide to [using Gemini 4 Neon](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-4-neon-access-and-thinking-levels/).

## What the APK Teardown Shows

The teardown found menu options labeled Copy sticker, Share sticker, and Download pack. A string reads “Download pack for %d stickers,” confirming grouped packs. Stickers will generate as WebP images that save to the gallery and share via Android’s native share sheet. Direct pack import into WhatsApp is not indicated yet.

These strings appear in unreleased code, so the final interface may change. Google has not announced a release date.

## Create Stickers Today with Gemini Image Generation

Open the Gemini app on Android and start a new chat. Tap the image icon or describe what you want. Use a clear prompt that specifies sticker style.

Example prompt: “Create a die-cut sticker of a happy cartoon cat waving, bold outlines, white background, WebP-friendly, suitable for messaging apps.”

Gemini will generate the image. Long-press or tap the download icon to save it to your gallery. The result is a standard image you can treat as a sticker.

Repeat the process for additional designs. Keep prompts consistent for a matching set: same character, different expressions or poses.

Google’s own short video demonstrates similar image-to-sticker workflows with Imagen models inside Gemini.

## Share Individual Stickers to WhatsApp or Telegram

After saving the image:

1. Open WhatsApp or Telegram.
2. Start or open a chat.
3. Tap the attachment or sticker icon.
4. Choose Gallery or Photos.
5. Select the saved Gemini image and send it.

On WhatsApp, you can also create a sticker from the image inside the app: tap the sticker button, then Create, and pick the image. The system treats it as a custom sticker for future use in that chat.

Telegram supports similar gallery import. The upcoming Gemini feature will streamline the share step through the native share sheet.

## What Changes When Sticker Packs Arrive

Once the feature ships, you will likely generate stickers inside a dedicated flow rather than general image chat. The app will offer one-tap copy, share, and pack download. Packs group related stickers so you do not manage files one by one.

WebP output keeps file sizes small for messaging apps. Gallery save remains available for individual stickers.

Until then, the image-generation method works on any Android phone with the Gemini app installed and an internet connection. No special subscription is required for basic generation, though higher-quality models may need Google AI Pro or Ultra.

## Tips for Better Results

Specify “die-cut sticker,” “white background,” and “bold outlines” in every prompt. Add the desired emotion or action: “surprised face,” “thumbs up,” “dancing.”

Generate several variations in one chat so the style stays consistent. Download each one immediately.

If the first result looks off, refine the prompt: “make the eyes larger” or “add a thin white border.”

Test the saved image in your messaging app before creating a full set. Messaging apps sometimes crop or compress images; a clean white background helps.

## Conclusion

You can create custom stickers with Gemini right now by prompting for die-cut images and saving them from the chat. The APK teardown shows Google is building a dedicated pack system with copy, share, and download options. Use the current method while waiting for the fuller experience.
