Google split the old Keep Activity switch into two separate controls. You can now keep your Gemini chats visible while telling Google not to use them for generative AI model training.

The change started rolling out in mid-November 2026 and is widely available as of late November. This guide shows the exact steps on web and mobile, what each setting does, and the limits you should know.

## What Changed in Gemini Privacy Controls

Until mid-November 2026, one setting controlled both chat saving and training. Turning it off to stop training also hid your history. The new controls separate the two.

History decides whether chats appear in your Gemini History and how long they stay. A second option, usually labelled “Chat content including text & attachments” or “text & uploads,” decides whether future chat text and files are used to train generative AI models.

Google announced the update on 16 November 2026. Existing preferences carry over: if Keep Activity was on, both new settings start on. If it was off, both start off. For adults over 18 on personal accounts, the chat-content training box is on by default once History is on.

You can change either setting at any time. Training applies only to future chats. Past conversations already processed are not retroactively removed from training data.

## Who Can Use the New Settings

The controls apply to personal Google Accounts in the Gemini app, on the web at gemini.google.com, and in the Mac app. Work and school (Google Workspace) accounts are managed by your administrator. You may not see or be able to change the options yourself.

History is on by default for users 18 and older. The chat-content training option requires History to be on before you can turn it on. Temporary chats remain available and are not used for training.

If you do not yet see the new History page, the rollout has not reached your account. Check again in a few days or force-stop and reopen the Gemini app.

## How to Keep History and Turn Off AI Training

Follow these steps on a computer first. The mobile path is similar.

1. Open gemini.google.com and sign in with the account you use for Gemini.
2. Click Settings at the bottom of the left panel (or the gear icon).
3. Select Gemini History. You can also go directly to myactivity.google.com/product/gemini.
4. Confirm History is set to On if you want chats saved and visible. Use the dropdown or toggle near the top.
5. Under the heading “Help train generative AI models with” (or similar), uncheck “Chat content including text & attachments” or “Chat content including text & uploads.”
6. Click Turn off if a confirmation appears.
7. Optionally uncheck the separate audio / Gemini Live videos & screenshares option if you use voice features.

On Android or iPhone:

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Tap your profile picture or the menu, then Settings.
3. Under Data & privacy, tap History.
4. Uncheck the chat content training box and confirm Turn off.
5. Leave History on.

After changing the setting, refresh the page or reopen the app and verify the box stays unchecked while History remains on.

## What Happens When You Change the Settings

With History on and chat-content training off:

- Past and future chats stay in your visible History according to your auto-delete period (default 18 months; options include 3 months, 36 months, or never).
- Future chat text and attachments are not used to train generative AI models, unless you submit feedback.
- You can still search and reopen old conversations.

With History off:

- New chats do not appear in your History.
- Google still keeps them with your account for around two months to respond to you, process feedback, and support safety. Human reviewers may see a subset for these purposes.
- Connected apps and some personalisation features stop working.

Temporary chats are never used for training and are also retained for about two months.

Chats already reviewed by humans can be kept for up to three years, separate from your account. Deleting History does not remove those.

## Extra Tips and Limits

- Check the exact wording on your account. Labels can say “attachments” or “uploads.” Identify the control by its purpose.
- Feedback you send can still be used for training even if the main box is off, and may include recent chat context.
- Auto-delete only affects what is stored in History. It does not erase data already used for training or reviewed copies.
- Workspace users should contact their admin for equivalent controls.

## Conclusion

The new Gemini controls give you a clear choice: keep useful chat history without automatically contributing future conversations to model training. Open Settings > History, leave History on, and uncheck the chat-content training box. Confirm the change and revisit the page later to make sure it sticks.

Google continues to process data for core service, safety, and security reasons even when training is off. Review the official privacy hub for the latest details.
