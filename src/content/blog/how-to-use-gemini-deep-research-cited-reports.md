You need a solid report with sources for a project, but tab-hopping across dozens of pages eats your afternoon. Gemini Deep Research handles the heavy lifting. It builds a plan, browses sources, and returns a cited report you can refine.

This guide follows the official Gemini Apps Help steps for desktop. You must be 18 or older and signed in to a personal Google account. Work or school accounts have limited access on the web app only.

## What Deep Research does

Deep Research is an agentic feature in the Gemini app. It does not stop at a quick answer. It creates a research plan, searches the web (and optional sources like Gmail or Drive), reasons across findings, and delivers a structured report with citations.

Google notes that reports typically take 5–10 minutes because the system analyzes many sources. Complex topics can take longer. You can leave the chat while it runs; Gemini notifies you when the report is ready.

Pro and Ultra subscribers get higher report limits and access to stronger models for Thinking.

## Start a Deep Research report

1. Open gemini.google.com in your browser and sign in.
2. Below the prompt box, click Tools, then select Deep Research.
3. (Optional) Click Add files to upload a PDF or image that should inform the research.
4. (Optional) Click Sources to include or exclude Gmail, Drive, or other connected apps. Google Search is on by default. Deselect it if you want only your selected sources.
5. Type a clear prompt that states your goal. Example: “Research current renewable energy trends in India and compare them with major policy updates from the last 12 months. Include citations.”
6. Click Submit. Gemini shows a research plan.
7. Review the plan. Click Edit plan if you want to add angles, exclude topics, or change the scope. Use plain language.
8. Click Start research.
9. When the report is ready, click Open or View report.

The report appears in the chat with inline citations. You can ask follow-up questions in the same thread; Deep Research can pull from the existing work or search further.

## Edit the plan and refine results

The research plan is your control point. Do not skip it. A vague prompt produces a broad report. A specific plan keeps the output focused.

After the report arrives, highlight sections and ask Gemini to expand, shorten, or restructure them. You can also request an Audio Overview, quiz, or other formats from the report.

Export options vary by account. Many users copy sections into Docs or share the chat link.

## Tips for better reports

- State the audience and desired format in the prompt. “Write for a product team. Use bullet points and a comparison table.”
- Upload your own source files when possible. This grounds the report in material you already trust.
- Check citations. Click through a few links to confirm the claim matches the source.
- Keep Activity history on if you want to find past reports later in the sidebar.
- For recurring needs, save strong prompts and reuse the structure.

## Conclusion

Gemini Deep Research turns a multi-tab research session into a single cited report. Start with a clear prompt, edit the plan, and review the sources. The official flow is simple: enable the tool, submit, refine the plan, and open the finished report.

Use it for competitive scans, policy summaries, or learning a new domain. Pair it with your own files when accuracy matters most.
