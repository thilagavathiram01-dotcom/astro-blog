Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals move fast. Switching between Amazon, Walmart, Target, or Flipkart to compare prices and return policies wastes time you could spend deciding.

Gemini’s floating overlay on Android now helps directly on the product page. Open it over a listing and it suggests prompts such as “Help me pick,” “Compare prices,” or “Check warranty and returns.” Tap one and Gemini captures a screenshot of the visible screen as context. No full screen share with Gemini Live is required.

Android Authority confirmed the feature works with Amazon, Flipkart, and quick-commerce apps such as Blinkit. It rolled out with Google app version 17.78.13 as a server-side update, so update the app and check a product page.

## How the shopping prompts work

The overlay already understands screen context for YouTube videos, PDFs, and general pages. The new chips appear automatically when Gemini detects a product page.

When you tap a prompt, Gemini takes a screenshot of what is currently visible and sends it with a more detailed version of the prompt. For “Compare prices” it checks the current listing against other retailers and notes available deals. Conversations stay in your Gemini history so you can review them later.

The analysis is limited to the visible screen. Content that requires scrolling is not included unless you scroll first and then invoke the overlay. This differs from how Gemini handles full PDFs or YouTube transcripts.

## Set up for shopping use

Make sure Gemini is ready before the sales start.

1. Set Gemini as your default digital assistant in Settings → Digital assistant app.
2. On many phones, assign the long-press power button gesture to the assistant.
3. Open the Gemini app and grant permissions for microphone, notifications, and appearing over other apps.
4. In Gemini settings enable screen context options for text and screenshots.
5. Update the Google app to version 17.78.13 or newer via the Play Store.

Test by opening a product page and long-pressing the power button. The compact overlay should appear with the shopping chips if the server-side rollout has reached your account.

You can minimize the overlay into a floating bubble to keep shopping while Gemini works. For more on the overlay itself, see our earlier guide on [how to use the Gemini overlay for shopping on Android](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-overlay-shopping-android/).

## Step-by-step: Compare a deal

Open the product page so the title, price, and key details are visible.

1. Invoke the Gemini overlay with the power button or “Hey Google.”
2. Tap “Compare prices” or “Help me pick.”
3. Wait for the reply. Gemini uses the screenshot to identify the product and check other stores.
4. Ask a follow-up such as “Include shipping costs and current promo codes” or “Is the warranty the same at Walmart?”
5. Minimize the overlay if you want to finish the purchase in the original app. The conversation remains available in the Gemini app.

For warranty checks, the “Check warranty and returns” chip looks at the terms shown on the page and summarizes return windows or coverage.

## Tips for Black Friday results

Scroll or zoom so the exact model number, price, and variant appear on screen before you start. Gemini only sees what is visible.

Confirm the model yourself. Screen context helps identification, but double-check the merchant page for the precise SKU.

Pair the overlay with manual stock and shipping checks. Gemini provides a snapshot comparison; it does not place orders or guarantee availability.

The feature is useful across regions. Reports include both US retailers and Indian apps such as Flipkart and Blinkit.

If the chips do not appear, force-stop the Google app, clear cache, and reopen the product page. Screen context must be enabled.

The latest Google app also lets you mute Gemini Live voice replies and use captions, which is handy in crowded stores.

## Limitations to know

Gemini reads only the current visible portion of the screen for these shopping prompts. Full page content behind the fold is not automatically included.

There is no official Google blog post announcing the feature yet. Availability depends on the server-side rollout, so some devices on the same app version may see it later.

Results rely on public web information about prices and policies. Always verify final details, taxes, and return rules on the retailer site before buying.

## Conclusion

The Gemini overlay puts price comparisons and warranty checks directly over the product page you are already viewing. Open a listing, invoke the overlay, tap a suggestion or type a focused question, and review the reply without leaving the app.

Update the Google app, enable screen context, and test it on a product you are considering. During Black Friday the chips can surface useful context quickly while you keep the original store visible.
