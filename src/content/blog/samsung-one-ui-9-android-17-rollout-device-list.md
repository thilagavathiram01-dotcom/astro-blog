Samsung is rolling out One UI 9, its skin on Android 17, to a wide range of Galaxy smartphones and tablets. The update started with the Galaxy S26 series in mid-September 2026 and has since reached many older flagships, foldables, and mid-range models in multiple regions.

This guide lists the devices confirmed to receive the stable update so far. It also covers the main new features and how to check whether your phone is eligible.

If you use a Pixel, check our [Android 17 QPR3 Beta 3 install guide](/blog/android-17-qpr3-beta-3-pixel-install-guide/) for the latest quarterly release details.

## Key features in One UI 9

One UI 9 builds on previous versions with more Galaxy AI tools and interface refinements rather than a full redesign.

Now Nudge expands contextual suggestions. It now appears in conversation notifications and as a floating button in the bottom-right corner, in addition to the keyboard. Suggestions can include adding reservation details to your calendar or saving places in Google Maps.

My FanCam lets you select a person in an existing video and automatically crop and track them. Open a video in Gallery, tap the Galaxy AI icon, choose My FanCam, pick the subject, set orientation and aspect ratio, then tap Follow.

The Quick Panel gains more customization. You can resize the brightness and volume sliders, the media player, and separate the sound mode button from the volume slider.

A new Warranty and care menu in Settings shows warranty status, diagnostic checks, and support options. AI privacy alerts notify you about unnecessary app permissions. Samsung Health receives a redesign with customizable cards, trend analysis, and new metrics like cardio load and fitness index.

Lock screen clocks can adapt to your wallpaper. DeX gets easier window movement between desktops and previews in Recents. Other additions include Bixby gesture activation, accessibility improvements such as text spotlight, and a blue location indicator when apps access location.

Features vary by device. Flagship models receive the full set of Galaxy AI tools. Older or mid-range devices may get a subset.

## How to check for the One UI 9 update

Samsung rolls out updates in stages by region, carrier, and device variant. Availability is not simultaneous worldwide.

1. Connect to Wi-Fi and charge your device to at least 50 percent.
2. Open Settings > Software update > Download and install.
3. If an update appears, download and install it. Restart when prompted.
4. After reboot, go to Settings > About phone > Software information to confirm the One UI version and Android version.

You can also check Samsung Members or the Samsung website for your specific model and country. Carrier-locked devices may receive the update later than unlocked ones.

Backup important data before installing. Large updates (often 3-4 GB) require sufficient storage.

## Tips for the update

Some Galaxy AI features require a Samsung account and may depend on region or language settings. Test Now Nudge and My FanCam after updating to see what is available on your device.

If the update does not appear, wait a few days. Staged rollouts continue for weeks. Clearing the software update cache in recovery mode can sometimes help, but only if you are comfortable with that process.

Older devices may not receive every AI feature. Check Samsung's official support pages for your exact model after the update lands.

One UI 9 brings Android 17 to a broad lineup of Galaxy devices, from the latest S26 and Z Fold 7 series back to the S23 and various A-series phones and tablets. Core additions include expanded Now Nudge, My FanCam video tracking, more Quick Panel options, and device care tools.

Check Settings > Software update on your device. The list grows as Samsung continues the staged global rollout through late 2026. Features differ by model, so verify what is available after the update installs.
