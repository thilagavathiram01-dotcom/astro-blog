Google expanded Tasks in Gemini to free users in the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India on November 17, 2026. The feature runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen and lets you hand off longer jobs so Gemini works in the background.

Starting in the U.S., you can also assign tasks by forwarding emails to gemini@google.com or by texting Gemini after you set up Channels.

## What Tasks in Gemini Does

Tasks differs from regular Chat. Chat answers questions in real time. Tasks takes a goal and works through the steps while you do other things.

Google designed it for multi-step work such as booking appointments, comparing options, or sorting school emails. You stay in control. Gemini pauses for confirmation before critical actions like purchases or calls.

Free users can run up to 15 tasks at once. Schedules are limited compared with paid plans. Advanced capabilities such as phone calls to businesses and Google Pay checkout launched first in the U.S.

## How to Start a Task in the App

1. Open the Gemini app or go to gemini.google.com.
2. Sign in with a personal Google Account. Work and school accounts are not supported for Tasks.
3. Flip the toggle from Chat to Task under the prompt box.
4. Describe the job. Include the goal, any limits, and timing.
5. Review progress in the sidebar. You can stop a task at any time.

Gemini uses isolated virtual machines on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine so each account stays separate.

## Set Up Text with Gemini

Texting lets you assign tasks from your phone without opening the app.

1. Open Gemini on Android or iOS.
2. Tap Settings.
3. Tap Channels.
4. Tap Set up under Text with Gemini.
5. Verify your phone number.

You need a U.S. phone number with an eligible carrier for texting. Once verified, send a message describing the task. Gemini treats it as a new task.

## Forward Emails to Create Tasks

Forward messages to gemini@google.com from a personal U.S. Gmail account.

The email becomes a task. Attachments are limited to 10. Gemini extracts dates, action items, or other details and can add them to Calendar or draft replies.

Josh Woodward, Vice President of Labs for the Gemini app, described forwarding school emails so every date landed on his calendar and a conflict was flagged.

## Tips for Better Results

- Give clear goals and boundaries. Tell Gemini what not to do.
- Check key steps before approving calls, bookings, or purchases.
- Connect relevant Google apps so Gemini has context from Gmail, Calendar, or Drive.
- Provide feedback with a thumbs down when a task misses the mark. This helps improve the system.

Paid Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers can choose Gemini 4 Neon for Tasks and get higher schedule limits.
