Google began rolling out Personal Intelligence for Gemini on Android Auto on 20 October 2026. With your permission, Gemini can pull details from Gmail, Calendar, Photos, Maps, YouTube, and Search so you ask shorter questions and keep your eyes on the road.

If you already turned the feature on in the Gemini app, it travels with you into the car. Cars with Google built-in get the same capability in the coming months. Everything else in the car—navigation, music, calls, and basic questions—continues to work if you leave Personal Intelligence off.

## What Personal Intelligence does behind the wheel

Personal Intelligence retrieves a specific fact (a flight gate from Gmail, a doctor’s address from Calendar, a car-wash location from a recent photo) and reasons across several sources at once. Google’s examples include:

- “What’s my flight gate? And is my flight on time?”
- “Take me to my doctor’s appointment.”
- “Find the car wash name from the photo I took last week.”
- “Find me an indoor restaurant along my route that matches my usual vibe.”

You still confirm any action that spends money or books something. The assistant does not act on its own.

For a full walkthrough of the phone settings, see our [Gemini Personal Intelligence setup guide](/blog/gemini-personal-intelligence-setup/).

## Who can turn it on

Personal Intelligence rolls out to eligible personal Google accounts for people aged 18 and older. It is not available for Workspace business, enterprise, or education accounts. The current rollout excludes the European Economic Area, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and Nigeria.

You need an Android phone that already runs Gemini (Assistant has been replaced by Gemini on most devices). Connect the phone to your car’s Android Auto head unit the usual way—USB or wireless.

## Step 1: Check or enable Personal Intelligence on your phone

1. Open the Gemini app on your Android phone.
2. Tap your profile photo or the menu, then Settings.
3. Tap Personal Intelligence.
4. Open Connected Apps.
5. Turn on only the sources you want Gemini to read while driving: Workspace (Gmail and Calendar), Photos, Maps, YouTube, Search.

If the Personal Intelligence row is missing, your account, country, or app version is not yet in the wave. Update the Gemini app from the Play Store and try again later. Do not force-enable anything through developer options.

You can turn the whole feature off later from the same screen. Disconnecting an app stops new lookups; it does not delete past chat history.

## Step 2: Connect your phone and start Android Auto

Plug in or pair the phone as you normally do. Android Auto launches on the car screen. Gemini is already the assistant if you have switched from Assistant on the phone.

If you have not yet switched:

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Go to Settings → Digital assistants from Google (or Voice settings).
3. Select Gemini instead of Google Assistant.
4. Restart Android Auto.

A tooltip may appear on the car display the first time Gemini is available. You can invoke it by saying “Hey Google,” pressing the microphone icon on the car screen, or long-pressing the voice button on the steering wheel.

## Step 3: Ask a question you can verify

Start with a prompt whose answer lives in one connected app so you can check it quickly.

Safe first tries:

- “What time is my next Calendar event?”
- “Read the subject of the latest flight confirmation in Gmail.”
- “Navigate to the place I saved in Maps yesterday.”

Gemini should answer without you having to open the email or type an address. If the answer is wrong, correct it in the same conversation: “That was last week’s flight, not today’s.”

Avoid medical, legal, or financial decisions while driving. Keep requests short. You can add “keep it short” or “just the answer” if Gemini starts a long reply.

## Privacy and control on the road

Connecting apps is off until you turn Personal Intelligence on. Google states that personal data is not used to train Gemini models directly. Conversation data can still be used to improve services after filtering steps; read the current Gemini Apps Help page for the exact wording.

On cars with Google built-in, personalized answers appear only when your phone is connected and you are in the vehicle. You can revoke access at any time from Gemini settings on the phone. Temporary chats on the phone skip personalization for that thread; the car inherits the same account settings.

## Tips for safer use

Connect only the apps you need for the drive. Photos can surface plate numbers or documents; leave it off if that bothers you in a shared car.

Test one new connection at a time so you know which source produced an odd answer.

Keep both hands on the wheel. If a reply is long, ask Gemini to shorten it rather than looking at the screen.

If the car does not show the Personal Intelligence indicator, confirm the phone is connected and the feature is on in the Gemini app. Restart Android Auto.

## Conclusion

Personal Intelligence on Android Auto turns a generic voice assistant into one that already knows the details sitting in your Gmail, Calendar, and Photos. Enable it once on the phone, connect the smallest useful set of apps, and ask the questions you used to answer by glancing at the passenger seat. The feature stays opt-in, and basic Android Auto functions continue if you leave it off.
