Google opened Googlebook pre-orders on 21 September 2026. The new laptops start at $899, ship in the U.S. on 4 October, and arrive in Canada, the U.K., Ireland, France, Germany, and Australia on 5 October.

This guide walks through what Google confirmed, how to pick a model, and how the machine is meant to work with the Android phone you already carry. Feature names and dates come from Google’s official launch posts.

## What a Googlebook actually is

A Googlebook is not a rebranded Chromebook. Google built the platform on the Android technology stack and added desktop foundations from ChromeOS. The result is Googlebook OS: Play Store apps, desktop Chrome with extensions, and Gemini Intelligence on the same device.

OEM partners for the first wave are Acer, ASUS, Dell, HP, and Lenovo. Google is not shipping a Pixel-branded model at launch.

Every unit includes 12 months of Google AI Pro (5TB of cloud storage and Gemini Advanced tools), plus three months of YouTube Premium, Adobe Photoshop, and related creative software. Googlebook OS is promised feature drops and updates for up to 10 years.

## Hardware you should compare before you buy

Google listed a shared floor across the first five flagships:

- Starting price: $899
- Materials: aluminum, magnesium alloy, and carbon fiber
- Displays: responsive touchscreens, up to 2.8K OLED
- Input: haptic glass trackpads and backlit keyboards
- Processors: Intel Core Ultra Series 3 or Qualcomm Snapdragon X Elite
- On-device AI: dedicated NPUs rated over 45 TOPS
- Memory: 16GB baseline, some models up to 32GB
- Battery: up to 14 hours of video playback and up to 16 hours of web browsing

The Glowbar returns on the exterior. It lights at startup, sweeps to show charge level when you plug in, and plays short animations when you talk to Gemini. Google says developers can use Glowbar for custom interactive animations.

Security is the same family as ChromeOS: Google Titan hardware root of trust, defense-in-depth, and on-device malware detection. Google also lists a Level 5 security-certified pKVM hypervisor that hosts an isolated Linux environment with a full terminal.

If you write code, that Linux VM plus Antigravity (Google’s on-device agent development platform) is the reason to look past the consumer talking points. You can run tools such as Claude Code or the Antigravity CLI inside that isolated environment.

## How to place a pre-order

1. Open the official catalog at [googlebook.google](https://googlebook.google/) or shop through Best Buy and other listed retailers.
2. Compare screen size, weight, CPU vendor (Intel vs Snapdragon), RAM, and storage. Do not assume every $899 SKU is identical.
3. Sign in with the Google account you use on your Android phone. Setup later copies Wi-Fi networks, saved passwords, settings, and messages with end-to-end encryption.
4. Confirm the bundle: 12 months of Google AI Pro, three months of YouTube Premium and Adobe Photoshop, and a complimentary year of GeForce NOW.
5. Check ship-to country. U.S. shelf date is 4 October 2026. Canada, the U.K., Ireland, France, Germany, and Australia follow on 5 October.

Keep the order confirmation. You will need the same Google account on first boot for phone pairing.

## Pair the laptop with your Android phone

Google designed three phone-to-laptop paths. Use them in this order on day one.

**Continue On.** Start a task on the phone and resume it from the Googlebook taskbar. Google’s example is a BandLab project begun on a commute and finished at the desk.

**Files.** Open the Files app on the laptop to search and open photos and documents that still live on the phone. Skip emailing attachments to yourself.

**Cast My Apps.** Stream a phone app into a desktop window when you need a delivery checkout or a one-time Messages code. The phone app stays available without leaving the laptop workflow.

Those three features sit on the shared Android foundation. They are the practical reason an Android user should consider Googlebook over a generic Windows box.

## Turn on Gemini where it helps, then leave it off if it does not

Googlebook ships with Gemini Intelligence on the desktop. Three tools matter for daily work:

- **Magic Pointer.** Wiggle the cursor to call Gemini on whatever is on screen. Ask it to tidy a calendar or translate selected text. Google states Gemini only acts when you ask, and you can switch the feature off.
- **Rambler.** The key next to Quick Insert turns speech into structured notes, headers, and checklists, including mixed-language dictation.
- **Create My Widget.** Describe a widget in plain language and pin it to the desktop for ongoing updates.

For a deeper walkthrough of Magic Pointer and Rambler, read our [Googlebook Magic Pointer and Rambler guide](/blog/googlebook-magic-pointer-rambler-guide/). Phone-side context lives in [Gemini Intelligence on Android](/blog/gemini-intelligence-android/).

If you prefer a quiet desktop, disable Magic Pointer first and keep Rambler on the keyboard only. Gemini is optional on these machines; it is not required to run Play apps or Chrome.

## Software you can run on day one

Googlebook runs desktop Chrome with extensions and millions of Android apps, many updated for large screens, mouse, and keyboard.

Creative apps called out at launch include Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Lightroom, and CapCut. Streaming apps such as Netflix and HBO Max support offline viewing. Games range from Play Store titles to AAA streaming through the included GeForce NOW year.

Developers get a full terminal, the isolated Linux environment behind pKVM, and Antigravity for building and testing agents on the device.

## Tips before October shipping starts

- Match RAM to workload. 16GB is the floor. Buy 32GB if you keep many browser profiles, Android apps, and a Linux VM open at once.
- Decide Intel vs Snapdragon based on the apps you already pay for. Googlebook OS supports both; MediaTek models are promised later, not at this first drop.
- Audit Play apps that you expect to use in a window. Large-screen and keyboard support still varies by developer.
- Plan encryption: first-boot transfer of passwords and messages is end-to-end encrypted, but only if you sign in with the same account used on the phone.
- Treat Glowbar as a status light, not a toy. Charge-level sweep is the useful part; Gemini animations can be ignored.

## Should you pre-order or wait?

Pre-order if you already live on an Android phone, want Play apps plus Chrome on one lid, and can use the $899 floor plus the 12-month Google AI Pro bundle. Wait if you need a Pixel-branded chassis, a MediaTek SKU, or independent battery reviews after the 4 October street date.

Chromebook owners should not assume every ChromeOS habit survives. Googlebook OS is a new product with a 10-year update promise, not a ChromeOS point release.

## Conclusion

Googlebook is Google’s first laptop line built for Gemini Intelligence and for the Android phone in your pocket. Pre-orders are open now from $899. Ship dates start 4 October 2026 in the U.S. and 5 October in six other countries.

Pick the OEM chassis that fits your bag, sign in with your phone account, and turn on Continue On before you touch Magic Pointer. That order gets work moving on day one.
