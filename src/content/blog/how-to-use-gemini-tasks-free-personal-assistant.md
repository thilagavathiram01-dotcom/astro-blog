Google rolled out Gemini Tasks on November 17, 2026. The feature lets you hand off multi-step jobs to Gemini so it can make calls, book appointments, shop, and finish chores while you move on with your day. It is free for hundreds of millions of people in the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India, running on the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model.

This guide shows exactly how to switch to Task mode, what you can ask it to do, and how to get reliable results.

## What Gemini Tasks Does

In Chat mode, Gemini answers questions and generates text. In Task mode, you describe a job and Gemini works through the steps, often using your connected Google apps and third-party services.

Official examples from Google include calling dealerships to book a service appointment, analyzing utility bills to find savings, shopping for a charger with a discount code, adding items to an Instacart order, and pulling dates from school emails into your calendar. Gemini discloses that it is an AI when it calls a business, and businesses can opt out.

Advanced actions such as certain purchases and more complex multi-app flows started rolling out first in the U.S. The free tier uses Gemini 4 Hydrogen. If you already use Personal Intelligence or connected apps, Tasks can draw on that context securely.

## How to Switch to Task Mode

Open the Gemini app or go to gemini.google.com. Under the text box you will see two options: Chat and Task. Tap Task. The prompt changes to something like “Describe a task” and the interface shifts to support longer-running work.

You can also start from Channels in Settings to set up texting Gemini (U.S. phone number required) or forward emails to gemini@google.com so Tasks can act on them.

Once in Task mode, type or speak the job clearly. Include the goal, any constraints (budget, preferred times, apps to use), and how you want the result delivered.

## Step-by-Step Examples You Can Try

Start with a simple, low-risk task so you can see the flow.

1. Open Gemini and switch to Task.
2. Type: “Find the three best USB-C chargers under $30 that work with my phone, compare reviews, and show me the top option with a link.”
3. Watch the progress panel. Gemini may ask a clarifying question about wattage or color.
4. Review the final recommendation and any files or links it creates.

For a calendar-style task (available where the feature has rolled out):

1. Switch to Task.
2. Say: “Our car needs a service. Call the two nearest dealerships, find the earliest Friday morning slot in the next month when I am free, and add it to my calendar.”
3. Gemini uses Personal Intelligence or connected Calendar and Maps data if you have enabled them.
4. It pauses for confirmation before finalizing if the action is sensitive.

For shopping:

1. Describe the item and budget.
2. Set a hard spending cap if the interface offers it.
3. Confirm before Gemini proceeds to checkout via Google Pay or connected services.

Results and files (such as a generated Google Sheet) appear in the work panel under Progress, Files, or Skills & apps.

## Tips for Better Results

- Be specific about constraints: preferred times, budget limits, stores, or apps.
- Connect only the Google services you need (Calendar, Gmail, Wallet, Drive) so Gemini has the right context without extra permissions.
- For recurring jobs, look for schedule options once they appear in your region.
- Give feedback with a thumbs-down when a task misses the mark. Google uses this to improve the system.
- Start with read-only or low-stakes tasks before enabling purchases or calls.

You can jump in at any time to add instructions or stop the task. Gemini is designed to ask for confirmation before critical steps such as clicking Buy.

## Safety, Limits, and Privacy

Tasks run in a hardware-isolated virtual machine on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine that is dedicated to your account. Google layers additional protections including prompt-injection filters and Safe Browsing.

Not every action is available everywhere. Calling through Gemini is restricted in certain U.S. states. Texting requires a U.S. phone number with an eligible carrier. Purchases still earn your card’s points and protections when using supported cards, and you can set a spending cap.

You can disconnect apps, adjust task settings, or delete history at any time. Google notes that while extensive testing has been done, tasks can still fail or miss the mark on some sites. In those cases the system may hand the final click back to you.

## Conclusion

Gemini Tasks turns the assistant into a worker that can finish multi-step jobs instead of just answering questions. With the free rollout on Gemini 4 Hydrogen, many people in the listed countries can start handing off appointments, research, and shopping today. Switch to Task mode, describe the job clearly, and review the results before you rely on them for important decisions.

Try a simple research or calendar task first, then expand as the feature reaches more regions and apps.
