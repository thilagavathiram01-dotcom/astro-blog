Google added Squarespace to Gemini Connected Apps on 23 September 2026, in the same creativity wave as Adobe, Picsart, and Webflow. You can design, build, and manage site content from chat or Gemini Spark instead of hopping into the Squarespace editor for every small change.

The catch is eligibility. Google lists Squarespace as a personal Google Account feature, for people 18 or over, in the United States, and in English only. It works in the Gemini web app at gemini.google.com and in the Gemini mobile app, in Gemini chat and Gemini Spark. A work or school account will not see it.

This guide covers the checks, the connect path, and prompts that stay inside what Google documents.

## Check eligibility before you open Settings

Open [Google's Connected Apps availability table](https://support.google.com/gemini/table/17434654) and find Squarespace. The row says the app lets you design, build, and manage websites and content with Squarespace. Account type is personal only. Age is 18 or over. Location is the US only. Language is English only. Supported surfaces are the web app and the mobile app. Supported modes are chat and Spark.

If you sign in with a Workspace account, switch to a personal account before you hunt for the toggle. Google's manage page says Connected Apps instructions for personal accounts do not apply the same way to work or school accounts.

Also turn on Gemini Apps Activity (Keep Activity). When that setting is off, Connected Apps are unavailable on gemini.google.com and on iOS. On Android, only a short list of device apps stays available. Squarespace is not in that short list.

Rollout still matters. The [23 September announcement](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/new-connected-apps-gemini/) from Mai Lowe, Group Product Manager for the Gemini App, said the new tools were beginning to roll out that day. If Squarespace is missing from your list, confirm country, language, age, and account type before you assume the feature failed.

## Turn Squarespace on in Connected Apps

Google documents the same control path for partner apps.

1. On a computer, go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) and sign in with the personal account you use for the site.
2. At the bottom of the sidebar, open Settings, then Connected Apps. If you do not see Connected Apps, open Personal Intelligence first, then Connected Apps.
3. Find Squarespace. The page only lists apps available for your account type, country, and the Gemini app you are in.
4. Turn Squarespace on and follow the on-screen sign-in. You will need the Squarespace account that owns the site.
5. Open the app row and click Learn more. That details page lists the full description, supported actions, unsupported actions, and example prompts.

You can also start from a chat. Type `@`, pick Squarespace, and submit a request. If the app is not connected and you are eligible, Gemini connects it or asks permission.

On a phone, open the Gemini app, go to settings, and look for Connected Apps or Apps. Google says Squarespace is supported on the mobile app as well as the web app, in chat and in Spark. If the row is missing on one surface, try the other. Availability can differ by device.

## Ask Gemini to use the site, then read the result

A connected app does not run on every message. Name it when the task is about the site.

Type `@` in the prompt box and select Squarespace, then describe the job in plain language. Google's help page says that if an app is available, Gemini can also pick it automatically, but the `@` mention removes the guess.

Useful prompts stay close to the published description: design, build, and manage website content. Examples:

- `@Squarespace List the pages on my site and the last updated date for each.`
- `@Squarespace Draft a short About page in my existing tone. Show the copy before you publish anything.`
- `@Squarespace Add a blog post draft titled Fall hours, with sections for weekday and weekend times. Leave it unpublished.`

After you submit, follow the permission prompts. Review any preview before you confirm a write. Connected Apps can take actions in the other product with your permission, which includes editing and managing content. Treat publish, delete, and billing steps as manual checks even if Gemini offers them.

The Learn more page on the Squarespace row is the source of truth for supported and unsupported actions. Do not assume every Squarespace control, such as domain DNS or commerce payouts, is exposed. If an action is listed as unsupported, do that part in Squarespace itself.

Spark can use the same connection. Google lists Gemini Spark as a supported mode for Squarespace, alongside chat. Hand Spark a bounded task, such as drafting three service descriptions from notes you paste in, rather than an open-ended "rebuild the site."

## Keep the connection limited

Turn Squarespace off when you are done with a batch of edits. In Connected Apps, find the app and switch it off, then follow the on-screen steps. Google documents disconnect and data-handling notes in the Gemini Apps Privacy Hub. Disconnecting stops new requests. It does not undo changes already saved in Squarespace.

A few practical limits:

- English and the United States are required for this connector. A VPN does not change the account country Google uses for eligibility.
- Picsart in the same creativity group is web-only. Squarespace is not. Do not copy settings from a different app's row.
- Gemini in Google Messages cannot use Connected Apps. Start the Squarespace task in the Gemini app or on the web.
- Custom MCP apps are a separate feature, also limited to personal accounts, 18 or over, and the US. Squarespace in this guide is the partner connector Google announced, not a server URL you paste in.

If you also publish on Webflow, the connect pattern is the same, with a different action list. See the [Webflow to Gemini setup](/blog/connect-webflow-to-gemini/) for that connector's US and English limits.

## Fix a missing Squarespace row

Work through the list in order.

1. Confirm the Google Account is personal, not work or school.
2. Confirm you are 18 or over and the account country is the United States.
3. Set the Gemini language to English.
4. Turn Gemini Apps Activity on, then reload gemini.google.com.
5. Open Settings, then Connected Apps. If the menu is nested, open Personal Intelligence first.
6. Search the page for Squarespace. Click Learn more and read supported actions before you write a prompt.
7. If the row is still absent, wait for the rollout. The 23 September post said the wave was beginning, not that every account had it the same hour.

A failed Squarespace login is separate from a missing row. Sign in to squarespace.com in the same browser, confirm you can open the site, then return to Gemini and turn the app on again.

## What to do next

Connect Squarespace only on the account that should edit the site. Read the supported-actions list. Run one read-only prompt, then one draft that stays unpublished. Disconnect the app when the batch is finished.

That keeps Gemini useful for page copy and site structure without handing it a standing login you do not need every day.
