Google rolled out 66 new regional voices for the Gemini app on November 16, 2026. These options cover U.S. English, U.K. English, Indian English, Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian, and Korean. The goal is a voice that matches local speech patterns instead of a generic accent.

The change applies across the app. Your choice works in Gemini Live conversations and when you tap Listen on a text response. Google previewed the feature at I/O earlier this year and began the global rollout on Android and iOS.

## What the new voices include

Each voice focuses on natural cadence, pitch, and inflection rather than exaggerated slang. Google tuned them for everyday clarity.

U.K. English receives the widest selection, with more than 10 options. These include Geordie, Scouse, Brummie, Scottish, Welsh, Northern Irish, Modern Received Pronunciation, and Multicultural London English. Other languages receive tailored regional variants as well.

The voices appear in the Gemini app on both Android and iOS. They may take time to reach every account as the rollout continues.

## How to change Gemini’s voice

You can switch voices from the app settings or during a Live session. The process takes only a few taps on either platform.

### From app settings

1. Open the Gemini app on your Android phone or iPhone.
2. Tap your profile picture or initial in the top corner.
3. Select Settings, then Gemini’s voice.
4. Swipe left or right to preview each option.
5. Tap Select when you find the one you want.

The selected voice stays active until you change it again. It carries over to Live mode and the Listen button on chat replies.

### During Gemini Live

1. Start a Gemini Live conversation.
2. Tap the three-dot menu.
3. Choose a voice from the list and confirm.

This method lets you test options without leaving the conversation. You can switch mid-session if needed.

Note that the menu does not label voices by city or region. Gemini also will not name the accent if you ask it directly. Preview a few and listen for the rhythm that fits.

## Tips for choosing and testing

Preview several options before settling on one. Ask Gemini about a local topic such as food, sports, or a neighborhood to hear how the cadence comes through.

The setting is account-wide, so the same voice follows you on every device signed into the same Google account. If the new options do not appear yet, check for app updates and try again later in the day.

For hands-free use, the regional voice works with Gemini Live on Apple CarPlay as well. See our guide on [setting up Gemini Live on Apple CarPlay](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-live-on-apple-carplay/) for the full steps.

## Why the update matters

A voice that matches local speech patterns makes longer conversations feel more natural. Students practicing a language, drivers using Live for navigation help, or anyone who prefers a familiar accent can benefit.

The rollout is global for Gemini app users. Mobile support was the focus of the November 16 announcement, with the feature available on both Android and iOS.

If you already use Gemini for daily briefings or quick questions, try a regional option and compare it to the previous default. Most people find a closer match within a few previews.
