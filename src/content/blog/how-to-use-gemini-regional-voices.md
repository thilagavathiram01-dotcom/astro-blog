Google rolled out 66 new regional voices for the Gemini app on 16 November 2026. These options cover U.S. English, U.K. English, India English, Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian, and Korean. Each voice aims for natural rhythm and clarity instead of exaggerated accents.

The selected voice works across Gemini Live conversations, language practice, and the Listen button that reads chat replies aloud. This update follows plans shared at Google I/O earlier in the year.

![Person holding a smartphone in a casual setting](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1551650975-87deedd944c3?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Why Regional Voices Matter

A voice that matches local speech patterns makes spoken interactions feel more familiar. Google designed the new set to capture everyday pitch, inflection, and cadence rather than cartoonish stereotypes. Users who speak Indian English or specific U.K. dialects can now choose a closer match.

The change applies on both Android and iOS. It does not require a paid Gemini plan. Rollout is gradual, so the full list may not appear for every account on the first day.

For context on other recent Gemini features such as task automation and Rambler, see our guide to [Gemini Intelligence on Pixel 10](/blog/gemini-intelligence-on-pixel-10-november-drop-guide/).

## How to Change the Voice in Gemini Live

Open the Gemini app and start a Gemini Live session. Tap the three-dot menu in the Live interface. Select a voice from the new regional options. Preview a few before confirming.

The same list appears under app settings. Go to Settings, then Gemini’s voice. Pick the option that sounds closest to your preferred accent. The choice sticks for future Live sessions and for read-aloud responses.

Test it immediately by asking a short question. If the menu still shows the older limited set, wait a few hours or restart the app. Google noted the rollout continues over the coming days.

## How to Use the Voice for Chat Read-Aloud

After you select a voice, open any chat. Tap the Listen icon on a response. Gemini reads the text using the regional voice you chose. This works for long answers, summaries, or language practice prompts.

The feature does not change the written text. It only affects the spoken version. Switch voices anytime in settings if you want a different option for a specific session.

## Supported Languages and Regions

Official details list these groups:

- U.S. English
- U.K. English
- India English
- Hindi
- Brazilian Portuguese
- Indonesian
- Korean

Each group receives tailored options. Reports indicate the U.K. English set includes more than ten variants covering different regional speech patterns. Google has not published the full named list in the main announcement, so try the available choices in the menu.

The voices are tuned for natural delivery. They avoid overdone accents so conversations stay clear for everyday use.

## Tips for Best Results

Start with a short Live session to compare two or three options side by side. Speak naturally and note how the voice handles pauses and questions. Save the one that feels most comfortable for longer chats.

If you practice a language, pair the regional voice with prompts that ask Gemini to correct pronunciation or expand sentences. The consistent voice helps track progress across sessions.

Check availability after system and app updates. The feature is rolling out to all Gemini app users on supported platforms. No extra subscription is required.

For multi-step tasks that pair well with voice interaction, review the current task automation support in India, the U.K., and Japan in our earlier posts.

## Troubleshooting

The new voices may not appear immediately. Restart the Gemini app or sign out and back in. Confirm you are on the latest app version from the Play Store or App Store.

If Live is unavailable in your region, the voice setting still affects the Listen button in regular chat. Contact support through the app if options remain missing after several days.

## Conclusion

The 66 regional voices give Gemini users a more personal spoken experience on Android and iOS. Switch once in Gemini Live or settings and the choice applies to conversations and read-aloud replies. Try the options after the 16 November 2026 rollout reaches your account.

Official details remain on the Google Blog post announcing the feature.
