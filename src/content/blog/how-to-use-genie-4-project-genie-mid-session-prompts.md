Google rolled out Genie 4 inside Project Genie on November 18, 2026. Eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers can now generate interactive worlds, change them while exploring, and hear native audio that matches the scene.

This guide shows how to access the new experience, use mid-session prompts with Genie 4, try the Project Genie agent, and remix worlds from the community gallery. Facts come from the official Google DeepMind and Google Labs announcement.

## Check your access

Project Genie is available to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers who are 18 or older. Google AI Pro members can join a waitlist on the same page.

Open [labs.google/projectgenie](https://labs.google/projectgenie) and sign in with the Google account that holds your Ultra plan. If you do not see the new experience, confirm your plan tier. Some reports note that the $199.99 Ultra 20x tier qualifies in certain regions while lower Ultra tiers may not.

All generated video and audio carries SynthID watermarking. Inputs and outputs pass through safety screenings.

## Start a Genie 4 world

Once inside Project Genie, select Genie 4 from the model picker. Genie 4 provides the highest-fidelity visuals, physics, and real-time native audio. Genie 3 stays available for longer sessions (up to 3 minutes per generation) or 360-degree panorama seeding.

Describe the world you want in a single prompt or upload an image. The system generates an explorable space you can navigate with keyboard controls or on-screen buttons.

## Use mid-session prompts

Genie 4’s key update is the ability to edit the world while you move through it. Type a new prompt during navigation and the environment changes in real time.

Examples that work well:

- Shift the weather from clear skies to heavy rain or snow.
- Drop in a character such as a dragon or a person who can speak.
- Redirect the action, for instance “make the path lead to a glowing cave.”
- Change lighting or time of day.

The native audio adapts: ambient soundscapes update, and characters can produce dialogue that fits the scene. Sessions stay relatively short compared with Genie 3’s extended runs, so plan key changes early.

## Build with the Project Genie agent

The new agent turns a conversation into a complete world. Describe the story or simulation you want. The agent orchestrates multimodal models to set the visual look and generate a matching background score.

While you explore, Gemini can observe the screen, surface suggestion chips, or trigger events automatically. This reduces the need to write every detail yourself.

Try a starting brief such as: “Create an explorable mountain village at dusk with a river and a market square. Ask me one question about the mood before you generate.”

## Explore and remix the gallery

The community gallery contains curated worlds and user-published creations. You can jump in immediately, remix the environment, rules, or audio, or publish your own world for others to explore.

Examples highlighted by Google include “Villa Giallo,” a gothic manor mystery, and “The Life of Ants,” a photorealistic colony viewed from an ant’s perspective.

## Tips for better results

Write prompts in the first person and describe what you stand on or hold. Keep scenes focused and forward-facing because coherence can degrade after roughly 90 seconds.

Prefer small, sequential changes over large rewrites. If a prompt breaks the scene, step back to the previous stable state and try a narrower instruction.

Test both Genie 4 (for audio and live edits) and Genie 3 (for longer sessions or panorama starts) on the same idea to see which fits better.

## Known limits

Genie 4 remains research-grade. Google notes ongoing work on camera latency, complex physical interactions, real-time audio precision, and long-term scene permanence. Daily usage limits apply and can vary with demand.

There is no free tier. Access depends on an eligible Ultra plan and supported location.

## Next steps

Open Project Genie, confirm your Ultra eligibility, and generate a short test world. Practice one mid-session weather change and one character drop. Then browse the gallery and remix one existing world.
