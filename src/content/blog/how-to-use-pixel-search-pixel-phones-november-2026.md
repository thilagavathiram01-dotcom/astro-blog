Google expanded Pixel Search to more devices in the November 2026 Pixel Drop. Pixel 6a and newer phones now get the enhanced search that first appeared on the Pixel 11 series.

This tool lets you search personal content with natural language instead of exact keywords. You can locate photos from a trip, emails about a purchase, calendar events, messages, files, and settings from one place.

## What Pixel Search Does

Pixel Search builds on the app drawer search bar. It indexes content from Photos, Gmail, Messages, Calendar, Google Drive, Files, and system settings.

Queries do not need to match exact names or menu titles. Type a description and the system surfaces relevant matches from your device and Google account.

Results appear alongside web results. Local content shows first so you reach personal items quickly.

## Access Pixel Search

Open the app drawer by swiping up from the home screen. Tap the search bar at the top.

You can also swipe up on the home screen if the setting is enabled. Touch and hold the home screen, open Home settings, then Apps list settings, and toggle Swipe up to start search.

Update your Pixel to the latest software and ensure the Google app and Pixel Launcher are current. The feature rolls out in phases, so it may appear after the main system update.

Supported devices include Pixel 6a and all newer models, including A-series and foldables, in all regions.

## Search with Natural Language

Type a phrase that describes what you need. Examples include:

- Photos from last summer vacation
- Email about flight confirmation
- Turn on dark mode
- Messages from Alex last week
- Calendar events this month

The system matches intent rather than literal text. It can surface a photo album, an email thread, a settings toggle, or a calendar entry.

Tap a result to open the item directly. Scroll for more matches or web results.

## Manage Search Sources

Control which apps contribute results. Touch and hold the home screen, open Home settings, then Apps list settings, and select the option for apps in search results.

Toggle individual sources on or off. This keeps results focused on the apps you use most.

Pixel Search also looks through active app screens for relevant matches in some cases.

## Practical Examples

You need a photo of a receipt from a recent store visit. Open the app drawer search and type receipt from grocery store last month. Matching images from Photos appear.

You want to check a meeting. Type project review meeting this week. The calendar entry shows up with the time and details.

You cannot find a setting. Type enable battery saver. The relevant toggle appears so you can jump straight to it.

These queries work because Pixel Search understands context across your data.

## Tips for Better Results

Be specific with time or people when possible. Add last week or from Sarah to narrow matches.

Update apps regularly so indexing stays current. Open Photos or Gmail occasionally so recent items appear.

If results feel limited, check the apps-in-search settings and ensure key sources are enabled.

Combine with Circle to Search when you see something on screen. Long-press the navigation bar, then circle the item. This pairs well with typed Pixel Search for broader needs. See our [guide to Circle to Search on Android](/blog/circle-to-search-android/) for details.

## Limitations and Availability

Pixel Search requires an internet connection for some processing on older devices. Results may vary based on what you have stored and your account permissions.

It does not replace dedicated apps for deep organization. Use it for quick lookups rather than bulk management.

The November Drop began rolling out on November 10, 2026. Check for updates in Settings if the feature has not appeared yet.

## Conclusion

Pixel Search turns the app drawer into a practical entry point for your phone’s content. Natural language queries reduce the steps needed to find photos, emails, messages, or settings.

After the November 2026 update, Pixel 6a owners and everyone with a newer model can use it. Open the app drawer, type what you need, and let the system surface the right item.

Keep the feature enabled and review source settings periodically for the best experience.
