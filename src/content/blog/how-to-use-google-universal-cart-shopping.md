Google Universal Cart launched in the U.S. on October 15, 2026. It lets you save products from different retailers into one cart while you browse Google Search (including AI Mode) or chat in the Gemini app. The cart tracks price drops and restocks, surfaces relevant promo codes, and helps you pick the best card from Google Wallet. When you are ready, you can check out with Google Pay or transfer items to the merchant site.

This guide shows exactly how it works today and how to use it for holiday shopping without juggling tabs and screenshots.

## What Universal Cart Does

Universal Cart is your hub for shopping on Google. It draws from Google’s Shopping Graph of more than 60 billion product listings that update continuously. Items you save stay in the cart across sessions and across Search and the Gemini app.

Four main capabilities:

1. Save products from multiple retailers in one place.
2. Get notifications when prices drop or items come back in stock.
3. See promo codes and card-perk recommendations powered by Google Wallet.
4. Check out with Google Pay inside the cart (where available) or transfer the items to the merchant’s own cart.

The merchant remains the seller of record either way. You receive confirmations and support directly from them. Participating merchants at launch include Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta Beauty, Walmart, Wayfair, and many Shopify stores.

## How to Add Items to Universal Cart

You do not need a separate app. The cart appears in Google Search and in the Gemini app.

**In Google Search (including AI Mode):**
1. Search for a product as usual.
2. Open a product listing or AI Mode result.
3. Look for the option to save or add the item to Universal Cart.
4. Confirm. The item appears in your cart immediately.

**In the Gemini app:**
1. Open the Gemini app and start or continue a shopping conversation.
2. Ask Gemini to find products or show options.
3. When a product appears, choose to add it to your cart.
4. Continue chatting or switch to Search—the cart stays the same.

Example from Google: save a handbag from Steve Madden in AI Mode, then add a sweater from Quince and lip gloss from Ulta Beauty in Gemini. All three wait in the same cart.

Your progress saves across Google so you can stop and resume later without starting over.

## Track Prices and Restocks

Once items are in the cart, it works in the background. Because it sits on the Shopping Graph, it can alert you when a saved product’s price falls or when an out-of-stock item returns.

Turn on notifications if prompted. You will receive updates for the whole list rather than checking each retailer site separately.

This is especially useful during holiday sales when prices move quickly.

## Use Card Perks and Promo Codes

Universal Cart connects to Google Wallet. If you have multiple cards saved, the cart can recommend which one offers the best rewards or benefits for the purchase. It also surfaces relevant promo codes it finds for the items in your cart.

You stay in control—review the suggestions and choose the payment method you prefer. For more on connecting Google Wallet to Gemini for spending insights, see our guide at [/blog/connect-google-wallet-to-gemini/](/blog/connect-google-wallet-to-gemini/).

## Check Out

When you are ready to buy, Universal Cart offers two paths, depending on the merchant and product:

- **Google Pay inside the cart.** Pay in a few taps for supported stores. Google Wallet can suggest the best card based on perks.
- **Transfer to the merchant’s cart.** Send the selected items to the retailer’s own site and finish checkout there.

Both options use the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP). Some products (subscriptions, personalized goods, final-sale, pre-order, or age-restricted items) may only support the transfer path.

After purchase, the merchant handles order confirmation, shipping, and support. You are not buying from Google; you are buying from the store.

## Tips for Better Results

- Start in AI Mode or Gemini when you want help comparing options, then save the winners to the cart.
- Check the cart regularly during sales seasons so you catch price-drop alerts.
- Confirm which cards are linked in Google Wallet if you want perk recommendations.
- If an item does not show a Google Pay option, use the transfer button—it still keeps everything organized until you leave Google.
- The feature is currently U.S.-only. Availability may expand later; YouTube and Gmail integration were mentioned earlier but are not live yet.

## Limitations to Know

Universal Cart is rolling out gradually in the United States on Search and the Gemini app. It is not available on YouTube or Gmail at launch. Checkout options depend on the merchant. Not every product supports in-cart Google Pay. Always review the final price, shipping, and return policy on the merchant’s side before completing a purchase.

## Conclusion

Google Universal Cart reduces the friction of multi-store shopping. You can gather items from Search and Gemini, watch for better prices, apply relevant savings, and finish with Google Pay or a clean transfer to the store. For holiday shopping or any multi-retailer list, it keeps everything in one place without losing your place.

Try it on Google Search or in the Gemini app today if you are in the U.S. Save a few items, wait for an alert, and see how the checkout options work for your favorite stores.
