Google Universal Cart launched in the United States on October 15, 2026. It gives shoppers one place to save products from different retailers while using Google Search or the Gemini app. The feature tracks price drops and restocks, surfaces promo codes, and supports checkout through Google Pay or the merchant site.

This guide shows exactly how to start using Universal Cart today. You need a Google account and to be located in the US. The cart works across Google Search (including AI Mode and Shopping results) and Gemini Apps.

## What Universal Cart Does

Universal Cart acts as a central hub for items you find on Google. Products stay in one list even if you save them from different surfaces. The cart organizes items by merchant so you can review details or remove products easily.

It runs on Google's Shopping Graph, which holds more than 60 billion product listings that update continuously. This lets the cart send notifications when a saved item drops in price or returns to stock. The feature also uses Google Wallet data to highlight card perks and relevant promo codes.

Checkout offers two paths for participating merchants: complete the purchase with Google Pay inside the cart, or transfer the items to the merchant's own cart and finish on their site. The merchant remains the seller of record either way. You receive order confirmations and support directly from them.

## How to Add Products from Google Search

Open Google Search on any device. Search for a product or open Shopping results or AI Mode. When product cards appear, look for an option to save or add the item to your cart.

Save a handbag from one retailer in AI Mode, then later add a sweater and lip gloss from other stores. All three stay in the same Universal Cart. Return later and the items remain exactly where you left them. No need to reopen multiple tabs or hunt through screenshots.

The cart icon may appear in the top right of the google.com homepage, search results, or the Google app on Android. Tap it to view saved items grouped by merchant.

## How to Add Products from the Gemini App

Open the Gemini app on your phone or visit gemini.google.com. Ask Gemini to find products that match what you need. For example, request a waterproof jacket under a certain price or specific household items.

When Gemini shows product results, use the save or add-to-cart option. Items added here appear in the same cart as those saved from Search. Gemini Apps and AI Mode can save items, though they have limited ability to list your full cart contents or status updates inside the chat itself.

Access your cart from the Gemini app account menu under Cart and orders on supported devices. Manage items, review details, or start checkout from there.

## Managing Your Cart and Checking Out

Open your Universal Cart from Search or the Gemini app. Products appear organized by merchant. Tap any item to review details or remove it if you change your mind.

When ready to buy:

1. Select the items you want.
2. Choose checkout with Google Pay for participating stores such as Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta Beauty, Walmart, Wayfair, and many Shopify merchants. Complete the purchase with your saved payment methods in a few taps.
3. Or transfer the items to the merchant site. Google helps populate their cart so you finish the purchase there.

After purchase, some merchants share order data back with Google. Check the My Orders tab inside the cart for status updates like shipped or delivered on supported orders. Not every merchant provides this data, so some orders may not appear.

Your cart and orders are stored in Google Wallet. Visit the Wallet privacy settings if you want to review or control related data.

## Price Alerts and Hidden Savings

The cart works in the background after you save items. It monitors the Shopping Graph and sends notifications for price drops or when out-of-stock products become available again.

Because it connects to Google Wallet, the cart understands your saved cards' perks. It can suggest which payment method offers the best benefits for a given purchase. Relevant promo codes also appear directly in the cart so you do not miss discounts.

These tools help during busy periods like the holiday season when prices change quickly. You stay informed without checking each retailer's site separately.

## Tips for Better Results

- Use clear product searches or Gemini prompts that include price ranges, sizes, or key features. This produces more relevant results you can save.
- Check the cart regularly. Notifications help, but reviewing items yourself confirms current prices and availability.
- Prefer Google Pay checkout on participating merchants for fewer steps. Transfer to the merchant site when you need store-specific options or loyalty programs not yet shown.
- Remember the US-only limit for now. YouTube and Gmail support are planned but not yet available.
- If an order issue arises, contact the merchant directly. Google does not handle purchase problems.

Connect Google Wallet if you have not already done so. This unlocks the card perk suggestions. Learn more about linking Wallet in our guide on [connecting Google Wallet to Gemini](/blog/connect-google-wallet-gemini/).

## Conclusion

Google Universal Cart simplifies multi-store shopping on Search and Gemini. Save products once, track changes automatically, and choose how to check out. The feature is live for US users as of mid-October 2026 and focuses on practical control rather than fully automated buying.

Start by searching for an item in AI Mode or asking Gemini, then save it to the cart. From there the tools handle organization and alerts while you decide when and where to buy. For the latest details, check the official Google announcement and Gemini help page.
