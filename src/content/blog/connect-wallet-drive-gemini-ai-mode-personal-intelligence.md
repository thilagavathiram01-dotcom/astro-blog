Google Search AI Mode now pulls context from more of your apps through Personal Intelligence. Starting 23 November 2026, you can opt in to connect Google Wallet. Drive and the Gemini app follow in the coming weeks. This lets AI Mode give answers based on your spending, documents and previous chats instead of generic web results.

The feature stays opt-in. Nothing turns on by itself. AI Mode can only read Wallet data. It cannot move, manage or invest money. You control every connection and can turn any of them off at any time.

## What Personal Intelligence Adds in AI Mode

Personal Intelligence already worked with Gmail, Calendar and Photos for users who opted in. The new connections expand that set.

Wallet (rolling out now) lets AI Mode review linked cards and spending patterns. It can suggest ways to trim everyday costs or pick the card that maximises cash-back or travel points for a purchase. Google states the access remains read-only.

Drive (coming weeks) brings Docs, Sheets and PDFs into answers. One example Google gave is building a tax-season checklist from your own files.

The Gemini app connection (coming weeks) shares context both ways. An idea you explore in the Gemini app becomes available in AI Mode, and the reverse also works. Recent search context already influences AI Overviews and AI Mode so you can continue a trip plan without repeating details.

## Step-by-Step: Connect Your Apps

Follow these steps on a personal Google Account. The feature is not available for Workspace business, school or enterprise accounts. You must be 18 or older.

1. Open Google Search while signed in.
2. Go to Search settings, then Search Personalization (or visit the Connected Apps page directly via myactivity.google.com/search-services/apps).
3. Turn on Personalized Recommendations if it is not already active.
4. Open Connected Apps or Additional Personal Intelligence Connected Content Apps.
5. Select Connect next to Google Wallet. Confirm the prompt.
6. When Drive and Gemini options appear in the coming weeks, repeat the Connect step for each one you want.

After connecting, try a query in AI Mode such as “Which of my cards gives the best rewards for groceries this month?” or “Summarise the tax documents in my Drive folder.” AI Mode should reference the connected data. A Personalization icon will appear under tailored responses soon so you can see exactly which context was used.

You can disconnect any app from the same settings page at any time. Changes take effect on the next query.

## Tips for Better Results and Privacy

Start with one app. Wallet is the most sensitive. Test it with a non-critical question before connecting more sources.

Keep Gemini History turned on if you plan to use the Gemini app connection. Tasks and some Personal Intelligence features require activity to be saved.

Review the Personalization icon once it rolls out. It shows the sources used for that answer. If something looks wrong, reply in the thread to correct it (for example, “I am no longer a vegetarian”).

Combine this with Tasks in Gemini. Once Personal Intelligence knows your context, Tasks can handle multi-step work such as comparing quotes or updating a calendar. See our related guide on [adding Gemini 4 Hydrogen to Android apps](/blog/add-gemini-4-hydrogen-android-firebase-ai-logic/).

## Availability and Limits

The Wallet connection is rolling out now to eligible personal accounts. Drive and Gemini app support arrive over the following weeks. Availability can vary by region and language. Some sources list Drive and Calendar as currently limited to the US in English.

AI Mode cannot take actions on your money or files. It only reads the data you explicitly allow. If a response uses the wrong context, tell it to ignore that source or disconnect the app.

## Conclusion

Connecting Wallet, Drive and the Gemini app turns AI Mode from a general search tool into one that already knows the relevant details from your accounts. The setup takes a few minutes and stays under your control. Start with Wallet today, then add the others as they become available. Check the official Google blog for the latest rollout status.
