Switching AI assistants no longer means starting from zero. Google lets you bring preferences, context, and full chat history from ChatGPT or Claude into Gemini.

This guide covers the official process for personal accounts. It uses the memory import prompt and ZIP upload for chats. You can continue conversations and keep personal context intact.

## Why transfer your history

AI assistants build context over time. Your preferred tone, ongoing projects, and past decisions live in those chats. Moving them saves hours of re-explaining. Gemini treats imported data as part of its memory system, so future responses stay relevant.

The feature works on free and paid personal Google accounts for users 18 and older. Work, school, and supervised accounts cannot use it. It is also unavailable in the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK, plus in Gemini within Messages, Chrome, or Android XR.

## Import memory and preferences

Memory covers how you like the AI to respond: writing style, interests, recurring instructions, and personal details.

1. Open gemini.google.com and sign in with your personal Google Account.
2. Click Settings at the bottom left, then select Import memory to Gemini.
3. Copy the prompt Gemini provides.
4. Paste that prompt into a new chat in ChatGPT or Claude.
5. Wait for the summary, then copy the full response.
6. Return to Gemini, paste the summary into the text field, and click Add memory.

Gemini creates a new chat and begins using the imported details. You can review or edit what it retained later in settings.

This step takes only a few minutes once you have the response.

## Import full chat history

Chat history moves the actual conversations so you can search and continue them.

First export the data from your previous platform.

### From ChatGPT

1. Click your username at the bottom left.
2. Open Settings, then Data controls.
3. Next to Export data, click Export and confirm.

OpenAI emails a download link. Exports can take up to a day. The link expires after 24 hours, so download promptly.

### From Claude

1. Click your username at the bottom left.
2. Open Settings, then Privacy.
3. Next to Export data, click Export.
4. Choose the date range and confirm.

Anthropic emails the download link.

Once you have the ZIP file:

1. Return to gemini.google.com.
2. Go to Settings > Import memory to Gemini.
3. Under Import chats, click Add.
4. Select the ZIP file (up to 5 GB).

You can upload up to five ZIP files per day. Processing time varies with file size and may take several hours.

Imported chats appear in the sidebar with a special icon. You can search them, open threads, and continue right where you left off. Individual chats or entire imports can be deleted later.

## What transfers and what does not

- Preferences, tone, and personal context move via the memory prompt.
- Full text conversations transfer via the ZIP.
- Uploaded files, images, custom GPTs, or Claude Projects do not transfer.
- Tool configurations and plugins stay behind.

Review the imported memory in a new chat by asking Gemini what it knows about you. Correct any details that feel off.

## Tips for a clean transfer

Export only the date range you need if your history is large. Check the ZIP size before uploading. Keep the original export as a backup. After import, test a few continued chats to confirm context carried over.

If an upload fails partway, delete the partial import entry and retry. Google processes the data under its privacy policy. You can manage or delete activity anytime in Gemini settings.

## Conclusion

The official import tools remove the biggest barrier to trying Gemini. Memory arrives in minutes. Full history arrives once the export finishes. Combined with recent model updates, this makes a practical switch for many users.

Start with the memory prompt if you mainly want style and preferences. Add the ZIP when you need searchable past threads. Both methods are free for eligible personal accounts.
