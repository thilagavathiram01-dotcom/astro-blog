Gemini Intelligence brings proactive, on-device AI to supported Android phones so your device handles multi-step work while you stay in control. It expanded in November 2026 to Pixel 10 series devices via the November Pixel Drop and to the Motorola Signature 27 (pre-order in the US starting 19 November).

Rambler turns spoken thoughts into clean, organised text in real time. Task automation lets Gemini order food, book rides, or complete other multi-step actions across supported apps in the US, UK, and India.

These features require devices that meet Gemini Intelligence hardware criteria (typically 12 GB+ RAM and qualified SoCs) and the latest software.

## Check if Your Device Supports Gemini Intelligence

Open Settings > System > Software updates and install the latest update. Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold receive Gemini Intelligence with the November 2026 Pixel Drop. Motorola Signature 27 ships with it. Select Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, Z Fold8, Z Flip8, and S26 series also support it.

Confirm the Gemini app is updated. Features appear gradually by region and carrier. If Rambler or task options are missing, wait for the staged rollout or check for a system update.

## Enable and Use Rambler for Clean Voice Typing

Rambler lives in Gboard. It removes filler words (“um”, “uh”), fixes self-corrections, adds punctuation, and structures sentences from natural speech. It also handles code-switching between languages in one utterance.

1. Open any app that uses Gboard (Messages, Gmail, Notes, etc.).
2. Tap the microphone icon or long-press the space bar to start voice typing.
3. Speak freely. Rambler processes the audio on-device or via Gemini and inserts the cleaned text.
4. Review and edit if needed. The result is ready to send.

Example: Say “Can you pick up milk and bread wait no actually eggs and milk from the store after work.” Rambler produces: “Can you pick up eggs and milk from the store after work?”

It is especially useful for quick notes, messages, or when you switch between languages mid-sentence.

## Automate Multi-Step Tasks

Gemini Intelligence can complete multi-step tasks such as ordering groceries, booking a ride, or making a reservation. These work through the Gemini app and selected partner apps.

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Describe the full request in natural language: “Order my usual coffee from the nearby café and book a ride to pick it up in 15 minutes.”
3. Confirm the plan Gemini shows. It interacts with the relevant apps in the background.
4. Review the confirmation. You stay in control and can cancel or edit.

Availability depends on country, language, and device eligibility. Background execution is live in the US, UK, and India on supported devices.

Custom widgets are also part of the same update. Long-press the home screen, choose Widgets, and look for AI-powered options such as a spending tracker or game-day widget. Describe what you want and Gemini generates it.

## Tips for Best Results

- Keep Gboard and the Gemini app updated.
- Speak naturally; Rambler is designed for real speech patterns, not perfect dictation.
- For tasks, include clear details (time, place, preferences) so the automation succeeds on the first try.
- Privacy controls remain in Gemini settings. You can manage activity history and training preferences separately.
- Test on Wi-Fi first if you are on a metered connection, as some features may use cloud processing.
