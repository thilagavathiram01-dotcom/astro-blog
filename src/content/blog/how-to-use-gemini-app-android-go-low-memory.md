Google expanded the Gemini app to eligible Android (Go edition) phones on 18 November 2026. These devices often have limited RAM, yet the rebuilt app now runs the core Gemini experience.

Hundreds of millions of people use lower-memory Android phones as their main internet access. Until this update, those phones offered only basic voice commands. The re-architected app changes that.

![Person holding a black Android smartphone](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1592890288564-76628a30a657?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Eligibility Requirements

The Gemini mobile app works on Android (Go edition) devices with 2GB of RAM or more. You need a signed-in personal Google Account, or a work or school account that includes Gemini Apps access.

Family Link managers can disable Gemini for users under 13, or the applicable age in their country. Availability follows the countries and languages where Gemini already works.

On these devices, Gemini arrives as part of the Google Search app. Update the Google or Google Search app in the Play Store if needed. You can also download the standalone Gemini app from Google Play. Some newer partner devices, including from Transsion, ship with it preloaded.

## How to Start a Conversation

Open Gemini by touching and holding the Home button. On supported devices, press and hold the Power button.

Note that "Hey Google" and "Ask about this screen" do not work on Android (Go edition) devices. Use the button methods instead.

Once open, talk or type normally. Gemini Live supports spoken conversations in more than 45 languages. Practice an interview, brainstorm ideas, or handle everyday tasks by voice.

![Android phone showing Gemini interface](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1556656793-08538906a9f8?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Key Features on Low-Memory Devices

The rebuilt app keeps the core experience intact:

- **Stay connected**: Ask Gemini to call or send text messages to contacts.
- **Camera help**: Point the camera at notes, forms, or signs to translate text or get step-by-step explanations.
- **Image creation**: Generate images with Nano Banana, such as a simple flyer for a sale.
- **Plan your day**: Set alarms or create calendar events.
- **Play media**: Request music by song, mood, or activity.
- **Upload files**: Add photos or documents for more context in a chat.
- **Local information**: Ask for drive times or complex local searches.

These features run efficiently because Google optimised the app for lower memory. Heavy multitasking may still strain a 2GB device, so close unused apps when using voice chat or image tools.

## Practical Tips for Smooth Use

Keep the Google Search app updated. Free storage space before long sessions. Use short, clear requests to reduce processing time.

If you have other AI tools, check our guide on [Gemini regional voices](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-regional-voices/) for related customisation options that also apply here.

Test camera features in good light. For image generation, start with simple prompts like a bakery flyer.

## Limitations to Know

No wake-word activation. No screen awareness. Performance depends on the specific device and network. Work and school accounts require the right plan or license.

The app replaces Assistant for Android (Go edition). Older phones below 2GB RAM stay on basic voice features.

## Conclusion

Eligible Android Go users can now access Gemini Live, camera assistance, and image creation without upgrading hardware. Download or update the app, sign in, and start with a voice conversation or camera scan.

Confirm your device meets the 2GB RAM threshold in Settings. Check Google’s support page for the latest eligibility details.
