Google rolled out Personal Intelligence for Gemini in Android Auto on October 20, 2026. The feature lets the assistant pull details from your connected Google apps so you can ask natural questions while keeping your hands on the wheel. It is rolling out now on Android Auto and arrives for cars with Google built-in in the coming months.

This guide shows how to turn it on, what it can do, and how to control the data it uses. Requirements are straightforward: an Android phone running Android 11 or newer with at least 2 GB of RAM, Gemini set as your assistant, and a personal Google account for users aged 18 or older. It is not available for Workspace business, enterprise, or education accounts, nor in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, or Nigeria.

## What Personal Intelligence does in the car

Personal Intelligence connects Gemini to apps you choose: Workspace (Gmail and Google Calendar), Google Photos, Google Maps, YouTube, and Search. With permission, it retrieves specific details and reasons across them.

Examples from Google include:

- Ask “What’s my flight gate and is my flight on time?” on the way to the airport. Gemini pulls the confirmation from Gmail and checks status without separate searches.
- Say “Take me to my doctor’s appointment.” It finds the event in Calendar and starts navigation.
- Request “Find the car wash from the photo I took last week and navigate there.” It locates the place from Photos.
- Ask for “an indoor restaurant along my route that matches my usual vibe.” It factors in preferences from your history.

Without Personal Intelligence, Gemini still handles navigation, music, calls, and general questions. Answers simply stay generic.

If you already enabled the feature in the Gemini app on your phone, the same settings carry over to Android Auto automatically. Cars with Google built-in share personal responses only when your phone is connected in the vehicle.

## How to enable Personal Intelligence

Open the Gemini app on the phone that connects to your car. The setting lives in the same place whether you use it on the phone or in the car.

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Tap your profile picture or the menu, then open Settings.
3. Select Personal Intelligence (or Connected Apps).
4. Choose “Connect all” or pick individual apps such as Workspace, Photos, Maps, YouTube, and Search.
5. Confirm the connections. You can turn any of them off later.

Once enabled, connect your phone to the car via Android Auto. Use “Hey Google,” tap the microphone on the screen, or press the voice button on the steering wheel. Gemini responds with the personalized context when the feature is active.

Privacy controls stay with you. Apps are not connected until you turn the feature on, and you can disable it anytime in Gemini settings. Google states that personal data is not used to train the models; your inputs and outputs while using Gemini are handled according to its privacy policy.

## Tips for using it while driving

Start with one or two apps if you want to limit what Gemini can see. Calendar alone covers appointments. Gmail helps with bookings and confirmations. Photos is useful for places you have visited.

Test simple requests first while parked. Ask about a calendar event or a recent photo location, then check that navigation starts correctly. Gemini can make mistakes, so verify important details such as flight times or addresses before relying on them.

Voice Match and “Hey Google” need to be enabled on your phone for hands-free use. Keep the phone unlocked or within range as required by Android Auto. If responses feel off, return to Gemini settings and review which apps are connected.

For cars with Google built-in, wait for the later rollout and confirm that Gemini is selected as the assistant in the vehicle’s Google settings. Personal answers appear only when the phone is present and connected.
