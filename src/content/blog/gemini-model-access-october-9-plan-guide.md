Google is narrowing which Gemini models a personal account can pick in the Gemini app. If you do not pay for a Google AI plan, the switch starts on October 9, 2026. AI Plus subscribers get a separate email with their own date.

The change is documented on Google's Gemini Apps help page, [Changes to Gemini model access and limits](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17004136). It applies when you use a personal account. Workspace and school accounts are not the subject of that October 9 note.

Update the Gemini mobile app before you rely on the model menu. Google says the latest app version is required for the best experience.

## What each plan can use

Google's table lists three model families: Flash-Lite, Flash, and Pro. After the change takes effect, reporting of that table lines up like this:

| Google AI plan | Flash-Lite | Flash | Pro | Deep Think |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No plan | Yes | No | No | No |
| AI Plus | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| AI Pro | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| AI Ultra | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Google states two points directly. People without an AI subscription see the change starting October 9. AI Plus subscribers should receive an email that explains when the change reaches them. AI Pro and AI Ultra also get Deep Think, which Google describes as maximum parallel reasoning.

Do not treat October 9 as the Plus cutoff. Watch the inbox tied to that Google Account.

## Check your current model before October 9

Spend two minutes in the app so you know what you will lose.

1. Open the Gemini app or go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) and sign in with the personal account you actually use.
2. Start a new chat so an old thread does not hide the picker.
3. Tap or click the model name near the composer. Labels vary by app version. You may see Flash-Lite, Flash, Pro, or an older nickname such as Fast or Thinking.
4. Note which options are selectable today. A greyed-out Pro row usually means the account already lacks that tier.
5. Open the account menu and confirm whether the account shows no plan, AI Plus, AI Pro, or AI Ultra.

If Pro is in your daily workflow and the account has no plan, that path ends on October 9. Plus users keep Flash, then lose Pro on the date in Google's email.

On desktop you can also type a model shortcut in the composer if your account still supports it. The menu is the reliable check, because shortcuts cannot unlock a model your plan no longer includes.

## Match the model to the task

Flash-Lite is the model left for unpaid accounts. Use it for short questions, rewrites, quick translations, and outlines. It is the wrong default for a long file, a multi-step coding task, or a prompt that needs careful checking.

Flash stays on AI Plus and above. Use it when the answer has to follow a longer brief, compare a few options, or work through a spreadsheet export. If you are building with the API instead of the app, the app picker and the API catalog are separate products. The [Gemini 3.8 Flash app and API guide](/blog/gemini-3-8-flash-app-and-api/) covers that split.

Pro stays on AI Pro and AI Ultra. Keep it for code review, dense documents, and prompts where a wrong step is expensive. Pro draws more of your usage budget than Flash-Lite. Google lists the Pro model among the features that can hit a limit faster.

Deep Think is the extra option on AI Pro and AI Ultra. Google calls it maximum parallel reasoning. Turn it on only when the question is hard enough to justify the extra usage. Everyday lookups do not need it.

## Set effort without burning the limit

For each available model, Google lets you pick an effort level: low, medium, or high. Higher effort can finish harder tasks and return a more complete answer. It also uses more of your limit.

A practical split:

- Low for lookups, short edits, and captions.
- Medium for email drafts, study notes, and routine code comments.
- High for plans, comparisons, and debugging that you would otherwise send to Pro.

If you are on the free tier after October 9, high effort on Flash-Lite is the main lever left. It will not turn Flash-Lite into Pro. It can make a single answer more thorough, then leave you with less room later in the same window.

## How the usage caps work

The October model change sits on top of the compute-based limits Google introduced for Gemini Apps on May 17, 2026 for users over 18, and on July 24, 2026 for users under 18. Usage refreshes every five hours until you hit the weekly limit. The calculation includes prompt complexity, the features you use, and chat length.

Google's published multipliers on the same help page:

- No plan: standard limits
- AI Plus: 2x standard limits
- AI Pro: 4x standard limits
- AI Ultra: 5x or 20x higher than AI Pro, depending on the subscription

Features that spend the budget faster include image, video, and music generation, Deep Research, the Pro model, extended thinking, and Deep Think.

When the window is empty, wait for the five-hour refresh or the weekly reset. Upgrading raises the cap. It does not make a finished window refill immediately.

## Decide whether to upgrade

Stay on the free tier if your chats are short and you can live with Flash-Lite. Move routine file work to a desktop tool you already pay for, and keep Gemini for quick drafts.

AI Plus is the middle step if you need Flash but not Pro. Wait for Google's email before you assume Pro is gone on October 9. If that email arrives and your work depends on Pro, Plus is no longer enough.

AI Pro is the lowest plan Google's help page pairs with all three models plus Deep Think. Choose it if you hit Flash-Lite limits every week or if coding and long documents are the reason you open Gemini.

AI Ultra is the plan for the higher usage multiple. Google lists Ultra at 5x or 20x the AI Pro limit, depending on which Ultra subscription you hold. Deep Think is already on Pro, so Ultra is a capacity buy, not the only way to get that option after this update.

You can subscribe, upgrade, change, or cancel a Google AI plan from Gemini Apps. Google documents those steps in [Manage your Google AI plan](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14517446).

## Tips before the cutoff

Export anything you still need from a Pro chat. Chat history should remain, but you will not be able to continue that thread on Pro if the plan no longer includes it.

Split heavy prompts. A short Flash-Lite question plus a later paid-plan pass often costs less than one oversized prompt that burns the five-hour window.

Avoid Deep Research and media generation on the same day you need a long writing session. Those features share the same compute budget.

If you use Gemini on more than one phone, sign out of extra accounts and confirm the plan on the account that owns the subscription. A work profile and a personal profile can show different model lists.

## Bottom line

October 9, 2026 is the start date for people with no Google AI subscription. They keep Flash-Lite. AI Plus keeps Flash and loses Pro on a date Google emails. AI Pro and AI Ultra keep Flash-Lite, Flash, and Pro, and both get Deep Think. Effort level is the control you still have inside whatever model your plan allows.

Check the picker this weekend, read the Plus email when it arrives, and match the plan to the model you actually use.
