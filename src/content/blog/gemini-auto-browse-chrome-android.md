Gemini in Chrome now lets you hand multi-step web tasks to Auto Browse on Android. Instead of switching between tabs to compare options or fill forms, you describe the goal and Gemini navigates the sites for you.

This feature rolled out to Android users in the US earlier in 2026 and expanded to India for eligible subscribers. It builds on the basic Gemini in Chrome tools that summarize pages and answer questions about what you are viewing.

![Laptop screen showing code next to a phone](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-oYzjGQ7LCVE?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What Auto Browse Does

Auto Browse turns Gemini into an agent that clicks, scrolls, and types on your behalf. You stay in control because it shows a plan first and pauses for confirmation on sensitive steps.

Google lists practical examples: booking parking for an event, updating a recurring online order, or organizing travel for a vacation. It can also compare products across pages, add items to a cart, or fill forms using details from your Google account.

It does not work in Incognito mode. Sensitive actions such as payments or final submissions require your explicit approval. The model is trained to detect threats like prompt injection.

## Requirements Before You Start

You need a few things in place:

- Android device with the latest version of Chrome.
- Personal Google account signed in to Chrome.
- Google AI Pro or Google AI Ultra subscription.
- Device language set to English.
- Age 18 or older.
- Location in the US or India (availability is expanding).
- Safe Browsing set to Standard or Enhanced protection.
- Permission granted for Chrome to auto browse on your behalf.

School accounts do not support it unless an admin enables the feature. Check that Chrome is updated from the Play Store before you begin.

If you already use Gemini in Chrome for page summaries, that does not automatically unlock Auto Browse. The subscription and opt-in are required.

## How to Open Gemini in Chrome on Android

Open Chrome on your Android phone. Look for the Gemini icon (sparkle) next to the address bar, or open the three-dot menu and select Ask Gemini.

Tapping it brings up a sheet similar to the desktop experience. You can add the current page as context or keep it docked at the bottom while you browse. Connected apps such as Calendar and Keep remain available in the tools menu.

Grant the auto browse permission when prompted. This step is required before Gemini can act on sites.

## Step-by-Step: Run Your First Auto Browse Task

1. Open Chrome and tap Ask Gemini.
2. Type or speak a clear multi-step request. Example: “Find parking near the downtown arena for Saturday at 7 PM and fill in my contact details from my Google account.”
3. Submit the prompt.
4. Review the plan Gemini presents. It lists the sites it intends to visit and the information it will share.
5. If the plan looks correct, tap Start Task.
6. Watch the dedicated auto browse tab. An indicator appears on the tab while Gemini works. You can leave Chrome and return later.
7. When Gemini reaches a sensitive step such as payment or final confirmation, it pauses and asks you to review or take over.

You can stop or pause the task from the Gemini panel at any time. After you intervene, you can resume control or hand the task back.

Daily limits apply. Google AI Pro users get up to 20 Auto Browse requests per day. Google AI Ultra users get up to 200. Simultaneous tasks are also limited.

## Tips for Better Results

Be specific in your prompt. Include dates, locations, budget limits, and any personal details Gemini should use. Vague requests produce incomplete plans.

Start with smaller tasks while you learn the flow. Booking parking or updating a known order is safer than complex multi-site research on the first try.

Check the plan carefully before starting. Gemini chooses the sites, so confirm it understood your constraints.

If a site requires a login, Gemini can use Chrome’s password manager. Still review the actions before approving.

## Safety and Control

Auto Browse is designed with safeguards. It asks for confirmation before completing high-stakes actions. You can take over a task at any point.

Keep Safe Browsing enabled. Review the sites listed in the plan. Do not use Auto Browse for accounts or data you would not share with a trusted assistant.

If the feature does not appear, confirm your subscription status, language setting, and that you are in a supported region. Rollouts can take time to reach every eligible account.

## Conclusion

Gemini Auto Browse in Chrome on Android reduces the manual work of multi-step web tasks. Once you meet the requirements and review the plan, Gemini handles the navigation while you stay in control of sensitive steps.

Try a simple task such as comparing parking options or updating an order. Adjust your prompts based on the results, and use the daily limits to experiment without overdoing it.

As agentic browsing expands, these controls become essential for using AI tools safely on mobile.
