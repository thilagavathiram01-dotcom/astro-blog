Gemini can run tasks in the background and on a schedule. You tell it what to do and when. It handles the work across connected apps while you focus on other things. This guide shows the official steps from Google Help to set up recurring tasks.

You need a Google AI Pro or Ultra plan, a personal Google Account, Keep Activity turned on, and Personal Intelligence enabled. Tasks are not available in the European Economic Area, Nigeria, Switzerland, or the United Kingdom. Work and school accounts are excluded.

## What you need before you start

Confirm your plan at one.google.com or in the Gemini app settings. Open Gemini on the web at gemini.google.com, the mobile app, or the Mac desktop app. Sign in with the same personal account.

Enable Keep Activity so Gemini can resume chats and tasks. Turn on Personal Intelligence if you want it to use context from Gmail, Calendar, and Drive. Connect the apps you need under Settings. Common ones include Gmail, Drive, Calendar, and Keep.

If Task mode is missing, update the app and check again. Rollouts reach accounts at different times.

## Create a one-time task

On the web or Mac app, go to gemini.google.com. Below the text box, click Task. On Android, open the Gemini app and tap Task above the prompt.

Describe the goal clearly. Include the finish line and any limits. Example: “Summarize unread emails from the last 24 hours that need a reply. List the top five with suggested responses. Do not send any emails.”

You can attach files from your device, Drive, or a Notebook. Mention @skills if you have custom instructions. Click or tap Submit. Gemini shows a plan. Review it, then confirm to start.

You can leave the app. Gemini continues in the background. Check progress in the thread or the Scheduled page. It may pause for your input on sensitive steps, such as browser logins or confirmations before sending messages.

## Add a schedule for recurring work

Schedules turn a task into a repeating job. You can create them conversationally or from the Scheduled page.

In a Task prompt, include the timing. Example: “Every weekday at 7 AM, review my calendar and Gmail for the day. Prepare a short priority list with meetings and emails that need attention. Send the summary as a note in Keep.”

Gemini creates the schedule. You can also build one manually. Open the sidebar and click Scheduled. Click Create a scheduled task. Enter a name, the instructions under Plan, and the frequency. Time-based schedules are supported. Event-based triggers depend on connected apps.

Save it. The schedule appears under Active. Gemini runs it at the set time and creates a new thread for each run.

## Monitor and manage scheduled tasks

Open the Scheduled page from the sidebar. You see Active, past, and paused items. Filter by status or search by keyword.

Click a task to open its thread. The progress chip at the top shows completed, current, and planned steps. Review updates before acting on results.

To edit, open the schedule and change the plan or timing. Pause or delete from the same view. If a run needs attention, Gemini notifies you. Common cases include a site asking for login or a step that requires confirmation before it spends money or sends a message.

You stay in control. Gemini does not complete sensitive actions without your approval.

## Practical examples that work

Start simple. A daily morning brief pulls from Calendar and Gmail. A weekly report summarizes Drive files in a folder and drafts a short update. An inbox monitor flags emails matching a keyword and logs them to a Sheet.

Use skills for consistent style. Create a skill that defines how you want summaries written, then reference it with @ in the task. Pair with Connected Apps for actions in third-party tools where available.

Limits vary by plan. Pro subscribers get a lower number of multi-step runs than Ultra. Check current limits in the app.

## Tips for reliable results

Write clear goals with a finish line. State what Gemini must not do. “Do not book, pay, or email anyone” keeps you in the loop.

Test a one-time version first. Watch the first scheduled run and adjust the prompt if the output misses key details. Update the model to Gemini 4 Argon when available on your plan for longer reasoning tasks. See our guide on [using Gemini 4 Argon in the app](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-4-argon-gemini-app/) for model selection.

Keep Activity must stay on. If a task stalls on a browser step, take over when prompted or switch to the remote browser option if offered.

## Conclusion

Scheduled tasks in Gemini turn one-off prompts into reliable automation. Set them up with a Pro or Ultra plan, connect the apps you trust, and review the first runs. Start with a daily brief or inbox check, then expand once you see consistent results.

The feature keeps you in control on sensitive steps. Official Help pages remain the source for availability and limits as Google expands the rollout.
