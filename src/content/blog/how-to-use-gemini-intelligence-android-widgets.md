Gemini Intelligence turns your Android phone into a proactive assistant that handles multi-step work across apps. Google introduced the suite in May 2026 for the latest Pixel and Samsung Galaxy devices, with wider rollout across watches, cars, and more later in the year.

You stay in control. Gemini acts only when you ask and stops when the task finishes. Final confirmations remain with you. This guide covers the practical ways to use the main features: task automation, Rambler in Gboard, and Create My Widget.

![Person holding a black Android smartphone](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-Z9fW8Nn7D24?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What Gemini Intelligence Includes

Gemini Intelligence is the name for several AI upgrades that work together on supported Android devices. The core pieces are:

- Multi-step task automation across selected apps
- Rambler for cleaner voice-to-text in Gboard
- Create My Widget for generative home-screen widgets
- Smarter Autofill that uses Personal Intelligence (opt-in)
- Gemini in Chrome for research and comparisons

Features roll out in waves. Check your device settings or the Gemini app for availability. Official details appear on the [Google blog announcement](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/gemini-intelligence/).

For developers building related experiences, Android also ships AppFunctions so apps can expose tools to the system. See our earlier post on [adaptive Glance widgets](/blog/adaptive-glance-widgets-phone-wear-auto/) for the underlying widget technology.

## Automate Multi-Step Tasks

On supported phones you can long-press the power button over content on screen and ask Gemini to act. Examples from Google include booking a spin-class bike, finding a course syllabus in Gmail then adding books to a cart, or turning a notes-app grocery list into a delivery order.

You can also snap a photo of a brochure and say “Find a tour like this on Expedia for a group of six.” Progress appears in notifications. You review and confirm the final step.

## Use Rambler in Gboard

Rambler cleans natural speech into polished text. You speak with ums, self-corrections, and pauses. Gemini keeps the important parts and produces a concise message.

It handles multiple languages in one utterance. Enable it when the waveform appears across the keyboard. Audio is used only for real-time transcription and is not stored.

Open Gboard, start voice input, and look for the Rambler indicator. Speak freely, then review the cleaned text before you send.

## Create Custom Widgets with Create My Widget

Create My Widget is Google’s first generative UI feature for Android home screens and Wear OS tiles. You describe what you want in plain language. Gemini builds a functional, resizable widget.

Examples from the official announcement:

- “Suggest three high-protein meal prep recipes every week”
- “Show only wind speed and rain” for a cyclist

The widget can combine data from Google Search, Calendar, or other supported sources. It uses Material 3 Expressive styling.

![Smartphone showing a custom Android home screen interface](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-3XzV9VJlIUo?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

### Step-by-step: Build a Widget

1. Confirm your device supports Gemini Intelligence (latest Pixel or Galaxy models initially).
2. Long-press an empty area of the home screen.
3. Tap Widgets.
4. Look for the Create option or open the Create My Widget experience (availability varies by software version).
5. Type a clear prompt that states the information and how often it should update.
6. Review the generated preview. Refine with a follow-up prompt if needed.
7. Adjust size and placement, then add it to the home screen.

A dedicated Create My Widget app later appeared on the Play Store for eligible devices. It offers guided categories such as weather alerts, daily brief, markets, and important dates, plus a free-form prompt box.

Widgets work on the phone and can appear on Wear OS watches. They stay up to date without opening multiple apps.

## Tips for Better Results

Be specific in prompts. Include the exact stats or update frequency you care about. Test one change at a time when refining a widget.

For automation, start with simple requests on supported apps. Watch the notification progress so you can stop the flow if needed. Keep Personal Intelligence connections opt-in and review what data is linked.

Rambler works best when you speak at a natural pace. Review the output for names, numbers, and mixed-language phrases before sending.

## Privacy and Control

Google states that you remain in control. Gemini acts only on your command and stops when the task is complete. Autofill connections to Personal Intelligence are strictly opt-in and can be toggled off in settings. Rambler does not store audio.

Full privacy details are in the linked Google security post from the announcement.

## Conclusion

Gemini Intelligence reduces switching between apps for routine work. Create My Widget puts the exact information you need on the home screen. Rambler turns messy speech into clean text. Task automation handles multi-step flows while you stay in the loop for final actions.

Check your Pixel or Galaxy phone for the features as they roll out. Start with a simple widget or a voice message to see the difference.
