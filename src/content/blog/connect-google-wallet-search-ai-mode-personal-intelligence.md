Google Search AI Mode now lets you connect more of your apps to Personal Intelligence. Starting 23 November 2026, you can opt in to link Google Wallet so AI Mode draws on your spending data for more relevant answers. Drive and the Gemini app follow in the coming weeks. These join existing options like Gmail, Calendar, and Photos.

The feature stays off until you choose it. You control which apps join and can turn any of them off later. This guide shows the exact steps from Google’s announcement and how to use the new connections safely.

## What the expansion adds

Personal Intelligence in AI Mode already used selected Google apps to tailor responses. The November update expands that list. Wallet is available to connect right away. It gives AI Mode a read-only view of your transactions and linked cards.

Google states that AI Mode cannot move, manage, or invest money. It only reads the information you allow. Drive will let AI Mode reference documents you store there. Linking the Gemini app will share context between Search and your chats so ideas move between the two without starting over.

You also start seeing more personally relevant responses in AI Overviews and AI Mode based on recent search context. If you researched a trip, a new thread can pick up where you left off.

## How to connect your apps

Google lists a short path. Open Search Personalization, select Connected Apps, and choose the ones you want.

On desktop or the Google app:

1. Go to Google Search and sign in with your personal account.
2. Tap your profile picture.
3. Select Search personalization.
4. Choose Connected Apps.
5. Toggle Google Wallet on. Confirm any permission prompts.
6. Save and return to AI Mode.

The same screen will show Drive and the Gemini app once those roll out. You can also reach the page at myactivity.google.com/search-services/apps.

If you already enabled Personal Intelligence for Gmail or Photos, the new toggles appear alongside them. Turning Wallet on does not affect settings in the Gemini app. Those remain separate.

## What you can ask after connecting Wallet

With Wallet linked, AI Mode can answer questions about your spending patterns. Google gives examples such as mapping financial milestones and finding ways to trim everyday costs. It can also suggest how to maximize credit-card benefits based on the cards already in Wallet.

Try prompts like these after the connection is active:

- Review my recent spending and suggest one area to cut this month.
- Which of my cards offers the best cash-back for grocery purchases?
- Summarize my subscription charges from the last 90 days.

Answers stay inside AI Mode. You still complete any payments or changes yourself. Link bank or card accounts inside Wallet through Plaid if you want deeper transaction detail; that step is optional and remains inside the Wallet app.

## Privacy controls and what stays off

Every connection is opt-in. You decide when to turn it on and can reverse the choice at any time from the same Connected Apps list. Google says you remain in control of personalization settings.

Soon a Personalization icon will appear under tailored responses so you can see the context AI Mode used. If something is wrong, reply in the thread to correct it—for example, “I’m no longer a vegetarian.” That update applies going forward.

You can also turn personalization off entirely in Search Settings. Temporary threads in AI Mode keep a session detached from your account so responses do not use or affect past preferences.

Wallet data is read-only. Google’s post confirms AI Mode cannot transfer funds or change accounts. The same limit applies in the Gemini app.

## Tips for using the new connections

Start with Wallet alone if finance questions are your main need. Test a few prompts, then add Drive or the Gemini app later. This makes it easier to notice which source shaped an answer.

Use the feature for planning and review, not for final decisions on money or sensitive topics. Double-check any figures against Wallet or your bank app.

If responses feel too broad, correct them in the thread. The Personalization icon, once it appears, gives a direct way to review the context.

Availability rolls out. If Wallet does not appear yet, check again later or confirm you are signed in with a personal Google Account in a supported region. Work and school accounts remain excluded, matching earlier Personal Intelligence rules.

## Conclusion

Connecting Google Wallet to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode adds spending context to Search without giving AI Mode control over your money. Drive and the Gemini app expand the same idea further. Follow the Search Personalization steps, keep only the connections you need, and adjust them whenever you want. Official sources below list the exact controls and limits.
