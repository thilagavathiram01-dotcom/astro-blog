**How to Watch the Android Dev Summit 2026 Keynote and Prepare for the Updates**

Android Dev Summit returns on October 28, 2026, after a four-year gap. The free keynote livestream starts at 10 AM PT from Google’s Event Center at Bay View. Official Android leadership will cover new tools, AI experiences, Android XR, and graphics features tied to Android 18 and Jetpack Compose.

This guide shows the exact steps to join the stream, what the agenda highlights, and how to prepare your projects so you can act on the announcements the same day.

## Why the Summit Matters This Year

The last full Android Dev Summit was in 2022. Since then the platform has shifted toward adaptive layouts, on-device AI, and agentic development. The 2026 event focuses on four tracks: AI experiences, Android XR, apps foundation, and tools and performance. Sessions already name “powerful new graphics features in Jetpack Compose and Android 18.”

Developers who watch live can note APIs and best practices before documentation lands on developer.android.com. If you already use Android CLI skills or Firebase AI Logic, the keynote often previews the next integration points.

## Step-by-Step: Join the Keynote Livestream

1. Open the official Android Developers page at developer.android.com/events/dev-summit or go directly to the YouTube channel @AndroidDevelopers.
2. The keynote begins October 28 at 10:00 AM Pacific Time. Convert for your zone (for example, 10:30 PM IST).
3. Use the “Tune in to the Android Dev Summit 2026 keynote” video or the live stream link posted by @AndroidDev. The short promotional clip is already public.
4. Sign up for updates on the event page if you want email reminders for session recordings.
5. For in-person or London Extended attendance, registration was invitation-only; the keynote itself is open to everyone.

After the stream, recordings appear on the Android Developers YouTube channel and the developer.android.com blog. Bookmark developer.android.com/blog because the official blog moved there from Blogger in mid-October 2026.

## What to Expect from the Agenda

Official materials list a keynote with Android leadership plus technical sessions. Early agenda items include:

- AI experiences covering Gemini integration, AppFunctions, and audio-first workflows for glasses.
- Android XR updates with Jetpack SceneCore and partner hardware.
- Tools and performance improvements, including Android CLI skills and agent support in Android Studio.
- Graphics sessions that pair new Jetpack Compose capabilities with Android 18.

No full session list was public days before the event, so treat early reports as directional. The keynote is the reliable source for confirmed APIs and timelines.

## How to Prepare Your Projects Before You Watch

Update your local tools so demos and code samples work immediately.

- Install or update the Android CLI and run `android init` to pull the latest official skills. These skills keep agent prompts aligned with current guidance. See our guide on [setting up Android CLI skills for any agent](/blog/android-cli-skills-setup-any-agent/).
- Switch Android Studio to the latest Canary if you plan to test Antigravity or Gemini 4 Argon agent workflows.
- For client-side Gemini features, confirm Firebase AI Logic is on a recent BoM and that App Check is configured; enforcement becomes required on November 2, 2026.
- If you maintain contacts-related code, review the upcoming WRITE_CONTACTS changes and prefer the standard insert/edit intents.

## Tips for Getting the Most from the Livestream

Take notes only on confirmed statements from the stage or official blog posts. Screenshots of slides are useful, but wait for the developer.android.com write-ups before changing production code. Follow @AndroidDev on X for real-time links to sample projects and documentation.

If the stream buffers, switch to the YouTube mobile app or lower the quality temporarily. After the keynote, the full session videos usually appear within a day or two. Check the new blog feed at developer.android.com/blog/latest for the first official recaps.

## Conclusion

The Android Dev Summit 2026 keynote is the clearest public signal of the next platform direction. Watch at 10 AM PT on October 28, note the graphics and AI announcements, then apply the changes to your Android CLI skills, Firebase AI Logic setup, and adaptive layouts. The official event page and Android Developers YouTube channel remain the only sources you need.
