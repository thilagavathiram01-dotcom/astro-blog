Google added new Connected Apps on November 20, 2026, so US users can finish year-end errands inside Search and the Gemini app. You can now build grocery orders from Target, Albertsons, Safeway, or Shaw’s and move toward checkout. Experian credit checks and loan shopping expand into Search, and H&R Block lets you calculate taxes and estimate a refund without leaving the interface.

These tools sit on the same Connected Apps platform that already links dozens of services. The grocery flows use the Universal Commerce Protocol so the assistant can request real inventory and prepare a cart. This guide walks through setup, grocery ordering, finance tasks, and practical tips so you can clear holiday lists faster.

For the general process of linking any app, see our earlier guide on [how to connect and use Gemini Connected Apps](/blog/connect-use-gemini-connected-apps-guide/).

## Prerequisites for the New Apps

You must be signed in to a personal Google account. Keep Activity (or Gemini activity history) needs to stay on for most third-party Connected Apps. Without it, the grocery and finance options stay unavailable on web, iOS, and Wear OS.

The features are US-only at launch and appear first in English. Availability rolls out gradually, so the toggles may not show on every account the same day. Free and paid Gemini users can access them once they reach your account. Work or school accounts follow separate admin controls and often lack these retail and finance links.

Open Gemini or Search and confirm you are signed in before you look for the new apps.

## Connect the Grocery and Finance Apps

1. Open the Gemini app or visit gemini.google.com.
2. Go to Settings, then Connected Apps or Connectors (sometimes listed under Personal Intelligence or Customize).
3. Locate Albertsons, Safeway, Shaw’s, Target, Experian, or H&R Block.
4. Toggle the app on and complete any sign-in or permission screens. Link a loyalty account if the store offers one.
5. Confirm the app shows as connected.

Repeat for each service you plan to use. You can disconnect later from the same menu. On Search, the same apps surface in AI Mode after the rollout reaches you. Google notes that Connected Apps in Search currently work in AI Mode, with broader surfaces planned.

## Build a Holiday Grocery Order

Once connected, describe what you need in plain language. Gemini pulls inventory where available, suggests items, and assembles a draft cart. You review and confirm before any purchase.

Try these prompts:

- “@Target add ingredients for a Thanksgiving dinner for eight: turkey, stuffing, cranberries, potatoes, green beans, and pumpkin pie supplies. Keep it under $120.”
- “Build a holiday party appetizer cart at Safeway: cheese, crackers, olives, dips, and sparkling water for twelve people.”
- “@Albertsons find milk, bread, eggs, and bananas that are in stock for pickup near me.”

Use the @ tag when you want a specific retailer. Gemini shows the proposed cart with prices and availability. Adjust quantities or remove items with follow-up messages. When the list looks correct, follow the checkout path. You may finish inside Gemini or Search, or the flow hands you to the store’s site or app to complete payment. The retailer remains the seller of record.

Always verify the final total, delivery or pickup details, and substitutions yourself. Inventory and prices can change after the draft cart appears.

## Handle Credit and Tax Tasks

Experian was already live in the Gemini app. It is now rolling out to Search so US users can check a credit rating and shop for a personal loan. Open Search or Gemini and ask:

- “Check my Experian credit score.”
- “Show personal loan options based on my Experian profile.”

H&R Block works from Search. Ask:

- “Estimate my 2026 tax refund with H&R Block.”
- “Calculate my taxes for this year using H&R Block.”

The assistant provides an initial summary or calculation. Sensitive steps and final filing still happen on the partner’s site. Do not treat the in-Search estimate as an official filing.

## Tips for Reliable Results

Name the exact store with the @ tag so Gemini does not choose a different retailer. Include budget, quantity, and dietary limits in the first prompt. Keep purchase confirmation enabled so the assistant asks before it buys. Link loyalty accounts early if the integration supports discounts. Start with a small test order to learn the confirmation flow. Check stock on the store site if the cart looks off. For finance tasks, complete any identity verification on the partner site after the summary appears.

Angi and Thumbtack account linking for home services arrives in the coming weeks. Until then, grocery and finance cover the immediate year-end needs.

## Conclusion

The November 20 Connected Apps update puts grocery ordering for Target, Albertsons, Safeway, and Shaw’s inside Gemini and Search, along with Experian credit tools and H&R Block tax estimates. US users can describe a holiday list, review a cart with live inventory, and move to checkout without opening separate store apps. Connect the services once in settings, use clear prompts or the @ tag, and always verify the final order yourself.

Start with one store and a short list. Then add finance tasks when you are comfortable with the flow. The same steps work on the web and in the Gemini mobile app.
