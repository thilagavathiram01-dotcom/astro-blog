Google Universal Cart gives you one place to collect products while you browse Search or talk with Gemini. It watches for price drops and restocks, then helps you finish the purchase through Google Pay or the merchant site.

The feature rolled out in the United States in mid-October 2026. You can add items from Google Search, Shopping, AI Mode, and the Gemini apps. YouTube and Gmail support is planned later.

![Miniature shopping cart on a rug](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-YmBgW57IPtk?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What Universal Cart Does

Universal Cart stores items from different retailers in one list. Once an item is saved, Google checks for lower prices, promo codes, and stock changes in the background. The cart runs on Gemini models, so suggestions improve over time.

Products appear grouped by merchant. You can review details, remove items, or proceed to checkout. For supported merchants you can pay with Google Pay on Google. For others the cart transfers the items to the merchant website so you complete the order there. The merchant remains the seller of record either way.

After purchase, some merchants share order status back to Google. You then see shipped or delivered updates in the My Orders section of the cart. Data is stored in Google Wallet.

## Add Products from Google Search

1. Open Google Search or Google Shopping on the web or the Google app.
2. Search for a product you want.
3. When a listing shows an add-to-cart option, select it. The item moves to your Universal Cart.
4. Repeat for other products from different stores. They all land in the same cart.

AI Mode on Search also supports adding items. Look for the cart icon or the explicit “add to cart” control on product results.

## Add Products While Using Gemini

In the Gemini app or web version, describe what you need. Gemini can surface product options. When an item is available to save, choose the add option. The product appears in your Universal Cart.

Note that Gemini and AI Mode have limited access to your cart data. They may not list every item already in the cart or show live order status inside the chat. You still manage the full list inside the cart view.

If you already use Gemini with other Google apps, see the guide on [connecting apps to Gemini](/blog/connect-apps-to-gemini/) for related setup tips.

## Open and Manage Your Cart

You can reach the cart from Search, Shopping, or the Gemini app once items are added. Inside the cart:

- Review each product and merchant.
- Remove anything you no longer want.
- Check for price-drop or restock alerts.
- View any promo codes Google found.

When you are ready, choose checkout. For participating merchants the flow stays on Google and uses your saved Google Pay methods. For others Google pre-fills the merchant cart so you finish on their site.

Google Wallet also looks at your linked cards and loyalty programs. It can suggest the payment method that earns the best perks for the purchase.

## Track Orders After Purchase

Open the cart and select the My Orders tab. Supported merchants send fulfillment updates such as shipped or delivered. You can also review recent purchases that ran through the cart.

If an order does not appear, the merchant may not share data with Google. Contact the merchant directly for any purchase problems. Universal Cart does not replace the merchant’s own support.

## Tips for Better Results

Add items as you research rather than waiting until the end. The background monitoring starts immediately and can catch a drop while you compare options.

Connect your payment methods and loyalty accounts in Google Wallet so the cart can surface relevant savings. Keep the Google app and Gemini app updated for the latest controls.

The feature is currently US-only. Availability can expand later. If you do not see the add-to-cart option, confirm you are signed in with a US Google account and that the product listing supports the cart.

## Limitations to Know

Gemini chat cannot always list every cart item or give live order updates. You must open the cart view for the complete picture. Not every merchant supports Google Pay checkout or order-status sharing. In those cases you finish the purchase on the merchant site and track it there.

YouTube and Gmail integration is not yet live. When it arrives you will be able to save products seen in videos or emails the same way.

## Conclusion

Universal Cart turns scattered product tabs into one managed list. You add items while searching or chatting with Gemini, let Google watch for better prices, and check out when ready. Start by searching for a product you already plan to buy and add it. Then open the cart to see the monitoring and checkout options in action.

The feature stays limited to the United States for now and depends on merchant participation for full checkout and tracking. As more retailers connect, the cart becomes more useful across everyday shopping.
