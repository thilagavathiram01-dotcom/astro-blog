Google Play added two practical tools on 13 October 2026 that change how you discover apps and games. Game Finder uses your play history and stated preferences to surface titles that match your taste. Ask Play now generates side-by-side comparisons so you can evaluate options without opening every listing.

These features rolled out first in English for users in the United States. Additional markets are planned. This guide shows the exact steps from the official announcement and the Android Developers Blog.

## What Game Finder and Gamer DNA do

Game Finder sits inside the Play Store and recommends games based on Gamer DNA. Google describes Gamer DNA as an analysis of how you play plus preferences you share. It looks at genres, mechanics, and your activity rather than only popularity charts.

You can fine-tune it by specifying games, genres, or mechanics you want more of. You can also filter out things you dislike, such as heavy in-app purchases. Recommendations explain why a title appears, show how players with similar Gamer DNA engage with it, list how many people you follow are playing it, and include user review summaries. Trending games appear with short reasons for their rise.

Game Playlists are also arriving. You can curate collections of games. Sharing and browsing other players’ lists will come later.

## How to open Game Finder and set up Gamer DNA

1. Update the Google Play Store app to the latest version from the Play Store itself.
2. Open the Play Store and look for Game Finder. It may appear on the Games tab, in the search area, or as a dedicated entry after the update rolls out to your account.
3. Start the onboarding flow when prompted. Share interests such as preferred genres or mechanics.
4. Rate a few games you already know so the system has clearer signals.
5. Review the initial recommendations. Tap into any title to see the explanation, similar-player activity, and review summary.
6. Return to Gamer DNA settings later to adjust preferences. You can add or remove genres and mechanics at any time.

If Game Finder does not appear, check that your Play Store language is set to English and that your account is in a supported market. The rollout is gradual.

## How Ask Play comparisons work

Ask Play has been available as a conversational layer for finding apps. Starting 13 October 2026 it can generate dynamic side-by-side comparisons directly in results. When you ask about subscription models, offline support, or other differences, Ask Play summarizes store listing details and user review sentiment.

This saves time when you are choosing between two or three similar tools. The comparison pulls from the public listing and reviews rather than inventing features.

Play in Gemini is rolling out next. It brings Play Store apps, games, and developer content into Gemini conversations, including Gemini Live voice sessions. Direct install links will appear in chat. Google reported more than six million app installs driven through Gemini conversations in the first two months after the earlier launch.

## Steps to compare apps with Ask Play

1. Open the Google Play Store.
2. Start a search or tap the Ask Play entry if it is visible.
3. Type or speak a natural question. Examples: “Compare the best offline note-taking apps” or “Which of these two fitness apps works without a subscription?”
4. Review the side-by-side comparison that appears. Check the highlighted differences in pricing, connectivity, and review sentiment.
5. Tap any app name to open its full listing for more details or to install.
6. Ask follow-up questions in the same session if you need clarification on one difference.

Comparisons work best when you name specific apps or describe clear criteria. Broad questions still return recommendations, but the side-by-side view shines when you are deciding between a short list.

## Tips for better results

- Update both the Play Store app and your device’s Google Play services before relying on the new surfaces.
- Be specific in Ask Play prompts. Mention the features that matter most to you (offline mode, one-time purchase, family sharing).
- In Game Finder, rate games honestly. Early ratings shape Gamer DNA more than later ones.
- Check review summaries critically. They are generated from user reviews and can miss recent updates.
- If you develop apps, the same discovery tools affect how users find you. See our guide on [optimizing Android apps for Google Play AI discovery](/blog/optimize-android-app-google-play-ai-discovery/) for listing checklist items.

Availability is still limited to English in the US for Game Finder and is rolling out for Ask Play comparisons. Expand your Play Store language or region settings only if you understand the impact on other recommendations.

## Conclusion

Game Finder and the new Ask Play comparisons give you clearer signals than scrolling charts. Set up Gamer DNA once, then refine it as your tastes change. Use Ask Play when you already have two or three candidates and want a quick side-by-side view drawn from listings and reviews.

Both tools are rolling out now. Check the Play Store after updating the app. For the full set of October 2026 Play changes, including personal vibe-coded apps and Game Playlists, read the official Google blog post.
