Google expanded the Gemini app to Android (Go edition) devices on November 18, 2026. Hundreds of millions of people who rely on lower-memory phones for daily internet access, education, and business can now use the personal AI assistant.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="0" />

Previously these phones offered only basic voice commands. Google re-architected the app so the core Gemini experience runs smoothly on hardware with less memory. This replaces Assistant for Android (Go edition) and delivers a conversational experience designed for efficiency even with lower storage.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="38" />

![Person holding a black Android smartphone](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1592890288564-76628a30a657?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Check Device Eligibility First

Confirm your phone meets the requirements before you start. The Gemini mobile app works on Android (Go edition) devices with 2GB of RAM or more. You need a personal Google Account or a work or school Google Account that includes Gemini Apps access. Availability covers all languages and countries where Gemini is offered.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="38" />

On these devices Gemini arrives as part of the Google Search app. Some newer partner devices, including certain Transsion models, ship with it preloaded. Family Link managers can turn off access for users under 13 (or the applicable age in their country) through Google Family Link settings.

## Update Apps and Install Gemini

Open the Google Play app on your Android Go device. Search for "Google" or "Google Search." If an Update button appears, tap it to install the latest version. If it shows Open, you already have the current version.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="38" />

You can also download the standalone Gemini app from the Play Store. After the update or install finishes, sign in with your eligible Google Account if prompted.

## Start a Conversation

Touch and hold the Home button to open Gemini. On supported devices, press and hold the Power button. Note that "Hey Google" and "Ask about this screen" do not work on Android (Go edition) devices.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="38" />

Once open, type or speak your request. Gemini handles multi-turn conversations and keeps context within the chat.

## What You Can Do with Gemini on Android Go

The rebuilt app supports several practical features:

- Stay connected: Ask Gemini to call or send text messages to contacts.
- Get help with images: Point the camera at notes or forms to translate text or receive step-by-step help. You can also create images with Nano Banana.
- Brainstorm and practice: Use Gemini Live for natural spoken conversations in more than 45 languages, such as interview practice or idea sessions.
- Find local information: Check drive times or ask complex questions like finding a ramen restaurant open for lunch on Tuesday with an EV charger nearby.
- Plan your day: Set alarms or create calendar events.
- Upload files: Add documents, photos, or other files for more context.
- Play media: Request music by mood or activity, for example "Play pop party jams!" or "Play quiet acoustic songs for a dinner party."<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="38" />

These capabilities focus on everyday utility while keeping resource use low. The experience emphasizes cloud-based processing optimized for the device constraints.

## Tips for Smooth Performance

Keep storage free. Android Go devices have limited space, so clear unused apps, photos, and cache regularly. Close background apps before starting a long Gemini Live session.

Update Google Play services and the Google app often. Newer versions include performance improvements tailored for lower-memory hardware.

Use short, clear prompts when possible. This reduces processing time and helps Gemini respond faster on constrained devices. For image tasks, ensure good lighting and a stable connection.

If you develop Android apps, check our guide on [adding Gemini to Android apps with Firebase AI Logic](/blog/add-gemini-3-8-flash-android-firebase-ai-logic/) for related implementation details.

## Limitations to Know

Voice activation via "Hey Google" is not supported. Screen-based questions through "Ask about this screen" are also unavailable. Feature depth matches the core Gemini experience rather than every flagship capability found on higher-memory devices.

Internet access is required for most functions. Offline use remains limited.

## Conclusion

Android Go users now have access to Gemini for help, connection, and creation tasks. Verify your device has at least 2GB RAM, update the Google app, and start with a Home or Power button long-press. Explore Live conversations, camera translation, and image generation to handle daily needs more efficiently.

This expansion puts useful AI tools in the hands of more people without requiring expensive hardware upgrades. Check official support pages for the latest availability in your region.
