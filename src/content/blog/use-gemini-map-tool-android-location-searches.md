You often know the neighborhood you mean but struggle to describe the exact streets or boundaries in words. The Gemini Map tool on Android solves this by letting you point at a live map area and attach it directly to your prompt.

Rolled out widely in early October 2026, this feature lives only in the Gemini mobile app on Android and iOS. It is not available on the web or desktop versions. You select a circular focus area on a Google Maps view, then ask questions about places inside it.

![Person holding a smartphone showing a map interface outdoors](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1512941937669-90a1b58e7e9c?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What the Gemini Map Tool Does

The tool opens a live map centered on your current location with a highlighted circle. You pan, zoom, or search for another place. Once the circle covers the area you care about, you tap Explore this area. Gemini adds a Map Area chip to your prompt box.

Results stay mostly inside that circle. Suggestions appear as cards with photos, ratings, price ranges, and opening hours. A map preview at the top of the answer shows pins for every suggestion. You can export the list to Google Maps as a custom list.

This works best in well-mapped urban areas. In places with sparse data, Gemini may note limited options or suggest nearby places outside the circle. You should still verify important details before traveling.

## Step-by-Step: Open and Attach a Map Area

1. Open the Gemini app on your Android phone. Make sure you are signed in and location services are enabled for the app.

2. Tap the plus (+) button on the left side of the chat input box. A row of tools appears, including Photos, Camera, Files, and others.

3. Scroll sideways to the end of the tools row and tap Map. A full-screen Google Maps view opens with a circle around your current position.

4. Drag the map, pinch to zoom, or tap the search icon in the top right to jump to a specific city, neighborhood, or landmark. Zoom out to cover a whole district or zoom in for a few blocks.

5. When the circle sits over the area you want, tap Explore this area at the bottom. The Map Area chip appears in your prompt.

6. Type your question after the chip and send it. Examples include asking for coffee shops within walking distance, restaurants open now, or parks good for kids.

The chip keeps the geographic context for follow-up questions in the same chat. After many messages, Gemini may lose the exact circle location and ask for a street name. Start a new chat and reattach the Map Area if this happens.

## Useful Example Prompts

After attaching the Map Area, try these prompts:

- Find good coffee places within walking distance and list them from closest to farthest with walking times.
- Suggest restaurants that are open now and good for a group of four.
- What are the best parks or green spaces in this area for a morning walk?
- List bookstores or libraries inside this circle and note which ones have cafes.
- Recommend a quiet spot to work on a laptop near public transit.

You can follow up with questions about specific results, such as which places have outdoor seating or positive reviews mentioning wifi. Gemini pulls from Google Maps data, so cards include the usual details and a link to open the place in the Maps app.

## Tips for Better Results

Enable precise location in your Android settings and for the Gemini app. The initial circle centers on you, so accurate GPS helps.

Start with a reasonably sized circle. Very large areas can return generic results. Very small ones may find nothing if data is thin.

Combine the Map tool with other Gemini features. You can ask about a Map Area and then use a Skill or Connected App in the same conversation. The @ symbol now unifies access to Skills and Connectors in recent app versions.

Check the map preview pins before you leave. Tap a card for review snippets. Export promising places to a Google Maps list so you can navigate later without reopening Gemini.

If results seem off, reattach a tighter Map Area or add a landmark name to the prompt. In less-mapped regions, cross-check with the Google Maps app itself.

## Limitations to Know

The feature is mobile-only. You cannot use the Map tool on gemini.google.com.

Availability depends on your region and account. Some advanced map-related responses require activity history to be on and may be limited to certain languages or age groups.

Long conversations can drop the precise circle context. Keep related questions in one focused chat or reattach the area as needed.

Results reflect Google Maps data, which varies by location. Always confirm hours, addresses, and current conditions for time-sensitive plans.

## Conclusion

The Gemini Map tool removes the need to describe locations in text. You select the area visually, attach the Map Area chip, and get targeted suggestions with cards, pins, and export options.

Update the Gemini app, grant location permission, and try it the next time you explore a neighborhood or plan a stop. Combine it with follow-up questions and Google Maps export for the most practical results.
