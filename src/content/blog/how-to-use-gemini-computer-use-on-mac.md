Google added computer use to the Gemini app for Mac on November 19, 2026. The feature lets Gemini click, type, navigate menus and fill forms across your local files, desktop apps and websites. It runs in an isolated background window so your main screen and cursor stay free.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="752" />

You describe the task in plain language. Gemini handles the repetitive clicks that normally take dozens of actions. A live preview appears in the chat so you can watch every step, pause at any time and approve key actions before they happen.

This guide covers the exact requirements, how to turn the feature on, how to give a task and practical ways to start using it safely.

## Requirements for Gemini computer use

Computer use is not available to everyone. Google lists these conditions in the announcement footnote:<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="759" />

- Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later)
- macOS Sequoia 15.0 or newer
- Personal Google account (not work or school)
- Age 18 or older
- Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra subscription
- English language and United States region at launch

Download the Gemini app for Mac from gemini.google/mac if you do not already have it. Sign in with your eligible account.

## How to enable computer use

Open the Gemini app on your Mac. Go to Settings (or press Command + comma). Find the Computer use section.

Turn on the option that allows Gemini to work with your Mac. macOS will ask for two permissions: Accessibility and Screen & System Audio Recording. Grant both so Gemini can see and control the apps you choose.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="766" />

You decide which individual apps Gemini may access. Start with a short list. You can change the list later.

Switch to Task mode under the chat box when you are ready to hand off work. In the prompt, include the phrase @Computer use so Gemini knows to operate the Mac rather than only reply in text.

## Step-by-step: run your first computer-use task

1. Open the Gemini app for Mac and confirm you are signed in with an eligible account.
2. Enable Computer use in Settings and grant the two macOS permissions.
3. Choose the apps Gemini is allowed to control.
4. Switch the home toggle from Chat to Task.
5. Type a clear request that ends with @Computer use. Example: “Extract the shipping weights and addresses from the PDFs in my Downloads folder and fill the multi-box quote form on the carrier site. @Computer use”
6. Watch the live preview in the chat. Pause or stop if anything looks wrong.
7. Approve any sensitive actions Gemini proposes.

Gemini works inside its own background window. You can keep typing or taking calls on your main screen while it runs.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="752" />

## Practical examples Google highlights

Google’s announcement lists four starting points:<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="759" />

- Speed up creative workflows by batch color-grading photos or setting up complex 3D scenes in design software.
- Skip manual form entry by pulling weights, dimensions and addresses from local PDFs into shipping-quote forms.
- Turn live data into ready-to-present decks by pulling metrics and screenshots from dashboards into Google Slides.
- Batch-list items for sale by analyzing clothing photos, researching prices and staging listings on resale platforms.

In the official demo, a user asks Gemini to take a child’s butterfly sketch, model it and rig it in Blender. The agent works in the background while the user continues other work.

## Safety controls and tips

You stay in control. Gemini pauses for logins or payments and asks before high-impact actions. You can stop the run at any moment from the chat preview.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="752" />

Start with small, reversible tasks. Keep original files in a separate folder. Do not grant access to password managers or banking apps on the first day.

Google notes that content visible in one allowed app could influence Gemini’s behavior in another. Review the live preview before you approve the next step.

If you already use Tasks in the Gemini app for multi-step work that continues in the background, computer use extends the same idea onto the Mac desktop. See our guide on [how to use Tasks in Gemini](/blog/how-to-use-tasks-in-gemini/) for the toggle and confirmation flow that also applies here.

## What is not available yet

The feature is rolling out in English in the United States only. Intel Macs are excluded. Work and school accounts cannot use it at launch. Windows is not supported. Google has not published a timeline for wider availability.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="750" />

Free-tier users do not receive computer use. The lowest paid plan that includes it is Google AI Plus.

## Conclusion

Gemini computer use on Mac removes the need to click through the same menus dozens of times. Describe the outcome, watch the background window, and keep working on your own screen. Enable the setting, grant the two macOS permissions, switch to Task mode and add @Computer use to your prompt.

Test with a low-risk task such as extracting text from a local PDF into a form. Expand the allowed-app list only after the first runs succeed. The feature is available now to eligible Google AI subscribers on Apple Silicon Macs in the US.
