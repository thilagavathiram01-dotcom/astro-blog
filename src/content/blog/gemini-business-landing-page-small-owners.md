Small business owners already turn to Gemini for product ideas, customer replies, and performance checks. On 20 October 2026 Google added a dedicated business landing page plus marketing and finance skills that pull those tasks into one place.

The hub connects to Google Business Profile, Google Ads, Google Workspace, and third-party apps including Adobe, Canva, Dropbox, Shopify, Wix, Zoho, and Xero. Brand style memory and faster financial summaries sit alongside it. Rollout to all Gemini app users begins in the coming days.

## What the business landing page does

The page acts as a central workspace inside the Gemini app. From it you build brand assets, review customer insights, and examine numbers without jumping between separate chats or external tools.

Google states that tens of millions of small business owners already use Gemini for brainstorming, feedback management, and analysis. The new page and skills aim to return time so owners can focus on the work that started the business.

You still control which apps connect. Nothing shares data until you grant permission.

## Set your brand style once

Upload your website, brand kit, fonts, or color palettes. Gemini stores the style. When you ask for product launch images or campaign visuals, Nano Banana 2.1 generates them in that style.

You do not re-upload guidelines for every request. The result is cohesive marketing assets that match your existing look.

This pairs with existing connected apps. If you already linked Canva or Adobe, you can move the generated assets into layouts without leaving the conversation.

## Turn messy records into clear insights

Gemini converts spreadsheets, receipts, and purchase records into financial graphics and interactive models in seconds. Instead of scanning rows yourself, you receive summaries you can ask follow-up questions about.

Examples from Google include owners tracking search impressions, direction requests, and call data once Google Business Profile is connected. The same pattern applies to uploaded financial files.

Keep sensitive files local until you decide to share them. Review the generated charts before acting on the numbers.

## Connect the apps you already use

Open Gemini settings or the Customize section and add the services you need:

- Google Business Profile for review replies and performance data
- Google Ads for campaign context
- Google Workspace for docs, sheets, and mail
- Shopify, Wix, or Zoho for store or CRM data
- Canva or Adobe for design handoff
- Dropbox or Xero for files and accounting

Once linked, you can ask questions that pull from those sources. “How did search traffic look this month?” or “Draft a reply to the latest review in my brand voice” become single prompts.

For longer workflows, create reusable skills. See our guide on [creating Gemini skills for repeated tasks](/blog/create-use-gemini-skills-task-automation/).

## Step-by-step: start with the hub

1. Update the Gemini app on your phone or open gemini.google.com.
2. Look for the business landing page as it rolls out. It may appear under the main menu or as a suggested starting point.
3. Define your brand style by uploading one reference file or URL.
4. Connect one Google tool first (Business Profile or Workspace) and test a simple prompt.
5. Upload a sample spreadsheet or receipt photo and ask for a summary chart.
6. Add a third-party app only after the first tests work.
7. Save any repeated prompt as a skill so you can call it with a slash command later.

Availability starts in the coming days and expands globally. Larger teams that need admin controls and data governance can look at Gemini for business on Google Cloud instead.

## Tips for useful results

- Start with one connected app. Adding everything at once makes it harder to spot what works.
- Upload a clear brand reference. A simple PDF of colors and fonts outperforms a vague description.
- Ask for specific outputs: “three social posts in my brand voice” or “bar chart of monthly expenses.”
- Review every financial summary. Models can misread messy receipts; confirm the numbers.
- Combine with skills for weekly tasks such as review replies or inventory notes.
- Turn off connections you no longer need in settings.

If you already use Gemini skills or connected apps, the landing page extends that workflow rather than replacing it.
