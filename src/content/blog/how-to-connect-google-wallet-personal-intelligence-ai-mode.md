Google expanded Personal Intelligence in Search’s AI Mode on November 23, 2026. You can now opt in to connect Google Wallet so AI Mode reads your spending and card details for more relevant answers. Drive and the Gemini app connections follow in the coming weeks. Everything stays under your control and read-only for Wallet.

This guide shows the exact steps to connect Wallet, what you can ask, privacy notes, and how to manage the settings. For related Pixel features that also use Gemini Intelligence, see our [Pixel Tag setup guide](/blog/how-to-setup-pixel-tag-find-hub-guide/).

## What Personal Intelligence adds with Wallet

Personal Intelligence already lets AI Mode draw on Gmail, Calendar, and Photos when you opt in. Wallet joins that list starting November 23. With it connected, AI Mode can help map financial milestones, suggest ways to reduce everyday spending, and recommend which linked card maximizes rewards for categories such as groceries, dining, or travel.

Google states that the connection is read-only. AI Mode can view account information but cannot move, manage, or invest money. The same Wallet link also works inside the Gemini app. A Personalization icon will appear under tailored responses soon so you can see which data was used.

## Connect Google Wallet to AI Mode

Follow these steps from Google’s official instructions:

1. Open Google Search on the web or in the Google app.
2. Go to Search settings, then select Search Personalization (or go directly to the Connected Apps page).
3. Choose Connected Apps.
4. Find Google Wallet in the list and turn the connection on.
5. Confirm the opt-in. You can turn it off at any time from the same screen.

The feature requires a personal Google Account, Search history enabled, and Personal Recommendations turned on. Availability depends on country and language. Only users 18 and older can connect apps.

## What you can ask after connecting

Once Wallet is linked, try prompts such as:

- Which of my credit cards gives the best cash back on groceries this month?
- Show ways I can cut my monthly spending on dining and subscriptions.
- Help me plan a savings goal based on my recent spending patterns.
- Compare travel rewards across the cards in my Wallet.

AI Mode uses the data only to answer your question. Review any financial suggestion before acting on it. You can also correct context in the conversation, for example by saying you no longer track a certain expense category.

## Upcoming Drive and Gemini app connections

Google will add Google Drive support in the coming weeks. That lets AI Mode reference your Docs, Sheets, and PDFs. One example is building a tax-preparation checklist from documents already stored in Drive.

The Gemini app connection will let context flow both ways. An idea you explore in the Gemini app can continue in AI Mode, and the reverse. You already can connect Search history to the Gemini app; the new link works in the other direction.

## Privacy and control tips

- Connections are strictly opt-in. Nothing turns on by itself.
- Turn any app off instantly in Search Personalization → Connected Apps.
- Wallet access cannot transfer funds or change accounts.
- On shared devices, review what appears in answers before leaving the screen open.
- A Personalization icon will soon show the exact context used for each response.
- You can adjust or disable overall personalization in Search Settings at any time.

## Conclusion

Connecting Google Wallet to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode takes less than a minute and keeps full control in your hands. Start with Wallet today, then add Drive and Gemini when they arrive. The result is Search answers that already know the context of your spending, files, and chats without requiring you to repeat information.
