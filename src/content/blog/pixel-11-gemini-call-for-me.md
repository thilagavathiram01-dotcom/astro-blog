Waiting on hold to ask if a part is in stock is a poor use of an afternoon. On Pixel 11 phones in the United States, Gemini can place that call for you.

**Call for Me** is an early preview in the Gemini mobile app. You describe the task. Gemini dials from your own number, discloses that it is an AI assistant, works the phone tree, and talks to the person who picks up. You watch a live transcript and can take over at any time.

Google documents the feature in [Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/18336420). This guide follows that page, plus the eligibility rules reported when the preview opened on 24 September 2026.

## What Call for Me can do

Google lists three everyday jobs:

- Get business hours, product availability, service quotes, and other information.
- Book, confirm, and manage appointments and reservations.
- Navigate phone menus and wait on hold.

Help-center examples include booking a table for two at 7 PM tomorrow, and asking a dry cleaner on Main Street how much it charges to hem pants. Press coverage of the preview also cites checking stock at a hardware store and moving a haircut to next Thursday.

The call uses **your mobile network and your phone number**. Standard carrier rates apply. Gemini starts every call by saying it is an AI assistant from Google calling on a recorded line on your behalf, and it states your name.

For now, Gemini can only call **US phone numbers**.

## Check eligibility before you hunt for the button

Google’s requirements are strict. You must meet all of them:

1. Be **18 or over** and physically in the **United States**.
2. Use a **Pixel 11** phone with a **US SIM card**. 9to5Google reports the preview also covers Pixel 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL, and 11 Pro Fold.
3. Sign in to the **Gemini** mobile app.
4. Hold a **Google AI subscription** (AI Pro or Ultra).
5. Install the **Public Beta** of Phone by Google.
6. Install the **latest Gemini** app from Play Store.
7. Set the device language to **English**.

The feature is rolling out gradually. Even on a matching Pixel, the **Call for me** button may be missing until your account is included.

Join the Phone beta from Google Play’s testing page for `com.google.android.dialer`. Update Gemini from the Play Store listing for the Gemini app.

If you already use other Pixel call tools such as Hold for Me or Direct My Call, this is a different path. Those features still expect you to place the call. Call for Me starts from Gemini and handles the conversation unless you take over.

## How to start a Call for Me task

1. Open the **Gemini** app on the Pixel 11.
2. Ask it to make the call. You can type or speak. Include **@Call for me** if you want to force the tool.
3. Be specific: business name, what you want, date, time, party size, and a fallback if the first slot is taken.
4. Read Gemini’s summary. Confirm the number, your name, and any contact details it plans to share.
5. Ask Gemini to edit the plan if a detail is wrong.
6. Tap **Call for me**.

Do not skip the summary. That screen is where you decide what personal information leaves the phone.

## Watch the call and take over

Your microphone stays **muted by default**. Follow the live transcript in Gemini, or tap **Audio** to listen.

- **Cancel** before the call connects if you change your mind.
- **Take over** unmutes your microphone. Gemini announces that you are taking over, then leaves the call.
- After the call, tap the **View results** notification to read Gemini’s outcome summary in the chat.
- Open **Phone** call history to read the full transcript and listen to the recording.

Give feedback with thumbs up or thumbs down on the summary. Phone by Google may also show a survey.

## Limits you should plan around

Google sets a **daily cap** on how many calls Gemini can place. When you hit it, wait until the next day.

Gemini will not:

- Call emergency services such as 911.
- Complete payments or share credit card numbers.
- Relay Social Security numbers, passwords, or health information.
- Make telemarketing calls to sell products or services.

Businesses can opt out of receiving these automated calls at [g.co/gemini/opt-out](https://g.co/gemini/opt-out). They can opt back in on the same page.

Using the feature means you accept Google’s Terms of Service, the Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy, and the Gemini Apps Agentic Calling Additional Terms.

## Practical prompts that work better

Vague requests waste a call. Tight prompts save one of your daily slots.

**Reservation:** “Call River Birch Bistro and book a patio table for two at 7 PM tomorrow. If that time is full, try 7:30 or 8:00. Name on the reservation is Alex Chen.”

**Stock check:** “Call Harbor Hardware on Oak Street and ask if they have a 3/8-inch flare nut wrench in stock today. If yes, ask them to hold one under the name Alex Chen.”

**Quote:** “Ask the dry cleaner on Main Street how much they charge to hem a pair of dress pants, and when the work would be ready if I drop them off Saturday morning.”

**Reschedule:** “Call Northside Barber and move my haircut from Thursday 3 PM to next Thursday at the same time. If that slot is gone, take the closest afternoon opening.”

Review the number Gemini chose. Local shops often have multiple listings. A wrong listing burns a daily call and bothers the wrong desk.

## Troubleshooting

**No Call for me button.** Confirm Pixel 11, US SIM, English, paid Google AI plan, Phone Public Beta, and latest Gemini. Then wait. Google says availability is gradual.

**Call fails to connect.** Confirm the destination is a US number and that you have mobile service, not Wi-Fi-only calling quirks. Carrier rates still apply.

**Wrong business.** Cancel before connect if the summary shows the wrong listing. Edit the prompt with a street name or a number you already trust.

**You need to talk.** Tap **Take over**. Do this before Gemini shares a detail you did not approve.

**Daily limit message.** Stop for the day. Place remaining calls yourself in the Phone app.

## Should you turn it on?

Use Call for Me for short, factual shop calls: hours, stock, a table, a moved appointment. Stay on the line yourself for anything involving money, medical details, or a dispute.

The preview is US-only, English-only, and Pixel 11-only today. Treat it as a time saver with a recording, a transcript, and a hard stop on sensitive data—not as a general-purpose secretary.
