Pixel 11 can now place some business calls for you. Call for Me is an early Gemini experiment: you describe the task, review what will be said, and tap to dial from your own number.

Google opened the preview on 24 September 2026 for paid Gemini subscribers in the United States who also run the Phone by Google public beta. The feature is English-only for now and only dials US numbers.

This guide walks through eligibility, the exact prompt flow from Google’s help page, how to take over a live call, and the hard limits (no 911, no payments, no health or Social Security data).

## What Call for Me actually does

Call for Me is not Call Screen and it is not Hold for Me. Those older Pixel tools still need you to start the call. Call for Me starts the call itself from the Gemini app.

Google says Gemini can:

- Check hours, stock, and service quotes
- Book, confirm, or move appointments and reservations
- Work through automated menus and wait on hold

Examples from Google’s Pixel community post include checking a hardware part, reserving patio seating, or moving a haircut to next Thursday.

Gemini uses your mobile network and your phone number. Carrier rates apply. At the start of every call it discloses that it is a Google AI assistant calling on a recorded line on your behalf, and it states your name.

If you already use Gemini for multi-step app tasks, this is the same idea pointed at the phone network. See our [Gemini Intelligence multi-step tasks guide](/blog/gemini-intelligence-multi-step-tasks/) for the in-app counterpart.

## Who can use it right now

Google’s Gemini Apps Help page lists every gate. You must meet all of them:

- Age 18 or over, physically in the US
- A Pixel 11 with a US SIM (coverage reports also include 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL, and 11 Pro Fold)
- Signed into the Gemini mobile app
- An active Google AI subscription (AI Plus, Pro, or Ultra)
- Phone by Google **Public Beta** from the Play testing page
- Latest Gemini app from Play Store
- Device language set to English

The preview is rolling out over about two weeks. If the @Call for me chip is missing, wait for the staged enablement. Do not sideload a random APK.

Join the Phone beta here: [play.google.com/apps/testing/com.google.android.dialer](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.google.android.dialer).

## Step-by-step: place a Call for Me request

Google’s official flow is short. Be specific before you tap dial.

1. Open the **Gemini** app on the Pixel 11.
2. Type or speak the task. You can add `@Call for me` in the prompt.
3. Include dates, times, party size, backup times, and the exact venue name.
4. Read Gemini’s summary: number it will call, your name, and any contact details it plans to share.
5. Ask it to edit the brief if a detail is wrong.
6. Tap **Call for me** to start the call.

Official example prompts from support:

- “Call [Restaurant Name] and book a table for two at 7 PM tomorrow.”
- “Ask the dry cleaner on Main Street how much they charge to hem pants.”

After the call, you get a notification. Tap **View results** for Gemini’s outcome summary. The Phone app keeps the full transcript and recording in call history.

## Monitor the line and take over

Your microphone stays muted by default. Watch the live transcript in Gemini, or tap **Audio** to hear the other party.

- **Cancel** before the call connects if you change your mind.
- **Take over** unmutes you. Gemini announces the handoff and leaves the call.
- Daily call caps apply. If you hit the limit, wait until the next day.

Give feedback with thumbs up or down on the summary, or complete the occasional survey in Phone by Google.

Businesses that do not want these automated calls can send people to [g.co/gemini/opt-out](https://g.co/gemini/opt-out). The same page lets you opt back in.

## What Gemini will not do on the phone

Google lists blocked categories in help. Do not try to force them:

- Emergency numbers such as 911
- Payments, card numbers, or other financial transactions
- Social Security numbers, passwords, or health details
- Telemarketing or selling products for you

Calls stay inside the US numbering plan during this preview. Gemini also will not invent a business number. If the listing is wrong, correct it in the pre-call summary.

By using the feature you accept Google’s Terms of Service, the Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy, and the Gemini Apps Agentic Calling Additional Terms.

## Tips that keep the preview useful

Write the outcome, not the script. “Move my 3 p.m. cut at Northside Barbers to Thursday after 5” beats “call the barber.”

Give one fallback. “If 7 is full, take 7:30 or Friday.” Gemini then has a second ask instead of ending the call empty-handed.

Stay nearby for the first few tries. Take over if the staff asks a question your brief did not cover.

Treat the recording as real customer audio. Google tells shops that these are real transactions from real customers, not a demo line.

Pair this with other Pixel 11 Gemini tools when you are away from the desk. The [Gemini Find Hub remembered items walkthrough](/blog/find-hub-remembered-items-gemini/) covers the “where did I put that?” side of the same assistant.

## Conclusion

Call for Me is a narrow, paid, US-only preview on Pixel 11. The value is simple: Gemini dials, identifies itself, works the menu, and talks, while you watch a transcript and can grab the handset at any second.

Join the Phone by Google public beta, keep Gemini updated, and start with a low-stakes quote or hours check before you trust it with a Saturday reservation. Read the pre-call summary every time. That one screen is where you decide what leaves your phone.
