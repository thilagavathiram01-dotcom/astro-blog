YouTube search just got smarter on your phone. Ask YouTube lets you type or speak natural questions instead of keywords and get structured answers that mix text summaries with relevant videos and Shorts.

The feature rolled out to mobile devices for US users searching in English on October 12, 2026, after earlier availability on web and TV. It works for signed-in viewers aged 13 and older.



![Person holding a smartphone while watching video content](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-2WRks8M4eIc?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)



## What Ask YouTube Does on Mobile

Ask YouTube draws from YouTube videos, Shorts, and real-time web information to answer complex questions. Responses include text explanations alongside timestamped video clips that start at the most relevant moment.

You can follow up in the same conversation to refine results. Creators benefit because views from featured videos and clips count toward normal metrics and YouTube Partner Program eligibility.

Standard search remains available. You can switch back anytime.

## How to Access Ask YouTube on Your Phone

The feature is gradually rolling out. Update the YouTube app on Android or iOS and sign in with a US account searching in English.

1. Open the YouTube app.
2. Tap the Search icon (magnifying glass) at the top.
3. Tap **Ask**.
4. Type a natural-language question, such as "plan a 3-day road trip from San Francisco to Santa Barbara."
5. Review the response. Read the text summary and tap video clips to jump to the relevant timestamp. Clips show the channel name and video title.
6. Select a suggested follow-up or type a new question in the **Ask a follow-up** field at the bottom.

Voice input works where available on your device. On TVs you can use the remote microphone, but mobile focuses on the in-app search flow.

## Tips for Better Ask YouTube Results

Ask detailed questions. Include context like budget, time available, skill level, or preferred style. For example, "What are beginner steps to teach a child to ride a bike without training wheels?" works better than "bike riding."

Specify tone or format in your prompt if you want bullet points, a short summary, or a step-by-step guide. Follow up to adjust.

Cross-check important facts across multiple sources. AI responses are informational only and do not replace professional advice. Accuracy can vary.

You can adjust answers by asking for different opinions or more detail. Human reviewers may see anonymized conversations to improve the tool, so avoid sharing sensitive personal information.

## Limitations and Privacy Notes

Ask YouTube is not available on supervised kid accounts or in the YouTube Kids app. Playlists and Premium-exclusive content do not appear in responses.

Responses may take longer to generate than standard keyword search because the system analyzes multiple sources. YouTube is working to improve speed.

Conversations are stored in My Activity. You can review or delete them there or from search history. Deleting from history removes the latest conversation on a topic; use My Activity for complete removal.

Feedback helps: thumbs up or down at the bottom of a response, with optional details. Report legal issues through the thumbs-down menu.

## Related Features and Next Steps

Ask YouTube on the watch page (the Ask button under videos) continues to expand, including upcoming support for Shorts and live streams. It lets you ask questions about the specific video you are watching.

If you create content, clear chapters, descriptive titles, and high-quality videos improve the chance of appearing in Ask YouTube results. The ranking prioritizes relevance, engagement, and quality, similar to regular search.
