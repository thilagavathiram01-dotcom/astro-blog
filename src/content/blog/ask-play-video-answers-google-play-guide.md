Google Play Store version 53.8, released on October 26, 2026, adds relevant videos with citations and timestamps to Ask Play responses. You can now jump straight to helpful clips when researching apps or games instead of reading only text descriptions and reviews.

This update arrives through the normal Play Store update process. It builds on earlier Ask Play features that already let you compare apps side by side and get review summaries. The new video layer gives you another way to evaluate software before you install it.

## What Ask Play Video Answers Include

Ask Play is Google's conversational tool inside the Play Store. You type a natural-language question and receive app or game recommendations along with supporting details. With the October 26 release, those answers can now include video results.

Each video entry shows a citation and a timestamp. You can tap the video to open it at the relevant moment rather than watching from the start. Google has not published details on how it selects or ranks these videos. The release notes simply state that you can find relevant videos with citations and timestamps in Ask Play responses.

The feature appears on phones. Availability can vary by account, region, language, and device because Google rolls Play Store updates out gradually. Check that your Play Store app is on version 53.8 or later.

## How to Access Ask Play Video Answers

Follow these steps to try the new video responses.

1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android phone.
2. Make sure the store is updated. Tap your profile picture, then Settings, then About. Confirm the version is 53.8 or higher. If an update is available, install it and restart the app.
3. Tap the search bar at the bottom of the screen.
4. Type a question in plain language. Examples include "What are good offline puzzle games?" or "Which budgeting apps work without a subscription?"
5. Review the Ask Play response that appears. Look for a Video results section or individual video cards that include a play icon, length, and timestamp.
6. Tap a video to open it. The player should start near the cited timestamp so you can see the relevant part immediately.
7. Use the app suggestions listed below the videos if you want to install something. You can also ask a follow-up question in the same conversation.

If you do not see videos, try a different query that is more likely to have tutorial or review content. Some answers may still be text-only while the feature finishes rolling out.

## Tips for Better Results

Ask specific questions rather than single keywords. "Show me free photo editors that support RAW files" tends to produce more useful video links than a broad search for "photo apps."

Check the timestamp before you invest time. A two-minute video that starts at 1:15 may answer your question faster than a longer review.

Cross-check video claims against the app's official listing and recent reviews. Videos can become outdated if the developer changes features after the clip was recorded.

Combine video answers with the side-by-side comparison tool that Ask Play already offers. You can ask for a comparison and then request videos that illustrate the differences. See our earlier guide on [comparing apps with Ask Play](/blog/ask-play-google-play-store/) for the text-based workflow.

Keep your Play Store app updated so you receive later improvements to video ranking or citation quality.

## Other Changes in the October 26 Play Store Update

The same Play Store version 53.8 release also adds Playbacks. This Sidekick feature captures the previous 30 seconds of gameplay retroactively and stores the clip locally. You can save a moment after it happens instead of starting a recording in advance.

Play Shorts can now be shared via a link. Recipients can view the short on Android devices, other platforms, or the web. This removes the requirement that the viewer must open the content on an Android phone.

Google Play services version 26.42, released the same day, includes smaller account and safety improvements. None of those changes affect the Ask Play video experience directly.

## Conclusion

Video answers in Ask Play give you a faster way to evaluate apps and games. Open the Play Store, update if needed, and ask a clear question. Look for the video results that include citations and timestamps, then jump to the useful section.

The feature is still rolling out, so results can differ by device and region. Official details come from the Google System Release Notes for Play Store v53.8 dated October 26, 2026.
