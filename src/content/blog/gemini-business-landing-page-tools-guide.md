Small business owners already use Gemini for product ideas, customer feedback, and performance analysis. The new business landing page announced on October 20, 2026, brings those tasks into one central hub inside the Gemini app.

From this page you can define your brand style, generate on-brand visuals with Nano Banana 2.1, connect everyday tools, and turn receipts or spreadsheets into clear financial graphics. The features are rolling out globally to all Gemini app users in the coming days.

This guide walks through how to access and use the new tools once they appear in your account.

## Access the business landing page

Open the Gemini app on your phone or at gemini.google.com. Look for a dedicated business section or landing page in the main navigation or suggested tools area. Google describes it as a central hub where owners can build their brand, analyze finances, and understand customer insights.

If the page is not visible yet, check again after a few days. The rollout is gradual. You do not need a paid plan to start; the tools are available to all Gemini app users.

## Define your brand style

On the business landing page, upload your website, brand kit, fonts, or color palettes. Gemini stores this style information. When you generate images with Nano Banana 2.1, the model uses the saved style so new product photos, campaign graphics, and marketing assets stay consistent without re-uploading guidelines each time.

Start with a clear brand kit or a link to your existing site. Test a simple prompt such as “create a social media graphic for our new product launch” and check that colors, fonts, and overall look match your brand. Adjust the uploaded materials if the results drift.

## Connect Google and third-party apps

The landing page makes it straightforward to link Gemini to tools you already use. Supported Google services include Google Business Profile, Google Ads, and Google Workspace. Third-party connected apps include Adobe, Canva, Dropbox, Shopify, Wix, Zoho, and Xero.

Follow the connection prompts on the page. Once linked, you can ask Gemini to pull data from those apps or push content into them. For example, after connecting Google Business Profile you can request summaries of recent reviews or performance metrics. With Shopify or Wix you can generate product descriptions or page copy that matches your store.

Keep connections limited to the apps you actively use so the hub stays focused.

## Generate financial insights

Upload messy spreadsheets, receipts, or purchase records. Gemini converts them into clear financial graphics and interactive models in seconds. Instead of manually sorting numbers, you receive visual summaries that highlight trends, totals, or comparisons.

Prepare a clean export from your accounting software or a scanned receipt. Ask for a specific view, such as “show monthly expenses by category” or “create an interactive model of cash flow for the next quarter.” Review the output and refine the prompt if any figures need correction.

## Practical tips from real owners

Google shared examples of owners already saving time with Gemini. A ceramicist in Bordeaux uses it for product descriptions, newsletters, and social hooks, reclaiming two to three hours each day. A farmer in Maharashtra treated Gemini as a virtual agronomist for soil evaluation and budgeting. A restaurant owner in New Jersey tracks local search performance and drafts updates between shifts.

Apply the same approach: use the business page for repetitive content and analysis tasks so you can spend more time on your core work. Start with one or two high-volume activities such as review responses or campaign visuals, then expand.

## Conclusion

The new Gemini business landing page puts brand building, connected apps, and financial insight tools in one place. Once it appears in your app, upload your style guidelines, link the services you already use, and test a few image and data prompts. The features are rolling out now for all Gemini app users.

Larger teams that need centralized controls can explore Gemini Enterprise separately. For most small business owners, the new hub is designed to reduce time spent on routine tasks.
