Android Go users with 2GB of RAM or more can now hand off multi-step jobs to Gemini Tasks for free. The feature, powered by the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model, runs in the background so you can keep using your phone.

This guide shows eligibility, setup, practical examples, and the limits that apply on lower-memory devices. If you already use the Gemini app on Android Go, start with Task mode today.

## Who can use free Gemini Tasks on Android Go

Tasks in Gemini became free on 17 November 2026 for adults in the United States, India, Canada, Australia, Japan, and South Korea. You need a personal Google Account, History turned on, and you must be 18 or older. Work and school accounts are not supported.

On Android Go, the Gemini app itself is available on devices with at least 2GB of RAM. Google re-architected the app so core features run on lower-memory hardware. Tasks use the same app, so eligible Android Go phones in the six countries can access Task mode without a paid plan.

Advanced options such as business calls, Google Pay checkout, text channels, and email forwarding remain US-only for now. Most users in India and the other free countries get the core Task experience: research, scheduling, file work, and recurring jobs.

## How to set up Gemini Tasks on a lower-memory phone

Confirm your phone meets the requirements before you start.

1. Check Settings > About phone for Android (Go edition) and at least 2GB of RAM.
2. Sign in with a personal Google Account and make sure History is on in Gemini settings.
3. Update the Google Search app or install the Gemini app from the Play Store if it is not already present. On many Android Go devices you can also press and hold the Home or Power button to open Gemini.
4. Open the Gemini app. Look above the text box for the Chat / Task toggle. Tap Task.
5. Describe the job in clear language. Include timing if needed, for example “every weekday at 8 a.m.” or “after I receive the email.”
6. Tap Submit. Gemini creates a dedicated task thread and works in the background.

You can add files with the Upload & tools button or reference skills with the @ symbol. Monitor the thread; Gemini pauses and asks for confirmation before high-impact steps such as purchases or sending messages.

## Features that work well on 2GB devices

Because the heavy model work happens in the cloud, Task mode places little extra load on the phone itself. You get the same background execution available on higher-end devices.

Supported actions include:

- Research across the web and connected Google apps (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Photos).
- Creating or updating calendar events and reminders.
- Generating documents, summaries, or images with Nano Banana.
- Recurring schedules for ongoing chores such as checking new emails or updating a family calendar.
- Gemini Live voice conversations in more than 45 languages.
- Camera help for notes, forms, or translation.

On Android Go the following are not available: the “Hey Google” wake word and “Ask about this screen.” Hold the Home or Power button instead, or open the app directly.

Personal Intelligence and Connected Apps require separate opt-in. Review the permissions Gemini requests before you allow access to Gmail or Calendar.

## Practical task examples for budget phones

Here are prompts that stay light on the device while delivering useful results.

- “Compare the three cheapest 10,000 mAh power banks on Google Shopping and summarise the best value options under ₹1,500.”
- “Scan my recent emails for school or work deadlines this week and add them to my Calendar with reminders.”
- “Every Monday at 9 a.m., list unused subscriptions from my emails and suggest which ones to cancel.”
- “Photograph this form and walk me through the required fields.” (uses camera)
- “Create a simple flyer image for a weekend market stall using Nano Banana.”

Start with one short task. Check the thread after a few minutes. If Gemini needs more detail, reply in the same thread.

## Tips for smooth performance on lower-memory devices

Keep the phone responsive while Tasks run in the background.

- Close unused apps and clear recent tasks before starting a long job.
- Free storage space; Android Go devices often have limited internal storage.
- Use Wi-Fi when possible for large file uploads or image generation.
- Prefer short, specific prompts. Vague requests lead to more back-and-forth and longer cloud sessions.
- Review privacy settings. You can disconnect apps or delete history at any time in Gemini Settings.
- If the Task toggle is missing, wait for the gradual rollout or check that your account and country are eligible.

For the basic Gemini app experience on the same devices, see our earlier guide to [using Gemini on Android Go lower-memory phones](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-on-android-go-lower-memory-devices/).

## Limits and what to expect

Free Task mode covers core personal assistance in the six countries. Business calling, Google Pay purchases (with approval and spending caps), texting Gemini, and email forwarding to gemini@google.com are currently limited to eligible US accounts. Calling is further restricted in five US states.

Gemini works inside a hardware-isolated virtual machine with multiple safety layers. It asks for confirmation before purchases or other high-impact actions. Provide feedback with a thumbs-down if a task misses the mark; Google uses this to improve results.

Availability rolls out gradually. Some accounts in eligible countries may see the toggle later than others.

## Conclusion

Gemini Tasks turns a 2GB Android Go phone into a capable personal assistant without a subscription in six countries. Open the app, switch to Task, and hand off research, scheduling, or recurring chores. Stay in control with confirmations and privacy settings.

Try one practical request today and note the time you save. For the latest eligibility, check the official Gemini help pages.
