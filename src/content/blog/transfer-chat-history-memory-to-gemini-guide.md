Switching AI assistants no longer means starting from a blank page. Google lets you bring both your preferences and full conversation history into Gemini so responses stay personal and past work remains searchable.

This guide covers the official process for personal Google Accounts. You can import a memory summary and upload ZIP exports of chats from providers such as ChatGPT and Claude.

![Person holding a smartphone showing a chat interface](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1788835363012-bcac6ca7a8f7?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What You Can Transfer

Gemini supports two distinct imports:

- **Memory / preferences**: A text summary of your style, instructions, relationships, and key facts. You generate this by pasting Gemini’s prompt into your previous AI, then paste the reply back.
- **Chat history**: Complete conversation threads exported as a ZIP file. Gemini processes the archive so you can search and continue those threads.

Both features require a personal Google Account and that you are 18 or older. They are not available on work, school, or supervised accounts, and they are currently unavailable in the EEA, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom.

## Step-by-Step: Import Memory (Preferences)

1. Open Gemini at gemini.google.com (desktop works best for the full import page).
2. Click Settings (gear icon) and select **Import memory to Gemini**.
3. Copy the ready-made prompt Google provides.
4. Paste that prompt into a new chat in your previous AI app (ChatGPT, Claude, or another).
5. Wait for the summary, then copy the entire response.
6. Return to Gemini, paste the summary into the designated box, and click **Add memory**.

Gemini stores the details and uses them for more relevant answers in future chats. You can review or edit what it remembers later in Personal Intelligence settings.

## Step-by-Step: Import Full Chat History

### Export from ChatGPT

1. Open ChatGPT and click your profile icon (bottom left).
2. Go to **Settings → Data controls**.
3. Next to Export data, click **Export** and confirm.
4. Check the email linked to your ChatGPT account for the download link (it can take time).
5. Download the ZIP file.

### Export from Claude

1. In Claude, click your profile.
2. Open **Settings → Privacy**.
3. Next to Export data, click **Export**.
4. Choose the date range (all chats or a limited period) and confirm.
5. Download the ZIP from the email you receive.

### Upload to Gemini

1. In Gemini, return to **Settings → Import memory to Gemini**.
2. Under **Import chats**, click **Add**.
3. Select your ZIP file (maximum 5 GB per file; up to five ZIPs per day).
4. Wait for processing—this can take up to a day depending on size.

Imported chats appear in your sidebar with a special icon. You can search them, open threads, and continue conversations. To remove an entire import, locate the entry in settings and delete it.

## Tips for a Smooth Transfer

- Enable Memory in Gemini settings if the import options do not appear.
- Keep ZIP files unmodified—do not extract and re-zip contents.
- Start with the memory summary if you mainly want style and preferences; add full history only when you need specific past threads.
- After import, test by asking Gemini about a detail from an old chat to confirm it can reference the history.
- For large exports, allow processing time and avoid uploading multiple large files at once.

You can also link this workflow with other Gemini capabilities. For example, once history is imported, try multi-step work with the [Gemini 4 Hydrogen guide](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-4-hydrogen-guide/) to run longer tasks that build on your prior context.

## Limits and Privacy Notes

Gemini processes the uploaded data securely on Google’s systems. The 5 GB limit and daily upload cap exist to keep processing reliable. Imported content remains under your control—you can delete individual chats or entire imports at any time.

Work and education accounts cannot use these tools. Always review what you import, especially if past chats contain sensitive information.

## Conclusion

Transferring chat history and memory to Gemini takes only a few minutes of setup once your previous provider sends the export. The memory summary gives Gemini immediate context, while the ZIP upload preserves searchable threads so you can resume work without rewriting prompts or re-explaining preferences.

Follow the official steps above, start with preferences if you are unsure, and verify results in a new chat. You will keep continuity across AI tools without losing the context you have already built.
