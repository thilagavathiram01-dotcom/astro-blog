Pixel 10 owners waited months for the full Gemini Intelligence experience that launched with the Pixel 11 series. The November 2026 Pixel Drop, which started rolling out on November 10, finally delivers Rambler, multi-step task automation, proactive suggestions, and Create My Widget to the Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="5" />

These features help your phone handle logistics while you stay focused. Task automation now reaches the UK, India, and Japan alongside the US and other markets. The update pairs with Android 17 QPR2 and continues over the next several weeks.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="2" />

## What the November Pixel Drop Adds for Pixel 10

Gemini Intelligence brings the strongest Gemini capabilities to select Android devices. On Pixel 10 series phones it adds:

- **Rambler** in Gboard: speaks naturally and receives clean, formatted text without filler words like “um.”
- **Task automation**: Gemini orders food, books rides, places grocery orders, or reserves tables across supported apps.
- **Proactive suggestions**: context-aware actions such as sharing an ETA or adding items to a Google Keep list.
- **Create My Widget**: describe a custom home-screen widget in plain language; it can pull data from Gmail, Calendar, and Search.

Device Assistance also arrives for natural-language help with settings and troubleshooting on supported devices running Android 17 or later. Availability depends on region, language, and account age (18+). Some features require a Gemini subscription for higher usage.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="29" />

## Check and Install the November Pixel Drop

Updates roll out in stages. Confirm your device first.

1. Open **Settings > System > System update**.
2. Tap **Check for update**. Install any available package labeled as the November Feature Drop or Android 17 QPR2.
3. Restart when prompted. The process can take 15–30 minutes depending on connection.
4. After reboot, open the Gemini app and confirm you are signed in with a Google account.

Pixel 10a is excluded from Gemini Intelligence features in this drop. If the update does not appear, wait 24–48 hours or check the community forum linked in Google’s Pixel Drop post for known issues.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="40" />

## Enable and Use Rambler for Clean Voice Typing

Rambler cleans dictation in real time on Gboard.

1. Open any app that uses the keyboard (Messages, Gmail, Docs).
2. Switch to Gboard if it is not already active.
3. Tap the microphone icon or long-press the space bar to start voice typing.
4. Speak naturally. Rambler removes fillers, corrects mid-sentence changes, and formats the result.
5. Review the cleaned text, then send or insert it.

The feature works on Pixel 10 series devices in supported languages and countries. Usage limits may apply. For more background on how Rambler works on newer hardware, see our earlier guide to [Rambler on Pixel 11](/blog/how-to-use-rambler-voice-typing-on-pixel-11/).

## Automate Multi-Step Tasks with Gemini

Task automation lets Gemini complete actions across apps after you confirm the plan.

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Type or speak a request such as “Order my usual coffee from the nearby café” or “Book a ride to the airport at 3 pm.”
3. Review the steps Gemini proposes. Approve the plan.
4. Gemini executes the actions in supported apps (food delivery, rideshare, reservations, and others).
5. You can monitor or cancel the task while it runs.

The capability expands to the UK, India, and Japan with this update. It remains limited to users 18+ and select languages. Always review the proposed actions before confirming, especially for payments or bookings. Pair this with our guide on [automating Gemini tasks from the power button](/blog/automate-gemini-tasks-android-power-button/) for additional shortcuts.

## Build Custom Widgets with Create My Widget

Create My Widget generates Material-style home-screen widgets from a text description. It rolls out over the next few weeks on Pixel 10 and 11 series phones (except Pixel 10a) in English for Australia, Canada, India, the United States, and the United Kingdom.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="50" />

1. Long-press an empty area on your home screen.
2. Tap **Widgets**.
3. Look for the **Create** button or search for Create My Widget.
4. Choose a category or select “Describe your widget.”
5. Enter a prompt such as “Monthly spending tracker from Gmail receipts” or “Countdown to my flight next Friday with airline details.”
6. Review the generated options. Edit, regenerate, or add the widget to your home screen.
7. Grant any requested permissions for Gmail, Calendar, or Search so the widget can stay current.

Widgets can show trip dashboards, race-day scores, package deliveries, or daily briefs. You can edit them later by long-pressing the widget. The same feature is coming to Pixel Watch 4 and 5 soon.

## Tips for Best Results

- Keep the Gemini app and Gboard updated from the Play Store.
- Grant only the permissions you need for widgets and automation.
- Test Rambler in Messages first to confirm language support.
- For automation, start with low-stakes requests before bookings that involve money.
- Check Google’s support page for Gemini Intelligence if a feature does not appear after the update.
- Device Assistance works best when you ask specific questions such as “Clear storage space” or “Which apps can access my location?”

Proactive suggestions and conversation summaries also arrive in more languages (German, French, Spanish, Italian, Hindi, Traditional Chinese). These help surface relevant actions without opening the full app.

## Conclusion

The November 2026 Pixel Drop brings Pixel 10 series phones closer to the Pixel 11 experience with Gemini Intelligence. Rambler cleans voice input, task automation handles logistics in more countries, and Create My Widget lets you design home-screen tools with a sentence. Install the update, open Gemini, and try one feature today. Availability continues to expand over the coming weeks, so check again if something is missing on day one.
