Gemini Intelligence is Google’s label for phones that meet the hardware bar for its strongest on-device Gemini features. Android Help currently lists the Pixel 11, 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL, and 11 Pro Fold, plus Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold8, Fold8 Ultra, and Flip8.

The badge is not a separate app. It means the handset can run multi-step app tasks, proactive screen suggestions, Rambler voice cleanup, sign-to-text, writing tools, and a pre-installed Gemini Notebook. Features still vary by country, language, and device type.

This guide follows Android Help and Google’s Gemini Intelligence product post. It covers how to confirm you are on a listed phone, connect Personal Intelligence, enable Proactive Assistance, and start one bounded task.

## Confirm you have a listed phone

Google’s help page names these models as Gemini Intelligence phones:

- Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, Pixel 11 Pro Fold
- Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8, Z Fold8 Ultra, Z Flip8

Hardware requirements listed on the same page: 12GB or more of RAM, a qualified system-on-chip, advanced on-device AI processing, current media processing, and software performance benchmarks Google does not publish as a public scoreboard.

A Pixel 10 or Galaxy S26 can still run many Gemini app features. It is not on the current Intelligence device list. Do not assume every Gemini overlay tool equals Intelligence.

Update the phone, the Gemini app, and Gboard from Play Store before you hunt for settings. Individual features can sit behind usage limits.

## Connect Personal Intelligence

Personal Intelligence lets Gemini use context from Google apps you choose, such as Gmail, Photos, or Keep. Android Help says certain Gemini features need this connection, plus Gemini as the default assistant.

On a new or factory-reset Pixel 11, the setup wizard shows **Supercharge Gemini with Personal Intelligence**:

1. Finish the basic device setup screens.
2. On the Personal Intelligence page, tap **Connect all**, **Choose apps**, or **Don’t connect**.
3. Complete any extra permission prompts for the apps you picked.

On a phone that is already set up, use Gemini’s connected-apps flow instead of waiting for a reset. Google documents the same account-level connection at [Connect your apps to Gemini](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598406).

Connect only apps you are willing to let Gemini read. You can change the list later. Do not connect a work account if your admin forbids it.

## Turn on Proactive Assistance

Proactive Assistance is the screen-aware layer. Gemini can use what is on the display and other on-device signals to surface a reservation code, travel detail, or similar hint when it is useful.

During first setup, Android Help describes a page titled **Gemini can help with what’s on your screen**:

1. Tap **Turn on**.
2. Choose which on-device sources to allow, including messages and screenshots.
3. Finish the remaining setup cards.

If you skipped that card, open Pixel’s Proactive Assistance help path later: [manage Proactive Assistance](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/17579905). Leave Gemini as the default digital assistant or the overlay will not see the same screen context.

A suggestion is not a completed booking. Multi-step tasks still wait for your final confirmation.

## Run one multi-step task in an app

Google spent early testing on food and rideshare apps on Galaxy S26 and Pixel 10. The Intelligence help page now describes the same class of work on listed phones: groceries, reservations, and online shopping across select Android apps.

A practical first test:

1. Open a notes list or email that names items or a reservation time.
2. Long-press the power button to open Gemini over that screen, or start Gemini Live if you prefer voice.
3. Give one job: “Build a delivery cart from this grocery list” or “Find a table for two at 7pm using this restaurant name.”
4. Watch the notification trail while Gemini works in the background.
5. Confirm or cancel the last step yourself.

Google’s May 2026 product post is explicit: Gemini acts on your command and stops when the task ends. You still own the tap that pays or books.

Do not paste card numbers, government IDs, or medical records into the prompt. Use a screenshot only if it contains no secrets.

## Other Intelligence tools on the same phone

Android Help groups several tools under the same badge. They are separate switches.

**Rambler** in Gboard turns messy speech into punctuated text and drops filler words. Google says audio is used to transcribe in real time and is not stored. Details live in [Gboard’s Rambler help article](https://support.google.com/gboard/answer/17468539).

**Writing tools** in Gboard draft or rewrite text in other apps and can follow your writing style. See [writing tools help](https://support.google.com/gboard/answer/17470061).

**Sign-to-text** translates American Sign Language from the front camera into English text in Gboard and Live Transcribe on Pixel 11. Setup steps are in [How to Use Pixel 11 Sign-to-Text for ASL Messages](/blog/pixel-11-sign-to-text-asl/).

**Gemini Notebook** is pre-installed on Intelligence phones. It stays grounded in sources you add. Use it for research notes, not as a replacement for the Gemini overlay.

**Create My Widget** lets you describe a home-screen widget in natural language. Google’s examples include a weekly meal-prep board and a weather tile that only shows wind and rain.

## Privacy controls that actually matter

Google published a separate security and privacy explainer for Gemini Intelligence. The help page adds the practical rule: manage features one by one in device settings, plus app permissions and location.

Do this after setup:

1. Settings → Apps → Gemini → Permissions. Revoke anything you did not mean to grant.
2. Review Personal Intelligence connected apps and drop the ones you never use.
3. Review Proactive Assistance sources. Turn off screenshots if you capture banking apps.
4. Keep Gemini as default assistant only if you want overlay and power-button access.

Chrome Auto Browse is a different product. It lives in the browser and is not the same as app automation on the phone. Use [Gemini in Chrome on Android](/blog/gemini-in-chrome-android/) when the job is a website, not an installed app.

The September Android Drop tools (Find Hub remembered items, Motion Assist, Guided vision) work on a wider set of Android versions. They do not require the Intelligence badge. Pair them from [How to Use Android’s September 2026 Drop](/blog/android-september-2026-drop-guide/) if your Pixel 11 already has that update.

## If a feature is missing

Country and language gates are real. Android Help repeats that features vary by country, language, and device type.

- Update system software and Gemini.
- Confirm you are signed into the Google account you used during setup.
- Set Gemini as the default digital assistant.
- Retry Personal Intelligence and Proactive Assistance after a reboot.
- Check usage limits if a tool worked yesterday and now refuses.

A missing Create My Widget tile or Rambler toggle does not mean the phone lost the Intelligence label. Those flags still roll out in waves.

## Conclusion

Gemini Intelligence on Pixel 11 is a hardware eligibility mark plus a set of opt-in switches. Confirm the model, connect only the Google apps you trust, turn on Proactive Assistance if you want screen-aware chips, and run one multi-step task you can cancel.

Leave the final confirmation to yourself. That is the control model Google documents, and it is the part worth keeping.
