Google rolled out the Gemini app on Apple CarPlay on November 11, 2026. iPhone users can now start or resume Gemini Live voice conversations from the car’s screen. The feature works across every supported Gemini Live language and is available to all Gemini users on iOS.

It follows the earlier rollout of Gemini on Android Auto and cars with Google built-in. Conversations sync back to the Gemini app on your iPhone after you park. Setup requires a few steps on the phone first.

This guide shows how to prepare your iPhone, connect to CarPlay, and use the features while keeping attention on the road.

## What you need before starting

Update the Gemini app on your iPhone to the latest version from the App Store. Grant microphone permission when prompted, or enable it later in Settings under Gemini. Open the Gemini app and start at least one Gemini Live conversation on the phone. Google lists this step as required before the CarPlay version works.

Connect Gmail and Google Calendar in the Gemini app settings if you want daily briefs or email summaries. Some personalization features, including Daily Brief, are unavailable to users under 18 or in the European Economic Area and Nigeria. Enable Daily Brief on the phone first if it is available in your region.

Complete these steps while parked. Permissions and the first Live session happen on the iPhone.

## Connect and launch Gemini on CarPlay

Plug in your iPhone with a cable or connect wirelessly to CarPlay as you normally do. The Gemini icon should appear on the CarPlay home screen or side rail. If it does not show yet, wait for the staged global rollout and confirm the app is updated.

Tap the Gemini icon. You can resume a recent chat or start a fresh conversation. The screen shows a waveform that indicates when Gemini is listening, thinking, or responding. End and Mute buttons sit in the top area.

You can interrupt Gemini mid-response to add a detail, change the subject, or pause while you focus on an exit. The interface is designed to minimize distraction.

## Use it for daily tasks while driving

Ask about your calendar to prepare for the day. Try: “What does my day look like?” or “Help me prep for my 10am with the design team.”

Catch up on email with connected Workspace apps. Say: “Give me my daily brief for today,” or “Catch me up on my unread emails and summarize Sarah’s Q4 budget thread.”

Request local recommendations: “What’s a quick lunch spot in downtown Austin that’s easy to get in and out of?”

Use the quiet cabin for brainstorming or rehearsal: “Let’s roleplay my Q4 pitch — act as a skeptical VP and challenge my strategy.”

Conversations appear in the Gemini chat history on your iPhone after you park. You can continue them later.

## What Gemini cannot do on CarPlay

Gemini on CarPlay handles voice conversations. It does not place calls, send messages, or start navigation the way Siri or Gemini on Android Auto can. Use the maps app or Siri for directions and phone controls. The feature prioritizes free-flowing discussion over vehicle commands.

Keep your eyes on the road and hands on the wheel. Set up and test while parked.

## Tips for better results

Speak in full sentences and interrupt when you need to clarify. Connect only the Google apps you need. Check that Daily Brief is enabled if you want the morning summary.

If the icon is missing after an update, restart the phone and reconnect to CarPlay. The rollout reaches accounts over several days.

## Conclusion

Gemini Live is now available on Apple CarPlay for hands-free conversations during drives. Update the app, enable the microphone, run Live once on the phone, then tap the icon on your car’s screen. Use it for calendar prep, email summaries, local suggestions, and brainstorming. Chats sync to your iPhone.

Official details are on the Google Blog post from November 11, 2026. Availability can vary by region and account during the rollout.
