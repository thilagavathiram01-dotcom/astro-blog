Gemini in Chrome on Android now lets eligible users hand off multi-step web tasks to an AI agent. Auto Browse handles things like booking parking, filling forms, or comparing products while you stay in control.

Google expanded the feature to Android users in India on October 7, 2026, after earlier rollouts in the United States. It works on devices running Android 12 or higher with the language set to English. You need a Google AI Pro or AI Ultra subscription, and you must be 18 or older.

![Person holding smartphone with Chrome open](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1511707171634-5f897ff02aa9?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Requirements Before You Start

Confirm these items so the feature appears and works:

- Latest version of Chrome, signed in with a personal Google account.
- Device language set to English (US or UK initially).
- Google AI Pro or AI Ultra plan active on that account.
- Safe Browsing set to Standard or Enhanced in Chrome settings.
- Not using Incognito mode or a restricted school/work account (unless an admin enables it).

Update Chrome from the Play Store first. The Auto Browse option may take time to roll out even if you meet the criteria.

## Open Gemini in Chrome

On a supported Android device:

1. Open the Chrome app.
2. Tap the Gemini sparkle icon in the toolbar (near the address bar) or open the three-dot menu and select Ask Gemini.
3. The Gemini panel slides up from the bottom.

You can ask questions about the current page, request a summary, or connect to Google apps such as Keep and Calendar. Nano Banana tools let you generate or edit images without leaving the browser.

## Start an Auto Browse Task

Auto Browse is the agentic layer. It plans and executes steps across websites.

1. In the Gemini panel, describe a clear multi-step task. Examples: “Find parking near the concert venue for Friday night and reserve one spot” or “Compare three budget hotels for next weekend and shortlist options with free cancellation.”
2. Submit the request.
3. Review the plan Gemini shows. It lists the sites it intends to visit and the actions it will take. Check that it understood your request and any personal details correctly.
4. Tap Start Task if the plan looks right.

Gemini opens a dedicated tab with an Auto Browse indicator. You can leave the app; a notification arrives when the task finishes or needs attention.

Limits apply: Pro accounts get up to 20 multi-step tasks per day; Ultra accounts get up to 200. Concurrent tasks are also limited.

## Monitor, Pause, or Take Over

You stay in charge:

- Tap Switch tabs next to the task in the chat to jump to the active tab.
- Pause or Resume from the Gemini panel.
- Stop the task at any time, or close the task tab.
- When Gemini reaches a sensitive step (purchase, password entry, final confirmation), it pauses and asks you to Review or Take over task.

To take over: open the task tab, tap Take over task, complete the step yourself, then return to Gemini and tap Resume or Give back task.

Gemini may use Google Password Manager to sign in to sites with your permission. Manage allowed sites under Chrome Settings > Gemini in Chrome > Sites Gemini can sign you in to.

## Safety Controls and Limits

Auto Browse is experimental. Google trains models to detect prompt injection and requires confirmation for sensitive actions. You remain responsible for the outcomes, including any mistakes or unexpected purchases.

Turn the feature off entirely: Chrome Settings > Gemini in Chrome > Let Gemini browse for you.

Review chats in Gemini Apps Activity and sites visited in Chrome history (marked with a Gemini action icon).

## Tips for Better Results

Write specific prompts with dates, locations, budgets, or constraints. Vague requests lead to broader plans that need more corrections.

Start with low-stakes tasks such as research or comparison. Monitor the first few runs closely so you learn where Gemini pauses.

Keep Chrome and Android system updates current. Check Safe Browsing settings remain at Standard or Enhanced.

If the feature is missing, wait for the rollout or confirm your subscription and language settings.

## Conclusion

Gemini Auto Browse in Chrome on Android turns the browser into an agent that completes everyday web chores. Eligible users open the Gemini panel, describe the task, review the plan, and start it. Confirmations and take-over options keep you in control.

The feature is rolling out gradually to AI Pro and Ultra subscribers on English-language devices in supported markets. Check your Chrome toolbar for the Gemini icon and test a simple request to confirm availability.
