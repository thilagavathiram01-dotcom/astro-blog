Google has rolled out its latest Gemini overlay design to every supported Android phone. The update brings the Neural Expressive visual language—previously limited to Pixel 11 devices—to the floating "Ask Gemini" bar that appears when you summon the assistant.

The change arrived through a Google app update around 29 October 2026. Reports from 9to5Google and Android Authority confirmed the sparkling animation and refined bar styling now appear on non-Pixel devices after the app refresh. No new Android version or Pixel hardware is required.

This guide shows exactly how to trigger the new look, what has changed, and what to do if it does not appear immediately.

## What the New Gemini Overlay Looks Like

The redesigned overlay keeps the familiar pill-shaped bar but adds clear visual upgrades drawn from Google’s Neural Expressive language.

When you activate Gemini, tiny sparkling dots and color swirls animate around the "Ask Gemini" text field. Once the animation settles, the bar shows a heavier blur effect. Its edges become translucent rather than solid, and lightly shaded borders give it subtle depth. The shading stands out more clearly in dark mode; in light mode the difference is milder.

Core controls stay the same: the plus menu, microphone, and Live shortcut remain in place. The update is purely visual. Functionality of the overlay itself does not change.

## How to Enable the New Overlay on Any Android

The redesign ships with the Google app, not a system update. Follow these steps on any compatible Android phone.

1. Open the Google Play Store and search for the Google app.
2. Tap Update if a newer version is available. Wait for the download and install to finish.
3. Force-stop the Google app: go to Settings > Apps > Google > Force stop. Confirm the action.
4. Return to your home screen and summon Gemini using your usual method—long-press the power button, say “Hey Google,” or swipe up from a bottom corner.
5. Watch the animation. You should see the extra sparkles, denser color particles, and the softer translucent edges on the bar.

If the old solid bar still appears, wait a few hours and repeat the force-stop step. Google rolls app features out gradually, so some accounts see the change later than others. Restarting the phone after the update can also help the new assets load.

The overlay works the same way on Samsung, Pixel, OnePlus, and other Android brands once the Google app is current.

## Why the Redesign Matters

Google introduced Neural Expressive at I/O earlier in 2026. The language emphasizes fluid motion, layered color, and lighter visual weight. Bringing it to the overlay means the quick-access Gemini experience now matches the fuller app and web interfaces more closely.

Users who rely on the overlay for quick questions, screen context, or starting Live sessions gain a more polished surface without opening the full Gemini app. The translucent edges also reduce visual clash against busy wallpapers and app icons.

If you frequently use Gemini Live from inside the app, note that a separate plus-button redesign for adding photos, files, and screen sharing landed a few days earlier. That change is covered in our [guide to the Gemini Live plus button](/blog/gemini-live-plus-button-android-how-to/).

## Tips If the New Design Does Not Appear

- Confirm you are signed into the same Google account that receives Gemini features. Some work or managed accounts lag behind consumer accounts.
- Clear the Google app’s cache (Settings > Apps > Google > Storage > Clear cache). Do not clear data unless you are prepared to re-sign in.
- Check that your phone meets the basic Gemini requirements for your region. The overlay update itself does not raise those requirements.
- Dark mode makes the new shaded edges easier to spot. Toggle it temporarily if you are unsure whether the update has arrived.
- The change is not tied to a specific Android version number. Any device that already ran Gemini through the Google app can receive it.

## What Stays the Same

The overlay still lets you ask questions about the current screen, attach photos or files via the plus menu, and jump into Live. PDF preview and editing inside full chats, announced around the same time, is a separate Gemini app capability and does not depend on this overlay visual update.

No settings toggle controls the new animation. Once the Google app serves the updated assets, the sparkling look is the default for everyone on that device.

## Conclusion

Updating the Google app and force-stopping it is the reliable way to bring the Neural Expressive Gemini overlay to any Android phone. The sparkling dots, stronger blur, and translucent edges arrive without a system upgrade or new hardware. Most users see the change within a day of the latest Google app version reaching their device.

Keep the Google app updated through the Play Store to catch future visual refinements as Google continues to expand Neural Expressive across its AI surfaces.
