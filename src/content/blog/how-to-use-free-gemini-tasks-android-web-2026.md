Google made Tasks free for users in the US, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia and India on 17 November 2026. The feature runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen and lets you hand off multi-step work instead of chatting back and forth.

This guide shows how to turn on Task mode, describe a job clearly, and track progress on both Android and the web. It draws only from Google’s official blog and Help Center.

## What Free Gemini Tasks Can Do

Task mode plans steps, uses connected apps and websites, and continues working after you close the app. Google says it connects with more than 60 popular apps.

Examples include booking appointments, sorting emails into calendar events, comparing shopping options, and drafting documents. In the US, eligible users can also text Gemini or forward emails to gemini@google.com.

Gemini asks for confirmation before purchases or other critical actions. You can stop a task at any time.

## How to Switch to Task Mode

1. Update the Gemini app or open gemini.google.com.
2. Sign in with a personal Google Account. You must be 18 or older and have Gemini History turned on.
3. Find the Chat / Task toggle. On mobile it sits above the prompt box. On the web it sits below the prompt box.
4. Switch the toggle to Task.
5. Describe the job with clear details: goal, deadline, budget, and any constraints.

If the toggle is missing, the rollout has not reached your account yet. Check again later or confirm your country is included.

For related setup on lower-memory phones, see our guide to [using Gemini on Android Go devices](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-on-android-go-lower-memory-devices/).

## Manage and Review Tasks

Open the Gemini app or gemini.google.com. Tap Menu, then look under All for recent task threads.

Progress icons show Ready, Needs attention, or In progress. Tap a thread to open it. At the top of the thread, tap the progress chip to review the steps Gemini completed.

You can pin, unpin, or rename threads from the menu. On the web, use the More menu next to a thread.

## Tips for Better Results

Write the outcome you want, not the clicks. Include dates, stores, or sources. Ask Gemini to pause before any purchase or form submission.

Start with low-risk tasks such as research or draft creation. Review every key step before you approve.

Free users can run up to 15 tasks at once. Schedules are more limited than on paid plans.
