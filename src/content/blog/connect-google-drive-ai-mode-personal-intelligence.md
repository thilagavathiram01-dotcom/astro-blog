Google’s AI Mode in Search can now pull context from more of your apps once you opt in. Wallet support is rolling out now. Google Drive and the Gemini app follow in the coming weeks. Connecting Drive lets AI Mode reference your Docs, Sheets, and PDFs so answers draw on the files you already keep there.

This guide shows how to prepare, where to turn the connection on, and what to expect when Drive appears in Connected Apps. It follows the steps Google published on November 23, 2026.

## What Drive adds to AI Mode

With Drive connected, AI Mode can use the content of documents, spreadsheets, and PDFs you store in Google Drive. Google’s example is a personal tax-season plan built from statements, receipts, and last year’s paperwork already in your Drive.

The connection stays read-only. You decide which apps to link and can turn any of them off at any time. A Personalization icon is also coming so you can see which data shaped a given answer.

## How to open Connected Apps

Google lists a single path for every Personal Intelligence app in AI Mode:

1. Open Google Search while signed in to your personal Google account.
2. Go to Search Personalization (or visit the Search personalization settings page directly).
3. Select Connected Apps.
4. Choose the apps you want AI Mode to use.

Wallet appears as the connection rolls out. Drive and the Gemini app will show up in the same list over the coming weeks. Each toggle is independent, so you can enable Drive without enabling Wallet, or the reverse.

You can also reach the same screen from the Google app or from AI Mode itself once the options are live for your account. Changes take effect the next time you ask a question in AI Mode.

## Prepare your Drive before the switch arrives

Because the Drive link is still rolling out, use the waiting period to organize the files you want AI Mode to see:

- Move tax forms, project notes, or recurring templates into a single folder so they are easy to find.
- Confirm sharing settings. AI Mode reads files you can access; it does not bypass Drive permissions.
- Remove or archive sensitive documents you do not want referenced in Search answers.
- Test a similar workflow in the Gemini app if you already have Workspace connected there, so you know what useful prompts look like.

Once Drive appears under Connected Apps, flip the switch and ask a concrete question such as “Summarize the action items from my Q3 planning doc” or “Build a checklist for the files I keep in my tax folder.”

## Tips for better results

Keep the connection on only for the accounts you use for personal Search. Workspace business, enterprise, and education accounts are excluded from Personal Intelligence in AI Mode.

When an answer looks off, correct it in the same thread. Google’s own example is telling AI Mode “I’m no longer a vegetarian.” The model updates from that feedback for future replies.

If you also use the Gemini app, the two-way context link that arrives with the Gemini app connection will let an idea you start in one place continue in the other. Pair this Drive setup with our earlier guide on [connecting Google Wallet to AI Mode Personal Intelligence](/blog/connect-google-wallet-ai-mode-personal-intelligence/) so you control both sources from the same settings page.

## What stays under your control

Every connection is off by default. You choose the apps, and you can reverse the choice at any moment in Search Personalization → Connected Apps. AI Mode does not move or edit your Drive files; it only reads the content you already store there when it builds an answer.

A Personalization icon will appear under tailored responses soon. Tap it to see which connected app contributed to the reply. Until that icon ships, the same Connected Apps page is the place to review or revoke access.

## Conclusion

Connecting Google Drive to AI Mode Personal Intelligence turns scattered Docs, Sheets, and PDFs into context for Search answers. The path is the same one Google already uses for Gmail, Calendar, Photos, and the new Wallet link: Search Personalization → Connected Apps.

Watch for Drive to appear in that list over the coming weeks, organize the files you want referenced, and turn the connection on only when you are ready. You stay in control the entire time.
