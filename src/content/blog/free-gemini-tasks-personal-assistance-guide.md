Google made Tasks in the Gemini app free on 17 November 2026 for users in the United States, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. The feature runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen and turns Gemini into an agent that can book appointments, shop, analyse bills, and handle multi-step work while you stay focused on other things.

You no longer need a Google AI Pro or Ultra plan to start handing work off. Open the Gemini app, flip the toggle from Chat to Task, and describe what you need done.

## What Gemini Tasks can do today

Tasks lets Gemini act across your Google data and connected services. Because your email, calendar, photos, files, and payment methods already sit inside Google’s systems, Gemini can use them without you exporting sensitive information elsewhere.

Common examples from Google’s own testing include calling nearby car dealerships to book a service slot, analysing utility bills stored in Gmail to spot peak-hour energy use, comparing product options and completing a purchase with Google Pay, and adding items to an Instacart order after you text a photo of an empty box. Receipts and tracking details land in Google Wallet.

Advanced actions such as placing phone calls to businesses and completing payments start in the United States. In other supported countries the core task-hand-off experience is available immediately.

## How to start a task

1. Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS (or go to gemini.google.com on the web).
2. On the home screen, switch the toggle from Chat to Task.
3. Type or speak a clear instruction. Example: “Call the two nearest Honda dealerships and book the earliest Friday morning service appointment in the next four weeks. Add it to my calendar.”
4. Review the plan Gemini shows you. Confirm or adjust before it continues.
5. Gemini works in the background. You can check progress or add more instructions at any time.

For routine actions you trust, you can set “Always allow” so Gemini does not pause for every confirmation.

## Set up email and text channels

Starting in the United States you can also give Gemini tasks by email or text message.

**Email:** Forward relevant messages to gemini@google.com. Gemini can extract dates, add calendar events, and invite other people when needed.

**Text (RCS / Messages):**
1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Tap Settings.
3. Tap Channels.
4. Tap Set up under Text with Gemini.
5. Verify your phone number.

Once active, you can text Gemini a request (for example, “Add lasagna, strawberries, cucumbers, and vanilla ice cream to our Instacart order”) and follow up with a photo if you forget something.

## Security and data protection

When you assign a task, Gemini runs inside a hardware-isolated virtual machine dedicated to your account. The environment is sandboxed, scanned for prompt injection, and limited by Google Safe Browsing. Gemini pauses for confirmation before critical steps such as clicking Buy, and it does not share payment or private account details unless you explicitly approve.

You can disconnect apps, adjust permissions, or delete history at any time in settings. Google also runs a vulnerability reward programme for agentic systems.

## Practical tips for better results

- Give concrete details (time windows, preferred stores, spending caps). Gemini asks clarifying questions when an instruction is ambiguous.
- Connect Google Password Manager if you want it to sign into utility or shopping portals on your behalf.
- Set a hard spending limit before any purchase. You still earn card rewards and purchase protections.
- Provide feedback with a thumbs-down when a task misses the mark. This helps improve future runs.
- Keep an eye on the first few tasks. Some websites still require you to finish the final click yourself.

## Limitations to know

Calling businesses is not yet available in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska. Texting requires a US phone number with an eligible carrier. Tasks are currently limited to personal Google Accounts (not work or school accounts) and users aged 18 or older.

The web and connected services are still adapting to personal assistants. Gemini may complete most of a flow and then hand the screen back to you for the last confirmation.

Free Gemini Tasks removes the previous paywall and gives hundreds of millions of people a practical way to offload multi-step personal work.
