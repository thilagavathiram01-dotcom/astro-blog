Google Home’s November 25, 2026 update adds a remote 30-second security siren for the Nest Cam Outdoor (wired, 2nd gen). You trigger it directly from the live view in the Google Home app while watching the feed. The same control works on two Walmart onn outdoor cameras. This guide walks through the exact steps, supported devices, and related changes that arrived in the same release.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="239" />

The feature appears only when you are already viewing live video. It does not require a separate menu or a Google Home Premium subscription for the Nest Cam itself. Onn models gain Familiar Faces support at the same time, which does need Premium.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="244" />

## Supported cameras and requirements

The siren control works on these models:

- Nest Cam Outdoor (wired, 2nd gen)
- onn Outdoor Camera Plug-In
- onn Floodlight Camera Hardwired

You need the latest Google Home app on Android or iOS. The update rolls out gradually, so force-close and reopen the app if the button is missing. The camera must be online and the live view must load successfully before the control appears.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="239" />

## Step-by-step: activate the siren

1. Open the Google Home app and select your Nest Cam Outdoor (or compatible onn camera).
2. Tap to start the live video stream. Wait until the feed is clear and controls are visible.
3. Look for the new siren button in the live-view controls. Tap it to start the 30-second siren.
4. The siren runs for 30 seconds. Tap the same control again at any time to stop it early.
5. Confirm the sound stopped by watching the live view or listening near the camera.

The button is remote-only. You cannot start the siren from a voice command in the current release. Keep the app in the foreground while the siren is active if you want to stop it manually.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="244" />

## Tips for reliable use

- Update the Google Home app from the Play Store or App Store before testing.
- Test the siren at a low volume or during daytime so neighbors are not surprised.
- Confirm the camera’s microphone and speaker are not muted in device settings.
- If the button never appears, check the camera firmware and restart the Google Home app.
- Familiar Faces on the onn cameras requires a Google Home Premium subscription.

The siren is intended as a short deterrent. It does not replace a dedicated alarm system. Pair it with existing activity zones and notifications for better coverage.
