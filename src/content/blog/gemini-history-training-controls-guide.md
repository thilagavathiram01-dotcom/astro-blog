Google announced on 16 November 2026 that Gemini will separate chat history controls from generative AI model training. You can now keep your conversations while choosing whether they train models.

The rollout starts globally in the coming weeks. Until it reaches your account, the older Keep Activity toggle may still appear. Check the History page after the update arrives.

This guide covers the new settings for personal accounts on the web and mobile apps. Work and school accounts follow Workspace admin rules.

## What changed in the Gemini controls

Previously, Keep Activity managed both how long chats were saved and whether they trained generative AI models. The new setup splits these.

History controls retention and visibility in your activity list. It is on by default for users 18 and over.

A separate control decides whether chat content, including text and attachments, trains generative AI models. That option is off by default. You need History on to enable it.

If you had Keep Activity turned off before the change, both new settings start off.

Even with History off, Google saves future chats for around two months. Those copies support responses, feedback processing, and protection of Google, users, and the public. They do not appear in your Gemini History.

## Access the History page on web

Sign in to the Google Account that uses Gemini.

1. Open [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. Click Settings at the bottom, then History.
3. Or go directly to [myactivity.google.com/product/gemini](https://myactivity.google.com/product/gemini).

Near the top you see the History status. Click On to turn it off or to turn it off and delete history.

You can also adjust the auto-delete period. The default removes history older than 18 months. Options include 3 months, 36 months, or no auto-delete.

On the same page, look for the training toggles. Select the box next to “Chat content including text & uploads” only if you want that use. A second option covers audio and Gemini Live videos and screenshares.

Click Turn on after reading the disclosure. Clear the box later to stop training use.

## Manage settings in the mobile app

Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS.

1. Tap the menu icon.
2. Tap your profile picture or initial.
3. Select Gemini Apps Activity or History.

The same History switch and training options appear. Changes apply across devices for that account.

Temporary chats remain available. They stay out of history, are not used for training, and are retained briefly for the service.

## Retention and safety notes

With History on, the default auto-delete is 18 months. You can review or delete items manually at any time from the History page.

With History off, the two-month retention still applies for service delivery and protection. Human reviewers may assist with those protective uses.

Reviewed content can be retained longer in disconnected form, up to three years in prior guidance. Google advises against entering confidential information you would not want reviewed.

Feedback you submit can share recent context even when training is off. Review that choice carefully.

Connected apps and Personal Intelligence features may have additional limits when History is off. Check those sections separately if you use them.

## Tips for choosing your settings

Keep History on and training off if you want conversation continuity without model improvement from your chats. This matches the new separation.

Turn both off if you prefer minimal storage and no training. Expect the two-month window.

Set auto-delete to 3 months for shorter retention while History stays on.

Use temporary chats for one-off sensitive prompts. They bypass normal history and training.

Review the page after the rollout completes for your account. Google emails users as the change arrives.

Export anything important before bulk deletion. The History page is a log, not a full backup of other Google services.

## Compare with the older Keep Activity approach

The prior Keep Activity setting combined retention and training. Turning it off stopped new activity from listing and blocked training use, with a 72-hour hold.

The update gives independent control. You can retain history for personal reference while opting out of training. The longer two-month hold when History is off reflects added safety measures.

For steps on the previous interface, see our guide to [finding and deleting Gemini Apps activity history](/blog/delete-gemini-apps-activity-history/).

## Additional practical checks

After changing settings, start a new chat and confirm it behaves as expected. With History off, new conversations should not list in the activity view.

On mobile, pull down to refresh the History page if the interface has not updated yet. The global rollout means some accounts see the change before others.

If you use Gemini Live, note that audio and video shares follow the separate training toggle. Leave that off unless you specifically want those recordings used for model improvement.

Human review still occurs for safety and service reasons in the retained window. Avoid pasting passwords, financial details, or health information into any chat.

For Android users who set Gemini as the default assistant, the account-level History setting still governs stored prompts. Uninstalling the app does not clear the Google Account history.

Check My Activity regularly if you keep History on. The search and date filters help locate specific past prompts quickly.

These steps keep the controls practical while the new separation rolls out. Revisit the page in a few weeks if the options look different from what is described here.

## Conclusion

The November 2026 Gemini update lets you keep chat history without automatically contributing to generative AI training. Open the History page on the web or in the app to set both options.

Confirm the current status for your account, adjust auto-delete if needed, and use temporary chats for extra caution. Official support pages list the exact current retention language.
