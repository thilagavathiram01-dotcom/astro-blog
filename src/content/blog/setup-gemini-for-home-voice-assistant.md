Google is rolling out Gemini for Home as the replacement for the classic Google Assistant on Nest speakers and displays. The update brings more natural conversations, multi-intent commands, and deeper smart home control.

If your home is eligible, you can switch today. Once enabled, the change applies to everyone in the household and cannot be reversed back to Google Assistant.

## Check Compatibility and Availability

Gemini for Home voice assistant works on Google speakers and displays made since 2016. Firmware requirements apply: Cast firmware 3.76 or higher for speakers, and Fuchsia firmware 28 or higher for displays.

Full experience devices include the Google Home Speaker (2026), Nest Hub (2nd gen), Nest Audio, Nest Mini (2nd gen), Nest Hub Max, and Nest Hub (1st gen). Older models such as the original Google Home, Home Mini, Home Max, and Nest Wifi point support most features but not Gemini Live.

Early access is available in Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Mexico, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and the United States. Supported languages include Danish, Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Norwegian, Spanish, and Swedish.

Basic features remain free. These cover smart home controls, media search and playback, alarms, timers, calendars, notes, lists, and reminders. Gemini Live, camera history search, and Home Brief require a Google Home Premium subscription.

## Request Early Access

Open the Google Home app (version 4.1 or later). Tap your profile picture or initial in the top right, then select Home settings. Scroll down and tap Early Access. Follow the prompts to join.

Confirm your Google Groups settings first so invitations can reach you. You will receive a notification in the app when the upgrade becomes available for your home.

If you already see a banner under Home settings that reads “Set up Gemini for Home for more natural conversations,” tap it and complete the in-app steps.

## Set Up the Voice Assistant

After invitation, follow the on-screen instructions. Choose a natural-sounding voice for the entire home. Options include brighter, calmer, or accented voices that apply to all speakers and displays.

On Android devices, update the Speech Recognition and Synthesis app before starting. The setup process also lets you configure response filtering and voice access to camera history if you have Nest cameras.

Once complete, compatible devices switch immediately. New devices added later adopt Gemini for Home automatically.

## Everyday Commands and Features

Say “Hey Google” as usual. You can now chain requests in plain language. Examples include turning off lights while leaving specific lamps on, or dimming hallway lights, locking the door, and playing rain sounds in one sentence.

Media search understands descriptions. Ask for “that song that goes [lyrics]” or “the acoustic version of [song].” Request podcasts by topic or category and play the second suggestion without repeating the wake word if Continued Conversation is on.

Enable Continued Conversation in the app under Home settings > Gemini for Home voice assistant. After the first response, the device listens for a few more seconds.

For longer discussions, say “Hey Google, let’s chat” to start Gemini Live (Premium required). Interrupt, ask follow-ups, or brainstorm freely until the session ends.

Household tasks work naturally. Add calendar events, set named timers, or build shopping lists by voice. Kitchen help includes recipe ideas from available ingredients and substitutions.

Camera features (Advanced Premium) let you ask “Were any packages delivered?” or “What happened at home today?” Home Brief delivers an evening summary of notable camera events.

## Tips for Best Results

Update all device firmware through the Google Home app before switching. Keep devices on stable Wi-Fi or cellular data. Location services must remain enabled for the best experience.

Test multi-intent commands gradually. Start with simple combinations and expand. Voice Match helps personalize results for different household members.

Review linked services under Gemini for Home voice assistant settings for Calendar, Media, Notes & lists, and Home control. Adjust “Hey Google” sensitivity if the device misses or false-triggers.

Parental controls and response filtering remain available. Voice-matched children always receive filtered responses.

Gemini for Home replaces Google Assistant with more conversational control across your Nest devices. Join early access, complete the one-time setup, and start using natural language for daily tasks. The switch is permanent for the home, so review features and subscription needs first.

Basic capabilities work without payment. Premium unlocks Gemini Live and camera intelligence. Official documentation at Google Home and Nest Help provides the latest availability and troubleshooting.
