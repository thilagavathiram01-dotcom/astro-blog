Free Gemini users on Android no longer see a model picker. The app shows only Auto. Google routes most prompts to Flash-Lite and sends harder ones to Flash or Pro when needed. You can still control how thoroughly the model works by setting a thinking level of low, medium, or high.

This change started on October 9, 2026 for accounts without a Google AI plan. Update the Gemini app first so the new controls appear.

## What Auto model selection does

Google's Gemini Apps help page states that Auto is the default for users without a plan. Gemini uses auto model selection to send each prompt to the model that suits it best. Most prompts go to Flash-Lite, the fastest model. Prompts that need deeper reasoning may go to Flash or Pro.

If you turn smart model selection off, free accounts stay on Flash-Lite for every response.

The model picker no longer lists Flash or Pro as selectable options for free users. Auto replaces the old manual choice. Paid plans still let you pick models directly. See the full plan breakdown in the [Gemini Model Access Changes October 9 guide](/blog/gemini-model-access-october-9-plan-guide/).

## Set low, medium, or high thinking levels

The new effort levels replace the previous Extended option. They match the thinking levels available in Google AI Studio. Low is quick and efficient. Medium offers balanced depth. High is extra thorough.

Higher levels improve the model's ability to finish complex tasks and return more complete answers. They also consume more of your usage limit.

Follow these steps on Android:

1. Open the Gemini app and sign in with your personal Google account.
2. Start a new chat so previous settings do not carry over.
3. Look for the model control near the prompt box. It should show Auto or Gemini 3 models.
4. Tap the thinking level or effort control next to it.
5. Choose Low for short facts, unit conversions, or simple rewrites.
6. Choose Medium for email drafts, study outlines, or routine comparisons.
7. Choose High for multi-step plans, debugging notes, or side-by-side analyses.
8. Send your prompt. If a limit message appears, drop one level and retry.

Do not leave High on for every message. A string of high-effort turns reaches the five-hour and weekly caps faster.

## Match the level to the task

Auto plus the right thinking level covers most everyday work without a subscription.

Use Low for:
- Quick definitions or translations
- Short subject lines or captions
- Simple list generation

Use Medium for:
- Meeting note summaries
- First-draft emails or messages
- Outlines for short documents

Use High for:
- Comparing options with constraints
- Breaking down a multi-step process
- Reviewing a pasted code snippet for obvious issues

Paste only the text you need. Longer chats count toward your limit. Media generation, Deep Research, and other premium features also spend the budget faster.

For more detail on effort settings and Deep Think on paid plans, read [Set Gemini Effort Levels After the October 9 Change](/blog/gemini-effort-levels-deep-think-october/).

## How usage limits work with Auto

Gemini Apps use compute-based limits that refresh every five hours until you hit the weekly cap. The calculation factors in prompt complexity, features used, and chat length. Free accounts have standard limits. AI Plus is 2x, AI Pro is 4x, and AI Ultra is higher still.

High thinking levels and any routing to Flash or Pro use more of that budget than a Low Flash-Lite reply. When the window is empty, wait for the next five-hour refresh or the weekly reset.

Update the app, then test one Low and one High prompt in a new chat. Note how long each takes and whether the answer quality changes for your usual tasks.

## Tips for free accounts

Update Gemini from the Play Store before you check the picker. Older builds may still show the previous model list or Extended toggle.

If Auto does not appear yet, the rollout has not reached your account. Google is pushing the change quickly across free accounts.

Turn smart model selection on if you want the system to escalate hard prompts. Leave it off only if you prefer consistent Flash-Lite speed.

Split heavy work. A Low rewrite of a draft plus a later High check often stays inside the limit better than one long High session.

Sign out of extra Google accounts on the phone. A work profile can show a different model list than your personal free account.

## Bottom line

Free Gemini users on Android now work with Auto model selection. Most prompts stay on Flash-Lite. Harder ones may escalate. Set the thinking level deliberately: Low for speed, Medium for everyday drafts, High when the answer needs more care. Check the picker after updating the app, then match the level to the task so you stay inside the five-hour windows.
