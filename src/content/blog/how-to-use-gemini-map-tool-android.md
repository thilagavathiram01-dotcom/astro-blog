Google rolled out a Map tool in the Gemini app on Android and iOS in early October 2026. You open a live map, adjust a circular area, and attach that region to your prompt as a Map Area chip. The feature sits at the end of the attachment carousel next to Photos, Camera, Files, Drive, and Notebooks. It is not available on the web version.

This removes the guesswork from location questions. Instead of typing a half-remembered street name, you point at the area on the map. Gemini then grounds its answers in that selected region using Google Maps data.

![Person holding a smartphone showing a navigation map outdoors](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1512499617640-ee7495110b80?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What the Map tool does

When you tap Map, Gemini opens a live Google Maps view centered on your current location. A circular focus area appears. You can drag the map, pinch to zoom, or use the search icon in the top-right corner to jump to another place. An X button closes the map without attaching anything. The Explore this area button at the bottom adds a Map Area chip to your prompt box.

Zoomed in, the circle covers only a few blocks. Zoomed out, it can cover a whole neighborhood or a small city. Once the chip is attached, you type your question. Gemini treats the selected area as the context for places, hours, ratings, and directions.

Reports from 9to5Google and Android Authority confirm the tool is widely available on both Android and iOS as of early October 2026. It first appeared in app code earlier in the year.

## How to open the Map tool on Android

1. Open the Gemini app on your Android phone or tablet. Make sure you are signed in with a Google account and location services are on if you want the map to start at your current position.
2. In the prompt box, tap the plus (+) button on the left. A horizontal carousel of tools appears.
3. Scroll sideways to the far end. Tap Map. It sits after Photos, Camera, Avatar, Files, Drive, and Notebooks.
4. The live map opens with the circular area centered on your location (or a default if location is off).

If the Map option is missing, update the Gemini app from the Play Store and restart it. The feature rolled out broadly but may still be staggered by account or region.

## Selecting and attaching an area

Once the map is open:

- Drag with one finger to pan.
- Pinch with two fingers to zoom in or out. The circle resizes with the zoom level.
- Tap the magnifying glass in the top-right to search for a city, neighborhood, or address. Select a result and the map jumps there.
- Tap the current-location button (usually bottom-right) to snap back to your position.
- When the circle covers the area you care about, tap Explore this area at the bottom.

Gemini adds a Map Area chip to the prompt. You can type your question next to it and send. Examples that work well:

- “Best coffee shops open now inside this area, ranked by rating.”
- “Family-friendly parks with playgrounds here.”
- “Restaurants with outdoor seating and vegan options within the circle.”
- “What is the walk time from the center of this area to the nearest train station?”

The chip stays attached for that message. You can remove it by tapping the X on the chip before sending if you change your mind.

## What you get back

Answers typically include place cards with photos, star ratings, price range, and open/closed status. Tapping a card shows review snippets and a button to open the place in Google Maps. A map preview at the top of the response often shows pins for the suggested places. Some results include an Export to Google Maps button that saves the set as a custom list.

Because the area is explicitly selected, results stay inside the circle more reliably than a free-text “near me” prompt. Follow-up questions in the same chat continue to respect the attached area until you start a new one.

## Tips for better results

Start with a tighter circle when you want specific streets or blocks. A wide circle works better for neighborhood overviews or comparing several options. Combine the Map Area chip with a clear instruction: “Only show places inside the selected area that are open right now.”

If you need directions, ask for walking or driving times from the center of the circle. Gemini can hand off to Google Maps for full navigation. For complex trip planning, pair this with other Gemini tools such as connected apps for Flights or Hotels once those are enabled on your account.

Location permission is required for the map to start at your position. You can still use the search icon without it. The tool does not work on the desktop or web Gemini interface as of mid-October 2026.

## Troubleshooting

- Map button missing: Update the Gemini app, clear cache, or check again later. The rollout began around 2 October 2026.
- Circle does not appear: Ensure you are on the mobile app, not the web version. Force-close and reopen Gemini.
- Results ignore the area: Confirm the Map Area chip is present in the prompt before sending. Try a more explicit prompt that mentions the selected region.
- No places returned: Zoom out slightly or move the circle to a denser area. Some remote regions have limited data.

## Conclusion

The Map tool turns vague location prompts into precise ones. Open the Gemini app, tap plus, scroll to Map, adjust the circle, tap Explore this area, and ask your question. The answers stay grounded in the region you selected and include ready-to-open Maps links.

Try it next time you need restaurants, parks, or services in a specific neighborhood. Update the app if the option is not yet visible, and keep location services enabled for the fastest start.
