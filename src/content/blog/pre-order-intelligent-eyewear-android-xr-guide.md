Google and Samsung opened pre-orders for Intelligent Eyewear on November 12, 2026. These Android XR audio glasses bring Gemini into everyday frames from Warby Parker and Gentle Monster. Full sales start December 10 in the United States and South Korea.

The glasses weigh about 41 grams, last up to nine hours on a charge, and include a case that recharges them up to seven times. They work with select Pixel, Galaxy, and iPhone models. You can try demos at Warby Parker stores or order online now.

## What Intelligent Eyewear Offers

These are audio-first glasses, not display glasses. Gemini responds to “Hey Gemini” or a tap on the frame. You can ask about objects in view, get Maps directions in your ear, translate speech in real time across more than 70 languages, or capture photos and short videos with the 12-megapixel ultra-wide camera.

Open-ear speakers play music from YouTube Music, Spotify, or Apple Music while keeping ambient sound audible. The glasses also support Guided Vision for audio descriptions and can connect to Aira for human visual interpreters. Battery life reaches up to nine hours depending on use. The Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1 chip, 2 GB RAM, and 64 GB storage handle on-device processing alongside your phone.

## How to Pre-Order

1. Visit warbyparker.com or the Gentle Monster site. Both list the Intelligent Eyewear collections.
2. Choose a style. Warby Parker offers Alden, Reid, and Wren frames starting at $395 in optical or sun versions. Gentle Monster starts at $449 with six designs and more color options.
3. Select lens type, width, and prescription if needed. Most frames support custom prescriptions.
4. Complete the order. Pre-orders ship for the December 10 availability date. Hands-on demos run at more than 360 Warby Parker locations and select Gentle Monster and Google Store sites.

Availability is limited to the United States and South Korea at launch. A compatible Google account (18+) from those regions is required. Check glasses.google for the latest status.

## Compatibility and Setup

Supported phones include:
- Google Pixel 7 series and later on Android 17 September 2026 update or newer
- Samsung Galaxy S, S FE, Z Fold, Flip, and Fold Ultra series on One UI 9.0 or higher
- iPhone 13 and later with at least 6 GB RAM on iOS 26.6 or newer

Pair through Bluetooth after unboxing. For the full setup flow once your pair arrives, see our guide on [connecting Android XR glasses with Play Services](/blog/connect-android-xr-glasses-play-services/).

Bixby is available when paired with Galaxy phones. Gemini handles most voice interactions across all supported devices.

## Privacy Controls

Camera visuals used by Gemini are not saved to your Gemini History and are not used for model training or ads. The glasses automatically redact bystanders’ faces before processing. Data on the device is encrypted, and any data that leaves the glasses is encrypted in transit.

You control camera and microphone access per app. An indicator light activates when you record photo or video for your gallery; the system stops recording if the light is covered. An off switch lets you use the frames as ordinary glasses.

## Tips Before You Order

Confirm your phone meets the OS and hardware requirements listed above. Test the fit if you can visit a demo location—lightweight does not guarantee comfort for every face shape. Factor in the charging case for multi-day use. Pre-order early if you want a specific color or frame, as popular styles may sell out before the December 10 release.

If you plan to use Guided Vision or Aira, note that those features require a compatible Android phone at launch.
