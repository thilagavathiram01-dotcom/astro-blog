Google rolled out 66 new regional voices in the Gemini app on 16 November 2026. The update covers Android and iOS. Voices now match local cadences for U.S. English, U.K. English, India English, Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian, and Korean.

This makes spoken replies feel more familiar. The change applies in Gemini Live, language practice, and when you tap Listen on a chat response. You preview options and pick one in two places.

## What the Regional Voices Offer

Each voice focuses on natural rhythm, pitch, and inflection. Google tuned them for everyday clarity instead of exaggerated accents. Text replies stay free of forced slang. The sound simply fits the region better.

U.K. English includes more than ten options. Reports list Geordie, Scouse, Brummie, Scottish, Welsh, Northern Irish, Modern RP, and Multicultural London English among them. Other locales receive tailored sets too. The exact count per language is not listed in the announcement.

Voices are not labeled by city or region in the picker. Gemini will not name the accent if you ask. You choose by listening to previews.

## How to Select a Regional Voice

Open the Gemini app on your Android phone or iOS device. Make sure you are signed in.

**From Gemini Live:**

1. Start a Live conversation.
2. Tap the three-dot menu.
3. Preview the available voices.
4. Select the one that sounds right.

**From app settings:**

1. Tap the menu icon.
2. Tap your profile picture or initial.
3. Open Settings.
4. Tap Gemini’s voice.
5. Tap a voice name to hear the sample.
6. Tap Confirm.

The choice takes effect immediately. It covers Live chats and the Listen button on written replies. The same setting works across the app. No separate control exists for Live only.

If the option is missing, check that your app is updated and your account language supports the feature. Some languages offer fewer choices. The rollout continues, so options may appear later.

## Tips for Finding the Right Voice

Listen to several samples. Raise the volume if a preview is quiet. Ask Gemini about local food, sport, or culture once you select a voice. The rhythm stands out more with relevant topics.

Test both Live and the Listen button on a short reply. Confirm the sound matches what you expect. You can switch at any time by returning to the same menu and confirming a different option.

Regional voices work best when your device language matches one of the supported locales. They do not change the language of text responses.

If you use Tasks for multi-step work, the voice still applies when Gemini speaks results. See our guide on [how to use Tasks in Gemini](/blog/how-to-use-tasks-in-gemini/) for the full workflow.

## Where the Voice Applies

Your selected voice carries through the entire Gemini app on Android and iOS. It covers:

- Gemini Live brainstorming and conversation.
- Language practice sessions.
- Read-aloud playback when you tap the Listen icon under a chat response.

The feature is currently limited to the mobile apps. Web users on laptop or Chromebook do not see the new options yet, according to the announcement.

Google first previewed regional dialects at I/O earlier in 2026. The November rollout delivers the first set of 66 voices. More may follow.

## Conclusion

The new regional voices make Gemini sound more local without changing what it says. Open the app, preview options in Live or Settings, and confirm your choice. The same voice works for Live chats and Listen playback.

Start with a short test reply. Switch if needed. The controls are simple and take effect right away. Official details come from the 16 November Google blog post by Neel Joshi.
