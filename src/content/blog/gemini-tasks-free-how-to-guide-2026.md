Google expanded Gemini Tasks to free users in six countries on 17 November 2026. The feature, previously limited to paid Google AI plans, now runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen. It turns the Gemini app into an active personal assistant that can book appointments, shop, manage calendars and handle routine admin while you focus on other work.

This guide shows exactly how to start using it, what it can do today, and how Google protects your data.

## What Gemini Tasks Does

Tasks is a mode inside the Gemini app. Switch from Chat to Task and describe a job. Gemini plans the steps, connects to your Google apps and third-party services, and works in the background. You can check progress, add instructions, or stop it at any time.

It differs from regular chat. Chat answers questions. Tasks complete multi-step work such as calling businesses, analysing bills, comparing products and checking out, or adding events from emails to your calendar. Google says the goal is to save you time and money.

The free rollout covers the United States, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia and India. Advanced actions such as placing phone calls, Google Pay checkout, texting Gemini and email forwarding started in the US and are expanding. You must be 18 or older and use a personal Google Account. Work and school accounts are not supported yet.

## How to Start a Task

Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS, or go to gemini.google.com. Sign in with your personal Google Account.

Look for the Chat and Task toggle. On mobile it sits above the prompt box. On the web it appears below. Switch to Task.

Type what you want done. You can include a schedule, such as “every weekday at 8 AM”. Connect the Google apps Gemini requests, such as Gmail, Calendar, Drive or Password Manager. Submit the task.

Gemini shows its plan and starts working. It asks for confirmation before sensitive steps such as clicking Buy or placing a call. You can reply with more details while it runs. When finished, results appear in the task thread and you receive a notification.

In the US you can also start tasks by text or email. To text Gemini:

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Tap Settings.
3. Tap Channels.
4. Tap Set up under Text with Gemini.
5. Verify your phone number (US number on an eligible carrier required).

Forward emails or PDFs to gemini@google.com for actions such as extracting school dates into Calendar.

## Practical Examples That Work Today

Google’s own examples illustrate the range.

One user asked Gemini to call two nearby dealerships for a minivan service appointment. Gemini used location and vehicle details from Personal Intelligence, placed the calls (disclosing it is an AI), found an open slot that matched the calendar, and added the appointment. Businesses can opt out of AI calls.

Another task analysed a surprise electricity bill. With permission to access the utility portal via Google Password Manager and past statements in email, Gemini identified that a heat pump was running during peak pricing hours.

Shopping works through Google’s Shopping Graph and the Agent Payments Protocol with tokenised cards from Mastercard and Visa (American Express coming). Set a spending cap. Gemini finds discount codes, completes checkout after your approval, and sends receipts to Google Wallet. You still earn card points and protections.

Daily routines include morning briefings that summarise important emails and Calendar events, or adding items to a grocery order after a photo of an empty box. Gemini connects to more than 60 apps.

## Safety and Controls

Each task runs in a hardware-isolated virtual machine on Google Cloud that is dedicated to your account and sandboxed. Prompt-injection filters and Google Safe Browsing limit risky links.

Gemini pauses for confirmation before critical actions. For repeated trusted steps you can set “Always allow”. You can disconnect apps, adjust settings or delete history at any time.

Google notes that some sites still treat automated assistants cautiously. Gemini may hand the final click back to you. The company is partnering with websites and security providers so personal assistants work more reliably.

Calling is currently unavailable in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana and Nebraska.

## Tips for Better Results

Be specific. Include dates, preferred times, budgets and constraints. “Call the two nearest Honda dealerships for a Friday morning service slot in the next month” works better than “book a car service”.

Connect only the apps the task needs. Review the plan Gemini shows before it starts.

Start with low-risk tasks such as calendar summaries or product comparisons before shopping or calls.

Give feedback with thumbs down when a result misses the mark. This helps improve the system.

For recurring work, state the schedule clearly in the prompt.

## What Comes Next

Google is expanding the advanced features beyond the US and integrating the Universal Commerce Protocol for smoother shopping. Gemini 4 Hydrogen powers the free experience, offering faster everyday performance than earlier Flash models.

Tasks is still rolling out, so the toggle may take a short time to appear on your account. Check that History is on and you are in a supported country.
