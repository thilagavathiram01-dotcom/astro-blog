Google expanded the Gemini app to lower-memory Android devices on November 18, 2026. Eligible phones now run the full conversational assistant instead of basic voice commands. This reaches hundreds of millions of people who rely on budget hardware for education, business, and daily tasks.

The update targets Android (Go edition) devices with at least 2 GB of RAM. Google re-architected the app so core features run efficiently on constrained hardware. It replaces Assistant for Android (Go edition) and integrates into the Google Search app on these devices.

![Person holding a smartphone showing AI apps](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1512941937669-90a1b58e7e9c?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Check Device Eligibility

Confirm your phone meets the requirements before installing. Open Settings and check the RAM amount under About phone or System. You need 2 GB or more. Sign in with a personal Google Account, or a work or school account that includes Gemini Apps access.

Family Link managers can control access for users under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). The app works in all languages and countries where Gemini is available. On Android (Go edition) devices, Gemini arrives as part of the Google Search app.

## Install or Update Gemini

Follow these steps to get the latest version:

1. Open the Google Play Store on your device.
2. Search for "Google" or "Google Search".
3. Tap Update if the button appears. If it says Open, you already have the latest version.
4. Alternatively, search for the Gemini app and install it directly from Play Store.

Some newer partner devices, including certain Transsion models, ship with Gemini preloaded. After updating, restart the phone if the app does not appear immediately.

## Start a Conversation

Launch Gemini in one of two ways on supported Android (Go edition) devices:

- Touch and hold the Home button.
- Press and hold the Power button.

Note that "Hey Google" and "Ask about this screen" are not supported on these devices. Once open, type or speak your request. Gemini handles multi-step queries and keeps the interface light so it does not overwhelm limited memory.

## What You Can Do with Gemini

The optimized app includes practical tools that work on lower-memory hardware:

- **Gemini Live**: Hold free-flowing spoken conversations in more than 45 languages. Practice interview answers or brainstorm ideas out loud without typing long prompts.
- **Camera help**: Point the camera at notes, forms, or printed text. Gemini translates content or walks you through step-by-step learning.
- **Image creation**: Generate simple images with Nano Banana, such as a flyer for a local sale or a quick graphic.
- **Everyday tasks**: Ask it to set an alarm, create a calendar event, make a call, or send a text message to a contact.
- **Local information**: Request drive times, nearby restaurants, or more complex questions that combine location and preferences.
- **File uploads**: Attach documents or photos for added context in a chat.
- **Media playback**: Tell Gemini to play music by mood or activity.

These features run by sending requests to Google's cloud, so the phone itself does not store large models. The lighter client reduces process restarts when you switch between apps.

## Tips for Smooth Performance

Budget phones work best when you keep background activity low. Close unused apps before starting a long Gemini Live session. Clear cache in the Google Search app if responses feel slow. Update the app regularly through Play Store so you receive further optimizations.

If you do not see the Chat/Task toggle or other advanced features, the free Tasks experience may still be rolling out in your region. Check our earlier guide on [how to use free Gemini Tasks](/blog/how-to-use-free-gemini-tasks-guide/) for details on the agent mode that works in six countries.

## Limitations to Know

Hands-free "Hey Google" activation is unavailable. Some advanced Gemini features that require higher-end hardware remain limited. Usage follows the same account and regional rules as the standard Gemini app. Work and school accounts need the proper license for full access.

## Conclusion

Lower-memory Android devices no longer face a basic voice-command barrier. Eligible phones with 2 GB of RAM or more can now run Gemini for conversations, camera help, image creation, and everyday tasks. Update the Google Search app or install Gemini from Play Store, then launch it by holding the Home or Power button. The re-architected experience puts Google's personal AI assistant in reach for hundreds of millions more people.
