Black Friday and holiday shopping mean endless tabs and price checks. Gemini now helps directly from the screen you already have open.

Google is rolling out shopping-specific prompts in the Gemini floating overlay on Android. When you open Gemini over a product page in a shopping app, it suggests actions like comparing prices or checking warranty terms. The feature uses a screenshot of the visible page for context.

This works without sharing your full screen with Gemini Live. Reports confirm it is active in Google app version 17.78.13 and is part of a server-side rollout. It has been tested on Amazon, Flipkart, and quick-commerce apps such as Blinkit.

![Person holding a smartphone](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1556656793-08538906a9f8?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What the Gemini shopping overlay does

The overlay appears when you long-press the power button or use your usual Gemini gesture while a shopping app is in the foreground. Suggested prompts appear, such as “Help me pick,” “Compare prices,” or “Check warranty and returns.”

Tapping a prompt makes Gemini capture the current screen and combine it with the prompt text. For a price comparison it checks the visible listing against other retailers. Warranty checks pull return and coverage details based on what is shown.

Conversations are saved in your normal Gemini history so you can review them later. Context is limited to the visible portion of the screen; scrolling content is not included automatically.

This builds on earlier screen-context capabilities that Gemini has offered for years. The difference is the shopping-specific suggestions that appear automatically on product pages.

## How to open the overlay and use the prompts

1. Update the Google app on your Android phone to the latest version from the Play Store.
2. Open a shopping app and navigate to a product page.
3. Long-press the power button (or use your configured Gemini gesture) to open the floating overlay.
4. Look for the new shopping suggestions.
5. Tap one, or type your own request such as “Compare this price with other stores.”

If the suggestions do not appear, the server-side rollout may not have reached your account yet. Force-stop the Google app and try again later.

The overlay stays on top of the shopping app so you can continue browsing while Gemini works.

## Comparing prices across stores

When you choose the compare-prices prompt, Gemini reads the product details visible on screen and looks for matching offers elsewhere. It can surface listings from major retailers and highlight differences in price, shipping, or availability.

Ask follow-up questions in the same conversation. Examples include “Show me the lowest price including shipping” or “Is this the same model at the other stores?”

Because the feature relies on the visible screen, make sure the product name, price, and key specs are on screen before you trigger the prompt. Results appear in the chat and can be revisited from your history.

This is especially useful during large sales periods when prices change quickly across sites.

## Checking warranties and returns

The warranty and returns prompt asks Gemini to examine the policy information visible on the product page. It can summarize coverage length, return windows, and any restocking fees shown.

You can refine the request: “Does the warranty cover accidental damage?” or “How long do I have to return this?”

As with price comparisons, Gemini only sees what is currently displayed. If the full policy is behind a link or further down the page, you may need to open that section first or ask Gemini to search for the official policy separately.

These checks appear in your Gemini chat history alongside the original screen context.

## Tips for better results

- Update the Google app and restart it if prompts are missing.
- Position the product details clearly on screen before opening the overlay.
- Use specific follow-ups instead of broad questions.
- Review the conversation later in Gemini history if you need the comparison again.
- Pair this with other Gemini shopping tools such as Tasks for multi-step purchases. See our guide on [using free Gemini Tasks for daily assistance](/blog/how-to-use-free-gemini-tasks-for-daily-assistance/).

The feature does not require a paid Gemini plan. It uses the same screen-context technology already available in the app.

## Limitations to know

Context is restricted to the visible screen area. Information below the fold or on other pages is not captured automatically.

Availability is still rolling out server-side, so not every account sees the prompts immediately even with the updated Google app.

Gemini’s answers depend on the accuracy of the on-screen text and the information it can retrieve. Always verify important details such as final price and return policies on the merchant’s site before buying.
