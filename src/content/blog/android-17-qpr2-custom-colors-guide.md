Android 17 QPR2 arrived with the November Pixel Drop and gives Pixel owners the Material You control they have wanted for years. You can now pick any accent color with a rainbow hue slider and then choose how intense that color appears across the system.

This guide shows exactly how to enable and customize those options on supported devices, starting with the Pixel 6a. The same controls also improve how wallpapers interact with your theme.

## Check That You Have Android 17 QPR2

Open Settings and go to System > Software updates. Tap Check for update. If the November Pixel Drop is available, install it and restart. The custom color tools live inside Wallpaper & style and appear only after this update.

Supported phones include the Pixel 6a and every later Pixel model, plus the Pixel Fold and Pixel Tablet. Older devices will not receive these controls.

## Open the Colors Menu

Long-press an empty area on your home screen. Tap Wallpaper & style. Then select Colors. You will see the familiar carousel of wallpaper-based palettes plus two new icons.

The palette icon sits in the top-right corner of the color picker area. The pencil icon appears on the currently selected swatch. Both are required for full customization.

## Use the Rainbow Hue Slider

Tap the palette icon. A rainbow hue slider opens labeled “Select a color.” Drag the slider until the preview matches the accent you want. The system updates the primary color, secondary accents, and blurred UI elements in real time.

Confirm the color. It replaces the previous wallpaper-derived accent and becomes the base for all Material You surfaces, including Quick Settings, notifications, and app icons that support dynamic color.

You can return to wallpaper colors at any time by selecting “Your wallpaper colors” in the carousel. The custom hue stays available so you can switch back without losing the choice.

## Choose an Intensity Style

With a color selected, tap the pencil icon on the swatch. Four intensity styles appear:

- Neutral keeps tones muted and close to gray.
- Soft applies subtle color without strong saturation.
- Bright increases vibrance across buttons and accents.
- Bold mixes stronger contrast and deeper shades.

Select one and watch the home-screen preview update. Tap the preview card itself if the automatic cycling starts; this pauses the loop so you can compare styles side by side.

The style affects the entire palette generated from your chosen hue. Bold works well for high-contrast environments, while Neutral suits minimal interfaces.

## Pair Colors with Adaptive Wallpapers

Android 17 QPR2 also adds platform support for wallpapers that include separate light and dark variants. Compatible collections automatically switch when you change the system theme. The new color tools still apply on top of either variant, so your chosen hue and intensity remain consistent.

Browse the Wallpaper section in the same menu to test these collections. The combination of custom color and adaptive wallpaper keeps the interface readable in both light and dark modes.

## Tips for Best Results

Start with a wallpaper that already contains the hue you like, then refine it with the slider. Extreme hues can reduce text contrast; test Bright and Bold on both light and dark themes before settling. If an app forces its own colors, the system theme still controls most system surfaces.

You can also combine these appearance changes with other Android 17 security tools. For example, the new App Lock feature lets you protect sensitive apps after you finish personalizing the look. See our guide on [how to lock apps on Pixel with Android 17 App Lock](/blog/android-17-app-lock-pixel/).

Reset anytime by choosing a wallpaper-based palette and tapping the home-screen preview to resume automatic cycling. Changes take effect immediately and survive reboots.

## Conclusion

Android 17 QPR2 finally lets Pixel users define their own Material You accent instead of accepting only the colors Google extracts from a wallpaper. The rainbow hue slider plus the four intensity styles give precise control while remaining simple to reach from the home screen.

Install the November Pixel Drop, open Wallpaper & style > Colors, and adjust the palette and pencil icons until the interface matches your preference. The same update also brings adaptive wallpapers and stronger platform support for custom theming across the system.
