Google’s November 2026 Pixel Drop brings Create My Widget to Pixel 10 and 11 series phones. This Gemini Intelligence feature lets you describe a widget in plain English and place a custom one on your home screen in seconds. No coding required.

It works on Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, Pixel 10 Pro Fold, and the full Pixel 11 lineup. The Pixel 10a is excluded. Availability is limited to English in Australia, Canada, India, the United Kingdom, and the United States for users aged 18 and over.

## What Create My Widget Does

Create My Widget uses Gemini to generate Material You styled widgets from a text prompt or a ready-made template. Examples include a monthly spending tracker, a sports game day widget in your team colors, or a vacation countdown that pulls flight and reservation details.

You can connect Google apps such as Gmail and Calendar so the widget shows personal information. In the UK, Connected Apps integration is not available. Always review the generated content for accuracy before adding it to your home screen.

## Requirements Before You Start

Confirm your phone meets these conditions:

- Pixel 10 or newer (except Pixel 10a) or any Pixel 11 model
- English language set on the device
- Located in Australia, Canada, India, the UK, or the US
- Age 18 or older
- Latest Pixel Drop update installed
- Gemini app up to date from the Play Store

If the feature is not visible yet, join the Create My Widget beta on the Play Store. Open the listing, tap Join the beta, wait a few minutes, then update the app.

## How to Create a Widget from a Prompt

1. Long-press an empty area on your home screen.
2. Tap Widgets, then tap Create in the top corner.
3. Choose whether to use connected Google apps or web data and location only.
4. Tap Describe your widget.
5. Type a clear request. Examples: “Show me my monthly shopping spend” or “Countdown to my vacation on July 15.”
6. Tap Send.
7. Review the preview. Tap Edit if you want changes, or Create new to start over.
8. Tap + Add to place the widget on your home screen.

The widget resizes and moves like any other Android widget. Grant any requested permissions so it can access the data it needs.

## How to Create a Widget from a Template

Templates save time when you know the category but not the exact wording.

1. Long-press the home screen and open Widgets > Create.
2. Select a category such as Important dates, Sports & fitness, or Finance.
3. Pick a suggestion or customize the prompt.
4. Review the preview and tap Add.

You can edit any widget later by long-pressing it on the home screen and choosing Edit.

## Tips for Better Results

Be specific in your prompt. Include the data source, time period, and visual style you want. “Monthly grocery spend from Gmail in green” produces clearer results than “spending widget.”

Check the preview carefully. Gemini can misread dates or numbers. Edit the prompt and regenerate if the layout or data looks wrong.

Start with public data if you prefer not to connect personal apps. Weather, sports scores, and stock trackers work without Gmail or Calendar access.

If the update has not reached your device, the Play Store beta method often unlocks the feature sooner. The full rollout continues over several weeks.

## Conclusion

Create My Widget turns a short description into a useful home screen tool. On supported Pixel 10 and 11 phones in eligible regions, it takes under a minute to set up. Experiment with prompts, review the output, and keep only the widgets that save you time.

The feature expands over the coming weeks and is planned for Pixel Watch 4 and 5 later. Keep your system and Gemini app updated to receive it as soon as it arrives on your device.
