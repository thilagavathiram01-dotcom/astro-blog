Google expanded Gemini task automation to the UK, India and Japan on 10 November 2026. Pixel 10 and 11 series owners aged 18 and older can now ask Gemini to order food, book rides or make reservations through supported apps.

The feature runs inside a virtual phone window so you stay in control. You confirm the final order or booking yourself. This guide covers setup, supported apps, daily limits and practical tips for the new regions.

## Check eligibility and update your phone

Task automation is available on Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, Pixel 10 Pro Fold, and the full Pixel 11 series. It also works on select Samsung Galaxy S26 devices and the upcoming motorola signature 27.

You need Android with the latest Gemini app. Open the Play Store and update Gemini and the Google app. Sign in with a Google account that is 18 or older. The feature is still in beta, so availability can vary by account even inside the three countries.

If you do not see the option, wait a few days. Google said the rollout continues over the coming weeks. For the full original multi-step overview, see our earlier guide on [automating multi-step tasks with Gemini on Android](/blog/gemini-intelligence-multistep-android/).

## How task automation works

You open the Gemini overlay by long-pressing the power button or saying “Hey Google.” State what you want in plain language. Gemini opens the right app inside a virtual window on your phone and taps through the steps.

You watch progress in a live notification. You can pause, jump in, or stop the task at any time. Gemini stops before the final payment or confirmation so you can review the details and approve them yourself.

Google lists food delivery, grocery, rideshare and restaurant reservation apps as the main categories. Exact partners differ by country. In India you will see local services alongside global ones such as Uber and Swiggy-style delivery apps. The same pattern holds in the UK and Japan.

## Start your first automated task

1. Open the food or ride app once and confirm you are signed in with a saved address and payment method.
2. Return to the home screen.
3. Long-press the power button to open Gemini.
4. Say a clear request such as “Order my usual coffee from the last café” or “Book a ride to the airport at 7 pm.”
5. Watch the notification. Gemini shows it is working in the virtual window.
6. When the notification asks you to finish, open the app, check the cart or trip, and confirm.

Keep the first request small. One meal or one short ride is easier than a multi-stop plan. Name the app if you use more than one rideshare service.

## Daily limits and subscription tiers

Free Gemini users receive a limited number of automation requests each day, typically around five. Higher tiers raise the limit. AI Plus, Pro and Ultra accounts can run more tasks.

Limits reset daily and can change as Google adjusts the beta. If you hit the cap, the overlay tells you to try again later or upgrade. Check your current allowance inside the Gemini app settings under task automation or screen automation.

You remain responsible for every order. Review the virtual window before you approve payment. A wrong address or size is still your order.

## Tips for India, UK and Japan

Save your home and work addresses inside the ride and delivery apps before you start. Gemini cannot invent a pin you never stored.

Speak the brand name when several apps exist. “Book an Uber” is clearer than “get me a ride.”

In India the feature supports both global and local delivery partners. Test with a small order first so you can see how the virtual window handles local payment methods.

In the UK and Japan the same virtual-window flow applies. Language support follows the Gemini Live languages already available in each country. Switch your Gemini language if the overlay replies in the wrong one.

Keep the phone unlocked until the first notification appears. Some carriers pause overlays on a locked screen.

After a successful run, open the real app and verify the receipt. The notification is progress, not the final record.

## Common problems and fixes

If Gemini only searches the web, update the Gemini app and confirm it is the default assistant. Long-press power should open the overlay, not Google Search.

If the virtual window opens the wrong app, stop the task and retry with the app name in the first sentence.

Missing the feature after the 10 November announcement usually means the account flag has not arrived yet. Wait 24–48 hours and try again on Wi-Fi.

Do not type card numbers or one-time codes into the Gemini prompt. Finish sensitive fields yourself after you take control of the virtual window.

## Conclusion

Gemini task automation now covers India, the UK and Japan on supported Pixel and Galaxy phones. Long-press power, state a clear food or ride request, watch the notification, and confirm the final step yourself.

Start with one small order today. Update the Gemini app, save your addresses, and test a single request. Official limits and partner lists continue to expand, so check Google’s support page if a specific app is missing.
