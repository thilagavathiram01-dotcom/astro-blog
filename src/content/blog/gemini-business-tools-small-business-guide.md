Small business owners juggle marketing, finances, and customer replies every day. Google released new Gemini tools on October 20, 2026, that group these tasks in one place. A business landing page, retained brand styles, and financial graphics now appear in the Gemini app for all users.

Tens of millions of owners already ask Gemini for product ideas and feedback summaries. The update reduces the time spent switching between apps. You define your visual style once and connect the services that already hold your data. This guide walks through setup and daily use so the features save real hours.

## Access the Business Landing Page

Open the Gemini app on the web, Android, or iOS and install any pending update. Google started the global rollout in the days following the October 20 announcement. The landing page may take a short time to appear on every account.

The page acts as your central hub. From it you build brand assets, review finances, and check customer insights. Google built the interface so owners spend less time on admin and more on the work that created the business.

![Small business owner reviewing work on laptop](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1743865318654-5586c91f5d3c?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

Connect the tools you already run. Google names Google Business Profile, Google Ads, and Google Workspace. Third-party connections include Adobe, Canva, Dropbox, Shopify, Wix, Zoho, and Xero. Begin with the accounts that contain this month's orders or reviews. You can add the rest later without starting over.

## Set Up Your Brand Style Once

Upload your website address, brand kit files, font samples, or color palette. Gemini stores the visual identity. Future image requests reuse the same style without new uploads. The system pairs the stored style with Nano Banana 2.1, Google's current image generation model.

Request product photos or campaign graphics. The results stay consistent with the colors and type you provided. Owners who publish social posts several times a week avoid repeating brand guidelines in every prompt.

Try a first request after the upload: "Generate three square product images for my new item using the brand colors and fonts I just saved." Inspect the files. Add a short follow-up note if a color needs adjustment. Subsequent requests keep the same look.

## Turn Records into Financial Insights

Drop in a spreadsheet, photos of paper receipts, or an export of purchase records. Gemini turns the raw numbers into charts and interactive models within seconds. You read a plain summary instead of scanning columns.

Owners who keep sales in Google Sheets or pull order data from Shopify can ask for a month-over-month comparison. The tool flags rising costs or top sellers without manual formatting. A ceramicist in Bordeaux told Google she saves two to three hours each day on writing product descriptions and newsletters. She uses the recovered time to make tableware.

![Person working at laptop in office setting](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1681184025442-1517cb9319c1?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

Begin with one document. Upload last month's sales sheet and type: "Show revenue, top three products, and any expense increases as a simple bar chart. Note anything that looks unusual." Compare the chart against your original file before you change pricing or inventory.

## Connect Apps and Review Customer Data

Link your Google Business Profile to surface search impressions, direction requests, and review patterns. Ask Gemini to draft replies that match your usual tone and mention the exact points a customer raised. The same link works with Google Ads when you want a quick campaign summary.

Storefront owners can connect Shopify or Wix for order trends. Accounting users link Xero or Zoho so purchase records feed the financial views. Grant access only to the accounts you will check this week. You can revoke connections later from the same settings area.

A single-location restaurant owner in New Jersey connects his Business Profile between shifts. He checks local Maps performance and writes weekend posts that mention nearby events. The connection takes a few minutes the first time and then stays available.

## Practical Tips for Daily Use

Write short, specific prompts. Name the saved brand style or the connected app so Gemini pulls the correct data. Always review images and financial charts before you post them or share them with staff.

Teams that need centralized admin controls and data governance should look at Gemini for business on Google Cloud. The app tools remain free for all Gemini users while the rollout continues. Return to the app if the landing page has not shown up yet.

Developers who already call Gemini models from Android apps can use the same Google account. Our earlier guide covers [adding Gemini 4 Argon to Android apps](/blog/add-gemini-4-argon-android-firebase-ai-logic/) if you need the technical steps.
