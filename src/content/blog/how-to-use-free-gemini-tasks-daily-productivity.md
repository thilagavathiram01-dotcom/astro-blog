Gemini Tasks just became free for hundreds of millions of people. On November 17, 2026, Google rolled the feature out at no cost in the United States, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. It runs on the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model and lets you hand off longer jobs so Gemini works in the background while you do something else.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="217" />

If you have ever wished your AI assistant could research options, update a calendar, or compare products without you babysitting every step, Tasks is the tool for that. This guide walks through exactly how to start, what it can do today, and how Google keeps you in control.

## What Gemini Tasks actually does

Tasks is not the same as regular Gemini Chat. Chat is for quick back-and-forth. Tasks is for jobs that take longer or need to run on a schedule. You flip a toggle, describe the outcome you want, and Gemini plans and executes the steps in a dedicated environment.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="217" />

Google designed it for everyday busywork: sorting school emails into calendar events, comparing product options, checking availability, or monitoring prices. In the United States, eligible users also get extra capabilities such as having Gemini call local businesses (with clear AI disclosure), complete purchases via Google Pay after your approval, forward emails to gemini@google.com, and text Gemini directly.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="217" />

The feature requires you to be 18 or older and signed in with a personal Google Account. History must be turned on. Work or school accounts are not supported for free Tasks in these markets.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="202" />

## How to start your first task

Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS, or go to gemini.google.com. Above the prompt box you will see a toggle between Chat and Task. Switch it to Task.

Describe the job in plain language. Be specific about the outcome, any budget limits, and what Gemini should not do. Examples that work well:

- “Compare the three cheapest USB-C chargers that support 65W and show me the one with the best reviews under $40.”
- “Look through my recent school emails and add every date to my calendar. Invite my partner to the ones that affect both of us.”
- “Find the next available service appointment at the two closest Honda dealers for early Friday morning in the next four weeks.”

Gemini confirms the plan, then works in the background. You can check progress, add more instructions, or stop the task at any time. Up to 15 tasks can run at once on the free tier.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="202" />

## What free users get versus paid plans

Free access covers the core Tasks experience in the six countries listed above. Paid Google AI Pro or Ultra plans add higher usage limits, more simultaneous schedules (50 instead of 10), the ability to set exact hourly timing, and Gmail/topic monitors.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="202" />

Calling businesses, Google Pay checkout inside a task, email forwarding, and texting Gemini are currently US-only and may have additional device or carrier requirements. Outside the six free countries, Tasks generally requires a paid plan except in markets where it is not offered at all (including the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and Nigeria).<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="202" />

## Privacy and control features

When you assign a task, Gemini runs it inside Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine. Each task gets a hardware-isolated virtual machine that is dedicated to your account and not shared with other users. The environment is sandboxed, and Google applies prompt-injection filters plus Safe Browsing checks on links.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="217" />

Gemini is designed to pause and ask for confirmation before critical actions such as purchases or form submissions. You can set a hard spending cap. For routine steps you trust (for example, adding an item to a grocery cart), you can mark them “Always allow” so the agent does not interrupt you every time.

You can disconnect apps, adjust task settings, or delete history at any time. Google also invites researchers to report issues through its AI Vulnerability Reward Program.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="217" />

## Practical tips for better results

1. Start with narrow, well-defined jobs. “Find three options and summarize the trade-offs” usually works better than “plan my entire vacation.”
2. Include constraints up front: budget, preferred time window, or brands to avoid.
3. For recurring needs, set a schedule. Free users can run schedules once a day in a rough window; paid users get tighter timing.
4. Give feedback with the thumbs-down button when a result misses the mark. Google uses this to improve the system.
5. If you also use Gemini on Android for app-level actions, check our earlier guide on [adding Gemini 4 Hydrogen to Android apps with Firebase AI Logic](/blog/add-gemini-4-hydrogen-android-firebase-ai-logic/).

Tasks is still rolling out, so not every eligible user sees the toggle on day one. If it is missing, wait a few days or check that your account meets the age and region requirements.
