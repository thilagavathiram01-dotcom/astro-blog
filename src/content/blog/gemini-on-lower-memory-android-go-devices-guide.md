Google expanded the Gemini app to lower-memory Android devices on 18 November 2026. Eligible Android (Go edition) phones with 2GB of RAM or more can now run the core Gemini experience after Google re-architected the app for constrained hardware.

This change reaches hundreds of millions of people who rely on budget Android phones for internet access, education, and daily tasks. Previously, these devices were limited to basic voice commands through Assistant Go. The updated experience replaces that with conversational Gemini.

![Person holding a smartphone showing AI assistant apps including Gemini](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1712002641088-9d76f9080889?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Check Device Eligibility First

Confirm your phone meets the requirements before installing. Android (Go edition) devices need at least 2GB of RAM. Sign in with a personal Google Account, or a work or school account that includes Gemini Apps access. The feature works in all languages and countries where Gemini is available.

On these devices, Gemini runs through the Google Search app or as a downloadable Gemini app from the Play Store. Newer partner devices from brands like Transsion may include it preloaded. If your phone has less than 2GB of RAM, it does not qualify under the current support documentation.

## How to Install and Update

Follow these steps to get the latest version running.

1. Open the Google Play Store on your Android Go device.
2. Search for "Google" or "Google Search".
3. On the app page, tap Update if available. If it shows Open, you already have the latest version.
4. Alternatively, search for and install the Gemini app directly from the Play Store.

After updating, sign in with your eligible Google Account. The app is designed to run efficiently on lower storage and memory, so clear unused apps or cache if you notice slowdowns during initial setup.

## Start a Conversation

You cannot use the "Hey Google" wake word or "Ask about this screen" on Android Go devices. Instead, launch Gemini with these methods:

- Touch and hold the Home button.
- Press and hold the Power button on supported devices.

This opens the conversational interface. Speak or type your request. Gemini Live supports natural voice conversations in more than 45 languages, letting you brainstorm, practice interviews, or discuss ideas aloud.

## What You Can Do with Gemini on These Phones

The re-architected app preserves core capabilities while fitting within memory limits. Official support documentation lists these practical uses:

- Call or text contacts by voice.
- Set alarms or create calendar events.
- Play music by song name, mood, or activity.
- Point the camera at notes, forms, or signs for translation or step-by-step help.
- Create images with Nano Banana.
- Upload documents, photos, or files for added context in chats.
- Ask complex local questions, such as finding a restaurant open for lunch with nearby charging options.

These features run by sending requests to Google's cloud models, so the phone handles interface, speech, and camera input without loading large models locally.

## Known Limits on Android Go

Hardware constraints mean some features available on higher-end phones are not supported. "Hey Google" activation is unavailable. Screen context queries like "Ask about this screen" do not work. Advanced on-device features that rely on Gemini Nano may also be limited or absent, depending on the specific device.

Performance can vary. Close background apps before starting long conversations or camera sessions to reduce memory pressure. Storage cleanup helps if the device has limited free space.

## Tips for Smoother Use

Keep the Google Search app updated through the Play Store. Sign out and back in if the interface does not appear after the update. For camera features, ensure good lighting and a stable connection. Test simple requests first, such as setting a timer or playing a song, before trying multi-step tasks.

If you use other Google services, connect relevant apps where available. This adds context without requiring extra local processing. ## Conclusion

Gemini on lower-memory Android Go devices brings a full conversational assistant to phones that previously offered only basic voice commands. Setup takes a few minutes through the Play Store or Google Search app. Core tools for communication, planning, media, camera help, and image creation are available, with clear limits around wake-word activation and screen context.

Test it on your eligible device and start with everyday requests. The update expands access without requiring a hardware upgrade. Check official Google support pages for the latest eligibility details as the rollout continues.
