Gemini Tasks (formerly Spark) is now free for eligible users in six countries. You flip a toggle from Chat to Task, describe a multi-step job, and Gemini works in the background under your direction. It can research options, call businesses, help with shopping, schedule follow-ups, and connect to apps you already use.

This guide follows Google’s November 17, 2026 announcement and current Gemini Apps behavior. Availability is rolling out gradually.

## Who can use free Gemini Tasks

Free access requires:

- Age 18 or older
- A personal Google Account (work and school accounts are not supported)
- Gemini Apps history turned on
- Residence in the United States, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia, or India

Elsewhere, Tasks still needs Google AI Pro or Ultra where Gemini Apps are available. It is not offered in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, or Nigeria.

Tasks runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen. You can keep up to 15 tasks running at once. Free schedules are more limited than paid ones (roughly once-a-day windows versus hourly precision on higher plans).

## Turn on Task mode

1. Update the Gemini app or open gemini.google.com.
2. Sign in with your personal Google Account.
3. Locate the Chat / Task toggle. On mobile it sits above the prompt box. On the web it sits below it.
4. Switch to Task.
5. Describe the job with clear details: dates, budget, preferred stores, and anything to avoid.
6. Answer follow-up questions and approve steps when Gemini pauses.

If the toggle is missing, the rollout has not reached your account yet. Check again after updating the app.

Starting in the US you can also text Gemini or forward emails to gemini@google.com. To enable texting:

1. Open Gemini → Settings → Channels.
2. Tap Set up under Text with Gemini.
3. Verify your US phone number on an eligible carrier.

## What Tasks can actually do

Chat answers questions. Task mode hands off a goal and keeps working.

Official examples include:

- Calling nearby dealerships, checking calendar availability, and booking a service appointment (US only; Gemini discloses it is an AI; some states are excluded).
- Analyzing past utility bills from email or a portal to find peak-pricing patterns.
- Comparing products via Google’s Shopping Graph, asking clarifying questions, and checking out with Google Pay while staying inside a spending cap you set.
- Adding items to an Instacart order from text or a photo of an empty box.
- Forwarding school emails so dates land on your calendar and conflicts are flagged.

Gemini connects with more than 60 apps. High-stakes actions (purchases, form submissions, sending messages) are designed to pause for confirmation. You can set “Always allow” only for routines you already trust.

## Safety and controls

Tasks run inside Google Cloud’s hardware-isolated Agent Compute Engine microVMs dedicated to your account. Layers include prompt-injection filters, Google Safe Browsing link rules, and an Action Safety Agent that evaluates proposed steps.

You can jump in at any time, stop a task from the text box, adjust Connected Apps, set a hard spending limit, or delete history. Receipts and tracking go to Google Wallet. Gemini is designed not to move money or share private payment details unless you approve.

Review results before relying on them. Sites are still adapting to personal assistants; Gemini may complete most of a flow and hand the final click back to you.

## Practical first tasks

Start with low-risk jobs that do not spend money:

- “Compare three USB-C chargers under $30 with good recent reviews and list pros/cons. Do not purchase.”
- “Scan last week’s Gmail for school or calendar-related emails and propose calendar events. Do not send any mail.”
- “Every Monday morning, give me a short recap of open tasks from my connected apps. Do not take external actions.”

You can turn a one-off task into a schedule inside the thread or from the Schedules page. Free users get fewer active schedules and coarser timing than Pro/Ultra.

For deeper agent patterns that predate the free rollout, see our earlier guide on [Gemini Spark as a 24/7 personal agent](/blog/gemini-spark-personal-agent/).

## Tips

- Write the goal, not a vague question. Include constraints (budget, dates, “do not book”).
- Watch the progress view the first few times.
- Enable only the Connected Apps the task needs.
- Set a spending cap before any shopping task.
- Provide feedback with a thumbs-down when a task misses the mark; Google uses this to improve reliability.
- Privacy controls for chat history and model training continue to roll out separately—review them in Gemini Settings.

## Conclusion

Free Gemini Tasks lets eligible users hand off multi-step work without a paid plan. Flip the toggle, describe a clear job, and supervise the critical steps. Start small, confirm results, and expand once you trust the workflow. Official starting point remains gemini.google.com or the Gemini app with the Chat/Task toggle.
