Gemini 4 Hydrogen arrived on 12 November 2026 and is now available to every user in the Gemini app. Google built it for speed and everyday work, so you can get clearer writing, faster research, and more reliable tool use without waiting on a frontier model.

This guide shows how to select Hydrogen, pick the right thinking level, and apply it to common tasks. All details come from Google’s official announcement.

## What Gemini 4 Hydrogen offers in the app

Hydrogen is the fast member of the Gemini 4 family. It handles writing, summaries, comparisons, and multi-step tasks more tightly than earlier Flash models. Google notes it follows instructions more closely, calls the right tools more often, and returns ready-to-use drafts on the first try.

In the Gemini app it appears in the model selector. Free users receive it when the system decides it fits the prompt. Paid Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers can choose it directly. It supports a 1 million token context window, so long documents and chat histories stay in play.

## How to select Gemini 4 Hydrogen

1. Open the Gemini app on your phone or go to gemini.google.com on the web.
2. Start a new chat or open an existing one.
3. Tap the model name at the top of the chat (it may show Auto or the previous model).
4. Choose Gemini 4 Hydrogen from the list. On free accounts it may appear under Auto; on paid accounts it is listed separately.
5. Confirm the selection. The next message will use Hydrogen.

If Hydrogen does not appear yet, check for an app update and restart the app. Rollout is global but can take a short time to reach every account.

## Choose a thinking level

Hydrogen offers four thinking levels: low, medium, high (the default), and xhigh. Higher levels give the model more time to reason before answering. Google says the progression is smooth, so you only wait and pay for the depth a task needs.

- Low: quick answers, simple rewrites, short summaries.
- Medium: balanced speed and quality for most daily requests.
- High: default for writing that must match your tone and format, or for multi-part questions.
- xhigh: longest reasoning for complex comparisons or dense material.

In the app, open the model selector again and look for the thinking level control. Change it per chat if needed. Start with high for important work, then drop to medium or low once you know the quality is enough.

## Practical ways to use it

### Draft and polish writing

Ask for an email, study guide, or rewrite and state the tone and length. Hydrogen sticks closely to those rules. Example: “Rewrite this paragraph in a professional but friendly tone under 150 words. Keep the three key points.” Review the result and request one specific change if needed.

### Research and comparisons

Upload a PDF or long note and ask for a table or visual breakdown. Hydrogen pulls relevant details into clear tables without dropping points. For product comparisons, list the criteria first so the model addresses every column.

### Everyday tasks and tool use

Hydrogen breaks goals into steps and calls connected apps more reliably. Tell it what you need: “Check my calendar for tomorrow and draft a reminder message for the team meeting.” It can also work with Gmail, Drive, and other connected apps when you have given permission.

### Quick prototypes

Describe a simple interactive idea. Hydrogen generates clean layouts in fewer rounds. Start with “Build a single-page HTML calculator that shows history” and then ask for one visual tweak.

For developers who want the same model in AI Studio or via the API, see our [guide to Gemini 4 Hydrogen in Google AI Studio](/blog/get-started-gemini-4-hydrogen-google-ai-studio/).

## Tips for better results

- State the exact format and any constraints in the first message.
- Use high or xhigh only when the task is multi-part or dense. Lower levels finish faster.
- Upload files early so the full context is available.
- After the first reply, ask for one focused revision instead of rewriting the whole prompt.
- Check connected apps under settings if tool calls fail.

Free users may see Auto select Hydrogen for harder prompts. Paid users get direct control and higher limits.
