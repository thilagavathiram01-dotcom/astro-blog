Google has updated the Gemini app with new controls that separate chat history from generative AI model training. You can now keep your conversations saved and still opt out of having future chats used to train models.

This change addresses a previous limitation where the single Keep Activity setting controlled both storage and training. The update rolls out globally over the coming weeks. This guide walks you through the settings based on Google's official announcement.

![Person working on a laptop with smartphone nearby](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1516321318423-f06f85e504b3?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What Changed in Gemini Privacy Controls

Previously, turning off activity meant losing easy access to past chats if you wanted to stop training. Google now splits this into separate options.

You can keep history enabled while disabling training on chat content. You retain the ability to set retention periods and manually delete items at any time.

If you turn the new History setting off, Google saves future chats for around two months. These are used to respond to you, process feedback, and protect Google, its users, and the public.

The rollout starts in the coming weeks. Check your settings after the update appears.

## How to Access the New Settings

Open the Gemini app or go to gemini.google.com. Sign in with your Google Account.

Tap or click Settings. Look under Data & privacy or History for the updated controls. The exact labels may appear as History and a separate option for chat content including text and uploads.

On mobile, the path is often Settings then History. On the web, use Settings & help then Activity or History.

If you still see the old Keep Activity switch, the update has not reached your account yet. Wait and check again later.

## Step-by-Step: Keep History but Opt Out of Training

Follow these steps once the new controls appear:

1. Open Gemini and go to Settings.
2. Select History or the equivalent privacy section.
3. Ensure History is turned on if you want saved chats. Default auto-delete is often 18 months; you can change this to 3 months, 36 months, or never.
4. Find the option for chat content including text and uploads (or similar wording for attachments).
5. Uncheck or turn off the training option.
6. Confirm if prompted. A message should note that future chats will not train generative AI models unless you provide feedback.
7. Optionally review the separate control for audio and Gemini Live videos and screenshares.

Verify the changes by returning to the page. History stays on while training is off.

## What Happens When History Is Off

Turning History off hides chats from your list. Google still retains them for around two months for response quality, feedback processing, and safety.

This period is longer than the previous 72-hour window in some earlier documentation. Reviewed chats can be kept longer (up to three years in some cases) and are disconnected from your account.

Temporary chats follow similar retention when the setting is off.

You can still delete individual items or ranges when History is on. Manual deletion remains available.

## Tips for Better Privacy Management

- Review settings after the rollout completes for your account. Defaults may carry over from the old Keep Activity preference.
- Shorten the auto-delete period if you prefer less long-term storage.
- Avoid sending feedback on sensitive chats if you want to limit further use.
- For highly private questions, consider temporary chats where available.
- Check connected apps or Workspace accounts separately; admins may control settings for work or school profiles.

These controls apply to future interactions. Past data already used or reviewed is not automatically removed.

## Conclusion

The updated Gemini controls give clearer choices between keeping your chat history and contributing to model training. Check the settings once the rollout reaches you, keep History on if you want access to past conversations, and turn off the training option for future content.

You still control retention length and manual deletion. For the latest details, refer to Google's official post and Gemini help pages.
