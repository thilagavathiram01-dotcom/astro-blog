Google’s Intelligent Eyewear brings Gemini to everyday glasses. Designed with Warby Parker, Gentle Monster, and Samsung, the frames deliver hands-free help without a screen in the lenses. Pre-orders opened on 12 November 2026 in the United States and South Korea. Devices ship and hit stores on 10 December 2026.

This guide covers eligibility, pre-order steps, pairing, core features, and privacy controls. All details come from Google’s official announcement and support pages.

## What the glasses do

The frames weigh about 41 grams. Battery life reaches up to nine hours. A portable case recharges them roughly seven times. Open-ear speakers play audio while you stay aware of surroundings. A 12-megapixel ultra-wide camera captures photos and video. An indicator light shows when the camera is active.

Gemini answers spoken questions about what you see. It translates speech across more than 70 languages, pulls Maps directions, adds items to lists, and checks Calendar availability. Guided Vision provides audio descriptions for low-vision users. Aira integration streams the camera to a human interpreter when needed.

No visual overlay appears in the lenses. Interaction happens through voice or a touchpad on the frame.

## Check compatibility first

Supported phones are limited at launch:

- Pixel 7 series and newer running the Android 17 September 2026 update or later.
- Samsung Galaxy S, S FE, Z Fold, Z Flip, and Fold Ultra series on One UI 9.0 or higher.
- iPhone 13 Pro and newer with at least 6 GB RAM on iOS 26.6 or later.

You need a Google account registered in the United States or South Korea. The account holder must be 18 or older. Guided Vision and Aira require a compatible Android phone at launch.

If your phone falls outside these lists, the glasses will not pair.

## How to pre-order

Warby Parker styles (Alden, Reid, Wren) start at $395. Gentle Monster designs start at $449. Prescription and sun-lens options raise the price. Both partners offer in-store demos.

1. Confirm your phone and account meet the requirements above.
2. Visit warbyparker.com/intelligent-eyewear or gentlemonster.com.
3. Choose frame, size, and lenses. Add prescription details if needed.
4. Complete the order. Early-access pricing applies until 17 November 2026.
5. Wait for 10 December shipping and store availability.

Hands-on demos run at select Warby Parker, Gentle Monster, and Google Store locations before the official sale date.

## Pair the glasses

After the glasses arrive:

1. Charge the frames in the included case.
2. Install the Glasses companion app from the Play Store or App Store on your supported phone.
3. Open the app and follow the pairing prompts. Bluetooth connects the frames to the phone.
4. Sign in with your eligible Google account and complete age verification.
5. Grant microphone and camera permissions. Set up Gemini when prompted.

The glasses need an internet connection through the phone for full Gemini features. Offline use is limited.

## Use Gemini hands-free

Say “Hey Gemini” or long-press the outer touchpad to start a conversation. Speak naturally. Gemini processes what the camera sees and answers through the speakers.

Examples that work at launch:

- Point at an object and ask “What is this?”
- Request translation of nearby speech.
- Ask for turn-by-turn directions while walking.
- Tell Gemini to add an item you see to a grocery list.
- Request a Calendar check for an event on a poster.

To stop, tap and hold then swipe backward on the touchpad. Media controls use single taps and two-finger swipes for volume.

You can also capture photos or video by button or voice command. Photos save to your phone gallery.

## Privacy controls

Google designed the system so camera visuals used by Gemini are not saved to your Gemini History and are not used to train models or target ads. Faces of bystanders are redacted before processing. Data on the glasses is encrypted. You control app permissions and can review or delete chats.

An indicator light activates during photo or video capture. The glasses stop recording if the light is covered. An off switch lets you wear the frames as ordinary glasses.

Read the full privacy details on Google’s Safety Center page for glasses.

## Tips for daily use

- Keep the phone nearby and connected for the best results.
- Test Guided Vision in a familiar space before relying on it outdoors.
- Review generated answers, especially for directions or translations.
- Update the Glasses app and phone OS when updates appear.
- Store the frames in the case when not in use to protect the battery.

If you already use adaptive widgets across phone, watch, and car, the same habit of short, glanceable information applies here. See [How to Build Adaptive Glance Widgets for Phone, Wear, Auto](/blog/adaptive-glance-widgets-phone-wear-auto/) for related Android surface ideas.

## Conclusion

Intelligent Eyewear puts Gemini in frames you can wear all day. Pre-order now if your phone and account qualify, then follow the pairing steps once the glasses arrive on 10 December. Start with simple voice queries and adjust permissions as you learn the controls.

The system stays useful when you treat it as a hands-free helper rather than a full visual computer.
