Google rolled out Gemini 4 Argon to the Gemini app on October 13, 2026, along with a clear Chat and Task toggle. Tasks Mode lets the model keep working on multi-step jobs even after you put the phone down. On Android this means you can hand off inbox cleanup, research reports, or shopping prep without staying glued to the screen.

You need a Google AI Pro or Ultra plan, a personal Google Account, and Keep Activity turned on. Tasks Mode is not available with work or school accounts, and it is currently unavailable in the European Economic Area, Nigeria, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom. Personal Intelligence must also be opted in.

## What Tasks Mode actually does

The toggle sits above the prompt box. Chat is the normal conversation. Task turns the request into a background job. Gemini can pull from Connected Apps, skills, your chats, websites you are signed into, and Personal Intelligence. It reports progress more frequently on longer runs and can create files such as PDFs, Docs, or Sheets.

Limits exist. You can have up to 15 tasks running at once. Usage draws from your Gemini compute allowance, which scales with your subscription tier. Gemini will ask you to take over for certain browser actions, such as signing in or final payment.

## Requirements before you start

Confirm these items on your Android phone:

- Gemini app updated from the Play Store.
- Signed in with a personal Google Account.
- Google AI Pro or Ultra active.
- Keep Activity enabled in Gemini settings.
- Personal Intelligence opted in.
- Connected Apps you plan to use already linked (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, and third-party services such as Instacart or OpenTable).

If the Task button does not appear, the rollout has not reached your account yet. Check again later or force-close and reopen the app.

## Step-by-step: start a task

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Above the text box, tap **Task**. The prompt area updates to show you are starting a task.
3. Optionally tap the model menu at the top and select **Gemini 4 Argon**. Set the thinking level to medium or high for complex work.
4. Describe the goal with a clear finish line. Include what Gemini must not do without asking. Example: "Summarize unread newsletters in Gmail from the past week, draft an archive list, and do not unsubscribe from anything until I confirm."
5. Attach files if needed by tapping Upload & tools, then Upload files, Add from Drive, or Add Notebook.
6. Tap Submit.

Gemini begins the work and posts progress updates in the thread. You can leave the app. Check back later for status or any request for input.

You can also start a task from a Live session by saying "start a task" when Gemini does not detect the request automatically.

## Monitor progress and take over

Open the task thread from your chat list. A progress chip at the top shows Ready, Needs attention, or In progress. Tap it to review completed steps.

Gemini may use a remote browser for web actions. You can take control at any time. For sensitive steps it pauses and asks you to continue. Files it creates appear in the thread for download or edit.

The Scheduled page (available in the app) groups recurring work under Active, Paused, Completed, and Suggested. Filters on the main list help you find threads that need attention.

## Practical examples that work well

- Inbox management: "Review Gmail newsletters from the last three days. List those I have not opened and suggest which to unsubscribe. Do not change anything until I reply."
- Research package: "Research three affordable hotels in Lisbon for next month that allow free cancellation. Create a short comparison PDF with prices and links. Do not book."
- Shopping prep: "Using Instacart, find ingredients for a simple pasta dinner for four under $40. Show the cart. Do not place the order."
- File creation: "Turn the notes I just attached into a two-page presentation with three key slides and speaker notes."

Always end prompts with an explicit boundary so Gemini stops before irreversible actions.

## Tips for better results with Argon

Argon answers more directly and shares intermediate progress. Use medium or high thinking for research or multi-app jobs. Attach source files early so the model works from your data instead of guessing.

Connect only the apps you need. Review Task settings periodically and delete remote browser data if you want to clear session history. If a task stalls, open the thread and reply with the next instruction rather than starting a duplicate.

## Safety and limits

Tasks Mode is experimental. Supervise any job that touches accounts, payments, or personal data. Gemini can interact with sites you are logged into, so review permissions in Connected Apps. Turning off Task Mode deletes remote browser and code-execution data but leaves your existing threads and files intact.

Compute limits still apply. Complex tasks with Argon consume more of your allowance than simple chats. The app notifies you when you approach the limit.

## Conclusion

Tasks Mode on Android turns Gemini from a chat partner into a background assistant you can assign and check later. Combined with Gemini 4 Argon it handles longer research and cross-app sequences more reliably than earlier models. Start with small, bounded jobs, confirm the boundaries in every prompt, and expand once you see how the progress updates work.
