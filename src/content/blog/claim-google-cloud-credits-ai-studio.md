Google AI Pro and Ultra plans include monthly Google Cloud credits for Gemini API usage. Since November 13, 2026, you can claim and manage them directly inside Google AI Studio.

This removes the need to switch between sites. You create a Developer Program profile, set up billing, and apply credits in one flow. Credits cover paid-tier API calls beyond free quotas.

## Who qualifies for the credits

Only the primary subscriber on a Google AI Pro or Ultra plan can claim them. Family members cannot share the credits. Google AI Plus plans do not include them.

Amounts depend on your plan:

- Google AI Pro: $10 per month
- Google AI Ultra 5x: $40 per month
- Google AI Ultra 20x: $100 per month

Each monthly grant lasts 12 months. Unused credits stack. The earliest-expiring credits are spent first. You can enable auto-apply so future grants go to the same billing account.

Credits work for Gemini API usage in AI Studio, your own apps, and other eligible Google Cloud services. They do not cover consumer Gemini app features or Antigravity overages.

## Step-by-step claim process

Sign in to [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com) with the Google account that holds your Pro or Ultra subscription.

Look in the left navigation for an alert about unclaimed credits or a “Claim free API credits” card. Click it.

A single dialog opens. If you lack a Google Developer Program profile, AI Studio creates one automatically. Review the terms and continue.

Next, set up or link a Cloud billing account. Choose prepaid billing. Deposit at least $5. This minimum prepayment activates the paid tier so promotional credits can apply. Your prepaid balance is used only after credits are exhausted. Prepaid amounts are not refundable and expire after 12 months.

Select the billing account. Enable the option to auto-apply future monthly grants. Confirm. Paid-tier API keys become available immediately.

Track your balance and usage at [aistudio.google.com/billing](https://aistudio.google.com/billing). New grants arrive on the monthly anniversary of your first claim.

You can also claim via the Google Developer Program benefits page at me.developers.google.com/benefits if preferred. The AI Studio flow is faster for most users.

## Using your credits with the Gemini API

After claiming, generate or select an API key in AI Studio. The key is tied to your billed project. Use it in code with the Google GenAI SDK or REST.

Credits are drawn before your prepaid balance. Monitor usage to stay within limits. Set spend caps in the billing section to avoid unexpected charges once credits run out.

These credits pair well with building full-stack Android apps in AI Studio. See our guide on [building full-stack Android apps with Google AI Studio and Firebase](/blog/build-full-stack-android-apps-google-ai-studio-firebase/) for more on integrating backends.

## Tips for maximizing value

Claim early so unclaimed prior months stack if available. Upgrade your plan and the monthly amount increases automatically on the next grant.

Test with smaller models first to stretch credits. Higher-cost models like Gemini 4 Carbon consume credits faster. Check current pricing in AI Studio before heavy use.

Keep the $5 prepaid balance active. If it drops to zero, promotional credits may stop applying until you top up. Verify identity if prompted during billing setup.

Only the primary account holder can redeem. Double-check the signed-in account matches the plan owner.

## Common questions

**What if I already claimed credits before?** The new AI Studio flow still works. Unclaimed grants appear in the alert. Auto-apply handles future ones.

**Can I use credits for video generation or other features?** Yes, for eligible Gemini API calls including video models when available. Check the billing page for exact coverage.

**What happens if I cancel my AI plan?** Existing claimed credits remain valid until they expire. Future monthly grants stop.

**Is the $5 prepay required every month?** No. It is a one-time activation minimum. You may need to top up if the balance reaches zero and credits are exhausted.

## Conclusion

Claiming Google Cloud credits inside AI Studio takes a few minutes and unlocks paid Gemini API usage included with Pro and Ultra plans. Sign in, follow the left-nav alert, complete billing with the $5 prepay, and enable auto-apply.

Track usage regularly. Pair the credits with your development workflow to build and test without immediate extra cost. Official details stay current on the Google blog and AI Studio help pages.
