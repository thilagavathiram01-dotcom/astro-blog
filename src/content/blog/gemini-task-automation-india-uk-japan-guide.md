Google expanded Gemini task automation to the UK, India, and Japan in the November 2026 Pixel Drop and Gemini Intelligence update. You can now ask Gemini to order food, book rides, or handle reservations in select apps on supported Pixel and Samsung devices.

This feature, called screen automation, runs in a virtual environment on your phone. Gemini opens the app, navigates steps, and pauses before payment or confirmation so you stay in control.

![Person holding smartphone showing food delivery app](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1760888549280-4aef010720bd?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What is Gemini screen automation?

Screen automation is a beta feature in the Gemini mobile app on Android. It lets Gemini complete multi-step tasks inside allowlisted apps instead of just providing links or answers.

Official Google support describes it for everyday tasks such as booking rides, ordering food and groceries, buying tickets, or making reservations. It differs from Gemini Tasks (for scheduled workflows) and Connected Apps (for direct integrations).

The feature launched earlier in the US and South Korea on Galaxy S26 and Pixel 10 series. The November expansion brings it to three more markets.

## Eligible devices and requirements

You need a supported device, a personal Google Account, and must be 18 or older. Work, school, or supervised accounts do not qualify.

Supported devices include:

- Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, Pixel 10 Pro Fold
- Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, Pixel 11 Pro Fold
- Samsung Galaxy S26 series (S26, S26+, S26 Ultra)
- Galaxy Z Flip 8, Z Fold 8, Z Fold 8 Ultra
- Galaxy Tab S12 series
- Motorola Signature 27 (pre-order in the US)

Pixel 10 devices are not supported for this feature in South Korea. Availability rolls out gradually and requires English, Japanese, or Korean language support on the device.

You must grant the Google app permission for screen automation on each app the first time.

## Supported apps by country

Google does not publish a public list, but Android 17 system data reveals a target app allowlist. Apps must match official Play Store signing certificates. Regional apps appear only in their markets.

For India (nine apps):

- Zomato
- Blinkit
- Rapido
- BookMyShow
- ixigo
- McDelivery
- KFC India
- Burger King India
- Starbucks India

For the UK and Ireland:

- ASOS, Argos, British Airways, Burger King UK, Deliveroo, Depop, Just Eat, KFC UK & Ireland, McDonald’s UK, Ocado, Starbucks UK, Tesco, Trainline

For Japan:

- ANA, Burger King Japan, GO, KFC Japan, McDonald’s Japan, Peatix, Rakuten Ichiba, Rakuten Mart, Rakuten Ticket, Starbucks Japan, TimeTree, Yahoo! Shopping, Yahoo! Travel

Global apps such as Uber, Uber Eats, Booking.com, Airbnb, and McDonald’s also appear on the broader list. Competitors like Swiggy or Zepto in India are not allowlisted and Gemini refuses automation on them.

Not every listed app supports full automation yet. Productivity apps like Microsoft Word appear on the list but Gemini currently declines direct editing tasks.

## How to start a task

1. Open the Gemini app on your supported Android device or say “Hey Google.”
2. Describe the task clearly. Examples: “Book a ride to the airport on Rapido,” “Order my usual coffee from Starbucks India,” or “Add milk and eggs to my Blinkit cart.”
3. Grant screen automation permission if prompted the first time for that app.
4. Review the action plan Gemini creates. Reply with changes if needed.
5. Gemini works in the background in a virtual phone environment. Watch progress via the chat or notification.
6. Complete the final step yourself when Gemini pauses for confirmation, payment, or sign-in.

You can view progress, take control on some devices, or stop the task anytime from the chat or notification. On Pixel 10 and Galaxy S26, full take-control is limited; stop the task instead.

If you start an order but do not finish, items may stay in the cart.

## Safety and limits

Gemini can make mistakes. Do not use screen automation for emergencies or sensitive information. Never enter passwords or payment details into the Gemini chat. Enter them only after taking control in the app itself.

You remain responsible for any purchases or actions. Google notes safeguards such as confirmation prompts before final actions and restrictions to relevant apps, but monitoring is essential.

Daily request limits apply and vary by Google AI subscription tier. The feature is beta and rolls out gradually, so it may not appear immediately on every eligible device.

Screenshots from automation sessions can be reviewed to improve the service if Keep Activity is enabled.

## Tips for better results

Be specific with app names and details when possible. “Reorder my last order on Zomato” works better than a vague request.

Install the official Play Store version of the app. Sideloaded or Galaxy Store versions may not match certificates.

Check Gemini settings under Screen automation to manage permissions per app (Always allow, Ask every time, or Do not allow).

Combine with other Gemini Intelligence features such as Rambler for voice input or proactive suggestions. For more on Pixel 10 Gemini features, see our guide to [Gemini Intelligence on Pixel 10](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-intelligence-on-pixel-10/).

## Conclusion

Gemini task automation now covers practical daily tasks in India, the UK, and Japan on the latest Pixel and Galaxy flagships. Start with food orders or ride bookings on allowlisted apps, review every plan, and confirm the final step yourself.

The feature continues to expand as Google adds more apps and regions. Check the official Gemini help page for the latest supported devices and languages.
