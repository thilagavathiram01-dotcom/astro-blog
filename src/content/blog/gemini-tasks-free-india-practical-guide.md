Google expanded Tasks in the Gemini app free of charge on 17 November 2026 for eligible users in India, the United States, Japan, South Korea, Canada, and Australia. The feature, previously limited to paid Google AI plans under the name Spark, now runs on the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model for free accounts in those countries.

You describe a multi-step job once. Gemini plans the steps, works in the background using connected apps and the web, and notifies you when it finishes or needs input. You stay in control and approve sensitive actions.

This guide shows Indian users how to turn on Task mode, write clear requests, set schedules, and stay within free limits. Details come from Google’s official announcement and help pages.

## Requirements for Free Access in India

You need a personal Google Account (not a work or school account), age 18 or older, and History turned on in the Gemini app. Open the Gemini app or visit gemini.google.com. The Chat / Task toggle appears on the main screen once the rollout reaches your account.

Free access covers core Task mode, Workspace actions (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Keep where available), scheduled tasks, and web browsing. Calling businesses, texting Gemini, emailing gemini@google.com, and in-task Google Pay checkout remain US-only at launch. Usage limits still apply; paid plans receive higher quotas.

## How to Switch from Chat to Task

1. Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS, or go to gemini.google.com and sign in.
2. Look for the toggle below the prompt box. It shows Chat on one side and Task on the other.
3. Tap or click Task. The prompt placeholder changes to “Describe a task” and the model shows Hydrogen.
4. Type a clear description of the work you want finished. Include any limits, preferred times, or sources.
5. Submit. Gemini confirms the request and starts working in the background. A task thread appears in the sidebar.

You can add more instructions while it runs. Gemini updates its plan without losing progress. Pause or stop anytime from the task thread.

For Android users who already use power-button automation on supported Pixel or Galaxy devices, see the existing guide on [automating multi-step tasks with Gemini on Android](/blog/automate-multi-step-tasks-gemini-android/).

## Practical Examples That Work in India

Start small. Ask Gemini to turn a set of meeting notes into a checklist, compare flight options for a trip, or extract dates from school emails and suggest calendar entries. Connect Gmail and Calendar in Gemini settings first so it can read relevant messages and add events.

Recurring schedules work well for daily briefings. Tell Gemini: “Every weekday morning at 8 a.m., summarise unread emails about my project and list any calendar conflicts.” Manage active schedules from the Scheduled page in the sidebar.

Research tasks also fit free limits. Request a comparison of three local service providers with sources, or compile a packing list for an upcoming trip based on weather and activities. Gemini can produce downloadable files such as PDFs or Docs when asked.

## Tips for Better Results and Control

Write specific prompts. “Sort last week’s newsletters in Gmail and archive anything older than 30 days that is not from my bank” gives clearer direction than “clean my inbox.”

Set spending or action limits where relevant, even if checkout is not yet available in India. Review connected apps in settings and disconnect any you do not want used.

Monitor the first few tasks. Gemini pauses for confirmation on sensitive steps and asks clarifying questions when instructions are ambiguous. Provide thumbs-down feedback when a result misses the mark so the system improves.

Watch usage. Complex or long-running tasks consume more of the free quota. Paid Google AI Pro or Ultra plans raise limits and unlock Neon or Carbon models for harder work.

## Limits and What Stays Unavailable

Free Tasks in India cover planning, research, Workspace actions, and scheduled routines. Business calling requires a US number and eligible device. Text and email channels for starting tasks outside the app are rolling out in the US first. In-task purchases through Google Pay also start in the US with explicit approval and spending caps.

Tasks run in isolated virtual machines on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine. Google states this keeps each user’s work separate. You can review or delete history, disconnect apps, and turn Task mode off anytime in settings.

Rollout is gradual. If the toggle is missing, check for app updates and try again later. The feature requires History on and a personal account.

## Conclusion

Free Gemini Tasks give Indian users a practical way to hand off multi-step digital chores without a subscription. Open the app, flip the toggle to Task, describe the job, and let Hydrogen handle the background work while you stay in control.

Begin with reversible tasks such as inbox summaries or research compilations. Add schedules once you see reliable results. As Google expands advanced features beyond the US, the same Task interface will support more of the daily work that currently requires manual switching between apps.
