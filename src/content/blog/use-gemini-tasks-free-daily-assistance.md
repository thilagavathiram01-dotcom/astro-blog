Gemini Tasks turns the Gemini app from a chat partner into a background assistant that finishes multi-step jobs. You flip a toggle, describe the outcome, and Gemini works while you do other things. Google made the feature free for eligible users in six countries on 17 November 2026, powered by the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model.

This guide shows exactly how to start a task, what it can handle, and how to stay in control. All steps come from Google’s official announcement and help documentation.

## Who can use free Tasks

Tasks is free for people aged 18 or older with a personal Google Account in the United States, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. History must be turned on. Work or school accounts are not supported.

Advanced actions such as calling businesses, Google Pay checkout, texting, and email forwarding start in the United States. Calling is unavailable in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska. Texting requires a verified US phone number on an eligible carrier.

## Start your first task

Open the Gemini app or go to gemini.google.com. Sign in with your personal account. Look for the Chat / Task toggle on the home screen. Tap Task.

Describe the job in plain language. Include the goal, any limits, and preferred output. Examples that work well:

- “Call the two nearest Honda dealerships and book the earliest Friday morning service appointment in the next four weeks. Add it to my calendar.”
- “Compare USB-C chargers under 65 W, find a discount code, and prepare a Google Pay checkout with a $40 spending cap.”
- “Forwarded school emails: extract every date, add them to my family calendar, and flag any conflicts.”

Submit the request. Gemini creates a plan and works in the background. You can close the app. Progress appears in a work panel that lists completed, current, and upcoming steps.

You can add instructions while the task runs. Gemini updates its work without losing progress. To stop, tap the Stop button in the text box. You can run up to 15 tasks at once.

## What Tasks can do

Tasks handles longer projects that Chat mode stops after one answer. Real examples from Google’s November 2026 update include:

- Calling businesses to compare availability and book appointments. Gemini discloses it is an AI and never shares payment details unless you approve.
- Analyzing utility bills from email and Password Manager access to find the cause of a high charge.
- Shopping with Google’s Shopping Graph, asking clarifying questions, applying discount codes, and checking out via Google Pay using the Agent Payments Protocol.
- Adding grocery items from text or photo to connected apps such as Instacart and handing off for final confirmation.
- Extracting dates from forwarded emails and writing them to Google Calendar, then spotting conflicts.

Gemini pauses for confirmation before critical actions such as clicking Buy. You can set a hard spending cap. Receipts land in Google Wallet. For routine trusted actions you can choose “Always allow.”

## Text and email a task

In the United States you can start Tasks without opening the app.

For texting:

1. Open Gemini and tap Settings.
2. Tap Channels.
3. Tap Set up under Text with Gemini.
4. Verify your phone number.

Once verified, text Gemini from Google Messages or iMessage. You can attach photos and reply while the task is active.

For email, forward messages or PDFs to gemini@google.com. Gemini replies with a link to the new task. Useful for school calendars, medical bills, or insurance renewals.

## Security and control

Each task runs inside a hardware-isolated virtual machine on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine that is dedicated to your account. The environment is sandboxed and regularly tested. Additional layers include prompt-injection filters and Google Safe Browsing.

You control connected apps, task settings, and history. Disconnect any service or delete history at any time. Gemini asks clarifying questions when instructions are ambiguous and seeks approval before purchases.

## Tips for better results

- Write the finished outcome, not a question. “Book the earliest Friday service slot and add it to Calendar” outperforms “help with car service.”
- Set clear limits: budget, date range, “do not purchase,” or “ask me before calling.”
- Start with low-risk tasks (research, calendar extraction) before enabling payments or calls.
- Review the plan and approval screens. Confirm seller, total, and delivery details.
- Turn History on so Tasks can use prior context.

## Conclusion

Free Gemini Tasks gives eligible users in six countries a practical way to offload multi-step personal admin. Flip the toggle, describe the result, and stay in the loop for critical steps. Start with a simple calendar or research task today and expand as you see how the assistant behaves.
