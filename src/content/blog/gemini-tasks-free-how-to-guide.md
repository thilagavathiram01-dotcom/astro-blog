Google made Tasks in the Gemini app free for hundreds of millions of people in the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. Powered by Gemini 4 Hydrogen, Tasks turns the chatbot into an agent that finishes multi-step jobs while you do other things.

You flip a toggle, describe the work, and Gemini handles research, scheduling, calls, or purchases under your direction. It pauses for confirmation on sensitive steps and keeps your data in a dedicated sandbox.

## Switch from Chat to Task

Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS, or go to gemini.google.com. On the home screen you see a Chat and Task toggle. Chat answers questions. Task accepts a goal and works through it.

Select Task. The prompt box changes to “Describe a task.” Type or speak what you need. Examples from Google’s announcement include booking a car service appointment, analyzing an electric bill, or adding items to a grocery order.

Gemini shows its plan, then executes. You can jump in with more instructions at any time. It asks clarifying questions when the request is ambiguous and requires approval before buying anything.

## Hand off everyday work

Tasks works with your Google apps and connected services. In the official example, a user asked Gemini to call two dealerships and book the earliest Friday morning slot that fit the calendar. Gemini found the shops, made the calls (disclosing it is an AI), checked availability, and added the appointment.

Another case involved a surprise electric bill. Gemini used Google Password Manager to access the utility portal, reviewed past statements from email, and identified that a heat pump was running during peak pricing.

You can also text Gemini or forward emails to gemini@google.com (U.S. rollout). To set up texting:

1. Open Gemini and tap Settings.
2. Tap Channels.
3. Tap Set up under Text with Gemini.
4. Verify your phone number.

Gemini then adds grocery items from a photo, schedules family events from school emails, or compares products and finds discount codes.

For shopping, Gemini uses the Agent Payments Protocol with supported cards. You set a spending cap. It stages the order and waits for your one-tap approval. Receipts go to Google Wallet. You still earn card rewards.

## Safety and controls

Each task runs in a hardware-isolated virtual machine dedicated to your account. Google layers prompt-injection filters and Safe Browsing on top. Gemini does not share payment details unless you approve.

You can disconnect apps, adjust permissions, or delete history in settings. Give feedback with a thumbs-down when a task misses the mark. Google notes that some websites still treat agents cautiously, so Gemini may hand the final click back to you.

Calling businesses is not available in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska. Texting requires a U.S. phone number with an eligible carrier.

## Tips for better results

- Be specific about outcomes and constraints. “Book the earliest Friday morning service appointment at the two nearest Honda dealerships that fits my calendar” works better than “fix my car.”
- Connect the apps you want Gemini to use. Personal Intelligence helps it remember details like your vehicle or location.
- For recurring work, include timing. “Every weekday at 7 a.m., summarize my calendar and priority emails.”
- Review the plan Gemini shows before it starts long jobs.

## Conclusion

Tasks in Gemini is now free in six countries. Open the app, switch to Task, and hand off the next item on your list. Start with something small, watch how Gemini plans and executes, then expand to scheduled briefings or shopping.
