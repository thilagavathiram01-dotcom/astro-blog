You no longer need to generate a file in Gemini, download it, open it in another app, fix the mistakes, and re-upload. As of 29 October 2026, the Gemini app lets you create, preview, and edit documents, PDFs, presentations, spreadsheets, and code directly inside the chat.

This works for every Gemini user in every language the app supports. No paid plan is required. PDF editing works on both the web and the mobile apps. All other formats currently work only on the web version at gemini.google.com.

## What Changed on 29 October

Google added an in-chat editor. When Gemini generates a file, a live preview appears in the conversation. You can:

- Expand the file to full screen and type changes yourself.
- Highlight any section (text, table, or visual) and ask Gemini to rewrite it, shorten it, or turn a table into a graph.
- Ask follow-up questions about the content while the file stays open.

Previously you could generate and download files. Now the editing step happens without leaving the chat. The official announcement and short demo video are on the Google blog.

## Step-by-Step: Turn Handwritten Notes into an Editable PDF

This is the exact workflow Google demonstrated.

1. Open the Gemini app (web or mobile) and sign in.
2. Upload a photo (or several photos) of your handwritten lecture notes or meeting notes.
3. Prompt: “Create a one-page PDF study guide from these notes. Use clear headings, bullet points, and five practice questions at the end.”
4. Gemini generates the file and shows a preview inside the chat.
5. Expand the preview to full screen if you want to type corrections yourself.
6. Highlight any section and type a follow-up such as “Make this paragraph shorter” or “Turn this table into a graph.”
7. When you are satisfied, export the PDF or save it to Google Drive.

The same process works for creating a proposal, a simple report, or a checklist. Keep sensitive personal or financial documents out of the chat.

## Editing Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides on the Web

On gemini.google.com the same three editing methods apply to the wider set of formats.

- **Google Docs**: Draft a document, expand it, and edit inline. Save directly to Drive and continue in Docs.
- **Google Sheets**: Generate a tracker, budget, or data table. Preview it, then open it in Sheets for further work.
- **Slide decks**: Create an outline or full slides. Adjust tone or structure in the preview, then open in Google Slides or download as PPTX/PDF.
- **Markdown, TXT, and code**: Useful for technical notes, README files, or short scripts. Edit them in the chat before saving.

Example prompt for a spreadsheet: “Create a Google Sheet that tracks weekly study hours for three subjects. Include columns for date, subject, hours, and a running total.”

## Tips for Better Results

- Be specific in the first prompt. Include the audience, length, and required sections. “One-page study guide for first-year biology, with key terms bolded and five multiple-choice questions” works better than “make a study guide.”
- Upload clear photos. Gemini handles handwriting, but neat writing and good lighting improve accuracy.
- Use highlight-and-ask for targeted changes instead of regenerating the entire file.
- Check numbers, names, and citations before sharing. The editor reduces friction; it does not remove the need to review.
- Combine with Gemini Notebook if you already have sources loaded. See our guide on [adding free textbooks to Gemini Notebook](/blog/add-free-textbooks-gemini-notebook/) for grounded study materials.

## Limitations and Privacy Notes

- Only files Gemini creates in the current chat can be edited this way. You cannot upload an existing PDF and edit it inside the chat (as of the October 2026 announcement).
- Mobile is limited to PDFs. Use the web version for Docs, Sheets, and slides.
- Everything you upload is processed by Gemini. Avoid bank statements, medical records, or confidential work documents unless your organisation has approved the tool.
- Availability follows the regions and languages where the Gemini app already works. No separate admin toggle is required for Workspace or personal accounts.

## Conclusion

The October 29 update removes the most annoying step in AI document workflows: the copy-paste-into-another-app loop. Students can turn lecture notes into editable study guides. Professionals can draft trackers and slide outlines and fix them on the spot. PDFs work on your phone; the full set of formats works on the web.

Open gemini.google.com or the Gemini app, upload a photo of your notes, and ask for a PDF. You will see the preview appear in the chat within seconds.
