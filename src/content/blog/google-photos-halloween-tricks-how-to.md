Halloween photos pile up fast. Costumes from years past sit buried in your library. Backgrounds look ordinary. Shared albums stay quiet. Google Photos gives you tools to surface old outfits, swap in graveyard scenes, add night lighting, and build collages—all without leaving the app.

These features come from the October 29, 2026 Google Photos tips. Ask Photos handles search and conversational edits. Moods applies adaptive filters. Collages and emoji reactions finish the set. Availability varies by region and account eligibility for some AI tools.

## Find Past Costumes with Ask Photos

Ask Photos uses Gemini models to search your library with natural language. Instead of scrolling, you type what you want.

Open the Google Photos app. Tap the Ask button at the bottom. Type a prompt such as “What costumes have I worn on Halloween?” or “Show me my Halloween costumes from the last five years.”

Results appear as a curated set of photos. Follow up with “Which ones have pumpkins in the background?” or “Group them by year.” The model keeps context across turns.

This works on mobile. The web version still uses classic search. If Ask Photos is unavailable in your country, fall back to searching for “Halloween” or specific costume keywords.

Check eligibility in the Google Photos help center. The feature requires meeting Gemini in Photos requirements and is rolling out selectively.

## Edit Spooky Scenes with Conversational Prompts

Once you find a photo, edit it by describing the change. Open the image, tap Edit, then the Help me edit field. Type a request and send it.

Examples from Google’s guidance:

- “Change the background to a graveyard at night.”
- “Add eerie lighting and fog.”
- “Remove the person photobombing in the background.”
- “Make the lighting look like a haunted house porch.”

The model applies the edit and shows a preview. Save as a copy if you like the result, or refine with another prompt such as “Make the fog denser” or “Keep the original costume details sharp.”

Results depend on the source photo quality and lighting. Clear subjects produce cleaner swaps. For sensitive areas like faces or personal info, pair this with the Redact tool covered in our [How to Use Google Photos Redact Tool on Android](/blog/how-to-use-google-photos-redact-tool-on-android/) guide.

Test a few prompts on the same photo. Start broad, then add details about lighting direction or color temperature. Save intermediate copies so you can compare.

## Apply Moods for Night Lighting

Moods are adaptive filters that shift color, contrast, and grain to match a style while preserving the photo’s core look. They differ from static filters because they adjust to existing lighting and colors.

Access them from the Create tab. Tap Moods, select a photo, then choose a preset. “Night lights” adds a soft glow suited to evening Halloween snaps. Other options include “2000’s Night” for deeper tones and grain.

Adjust the intensity slider. It starts around 80 percent. Move it lower for subtlety or higher for stronger effect. Preview and save a copy.

Moods is available globally on Android. It works without a paid Gemini subscription for basic use. Pair it with Ask Photos edits for layered results—first swap the background, then apply Night lights.

![Scary carved pumpkin sitting on a dark forest floor](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1783712990042-27a3a5504798?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

Use Night lights on porch or street photos to emphasize string lights and jack-o-lanterns. For indoor costume shots, try Crisp 35mm or Retro Contrast to add depth without changing the subject.

## Build Custom Costume Collages

Select multiple photos, then open the Create menu and choose Collage. Google offers templates plus freeform layouts.

Customize borders with any color. Pick orange, black, or purple to match the theme. Add captions or leave space for later text overlays.

Export the collage and share it to an album. This works for year-over-year costume roundups or group party shots.

You can also start from a shared album and pull favorites into a new collage. The process stays inside Photos, so originals remain untouched.

## Add Emoji Reactions in Shared Albums

Open a shared album. Tap a photo, then the reaction area. Choose any emoji, including pumpkin, ghost, bat, or candy ones.

Reactions appear for other album members. They keep the conversation visual without extra messages. This matches the “react with any emoji” tip in the official Halloween post.

Reactions do not edit the photo. They stay as lightweight comments. You can remove yours later from the same menu.

## Tips for Better Results

Keep originals. Always save as copy so you can revert. Ask Photos and Moods generate new versions.

Be specific in prompts. “Graveyard with moonlight” beats “spooky background.” Mention what to keep: “Keep the costume and face unchanged.”

Check lighting first. Photos with strong subject lighting edit more cleanly than dark or backlit shots.

Verify eligibility. Ask Photos search and editing are limited by region and account. Moods and collages are broader.

Combine tools. Search with Ask Photos, edit the best shot, apply a Mood, then drop it into a collage.

Watch storage. Edited copies count toward your Google storage quota. Review the Recently added album to manage them.

For privacy, review the Gemini in Photos privacy settings. You control what data is used for model improvement.
