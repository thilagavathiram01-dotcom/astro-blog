Google rolled out 66 new regional voices for the Gemini app on Android and iOS starting November 16, 2026. The voices cover U.S. English, U.K. English, Indian English, Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian, and Korean. Each one focuses on natural local rhythm, pitch, and inflection instead of exaggerated accents.

Your chosen voice applies across the whole app. It works in Gemini Live conversations, language practice, and when you tap the Listen icon under a chat response. The change only affects how Gemini sounds, not the text it writes.

## What the New Regional Voices Include

Google announced the rollout on its blog after previewing the idea at I/O earlier in 2026. The 66 voices are distributed across the seven language groups listed above, with tailored options for each.

U.K. English receives the widest selection, with more than 10 distinct options. Reports from 9to5Google and other outlets list Geordie, Scouse, Brummie, Scottish, Welsh, Northern Irish, Modern Received Pronunciation, and Multicultural London English among them. Other regions get fewer but still localized choices.

The voices are tuned for everyday clarity. Google says they capture familiar local cadences so conversations feel more personal without forcing slang into written replies.

## How to Preview and Select a Voice

You can change the voice in two places inside the Gemini app. Both methods let you listen to samples before confirming.

### From Gemini Live
1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Start a Live conversation or open the Live interface.
3. Tap the three-dot menu.
4. Browse the voice options and tap one to preview it.
5. Confirm your selection.

### From App Settings
1. Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS.
2. Tap your profile picture or initial.
3. Go to Settings.
4. Select Gemini’s voice (or Gemini’s Voice).
5. Tap a voice name to hear a sample.
6. Tap Confirm.

The new voice takes effect immediately for Live chats and for Listen playback on written responses. There is no separate setting for different modes.

## Tips for Finding the Right Accent

The voice picker does not label options by city or region. Gemini also will not identify which accent it is using if you ask it directly. You need to listen to the previews and decide by ear.

Google suggests prompting with local topics to bring out the natural rhythm. Ask about regional food, sports teams, or neighborhood details. This helps the cadence stand out more clearly than generic questions.

The rollout is gradual. If the new options are missing, update the Gemini app and the Google app on Android, then restart the app. Force-stop the Google app if needed. Availability also depends on your account language and region; some languages may show fewer choices.

## Where the Voices Work and Current Limits

The feature is available in the Gemini mobile apps on Android and iOS. Google’s announcement does not mention the web version or desktop. If you use Gemini mainly in a browser, the regional voices may not appear yet.

The selected voice carries through Gemini Live and the Listen button. It does not change Gemini’s written output. Earlier issues with English voices occasionally shifting accents mid-conversation may improve with these options, though Google has not confirmed a full fix.

No paid subscription is required according to available reports. The voices are part of the standard Gemini app experience as the update reaches accounts.
