Google added in-chat file generation and editing to the Gemini app on 29 October 2026. PDF preview and editing now work on both the web and the mobile Android app. You can create a PDF from notes or a prompt, expand it, type changes, highlight sections, and ask Gemini to refine the content without leaving the chat.

This guide shows how to generate and edit PDFs on an Android phone using the Gemini app. All steps follow the official announcement on the Google blog.

![Gemini and other AI apps on a smartphone home screen](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1757310998309-87a97e562ee5?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What the October 2026 update changed

Before this update, Gemini could generate files, but editing required downloading them and opening another app. Now the preview appears inside the chat. On Android, PDF support covers both preview and editing. Other formats such as Google Docs, Sheets, slide decks, Markdown, TXT, and code remain web-only for the moment.

The feature is available to all users in every language where the Gemini app runs. No paid plan is required for basic access.

## Step-by-step: generate a PDF on Android

1. Open the Gemini app on your Android phone. Sign in with your Google account if prompted.
2. Start a new chat or continue an existing one.
3. Upload source material if you have it. Tap the plus or attachment icon and select photos of handwritten notes, a PDF, or text. You can also work from a pure text prompt.
4. Type a clear request. Example: "Create a PDF study guide from these lecture notes. Include key terms, a short summary for each section, and two practice questions."
5. Wait for Gemini to generate the file. A preview card appears in the chat.

![Smartphone showing an AI chat interface](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1762340277380-04c2c30d0ef8?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## How to edit the PDF inside the chat

Once the preview loads:

- Tap the preview to expand it to full screen.
- Type directly in the text areas to make your own changes.
- Highlight a sentence or paragraph, then type a follow-up such as "make this shorter" or "turn this list into a table."
- Ask a question about a highlighted section to get an explanation without altering the file.
- Use any undo or export controls that appear in the full-screen view.

Google’s own example shows turning handwritten notes into a study guide, then refining specific parts. The same flow works for reports, checklists, or simple one-page summaries.

## Tips for better results

Keep prompts specific. Name the audience, length, and required sections. "Create a one-page PDF checklist for packing a weekend trip" produces tighter output than a vague request.

Upload clean source material. Clear photos of notes or a short PDF help Gemini stay accurate. Large or blurry uploads may reduce quality.

Review before sharing. The AI can miss details or invent supporting facts. Expand the file, scan it, and correct anything that looks wrong.

For formats beyond PDF, switch to gemini.google.com on a computer. The mobile app currently limits full editing to PDFs.
