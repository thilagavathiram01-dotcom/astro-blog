Google Universal Cart turns scattered product finds into one organized list that follows you between Search and the Gemini app. Launched in the United States on October 15, 2026, it draws from more than 60 billion listings in Google’s Shopping Graph so you can save items, watch prices, and finish purchases without losing track of where you started.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="257" />

The feature is rolling out now for Google Account holders in the US on mobile and desktop. You do not need a separate app. It works in Google Search (including AI Mode) and inside the Gemini app. YouTube and Gmail integrations remain planned for later. Merchants such as Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta Beauty, Walmart, Wayfair, and many Shopify stores support checkout options at launch.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="285" />

## What Universal Cart does

The cart collects products from different retailers into a single place tied to your Google Account. Add an item in AI Mode on desktop Search and it appears later when you open Gemini on your phone. Price-drop and restock alerts arrive as notifications. Relevant promo codes surface inside the cart. Google Wallet looks at your saved cards and suggests which one offers the best perks for the purchase.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="257" />

Checkout has two paths. On participating stores you can pay with Google Pay in a few taps directly from the cart. Or transfer the items into the merchant’s own cart and finish on their site. In both cases the retailer remains the seller of record, sends the confirmation, and handles support. You still review totals, shipping, and return policies before you confirm.

## How to add items from Google Search

Open google.com or the Google app and start a product search. Switch to AI Mode if you want conversational results, or stay on the Shopping tab. When a product card shows an “Add to Universal Cart” control, tap it. The item lands in your cart immediately.

On desktop the cart icon often sits in the top-right of the homepage and search results. On Android it appears near the profile switcher in the Google app. Clicking the icon opens a panel with Cart and My orders tabs. Items group by retailer so you can see the source of each one.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="216" />

You can also save from ordinary product listings that support the feature. If the button is missing, the retailer may not yet be connected, or the rollout has not reached your account. Refresh or try a different query.

## How to add items from the Gemini app

Open the Gemini app and describe what you need in plain language. Examples that work well:

- “Find waterproof trail running shoes under $150 for flat feet and add the top two options to my cart.”
- “Add the brass desk lamp I saw earlier and remind me if the price drops below $80.”
- “Show me sweaters similar to the one from Quince and save the navy version.”

Gemini pulls current listings, compares details, and can add, remove, or change quantities when you ask. Because the cart is shared, anything you saved earlier in Search is already waiting. If Personal Intelligence or Connected Apps are active, Gemini can also factor in past preferences, but the core cart works without them.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="220" />

## Track prices and open the cart

Once items sit in the cart, Google monitors them against the Shopping Graph. You receive notifications for price drops and restocks. Inside the cart view you also see return windows, stock notes, and any promo codes the system finds. Tap a code to copy it.

Open the cart from the icon on Search results, from the Gemini app menu (look for Cart and orders), or by asking Gemini “Show my Universal Cart.” Review the list, adjust quantities, or remove items you no longer want. The cart persists across devices signed into the same Google Account.

## Check out with Google Pay or transfer to the store

When you are ready, choose one of the two paths shown for each retailer group:

1. Pay with Google Pay inside the cart. Review the order summary, select a recommended card if offered, confirm shipping, and complete the purchase. Eligible stores include the launch partners listed above.
2. Transfer to the merchant’s cart. Google creates a pre-filled cart on the retailer’s site via the Universal Commerce Protocol. You finish payment and any extra options there.

Always double-check the final total, shipping cost, and return policy before you confirm. The merchant remains responsible for fulfillment. If a store does not support native checkout, the transfer option is the only path.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="285" />

## Tips for better results

Start with a clear product description in Gemini so the first suggestions are relevant. Save several options while you compare, then prune the cart later. Turn on notifications if you want price alerts; you can mute them per item if needed. Because the cart is account-based, sign out of shared devices when you finish. For holiday shopping, add items early so you catch drops before peak demand.
