Online shopping often means a mess of browser tabs, screenshots, and forgotten carts across different stores. Google Universal Cart aims to fix that by giving you one shared cart that works across Google Search and the Gemini app.

Launched in the United States on October 15, 2026, Universal Cart lets you save products from multiple retailers in a single place. It watches for price drops and restocks, surfaces relevant promo codes and card perks, and offers checkout options. This guide shows exactly how to use it based on Google’s official launch details.

## What Universal Cart does

Universal Cart is an intelligent shopping hub powered by Gemini models and built on Google’s Shopping Graph of more than 60 billion product listings. It also sits on Google Wallet, so it can read your saved payment methods.

Google lists four main capabilities:

1. Save products from different retailers while shopping on Google surfaces. Your progress stays in one cart whether you found an item in AI Mode on Search or in a Gemini conversation.
2. Receive background alerts for price drops or when an out-of-stock item returns.
3. See relevant promo codes and help choosing between your cards based on perks.
4. Check out with Google Pay on participating merchants or transfer items to the merchant’s own site.

Participating merchants at launch include Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta Beauty, Walmart, Wayfair, and many Shopify stores. The merchant always remains the seller of record.

The feature is currently available only in the US on Google Search (including AI Mode) and the Gemini app. YouTube and Gmail integration, mentioned in the May 2026 preview, has not shipped yet.

## How to add items from Google Search

1. Open Google Search on the web or in the Google app and sign in with your Google Account.
2. Switch to AI Mode or use regular shopping results.
3. Search for a product, for example “waterproof jacket under $150” or a specific brand and model.
4. When product cards appear, look for an add-to-cart or save option tied to Universal Cart.
5. Add the item. It moves into your shared cart.

Google’s own example: save a Steve Madden handbag in AI Mode on Search, then later add a Quince sweater and Ulta Beauty lip gloss from the Gemini app. All three stay in the same cart.

## How to add items from the Gemini app

1. Open the Gemini app and make sure you are signed in.
2. Describe what you want: “Find a black cashmere sweater under $100 from reliable retailers.”
3. Gemini returns product options with links or cards.
4. Use the save or add-to-cart action on the items you like.
5. Confirm they appear in your Universal Cart.

You can switch between Search and Gemini freely. The cart is shared, so items added in one place show up in the other.

## View your cart, alerts, and savings

Once items are saved, open the Universal Cart interface (it appears as a dedicated cart or hub in Search and Gemini). You will see your collected products from different stores.

The cart works in the background:

- It monitors pricing and inventory using the Shopping Graph.
- You receive notifications when a saved item drops in price or comes back in stock.
- It can surface relevant promo codes.
- Because it is built on Google Wallet, it understands your card perks and can suggest which payment method offers better value for a given purchase.

Check notifications regularly during sales periods. The system does the watching so you do not have to open every retailer site yourself.

## Checkout options

When you are ready to buy:

- For many participating merchants you can complete checkout with Google Pay directly from the cart in a few taps.
- Alternatively, transfer the items to the merchant’s own cart and finish on their site.

Either path keeps the merchant as the seller of record. You receive order confirmation and support from them. Checkout is powered by the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), the open standard Google introduced earlier for agentic commerce.

Note that simultaneous multi-retailer checkout in one transaction is not the primary flow; you typically handle purchases per merchant or via transfer.

## Tips for better results

**Use AI Mode for discovery.** Start broad in Search AI Mode or Gemini, then narrow with price, size, or brand constraints. Save only what you might actually buy.

**Keep Wallet updated.** Add your main cards and loyalty info so the cart can surface accurate perk suggestions.

**Watch notifications.** Price-drop alerts are useful only if you act on them. Turn on relevant Google notifications.

**Compare before transferring.** Review the merchant site after transfer for shipping, returns, and final taxes.

**Stay in the US for now.** The rollout is US-only. Users outside the United States will not see the cart yet.

If you also shop for apps, the related [How to Use Ask Play for App Discovery in Play Store](/blog/ask-play-google-play-store/) covers a different Google AI shopping surface inside the Play Store.

## Limitations to know

- Availability is limited to the United States on Search and Gemini.
- YouTube and Gmail support remains on the roadmap with no confirmed date.
- Not every retailer supports direct Google Pay checkout or seamless transfer.
- Compatibility checks (for example, PC parts that do not fit together) were described in the May preview but are not highlighted in the October launch details.
- The feature is rolling out gradually, so it may not appear on every account immediately.

## Conclusion

Google Universal Cart turns scattered shopping across Search and Gemini into one organized list. Add items from multiple retailers, let it watch prices and stock, review perks and codes, then check out with Google Pay or transfer to the merchant.

It is a practical tool for holiday shopping in the US right now. Update your apps, sign in, and try saving a few products to see the shared cart in action. For the latest merchant support or regional expansion, check Google’s Shopping pages.
