Google's Universal Cart is now rolling out in the United States across Google Search and the Gemini app. The tool lets you save products from different retailers into one place, track price changes, surface relevant promo codes, and complete purchases with Google Pay or by transferring items to a merchant site.

Suresh Ganapathy, Senior Director of Product Management for Consumer Shopping, announced the launch on October 15, 2026. It builds on the preview shown at Google I/O in May. The cart uses Google's Shopping Graph, which contains more than 60 billion product listings that update continuously.

This guide explains how to start using Universal Cart for holiday shopping and everyday purchases. For related AI features in developer tools, see our guide on [adding Gemini 4 Argon to Android with Firebase AI Logic](/blog/add-gemini-4-argon-android-firebase-ai-logic/).

## What Universal Cart does

Universal Cart acts as a hub for shopping activity that happens inside Google products. You can add items while using AI Mode in Search or while chatting in the Gemini app. Those items stay together when you return later.

The cart works in the background. It monitors saved products for price drops and restocks. It also draws on Google Wallet to suggest which of your cards offers the best perks for a given purchase and displays applicable promo codes.

Checkout options depend on the merchant. For participating retailers you can pay directly with Google Pay inside the cart, or transfer the items to the merchant's own site and finish the purchase there. In both cases the merchant remains the seller of record and handles confirmation and support.

Named merchants at launch include Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta Beauty, Walmart, Wayfair, and many Shopify stores.

## How to add products to your cart

Open Google Search on the web or in the app and switch to AI Mode if it is available in your region. Search for a product or describe what you want. When results appear, look for the option to save an item to your Universal Cart.

In the Gemini app, start a conversation about a product or shopping need. When Gemini shows product cards or links, use the save action to add them to the same cart. Items added in Search and in Gemini appear together.

You do not need a special account beyond a standard Google account signed in on the device or browser. The cart is tied to your Google account so progress follows you across devices.

## Track prices and stock

After you save items, Universal Cart continues to watch them. Google sends notifications when a price drops or when an out-of-stock item becomes available again. These alerts come from the live data in the Shopping Graph.

Open the cart from Search or the Gemini app to review your full list. You can remove items or adjust quantities before you decide to buy. The background monitoring reduces the need to keep multiple browser tabs open or take screenshots of product pages.

## Use card perks and promo codes

Because the cart connects to Google Wallet, it can compare the benefits of cards you have stored there. It highlights which card may give you the highest cashback, points, or other rewards for the items in your cart.

Relevant promo codes also appear inside the cart when they apply to saved products. Apply them before checkout to confirm the final price. This step helps you avoid missing discounts that would otherwise require separate searches.

## Complete a purchase

When you are ready to buy, open the cart and choose your preferred path. For supported merchants, select Google Pay to complete the order in a few taps. The payment uses the card you selected earlier or another method from Wallet.

Alternatively, choose the transfer option. This moves the selected items into the merchant's cart on their website. You then finish the purchase on the retailer's site using whatever payment method they accept. Order confirmation and customer service come from the merchant in either case.

Not every retailer supports in-cart Google Pay checkout yet. The transfer option remains available more widely. Test both flows with a small order if you want to see which works best for your preferred stores.

## Tips for better results

Start with a few items from different retailers to see how the unified view works. Add products during normal Search sessions or Gemini conversations rather than hunting for a dedicated button.

Check the cart periodically for price alerts, especially on items you plan to buy during holiday sales. Enable notifications for your Google account so you do not miss restock or discount messages.

Compare the suggested card with your own knowledge of current promotions. Wallet data helps, but issuer apps sometimes show temporary offers that are not yet reflected.

If an item does not offer in-cart checkout, use the transfer feature and note the merchant for next time. Availability of direct Google Pay checkout expands as more retailers adopt the Universal Commerce Protocol.

## Current availability and next steps

Universal Cart is rolling out in the United States across Google Search and the Gemini app. YouTube and Gmail integration, mentioned in the May preview, have not launched yet and have no announced timeline.

The feature relies on the Universal Commerce Protocol for checkout handoffs. Merchants that have integrated with UCP can support smoother transfers and in-cart purchases. Expansion to additional countries was outlined earlier for Canada, Australia, and the UK, but the October launch focuses on the US.

## Conclusion

Universal Cart gives US shoppers a single place to collect products found in Search and Gemini, watch for better prices, apply available savings, and check out with less friction. Open Search or the Gemini app, save a few items, and review the cart to see the tools in action. The merchant stays in control of the sale, while Google handles the organization and alerts.
