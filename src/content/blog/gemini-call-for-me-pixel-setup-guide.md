Routine business calls waste time on hold music and phone menus. Gemini Call for Me lets eligible Pixel owners hand those tasks to AI. You describe the goal in the Gemini app. Gemini dials from your number, navigates the system, waits on hold, and talks to the person who answers. You watch a live transcript and can take over at any moment.

The feature is an early preview limited to specific Pixel phones in the United States. It builds on older Pixel Call Assist tools like Hold for Me and Direct My Call. This guide covers eligibility, setup, step-by-step use, practical examples, safety limits, and what to expect after the call.

## What you need to use Call for Me

Google lists clear requirements on its support page. You must meet every one:

- Be 18 or older and located in the United States.
- Use a Pixel 10 or Pixel 11 series phone with a US SIM card.
- Sign in to the Gemini mobile app.
- Hold an active Google AI subscription (AI Plus, Pro, or Ultra).
- Run the Public Beta version of the Phone by Google app.
- Have the latest Gemini mobile app installed.
- Set your device language to English.

Calls are limited to US phone numbers for now. The feature is rolling out gradually, so even if you meet the criteria it may not appear immediately. Join the Phone by Google public beta through Google Play to access it.

Gemini uses your mobile network and your phone number. Standard carrier rates apply. Every call begins with Gemini stating it is an AI assistant from Google calling on a recorded line on your behalf and giving your name.

## How to start a Call for Me task

Open the Gemini app on your Pixel. Describe what you need in plain language. You can also add “@Call for me” to the prompt if you want to be explicit.

Be specific. Include the business name, the exact outcome, dates, times, preferences, and any backup options. Examples from Google:

- “Call [Restaurant Name] and book a table for two at 7 PM tomorrow.”
- “Ask the dry cleaner on Main Street how much they charge to hem pants.”

Gemini replies with a summary. It shows the phone number it will dial and the details it plans to share, such as your name and contact information. Review it carefully. Ask Gemini to revise anything that looks wrong.

When the summary is correct, tap **Call for me**. The call places from your phone. You stay in the Gemini app or switch to the Phone app to follow progress.

## Monitor the call and take over when needed

While the call is active you see a live transcript in the Gemini app. You can also listen to the audio. Your microphone stays muted by default so Gemini handles the conversation.

Controls available during the call:

- Tap **Cancel** before the call connects to stop it.
- Tap Audio to turn the live audio on or off.
- Tap **Take over** to unmute your microphone and join the conversation. Gemini announces that you are taking over and then exits the call.

After the call ends you receive a notification. Tap **View results** to read Gemini’s summary of the outcome inside the Gemini app. The full transcript and a recording of the call also appear in your Phone app call history.

Daily limits apply to the number of calls Gemini can place for you. If you hit the limit, wait until the next day.

## Practical examples that fit the feature

Call for Me works best for low-stakes, structured business tasks:

- Check whether a hardware store has a specific tool or part in stock.
- Book or change a restaurant reservation, including patio seating or a preferred time.
- Move a haircut or other appointment to a different day.
- Request a service quote, such as dry-cleaning prices.
- Update an existing order (for example, add an extra item to a pizza order).

Gemini can navigate automated phone menus and wait on hold. It introduces itself clearly so the person on the other end knows they are speaking with an AI assistant acting for you.

## Safety limits and what Gemini will not do

Google restricts certain actions to protect users and businesses. Gemini cannot:

- Place emergency calls (such as 911).
- Complete financial transactions or share credit-card numbers, passwords, Social Security numbers, or health information.
- Make telemarketing or sales calls.

Using the feature means you accept Google’s Terms of Service, the Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy, and the Gemini Apps Agentic Calling Additional Terms. Businesses that do not want to receive these calls can opt out through Google’s form.

If a call goes poorly or the business asks for information Gemini is not allowed to share, take over immediately or end the call.

## Tips for better results

Give Gemini clear, complete instructions the first time. Mention alternative times or options so it can finish the task without needing you to step in. Review the summary every time before you tap Call for me—especially the phone number and the personal details that will be shared.

Keep the live transcript open while the call runs so you can intervene quickly if the conversation drifts. After the call, check the Phone app recording if you need exact wording later.

If you also use Gemini for hands-free calling in the car, note that Android Auto has a separate known issue where Gemini sometimes loops on contact names. See the related guide on fixing the [Android Auto Gemini call loop](/blog/fix-android-auto-gemini-call-loop/) for workarounds while that bug is addressed.

## What happens after the call

Gemini sends a short summary of the result. You can rate the outcome with thumbs up or down inside the chat. The Phone by Google app may occasionally show a survey asking for more detailed feedback. Both help Google improve the feature.

Because this is still an early preview, expect occasional errors, incomplete tasks, or slower performance on complex menus. Report problems through the Gemini app feedback option so the team receives concrete examples.
