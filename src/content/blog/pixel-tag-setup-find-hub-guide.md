Google Pixel Tag is now available, giving Android users a simple way to track keys, wallets, bags, and luggage. At $29.99 for one or $99.99 for a four-pack, it works with the Find Hub network of more than one billion Android devices.

The tag is 5.4 mm thin, made of stainless steel, IP67 rated, and uses a replaceable CR2032 battery that lasts up to a year. It supports Bluetooth, UWB for precision finding on compatible phones, and a reverse-ring feature so the tag can sound your phone.

This guide walks through official setup steps, daily use, sharing, and maintenance.

## What you need before starting

Your phone must run Android 9 or later with updated Google Play services. Turn on Bluetooth, Location, and Wi-Fi. Keep the phone unlocked on the home screen with an internet connection. Fully remove the plastic battery tab from the Pixel Tag; it should make a short sound when powered on.

Carbon-fiber cases, aluminum luggage, or RFID-blocking wallets can block the signal. Place the tag in an exterior fabric pocket when traveling.

## Pair the Pixel Tag with Fast Pair

1. Place the Pixel Tag next to your unlocked Android phone.
2. Remove the plastic tab completely from the battery contact point.
3. A Fast Pair pop-up appears. Tap Connect.
4. Read the Acceptable Use Policy and tap Continue.
5. Read the Terms and Conditions and tap I accept.
6. When prompted for Location permissions, select When using this app.
7. The tag is added to the Find Hub app.

After setup, open Find Hub, select the tag, choose a category such as Keys, Backpack, or Other, and edit the name. It defaults to something like “Your name’s keys.”

If the pop-up does not appear, wait five minutes (or 24 hours after a second dismissal). Toggle Bluetooth off and on, or restart the phone. You can also go to Settings > Connected devices, or open Find Hub and tap Add Device.

## Find nearby items with precision guidance

Open the Find Hub app and tap your Pixel Tag.

When the tag is within Bluetooth range, use Play sound to make it ring. Adjust volume in the tag settings.

For closer guidance, tap Find Nearby. Compatible phones with UWB show an on-screen arrow, direction, and distance meter. Bluetooth Channel Sounding improves accuracy at longer ranges. This helps locate items under cushions or in clutter.

The Find Hub network reports the last known location on a map even when the tag is miles away. Location data is end-to-end encrypted; only you and people you share with can see it.

Double-press the physical button on the back of the Pixel Tag (below the battery cover) to ring your phone if both are nearby. Press once or unlock the phone to silence it. Enable “Ring this phone” during or after setup.

## Share ownership and set left-behind alerts

You can share a Pixel Tag with up to 10 people so family or colleagues can locate shared items like car keys.

1. Open Find Hub.
2. Tap the Pixel Tag.
3. Under the device name, tap Share ownership.
4. Select contacts. They must use Android devices.

Set Left Behind Reminders so the app notifies you if you leave the tagged item behind. This is useful for bags or wallets at restaurants or offices.

Pixel Tag also works with Pixel Watch for locating and ringing via the Find Hub app or browser. You can ask Gemini on Pixel Buds to ring the tag.

## Replace the battery and basic care

The CR2032 battery lasts up to one year and is user-replaceable. Twist the circular battery door counterclockwise to open it (the NFC area is below the door). Insert a new battery, close the door, and the tag reconnects.

The tag is IP67 water- and dust-resistant. It is TSA/FAA/IATA compliant for air travel because the battery meets airline rules. Avoid metal or RFID materials that block signals.

If setup fails repeatedly, reset the tag using the instructions in Google’s Pixel Phone Help.

## Tips for reliable tracking

Keep the Find Hub app updated. Test the ring sound and reverse-find button after setup. For shared items, confirm all users have accepted the share request.

Pixel Tag does not store continuous location history; it shows the current or last reported position. Combine it with Android features such as [App Lock on Pixel](/blog/android-17-app-lock-pixel/) for better device security.

Check compatibility for UWB Precision Finding; not every Android phone supports the full arrow-and-distance view.

## Conclusion

Pixel Tag pairs quickly via Fast Pair and integrates with the large Find Hub network. Setup takes a couple of minutes, finding works with sound, map, and precision guidance on supported phones, and sharing covers family use. The replaceable battery and reverse-ring button add practical value for everyday items.

Buy from the Google Store or authorized retailers. Follow the official support steps if the pop-up is missing or the signal seems weak.
