Google Fi launched its Super Network on November 10, 2026. Pixel 9 and newer phones can now switch automatically between two of America’s top 5G networks. This reduces dead zones and keeps connections stable without extra cost on any Fi plan.

The feature uses Multiple Enabled Profiles technology on eSIM. It monitors signal quality and hands off to the stronger network in real time. Wi-Fi Auto Connect+ also expands secure hotspot access in crowded places. Both tools work internationally on Unlimited Premium and Flexible plans.

This guide walks through eligibility, setup, verification, and everyday use on a compatible Pixel.

## Check Eligibility Before You Start

Super Network requires three conditions. Your phone must be a Pixel 9, Pixel 9 Pro, Pixel 9 Pro XL, Pixel 9 Pro Fold, Pixel 10 series, or Pixel 11 series. Google Fi must run on eSIM. All other SIM profiles must stay disabled.

Older Pixels and physical SIM setups do not support network switching yet. Wider device support arrives in 2027. Confirm your model under Settings > About phone. Open the Google Fi app and verify your plan status.

Network switching and Wi-Fi Auto Connect+ are included on every Google Fi plan in the US. International high-speed data follows the limits of Unlimited Premium or Flexible. Performance still depends on local coverage, signal strength, and device configuration.

## Activate Google Fi on eSIM

If you are already on Google Fi, skip to the next section. New users start at fi.google.com or the Google Fi app.

1. Download the Google Fi app from the Play Store.
2. Sign in with your Google account.
3. Choose a plan (Flexible, Unlimited Essentials, Unlimited Standard, or Unlimited Premium).
4. Select eSIM as the activation method.
5. Follow the on-screen prompts to download and activate the eSIM profile.
6. Restart the phone when prompted.

Disable any other SIMs. Go to Settings > Network & internet > SIMs. Toggle off every non-Fi profile. Leave only the Google Fi eSIM active. This is required for the dual-network handoff to function.

Existing Pixel owners without Fi can join now and receive 50 percent off select unlimited plans for 12 months. The offer ends December 1, 2026. Terms apply on the Google Fi site.

## Enable and Verify Super Network

Once Fi is active on eSIM with other SIMs off, the Super Network rolls out automatically. No separate toggle exists.

1. Open the Google Fi app.
2. Look for the “Your super network” card on the Home tab.
3. Tap it to see your current connection (Primary or Secondary network).
4. Select “See activity” to review recent switches and timestamps.
5. Confirm Wi-Fi Auto Connect+ status if shown.

The app notes when you are in range of eligible Wi-Fi networks. Switching happens in the background. You stay on the primary network until the secondary offers a meaningful improvement based on Google’s smart thresholds.

Test the feature by moving between areas with known coverage differences. Check the activity log after travel or in places that previously dropped signal. Ultra Wideband is supported on the secondary network where available.

## Use Wi-Fi Auto Connect+ for Indoor Speeds

Wi-Fi Auto Connect+ connects your Pixel to high-quality open networks in millions of locations across North America, Europe, Asia, and Brazil. It works on Pixel 5a and newer without manual login.

The connection runs through Google Fi’s Virtual Carrier Network and receives automatic encryption. Traffic does not count against your monthly data allowance. In airports, malls, or stadiums, the phone prefers these networks when they outperform nearby cellular.

No settings change is required on supported Pixels. The feature is already live. On Samsung devices running One UI 9 or later and select Motorola phones, rollout continues separately.

## Monitor Performance and Troubleshoot

Open the Google Fi app regularly to review switch history. Note the time and network type of each handoff. Compare speeds with a simple browser test or speed-check app before and after a switch.

If switching does not appear:

- Confirm the phone is Pixel 9 or newer.
- Verify only the Fi eSIM is enabled.
- Restart the device and reopen the Fi app.
- Check for the latest Android and Fi app updates under Settings > System > System update and the Play Store.
- Ensure you are in an area where both networks operate. Coverage is not universal yet.

For account questions, use Ask Fi inside the Google Fi app. The AI support agent handles billing, plan details, and basic connectivity troubleshooting.

International travel works the same way on Unlimited Premium and Flexible plans. High-speed data is capped (up to 50 GB on Unlimited Premium, 15 GB on Flexible) before throttling. The 90-day consecutive international limit still applies.

## Tips for Best Results

Keep the phone updated. Android 17 and later system images improve adaptive connectivity. Disable battery optimization for the Google Fi app if switches feel delayed.

Use the feature on highways, rural routes, and dense urban cores where single-network dead zones appear. Pair it with Pixel’s existing Adaptive Connectivity setting under Settings > Network & internet.

If you also use a Pixel Watch or other devices, the Super Network remains phone-specific for now. Data sharing rules stay unchanged.

## Conclusion

Google Fi Super Network brings automatic dual-5G switching and expanded secure Wi-Fi to Pixel 9 and newer phones at no extra cost. Activate Fi on eSIM, disable other profiles, and the feature rolls out automatically. Check the Fi app for confirmation and activity logs. The combination reduces dropped connections in everyday use and while traveling.

Official details appear on the Google Fi blog and support pages. Results vary by location and conditions. Update your Pixel and Fi app to receive the latest improvements as they roll out.
