Google Messages is testing a redesigned pinned chats layout in its beta channel. The change moves pinned conversations into a dedicated row of large circular contact photos at the top of the inbox, similar to iMessage. A floating search button is also appearing for some testers.

This layout makes frequent contacts easier to spot at a glance. It does remove the last-message preview from those pinned threads, so you must open the chat to see recent activity. The redesign is not yet on the stable track for most users.

![Android phone showing messaging app](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1760360497581-0606569510a6?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What the New Layout Looks Like

In the current stable version, pinned chats sit at the top of the regular conversation list with a small pin indicator. In the beta redesign, those chats appear as oversized contact avatars above the rest of the inbox.

Reports from Android Authority and 9to5Google confirm the change is rolling out server-side to some beta users on build 20261030_00_RC00. Not every beta tester sees it yet. Google is also testing a centered floating action button for search that expands into a pill while you scroll.

The redesign prioritizes visual recognition of contacts over seeing message previews. Some users prefer the cleaner look. Others miss the ability to scan recent text without opening each thread.

## How to Join the Google Messages Beta

The new layout is currently limited to the beta channel. Official steps come from Google’s support page.

1. Open the Google Play Store and search for Google Messages.
2. Scroll to the “Join the beta” section on the app listing.
3. Tap Join and confirm.
4. Wait for the Play Store to offer the beta update, then install it.

The beta program can fill up. If the Join button is unavailable, try again later. Google notes that beta versions can be less stable, so avoid using them as your only messaging app on a primary device if possible.

Once installed, force-close and reopen Google Messages. The pinned chats row may appear after a short delay because of server-side flags.

## How to Pin Conversations

Pinning works the same way in both stable and beta builds.

1. Open Google Messages.
2. Long-press the conversation you want to keep at the top.
3. Tap the pin icon that appears at the top of the screen.
4. The conversation moves into the pinned section.

You can currently pin up to 20 conversations in the public version. Earlier beta teardowns suggested a possible limit of six under the new visual treatment, but that cap has not replaced the existing limit for most users. Unpin by long-pressing again and selecting the unpin option.

## Tips for the Beta Redesign

Check for updates in the Play Store after joining the beta. Some users report receiving the layout without a new APK because Google enables it remotely.

If you do not see the large avatars, close the app completely from the recent-apps screen and reopen it. Clearing the cache is rarely needed, but it can help if the interface stays on the old design after several hours.

The floating search button improves one-handed use. It sits next to the Start chat button and expands when you scroll. Gemini’s presence in that area may move, but Google has not removed the assistant entirely.

## What Happens Next

Google has not announced a stable rollout date for the pinned chats redesign. Features that start in the Messages beta often reach the public track weeks or months later. The company continues to test search placement and other interface tweaks at the same time.

If you prefer the current layout with message previews, stay on the stable version. You can leave the beta at any time through the same Play Store listing.
