Raise to Talk lets you speak to Gemini on a Pixel Watch without a hotword and without holding a button. Lift the watch near your mouth, wait for the indicator, then talk at a normal volume.

Google shipped the gesture with Pixel Watch 4. Official Help now covers **Pixel Watch 4 and Pixel Watch 5** on Wear OS 6 or later. The September 2026 Pixel Drop listed further Raise to Talk reliability work, and Pixel Watch 5 marketing added offline actions for simple commands such as starting a timer.

This guide follows Pixel Watch Help and Google’s own how-to post. It covers who can use the feature, how to turn it on, how to trigger it, and how to stop false activations.

## Who can use Raise to Talk

Pixel Watch Help states the limits in one sentence: the feature is available on **Google Pixel Watch 4 or 5 with Wear OS 6+**, paired with a **compatible Pixel phone**, with **Gemini set as the default assistant**.

You still need a working microphone and an unlocked watch on your wrist. Gemini on the watch is a companion to the phone app, not a separate account.

If Gemini is not your default assistant on the paired Pixel, fix that first. Then update Wear OS and the Pixel Watch app from Play Store.

## Run the on-watch tutorial

Help says Pixel Watch 4 and 5 include an interactive tutorial. After an update you may see a **What’s new** notification that opens it. A **Day 1** prompt can also appear about 24 hours after the update.

You can start the lesson any time:

1. On the watch, open **Settings**.
2. Tap **General**, then **Pixel Tips**.
3. Tap **Raise to talk**, then **Continue**.
4. Perform the raise when the watch asks.

The tutorial is the fastest way to learn how close the watch needs to be. Most first-time misses come from raising the watch to eye level instead of toward the mouth.

## Turn the gesture on

Raise to Talk is **off by default**. Enable it in the phone app or on the watch.

**From the Pixel Watch app**

1. Open the **Pixel Watch** app on your phone.
2. Under Settings, tap **Watch Preferences**, then **Gestures**.
3. Turn **Raise to talk** on.

**From the watch**

1. Swipe down on the watch face.
2. Tap **Settings**, then **Gestures**.
3. Open **Hand gestures** or **Raise to talk**.
4. Turn **Raise to talk** on.

Google’s October 2025 how-to post matches this path: use Watch Preferences in the phone app, or Gestures on the watch itself.

## How to trigger Gemini

Help’s activation sequence is short. Follow it in order.

1. Bring the watch to about **2 to 4 inches** from your mouth.
2. Wait for the **curved indicator** at the bottom of the watch face.
3. Start speaking at your normal volume **within 2 seconds** after the indicator appears.
4. Keep the watch near your mouth while you talk.

Posture examples from Help:

- **Standing:** raise from the arm at your side.
- **Sitting:** raise from an armrest, desk, or keyboard.
- Think of a walkie-talkie, not a glance at the time.

You do not say “Hey Google.” The October 2025 Pixel blog describes a subtle light at the bottom of the display when Gemini is listening.

The status line currently shows on the **watch face**. Raise to Talk can still work on other surfaces, but you may not see the indicator there.

## When the watch will not listen

Help lists cases where Gemini will not start from a raise:

- The watch is **off-wrist** or **locked**.
- **Bedtime** or **Theatre** mode is on.
- The microphone is already used by Recorder, Gboard, or Search voice input.
- You are on an **active phone call**.
- A **fall** was just detected.

If you speak after the indicator disappears, the watch will not treat that audio as a Raise to Talk start. Wait for the curve, then talk.

## Cancel a false start

Accidental raises happen. Help documents several exits.

- Lower your arm. The watch treats that as a cancel.
- Press a physical button, including the power button.
- Tap or swipe the display.
- Cover the face with your palm to blank the screen and stop the prompt.

Timeouts also clear a miss:

- About **1 second** if the watch hears no sound.
- About **2 seconds** if it hears sound but no speech from you.

Help also notes you can leave Gemini by pressing the **Crown** if a raise opened it by mistake.

## Tune gesture and voice sensitivity

If the watch wakes Gemini when you check the time, lower gesture sensitivity. If it never wakes when you want it, raise the level.

In the Pixel Watch app:

1. Open **Settings** → **Watch Preferences** → **Gestures**.
2. Find the Raise to Talk sensitivity controls.

Help defaults:

- **Gesture Sensitivity** starts at **level 2**. Higher is easier to trigger and more accident-prone. Lower is stricter.
- **Voice Sensitivity** starts at **level 3**. Higher picks up quieter speech after a valid raise. Lower ignores more background noise.
- **Visual Indicator** is on by default. You can turn the curve off, but first-time users should leave it on.

Change one slider at a time. Test for a day before you touch the other.

## What to ask once Gemini is listening

Use short, concrete requests. Official Pixel Watch and Gemini materials show everyday examples: set a timer, start a workout, draft a message, build a playlist, or look up a place from recent mail.

Pixel Watch 5 materials also describe **offline** handling for some local actions, such as starting a run, setting a timer, or turning on Do Not Disturb. Treat that as device-dependent. Confirm on your watch after the latest system update.

For one-handed controls that do not require speech, use [Pixel Watch one-handed gestures](/blog/pixel-watch-one-handed-gestures/) for pinch and wrist-turn actions such as scrolling notifications or answering a call.

## Tips that cut missed prompts

- Raise toward the mouth, not toward your eyes.
- Speak within two seconds of the indicator.
- Keep the watch on-wrist and unlocked.
- Disable Theatre or Bedtime when you want the gesture.
- Update Gemini on the paired Pixel and the watch apps together.
- If Raise to Talk never appears, confirm Wear OS 6 or later and that Gemini is the default assistant.

September 2026’s Pixel Drop called out Raise to Talk improvements on Pixel Watch. If your watch still times out mid-sentence after that drop, raise Voice Sensitivity one step and repeat the Pixel Tips tutorial.

## Conclusion

Raise to Talk is a wrist-to-mouth gesture, not a glance. Turn it on under Gestures, complete the Pixel Tips lesson, lift the watch two to four inches from your mouth, then speak after the indicator. Cancel misses by lowering your arm or pressing the crown. Use sensitivity sliders only after you have the motion right.
