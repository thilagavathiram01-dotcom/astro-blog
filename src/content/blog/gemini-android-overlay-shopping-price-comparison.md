Shopping on your phone often means jumping between apps to check prices and return policies. Gemini on Android now shortens that step. When you open the floating overlay over a product page, it suggests prompts that use the visible screen content to compare options and review terms.

This update arrives as Black Friday and holiday shopping peak. Android Authority confirmed the prompts appear in Google app version 17.78.13. The change looks like a server-side rollout, so not every device shows it immediately. No official Google blog post has detailed it yet.

## What the Overlay Does with Shopping Pages

Gemini’s floating overlay has long let you ask questions without leaving your current app. The new behavior adds context-aware suggestions when it detects a product page. Three common prompts appear: “Help me pick,” “Compare prices,” and “Check warranty and returns.”

Tapping a prompt makes Gemini capture a screenshot of the visible page. It uses that image plus your request as context. You do not need to start Gemini Live or share your screen. The overlay stays on top of the shopping app.

Android Authority tested the feature on Amazon, Flipkart, and quick-commerce apps such as Blinkit. Results include comparisons against other retailers. For example, an Amazon listing can be checked against Walmart or Target pricing. Warranty and return details pull from the on-screen information and Gemini’s broader knowledge.

The overlay only sees what is currently visible. Scroll the product page first if key details sit below the fold. Gemini saves the resulting conversation in your history so you can reopen it later.

## How to Trigger the Shopping Prompts

Follow these steps on an Android device with the Google app updated:

1. Open a shopping app such as Amazon, Flipkart, or Blinkit.
2. Navigate to a specific product page and scroll so the title, price, and key details are visible.
3. Summon Gemini the way you normally do. Most people long-press the power button or say “Hey Google.”
4. Look for the suggested prompts in the floating overlay. They appear near the prompt box when the system recognizes a product page.
5. Tap “Compare prices,” “Help me pick,” or “Check warranty and returns.”
6. Review the response. Gemini may list other stores, highlight differences in return windows, or note whether the current price looks competitive.
7. Ask follow-up questions in the same thread if you need more detail.

If the prompts do not appear, update the Google app through the Play Store and try again later. The rollout is gradual.

## Practical Tips for Better Results

Keep the most relevant part of the product page on screen before you open the overlay. Gemini works from the screenshot it captures, so lower sections stay out of view unless you scroll first.

Use the prompts as a starting point, then refine the request. After “Compare prices” returns a list, ask which option has the fastest shipping or the longest return window. Combine this with other Gemini tools. For multi-step shopping tasks that run in the background, see our guide on [using free Gemini Tasks](/blog/how-to-use-free-gemini-tasks-mode-step-by-step/).

The feature pairs well with Google’s existing shopping graph. Price data comes from the same listings that power Search and the Gemini app. Always double-check the final price and stock on the merchant site before you buy, since listings can change quickly.

Privacy controls still apply. The conversation lands in your Gemini history. If you want to keep history while limiting model training, review the new settings Google rolled out this month.

## What to Expect During the Rollout

Availability depends on the Google app version and server flags. Android Authority observed the prompts in version 17.78.13. Users on older builds may need to wait. The same update also adds the option to mute Gemini Live voice replies and receive text captions instead.

No settings toggle controls the shopping prompts. They appear automatically when the overlay opens over a recognized product page. If you do not see them, continue using the overlay for regular questions. You can still type or speak a request such as “Compare this product’s price on other sites.”

The change fits Google’s broader push to make shopping tools available inside both Search AI Mode and the Gemini app. Earlier updates added grocery Connected Apps and a Universal Cart. This overlay feature brings similar help into third-party shopping apps without leaving the page you already opened.

## Conclusion

Gemini’s floating overlay now offers practical shopping help on Android. The three prompts let you compare prices, evaluate a product, and review warranty terms without switching apps. The feature is rolling out gradually with Google app 17.78.13 and later. Test it on a product page in Amazon or a similar app, then refine the results with follow-up questions.
