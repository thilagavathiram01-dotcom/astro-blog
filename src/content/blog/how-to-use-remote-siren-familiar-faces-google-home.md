Google rolled out a November 25, 2026 update for the Google Home app that adds a practical remote siren control and expands Familiar Face alerts to more cameras. If you own a Nest Cam Outdoor (wired, 2nd gen) or one of Walmart’s Onn outdoor models, you can now trigger a 30-second security siren straight from the live video feed.

This guide walks through exactly how to use the new remote siren and set up Familiar Faces so your cameras send clearer alerts when someone you know arrives.

![Outdoor security camera mounted on a house](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1558002038-1055907df827?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Which cameras support the remote siren

The remote siren button appears only on three models right now:

- Nest Cam Outdoor (wired, 2nd gen)
- Onn Outdoor Camera Plug-In
- Onn Floodlight Camera Hardwired

On these cameras you can start or stop the 30-second siren while you are watching the live feed in the Google Home app. The feature does not require a Google Home Premium subscription for the siren itself.

Familiar Face alerts, however, do need an active Google Home Premium plan and are newly available on the two Onn cameras (they were already present on many Nest models).

## Update the Google Home app first

1. Open the Google Play Store or Apple App Store on your phone.
2. Search for Google Home and tap Update if a new version is available.
3. Force-close the app and reopen it so the new controls load.

The update is rolling out gradually, so the siren button may not appear for every user on day one even after the app updates.

## How to trigger the remote siren

1. Open the Google Home app.
2. Tap your home, then select the supported outdoor camera.
3. Start the live video feed.
4. Look for the siren control on the live-view overlay (usually a speaker or alarm icon).
5. Tap it once to start the 30-second siren. Tap again to stop it early if needed.

The siren is loud enough to deter most casual activity near the camera. Because it is manual, you decide when to sound it after you have seen the live footage.

## Set up Familiar Face alerts

Familiar Faces lets the camera recognize people you have labeled and send more useful notifications.

1. Confirm you have an active Google Home Premium subscription (Standard or Advanced).
2. In the Google Home app go to Home settings → Subscriptions → Google Home Premium.
3. Turn on Familiar face detection for the camera.
4. Open the camera’s video history or face library and name any unknown faces that appear.
5. Under the camera’s notification settings, enable People and Your notifications so you receive the alerts.

Once labeled, the camera improves over time. You can merge duplicate profiles or remove incorrect snapshots if the system misidentifies someone.

## Extra changes in the same update

The November 25 release also brought:

- Ability for homes with multiple admins to share camera clip feedback (supported regions).
- One-tap copy of a Nest thermostat schedule from one day to another.
- Faster Gemini for Home commands and improved storytelling for early-access users.
- Android 17 camera notification preview fixes and better landscape controls on Pixel phones.

These extras make the Google Home app more useful if you already rely on Nest devices.

## Tips for better results

- Mount the camera so faces are clearly visible and not backlit.
- Review the face library in the first weeks after setup and correct any mistakes.
- Test the siren once during daylight so you know how loud it is and how neighbors react.
- If the button does not appear, wait a few days for the staged rollout or confirm your camera model matches the supported list.

## Conclusion

The remote siren gives you a quick way to respond while watching live video, and Familiar Faces on the affordable Onn cameras makes person recognition more accessible. Update the Google Home app, confirm your camera is supported, and follow the steps above to turn both features on.

If you are still using the Nest app, note that Google plans to retire it on January 25, 2027, so migrating to Google Home now prepares you for the full set of controls.
