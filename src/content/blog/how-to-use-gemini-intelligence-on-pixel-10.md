Google expanded Gemini Intelligence to the Pixel 10 series on November 10, 2026. Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold owners now get Rambler voice typing, multi-step task automation, and the ability to create custom home screen widgets with plain language.

This update brings features that launched earlier on the Pixel 11 line to more devices. Availability depends on your region, language, and whether you are 18 or older. Check the official Google support pages for the latest details in your area.

![Person holding a smartphone showing AI apps](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1721988348117-01a350266d26?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What is Gemini Intelligence?

Gemini Intelligence is Google’s system-level AI layer for Android. It goes beyond the Gemini app by helping your phone handle everyday work across apps and screens.

On supported Pixel devices it includes cleaned-up voice input, proactive suggestions, and the ability to complete multi-step tasks such as ordering food or booking a ride. The November expansion also adds Create My Widget and Device Assistance on qualifying hardware.

Pixel 10a is not included in these Gemini Intelligence features. Pixel 11 series devices already had them. Motorola’s signature 27 also gains support and opens for US pre-order on November 19.

## How to use Rambler for better voice typing

Rambler upgrades Gboard voice typing. It removes filler words such as “um,” smooths mid-sentence corrections, and organizes your spoken thoughts into clear text. You can switch languages mid-sentence in supported cases.

### Steps to try Rambler

1. Open any app that uses Gboard (Messages, Gmail, or a note-taking app).
2. Tap the microphone icon on the Gboard strip. On supported devices it appears with a star.
3. Speak naturally. Do not worry about perfect phrasing or pauses.
4. Tap the checkmark when finished. Review the cleaned text before you send or save it.

Rambler is available on the listed Pixel 10 models and the Pixel 11 series in select countries and languages. It is subject to usage limits. Results can vary, so always review the output.

## Complete multi-step tasks with Gemini

Task automation lets you ask Gemini to handle logistics across your favorite apps. Examples include ordering groceries, hailing a ride, ordering takeout, or finding a restaurant reservation.

As of the November 10 announcement, this capability is expanding to the UK, India, and Japan for users 18+. It was already available in other markets on qualifying devices. You stay in control: you can view progress and end the task at any time.

### How to start a task

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Describe the full request in one prompt. Example: “Order my usual coffee from the nearby cafe and have it ready for pickup in 20 minutes.”
3. Confirm any details Gemini asks for.
4. Watch the progress on screen. Tap to take over or cancel if needed.

Feature availability varies by country, language, and device. Some higher usage may require a subscription. Always double-check the final order or booking before it completes.

## Create custom widgets with a simple prompt

Create My Widget lets you build personalized home screen widgets by describing what you want. No coding is required. Gemini can pull information from Gmail, Calendar, and Search when you connect those services.

Examples include a monthly spending tracker, a countdown to a trip that shows flight details, or a game-day widget in your team colors.

The feature is rolling out over the next few weeks on Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 series phones (except Pixel 10a). It is currently limited to English in Australia, Canada, India, the United States, and the United Kingdom for users 18 and older. Gemini Connected Apps integration is not available in the UK.

### Steps to create a widget

1. Touch and hold an empty area on your home screen.
2. Tap Widgets, then Create.
3. Choose a suggested template or tap Describe your widget.
4. Type your request. Example: “Countdown to my vacation on July 15 with flight times.”
5. Review the preview. Edit if needed, then tap Add.

You can later long-press the widget and choose Edit to refine it. If the widget needs access to device or app data, allow the permission when prompted. Check the content for accuracy.

## Device Assistance and proactive suggestions

Device Assistance helps you control or fix your phone by asking Gemini. You can clear storage space, check which apps can access location, or mute distractions while still receiving important calls. It is available on select Android devices running Android 17 or later.

Proactive suggestions surface timely actions. When someone texts asking when you will arrive, your Pixel can suggest sharing your ETA with one tap. Similar suggestions are coming to Android Auto for Pixel phones that have Gemini Intelligence.

These features require the Gemini app and work in select countries and languages for users 18+.

## Tips for the best results

- Update your Pixel to the latest software. The November Pixel Drop rolls out over several weeks.
- Enable the Gemini app and grant necessary permissions for Connected Apps if you want widgets or tasks that use Gmail or Calendar.
- Speak or type clearly but naturally. Rambler and Create My Widget both handle imperfect input well.
- Review every AI-generated result before you act on it. Order confirmations, widget data, and suggestions can contain errors.
- Check Google’s support pages for your exact model and region because availability is limited.

## Conclusion

The November 2026 expansion of Gemini Intelligence makes Pixel 10 phones more useful without a hardware upgrade. Rambler cleans up voice input, task automation handles multi-step errands in more countries, and Create My Widget turns plain descriptions into home screen tools.

Start with one feature that matches your daily routine. Update your device, try the steps above, and adjust permissions as needed. Official details live on the Google blog and Pixel support pages.
