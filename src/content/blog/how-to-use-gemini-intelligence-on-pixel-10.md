Google expanded Gemini Intelligence to Pixel 10 series phones in the November 2026 Pixel Drop. Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold now get features that first appeared on Pixel 11 devices. These include cleaner voice typing, multi-step task handling, and prompt-based custom widgets.

The update started rolling out on November 10, 2026, and continues over several weeks. Availability depends on your region, language, and device. Most features require the Gemini app and work for users 18 and older. Some need a Google AI subscription for higher usage.

This guide shows how to access and use the main Gemini Intelligence tools on a supported Pixel 10 phone.

## What Gemini Intelligence adds on Pixel 10

Gemini Intelligence brings proactive help across apps and screens. On Pixel 10 it adds three practical tools:

- Rambler cleans spoken text in real time.
- Task automation handles logistics in supported apps.
- Create My Widget builds personalized home screen widgets from a description.

These features run on top of Android 17. They work best when your phone is updated to the latest Pixel Drop software. Motorola’s signature 27 also receives some of the same tools, and more devices will follow.

If you develop Android apps that call Gemini models, see our earlier guide on [adding Gemini 4 Carbon to Android with Firebase AI Logic](/blog/add-gemini-4-carbon-android-firebase-ai-logic/).

## Check device support and update your phone

Confirm your model. Gemini Intelligence features in this Drop reach Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold. The Pixel 10a is excluded from most of them.

Open Settings > System > System update. Download and install any available update. The Drop arrives over the air and may take days to reach every carrier.

Make sure the Gemini app is installed and signed in with your Google account. Some tools also appear in Gboard.

Feature limits vary. Rambler works in more regions and languages than the full automation suite. Task automation currently expands to the UK, India, and Japan in addition to earlier markets. Create My Widget is limited to select countries including the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and India, and currently supports English.

## Use Rambler for cleaner voice typing

Rambler turns spoken thoughts into polished text. It removes filler words such as “um” and “uh,” fixes self-corrections, and formats the result.

On a supported Pixel 10:

1. Open any app that uses Gboard (Messages, Gmail, Docs, or a note-taking app).
2. Tap the microphone icon or long-press the space bar to start voice typing.
3. Speak naturally. Pause when you finish a thought.
4. Rambler processes the audio and shows cleaned text.
5. Review the result, edit if needed, and send or save.

The feature works in all regions and languages supported by Gboard on these devices. Results can vary. Always check the output before you share it.

Google designed Rambler for real-time use while you type. It keeps the original meaning and removes the noise of spoken language.

## Run multi-step tasks with Gemini

Task automation lets you ask Gemini to complete logistics across supported apps. Examples include ordering groceries, booking a ride, ordering takeout, or finding a restaurant reservation.

Open the Gemini app or invoke Gemini from a supported surface.

Try a clear request such as:

- “Order my usual coffee from the nearby café and schedule pickup for 8:30.”
- “Book a ride to the airport for 6 a.m. tomorrow.”
- “Find a table for two at a Italian restaurant downtown tonight.”

Gemini shows the steps it will take and asks for confirmation before it acts. It works with allowlisted apps. Availability is still expanding; check the Gemini app for the current list of supported services in your country.

Proactive suggestions also appear. When a message asks for your location or a meeting time, Gemini can surface an ETA or calendar details with one tap.

## Build a custom widget with Create My Widget

Create My Widget lets you describe a widget in plain language. Gemini builds it and places it on your home screen. No coding is required. It can pull data from Gmail, Calendar, and Search.

On a Pixel 10 or Pixel 11 series phone (where available):

1. Long-press an empty area on the home screen.
2. Tap Widgets.
3. Look for the Create My Widget option.
4. Type or speak a description. Examples: “monthly spending tracker,” “countdown to my vacation with flight details,” or “game day widget with team colors.”
5. Review the preview Gemini generates.
6. Tap Add to place it, or Edit to refine the design.

Widgets update with live information when connected services are enabled. The feature rolls out over the next few weeks and will also reach Pixel Watch models later.

Results depend on how specific your prompt is. Start simple and iterate.

## Tips for better results

Speak or type clearly when using Rambler. Short pauses help the system segment thoughts.

For task automation, give concrete details: preferred app, time, location, and any constraints. Confirm each action before Gemini proceeds.

When creating widgets, include the data source you want (Calendar events, Gmail labels, or a countdown date). Test the widget after adding it and edit if the layout does not match your needs.

Keep your phone updated. New capabilities continue to arrive in the weeks after the initial Drop.

Device Assistance is another Gemini Intelligence tool on select Android 17 devices. You can ask it to free storage, review location permissions, or mute notifications while important calls still ring through.

## Conclusion

Pixel 10 owners can now use Gemini Intelligence features that previously required a Pixel 11. Rambler improves voice typing, task automation handles everyday logistics, and Create My Widget personalizes the home screen.

Update your phone, open the Gemini app, and try the tools that match your region. Features expand over time and depend on language, country, and subscription status.

Official details live on the Google blog and Pixel support pages. Check those sources for the latest availability notes.
