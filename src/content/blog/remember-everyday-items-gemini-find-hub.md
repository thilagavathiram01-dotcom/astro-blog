You put the spare keys under the mat last week. Now you need them and cannot remember the exact spot. Google Find Hub plus Gemini solves this without a Bluetooth tracker.

On Android 16 and later, you tell Gemini where an item is. It stores the details in a new Remembered tab inside the Find Hub app. Later you ask again and get the answer instantly. The feature works for physical objects only: documents, keys, tools, or the place you parked the car.

## Requirements before you start

Your phone must run Android 16 or higher. Install both the Find Hub and Gemini apps and sign them into the same Google Account. Set Gemini as the default digital assistant under Settings > Apps > Default Apps > Digital Assistant App.

You also need an internet connection when you first save an item. Once saved, you can usually view the list offline. Remembered items stay private to your account; you cannot share them with family members yet.

## Save a new item with your voice

Activate Gemini by saying “Hey Google” or by pressing and holding the power button (if touch activation is on). Speak a clear command that names the item and its location.

Examples that work well:

- “Hey Google, remember in Find Hub that my passport is in the top bedroom drawer.”
- “Save to Find Hub that the spare house key is under the front porch mat.”
- “Gemini, my green umbrella is by the front door.”

Gemini confirms the save and adds the entry to Find Hub. Be specific. Vague phrases like “remember my keys” often fail. Mention “Find Hub” or the exact storage spot for better results.

## Add an item manually in the app

Open the Find Hub app. Tap the Remembered tab at the bottom right. Tap Add.

Fill in three fields:

1. **Name** – what the item is (Passport, Spare keys).
2. **Storage spot** – the precise place (Top bedroom drawer, Under front porch mat).
3. **Location** – the broader address or place name (Home, 123 Main St.).

If you grant Find Hub the Location permission and set it to “Allow all the time,” the app can automatically fill your current address when you add or update an item. Tap Save when finished.

You can also add up to two photos and a notes field later by opening the item and tapping the relevant options. Photos help when several similar objects exist.

## Find an item later

Say “Hey Google, where is my passport?” or “Where did I put the AA batteries?” Gemini replies with the stored location and can open Find Hub for you.

Alternatively, open Find Hub, go to the Remembered tab, and browse or search the list. The tab uses a bookmark icon with the Gemini spark badge so you can spot it quickly.

## Update or remove an entry

To move an item, speak a command that includes “update” or “move”:

- “Hey Google, update in Find Hub that my passport is now in the home safe.”
- “Move my spare house key to the kitchen drawer in Find Hub.”

Using the exact existing name causes Gemini to update rather than create a duplicate. You can also open the item in the app and edit the Storage spot or Location fields directly.

To delete an entry, open it in the Remembered tab and tap Remove from Find Hub, then confirm.

## Tips for reliable results

- Keep Gemini as the default assistant. If commands fail, check Settings > Gemini > Connected Apps and confirm Find Hub is enabled.
- Use consistent names. “Passport” and “My passport” may create separate entries.
- Grant location permission if you want automatic address detection.
- Remembered items do not track movement. If you relocate an object without updating the entry, the old location remains until you correct it.
- The feature does not work for digital files, ideas, or events.

## Troubleshooting common issues

If Gemini will not save items, verify the default assistant setting and that both apps use the same Google Account. Explicitly include “Find Hub” in the command. Restart the Gemini app or clear its cache if problems continue.

Offline mode lets you view previously saved items, but new voice saves require internet. The list itself stays available in the Find Hub app.

## Conclusion

Find Hub’s Remembered tab turns Gemini into a simple memory for the physical items you never want to tag with a tracker. A short voice command or a few taps stores the location. The next time you need the spare keys or the passport, you ask and get the answer instead of searching drawers.

The feature is free, private to your account, and available on Android 16 and later once the Find Hub app is updated. Try it with one everyday item today so the habit is ready when you need it most.
