On 16 September 2026 Samsung India unveiled its **2026 Bespoke AI** appliance lineup at The Brief Gurugram. The headline product is a 32-inch **Family Hub refrigerator with AI Vision built with Google Gemini**. Two new air conditioners join the same SmartThings and Knox stack: the Bespoke AI WindFree Pro and the Infinite 1-Way Cassette with WindFree.

This is a setup guide, not a spec recap. Official prices and feature names below come from [Samsung Newsroom India](https://news.samsung.com/in/samsung-india-launches-2026-range-of-bespoke-ai-appliances-designed-to-be-your-true-home-companion) and the earlier [global Family Hub Gemini update](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-expands-ai-capabilities-of-bespoke-ai-refrigerator-family-hub-with-major-updates). Gemini functions can vary by country, language, model, and account age (Samsung requires users to be 18 or older for Gemini features).

## What launched in India

From the official press release:

- **Family Hub refrigerator** with a 32-inch screen, AI Vision, AI Food Manager, Video Recipes, Now Brief, notes, schedules, and localised Family Care and Pet Care. India price: **INR 400,690**.
- **Bespoke AI WindFree Pro AC** with AI-enabled controls, PM1.0 purification, and SmartThings plus Home Appliance Remote Management. Price: **INR 69,990**.
- **Infinite 1-Way Cassette AC with WindFree** in 3.6 kW, 5.2 kW, and 7.1 kW capacities, Edge Lighting, draft-free cooling through micro-holes, 13.8 cm depth. Starting price: **INR 160,000** through authorised partners.

Shared software: AI Home screen on select devices, Bixby with Voice ID, Knox security, SmartThings, and **seven years of software updates** through SmartThings.

Samsung’s own footnote matters: Google Gemini is provided by Google LLC. Availability and accuracy vary. Treat food recognition as a kitchen aid, not a food-safety lab.

## Before you unbox: accounts and network

1. Create or sign in to a **Samsung account** you will keep for years. Family Hub features, SmartThings, and software updates hang off that account.
2. Put the fridge and ACs on a **2.4 GHz / 5 GHz Wi-Fi network** the phone can also see. Isolated IoT VLANs work only if SmartThings can still reach the devices.
3. Install **SmartThings** from the Play Store or App Store and complete the first-run location and notification prompts.
4. If you already own Galaxy phones or wearables, sign them into the same Samsung account. Wearable Good Sleep on the WindFree Pro AC uses Galaxy Wearables sleep data.
5. Update the fridge screen when the first OTN (over-the-network) prompt appears. The May 2026 US Family Hub update and the September 2026 global Tizen 10.0 wave both arrived as software, not new cameras.

Existing 21.5-inch and 32-inch Family Hub models from 2021–2025, plus select 2025 9-inch models, were already in line for Gemini AI Vision in Samsung’s 7 September 2026 global software note. The India 2026 SKU ships with that stack from day one.

## Set up AI Vision and the food list

AI Vision uses the downward-facing interior camera. Gemini is used to recognise more fresh and packaged items than the older list of about 40 pre-studied foods.

1. On the Family Hub screen, open **AI Food Manager** or the Fridge View / AI Vision tile.
2. Allow the interior camera and confirm the door can close fully so the camera sees shelves, not the room.
3. After you stock the fridge, wait for a scan or tap refresh. Check the generated list against what you actually put in.
4. Correct mislabels the first week. Packaged regional brands are in scope on the Gemini update, but lighting, stacked boxes, and covered bowls still confuse cameras.
5. Turn on use-by reminders only for items you will date yourself (opened milk, leftovers). Do not treat an inferred date as a lab result.

Use the list for shopping and recipes. Do not use it as the only inventory for medication or infant formula.

## Use Video Recipes and Now Brief on the 32-inch screen

Samsung’s India launch calls out **search for Video Recipes** as a contextual Gemini experience. The earlier US software post describes related cards: Trending Recipes and FoodNote, which uses the AI Vision list to highlight ingredients you used last week.

Practical flow:

1. Open Video Recipes or SmartThings Food on the Family Hub.
2. Search by dish name or tap a suggestion built from items the camera last saw.
3. Play the video on the door while you cook. Keep hands free; use Bixby for pause or next step if voice control is enabled.
4. Open **Now Brief** and pick cards you actually want: schedule, weather, family care, food. Generative cards have daily generation limits on some One UI / Tizen builds — use them for a morning summary, not infinite remixes.
5. Add notes and a shared calendar so the door is a household board, not a second unused tablet.

Samsung Health integration can surface calorie-aware meal ideas when both accounts are linked. That is optional and only as accurate as the Health data you already log.

## Family Care, Pet Care, and Voice ID

These are the India-localised services in the press release.

**Family Care** (SmartThings) can alert the household to unusual inactivity at times you schedule. Set the window yourself. It is not a medical monitor and should not replace a dedicated care system.

**Pet Care** in SmartThings can track activity, offer breed-based health insights, and remind you about walks. Pair it only if you already use SmartThings cameras or compatible sensors. The fridge camera points inside the cabinet; it does not watch the dog.

**Bixby Voice ID** recognises individual speakers and personalises responses. Enroll each adult in Bixby settings on the Family Hub or a Galaxy phone. Do not enroll children if you do not want their voice to unlock personal cards.

**Knox** is the security layer Samsung cites across the lineup. Keep the Samsung account on 2-Step Verification and review SmartThings locations shared with guests.

## Connect the new ACs without turning the house into a science project

WindFree Pro and the Cassette model are useful when they follow a routine, not when every degree is a chat prompt.

1. Add each AC in SmartThings → Add device → scan the QR / AP setup in the manual.
2. Enable **AI Energy Mode** after a week of normal use so the model has a baseline. Samsung describes energy savings as usage-dependent; do not bank a fixed percentage until you see your own bill.
3. If you wear a Galaxy Watch, turn on **Wearable Good Sleep** so the AC can ease cooling when the watch reports you are asleep. Test it on one night before leaving it on for guests.
4. Use Home Appliance Remote Management only with a technician you trust. Remote diagnostics are for service, not a public MCP experiment.
5. Cassette units add Edge Lighting (nine modes) and an Intertek-certified antibacterial copper filter. Lighting is cosmetic; change filters on the interval in the manual.

## A 20-minute first-week checklist

1. Samsung account + SmartThings on every phone that should control the kitchen.
2. Fridge on Wi-Fi, first software update applied.
3. One full AI Vision scan, then three manual corrections.
4. One Video Recipe cooked from items that were actually on the shelf.
5. Now Brief limited to four cards.
6. Family Care schedule set or left off — do not leave default alerts that fire at 3 a.m.
7. ACs on AI Energy Mode after seven days, not during the first afternoon of setup.

## What this lineup is not

It is not a Google Home speaker. Gemini here is used inside Samsung’s vision and recipe stack. Speaker routines still live in SmartThings, Bixby, or a separate Google Home if you own Nest devices.

It is not a reason to skip a service contract. Seven years of software updates do not include unlimited parts. Keep the invoice and register the product on samsung.com/in/support.

Food recognition will miss items. Voice ID will confuse similar voices. Treat both as convenience features.

## Conclusion

The useful part of the 2026 Bespoke AI launch is the kitchen loop: camera sees food, Gemini helps label it, Video Recipes and Now Brief use that list, SmartThings ties the AC to the same household account. Set that loop up once. Ignore the rest of the marketing pillars until a feature saves you a trip to the store or a 2 a.m. temperature fight.

If you already own an older Family Hub with a camera, check the SmartThings software-update tile before you buy a new door. Samsung has been pushing Gemini AI Vision as an OTN update to earlier 21.5-inch and 32-inch models through 2026.
