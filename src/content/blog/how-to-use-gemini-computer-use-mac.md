Google has added computer use to the Gemini app for Mac. You describe a multi-step task, and Gemini clicks, types, navigates menus, and fills forms across your desktop apps, local files, and the web. It runs in an isolated background window with a live preview in your chat, so your main screen and cursor stay free.

This feature started rolling out on 19 November 2026 in English for personal Google accounts in the United States. It requires a Google AI Plus, Pro, or Ultra subscription and an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS Sequoia 15.0 or later. Work and school accounts are not eligible yet.

If you already use free Gemini Tasks on mobile or the web, computer use extends similar agent capabilities to desktop apps on Mac. See our earlier guide on [how to use free Gemini Tasks](/blog/free-gemini-tasks-personal-assistance-guide/) for the mobile and web version.

## What you need before you start

Computer use has specific requirements. Check these first:

- An Apple Silicon Mac (M1, M2, M3, or later) running macOS Sequoia 15.0 or newer. Intel Macs are not supported.
- At least 8 GB of RAM and 200 MB of free disk space for the Gemini Mac app.
- A personal Google Account for someone aged 18 or older.
- A Google AI Plus, Pro, or Ultra subscription. The free Gemini tier does not include computer use.
- Gemini used in English while located in the United States.

Download the Gemini app for Mac from gemini.google/mac if you do not already have it. Sign in with your personal account.

## How to enable computer use

Open the Gemini app on your Mac and follow these steps:

1. Go to Settings (or press Command + comma).
2. Select Computer use in the side menu.
3. Turn on Allow Gemini to work with your Mac.
4. Grant the required macOS permissions: Accessibility and Screen & System Audio Recording. You can find these in System Settings > Privacy & Security.

The first time Gemini needs access to a specific app, it will ask you to deny, allow once, or always allow that app. Allowed apps appear in your settings list. You can change this later.

Google recommends using Gemini 4 Hydrogen or a newer model for better results on longer tasks.

## How to start a computer use task

Switch to Task mode if needed, then type your request and include @Computer use. Be specific about the goal, the apps involved, and the desired output.

Example prompts:

- Could you take my kid’s butterfly sketch, model it and rig it for me in Blender? @Computer use
- Extract the shipping details from the PDFs in my Downloads folder and fill out the quote form on the carrier’s website. @Computer use
- Pull last week’s metrics from the dashboard and create a summary slide deck in Google Slides. @Computer use

Gemini plans the steps, then works in the background window. You see a live preview in the chat. You can pause or stop the task at any time. It pauses for logins, payments, and other sensitive actions so you can approve them.

While it runs, you can continue using your Mac normally. The background window keeps the work separate.

## Practical examples

Here are scenarios Google highlighted and others that fit the design:

**Creative workflows.** Batch color-grade a folder of photos in your editing app, or build and texture a 3D model from a sketch in Blender. The demo video shows Gemini turning a child’s drawing into a rigged butterfly model.

**Data entry.** Read measurements, addresses, or item details from local PDFs or spreadsheets and complete multi-field web forms.

**Reporting.** Collect screenshots and numbers from several dashboards, then assemble a presentation in Google Slides or another tool.

**Listing items.** Review product photos, research prices, and prepare listings on resale sites.

Start with small, low-stakes tasks while you learn how it behaves. Watch the live preview the first few times.

## Safety and control features

Google built in several controls:

- You choose which apps Gemini can access.
- A live preview shows every step.
- You can pause or stop at any moment.
- Sensitive actions require your approval.
- It attempts to back up files before changing them (backups last 24 hours or until the next prompt).

It can see content in signed-in apps and on-screen information, so avoid tasks that involve highly sensitive data until you are comfortable. Review the official help page for the latest privacy details.

## Tips for better results

Write clear, complete prompts. Include the goal, the specific apps or files, and what “done” looks like. Vague requests often lead to clarifying questions.

Break very long jobs into stages if the first attempt struggles. Use the output of one task as the input for the next.

Keep the Gemini app updated. New connectors for desktop apps such as Blender and Autodesk Fusion appear under Customize > Connectors.

If a task fails or behaves unexpectedly, check the permissions for that app and try a simpler version of the request.

## Limitations to know

Availability is currently limited to English in the United States for personal accounts on paid plans. It is not on Windows, Chromebooks, or Intel Macs. Work and school accounts cannot use it yet.

It is designed for repetitive multi-step work rather than fully open-ended exploration. Complex creative or research tasks may still need your input.

Usage limits apply according to your subscription tier. Higher plans allow more frequent or longer tasks.

Gemini computer use on Mac lets you hand off repetitive desktop chores while you keep working. It requires an Apple Silicon Mac, a paid Google AI plan, and a personal account in the US, but the setup is straightforward once those are in place.

Start with a clear, low-risk task, watch the preview, and expand from there. Combined with free Gemini Tasks on other devices, it covers more of your daily automation needs.
