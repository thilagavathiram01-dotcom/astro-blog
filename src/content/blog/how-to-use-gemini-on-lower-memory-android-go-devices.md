Google expanded the full Gemini app to eligible Android (Go edition) phones on 18 November 2026. These devices, often the only internet link for hundreds of millions of people, previously offered only basic voice commands.

The company re-architected the app so the core Gemini experience runs on hardware with less memory. You get Gemini Live voice conversations, camera-based help, and image creation without needing a flagship phone.

![Person holding a smartphone](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1512941937669-90a1b58e7e9c?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Check Eligibility First

The Gemini mobile app works on Android (Go edition) devices with 2 GB of RAM or more. You must sign in with a personal Google Account, or a work or school account that includes Gemini Apps access.

It is available in all languages and countries where Gemini itself is offered. On these phones, Gemini is delivered as part of the Google Search app and replaces Assistant for Android (Go edition).

Some newer partner devices, including certain models from Transsion, ship with Gemini preloaded.

## Update or Install the App

Open the Google Play Store on your phone. Search for “Google” or “Google Search.” If an Update button appears, tap it.

You can also download the standalone Gemini app directly from Play. If the store shows the app as incompatible, your device does not yet meet Google’s eligibility criteria.

After installation, sign in with your Google Account.

## Start a Conversation

Touch and hold the Home button to open Gemini. On supported phones, press and hold the Power button instead.

“Hey Google” wake-word activation and “Ask about this screen” are not supported on Android Go devices. Launch methods stay limited to the long-press gestures.

## Everyday Tasks You Can Handle

Ask Gemini to call or send a text message to a contact. Set an alarm or create a calendar event. Play music by song name, mood, or activity.

Upload documents or photos to give the conversation extra context. Find local information, such as drive time to work or a restaurant that matches specific requirements.

Gemini Live lets you hold a free-flowing spoken conversation in more than 45 languages. Practice an interview out loud or brainstorm an idea without typing long prompts.

## Use the Camera for Help and Translation

Point the camera at notes, forms, or signs. Gemini can translate the text or walk you through the steps needed to complete a form.

You can also ask Gemini to create images with Nano Banana. One example from Google’s announcement is a flyer for a weekend bakery sale.

These camera and image features run on the same lower-memory hardware after the re-architecture.

## Tips for Smoother Use

Keep storage space free. Budget phones run more smoothly when fewer apps stay open in the background.

Update the Google Search app regularly. Newer versions include the optimised Gemini experience.

If you also use higher-end Android features on other devices, note that Motion Assist and similar advanced tools appear on standard Android 17 builds rather than Go edition phones. See our guide on [Android 17 Motion Assist](/blog/android-17-motion-assist/) for those capabilities.

Test simple voice requests first. Confirm calls, texts, and alarms work before relying on longer Gemini Live sessions.

## Limitations to Expect

Hey Google remains unavailable. You must use the Home or Power long-press to start every conversation.

Ask about this screen is not supported. Screen-aware help stays limited to the camera input method.

Phones with less than 2 GB of RAM do not qualify. Google has not published a full device list; eligibility is confirmed inside the Play Store.

## Conclusion

Eligible Android Go phones now run the full Gemini app. Update the Google Search app or install Gemini from Play, then long-press Home or Power to begin.

Voice conversations, camera assistance, and image creation become available on the same hardware that previously offered only basic commands. The experience is free on the standard Gemini plan, subject to normal usage limits.
