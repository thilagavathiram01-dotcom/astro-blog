Switching between Gmail, Drive, Gemini, and Adobe apps slows creative and document work. Google and Adobe announced an expanded partnership on November 10, 2026 at Adobe MAX to reduce that friction.

The companies will place Adobe tools inside Google products and bring Google’s frontier models into Adobe apps. This guide covers the confirmed integrations, what they change for daily use, and practical next steps.

## PDF editing from Gmail and Drive

Acrobat gains a direct pathway from Google Drive and Gmail. File previews will surface Acrobat as a suggested app, including an “Edit with” entry point.

You open a PDF attachment or Drive file, choose the Acrobat option in the preview, and edit without downloading or switching apps. Google listed this as the first concrete change in its November 10 announcement.

Teams that handle contracts, reports, or design specs in Drive or Gmail gain the shortest path to edits. No separate Acrobat login is required for the basic pathway once it rolls out.

## Creative tools inside Gemini and Search

Adobe is expanding connectors in Gemini and Gemini Enterprise to include Acrobat and additional creative tools. The same tools will appear in AI Mode in Google Search.

Users can ask Gemini or Search AI Mode to summarize a PDF, extract key points, or start a creative asset without leaving the Google surface. Businesses get Adobe’s CX Enterprise Coworker agent inside Gemini Enterprise. The agent helps design graphics, work with PDFs, and launch marketing campaigns in one place.

Google’s post states the agent is already referenced for Gemini for business users. Exact rollout timing was not published on November 10.

For developers building with Gemini, the connectors add structured document and creative actions to existing agent flows. See the existing [Gemini 4 Hydrogen agentic workflows guide](/blog/gemini-4-hydrogen-agentic-workflows-guide/) for model selection patterns that pair well with these tools.

## Google models inside Adobe apps

Google’s latest imaging and video models arrive in Adobe Firefly, Photoshop, Premiere, and Adobe Express. Gemini models help power AI features inside Acrobat.

Creators gain access to Google’s generation quality and speed inside the apps they already use for production. Adobe and Google DeepMind will also explore new editing capabilities for professionals, starting with an image model for Photoshop aimed at near-real-time edits.

Adobe’s official release confirms the model integration direction and the DeepMind collaboration. No public beta dates were given beyond the November 10 announcement.

## How to prepare and use the integrations

1. Check Google Drive and Gmail for PDF previews that list Acrobat as a suggested app. The “Edit with” entry appears in the file preview pane once the pathway is live for your account.

2. Open Gemini or Gemini Enterprise and look for Acrobat or creative tool connectors in the available tools list. Test a short document summary or asset request against a sample PDF.

3. In AI Mode on Google Search, try creative or document queries that reference attached or linked PDFs. Results may surface Adobe actions when the connector is active.

4. Inside Adobe Firefly, Photoshop, Premiere, or Express, check model selectors for Google imaging and video options. Compare output quality and speed against existing Firefly models on the same prompt.

5. For enterprise teams, enable the CX Enterprise Coworker agent in Gemini Enterprise if it appears in your admin console. Assign it to marketing or design workflows that currently move files between apps.

Keep API keys and account permissions limited to the minimum required. Google and Adobe both note that features roll out progressively; availability depends on product tier and region.

## Tips for productive use

Start with one high-volume task. Route PDF reviews through the Drive/Gmail pathway first so the team measures time saved before expanding to generative work.

Pair the new connectors with existing Gemini thinking levels when the request involves multi-step document analysis. Low or medium thinking often suffices for extraction; higher levels help when the output must match brand guidelines.

Monitor token or credit usage in both Google AI Studio / Gemini Enterprise and Adobe accounts. Shared workflows can consume resources on both sides.

Document the exact prompt or instruction that produces usable output. Shared templates reduce variation when multiple people trigger the same creative or PDF task.

## Limitations and current status

The November 10 posts describe direction and first surfaces. They do not list exact launch dates for every feature or guarantee universal availability on day one. Some capabilities (DeepMind Photoshop model, full connector set) remain exploratory or staged.

Enterprise features require appropriate Google Workspace or Gemini Enterprise licenses. Consumer Gemini and Search access follows existing Google AI plan rules.

Test with non-sensitive files until your organization confirms data handling for the combined surfaces.

## Conclusion

The expanded Google-Adobe partnership reduces context switches for PDF work and creative production. Acrobat pathways in Gmail and Drive, connectors in Gemini and Search, the CX Enterprise Coworker agent, and Google models inside Adobe apps form the practical surface.

Check your Drive, Gmail, Gemini, and Adobe apps for the new entry points as they roll out. Use one workflow as the pilot, measure the time saved, then expand.
