Google’s November 2026 Pixel Drop expanded Gemini Intelligence to the Pixel 10 series and introduced Create My Widget. This feature lets eligible Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 owners build personalized home-screen widgets by describing them in plain English—no coding required. Widgets can pull live data from Search, weather, or (where available) connected Google apps such as Calendar and Gmail.

This guide covers eligibility, step-by-step creation, editing, practical tips, and current limitations based on official Google support and the November 2026 announcements.

![Custom Android home screen interface on a smartphone](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1760900051041-90417b9c110e?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Who Can Use Create My Widget

Create My Widget is available if all of the following are true:

- You own a Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, Pixel 10 Pro Fold, or any Pixel 11 series model (Pixel 10a is excluded).
- You are 18 or older.
- Your device language is set to English.
- You are in Australia, Canada, India, the United Kingdom, or the United States.

Gemini Connected Apps integration (for personal data from Gmail, Calendar, etc.) is not available in the UK. Some higher-usage features may require a subscription. Always verify widget content because generative results can contain errors.

The feature is rolling out gradually with the November Pixel Drop (based on Android 17 QPR2). Eligible users can also join the Create My Widget beta on the Play Store for earlier access, even before the full system update arrives.

## How to Create a Widget with an Open Prompt

1. On your Home screen, touch and hold an empty area.
2. Tap **Widgets**, then tap **Create**.
3. Tap **Describe your widget**.
4. Type a clear description and tap **Send**. Examples include “Show me the next soccer game” or “Countdown to my vacation on July 15.”
5. Review the preview. If the widget needs device or app data, tap **Allow**.
6. Tap **+ Add** to place it on your Home screen.

You can also start from suggested templates (Important dates, Sports & fitness, Finance, etc.) and customize them.

During setup you can choose “Use Google apps connected to Gemini” (for personal data) or “Use web data & location only” (for public info such as weather or scores).

## How to Edit a Custom Widget

**During creation (preview screen):**
1. Tap **Edit**.
2. Type the changes you want.
3. Tap **Send** to refresh the preview.
4. Tap **+ Add** when satisfied.

**After the widget is on your Home screen:**
1. Touch and hold the custom widget.
2. Tap **Edit**.
3. Enter your updates and tap **Send**.

Widgets remain editable later, so you can refine prompts without starting over.

## Practical Tips for Better Widgets

- Be specific in prompts. “Monthly spending tracker showing total for November” works better than “money widget.”
- Prefer public data first if you are testing (weather, sports scores, countdowns) before connecting Workspace apps.
- Check accuracy after creation. Generative results are not guaranteed to be perfect.
- Combine with other Pixel Drop features. Pixel 10 users also gain Rambler (filler-word removal in voice typing) and expanded Gemini task automations (groceries, rides, reservations) in supported regions.
- For developers building app widgets instead of personal ones, see adaptive Glance approaches in related guides.

Create My Widget is a user-facing Gemini Intelligence tool, distinct from developer widget APIs. It is designed for personal dashboards rather than third-party app distribution.

## Limitations and Current Availability

- English only, five countries only, 18+ only, Pixel 10/11 series only (no Pixel 10a).
- UK users lack Connected Apps support for personal data.
- Rollout is staged; beta enrollment on the Play Store listing is the fastest official early-access path.
- Coming later to Pixel Watch 4 and Pixel Watch 5.
- Always review generated content. Google notes that some features may require subscription for higher usage.

If the Create button does not appear, confirm your device model, language, age setting, and region, then try joining the Play Store beta and checking for updates.

## Conclusion

Create My Widget turns natural-language descriptions into live home-screen cards on supported Pixel 10 and 11 devices. Start with a simple countdown or sports score, review the preview carefully, and add it in one tap. The feature is part of the broader Gemini Intelligence expansion that also brings Rambler and task automation to more devices.

Official support and the November 10, 2026 Pixel Drop announcements are the authoritative sources. Check the Pixel Phone Help page for the latest eligibility details as the rollout continues.
