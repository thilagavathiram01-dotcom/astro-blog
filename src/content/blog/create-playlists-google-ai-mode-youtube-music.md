Google AI Mode lets you build playlists with YouTube Music without switching apps. Ask for a mood or activity and the tool returns song cards you can save or play right away.

This works on the Google app for Android and at google.com/ai on the web. You need a signed-in Google account. YouTube Music connection is limited to the US in English for now.

![Person using a smartphone to listen to music](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1511707171634-5f897ff02aa9?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What Connected Apps do in AI Mode

Connected Apps give AI Mode access to services you already use. For YouTube Music it generates playlists based on your request and shows an inline card with tracks. Tap Add to library or Play to open the songs in YouTube Music.

Other Connected Apps include Gmail, Google Photos, Drive, Calendar, Canva, and Instacart. Each one pulls relevant data only when your prompt needs it. You control which apps stay linked in your Google account settings.

## Connect YouTube Music

1. Open the Google app on Android or go to google.com/ai in a browser.
2. Tap or click AI Mode.
3. Ask something like “Create a two-hour playlist of lofi beats for sushi night.”
4. If YouTube Music is not linked, follow the on-screen prompt to connect it.
5. Sign in with the same Google account you use for YouTube Music.
6. Grant permission and return to the conversation.

Once connected, future requests skip the linking step. You can review or remove the connection anytime under Connected Apps in your Google account.

## Generate a playlist

Type or speak a clear prompt. Good examples include:

- “Add the main desert theme from Dune to my playlist.”
- “Make a 90-minute mix of city pop and lo-fi for late-night driving.”
- “Playlist for a barbecue: upbeat 2000s rock and pop.”

AI Mode shows “Asking YouTube Music” while it works. The reply includes an inline card listing songs. Use the buttons on the card to save tracks to your library or start playback in the YouTube Music app.

The feature cannot yet add songs to an existing playlist other than Liked Songs, or remove tracks from a generated list. You finish those edits inside YouTube Music.

## Manage Connected Apps

Open your Google account settings and look for Connected Apps or AI Mode settings. Toggle YouTube Music off if you want to stop sharing listening data. The same page lists other linked services.

Enable search history and personalized recommendations for better results. Without history, AI Mode still answers but cannot continue earlier conversations as easily.

## Tips for better playlists

Be specific about length, mood, or occasion. Mention artists or genres you like or want to avoid. If the first result misses the mark, follow up with “make it more upbeat” or “replace the last three songs.”

Test the same prompt on both mobile and desktop. The card layout differs slightly but the underlying connection stays the same.

## Limitations and availability

YouTube Music Connected Apps support is rolling out in the United States for English queries. Other regions may see it later. AI Mode itself is free with a Google account in supported countries.

The tool does not replace the full YouTube Music app. You still open the app to download songs offline, manage full playlists, or use Premium features.

## Conclusion

Connecting YouTube Music to AI Mode turns a simple search into a ready playlist. Open AI Mode, link the app once, and describe the soundtrack you want. The card appears with tracks you can save or play immediately.

Keep the connection active only while you use the feature, and check your account settings if you want to review what data is shared.
