Google has rolled out its latest Gemini overlay design to every Android phone. The update brings the Neural Expressive look—complete with sparkling particles, heavy blur, and shaded edges—to the floating “Ask Gemini” bar that appears over other apps.

This design first appeared on Pixel 11 devices with Gemini Intelligence. It now reaches other Android phones through a Google app update that began appearing on October 29, 2026. Core Gemini features stay the same. Only the visual treatment of the overlay changes.

![Android phone showing AI chatbot interface](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1762330467475-a565d04e1808?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What the New Gemini Overlay Looks Like

When you summon Gemini from any screen, a heavy blur covers the pill-shaped bar. Boundaries become harder to see, and tiny colored specks and swirls surround the input area. The effect gives the overlay an ethereal quality.

After the opening animation finishes, the bar shows translucent edges instead of a solid outline. Slight shading along the perimeter adds depth, especially noticeable in dark mode. In light mode the change is subtler and closer to the previous look.

These elements come from Google’s Neural Expressive design language, introduced earlier for the full Gemini app. The same language now applies to the system-wide overlay.

## How to Get the Sparkling Overlay on Your Phone

The redesign arrives through the Google app, not a system update. Follow these steps:

1. Open the Play Store on your Android device.
2. Search for the Google app.
3. Tap Update if a newer version is available.
4. Force-stop the Google app if the new animation does not appear after the update. Go to Settings > Apps > Google > Force stop, then reopen Gemini.

The change has been confirmed on non-Pixel devices, including a vivo phone. Availability can vary by account or region while the rollout continues.

## Why the Overlay Matters for Daily Use

The overlay lets you ask Gemini questions or give commands without leaving your current app. You can trigger it with voice, the power button, or other shortcuts depending on your device settings.

A clearer visual hierarchy helps you see that Gemini is active over your screen. The sparkling particles and blur draw attention to the input field while the shaded edges make the bar feel more dimensional. Functionality remains identical: you still type, speak, or share screenshots the same way.

## File Generation Upgrade in the Same Update Cycle

Around the same time, the Gemini app gained the ability to generate, preview, and edit documents, PDFs, presentations, and spreadsheets directly in chats. PDF preview and editing works on both web and mobile. Other formats such as Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Markdown, TXT, and code remain web-only for now.

You can ask Gemini to create a file from a prompt, view it inline, highlight sections, and request further edits. Export options include Drive and downloadable formats. For a full walkthrough of the file tools, see our guide on [editing documents and PDFs directly in the Gemini app](/blog/edit-documents-pdfs-directly-gemini-app/).

## Tips for the Best Experience

- Update the Google app first, then force-stop it if the overlay looks unchanged.
- Test the animation in both light and dark modes to see the shaded edges clearly.
- The overlay works over most apps; screen-sharing or full-screen games may limit it.
- Pair the refreshed overlay with Gemini Live for longer voice conversations.
- If you create files often, use the web version of Gemini for full format support beyond PDFs.

## What Remains Unchanged

No new model capabilities or tool permissions arrived with this visual update. Gemini still relies on the same backend models and the same connection settings you already configured. The overlay simply looks more polished and consistent with the Neural Expressive style used inside the main Gemini app.

Google has refined the overlay multiple times this year, adding tools menus and other interface adjustments. The sparkling design is the latest step in that sequence.

## Looking Ahead

The wider rollout means more Android users can see the same polished interface previously limited to Pixel 11 phones. Future Google app updates may refine the animation further or extend similar treatments to Gemini Live and other system surfaces.

For now, the change is purely cosmetic and available after a standard Play Store update. Check your Google app version if you want the new look today.
