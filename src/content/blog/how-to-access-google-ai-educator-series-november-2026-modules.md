Google released new modules for the AI Educator Series on November 4, 2026. These short sessions help K-12 teachers and higher education faculty save time on assessments, game-based learning, and assignment design using Gemini.

The series is free, self-paced, and built with ISTE+ASCD. Sessions last 10–15 minutes for K-12 or 30–45 minutes for higher education. You can complete them in any order and earn a digital badge after a short quiz.

This guide shows exactly how to find and start the November modules.

## What the November Modules Cover

The November content focuses on practical tasks educators said take the most time. Google listed three main areas in its update:

- Build interactive assessments with Gemini, including practice tests and exit tickets that give instant feedback.
- Guide students through game-based learning experiences that show the value of accurate data and human oversight.
- Strengthen academic assignments by spotting questions that encourage shortcuts, then refining them for deeper thinking.

These build on earlier modules about Gemini Notebook, Canvas, and foundational AI use. The series remains non-sequential, so you can jump straight to the new sessions if you already know the basics.

## How to Access the Series

Follow these steps to reach the November modules.

1. Open the official Google AI Educator Series page at edu.google.com/learning-center/google-ai-educator-series.
2. Choose the track that matches your role: K-12 or Higher Education.
3. Select “Explore sessions” under Foundational Understanding, Pedagogical Applications, or Administrative Applications.
4. Look for the newest modules dated November 2026. Google releases new content on the first Wednesday of each month.
5. Click a session title to start. Most require only a free Google account.
6. Complete the short quiz at the end and claim your badge from the Google for Education certifications site.

If you do not see the November sessions yet, wait a few hours or clear your browser cache. Availability can vary by region while the rollout finishes. Educators in the United States should see them first, with other countries following the expansion schedule announced earlier in the month.

## Earn Badges and Track Progress

Each completed session offers a digital badge. Visit the “Earn Your Badge” link on the series site or go directly to educertifications.google/google-ai-educator-series. Take the quiz linked to the module you finished. The badge appears in your account once you pass.

Badges can be shared on LinkedIn or added to a portfolio. Some partners convert them into continuing education credits. Check the series site for current credit options through ISTE and other institutions.

If you already completed earlier modules, the new ones simply add to your collection. There is no required sequence.

## Tips for Busy Educators

- Block 15–45 minutes during a prep period or after school. The modules are designed to finish in one sitting.
- Start with the assessment-building session if you need quick wins for grading and feedback.
- Use Gemini Notebook alongside the modules if you want to turn notes into study materials. See our guide on [Guided Learning in Google Classroom](/blog/guided-learning-google-classroom-guide/) for related student tools.
- Check back on the first Wednesday of December for the next set of modules.
- Sign up for updates on the series page if you want email alerts for new content.

The training uses free Gemini features available to most educators. No paid subscription is required for the core sessions.

## Who Should Start Today

K-12 teachers looking for faster assessment creation and higher education faculty refining assignments will find the November modules most useful. The content is available in English and expands to additional languages over time.

If you teach in a region where the series is already live (United States, India, Korea, Malaysia, Philippines, UK & Ireland), you can begin immediately. Educators in the newly announced countries can watch for the self-paced modules that follow the expansion.

## Conclusion

The November 2026 modules give educators concrete ways to reclaim time while building AI skills. Open the series site, pick one session, and finish it in a single sitting. The badge and the practical workflow are both ready the same day.

Start with the interactive assessments module if you want the fastest return on your time.
