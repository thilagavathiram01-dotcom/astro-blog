Android 17 QPR2 finally brings dedicated status bar controls to Pixel phones. After the November 2026 Pixel Drop, you can decide exactly which notification icons appear and which system indicators stay visible at the top of your screen.

For years Pixel software offered almost no status bar customization. Other Android skins let users hide icons years earlier. The stable QPR2 release changes that for every Pixel from the 6a onward.

This guide shows the exact settings, what each option does, and how the change fits with other Android 17 QPR2 updates.

![Person holding a modern smartphone showing the home screen](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1551650975-87deedd944c3?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What the New Status Bar Settings Do

Google added a Status bar page in Android 17 QPR2. You reach it from two places:

- Settings > System > Status bar
- Settings > Notifications > Status bar

Both routes open the same page. The page splits into two groups: Notification icons and System icons.

Notification icons has three choices:

- Show all notification icons
- Show priority notifications only (the default)
- Show no notification icons

System icons has three independent toggles:

- Mute — Show when device is muted
- Vibrate — Show when device is set to vibrate
- Battery percentage — Show battery level

These toggles only control visibility. Turning them off does not disable the underlying notifications or features. Battery percentage and vibrate indicators already existed in other menus; the new page simply gathers them in one place.

## How to Open Status Bar Settings

1. Open the Settings app on your Pixel.
2. Scroll to System and tap it.
3. Tap Status bar.

Alternatively:

1. Open Settings.
2. Tap Notifications.
3. Tap Status bar.

The page loads immediately. Changes apply as soon as you select them. No restart is required.

If you do not see the Status bar entry, confirm your phone is on Android 17 QPR2. Check Settings > System > Software updates. The November 2026 build is CP4A.261105.040.A1 on most models and CP4A.261105.040.D1 on the Pixel 11 series.

## Control Notification Icons

Priority only is the default. It shows icons only for notifications you have marked as priority. This keeps the status bar cleaner while still alerting you to important items.

Choose Show all notification icons if you want every app’s icon visible. Choose Show no notification icons for a minimal look. Notifications still arrive in the shade and can still make sound or vibrate; only the icons disappear from the status bar.

Many users set the option to None for a cleaner home screen and rely on the notification shade or the Always-On Display for alerts. You can switch between the three options at any time.

## Hide or Show System Indicators

The Mute toggle controls the speaker-with-slash icon that appears when the phone is muted. The Vibrate toggle controls the vibration icon. The Battery percentage toggle controls the numeric percentage next to the battery icon.

You can turn any combination on or off. For example, you might keep the battery percentage visible while hiding both the mute and vibrate icons. The system still respects Do Not Disturb and vibration settings; the icons simply stay hidden.

Note that some of these controls duplicate existing options. Battery percentage can also be toggled under Settings > Battery. Vibrate can be managed under Sound & vibration. The Status bar page centralizes the visual choices.

## Practical Tips for Daily Use

- Start with Priority only and adjust after a day or two. You will quickly see which apps generate the most status bar clutter.
- Pair a clean status bar with the new custom color options in Wallpaper & style. See our guide on [Android 17 QPR2 custom colors](/blog/android-17-qpr2-custom-colors-guide/).
- If you hide all notification icons, check the notification shade periodically so you do not miss quiet alerts.
- The setting is per-user on devices that support multiple users.
- Changes survive reboots and software updates within the same Android 17 release.

The brightness override added in the same update is unrelated but useful. Quick Settings brightness now works even when an app forces maximum brightness. That change lives in the Quick Settings panel itself, not on the Status bar page.

## Who Gets the Feature

Android 17 QPR2 rolled out starting 10 November 2026 to supported Pixel devices from the Pixel 6a through the Pixel 11 series, plus the Pixel Fold and Pixel Tablet. Older Pixels do not receive it. Non-Pixel phones will get the controls only if their manufacturers adopt the platform change in later updates.

The feature first appeared in QPR2 Beta 4 in late August 2026 and reached stable with the November Pixel Drop.

## Limitations to Know

You cannot yet rearrange icons, change their size, or hide every system icon (Wi-Fi, signal, battery outline, etc.). Google has not added a full status bar editor. The current controls focus on notification volume and the three most requested system indicators.

Some reports note slight differences in menu wording across builds, but the three notification options and three system toggles remain consistent.

## Conclusion

The Status bar page in Android 17 QPR2 is a small but practical addition. It gives Pixel owners direct control over visual clutter without requiring third-party apps or custom ROMs. Open Settings > System > Status bar, pick your notification icon level, and toggle the system indicators you want visible. The change takes effect immediately and works on every Pixel that received the November 2026 update.
