College students still have a window to claim a year of Google AI at no charge. Google opened the offer on 19 August 2026, and the published terms say it can be redeemed until 31 December 2026. After that date, the claim page is not the path Google listed.

The plan you get depends on where you study. Eligible students in the United States can get Google AI Pro for 12 months. Eligible students in more than 140 markets outside the United States can get Google AI Plus for 12 months. A separate bundle pairs Google AI Pro with YouTube Premium at up to 70% off in select markets.

## What the student offer includes

Google’s 19 August post values the U.S. Google AI Pro year at $19.99 a month if you paid retail. That year unlocks 4x higher Gemini usage limits than non-AI subscribers, Gemini Spark, Gemini in Gmail and Docs, 5 TB of storage, and Google Health Premium.

Outside the United States, the free year is Google AI Plus. It includes Gemini Omni, 2x higher Gemini usage limits than non-AI subscribers, and 400 GB of storage. Footnotes exclude Albania, Bolivia, Canada, Macau, Hong Kong, and Tunisia. The United States uses the Pro offer instead.

A payment method is required at sign-up even when the first year is free. Unless you cancel earlier, Google AI Pro charges $19.99 a month after the trial. Google AI Plus charges $4.99 a month, or the local equivalent, after its trial. Cancel anytime from Google One.

School-issued accounts cannot use the YouTube bundle. Google’s terms say verification runs through SheerID, the offer is for individual use, and the YouTube bundle can last up to four years with re-verification.

## Check eligibility before you start

Google One Help lists the general rules for a student offer:

- You study at a higher-education institution in a country where Google AI Plus or Google AI Pro is supported.
- You verify student status through SheerID, usually with a valid school email.
- You use one personal Google Account, not a family-group seat.
- You are not redeeming through a third-party partner plan, Pixel Pass, or Google Fi.

Family-group members must leave the shared plan first. Signing up for a student offer while you are on another Google One extended trial replaces that offer. If you already pay for Google AI Ultra and switch to a student offer, Ultra stays active until the current billing cycle ends.

Google One Help also says recurring add-ons, including upgraded storage packs, are not available on a student offer. Treat the storage figure in the offer you select as the storage you get. Do not expect a separate 10 TB add-on to stack on top.

If you already redeemed an earlier Pro student trial, Help says SheerID may ask you to verify again. Read the on-screen price before you confirm.

## Claim the plan step by step

Use the official landing page. Google One Help warns against opening a SheerID link someone pasted in a forum.

1. Sign out of extra Google accounts. Stay signed in only on the personal account that should hold the membership.
2. Open [one.google.com/ai-student](https://one.google.com/ai-student).
3. Select the student offer shown for your country. In the U.S. that is the 12-month Google AI Pro trial. Elsewhere it is usually Google AI Plus, or the YouTube Premium bundle if you want streaming included.
4. Complete SheerID verification when asked. Have a school email ready. SheerID’s own FAQ covers document checks if the email domain is not recognized.
5. Add or confirm a payment method, then subscribe.

Google redirects you back to Google One after SheerID approves you. The button is **Get student offer**. Confirm the plan name and the renewal price before you tap Subscribe.

If the page says the offer is not available, do not keep refreshing a third-party link. Recheck country, age of the account, and whether the account is already on a partner membership.

## Open the Gemini student hub

The paid plan and the study tools are separate. Google said the student hub, study notebooks, interactive visualizations, and Deep Research in Gemini Live rolled out to Gemini app users, not only to people on the student plan.

On 1 October 2026, Google Workspace said the student hub is also available to Google Workspace for Education users. Rapid Release and Scheduled Release domains both have it. Personal accounts still reach it on the web and in the mobile app, in languages the Gemini app supports.

Open the hub at [gemini.google.com/students](https://gemini.google.com/students). From there you can start a study notebook, make flashcards, or take a practice quiz. New learning tools are meant to land in the same hub rather than in a separate app.

For a full notebook setup, follow [How to Set Up Gemini Study Notebooks for Any Course](/blog/gemini-study-notebooks-setup/). Upload the syllabus and lecture files first so the diagnostic quiz is based on your course, not a generic outline.

## Use the study tools that shipped with the offer

Google listed four habits next to the claim link. Name a goal in a study notebook, upload notes, and take the diagnostic quiz so lessons follow your files. Ask for a visual topic, such as a 3D DNA structure, when you want an interactive model you can rotate. In Gemini Live, ask for a Deep Research pass, then lock the screen. Google said the report runs in the background and a notification arrives when it is ready. Check citations before you use them in an assignment.

Start from the student hub instead of hunting in settings. Usage limits still apply. A free year raises those limits. It does not remove them.

## What happens when the year ends

Google One Help says the student offer converts to a regular paid Google AI Plus or Google AI Pro plan when it ends, except where local law requires extra consent. Google notifies you before the price change.

If you have the YouTube Premium student bundle with Google AI Pro, the plan can move to Google AI Pro with YouTube Premium Lite in markets where Lite is included. Check the country list in Help before you assume ad-free Premium continues at the student rate.

People on the Google AI Pro student offer or the YouTube bundle must re-verify with SheerID, usually every 12 months, for up to four years. Google emails you when re-verification is due. Skip it and the discount can drop, with the regular price charged.

If you already have a separate YouTube Premium or Premium Lite subscription, a student bundle can create a duplicate. Manage that duplicate in YouTube settings so you are not billed twice.

## Tips before you subscribe

Use a personal Gmail address. Campus accounts are excluded from the YouTube bundle terms, and some school admins block consumer Gemini features.

Read the renewal line on the subscribe screen. The first year is free only if you stay inside the offer terms and cancel before a charge you do not want. Set a reminder for late November 2026 if you have not claimed yet. The published redeem-by date is 31 December 2026.

Keep verification on one account. After you subscribe, open the student hub the same day and upload one course pack. Do not treat Gemini output as a graded answer key.

## Conclusion

The 2026 student offer is a 12-month Google AI Pro trial in the United States and a 12-month Google AI Plus trial in most other supported markets, both free until the trial ends, both requiring a card and SheerID. The claim window listed by Google runs through 31 December 2026.

Verify on one personal account at one.google.com/ai-student, confirm the renewal price, then open the Gemini student hub and attach your real course files. Cancel from Google One before the trial converts if you do not want the paid plan.
