OpenAI launched the Rosalind Academic Plan on October 15, 2026. It gives eligible U.S. university research labs up to 30 free ChatGPT Business Standard seats for 12 months. Labs also get a path to GPT-Rosalind, the company’s specialized life sciences model.

Principal investigators and lab leads can apply on behalf of their teams. The plan targets life sciences work in areas such as medicinal chemistry, genomics, and wet-lab assistance. Research data shared through the plan is not used to train OpenAI models by default.

This guide covers eligibility, the application steps, what you receive, and practical tips. All details come from OpenAI’s official announcement and help center.

## Check Eligibility First

The Rosalind Academic Plan is limited to academic labs at U.S. universities. Individual researchers or labs outside the United States cannot apply under the current rules.

A principal investigator (PI) or designated lab lead must submit the application. You need an institutional email address that you can verify. Provide information about your university, lab, and planned research use.

Labs at universities that already have an OpenAI Enterprise agreement follow a different path. They request access through their university’s Enterprise process instead of the public academic application.

If your lab does not have an Enterprise agreement, use the direct academic-lab application.

## What the Plan Includes

Approved labs receive:

- Up to 30 Business Standard seats at no cost for up to 12 months. A payment card is required during setup even though the seats are free during the offer period.
- Access to all commercially available frontier OpenAI models within standard Business usage limits.
- ChatGPT and Codex for literature review, data work, and analysis code.
- Path to GPT-Rosalind and Rosalind Workbench after separate trusted-access approval. The workbench supports specialized tool orchestration for life sciences tasks.

API access and API credits are not included. Usage limits still apply. After approval, the PI manages which lab members receive seats.

OpenAI states that research data is not used for model training by default. Zero Data Retention is not available under this offer.

## How to Apply

1. Confirm your lab is at a U.S. university and you are the PI or lab lead.
2. Prepare details on your institution, lab focus, and intended research uses of the tools.
3. Go to the academic-lab application linked from OpenAI’s Rosalind Academic Plan page or help center article.
4. Verify your institutional email and submit the form.
5. Wait for review. OpenAI evaluates the institution, lab leadership, and planned use as part of the trusted-access process.
6. If approved, complete the offer setup. You will provide a payment card. The PI then adds approved members to the workspace.

Labs with existing Enterprise agreements should contact their university administrator or OpenAI account team instead.

After approval, GPT-Rosalind access requires the additional trusted-access step. The PI can enable it for members once that clears.

## Tips for a Strong Application

Focus the description on legitimate life sciences research goals. Mention specific areas such as genomics analysis, protein work, or experiment planning where the tools would help.

Have a clear institutional safety contact ready if requested. OpenAI reviews for appropriate use given the specialized nature of the model.

Apply soon if your lab qualifies. The offer has fixed terms, including the 12-month window once set up.

Once you have access, explore ChatGPT features for research workflows. You can also check related guides on reusable AI skills in ChatGPT for organizing repeated analysis tasks.

## After Approval

The PI receives control of the Business workspace. Add lab members who need access. Each seat follows normal Business limits.

Use the tools for literature synthesis, hypothesis generation, data review, and code for analysis. GPT-Rosalind, once enabled, supports more specialized reasoning across biological data and tools via Rosalind Workbench.

Monitor usage so the free period covers your highest-value work. After 12 months, standard pricing applies unless OpenAI extends the offer.

## Conclusion

The Rosalind Academic Plan lowers the barrier for U.S. university labs to try OpenAI’s life sciences tools. Eligible PIs can apply now through the official process for free seats and a route to GPT-Rosalind.

Confirm your status, gather the required lab details, and submit. Review OpenAI’s announcement and help center for the latest application link and any updates.
