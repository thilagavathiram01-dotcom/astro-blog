Google expanded the Gemini app to eligible Android (Go edition) devices on November 18, 2026. These phones serve as the main internet connection for hundreds of millions of people. Hardware limits previously restricted them to basic voice commands. The re-architected app now delivers the core Gemini experience on devices with less memory.

This guide covers eligibility, setup steps, available features, and current limitations. All details come from Google’s official blog and support pages.

## Who Qualifies for the Gemini App on Android Go

The Gemini mobile app runs on Android (Go edition) devices with 2GB of RAM or more. You need a personal Google Account, or a work or school account that includes Gemini access. Family Link managers can disable access for users under 13, or the applicable age in their country.

Availability matches the languages and countries where Gemini already works. On these devices, Gemini appears as part of the Google Search app. Some newer phones from partners such as Transsion may ship with it preloaded.

Google notes the rollout is gradual. Check the Google Search app in the Play Store for updates.

## How to Set Up Gemini on Your Android Go Device

Follow these steps to confirm eligibility and start using the app.

### Step 1: Update the Google Search App

1. Open the Google Play Store on your device.
2. Search for “Google” or “Google Search.”
3. Tap Update if the button appears.
4. If it says Open, you already have the latest version.

You can also download the standalone Gemini app from the Play Store on supported devices.

### Step 2: Sign In and Launch

Sign in with your eligible Google Account. To start a conversation:

- Touch and hold the Home button.
- Or press and hold the Power button on supported devices.

The app replaces Assistant for Android (Go edition) and offers a conversational experience.

### Step 3: Test Basic Functions

Ask a simple question to confirm it works. You can request a call to a contact, set an alarm, or check local drive times.

## Key Features Available on Android Go

The re-architected app keeps the main Gemini tools that work well on limited hardware.

### Gemini Live Conversations

Talk naturally with Gemini Live in more than 45 languages. Use it to brainstorm ideas or practice for an interview without typing long prompts. The voice conversation flows without switching modes.

### Camera Help for Notes and Forms

Point the camera at notes, paper forms, or text. Gemini can explain the content or translate it. This helps with everyday documents on lower-memory phones.

### Image Creation with Nano Banana

Create images directly in the app. Google’s example includes generating a flyer for a bakery sale. The feature uses the Nano Banana model.

### Everyday Tasks

You can also:

- Call or text contacts.
- Find local information such as restaurants or drive times.
- Set alarms or create calendar events.
- Upload documents, photos, or files for more context.
- Play music by mood or activity.

## What Does Not Work on Android Go Devices

Two features stay off on these phones. “Hey Google” voice activation is not supported. “Ask about this screen” also does not work. You must use the Home or Power button gestures instead of hands-free triggers.

The app focuses on efficiency for lower storage and memory. Performance may vary by exact device model. Google has not published a full list of specific phone models.

## Tips for Better Results

- Keep the Google Search app updated through the Play Store.
- Sign in with a personal account for the broadest access.
- Use Gemini Live for spoken practice in supported languages. Pair it with the new regional voices if they appear in your language settings. See our guide on [how to use new regional voices in the Gemini app](/blog/gemini-app-regional-voices-how-to-guide/).
- For camera tasks, ensure good lighting and hold the phone steady.
- Upload relevant files when you need detailed help with documents.
- Check Family Link settings if the device belongs to a younger user.

These steps help the limited hardware deliver useful responses without extra load.

## Why This Expansion Matters

Lower-memory Android devices reach large populations in many regions. Bringing the full Gemini conversational tools to them expands access to AI assistance for education, business, and daily tasks. The engineering change focused on running the core experience smoothly rather than adding every advanced option.

Users on standard Android phones see no change. This update targets the Go edition specifically. Upcoming Transsion devices will include the app out of the box in markets where those brands sell widely.

Google continues to adjust Gemini features across device classes. This November 2026 release marks a practical step for entry-level hardware.
