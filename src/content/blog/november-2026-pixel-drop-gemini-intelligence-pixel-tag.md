Google’s November 2026 Pixel Drop began rolling out on November 10. It expands Gemini Intelligence to Pixel 10 series phones (except the Pixel 10a) and launches the Pixel Tag tracker. The drop also adds Create My Widget, upgraded Call Screen on Pixel 11, new icon styles, and more. Updates continue over several weeks, so check Settings regularly.

This guide shows what arrived, which devices get each feature, and how to start using the main ones. Pair the new tools with the security controls covered in our [Advanced Protection features guide](/blog/android-17-advanced-protection-six-features/).

## What the November 2026 Pixel Drop includes

The official announcement lists these highlights:

- Gemini Intelligence on Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold. Features include Rambler, multi-step task automation, proactive suggestions, and Create My Widget.
- Upgraded Call Screen on Pixel 11 series (U.S.). Gemini handles the conversation with unknown callers until you join or end it.
- Pixel Tag, Google’s first-party Find Hub tracker, available starting November 11.
- New custom icon styles on Pixel 6a and newer.
- Pixel Search expanded to Pixel 6a and newer.
- HiLight support for Google Messages on select Pixel 11 models.
- Step-by-step workouts on Pixel Watch 3 and newer.

Availability depends on device, region, language, and account age (many Gemini features require users 18+). Check the footnotes on Google’s blog post for exact limits.

## Enable Gemini Intelligence on Pixel 10

Gemini Intelligence previously launched with the Pixel 11 series. The November drop brings core pieces to last year’s flagship line.

1. Update your phone. Go to **Settings → System → System update** and install any available package.
2. Open the Gemini app and confirm you are signed in with a Google Account that meets the age and region requirements.
3. For Rambler (voice typing cleanup): Open Gboard, start a message or note, and use voice input. Rambler removes filler words such as “um,” fixes mid-sentence corrections, and handles language switches. Review the text before sending.
4. For multi-step tasks: In Gemini, ask it to order groceries, book a ride, or place a takeout order using supported apps. You can watch progress and stop the task at any time. Currently listed for Japan, UK, and India in the announcement (availability may expand).
5. Proactive suggestions appear in context—for example, sharing your ETA or summarizing selected text. No extra toggle is required once the feature reaches your device.

Create My Widget lets you describe a widget in plain language (monthly spending tracker, game-day widget in team colors, trip countdown that pulls flight details). It works on Pixel 10 and 11 series in select markets. Look for the option in the widget picker or Gemini after the update arrives.

## Set up Pixel Tag with Find Hub

Pixel Tag is a slim, IP67-rated tracker that uses Bluetooth, Ultra-Wideband (UWB) on compatible phones, and the Find Hub network of more than one billion Android devices. Battery life is rated up to one year on a replaceable CR2032 coin cell. Price at launch: $29.99 for one or $99.99 for a four-pack (availability limited to select countries).

1. Buy the tag from the Google Store or authorized retailers once it is in stock in your region.
2. On an Android 9 or newer phone, open the Find Hub app (or the device’s Find My Device interface).
3. Follow the on-screen Fast Pair prompt to connect the tag. Place it near your phone and confirm the pairing.
4. Name the tag and attach it to keys, a bag, wallet, or other item.
5. To locate it: Open Find Hub, select the tag. When nearby and your phone supports UWB, you get directional guidance and distance. Farther away, the network provides approximate location.
6. To ring the phone from the tag: Press the button on the tag twice. The phone should sound.
7. Replace the battery when the app notifies you. Twist the case open, swap the CR2032, and close it.

The tag works with any Android 9+ phone, not only Pixels. Sharing with up to 10 people is supported through Find Hub.

## Other practical tips

- Icon styles: After the update, go to home-screen settings or the wallpaper and style menu. New styles apply across app icons on Pixel 6a and newer.
- Call Screen (Pixel 11, U.S.): When an unknown number calls, choose to screen it. Gemini conducts a more natural conversation and shows a live transcript. Join or end the call from the screen.
- Pixel Search: Available on Pixel 6a and newer. It searches photos, emails, messages, calendar events, and settings from one place.
- Watch features: Step-by-step workouts roll out to Pixel Watch 3+. Automatic watch unlock when the phone is unlocked nearby arrives on compatible UWB models in the coming weeks.

Rollouts are phased. If a feature is missing, wait a few days, check for system and Play services updates, and confirm your region and language settings. Some tools stay limited to specific markets or require the Gemini app.
