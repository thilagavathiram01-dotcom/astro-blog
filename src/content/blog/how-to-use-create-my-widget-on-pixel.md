Google’s November 2026 Pixel Drop added Create My Widget to eligible Pixel phones. You describe the information you want in plain language, and Gemini builds a Material-style home screen widget. The feature works for users 18 and older on Pixel 10 and newer devices (except the Pixel 10a) in English in Australia, Canada, India, the United States, and the United Kingdom.

This guide covers the exact Pixel Launcher flow, data access rules, creation from prompts or templates, editing, and practical limits. It focuses on what the official Pixel Phone Help page documents so you can set up useful widgets without guesswork.

## Who can use Create My Widget on Pixel

All four conditions must be true:

- Device: Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, Pixel 10 Pro Fold, or any Pixel 11 series model. Pixel 10a is excluded.
- Language: English.
- Country: Australia, Canada, India, the United States, or the United Kingdom.
- Age: 18 or older.

Gemini Connected Apps integration is not available in the UK. Widgets that need personal data from Gmail, Calendar, or other Workspace services may be limited there. Some higher-usage scenarios may require a subscription. Always check generated widget content for accuracy before relying on numbers or dates.

The feature arrives with the November Pixel Drop system update. Go to Settings > System > System update and install any available package. The Create option appears in the Widgets menu once the update reaches your device. Pixel Watch 4 and Pixel Watch 5 will gain support later in select regions.

## Create a widget from an open prompt

This path gives the most control. Be specific about the data and layout you want.

1. On your Home screen, touch and hold an empty space.
2. Tap **Widgets**, then tap **Create**.
3. In the text box, tap **Describe your widget**.
4. Type what you want the widget to show. Examples: “Show me the next soccer game” or “Countdown to my vacation on July 15.”
5. Tap **Send**.
6. Review the preview. Tap **+ Add** to place the widget on your Home screen.

If the widget needs device or app data, tap **Allow** when prompted. You can connect Google Workspace accounts during setup so the widget can pull calendar events, email summaries, or similar information. Manage Connected Apps later in the Create My Widget settings.

The preview screen also offers **Edit** and **Create new**. Use Create new if the first result does not match what you described.

## Start from a template

Templates help when you know the topic but want suggested layouts.

1. Touch and hold an empty space on the Home screen.
2. Tap **Widgets**, then **Create**.
3. Select a pre-defined category or suggestion such as **Important dates**, **Sports & fitness**, or **Finance**.
4. Optionally customize the prompt or template details.
5. Tap **Add** to place the widget.

Common starting points include weather alerts, world clocks, daily briefs, and finance trackers. You can still edit the result after adding it.

## Edit a custom widget

You can refine a widget while creating it or after it is on the Home screen.

During creation:
1. On the preview screen, tap **Edit**.
2. Type the changes you want.
3. Tap **Send** to update the preview.
4. Tap **+ Add** when finished.

After the widget is placed:
1. Touch and hold the custom widget on the Home screen.
2. Tap **Edit**.
3. On the “Edit your widget” screen, enter your updates.
4. Tap **Send**.

Resizing and removal work like standard Pixel widgets. Long-press the widget and drag the handles or drag it to Remove.

## Data access and privacy notes

Widgets that display personal information require permission to access the necessary Google apps or device data. You choose what to allow during setup. In the UK, Connected Apps integration is unavailable, so personal-data widgets may not function the same way.

Google recommends checking custom widget responses for correctness. Generative results can contain errors. Higher usage may hit subscription limits. Review the permissions you grant and adjust them later through the feature’s settings or Android’s standard app permissions controls.

## Tips for better results

Write clear prompts. Include the data source, preferred style, and any limits. “Show monthly shopping spend with icons” works better than “spending.” Mention connected apps when you want live personal data.

Test a few variations before settling on one design. Use templates for common needs and free-form prompts for specific requests. Combine Create My Widget with standard Google widgets so your Home screen stays balanced.

If a widget fails to load data, check the permissions and Connected Apps settings. Confirm the November Pixel Drop update is fully installed. Availability can still roll out in phases depending on carrier and device.

## Limitations to know

Create My Widget is not available on the Pixel 10a or older Pixel models. Language and region support is limited at launch. Widgets cannot replace fully custom third-party tools that support arbitrary layouts or animations. They focus on glanceable information generated from natural language and connected Google data.

Watch support is coming later. Results vary by prompt quality and available data, so review previews carefully.

## Conclusion

Create My Widget lets eligible Pixel 10 and 11 owners build personalized Home screen widgets by describing them. Start from a template or an open prompt, allow the needed data access, review the preview, and edit as required. The feature is part of the November 2026 Pixel Drop and works within the listed device, language, country, and age limits. Pair it with other Pixel personalization options for a Home screen that surfaces the information you check most often.
