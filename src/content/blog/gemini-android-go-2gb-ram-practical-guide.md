Google’s Gemini app now runs on eligible Android (Go edition) phones with 2 GB of RAM or more. This brings conversational AI, camera help, and image creation to hundreds of millions of people who rely on budget smartphones for education, work, and daily tasks.

Previously these devices offered only basic voice commands through Assistant Go. Google re-architected the Gemini experience so core features work smoothly on constrained hardware.

## Who Can Use Gemini on Android Go?

You need an Android (Go edition) device with 2 GB RAM or more. Sign in with a personal Google Account or a work/school account that has Gemini access. Availability matches the countries and languages where Gemini is already supported.

Devices with less than 2 GB RAM are not eligible. “Hey Google” wake word and “Ask about this screen” are not available on Go edition phones.

For more on related Android features, see our guide to [adaptive layouts on Googlebook](/blog/adapt-android-apps-googlebook/).

## How to Get Started

1. Open the Google Play Store and search for “Google” or “Google Search”. Update the app if an update is available.
2. Search for “Gemini” and install the official app. Some newer devices include it preloaded. Google is also working with partners such as Transsion to preload it on upcoming phones.
3. Launch Gemini by touching and holding the Home button, or by pressing and holding the Power button on supported phones.

On Android Go, Gemini is delivered as part of the Google Search app and replaces Assistant Go.

## What You Can Do

Gemini on these devices supports the core experience:

- **Gemini Live**: Hold natural conversations in more than 45 languages. Practice job interviews, brainstorm ideas, or talk through problems out loud.
- **Camera help**: Point your camera at notes, forms, signs, or documents for instant translation, summaries, or step-by-step explanations.
- **Image creation**: Generate custom images with Nano Banana. For example, ask for a simple flyer with bright colors.
- **Everyday tasks**: Make calls or send texts to contacts, set alarms, create calendar events, play music by mood or artist, and upload photos or files for extra context.

These features run by sending requests to Google’s cloud, so the phone itself does not need to store large models.

## Practical Examples

**Practice an interview**  
Open Gemini Live and say: “Help me practice for a job interview as a retail assistant.” Gemini role-plays in real time.

**Translate a form**  
Photograph a business permit or school form and ask: “Walk me through what I need for this form.”

**Create a flyer**  
Ask: “Create a simple flyer for my weekend bakery sale using bright colors and fresh bread images.”

## Tips for Best Performance

Close background apps before long conversations or image generation. Use short, focused prompts. Keep your device updated and clear the cache regularly. Connect to Wi-Fi when possible for faster responses on image creation or Live sessions.

## Limitations to Know

No “Hey Google” wake word and no “Ask about this screen” on Go devices. Some advanced capabilities available on higher-end phones may be limited. Performance varies by exact model and current background load. Older phones below 2 GB RAM remain unsupported.

## Why This Matters

Hundreds of millions of people in emerging markets use Android Go phones as their primary connection to the internet, learning, and small business. Bringing Gemini Live, camera tools, and image creation to these devices expands practical AI help without requiring a flagship phone.

## Try It Today

Update your Google app or download Gemini from the Play Store. Long-press the Home or Power button and start asking questions. Whether you are practicing for work, translating documents, or creating simple marketing materials, the core Gemini experience is now available on more devices.
