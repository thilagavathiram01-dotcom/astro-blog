Google rolled out the Gemini app on Apple CarPlay starting 11 November 2026. iPhone users can now hold natural, hands-free conversations with Gemini Live directly from the car display. Conversations sync back to the Gemini app on your phone the moment you park.

This guide covers the exact setup steps from Google’s announcement, what the feature can do, and the limits you need to know.

## Requirements before you start

You need an iPhone that supports CarPlay (wired or wireless). Update the Gemini app from the App Store to the latest version. Grant microphone permission when prompted, or enable it later under Settings > Gemini > Microphone.

Google requires you to open the Gemini app on your phone and start a Gemini Live conversation at least once before the CarPlay icon works. Do this while parked. Daily Brief, if you plan to use it, must also be enabled in the Gemini app first. Certain personalisation features, including Daily Brief, are unavailable to users under 18 or in the European Economic Area and Nigeria.

The rollout is global across every language Gemini Live supports, but it arrives in stages. If the icon does not appear immediately after connecting, check for the latest iOS and Gemini updates, then try again later.

## Step-by-step setup

1. Update the Gemini app on your iPhone from the App Store.
2. Open Gemini, accept microphone access, and start one Gemini Live session on the phone. Speak a short test phrase and end it.
3. Connect your iPhone to the car via cable or wireless CarPlay as you normally do.
4. On the CarPlay home screen or side rail, look for the Gemini app icon. Tap it.
5. Choose “New Conversation” or select a recent chat to continue.

If the icon is missing, go to Settings on the iPhone > General > CarPlay > your car > Apps and confirm Gemini is included. You can also drag the icon to the first page under Customise.

Once open, the interface shows listening, thinking, and responding states along with End and Mute controls. You can interrupt Gemini mid-response, change the subject, or pause when traffic needs your full attention.

## What you can do during the drive

Gemini on CarPlay works as a conversational companion rather than a command-only assistant. Because it connects to Google Workspace apps such as Gmail and Google Calendar (when you have enabled them), you can:

- Ask “What does my day look like?” or “Help me prep for my 10am with the design team.”
- Request your Daily Brief, catch up on unread emails, or get a summary of a long thread.
- Ask for local recommendations: “What’s a quick lunch spot in downtown Austin that’s easy to get in and out of?”
- Brainstorm or rehearse out loud: “Let’s roleplay my Q4 pitch — act as a skeptical VP and challenge my strategy.”

You cannot place calls, send messages, or start navigation through this Gemini app on CarPlay. Those remain Siri tasks. Gemini in Google Maps on CarPlay continues to handle route-related questions separately.

All conversations automatically appear in the chat history of the Gemini app on your iPhone after you park. For more on using Gemini across devices, see our guide on [how to use Gemini 4 Hydrogen in the Gemini app](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-4-hydrogen-in-gemini-app/).

## Tips and current limits

Set up and test everything while the car is parked. The first Live session and microphone permission happen on the phone, not the car screen.

Keep your eyes on the road and hands on the wheel. Use short prompts and interrupt freely when needed. Mute is available if a passenger needs quiet.

Daily Brief and some other personalisation features require prior enablement and are restricted by age and region. Free and paid Gemini tiers both receive the CarPlay app; limits on usage still apply according to your plan.

If the icon does not appear after several days, confirm your iOS version supports third-party voice AI apps (iOS 26.4 or later is commonly required for such apps) and that the Gemini app is fully updated.

## Conclusion

Gemini Live on Apple CarPlay turns commute time into useful conversation. Update the app, run one Live session on the phone, connect, and tap the icon. Your chats stay in sync so nothing is lost when you arrive.

Check Google’s official announcement for the latest availability notes as the staged rollout continues.
