Google's November 2026 Pixel Drop adds Pixel Solver to the Calculator app on Pixel 11 series devices. This AI-powered Ask Math feature solves everyday math problems in plain language and shows the steps.

It works alongside the new Unit Conversion tool available on more Pixel models. Both arrive with the update that started rolling out on 10 November 2026.

This guide covers setup, usage, and tips based on official Pixel support documentation.

![Smartphone calculator on a desk with financial charts](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1707761918029-1295034aa31e?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What is Pixel Solver?

Pixel Solver, labeled Ask Math in the app, uses generative AI to handle word problems and multi-step calculations. It covers consumer math, financial questions, and standard educational topics such as pre-algebra or square footage.

The feature is currently available on Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold. Supported languages include English, French, German, Japanese, and Spanish.

You receive the solution plus a step-by-step breakdown. You can ask follow-up questions or export the result to the main calculator screen. Custom formula cards can be saved for reuse under My tools.

## How to open and use Ask Math

Start by ensuring your Pixel 11 device has the November 2026 update installed. Check under Settings → System → Software update.

**Steps:**

1. Swipe up from the bottom of the screen and open the Calculator app.
2. At the top right, select Ask math.
3. Type your question or pick a suggested one. Examples include splitting a restaurant bill or calculating room dimensions.
4. Tap Send.
5. Review the solution and steps. Ask a follow-up if needed.
6. Select Export to calculator if you want to continue with the numbers on the keypad.

When a custom formula card appears, tap Save to store it. Manage saved formulas, rename, or delete them from My tools in the top left of the Calculator screen.

You can also review past conversations. Open History inside Ask Math, resume a thread, delete one, or start a New chat.

## Unit Conversion in Calculator

Unit Conversion is a separate addition that works on Pixel 6a and newer devices. It handles measurements and currencies without leaving the app.

**How to convert units:**

1. Open Calculator.
2. Select Unit conversion above the keypad.
3. Choose a category such as Length, Weight, Temperature, Volume, Time, Speed, Area, or Currency.
4. Pick source and target units.
5. Enter the value. Results appear instantly.
6. Use Swap to reverse the direction.

For currencies, it pulls real-time rates from Google Finance. The app suggests your base currency from location. Swipe down in the conversion area to refresh rates manually. It supports over 150 global currencies.

This pairs well with Pixel Solver for problems that mix units, such as converting measurements in a word problem.

## Tips for accurate results

Phrase questions clearly and include all relevant numbers. “Split a $85.40 bill among 4 people with a 18% tip” works better than a vague request.

Check the step-by-step explanation. AI results can vary, so verify important calculations manually or with the standard keypad.

Save useful formulas early. Reuse them for recurring tasks like tip calculations or area measurements.

Keep the Calculator app and system updated. Features roll out in phases depending on carrier and region.

If Ask Math is missing, confirm you have a Pixel 11 series device and the supported language is set.
