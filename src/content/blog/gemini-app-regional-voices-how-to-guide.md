Google rolled out 66 new regional voices for the Gemini app on Android and iOS on November 16, 2026. These options cover U.S. English, U.K. English, Indian English, Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian, and Korean. Each voice matches local speech rhythms instead of generic accents.

The update builds on a preview from Google I/O. Your choice applies across Gemini Live conversations, language practice, and the Listen button that reads chat replies aloud. This guide shows the exact steps to preview and select a voice.

## What the New Regional Voices Offer

The voices focus on natural cadence, pitch, and inflection. Google tuned them for everyday clarity rather than exaggerated stereotypes or forced slang. A selected voice carries through the full app experience so conversations feel more personal wherever you are.

Options vary by language and region. Reports note more than 10 U.K. English variants that include Geordie, Scouse, Brummie, Scottish, Welsh, and Northern Irish styles. The app menu does not label every voice by city or region, so previewing helps you pick the one that fits.

## How to Change Gemini’s Voice on Android and iOS

The setting lives in the Gemini mobile app. It does not require a paid plan. Rollout may take time, so update the app from the Play Store or App Store if the new options do not appear yet.

### Step 1: Open the Gemini App

Launch the Gemini app on your phone. Sign in with your Google account if prompted.

### Step 2: Go to Voice Settings

You have two paths:

1. Start a Gemini Live session. Tap the three-dot menu in the Live interface and look for voice options.
2. Or open app settings: tap your profile picture or initial at the top, then Settings, then Gemini’s voice.

### Step 3: Preview the Voices

Swipe through the list or tap individual options. Each plays a short sample. Check that your phone volume is up and silent mode is off. Listen for rhythm and tone that match how people speak in your area.

### Step 4: Select and Confirm

Tap the voice you prefer. Confirm the selection if the app shows a button. The change takes effect immediately for Gemini Live and for any Listen playback in regular chats.

You can return to the same menu later to switch voices again.

## Tips for Getting the Most from Regional Voices

- Update the Gemini app first. Server-side changes sometimes need a refresh.
- Test in Gemini Live with a short prompt about local food or daily routines. The voice should sound natural without slang overload.
- Use the Listen icon on a long chat reply to hear the same voice read text aloud.
- If options seem limited, check your device language settings. The available set follows your primary language.
- For language practice, pick a voice that matches the accent you want to hear regularly.

## Why Regional Voices Matter

A matching cadence makes spoken replies easier to follow during driving, cooking, or quick lookups. Language learners gain consistent exposure to local rhythms. The same setting covers both interactive Live sessions and passive Listen playback, so you do not manage separate voices.

Google continues to expand language support across Gemini products. This voice update is one practical step that lands directly in the mobile app.
