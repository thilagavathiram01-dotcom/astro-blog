Holiday shopping often means jumping between tabs, saving screenshots, and losing track of the best price. Google’s Universal Cart, launched on October 15, 2026, gives U.S. shoppers one persistent cart that works across Google Search (including AI Mode) and the Gemini app.

You can save items from different retailers in one place. The cart watches prices and stock for you. When you are ready, you check out with Google Pay or transfer items to the merchant’s own site. Merchants stay the seller of record either way.

## What Universal Cart Does

Universal Cart is built on Google’s Shopping Graph, which contains more than 60 billion product listings that refresh continuously. It is not a full replacement for every retailer cart. It is a layer that keeps your saved items together across Google surfaces and adds alerts and payment help.

Four core functions stand out:

- Save products from multiple retailers while you browse Search or chat in Gemini.
- Receive notifications when a saved item drops in price or returns to stock.
- See relevant promo codes and card-perk suggestions drawn from Google Wallet.
- Complete checkout with Google Pay in the cart (on supported stores) or transfer the items to the merchant’s site.

Participating merchants at launch include Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta Beauty, Walmart, Wayfair, and many Shopify stores. YouTube and Gmail integration is planned but has no timeline yet.

## How to Save Products Across Search and Gemini

Start in a place that supports the cart. Open Google Search, switch to AI Mode if it is available, or open the Gemini app. Describe what you need in plain language: “waterproof walking shoes under $120” or “a leather tote in black from several brands.”

Product cards appear with a way to save the item. Tap the save or cart control instead of visiting the retailer site immediately. The item lands in your Universal Cart. Later, open Gemini and ask for a related product. Save that one too. Both items stay in the same cart when you return.

Google’s example is a handbag from Steve Madden saved in AI Mode, then a sweater from Quince and lip gloss from Ulta Beauty added in the Gemini app. All three appear together later. The cart remembers your progress across sessions, so you do not restart the search each time.

If the cart option does not appear yet, the feature is still rolling out gradually. Sign in with a Google account and try again after a short wait.

## Track Prices and Stock Automatically

Once items are in the cart, the Shopping Graph keeps checking them. You receive notifications about price drops or restocks. You no longer need to open each retailer site every day.

Open the cart to review the full list. Items are grouped by store. Each entry shows the current price, stock status, and return window when available. You can remove items you no longer want. The cart continues monitoring the rest in the background.

This is especially useful for gifts you want to buy later. You lock in the research now and let the system watch the numbers.

## Use Wallet Insights and Promo Codes

Universal Cart connects to Google Wallet. If you have several cards saved, the cart can suggest which one offers better perks for the purchase—points, cash back, or extended warranty coverage. It also surfaces relevant promo codes it finds.

Review the suggestions before you pay. You still choose the final payment method. The feature only surfaces options; it does not apply them automatically.

## Check Out Two Ways

When you are ready to buy, you have two paths:

1. **Google Pay inside the cart.** On supported stores you review the order, confirm the address and shipping, and pay with a card from Google Wallet. Google Pay handles the transaction. The merchant still sends the confirmation and provides customer support.
2. **Transfer to the merchant cart.** Move the selected items to the retailer’s own site. A pre-filled cart opens there so you finish the purchase on their page. This option works even when native Google Pay checkout is not available for that store.

In both cases the merchant remains the seller of record. You receive order updates directly from them. You cannot check out items from several retailers in a single simultaneous payment; each store processes its own order.

## Practical Tips for Everyday Use

Sign in with the same Google account on every device you use for shopping. The cart follows the account, so a phone save appears on a laptop later.

Check notification settings for Google apps if price alerts are important. Enable them so drops and restocks reach you promptly.

Compare the two checkout options on a small test purchase first. Some stores support both; others only support the transfer. Seeing the difference once makes later decisions faster.

Keep Google Wallet current. Updated cards and addresses improve the perk suggestions and speed up Google Pay checkout.

For broader Google shopping tools, see our guide on [connecting Google Wallet to Gemini](/blog/connect-google-wallet-to-gemini/).

## What Is Not Available Yet

Universal Cart is U.S.-only at launch. Google has not announced a date for other countries. YouTube and Gmail support remains on the roadmap without a schedule. Compatibility checks between products (for example, PC parts) were mentioned in the May 2026 preview but are not part of the October launch description.
