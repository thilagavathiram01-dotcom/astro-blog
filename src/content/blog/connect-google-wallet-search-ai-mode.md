Google Search AI Mode now lets you opt in to connect Google Wallet so responses can use your spending context. Drive and the Gemini app connections follow in the coming weeks. These join existing options like Gmail, Calendar, and Photos.

The rollout started today. AI Mode can only read Wallet information. It cannot move, manage, or invest money. You stay in control and can disconnect anytime.

If you already connected Wallet inside the Gemini app, the Search version follows a similar opt-in pattern. See [How to Connect Google Wallet to Gemini for Insights](/blog/connect-google-wallet-gemini/) for the app-side setup.

## What the Wallet connection adds in AI Mode

Personal Intelligence in AI Mode already draws from connected Google apps to make answers more relevant. Wallet adds financial context from accounts linked in Wallet.

With Wallet connected you can:

- Map financial milestones from your spending data.
- Identify ways to trim everyday spending.
- Maximize credit card benefits such as cash-back categories, travel perks, or reward points based on cards linked via Wallet.

Google states that AI Mode receives read-only access. It does not perform transactions or change accounts. The same read-only rule applies when Wallet is connected in the Gemini app.

Support for Google Drive and the Gemini app is coming in the coming weeks. Once available, AI Mode can use document context from Drive and chat context from Gemini. You can already connect Search history to Personal Intelligence in the Gemini app; the reverse connection arrives soon.

## How to connect Google Wallet in AI Mode

Connection is strictly opt-in. You choose which apps to link and can turn them off later.

1. Open Google Search and go to AI Mode (or start an AI Mode query).
2. Open Search Personalization settings. Look for the profile menu or personalization controls.
3. Select Connected Apps.
4. Choose Google Wallet and complete any confirmation prompts.

You can also reach the same controls via the direct link Google provides for managing connected apps in Search personalization. After connecting, tailored responses in AI Mode and AI Overviews can reference your recent search context plus the new app data.

Soon a Personalization icon will appear below tailored responses so you can see the context AI Mode used. If something is wrong, reply in the thread to correct it (for example, “I’m no longer a vegetarian”).

The feature is rolling out. Eligible accounts may see the Wallet option immediately or after a short delay.

## What you can ask once Wallet is connected

Try prompts that combine spending data with other connected sources:

- “Show my spending patterns from the last quarter and suggest ways to reduce everyday costs.”
- “Which credit card benefits match my recent travel spending?”
- “Summarize my financial milestones this year based on Wallet data.”

Answers stay inside the read-only limits. Do not expect AI Mode to pay bills, transfer money, or update card settings. Confirm any numbers in the Wallet app or your bank before acting on them.

Personally relevant responses also appear in AI Overviews. If you have been researching a trip, a new AI Mode thread can continue from that context without you repeating details.

## Privacy controls and turning it off

You decide when to connect and when to disconnect. The same Connected Apps screen lets you turn Wallet (or other apps) off at any time.

AI Mode does not train directly on your full Wallet, Gmail, or Photos data. Google has stated this for earlier Personal Intelligence connections and applies the same principle here. You can also adjust broader Search personalization in your Search settings and turn personalization off entirely if you prefer.

When Drive and Gemini app connections arrive, they will use the same opt-in model. Review the list periodically so only the apps you want remain active.

## Tips for better results

Connect only what you need. Wallet helps with spending questions; leave it off if you never ask about finances.

Use specific prompts. “Trim my coffee spending” works better than a vague “help with money.”

Check the Personalization icon once it appears. It shows which context shaped the answer so you can correct or ignore it.

If Wallet data looks incomplete, confirm the accounts are linked inside the Google Wallet app first. AI Mode only reads what Wallet already holds.

Keep the same personal Google Account across devices. Work or school accounts follow different rules for connected experiences.

## Conclusion

Connecting Google Wallet to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode adds spending context to Search without giving AI Mode the ability to move money. Follow the Search Personalization → Connected Apps steps, stay on a personal account, and disconnect whenever you want.

Drive and Gemini app connections arrive in the coming weeks and use the same controls. Start with Wallet if spending insights matter to you, then expand only as needed.
