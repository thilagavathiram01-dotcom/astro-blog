Indian Android users can now store an Aadhaar Verifiable Credential directly in Google Wallet. The October 2026 Google System Updates include the ability to upgrade an existing digital ID to an Aadhaar card inside the app. This creates a secure digital ID for age and identity checks without carrying the physical card everywhere.

A digital Aadhaar in Wallet does not replace your physical card issued by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI). It works for supported partners and online services that accept verifiable credentials.

![Android smartphone on a desk](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1511707171634-5f897ff02aa9?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Requirements Before You Start

You need an Android phone running version 9 or higher. Check your version under Settings > About phone > Android version. A screen lock must be set—PIN, pattern, password, or biometrics. The official Aadhaar (mAadhaar) app must be installed from the Play Store and signed in.

Update Google Wallet through the Play Store if a newer version is available. The feature is available to users in India only.

## Add Aadhaar from the Official Aadhaar App

1. Open the Aadhaar app on your phone.
2. Sign in with your Aadhaar number and verify your identity using the OTP sent to your registered mobile number.
3. Tap **Add to Wallet**.
4. Select Google Wallet from the list.
5. Tap **Continue** and follow the on-screen instructions.

The app may show a “Reviewing your request” message while UIDAI verifies the details. You receive a notification when the credential is ready.

## Add or Upgrade from Google Wallet

1. Open the Google Wallet app.
2. Tap the **+** (Add) button.
3. Select **Aadhaar**.
4. The app redirects you to the Aadhaar app if it is not already open.
5. Complete the sign-in and verification steps listed above.
6. Confirm the addition.

If you already hold another digital ID in Wallet, the October Play services update lets you upgrade it to an Aadhaar credential through the same flow. The Wallet app lists Aadhaar under available IDs once the Play system update reaches your device.

![Person holding a smartphone](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1556742049-0cfed4f6a45d?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## How to Use Your Digital Aadhaar

Once added, open Google Wallet and tap the Aadhaar card. Authenticate with your screen lock. You can present it in person via NFC or QR code at supported locations, or share it online when an app or website requests a verifiable credential.

Google lists early partners such as PVR INOX for age checks, BharatMatrimony for verified profiles, and Atlys for visa forms. Selective disclosure means the service receives only the attributes it needs—such as age—rather than your full details.

## Tips for Smooth Setup

- Keep your phone connected to the internet during verification.
- Ensure the mobile number linked to your Aadhaar matches the one on the device.
- If the Aadhaar option does not appear, wait for the latest Google Play services update and restart the Wallet app.
- The credential stays encrypted on the device and requires your screen lock to view.

## Conclusion

Adding an Aadhaar Verifiable Credential to Google Wallet takes a few minutes once the Aadhaar app is ready. The October system updates make the upgrade path clearer for users who already store other digital IDs. Always verify the source of any request before sharing credentials, and remember that the digital version supplements—not replaces—your physical Aadhaar card.
