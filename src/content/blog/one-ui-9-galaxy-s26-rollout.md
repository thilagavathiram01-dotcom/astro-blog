Samsung Electronics America announced on **16 September 2026** that **One UI 9** is expanding beyond the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, Z Fold8, and Z Flip8. The first slab phones in this wave are **Galaxy S26 Ultra, S26+, and S26**. The company says the update will reach other Galaxy models gradually after that.

This guide is a working path: confirm the update is for your device, install it, then turn on the features Samsung actually listed in the official newsroom post. Availability of some tools still depends on country, language, Samsung account, and model.

## Who gets the update first

Samsung’s U.S. newsroom post is explicit about the current official wave:

- Already shipping at launch: **Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, Z Fold8, Z Flip8**
- Starting 16 September 2026: **Galaxy S26 Ultra, S26+, S26**
- Later: other Galaxy devices, “gradually”

Do not assume an S25, Tab, or older Fold is in this batch just because a leak said so. Wait for **Settings → Software update** on that exact model.

One UI 9 sits on **Android 17**. Galaxy AI *basic* features from Samsung are free; enhanced Samsung AI and third-party AI can have different terms and fees. Samsung lists the basic set under “Advanced intelligence” in its Services Terms.

## Check for and install One UI 9

1. Connect to Wi-Fi and plug in the phone (or keep the battery above about 50%).
2. Open **Settings → Software update → Download and install**.
3. If One UI 9 is staged for your model and region, you will see a new One UI / Android 17 package. Read the size and changelog on that screen.
4. Download, then tap **Install**. The phone will restart.
5. After first boot, open **Settings → About phone** and confirm the One UI version.
6. Sign in with the **Samsung account** you use for Galaxy AI, Gallery, and Care+ if those tools matter to you.

If the button still says “Your software is up to date,” you are not in this wave yet. Checking every hour does not jump the queue. Wait a day and try again, or use **Download updates manually** only if Samsung published a firmware code for your CSC.

Back up first: **Settings → Accounts and backup → Back up data**. An OS jump is the wrong time to skip a copy of photos and messages.

## Use My FanCam on a recorded video

**My FanCam** tracks one person in a clip and keeps them centered as the scene moves. Samsung positions it for recitals, dance floors, and sports—moments you would otherwise crop by hand.

Official limits from the newsroom footnotes:

- Samsung account required
- Video must be **720 × 720 pixels or higher**
- Results vary

A practical path:

1. Record or import a clip that is at least 720p on a side.
2. Open the video in Gallery (or the editor Samsung surfaces after the update).
3. Choose the **My FanCam** / person-tracking option and tap the subject once.
4. Let the tool reframe. Export a social-ready crop instead of editing every second by hand.

If the subject leaves the frame or a second person crosses in front, pick the clip again and re-tag. FanCam is a tracker, not a multi-cam director.

## Customize Now Brief with generative cards

**Now Brief** is the daily summary surface. One UI 9 adds **generative and editable cards** so the brief is not a fixed stack.

Samsung says you can create cards across **health, weather, and video**, and that:

- Some apps need internet and consent to share data
- **Personal data intelligence** must be enabled

Setup:

1. Open Now Brief from the home screen, lock screen module, or Galaxy AI entry Samsung shows after the update.
2. Enable personal data intelligence if you want cards that use your routines.
3. Add or generate a card for the slice you actually check (sleep and steps, local weather, or a video recap).
4. Edit or remove cards that add noise.

If a card is empty, the source app is not connected or consent is off. Fix the source instead of regenerating the same card.

## Open Warranty and Care from Settings

**Warranty and Care** moves support into Settings so you are not hunting through a browser bookmark.

Samsung lists these actions (features can differ by country):

- Check warranty coverage across connected mobile devices
- Run self-diagnostics
- View repair estimates
- Manage Samsung Care+
- Book a service appointment

Path:

1. Open **Settings** and search **Warranty** or **Care**.
2. Select the device in the list if you own more than one Galaxy.
3. Run diagnostics before you assume hardware failure.
4. Use Care+ or booking only after the estimate makes sense for your region.

## Turn on Now Nudge, Interpreter, scan, and recorder upgrades

These are the everyday tools Samsung grouped as “do more with less effort.”

**Now Nudge** surfaces context-aware next steps. Example from Samsung: when you plan over text, pull reservation details or save a shared location without leaving the thread. Auto suggestion must be on; location needs location permission.

**Creative Studio** can suggest image ideas tied to upcoming events (a friend’s birthday is Samsung’s example). It needs the Creative Studio app, a network connection, a Samsung account, and it is not available in every country or language. Output is not guaranteed. Stickers share only to other Galaxy devices.

**Interpreter** adds a **Thread View** so you can scroll earlier translated turns. Conversation Mode is faster, and quick playback works with **Galaxy Buds4 Pro, Buds4, Buds3 Pro, and Buds3**. English and Spanish are pre-installed; other languages need a free download. Samsung account required.

**Document Scan** handles curved book pages and folded corners with less manual flatten. Large-screen layouts help on foldables and tablets. Finger Eraser is improved for stray thumbs.

**Voice Recorder** summaries use headings and tables so you can skim a meeting instead of rereading a wall of text.

## Review Security Brief, Privacy Alerts, and Scam Detection

One UI 9 puts more security status in the same daily surface as weather.

- **Security Brief** can show malware alerts, apps from unknown sources, and extra permissions inside Now Brief.
- **Privacy Alerts** use on-device AI to flag background permission grabs. Samsung lists support on S26 / S26 Ultra, several S25–S23 and FE models, recent Z Fold/Flip generations, and selected Tab S11–S9 devices. The tool “may not account for all relevant factors.”
- **Scam Detection** expands to more countries and languages. The September 16 footnotes name **Japan, Canada, and India** for this expansion. Availability still varies by region, language, model, and One UI version.

Open the alert, read what permission or app triggered it, then revoke or uninstall. Do not swipe past a malware card because Now Brief also shows the weather.

## What this update is not

One UI 9 is not a full visual reboot. Reviewers who compared it with One UI 8.5 describe thicker Quick Settings sliders, more blur, a docked search bar on some builds, and new Settings menus more than a new design language. Some Android 17 features also arrive on a Google schedule, so they may show up after the Samsung package.

Treat the newsroom list as the contract. If a feature is missing, check footnotes first (account, resolution, country, Personal data intelligence) before assuming your install failed.
