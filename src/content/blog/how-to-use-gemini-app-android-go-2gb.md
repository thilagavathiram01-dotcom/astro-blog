Google expanded the full Gemini app to eligible Android (Go edition) phones on November 18, 2026. These devices often have limited RAM, yet the re-architected app now delivers core features including Gemini Live voice conversations, camera-based help, and image creation. Hundreds of millions of people who rely on lower-memory phones for daily internet access can now use the assistant beyond basic voice commands.

This replaces the previous Assistant experience on Android Go. The app runs as part of the Google Search app on these devices and is available in all languages and countries where Gemini is supported.

## Check Device Eligibility First

Confirm your phone meets the requirements before installing. You need an Android (Go edition) device with 2GB of RAM or more. Sign in with a personal Google Account, or a work or school account that has Gemini Apps access. Family Link managers can restrict access for users under the applicable age.

Android Go is the lightweight version of Android designed for entry-level hardware. Many phones from 2023 onward meet the 2GB threshold. Older models with less RAM remain unsupported. Regular non-Go Android phones already supported the standard Gemini app with a 2GB minimum and Android 9 or later.

On supported Go devices, Gemini appears through the Google Search app. Update the Google Search app via the Play Store if needed. You can also download the standalone Gemini app from Google Play on eligible phones. Some upcoming partner devices, including models from Transsion, will ship with it preloaded.

## How to Start a Conversation

Launch Gemini by touching and holding the Home button. On supported devices, press and hold the Power button instead. The interface opens directly for voice or text input.

Note that "Hey Google" wake-word activation and "Ask about this screen" are not supported on Android Go edition devices. Use the button hold method each time.

Once open, speak naturally or type. Gemini Live supports flowing voice conversations in more than 45 languages. You can practice for an interview, brainstorm ideas, or walk through a task without typing long prompts. Camera input lets you point the phone at notes, forms, or signs for translation or step-by-step help. Image creation uses Nano Banana to generate custom visuals, such as a simple flyer.

## Practical Tasks You Can Complete

The app handles everyday actions on these lower-memory phones:

- Ask it to call or send text messages to contacts.
- Set alarms or create calendar events.
- Check drive times or find local places with specific details, such as a restaurant open on a certain day near an EV charger.
- Upload documents, photos, or other files to add context to a chat.
- Play music by mood or activity, for example "Play quiet acoustic songs for a dinner party."

These features run efficiently because Google re-architected the client to reduce its memory footprint while keeping the core experience intact. Model inference still happens in the cloud, so the phone mainly handles input capture, networking, and display.

## Known Limits on Android Go

Not every Gemini feature transfers to these devices. Hands-free "Hey Google" activation is unavailable. Screen-aware queries that analyze the current app content are also disabled. Performance depends on available storage and background processes; close unused apps if responses feel slow.

The free Gemini plan applies by default with standard usage limits. Paid Google AI Pro or Ultra plans unlock higher limits and additional models where available. Work and school accounts follow their organization's access rules.

Google has not published detailed performance benchmarks for specific 2GB models. Expect reliable results for the listed core tasks, with the same availability constraints that apply to Gemini worldwide.

## Tips for Smoother Use

Keep the Google Search app updated through the Play Store. Clear cache if the hold-button launch fails after an update. Sign out and back in if account access issues appear.

For camera tasks, ensure good lighting and hold the phone steady. When uploading files, start with smaller documents to test connectivity. Use short, clear prompts for voice sessions to reduce processing time.

If your phone has expandable storage, free up space so the app can cache temporary files. Restart the device after the first install to ensure system integration with the Home and Power buttons.

## What Comes Next

Google continues expanding Gemini access across hardware tiers. The November 18 update specifically targets the gap for Android Go users who previously had only basic commands. Partner preloads on future Transsion and similar devices will make the experience available out of the box.

Monitor the official Gemini Apps Help page for any regional rollout adjustments or new supported features. The core capabilities—voice conversations, camera help, image creation, and everyday actions—are available now on eligible 2GB Android Go phones.

This change brings consistent AI assistance to a much larger share of Android users without requiring flagship hardware.
