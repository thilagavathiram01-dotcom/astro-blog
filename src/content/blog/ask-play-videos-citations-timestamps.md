Google updated the Play Store to version 53.8 on 26 October 2026. One change stands out for everyday users: Ask Play responses can now include relevant videos that carry citations and timestamps.

Instead of a text-only answer, you may see a video clip that jumps straight to the moment that matches your question. This builds on earlier Ask Play video highlights that arrived in August.

## What Ask Play does

Ask Play is Google’s conversational tool inside the Play Store. You type or speak a natural-language question about an app or game, and it replies with summaries drawn from store listings and user reviews.

It already understands follow-up questions and can compare apps side by side. The October update adds visual evidence so you spend less time hunting through long videos or reviews.

Earlier versions showed short video highlights inside Ask Play. The new citations and timestamps make those clips more precise.

## How the new video responses work

When you ask a question that a video can answer, Ask Play surfaces a relevant clip. The response includes a citation that points back to the source and a timestamp that starts the video at the useful section.

This is useful for how-to questions, gameplay mechanics, or feature walkthroughs. You see the exact part of the video instead of watching from the beginning.

Google listed the change in the official Google System Release Notes for Play Store v53.8: “You can now find relevant videos with citations and timestamps in Ask Play responses.” The feature is tagged for phones.

Rollouts of Play Store updates are gradual. Update the Play Store app and check again if the videos do not appear right away.

## How to use Ask Play video citations

1. Open the Google Play Store on your Android phone.
2. Tap the search bar at the top.
3. Type a clear question. Examples: “How do I enable offline mode in this app?” or “Show me the best way to complete the first level.”
4. Send the question. Wait for the response.
5. If a video appears, note the timestamp and citation. Tap the video to play it from that point.
6. Ask a follow-up if you need more detail. Ask Play keeps the context of the conversation.

You can also open an app’s listing and use the “Ask Play about this app” card for questions specific to that title. The same video treatment applies when a clip is relevant.

## Tips for clearer answers

- Be specific. “How do I change the language in Spotify” works better than “Spotify settings.”
- Mention the app name when the question is broad.
- Check the citation. It usually links to a developer video, official tutorial, or well-known walkthrough.
- Update the Play Store to v53.8 or later. Go to Settings > Apps > Google Play Store > App details, then check for updates.
- If no video appears, the question may not have a matching clip yet. Ask again with different wording or check the app’s listing videos manually.
- Videos work on phones. The changelog does not list the feature for tablets, Wear, or other form factors.

## Conclusion

Ask Play’s addition of cited, timestamped videos turns the Play Store search into a faster way to learn about apps. You get a direct jump to the useful section instead of scrolling through results or full videos.

Update the Play Store, ask a specific question, and see whether a video clip appears in the response. The feature is live for phones as of the 26 October 2026 system notes.
