Google added a dedicated Unit Conversion mode to the Calculator app as part of the November 2026 Pixel Drop. Pixel 6a and newer phones can now convert measurements and currencies without leaving the app or opening a browser.

This guide shows exactly how to open the feature, switch between currency and measurement categories, and get accurate results.

## Check your phone and update

Unit Conversion works on the Pixel 6a, Pixel 7 series, Pixel 8 series, Pixel 9 series, Pixel 10 series, and Pixel 11 series. It does not require Gemini Intelligence or any AI model.

Open Settings, tap System, then System update. Install any pending Pixel Drop or security update. After the update finishes, force-close the Calculator app and reopen it so the new mode appears.

If the option is missing, wait a few hours. Google rolls the feature out in phases.

## Open Unit Conversion mode

Swipe up from the bottom of the screen and tap the Calculator app. Above the keypad you will see three modes: Basic, Scientific, and Unit conversion. Tap Unit conversion.

The screen changes to a conversion layout with a category menu at the top and two unit selectors below it.

![Calculator app open on a smartphone](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1728825445493-1a6e89164511?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Convert currencies in real time

1. With Unit conversion selected, tap the category menu and choose Currency.
2. The app suggests a base currency based on your current location. Tap the base currency to change it if needed.
3. Tap the target currency and pick the one you want (for example, USD to EUR).
4. Enter the amount on the keypad. The converted value appears instantly.
5. Tap Swap to reverse the direction.

Rates come from Google Finance. Swipe down on the conversion area to refresh the rates manually. Over 150 currencies are supported.

## Convert measurement units

1. Stay in Unit conversion mode.
2. Open the category menu and pick Length, Weight, Temperature, Volume, Time, Speed, or Area.
3. Select the source unit on the left and the target unit on the right.
4. Type the number you want to convert. The result updates live.

You can also perform basic math on the converted value without switching modes. For example, convert 5 kilograms to pounds, then multiply the result by 2 directly in the same screen.

## Tips for faster conversions

- Long-press a recent conversion to copy the result or reuse the pair of units.
- If rates look outdated, swipe down once to force a refresh.
- For mixed-unit problems (for example, feet and inches), enter the full expression in Basic mode first, then switch to Unit conversion if needed.
- Pixel 11 users can combine this with Ask Math for step-by-step explanations. See our guide on [using Pixel Solver in Calculator](/blog/how-to-use-pixel-solver-calculator-pixel-11/).

## Common questions

**Does it work offline?** Measurement conversions work offline. Currency rates need a network connection for the latest values, but the last cached rate is used when offline.

**Why don’t I see the option?** Confirm your Pixel is on the November 2026 software or later and that you are on a supported model. Restart the phone if the mode is still missing.

**Can I save favorite units?** The app remembers the last pair you used in each category.

## Conclusion

Unit Conversion turns the built-in Calculator into a practical everyday tool for travel, cooking, shopping, and quick math. Once the November Pixel Drop is installed, the feature is available with a single tap and needs no extra apps or accounts.
