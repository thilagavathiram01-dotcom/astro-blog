Google rolled out Tasks in Gemini on 17 November 2026. The feature lets you hand off multi-step work so the AI acts while you move on. It is free for eligible users in the US, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India and runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen.

Chat answers questions and stops. Tasks keep going. You describe a goal, set limits, and Gemini plans the steps, opens connected apps, and checks back when it needs approval. This guide shows exactly how to start, what it can do, and how to stay in control.

## What Tasks in Gemini Can Do

Tasks connect to more than 60 apps. Google lists Gmail, Calendar, Maps, Drive, and partners such as Instacart. In the US, Gemini can place calls, shop with Google Pay, accept forwarded email at gemini@google.com, and receive texts after you verify a number.

It runs inside an isolated virtual machine on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine. The sandbox is dedicated to your account. Gemini pauses before purchases and other critical actions. You can set a hard spending cap. Receipts land in Google Wallet.

Calling is unavailable in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska. Work and school accounts are not supported. History must stay on.

## Switch from Chat to Task

Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS, or go to gemini.google.com. Sign in with a personal Google Account. Look for the toggle under or above the prompt box. On mobile it often sits above the box; on the web it sits below.

Tap or click Task. The interface changes to a task prompt. Describe the outcome you want, not the clicks. Include the goal, any budget or time limit, and what Gemini must not do without asking.

Example: “Book an early Friday morning service appointment for our 2019 Honda Odyssey at the nearest dealership that takes appointments. Check my calendar for free slots in the next month. Confirm before you book.”

Submit. Gemini shows a work panel with completed, current, and planned steps. You can add more instructions at any time. The Stop button ends the task.

## Hand Off Everyday Work

Josh Woodward, Vice President for the Gemini app, described real uses. One involved a check-engine light. Gemini called two dealerships at once, sat through hold music, found a slot, and added the appointment to Calendar. It told the businesses it was an AI.

Another case analyzed past utility bills after password-manager access and spotted a heat pump running during peak pricing. Shopping examples compare options through the Shopping Graph, apply discounts, and check out with Google Pay while staying inside a pre-set budget. You still earn card points and protections.

Groceries work the same way. Text or type “Add lasagna, strawberries, cucumbers, and vanilla ice cream to our Instacart order,” then send a photo of an empty box. Gemini adds the items and hands you off to finish. Forward school emails to gemini@google.com and dates appear on the calendar with conflicts flagged.

These examples come from Google’s 17 November post. Results vary by site and account permissions.

## Set Up Channels for Email and Text

In the US you can start tasks from outside the app.

1. Open Gemini and tap Settings.
2. Tap Channels.
3. Under Text with Gemini, tap Set up and verify your phone number (US number on an eligible carrier).
4. Enable email if shown.

Forward messages to gemini@google.com. Gemini replies with a link to the new task. Text the verified number the same way.

## Stay in Control

Gemini asks before it clicks Buy and before other irreversible steps. You can mark routine actions as “Always allow.” Jump in mid-task to change direction. Disconnect apps or delete history at any time in settings.

Security layers include prompt-injection filters, Google Safe Browsing, and the isolated VM. Google publishes a technical paper on the defense-in-depth approach and runs a bug-bounty program for agentic systems.

If a task misses the mark, give the response a thumbs down. The feature is still evolving; some sites hand the final click back to you.

## Tips for Better Results

Write the job like a note to a helper. State the goal, the limits, and the timing. “Compare the three cheapest flights to Tokyo in March under $900 and put them in a Sheet” works better than “flights to Tokyo?”

Use Personal Intelligence if you want context from Gmail, Calendar, and Photos. Connect only the apps you need. For complex analysis, Gemini 4 Neon can power Tasks for faster multi-step work.

Start small. Book a single appointment or add items to a grocery list. Watch the work panel. Approve only what you expect.

## Conclusion

Tasks in Gemini turn the app from a chat partner into a background assistant. Flip the toggle, describe the outcome, and let it handle calls, shopping, calendar entries, and connected-app work while you stay in control of every critical step. The feature is free in six countries today and expanding.

Open Gemini, switch to Task, and hand off one item from your list. The official announcement and support pages have the latest availability details.
