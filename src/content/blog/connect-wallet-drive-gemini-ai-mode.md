Google Search AI Mode now lets you opt in to connect more personal apps so responses draw on your own context. Starting November 23, 2026, Wallet is available immediately. Drive and the Gemini app follow in the coming weeks. These join existing connections for Gmail, Calendar and Photos.

The feature lives inside Personal Intelligence. You choose what to connect. You can turn connections on or off at any time. AI Mode can only read data; it cannot move money, send messages or change files.

## Why connect apps to Personal Intelligence

Without connections, AI Mode answers from public web information alone. With connections it can reference your spending patterns, documents and previous chats. That produces replies that pick up from your recent activity instead of starting from zero every time.

For example, after researching a trip you can ask a follow-up question in a new thread and AI Mode already knows the dates and destinations you examined. Wallet connection surfaces cash-back categories and reward balances so you can ask which card maximizes points on an upcoming purchase.

Google states that when Wallet is connected, AI Mode only reads account information. It cannot initiate transfers or investments.

## How to connect your apps

Follow these steps on a personal Google account. Work or school accounts may have different controls.

1. Open Google Search and go to AI Mode.
2. Open Search Personalization settings (usually via the gear icon or your account menu).
3. Select Connected Apps.
4. Choose the apps you want to connect. Wallet appears first. Drive and Gemini app options roll out over the following weeks.
5. Confirm the opt-in. You can review or revoke permissions later from the same screen.

Google also notes that a Personalization icon will appear under tailored responses so you can see which context was used. You can reply in the thread to correct details, for example “I’m no longer a vegetarian.”

You can adjust or disable personalization entirely in Search Settings at any time.

## What each connection unlocks

**Google Wallet**  
Connect Wallet to map financial milestones and spot ways to reduce everyday spending. AI Mode can recommend which linked card gives the best cash-back or travel perks for a specific purchase. All of this stays read-only.

**Google Drive**  
Once available, Drive connection lets AI Mode reference documents you have stored. Planning a project or reviewing a report becomes faster because relevant files surface without you having to upload them again.

**Gemini app**  
Connecting the Gemini app lets context flow both ways. Ideas you explore in the Gemini app become available in AI Mode, and Search history you already opted into Gemini can inform new searches. Google describes this as sharing the foundation so you do not repeat yourself across products.

These three join Gmail, Calendar and Photos, which were already supported.

## Tips for safer use

- Start with Wallet if you mainly want spending insights. Add Drive and Gemini later once you see the value.
- Review the Personalization icon after a few responses to confirm the context matches what you expect.
- Keep personalization off for sensitive research if you prefer fully public answers.
- Remember that connected data remains under Google’s existing privacy controls. You can delete activity or pause personalization independently.

If you also use Gemini Tasks for calls and shopping, the same personal context can make those background jobs more accurate. See our guide on [free Gemini Tasks](/blog/how-to-use-free-gemini-tasks-calls-shopping-more/) for setup steps.

## Limitations to know

Rollout is gradual. Wallet is live for eligible users now. Drive and Gemini app support arrives in the coming weeks. The feature requires a personal Google account and is available where AI Mode is supported.

AI Mode cannot act on your behalf with connected data. It only reads. For actions such as booking or purchasing, you still confirm inside the relevant app or through Gemini Tasks.

## Conclusion

Connecting Wallet, Drive and the Gemini app to Personal Intelligence turns AI Mode into a search experience that already knows your recent context. The opt-in process takes under a minute and remains fully reversible. Start with the apps you use most and expand as the remaining connections roll out.
