Gemini Intelligence turns supported Android phones into proactive assistants that handle multi-step work across apps. It runs on devices that meet specific hardware standards and keeps you in control of every action.

This guide shows what the feature set includes, how to confirm your phone qualifies, and the exact steps to turn on the most useful capabilities.

![Person working on a laptop with code on the screen](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1498050108023-c5249f4df085?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What Gemini Intelligence includes

Google introduced Gemini Intelligence in May 2026 as the package of advanced Gemini experiences reserved for the most capable Android hardware. It combines on-device processing with cloud models so the phone can complete longer workflows without constant manual switching between apps.

Core capabilities include multi-step task automation, Rambler for cleaning spoken input into polished text, Create My Widget for generating custom home-screen widgets from a description, and Device Assistance for controlling settings by voice or text. Proactive suggestions can surface relevant details from email or calendar at the right moment. Intelligent autofill and Gemini Notebook also appear on qualifying devices.

Availability varies by country, language, and device. Some features require an age of 18 or older and may have usage limits. You stay in charge: Gemini only acts on your command and pauses for final confirmation on purchases or bookings.

## Confirm your device meets the requirements

Gemini Intelligence is not available on every Android phone. Google lists these baseline requirements on its support pages:

- At least 12 GB of RAM
- A qualified system-on-chip
- Advanced on-device AI processing
- Latest media processing capabilities
- Software performance that meets current benchmarks

Devices that launched with the feature include the Pixel 11 series, Samsung Galaxy S26 / Z Fold8 / Z Flip8 lines, and later models. In November 2026 Google expanded support to the Pixel 10 family via a Pixel Drop and to the Motorola Signature 27. Check the official device list at android.com/gemini-intelligence for the current roster.

If your phone does not qualify, the standard Gemini app remains available, but the full Intelligence package (including certain automation and widget tools) will not appear.

## Turn on task automation

Task automation lets you ask Gemini to order food, book a ride, or add items to a shopping cart across supported apps. The feature has expanded to the UK, India, and Japan in addition to earlier markets.

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Make sure Gemini is set as your default digital assistant.
3. Describe the task in natural language. Example: “Order my usual lunch from the food app and have it delivered to the office.”
4. Watch the progress in the notification shade. Gemini works in a background session and stops before the final payment or confirmation step.
5. Review the summary and approve or edit.

You can also trigger automation from screen context. Long-press the power button over a grocery list in Notes and ask Gemini to build the delivery cart. The same pattern works with photos: snap a travel brochure and request matching tours.

For developers building apps that expose functions to these agents, see our guide on the [Connected Apps Platform](/blog/connect-android-app-gemini-connected-apps-platform/).

## Use Rambler for cleaner voice-to-text

Rambler in Gboard turns natural, filler-filled speech into concise written text in real time. It removes “ums,” fixes grammar, and adds punctuation. Audio is used only for live transcription and is not stored.

Rambler is now available on the Pixel 10 series and the Motorola Signature 27, among other devices.

1. Open Gboard on any text field.
2. Switch to voice input.
3. Look for the Rambler indicator and enable it.
4. Speak naturally, even if you switch languages mid-sentence.
5. Review the cleaned output before sending.

It handles mixed-language input, which is useful for bilingual users.

## Create custom widgets with a prompt

Create My Widget generates functional home-screen widgets from a plain-language description. It can pull data from Gmail, Calendar, and Search. The feature is rolling out over the coming weeks and will later reach Pixel Watch.

Examples that work well:

- “Show a countdown to my next race and the current weather at the venue.”
- “Weekly high-protein meal ideas with ingredient lists.”
- “Monthly spending tracker pulled from recent receipts.”

1. Open the Gemini app or the widget picker on the home screen.
2. Choose Create My Widget.
3. Type or speak the description.
4. Preview the generated layout.
5. Add it to the home screen and resize as needed.

The widget updates automatically with fresh data from the connected sources.

## Device Assistance and proactive help

Device Assistance lets you manage the phone itself by asking Gemini. Clear storage, check location permissions, mute notifications while keeping important calls, or open apps. It is available on select devices running Android 17 or later.

Proactive suggestions appear when Gemini has relevant context, such as an airline confirmation in Gmail when a friend asks for the meeting address. You can enable or disable these in settings.

To set up Personal Intelligence (which powers richer autofill and suggestions):

1. During device setup or in Gemini settings, choose which Google apps to connect.
2. Select Connect all or pick specific apps.
3. Confirm the permissions.

You can change these connections later.

## Practical tips for better results

- Start with low-stakes tasks so you learn how Gemini phrases confirmations.
- Keep Personal Intelligence connections limited to the apps you actually want it to read.
- Review every final confirmation step; automation stops before irreversible actions.
- Check usage limits in the Gemini app if a feature suddenly stops responding.
- Update your system and Gemini app regularly; new device support and language coverage arrive through Pixel Drops and Play Store updates.

## Conclusion

Gemini Intelligence reduces the friction of everyday phone tasks on supported hardware. Once your device qualifies, enabling task automation, Rambler, and Create My Widget takes only a few minutes. Combine these tools with careful permission settings and you gain a more proactive assistant that still requires your final say on important actions.

Check the official Android help pages for the latest device list and feature availability in your region.
