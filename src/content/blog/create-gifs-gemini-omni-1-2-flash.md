Google added native GIF creation to the Gemini app on 20 November 2026. The feature runs on Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash and produces short looping animations of roughly three seconds. You can start from a text prompt, add reference images, or use a short video clip. The result copies into chats, emails, and documents as a live GIF or downloads as a standard .gif file.

This guide shows exactly how to make one, who can access it today, and how to get cleaner results. It draws only from Google’s official Gemini Apps Help page and the Omni 1.2 Flash announcements.

## Who can create GIFs right now

GIF generation is rolling out gradually. Official requirements from the Gemini Apps Help page are:

- You must be 18 or older.
- You need a personal Google account (work or school accounts have extra restrictions).
- You must be in one of these countries: Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Japan, Korea, the United Kingdom, or the United States.
- The feature is rolling out to Google AI subscribers. A follow-up note from the official Gemini account also listed free access in the same eight countries, but the Help page currently states that a Google AI plan is required for personal accounts. Check your own account if you do not see the option yet.

If you are outside those markets or still on a free tier and do not see the tool, the rollout simply has not reached you. No timeline for other regions has been published.

## How to generate a GIF

Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS, or go to gemini.google.com on a computer. Sign in with your personal account.

1. Start a new chat or open the existing one.
2. Either type a clear request such as “Create a GIF of a dog riding a surfboard” in the main prompt box, or open the sidebar and choose **Create video** (or the equivalent image/video tool).
3. When the format picker appears, select **GIF**.
4. Describe the scene. Keep the prompt specific: subject, action, style, and any motion details. Example: “A three-second looping GIF of a cat batting at a yarn ball on a wooden floor, soft daylight, playful motion.”
5. Optional: tap **Add image** or **Add video**. You can upload up to five images or one video as references. Only use files you have the rights to use.
6. Submit and wait. Generation can take a few minutes. You can start a new chat while it works.
7. Once the GIF appears, copy it (it pastes as an animated GIF into many apps) or download the .gif file.

Google’s Help page notes that you can refine ideas in the main chat first, then switch to the video/GIF tool. Templates for reaction-style memes are also available in the creation screen.

## What the GIFs look like

Each GIF loops for about three seconds. The model is Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, the same video model that generates longer clips (3–10 seconds for most users, up to 15 seconds for AI Ultra). For GIFs the output stays short and silent so it stays lightweight for messaging and social use.

You can paste the GIF into Google Docs, Gmail, Messages, and many third-party apps where it remains animated. Downloading gives a standard .gif file you can share anywhere.

## Tips for better results

- Write concrete prompts. “A golden retriever jumping into a pool, water splash, looping motion” works better than “fun dog GIF.”
- Use reference images when you want a specific character, product, or style. Omni 1.2 Flash is designed to hold visual consistency across frames.
- Keep motion simple. Complex multi-step actions can blur or distort in a three-second loop.
- If the first result misses the mark, stay in the same conversation and ask for a change. Multi-turn editing is supported for Omni video work.
- Check the country and plan status first. The feature is still rolling out, so the button may appear later even if you meet the listed requirements.

If you already use Tasks in the Gemini app for background work, the same account and history settings that enable Tasks also keep the creation tools available. See our guide on [using free Gemini Tasks on Android](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-tasks-free-android/) for the related setup steps.

## Limits and safety notes

- Only upload images and videos you have the right to use. Google’s Prohibited Use Policy applies to all generated content.
- The feature is not available to users under 18.
- Generation times vary. Busy periods can add minutes.
- No public resolution, frame-rate, or daily-quota numbers have been published for the GIF output itself. Longer video generation on the same model has published pricing and length limits, but GIFs follow the shorter ~3-second path.

## Conclusion

GIF creation in the Gemini app removes the need to search stock libraries for the perfect reaction. With Omni 1.2 Flash you describe the motion, optionally add a reference, and receive a short looping file ready to paste or download. Access is currently limited to eight countries and is rolling out gradually, with plan requirements listed on the official Help page.

Open Gemini, select the GIF format, and test a simple prompt. If the option is missing, check back later as the rollout continues.
