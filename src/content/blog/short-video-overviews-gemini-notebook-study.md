Gemini Notebook turns your uploaded sources into short, shareable videos that explain a single concept in about 60 seconds. These Short Video Overviews combine narration with educational animations. They help when a formula, diagram, or process is easier to understand visually.

Google introduced and expanded these study tools through September and October 2026. You generate them directly from the Studio panel after adding sources. The output stays grounded in the material you provide.

## What Short Video Overviews Do

A Short Video Overview focuses on one topic from your notes or readings. It runs roughly 60 seconds. Narration walks through the idea while simple animations illustrate key points. You can share the video with classmates or review it yourself on the go.

Other formats exist: Explainer for fuller overviews and Cinematic for more narrative styles (availability varies). Short is the quick option for targeted review.

## How to Generate a Short Video Overview

1. Open Gemini Notebook at notebook.google and sign in. Create a notebook or open one you can edit. Add sources such as PDFs, Google Docs, lecture recordings, websites, or YouTube links.

2. Go to the Studio panel. Select Video Overview.

3. Open the customization options. Under Format, choose Short. Pick a narration language. Add an optional steering prompt to focus the video, for example "explain Newton's First Law using the free-body diagram from the notes."

4. Select Generate now. Generation takes time. You do not need to keep the tab open. Return later to play or download the result.

5. Review the video. If the focus is off, create a new one with a tighter steering prompt. Short videos do not offer the same visual style picker as longer Explainer formats.

The feature works on the free plan, though higher limits come with Google AI Pro or Ultra. Availability rolls out gradually, so the Short option may appear later on some accounts.

## Tips for Effective Study Videos

Keep sources focused on the topic. A notebook full of unrelated material produces less precise videos. Upload the specific chapter, lecture slides, or problem set you want explained.

Use steering prompts. Naming the exact concept or diagram guides the narration and animations. After generation, ask the notebook chat for feedback on your understanding of the topic.

Pair with other tools. Create an Interactive Learning Overview or quiz from the same sources, then generate a Short Video Overview for the sections you missed. Review the video, then retest yourself.

Share carefully. Videos are grounded in your sources, but always check key facts against the original material before sending them to others.

Language support covers more than 80 languages. You can generate a video in a different language from English sources when studying with international classmates.

## Combine with Other Gemini Notebook Features

Short Video Overviews sit alongside Interactive Learning Overviews, expanded quiz formats, and voice conversations with your notebook. Upload lecture audio or photos of handwritten notes, then generate multiple study aids from the same sources.

You can also access notebooks from the Gemini app as a Connected App. This lets you query or create materials without switching tools.

## Conclusion

Short Video Overviews give you a fast, visual way to review a single concept from your own materials. Add sources, choose the Short format in the Studio panel, add a steering prompt if needed, and generate. The result is a shareable 60-second video grounded in what you uploaded.

Try it with your next set of lecture notes or textbook chapter at notebook.google. Pair it with a quiz to check what the video helped you retain.
