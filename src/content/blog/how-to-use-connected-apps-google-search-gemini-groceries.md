Holiday season stacks grocery runs, party prep, and year-end finances on top of normal life. Google just rolled out new Connected Apps so you can handle several of those jobs inside Search and the Gemini app instead of switching tabs or opening separate store apps.

The November 20, 2026 update adds Albertsons, Safeway, Shaw’s, and Target for building grocery orders and checking out. Experian expands from the Gemini app into Search for credit checks and personal loan shopping. H&R Block joins Search so US users can calculate taxes and estimate refunds. Angi and Thumbtack for local home services arrive in the coming weeks. All of this is currently available in the United States.

## What the New Connected Apps Actually Do

Connected Apps let Google’s AI surfaces talk to outside services so tasks finish where you already are. For the grocery partners, the connection uses the Universal Commerce Protocol. You describe what you need, the app helps assemble a cart with real-time inventory where available, and you move toward checkout without starting from a blank store page.

Experian (already live in Gemini) now reaches Search. You can check a credit rating and explore personal loan options. H&R Block lets you run tax calculations and refund estimates directly from Search. These are optional links—you connect the accounts you want and can disconnect them later.

The feature builds on earlier Connected Apps work that already covered services such as Instacart, Canva, and YouTube Music in AI Mode and the Gemini app. The new batch focuses on practical year-end errands.

## How to Connect an App in Gemini or Search

You need a personal Google account signed in, and for Gemini you generally need History (or the equivalent activity setting) turned on. Work or school accounts follow different rules set by an administrator.

In the Gemini app or at gemini.google.com:

1. Open the menu or sidebar and go to Customize (it may sit under More).
2. Choose Connectors or Connected Apps.
3. Find the service (Target, Albertsons, Experian, H&R Block, etc.) and tap Add or the toggle.
4. Follow the sign-in or permission prompts for that partner.
5. Return to chat and test with a simple request.

In Google Search, switch to AI Mode where available. When a request matches a connected service, Search offers to link the app if it is not already connected. You can also manage connections from account settings. Availability still rolls out gradually, so the exact list you see may lag the announcement by a day or two.

Once linked, you can type @ followed by the app name to force its use, or simply describe the job and let Gemini or AI Mode choose when the match is clear.

## Build a Grocery Order Step by Step

Start with a clear request that names the store or the kind of items. Examples that work well:

- “Build a Target cart for a small Thanksgiving side-dish list: stuffing, cranberry sauce, green beans, and rolls.”
- “Add milk, eggs, bananas, and cereal to my Safeway order and show me the cart.”
- “Help me put together Albertsons groceries for a holiday party appetizer tray under $40.”

The interface shows progress while it checks inventory or updates the cart. You review the items, adjust quantities, and continue to the partner’s checkout when you are ready. Loyalty accounts can be linked so deals and points apply. Google notes that you still complete the purchase through the store’s normal flow, and you keep any card rewards or protections that apply.

If the first attempt misses something, add a follow-up in the same conversation: “Swap the regular rolls for gluten-free and remove the cranberry sauce.” The cart updates without starting over.

## Check Credit or Estimate Taxes

For Experian, open Search or Gemini and ask something specific: “Check my Experian credit rating” or “Show me personal loan options based on my Experian profile.” The service returns the information you have authorized and can surface loan offers. Review any terms before you proceed.

For H&R Block, try “Estimate my 2026 tax refund with H&R Block using the documents I already have” or “Calculate my taxes for this year.” The tool walks through the inputs it needs and produces an estimate. This does not file your return; it helps you see numbers earlier.

Both features require you to connect the account first and are limited to US users. Double-check the figures against official statements before you rely on them for decisions.

## Tips for Better Results

- Name the store or service when you want a specific partner. Vague requests may pick a default or ask you to choose.
- Include constraints up front: budget, dietary needs, delivery window, or “do not purchase yet—just build the cart.”
- Keep History on if Connected Apps disappear. The setting is required for most third-party links on web and iOS.
- Disconnect any app you no longer want from the same Connectors page. You can re-link later.
- For multi-step jobs that continue in the background, combine this with Gemini Tasks if it is available on your account. See the existing guide on [free Gemini Tasks](/blog/gemini-tasks-free-how-to-guide/).
- Test with a small order first so you learn the confirmation steps before a large holiday run.

## What to Watch Next

Angi and Thumbtack linking is expected in the coming weeks, which will let you find local pros for repairs or last-minute decorations from inside Search. More retailers and services will join through the Connected Apps Platform over time.

The feature is still rolling out, so if the new partners do not appear yet, check again after a day and confirm you are signed in with a personal US account. Official details live on the Google blog post from November 20 and the Gemini Apps Help pages for Connected Apps.

These tools reduce the number of tabs you open for ordinary errands. Start with one grocery list or one tax estimate, confirm the connection works the way you expect, and expand from there.
