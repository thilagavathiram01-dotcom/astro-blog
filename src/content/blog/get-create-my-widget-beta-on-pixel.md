Google’s Create My Widget feature lets eligible Pixel owners describe a home screen widget in plain English and have Gemini Intelligence build it. The November 2026 Pixel Drop brought the tool to Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 series phones, but the update rolls out in stages. Eligible users can join the Play Store beta to get it sooner.

This guide covers eligibility, the beta enrollment steps, how to create and edit widgets, practical prompt ideas, and current limits. All details come from Google’s official Pixel support page and verified reporting on the rollout.

## Who can use Create My Widget

Google’s support page lists clear requirements. All must be true:

- Device: Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, Pixel 10 Pro Fold, or any Pixel 11 series phone. The Pixel 10a is excluded.
- Language: English.
- Country: Australia, Canada, India, the United Kingdom, or the United States.
- Age: 18 or older.

UK users face an extra restriction. Gemini’s Connected Apps integration is not available there, so widgets that rely on personal data from Gmail, Calendar, or similar services may be limited.

Google has also said the feature will come to Pixel Watch 4 and Pixel Watch 5 in select regions later.

## How to get early access via the Play Store beta

The November Pixel Drop update does not arrive on every phone at once. You can skip the wait by joining the Create My Widget beta on the Play Store. This works even if your phone has not yet received the full system update, as long as you meet the eligibility rules above.

1. Open the Google Play Store on your Pixel and search for “Create My Widget.”
2. Open the app listing and scroll to the “Join the beta” section. Tap Join (or Become a tester).
3. Wait a few minutes for enrollment to complete. It can take up to 10 minutes.
4. Return to the Play Store, go to My apps, and check for updates. Install the beta version of Create My Widget.

Once the app is updated, long-press an empty area on your home screen, tap Widgets, and look for the new Create button.

## How to create a custom widget

After the feature is active, the process is straightforward. Google’s support page outlines two main paths.

### Describe your own widget

1. Touch and hold an empty space on the Home screen.
2. Tap Widgets, then tap Create.
3. Tap Describe your widget.
4. Type what you want the widget to show (for example, “Show me my monthly shopping spend” or “Countdown to my vacation on July 15”). Tap Send.
5. Review the preview. If the widget needs access to device or app data, tap Allow.
6. Tap + Add to place it on your Home screen.

On the preview screen you also see Edit and Create new options.

### Start from a template

1. Touch and hold an empty space, then tap Widgets and Create.
2. Choose a category or suggestion such as Important dates, Sports & fitness, or Finance.
3. Optionally customize the prompt.
4. Tap Add.

Gemini may ask follow-up questions or show a progress indicator while it builds the widget. The result is a Material-style widget you can resize and place like any other.

## Editing a widget

You can refine a widget without starting over.

- During creation: On the preview screen, tap Edit, type the changes you want, and tap Send.
- After it is on your home screen: Long-press the widget and choose the edit option if available, or return to the Create flow.

Google notes that you should always check custom widget responses for accuracy. Some features may require a subscription for higher usage limits.

## Prompt ideas that work well

Early users and Google examples show the feature is strongest for simple, glanceable information:

- Monthly spending tracker that pulls from connected apps.
- Countdown to a specific date, trip, or event.
- Sports scores and headlines for a favorite team.
- Weather focused on wind speed and rain for cycling.
- High-protein meal suggestions for the week.

Be specific. “Show the next soccer game for my team with kickoff time and channel” produces better results than a vague request. Complex live data (such as real-time transit) often becomes a button that opens a Gemini query instead of a fully live widget.

## Current limitations

- Only available on the listed Pixel models in the five countries, in English, for users 18+.
- UK users cannot use Connected Apps integration for personal data.
- The feature is better for simple dashboards than full mini-apps.
- Rollout is staged; the beta is the fastest path for most people.

## Conclusion

Create My Widget gives eligible Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 owners a fast way to put exactly the information they want on the home screen. Joining the Play Store beta is the simplest way to try it before the full system update arrives. Start with a clear prompt, review the preview carefully, and edit as needed.

The feature is still early. Google continues to expand it, including planned support for Pixel watches. Check Google’s official support page for the latest eligibility and steps.
