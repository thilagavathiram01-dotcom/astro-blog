Android 17 brings a simple but useful customization to Pixel phones: the option to hide app names on the home screen. This removes the text labels under icons so your layout looks cleaner and less cluttered. The feature works on Pixel 6 and newer devices that have received the Android 17 update.

Many users prefer a minimalist home screen where icons stand alone. App names remain visible in the app drawer, so you never lose the ability to identify apps. You can turn the labels back on at any time.

## Why Hide App Names?

Pixel home screens have long shown app names by default. On busy layouts or with complex wallpapers, the text can compete with the icons and reduce visual clarity. Hiding the names creates more breathing room and lets the wallpaper show through better.

The change is Pixel-specific for now. Other Android skins have offered similar options for years. With Android 17 stable rolling out, Pixel owners finally get the control built into the system launcher.

If you also use floating apps for multitasking, check our guide on [Android 17 app bubbles](/blog/android-17-app-bubbles/) for another way to free up screen space.

## How to Hide App Names on Your Pixel

The setting lives in the Wallpaper & style menu. No extra apps or ADB commands are required.

1. Long-press an empty area on your home screen (avoid icons and widgets).
2. Tap **Wallpaper & style** in the menu that appears.
3. Scroll down and tap **Icons**.
4. Tap the **Names** tab.
5. Toggle **Show app names** off.
6. Tap **Apply** in the top-right corner.

Your home screen updates immediately. Icons appear without labels. Open the app drawer to confirm names still show there.

To reverse it, return to the same menu and toggle **Show app names** back on, then Apply.

## Confirm Your Device Is on Android 17

The option only appears after the Android 17 update. Eligible devices include Pixel 6, 6 Pro, 6a, 7 series, 8 series, 9 series, 10 series, Pixel Tablet, and Pixel Fold models.

Check your version:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **System**.
3. Tap **Software updates**.
4. Tap **System update**.
5. Tap **Check for update** if needed.

If the update is available, install it, then restart and follow the steps above.

## Tips for a Cleaner Home Screen

- Pair the hidden names with a simple wallpaper so icons remain easy to recognize by color and shape.
- Keep frequently used apps in the dock; the dock still shows names on some layouts, but the main grid stays clean.
- Combine with app bubbles for quick access without returning to the home screen.
- If an icon is hard to identify without a name, long-press it to see the label in the menu, or move it to the app drawer.

The setting does not affect widgets, the search bar, or the app drawer. It only removes labels from home screen icons.
