ChatGPT Space brings pages, files, and shared work into one place. Starting November 20, 2026, it began rolling out to Plus users ages 18 and older worldwide. You can create interactive pages and presentations, then invite others with Space access to build on them.

This guide shows how to get started on a Plus plan. It follows the official OpenAI documentation for Space.

## What ChatGPT Space Includes

Space is the home for pages, uploaded files, and other work. A page is a document you and ChatGPT edit together. It holds notes, plans, or handbooks, with child pages for related work.

You also get ChatGPT Slides for presentations and the ability to connect apps like Google Drive. Projects remain separate from Space. Private chats stay private even when you share a page.

Plus users can create and edit on web, desktop, iOS, and Android. Free users cannot open shared pages. Public links are not available.

For earlier context on Space after DevDay, see our guide to [using ChatGPT Space for shared team pages](/blog/chatgpt-space-shared-team-pages/).

## Open Space and Create Your First Page

Go to chatgpt.com and sign in with your Plus account. Look for Space in the left navigation. If it does not appear, update the desktop app or wait for the rollout on your account.

Once inside, the All view shows your saved work. Use Your items for your own content and Shared with you for content from others.

To create a page:

1. Select New page in the sidebar.
2. Give it a title.
3. Start typing, paste notes, or add a file.

You can also ask ChatGPT to create a page from an existing conversation. Open the conversation, then ask: "Create a page from this conversation with the project goals, decisions, and next steps."

Add context by including notes, files, or links. For Google Drive content, connect the app and point ChatGPT to the relevant files.

Ask ChatGPT to help by opening the chat alongside the page. Example prompt: "Organize the notes on this page into a clear draft. Use headings and bullet points where they help. Preserve the facts and source links, and list any open questions at the end."

Review the result before sharing. You can edit text directly or ask ChatGPT for changes.

## Create and Refine Slides

ChatGPT Slides lets you build a presentation with your team and ChatGPT in Space. On mobile you create through conversation and view decks. Use web or desktop to edit slide elements directly.

Start by asking ChatGPT to create a presentation from a conversation, outline, or source files. Describe the audience, goal, and length. Provide a reference deck or template if you want specific layout and style.

If the Windows desktop app creates a PowerPoint file instead of a collaborative deck, switch to cloud mode or use the web version.

## Group Related Pages in a Space

For a topic or team with related pages, create a space. From All, open New and select Space. Name the topic or group. Optionally expand Invite people, then select Create. You can share the space later.

Nest pages under a parent page. For example, a project overview can contain subpages for meeting notes and weekly updates. Ask ChatGPT: "Create a Meeting notes subpage under Project overview."

Pages can stay automatically updated from connected apps and tools. Create a to-do list that updates itself, a study guide with a 3D model, or a project plan that stays current with Slack decisions.

## Share and Collaborate

Open a page's sharing controls. Choose a person or team, set view or edit access, and share the link. Only people with Space access on Plus, Pro, Business, or Enterprise can open shared items.

Linked files keep their original permissions. Content you copy or summarize onto a page is visible to people who can access that page. Sharing a page does not share your private chats or saved memory.

Leave comments for collaborators. Mention @ChatGPT or @dot in a comment or type it directly in the page for AI help.

## Tips for Productive Use

Start with one focused space for a single project. Keep the first page as an overview with goals and next steps. Add subpages only when the volume of notes grows.

Use templates from Space home for common structures like to-do lists or project plans. Connect Google Drive early so ChatGPT can reference the latest files without repeated uploads.

On mobile, create and edit pages, then switch to desktop for detailed slide layout work. Check the result on the page before sharing so facts and links stay accurate.

If Space does not appear after updating the app, wait a few days. The rollout is gradual.

## Conclusion

ChatGPT Space turns conversations into living documents you can organize and share. Plus users now have access to pages, slides, and collaboration features that previously required higher plans.

Open Space, create one page from a recent conversation, and invite a colleague. The official getting-started guide covers the same flow with the latest interface details.
