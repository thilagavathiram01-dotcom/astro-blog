Google rolled out 66 new regional voices for the Gemini app on November 16, 2026. These options cover U.S. English, U.K. English, India English, Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian, and Korean. Each voice aims for natural rhythm and clarity instead of exaggerated accents.

The selected voice works across Gemini Live chats, language practice sessions, and the Listen button that reads text responses aloud. You can try them on both Android and iOS once the update reaches your account.

This guide shows the exact steps to preview and switch voices. It draws from the official Gemini blog post and app settings flow.

![Person using smartphone with headphones](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1758876021591-4a48ab8968b6?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Why Regional Voices Matter

Standard AI voices often sound generic. Regional options let Gemini match local speech patterns in pitch and cadence. Users in the U.K. gain more than ten English variants, including Geordie, Scouse, Brummie, Scottish, Welsh, and Northern Irish styles.

Google tuned the voices for everyday clarity. They capture familiar local inflection without forcing slang into responses. Once chosen, the voice stays active for the whole app until you change it again.

The rollout started after a preview at Google I/O. Availability depends on your account and region. Some users see the options immediately; others wait for the staged update.

## Prerequisites

Update the Gemini app from the Play Store or App Store. Sign in with your Google account. The feature works for free and paid Gemini users on supported devices.

You need Android or iOS. The official announcement covers mobile apps only. Web versions on Chrome or Googlebook do not show the new voices yet.

If voices do not appear, wait a few hours and restart the app. Google notes the rollout is gradual.

## Switch Voices Inside Gemini Live

Open the Gemini app. Start a Gemini Live session by tapping the Live icon or saying the activation phrase.

Tap the three-dot menu in the Live interface. Select the voice option. Preview several voices by tapping them. Listen to sample phrases.

Choose the one that fits. Confirm the selection. The change applies right away. End the session and start a new one to hear the voice in a fresh conversation.

This method is fastest for quick tests. The menu does not label voices by city or accent name. You must listen to identify the one you want.

## Switch Voices from App Settings

Open the Gemini app. Tap your profile picture in the top right. Select Settings.

Find and open Gemini’s voice. Swipe through the available options. Each entry plays a short preview when selected.

Pick your preferred voice. Exit the settings. The choice applies to both Live sessions and the Listen feature on chat replies.

Restart the app if the new voice does not appear immediately. The setting syncs across devices signed into the same Google account.

## Test the Voice in Practice

After switching, open a regular Gemini chat. Type a question and tap the Listen icon. The response should use the new voice.

Start a Gemini Live conversation. Ask about local topics such as food, sports, or weather. The voice keeps its cadence throughout the exchange.

Try language practice mode if available in your region. The same voice handles practice dialogues.

If the voice reverts, recheck the settings. Some users report temporary resets after app updates. Re-select the voice to lock it in.

## Tips for Best Results

Preview at least five voices before deciding. Regional options differ more in rhythm than in vocabulary.

Use the Listen button on long answers. It reveals how the voice handles extended speech.

Switch back anytime. No permanent lock exists. Keep one default for daily use and test others for specific tasks.

Pair the voice change with other Gemini features. For example, combine it with on-device tools or intelligence features covered in our [guide to Gemini intelligence on Android](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-intelligence-features-on-android/).

Update the app regularly. Google continues to expand voice options after the initial 66.

## Common Issues and Fixes

Voices missing after update: Force-stop the Gemini app and clear its cache from system settings. Reopen and check again.

Preview audio silent: Check device volume and Bluetooth connections. Restart the phone if needed.

Voice does not match expected accent: The picker lists options without regional labels. Cycle through previews until you find the match.

Feature not yet available: Confirm your account region and wait 24–48 hours. The official blog states the rollout is ongoing.

## Conclusion

The new regional voices make Gemini conversations feel closer to everyday speech. Switching takes under a minute once the options appear.

Follow the Live menu or settings path, preview the samples, and confirm your choice. The voice then covers Live, practice, and Listen playback.

Try several options to match your preference. The change improves clarity without altering response content.
