Google split Gemini's single Keep Activity switch into two separate controls on 16 November 2026. You can now keep your chat history saved and still tell Google not to use the content of future chats to train its generative AI models.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="191" />

The change is rolling out globally in the Gemini app. Until it reaches your account you may still see the old Keep Activity label. Once the new settings appear, History controls whether chats stay in your visible history. A second checkbox controls whether chat content (text and attachments) is used for model training.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="193" />

This guide shows exactly where to find the controls and how to set them so you keep useful history without contributing future conversations to training.

## What the New Controls Do

History decides if past and future chats appear in your Gemini History. With it on, chats are saved and auto-deleted after 18 months by default (you can change that to 3 months, 36 months, or never). You can still delete individual chats or all history manually at any time.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="191" />

The training control sits under “Help train generative AI models with” and is labelled “Chat content including text & attachments” (wording can vary slightly). When this box is unchecked, future chats are not used to train generative AI models unless you submit feedback. Audio and Gemini Live videos & screenshares have their own separate checkbox that remains opt-in.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="193" />

If you turn History off, future chats do not appear in your History. Google still keeps them for around two months so it can respond to you, process feedback, and help protect users and the public (including with help from human reviewers). Previously the retention window was 72 hours.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="191" />

## How to Set the Controls on the Web

1. Open gemini.google.com and sign in with the account you use for Gemini.
2. Click the settings gear, then choose Gemini History (or go directly to myactivity.google.com/product/gemini).
3. Confirm History is set to On if you want chats saved and visible.
4. Under “Help train generative AI models with”, uncheck “Chat content including text & attachments”.
5. Confirm Turn off if prompted.
6. Review the separate audio/Live checkbox and leave it off unless you specifically want those recordings used for improvement.

Your existing Keep Activity preference carries over when the change reaches your account. If Keep Activity was on, both new settings start on. If it was off, both start off. Check after the rollout and adjust if needed.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="200" />

## How to Set the Controls in the Mobile App

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Tap your profile or the menu at the top, then Settings.
3. Under Data & privacy, tap History.
4. Ensure History is On.
5. Uncheck the chat content training box.
6. Confirm the change.

The exact menu path can differ slightly between Android and iOS, but the History page is the place to look. Temporary chats continue to exist and are not used for training.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="201" />

## Important Limits to Know

The training opt-out applies only to future chats. Content already used for training or reviewed by humans is not removed. Google has said reviewed chats can be retained for up to three years after they are disconnected from your account.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="221" />

Turning History off does not give you a pure “nothing is stored” mode. The two-month retention still applies for service, feedback, and safety purposes. If you submit feedback while History is off, the last 24 hours of context can still be used.

Connected apps and Workspace data have their own controls. Turning off chat-content training in the Gemini app does not automatically change how Gemini behaves inside Gmail, Docs, or other Workspace products. Those follow separate Workspace privacy terms.

Paid Google AI plans do not automatically opt you out of training on personal Gemini chats. The same settings apply whether you are on the free tier or a paid plan.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="209" />

## Practical Tips

- Leave History on and uncheck the chat-content training box if you want the best of both: searchable history plus no future training contribution.
- Shorten the auto-delete period to 3 months if you want less long-term storage even with History on.
- Use Temporary Chat for one-off sensitive questions. Those are not used for training.
- Review and delete specific old chats manually if you want to reduce what remains in your account.
- Check the settings again after the rollout finishes; the interface is still being updated for many accounts.

If you also use Gemini Tasks (the feature previously called Spark), note that Tasks requires History to be on for full functionality. You can still turn the training checkbox off while keeping History enabled. ## Conclusion

The November 2026 privacy update finally separates two choices that used to be locked together. You no longer have to sacrifice chat history to stop future conversations from training Gemini models. Open Settings, leave History on, uncheck the chat-content training box, and confirm. That combination gives you visible history without contributing new chats to model training, subject to the retention and feedback exceptions Google has documented.

Check your account today. The controls are rolling out now, and the default for the training box is on for most adult users until you change it.
