Google made Tasks in the Gemini app free on November 17, 2026. Eligible users in six countries can now hand off multi-step jobs and let Gemini work in the background on the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model.

Chat mode answers questions in real time. Task mode plans longer work, connects to your Google data, and reports back when finished. You stay in control and approve key steps.

This guide shows how to turn it on, what it handles, and how to use it safely.

## Who can use Gemini Tasks free

Tasks is free for adults 18 and older in the United States, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. You need a personal Google Account with Gemini Apps Activity turned on. Work and school accounts are not supported.

Outside those six countries the feature still requires Google AI Pro or Ultra. It is not available in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or Nigeria even with a paid plan.

The free rollout uses Gemini 4 Hydrogen. Paid subscribers can choose higher models for some Tasks. For details on integrating Hydrogen in Android apps, see our guide on [adding Gemini 4 Hydrogen to Android with Firebase AI Logic](/blog/add-gemini-4-hydrogen-android-firebase-ai-logic/).

## How to turn on Task mode

Update the Gemini app or open gemini.google.com. Sign in with your personal account.

1. Locate the Chat / Task toggle. On mobile it sits above the prompt box. On the web it appears below the box.
2. Switch the toggle to Task.
3. Describe the job in plain language. Include dates, budgets, locations, or constraints.
4. Answer any clarifying questions Gemini asks.
5. Approve steps when prompted. You can stop a task at any time.

If the toggle is missing, the rollout has not reached your account yet. Check again later or confirm your country and account type.

## What Tasks can do

Gemini works on multi-step projects in the background. Common uses include:

- Research and comparison of options from connected apps or the web.
- Adding events to your Google Calendar from emails or notes.
- Sorting school or work emails into organized lists.
- Finding unused subscription perks or analyzing bills when you grant access.
- Recurring schedules such as daily calendar updates or weekly research.

You can run up to 15 tasks at once. Free users get 10 active schedules that run once a day in a rough time window. Paid plans raise the schedule limit to 50 and allow hourly or exact timing.

Gemini connects to more than 60 apps. It uses your existing Google data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Photos, Wallet) without sending it to other companies.

## US-only advanced features

Several capabilities roll out first in the United States:

- Calling local businesses to check availability or book appointments. Gemini discloses it is an AI. Businesses can opt out. This is not available in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, or Nebraska.
- Google Pay checkout. Set a spending cap. Gemini asks before completing a purchase. Receipts go to Google Wallet. The feature uses the Agent Payments Protocol.
- Texting Gemini via RCS on Android or iOS with a verified US phone number on an eligible carrier.
- Forwarding emails to gemini@google.com so they become tasks.
- Signing into supported sites through Google Password Manager. Passwords stay inside Google.

These extras are not available in the other five free countries yet.

## Tips for better results

Write clear instructions. Specify the outcome, deadline, and any limits. Example: “Compare three local mechanic shops for a Friday morning oil change next month and book the earliest slot that fits my calendar.”

Set spending caps before any shopping task. Review the plan Gemini shows before approving purchases or calls.

Use recurring schedules for routine work such as scanning school emails every weekday morning. Start a new chat when the topic changes so context stays relevant.

Provide feedback with the thumbs-down button when a result is incomplete. Google uses this to improve the system.

Tasks run in a hardware-isolated virtual machine dedicated to your account. Google applies prompt-injection filters and Safe Browsing. You can disconnect apps, adjust settings, or delete history at any time.

## Safety and limits

Gemini pauses for confirmation before critical actions such as purchases. For routine steps you can mark “Always allow.” It asks clarifying questions on ambiguous requests.

Mistakes still happen. A task may stop early or hand the final click back to you on some websites. Always review results before acting.

Usage limits apply even on the free tier. Paid plans raise usage and unlock more precise scheduling.

## Conclusion

Gemini Tasks is now free for eligible adults in six countries. Flip the toggle from Chat to Task, describe the job, and let Gemini handle multi-step work in the background on Gemini 4 Hydrogen. Advanced calling, texting, and payment features start in the US.

Open the Gemini app or gemini.google.com to try it. Check the official Google blog for the latest rollout status.
