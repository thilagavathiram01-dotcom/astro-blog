Anthropic launched Claude Meetings on November 10, 2026. The beta feature lets Claude join Google Meet calls as a visible participant. It follows the conversation, drafts plans, builds timelines, answers questions in chat, and pulls data from connected apps while people are still talking.

Unlike tools that only summarize after the call ends, Claude starts work during the discussion. Notes, action items, and outputs appear in claude.ai afterward. The feature is available in beta for Team and Enterprise customers on Google Meet only. Anthropic plans support for other platforms later.

Pair this with related tools in our guide to [How to Use Claude Sheets for Team Spreadsheets](/blog/how-to-use-claude-sheets-for-team-spreadsheets/).

## What Claude Meetings Does

Claude joins as a named participant. It posts a notice in the meeting chat so everyone knows it is present. During the call it can:

- Draft the plan or project outline being discussed.
- Map a timeline on a Claude Whiteboard.
- Answer questions in the chat.
- Retrieve figures or documents from connected work apps.

After the call ends, meeting notes, action items, and materials Claude created remain available in claude.ai. Anthropic tested the feature internally for about two months. Product lead Nicholas Lin said Claude is now in nearly all of the company’s own meetings.

## Availability and Requirements

Claude Meetings is in beta for Team and Enterprise plans. Individual Pro, Max, or free accounts do not have access yet. An admin must enable the feature for the organization. It works only with Google Meet at launch.

Claude identifies itself and will not join calls that include external guests unless an admin has authorized it. If an external guest joins mid-call, Claude leaves automatically.

## How to Add Claude to a Google Meet Call

Exact UI steps may vary while the feature is in beta. Based on Anthropic’s announcement and early coverage, the flow is:

1. Start or join a Google Meet call on a Team or Enterprise account where Claude Meetings is enabled.
2. Add Claude as a participant the same way you invite a colleague. It appears as a visible tile with the Claude name and avatar.
3. Confirm the notice Claude posts in the meeting chat.
4. In the chat or by speaking, ask Claude to start a task. Examples include “Claude, start a whiteboard for the launch timeline” or “Claude, draft the project plan from what we just discussed.”
5. Claude responds in chat with links or updates. Outputs land in claude.ai after the call.

For now Claude mostly waits for instructions rather than acting fully proactively. Anthropic expects that behavior to expand over time.

## What Happens During and After the Call

While participants talk, Claude can pull last year’s numbers from a connected app, start a whiteboard timeline, or answer a factual question in chat. When someone describes an app idea or a reporting view, Claude can begin building it.

After everyone leaves, open claude.ai. You will find the meeting notes, action items, any whiteboards or sheets started during the call, and other work products. These are ready for further editing or sharing.

## Tips for Better Results

- Enable relevant connectors (Google Workspace, data tools, etc.) before the meeting so Claude can retrieve live information.
- Ask clear, specific requests in chat. Vague prompts produce weaker outputs while the feature is in beta.
- Keep external guests off the call if you want Claude to stay for the full session, or have an admin pre-authorize them.
- Review Claude’s work immediately after the call. Early versions may need light editing before you share them.
- Combine with Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets for visual plans and trackers created in the same session.

## Limits to Know

The beta covers Google Meet only. Other meeting platforms are planned but not available yet. Claude does not join external-guest calls without admin approval and exits if one joins. It currently leans toward waiting for instructions rather than volunteering tasks. Anthropic has not published detailed rate limits or pricing add-ons beyond the existing Team and Enterprise plans.

## Conclusion

Claude Meetings moves the assistant from post-call summarizer to active participant. Teams on Team and Enterprise plans can test it in Google Meet today. Start with a short internal call, add Claude, request a simple timeline or plan, and check the outputs in claude.ai. As Anthropic expands the feature to more platforms and makes it more proactive, the same workflow should cover a wider set of meetings.
