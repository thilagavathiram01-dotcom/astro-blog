Google launched Universal Cart in the United States on October 15, 2026. The feature lets you save products from different retailers into one shared cart that works across Google Search (including AI Mode) and the Gemini app.

You no longer need to keep dozens of browser tabs open or take screenshots of items you like. The cart holds everything in one place, watches for price drops and restocks, surfaces promo codes, and helps you choose the best payment method from cards saved in Google Wallet.

This guide walks through how to start using Universal Cart, what it can and cannot do right now, and practical tips for getting the most out of it during holiday shopping.

## What Universal Cart Does

Universal Cart acts as a single shopping hub for Google surfaces. According to Google's official announcement, it is built on the Shopping Graph of more than 60 billion product listings and uses Google Wallet for payment insights.

Four main capabilities are available at launch:

1. Save products from multiple retailers while browsing Search, AI Mode, or chatting in the Gemini app. Items stay in the same cart across sessions.
2. Receive notifications about price drops or when an out-of-stock item returns.
3. See relevant promo codes and card perks (cashback, points) so you can pick the best payment method.
4. Check out with Google Pay for participating merchants or transfer the items to the retailer's own cart to finish the purchase there.

Merchants remain the seller of record. You get confirmations and support directly from them. Launch partners include Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta Beauty, Walmart, Wayfair, and many Shopify stores.

The feature is currently limited to the United States. Google has not announced a timeline for other countries. YouTube and Gmail integration, which was mentioned at Google I/O in May 2026, has not shipped yet.

## How to Add Products to Universal Cart

You can add items from three places right now:

- Google Search and Shopping results
- AI Mode in Search
- The Gemini app

Open Search in AI Mode and describe what you need. For example: "waterproof jacket under $150 for rainy dog walks." Product results appear with an option to save them to your cart.

In the Gemini app, ask for product suggestions the same way you would ask a friend. When results show, save the ones you want. Google's example is saving a Steve Madden handbag in AI Mode, then adding a Quince sweater and Ulta Beauty lip gloss later in Gemini. All three appear together when you return.

You must be signed in with a personal Google Account, be at least 18 years old, and have Keep Activity enabled for Gemini shopping features.

Note that Gemini and AI Mode have limited access to your cart data. They may not list your current cart items or order status inside the chat conversation itself. Use the cart interface to review everything.

## Managing Your Cart and Getting Alerts

Inside the cart, products are grouped by merchant. You can review details or remove items. The cart works in the background using Gemini models, so it improves as the models get better.

You receive notifications for price drops and restocks. This is useful if you are waiting for a deal on a specific product without checking each retailer's site every day.

Promo codes that apply to items in your cart appear automatically. Because the cart connects to Google Wallet, it can also compare the benefits of your saved cards and suggest which one gives the best return for a purchase.

## Checking Out

When you are ready to buy, two options appear depending on the merchant:

- Check out with Google Pay directly in the cart for participating stores. Review the order, shipping address, and payment method, then pay.
- Transfer the items to the merchant's own cart and complete the purchase on their website.

Either path uses the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP). You stay in control, and the merchant handles fulfillment and support.

After purchase, supported orders show status updates (shipped, delivered) in the My Orders tab inside the cart.

## Tips for Better Results

Start with clear product descriptions. Specific details such as size, color, budget, and use case help Search and Gemini surface better matches.

Add items over multiple sessions. The cart persists, so you can research one day and finish later.

Enable notifications so you do not miss price drops. This is especially helpful for holiday gifts that fluctuate in price.

Review card perks before paying. Universal Cart surfaces them, but double-check the actual benefits on your card issuer's site if the purchase is large.

If a product is not available for Google Pay checkout, use the transfer option. It still saves time compared with searching the retailer's site again.

For more ways Gemini connects to your Google services, see our guide on [connecting apps to Gemini](/blog/connect-apps-to-gemini/).

## Limitations to Know

Universal Cart is US-only at launch. Users outside the United States will not see the feature yet.

You cannot complete a single checkout that mixes items from multiple retailers at the same time. Checkout happens per merchant or via transfer.

Gemini and AI Mode cannot fully manage the cart through conversation. You will still open the cart view to see the full list and status.

Gmail and YouTube support is planned but not available. Do not expect to add products from those surfaces today.

## Conclusion

Universal Cart brings a practical shopping tool to Google Search and the Gemini app. It solves the common problem of losing track of products while browsing across retailers and gives you price alerts, promo codes, and flexible checkout without handing full control to an AI agent.

If you shop often on Google services and live in the United States, try saving a few items this week. Start in AI Mode or Gemini, open the cart later, and see which notifications arrive. The feature is still early, so expect refinements as more merchants join and additional Google surfaces are added.
