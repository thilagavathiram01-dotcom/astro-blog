A long product page or a dense diagram is a poor prompt. You can describe the section you care about, or you can point at it. Select from screen in Gemini for Chrome does the second job: you draw a box on the current tab, and Chrome attaches that region to your Gemini prompt.

Google documents the control in Chrome Help under “Share specific parts of your screen with Gemini in Chrome.” It is the desktop counterpart to pointing at something on a phone. If you already use Circle to Search, the habit transfers. The [Circle to Search guide](/blog/circle-to-search-android/) covers the Android gesture. This article covers the pointer version in Chrome.

## What you need before the menu appears

Chrome Help lists three requirements. You must be 13 or older and already have access to Gemini in Chrome on desktop. You must be in a supported country for that product. The selection tool itself runs only on a Chromebook Plus, a Mac, or a Windows computer.

If Ask Gemini is missing from the toolbar, update Chrome and restart the browser. The Add menu item will not appear until Gemini in Chrome is available on that profile. Work and school accounts can have extra admin limits, so a missing control on a managed device is often a policy issue, not a broken install.

Sign in with the Google account you use for Gemini. Selection attaches page regions to that chat, so a signed-out window cannot start the flow.

## Turn on Select from screen

Google’s help steps are short. Follow them in order on the tab you want to ask about.

1. Open Chrome on your computer.
2. At the top of the browser, click **Ask Gemini**.
3. In the side panel, open the **Add** menu. On many builds this is the **+** control in the prompt area.
4. Click **Select from screen**.
5. Draw boxes around the parts of the page you want Gemini to see.
6. Type your question or request in the text box, then submit.

Press **Escape** to leave selection mode without sending a prompt.

Before you submit, resize or move a box. Hover a corner and drag to change the shape. Drag the box body to shift it. Chrome Help also says you can draw boxes on top of each other for an irregular region. Overlapping areas are both captured, so a second box does not erase the first.

## Remove a bad selection

A wrong box is easy to undo if you catch it before you send.

Hover the selection and click **Close** on its top-right corner to drop that region. To clear every attached selection at once, close the Gemini in Chrome side panel. Reopen Ask Gemini and start again if you still need the chat.

Do not rely on the browser Back button to cancel a box. Escape exits selection mode. Close removes a single region. Closing the panel clears the set.

## Prompts that match the tool

Google lists three uses on the help page. They are a better starting set than a vague “explain this page” prompt.

- Highlight a few pairs of sneakers and ask which pair fits your running style.
- Capture part of a diagram or a block of text when you want a tighter explanation than a full-page summary.
- Select one object or one section of a page and ask Gemini to reimagine, redesign, or transform that idea.

Keep the question specific to the box. “Compare the two price rows I selected and list what changes after the first year” beats “Is this a good deal?” The model still only sees what you attached plus the words you type. It does not automatically read the rest of the tab unless you add another box or ask about the open page in a separate prompt.

Useful follow-ups after the first answer:

- “Quote only the claims that appear inside the selection.”
- “Turn the selected steps into a checklist.”
- “Rewrite the selected paragraph for a non-expert, and flag any term you are unsure about.”

Check the reply against the page. Chrome’s own product notes say results can vary and should be verified.

## Worked examples

**A spec sheet.** Draw one box around the comparison table and a second box around the footnote that defines the units. Ask which row uses a different measurement. Two boxes keep the footnote in context without sending the whole marketing page.

**A recipe.** Box the ingredient list only. Ask for a version that swaps one item you do not have, and ask Gemini to keep the original quantities visible in the answer. Leave the story intro outside the box so it does not dilute the list.

**A chart.** Box the plot, not the sidebar ads. Ask what the axis labels say and which point is highest. If the labels are small, enlarge the box until the tick marks sit inside the border, then drag the edges in so ads stay out.

**A product photo.** Box a single item, not the whole grid. Ask for a redesign idea or a plain description of visible details. Google explicitly calls out redesign and transform requests for a selected object.

## Limits to plan around

Select from screen is a current-tab tool. It does not jump to another window. Switch to the tab first, then open the Add menu.

The help page limits the feature to Chromebook Plus, Mac, and Windows. A standard Chromebook or a Linux desktop may not show the item even if Ask Gemini is present. Mobile Chrome is a different surface. On Android, screen questions usually go through Gemini over the phone UI, which is closer to Circle to Search than to this desktop box tool.

Selection captures what you draw. If a paywall, cookie banner, or collapsed section hides the text, the box will include the banner, not the hidden copy. Expand the content, dismiss the overlay, then draw.

Sensitive pages deserve a pause. A box can include account numbers, medical details, or private messages if they are on screen. Do not select those regions unless you intend to send them to Gemini. You can pause Gemini in Chrome activity, limit what it can access, and delete history from Chrome’s Gemini controls.

## When the control is missing

Work through this list before you assume the feature was removed.

- Confirm the computer is a Chromebook Plus, Mac, or Windows PC.
- Update Chrome, then quit and reopen it so the toolbar reloads.
- Open Ask Gemini and look in the Add menu, not in the page right-click menu.
- Check that the Google account can use Gemini in Chrome in your country.
- On a work profile, ask the admin whether Gemini in Chrome is allowed.

If the panel opens but Select from screen is absent, the profile may have Gemini chat without the selection add-on yet. Availability still depends on country and access, even after the help article was published.

## Put the pointer to work

Select from screen is a small control with a clear job. Open Ask Gemini, choose it from the Add menu, draw only the region that matters, and ask a question that refers to that region. Resize before you send. Close a bad box, or close the panel to drop them all. Escape leaves selection mode.

Pair it with a precise prompt and a quick check of the source page. That is the whole workflow Google documents, and it is enough for tables, diagrams, product shots, and any paragraph you would rather not retype.
