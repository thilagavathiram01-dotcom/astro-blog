Google has expanded the rollout of Gemini for Home, replacing the older Google Assistant on Nest speakers and displays with a more conversational voice assistant. Users in supported countries can now opt in through early access and switch their home devices to Gemini.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="77" />

This change brings natural language control for smart home devices, multi-intent commands, and improved answers. Basic features remain free, while Gemini Live and camera search require a Google Home Premium subscription.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="77" />

## What Gemini for Home changes on your speakers

Gemini for Home voice assistant handles everyday tasks like controlling lights, playing media, setting timers, and answering questions with better context. You still start with "Hey Google," but responses feel less scripted.

Once switched, every compatible device in your home uses Gemini instead of Assistant. The switch is permanent for that home—you cannot return to the old Assistant.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="77" />

Compatible devices include Nest Hub (1st and 2nd gen), Nest Hub Max, Nest Audio, Nest Mini (2nd gen), Google Home Speaker (2026), and older models like the original Google Home and Home Mini. Speakers need Cast firmware 3.76 or higher; displays need Fuchsia firmware 28 or higher. Older devices support most features but not Gemini Live.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="77" />

## Requirements before you start

Update the Google Home app to the latest version on Android or iOS. Confirm your home is in a supported country and language. Early access covers Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Mexico, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom, and United States, with languages including English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Spanish, and others.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="77" />

On Android, also update the Speech Recognition and Synthesis app from the Play Store. Ensure devices connect to the same Wi-Fi and Google account as your phone.

For firmware on older speakers, see our guide on [joining the Google Home Preview Program](/blog/google-home-preview-program-firmware-update/).

## Request early access in the Google Home app

Early access is separate from the Public Preview for the app itself. Join it to receive an invite when Gemini becomes available for your home.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="77" />

First, check Google Groups settings:

1. Sign in to Google Groups.
2. Open Settings, then Global settings.
3. Confirm "Add me to their groups" is checked and save.

Then in the Google Home app:

1. Open the app.
2. Tap your profile picture or initial at the top right.
3. Tap Home settings, then Early Access.
4. Follow the prompts to request Gemini for Home.

You will receive a notification in the app when ready. Availability rolls out gradually by server, so it may take time even after joining.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="78" />

## Set up Gemini for Home voice assistant

When invited, a banner appears in Home settings: "Set up Gemini for Home for more natural conversations." Tap it and follow the in-app steps.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="77" />

During setup you choose a voice and set response filtering. The voice applies to the entire home. After confirmation, all compatible speakers and displays switch over.

If you dismissed the notification, return to Home settings and look for the setup option under Gemini for Home voice assistant.

Test it by saying "Hey Google, are you Gemini?" The response should confirm the new assistant.

## Useful features and voice commands

Gemini handles multi-step requests in one sentence, such as "dim the kitchen lights and play soft music in the living room." It supports continued conversation without repeating "Hey Google" if you enable the setting.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="77" />

Turn on Continued Conversation:

1. Open Google Home app.
2. Tap profile picture or initial.
3. Go to Home settings > Gemini for Home voice assistant > Continued Conversation.
4. Toggle it on.

For Gemini Live (requires Google Home Premium Standard or Advanced), say "Hey Google, let's chat" on supported devices like Nest Hub 2nd gen, Nest Audio, Nest Mini 2nd gen, and Nest Hub Max. You can resume later with "let's chat again."<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="77" />

Camera search (Advanced plan) lets you ask "Were any packages delivered today?" on devices with Nest Cameras that have event descriptions enabled. Home Brief provides evening summaries of notable camera events.

## Tips for better results

Enable Voice Match so Gemini personalizes responses for household members. Adjust "Hey Google" sensitivity if it misses or over-triggers.

Use natural phrasing. Instead of rigid commands, describe what you want. For media, try "play the song from the movie where the characters dance in the rain."

Check activity at myactivity.google.com/product/home to review what Gemini processed.

If devices lag, confirm firmware updates completed and restart the speaker by unplugging it for 30 seconds.

## Troubleshooting common issues

No setup banner appears: Confirm Early Access enrollment, correct Google account, and latest app version. Wait for the gradual rollout.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="83" />

Voice commands fail after switch: Update Speech Recognition and Synthesis on Android. Check internet connection and device firmware.

Cannot switch back: This is by design. The home stays on Gemini once upgraded.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="77" />

Gemini Live unavailable: Verify Premium subscription and that the device is on the supported list. Older speakers do not support it.

## Conclusion

Setting up Gemini for Home takes a few minutes once the invite arrives. Join Early Access today, follow the app prompts, and start using more natural voice control across your Nest speakers and displays. Basic smart home and information features work without a subscription, while Live and camera tools unlock with Premium.

Check official Google Home help pages for the latest country availability and feature tables, as the rollout continues to expand.
