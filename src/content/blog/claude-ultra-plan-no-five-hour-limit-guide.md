Anthropic renamed its top individual plan from Max 20x to Ultra on October 30, 2026. Existing subscribers moved over automatically with no price change. The bigger shift is the removal of the five-hour session limit for verified Ultra accounts.

This lets power users and Claude Code sessions run longer without hitting a rolling window mid-task. Weekly limits stay in place. If you regularly run long agentic coding or multi-step work, Ultra removes one common interruption.

## What changed with the Ultra plan

Anthropic announced the rename on its official Claude account. Max 5x became simply Max at $100 per month. Max 20x became Ultra at $200 per month. Prices stayed the same. Current subscribers need to do nothing.

Ultra keeps 10 times the Pro plan’s weekly usage. For verified accounts it has no five-hour limit. A temporary limit still applies to newer or unverified accounts until Anthropic completes automatic verification based on account and payment history. You receive an email when the limit is lifted. Phone verification at checkout does not remove it by itself.

Check your status in Settings > Usage. If no Current session bar appears, your account is verified and the five-hour limit is gone.

## How to upgrade or confirm Ultra

If you are already on Max 20x, the change is automatic. Open Claude on the web, desktop, or mobile and look under Settings > Usage or the plan details. It should now show Ultra.

To upgrade from Pro or Max:

1. Go to claude.ai and sign in.
2. Open Settings or the upgrade page linked from the pricing page.
3. Select Ultra at $200 per month (monthly billing only).
4. Complete payment. Mobile app pricing can differ from web.
5. After confirmation, check Settings > Usage for the verification status.

Ultra is monthly only. If you move from an annual Pro plan and the remaining balance exceeds the Ultra price, Anthropic applies a credit when billing addresses match. Otherwise you may need to let the Pro term end first.

## Usage limits that still apply

Ultra gives 10x Pro weekly usage across models. There is also a separate weekly limit for Fable. How quickly you reach the weekly cap depends on model choice, conversation length, and file sizes. Claude Code shares the same pool as chats.

Anthropic can still apply other capacity limits at its discretion. When you hit the weekly limit you can wait for reset, use a limit reset if available, or turn on usage credits on paid plans. Priority access at high-traffic times remains.

Monthly API credits rose to $200 on Ultra (from $100 on Max). These are for the Claude Platform and do not expand the interactive subscription allowance.

## Practical tips for longer sessions

With no five-hour window on verified Ultra, plan multi-hour Claude Code runs on large codebases. Start with Sonnet for most edits and switch to Opus only when the extra quality is worth the higher token use. Pair this with effort settings on Opus 5.5 to control cost, as covered in our guide on [using Claude Opus 5.5 for agentic coding](/blog/how-to-use-claude-opus-5-5-agentic-coding/).

Break very long tasks into checkpoints. Even without a session cutoff, the weekly limit still ends the week. Monitor Settings > Usage regularly so you know when you are approaching the weekly cap.

For heavy parallel work, consider the API with the monthly credits or pay-as-you-go if interactive limits become the constraint. Ultra’s priority access helps during peak times.

## Who should choose Ultra

Choose Ultra if you hit the five-hour limit multiple times per week on Max or Pro while doing productive work. The uninterrupted sessions matter most for agentic coding, long reports, or multi-step workflows that used to stop mid-stream.

Stay on Max if 5x Pro weekly and the five-hour window are enough. Pro remains the right starting point for lighter daily use. Free covers casual questions only.

Features such as Claude Code, Projects, Research, and model access are available on all paid plans. Ultra mainly buys headroom and fewer forced pauses.

## Conclusion

Claude Ultra replaces Max 20x at the same $200 monthly price and removes the five-hour limit for verified accounts while keeping the 10x Pro weekly allowance. Existing subscribers transitioned automatically. Check Settings > Usage to confirm verification, then plan longer Claude Code sessions without the old mid-task cutoff.

Weekly limits and other capacity controls still apply. For most heavy individual users the change reduces one common source of interruption.
