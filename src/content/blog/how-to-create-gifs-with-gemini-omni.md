Google added native GIF creation to the Gemini app on 20 November 2026. You can now turn a short text prompt, up to five images, or one video into a roughly three-second looping GIF powered by Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash.

This replaces the need to hunt through external GIF libraries for reactions or simple animations. Results can be copied into messages, emails, or documents, or downloaded as a standard .gif file.

## Who can create GIFs in Gemini

Availability is limited and rolling out gradually. Official Gemini Apps Help and Google posts list these requirements:

- You must be 18 or older.
- Sign in with a personal Google Account (or a qualifying work/school Workspace account).
- Location: Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Japan, Korea, the United Kingdom, or the United States.
- Google AI subscribers get access more broadly; free users can use it in the listed countries where the rollout has reached their account.

If the option does not appear, wait for the gradual rollout or check your account region and plan. For other Gemini capabilities, see our guide on [using Gemini 4 Neon for complex coding](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-4-neon/).

## How to generate a GIF step by step

Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS, or go to gemini.google.com on the web.

1. Start a new chat or open the image/video tools. On desktop, look for Create video in the sidebar.
2. Choose GIF in the format picker (or type a prompt that starts with “Create a GIF of…”).
3. Describe the scene clearly. Example: “A dog riding a surfboard on a sunny beach, looping.”
4. Optional: Attach up to five images or one video you have rights to use as reference material.
5. Submit and wait. Generation can take a few minutes. You can start other chats while it runs.
6. When ready, copy the GIF to paste into supported apps, or download the .gif file.

You can refine the result with a follow-up prompt in the same thread, such as changing colours or swapping an object.

## Tips for better results

Keep prompts specific about motion, duration feel, and style. Omni 1.2 Flash produces consistent frames across the short loop.

- Mention “looping” or “seamless loop” if you want cleaner repeats.
- Use reference images for personalised results (only content you own or have permission to use).
- Templates for reaction-style memes are available in the video tool on some platforms.
- Avoid complex multi-character scenes; three seconds works best for simple, clear actions.

Generation runs on Google’s servers, so device storage or battery impact is low.

## Limits and practical notes

GIFs are fixed at about three seconds. There is no public detail on exact resolution, frame rate, or daily quotas beyond the gradual rollout note.

Only upload photos or videos you have the right to use. Google’s Prohibited Use Policy applies to all generated content.

Free access is country-limited; paid Google AI plans expand availability. Check the Gemini Apps Help page for the latest eligibility if the feature is missing.

## Conclusion

GIF creation in Gemini is a quick way to make custom short animations without leaving the app. Start with a clear prompt, attach references if needed, and download or copy the result once Omni 1.2 Flash finishes.

The feature continues rolling out, so eligible users should check the app or gemini.google.com regularly.
