Google made Gemini Tasks free for hundreds of millions of people on November 17, 2026. The feature, previously limited to paid Google AI Pro and Ultra plans and known as Spark, now runs on the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model in six countries.

You can hand off multi-step jobs such as booking appointments, comparing products, analyzing bills, or updating calendars. Gemini works in the background and checks with you before critical actions like purchases.

This guide shows the exact requirements, how to switch modes, and the new US-only channels for text and email.

## What Gemini Tasks Does

Gemini Tasks turns the Gemini app into a personal agent. In Chat mode it answers questions and stops. In Task mode it plans steps, uses connected apps, browses the web, and continues until the job finishes or needs your input.

Google says the free rollout covers the United States, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. It uses the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model, which the company describes as built for rapid execution across everyday agentic workflows.

In the United States, advanced capabilities include calling businesses (with AI disclosure), checking out via Google Pay within a spending cap, signing in with Password Manager, texting Gemini, and forwarding emails to gemini@google.com. These features start in the US only.

Tasks run in a hardware-isolated virtual machine dedicated to your account. Google layers prompt-injection filters and Safe Browsing on top. You can jump in mid-task, set routine actions to “Always allow,” and stop any task instantly.

## Requirements Before You Start

You must meet these conditions:

- Age 18 or older.
- Personal Google Account (work and school accounts are not supported).
- Gemini Activity / History turned on.
- Location in one of the six free countries, or a Google AI Pro / Ultra subscription elsewhere (except the European Economic Area, UK, Switzerland, and Nigeria, where Tasks are unavailable).

The feature works in the Gemini mobile app (Android and iOS), the Gemini Mac app, and at gemini.google.com. It is rolling out, so the Task toggle may not appear immediately for every account.

## How to Start Your First Task

Open the Gemini app or go to gemini.google.com and sign in with your personal account.

Look for the Chat / Task toggle. On mobile it sits above the prompt box. On the web it sits below. Tap or click Task. The interface updates to “What can I get done for you?” and the input placeholder changes to “Describe a task.”

Type a clear instruction that includes any constraints. Examples that work well:

- “Compare three USB-C chargers under $30 with good reviews and list the top option with a link.”
- “Find open service appointments for my 2019 Honda Odyssey at the two nearest dealerships for Friday morning and add the best one to my calendar.”
- “Review my last three electric bills from email and explain why the latest one is higher.”

Submit the task. Gemini begins working in the background. You receive updates in the thread. It pauses and asks for confirmation before purchases or other critical steps. You can add more instructions at any time or hit Stop to cancel.

For scheduled tasks, tell Gemini “do this every Monday morning” or use the Scheduled page once it appears.

## US-Only Channels: Text and Email

US users can start tasks without opening the app.

To set up texting:

1. Open Gemini on Android or iOS.
2. Tap Settings.
3. Tap Channels.
4. Tap Set up under Text with Gemini.
5. Verify your US phone number on an eligible carrier.

Once active, text Gemini from Google Messages or iMessage. The channel is not end-to-end encrypted.

To use email, forward messages to gemini@google.com. Gemini replies with a link to the new task. This works for school emails, bills, or appointment confirmations.

Google notes that calling is unavailable in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska. Shopping uses the Agent Payments Protocol with tokenized cards from Mastercard and Visa (American Express coming soon). Receipts land in Google Wallet. You can set a hard spending cap in advance.

## Practical Tips for Better Results

Write the job, not a question. “Book a plumber who can come this week and compare three quotes” outperforms “plumbers near me?”

Include budget, deadline, and exclusions. “Under $50, no subscriptions, prefer Amazon” keeps Gemini on track.

Connect the apps you need (Gmail, Calendar, Maps, Password Manager, Instacart, and others). Gemini already connects to more than 60 services.

Review task settings regularly. Delete remote browser data if you prefer not to keep site sign-ins. You can also turn Task mode off entirely; past chats remain.

Expect occasional misses. Google has not eliminated every error. Use the thumbs-down feedback when a task fails or produces incomplete results.

Free users share usage limits based on compute. Complex or frequent tasks consume quota faster. Paid plans increase concurrent schedules and usage multipliers.

## Safety and Privacy Controls

Every task runs in an isolated virtual machine that is never shared. Prompt-injection filters scan web content. Google Safe Browsing limits which links open.

Gemini is designed to ask before clicking Buy and to clarify ambiguous instructions. Routine actions can be pre-approved so it does not interrupt every grocery add.

You control connected apps, can disconnect them anytime, and can delete history. Google publishes a technical paper on its defense-in-depth approach and runs a bug-bounty program for agentic systems.

## Conclusion

Gemini Tasks is now free for adults in six countries on the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model. Switch from Chat to Task, describe the job with clear constraints, and let it run. US users gain calling, Google Pay checkout, text, and email channels.

Start with a low-stakes task such as product comparison or calendar cleanup. Review the results, adjust settings, and expand from there. The feature continues to roll out, so check your account if the toggle is missing.
