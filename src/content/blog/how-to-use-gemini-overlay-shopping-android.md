Google is rolling out new shopping prompts on the Gemini floating overlay for Android. When you open Gemini over a product page in a shopping app, it now suggests actions like comparing prices or checking warranty terms.

This feature uses a screenshot of the visible product page as context. It does not require sharing your full screen with Gemini Live. The prompts appear during the Black Friday and Cyber Monday period, helping shoppers evaluate listings without switching apps.

![Person scrolling through online shopping on phone](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1768987439370-bd60d3d0b28b?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What the Gemini Overlay Shopping Prompts Do

The overlay detects when you are viewing a product in supported shopping apps. It offers three main suggestions:

- Help me pick
- Compare prices
- Check warranty and returns

Tapping a prompt sends a screenshot of the current page plus the selected text to Gemini. For a price comparison, Gemini checks the current listing against other retailers such as Walmart or Target. Results draw from Google’s Shopping Graph.

The feature works with Amazon, Flipkart, and quick-commerce apps like Blinkit, according to testing reported by Android Authority. It is live with Google app version 17.78.13 and appears to be a server-side rollout.

Conversations created this way appear in your Gemini chat history, so you can return to them later.

## How to Activate the Overlay and Use the Prompts

1. Open a supported shopping app and navigate to a product detail page.
2. Trigger the Gemini overlay the same way you normally open Gemini on your device (long-press power button, or the method set in your system settings).
3. Look for the new shopping suggestion chips above or near the input field.
4. Tap “Compare prices,” “Help me pick,” or “Check warranty and returns.”
5. Gemini captures the visible screen content and processes the request.
6. Review the response. For price comparisons, note the listed retailers and any deals or promo codes it surfaces.
7. Follow up in the same chat if you need more details, such as delivery times or return policies.

The overlay only sees what is currently visible on screen. Scrolling the product page before triggering the overlay improves the context Gemini receives.

## Tips for Better Results During Sales

Focus the screen on the product title, price, and key specifications before opening the overlay. This gives Gemini clearer visual context.

Always verify the prices and availability Gemini returns. AI shopping results can lag or miss regional variations, so cross-check the final listing on the retailer’s site before buying.

You can combine this with other Gemini features. For longer multi-step shopping tasks such as monitoring a price or preparing a cart, switch to Task mode in the Gemini app. See our guide on [using free Gemini Tasks for shopping and automation](/blog/how-to-use-free-gemini-tasks-book-shop-and-automate/).

Set clear limits if you later hand a purchase task to Gemini. Confirm any checkout steps yourself.

## Privacy and Limitations

The feature takes a one-time screenshot for context. It does not continuously share your screen unless you separately start Gemini Live screen sharing.

Shopping suggestions are currently limited to the visible portion of the page. Full-page analysis is not available for product listings the way it is for PDFs or web pages.

Availability depends on the Google app version and server-side flags. Update the Google app from the Play Store and ensure you are signed in with a personal Google Account.
