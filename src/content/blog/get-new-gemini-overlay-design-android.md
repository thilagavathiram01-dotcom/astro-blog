Google rolled out a refreshed Gemini overlay design to every Android phone on October 29. The update brings a heavy blur effect, colorful specks, and a translucent pill-shaped bar that first appeared on Pixel 11 devices with Gemini Intelligence.

This server-side change arrives through the Google app. Most users see it after a simple force stop. The new look softens the background under the overlay while keeping icons and text readable.

## What the New Overlay Looks Like

The updated overlay applies a strong blur over the area behind the input bar. Boundaries of the pill become hard to distinguish at first glance. Tiny colorful specks and swirls appear during the entrance animation.

Once the animation finishes, the bar shows a translucent edge instead of a solid border. This matches the Neural Expressive style Google has tested across Gemini surfaces.

![Close-up of a smartphone with a soft blur background](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1689443111025-e3093c108915?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

The design started on phones running Gemini Intelligence. It now reaches other Android devices after a backend push. No Play Store download is required.

## Requirements Before You Start

Confirm these points first:

- Your phone runs a recent version of the Google app (stable channel works for most people).
- Gemini is set as your default digital assistant.
- You have an active Google account signed in.
- Screen context or related permissions are enabled if you use on-screen help.

The change is server-side. Two phones on the same app version can show different interfaces until the backend flags your account.

## How to Force the New Design to Appear

Follow these steps if the old solid pill still shows up:

1. Open Settings on your Android phone.
2. Tap Apps, then See all apps.
3. Find and select Google.
4. Tap Force stop, then confirm.
5. Return to your home screen.
6. Long-press the power button or say “Hey Google” to trigger the overlay.

The new blur and translucent edges should appear on the next launch. If nothing changes, wait a few minutes and try again. Some accounts receive the flag later in the day.

![Smartphone showing an AI interface with blurred elements](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1716637644831-e046c73be197?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

Clearing the Google app cache is optional. Go to Storage & cache inside the same app info screen and tap Clear cache. Avoid clearing data unless you want to re-sign in.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

The overlay sometimes opens the full Gemini app instead of the floating bar. This happened in earlier builds. Force stop usually restores the correct behavior.

If the design still looks old:

- Check for Google app updates in the Play Store.
- Restart the phone after the force stop.
- Switch the digital assistant to Google Assistant, test the shortcut, then switch back to Gemini.
- Confirm “Display over other apps” is allowed for the Google app.

Users on secondary Google accounts sometimes see the update first. Sign out and back in if the primary account lags.

## Tips for Daily Use

Keep the Google app updated so future server-side flags arrive quickly. The overlay now pairs well with the minimize bubble for multitasking. Trigger a query, minimize to the floating icon, and continue using other apps while Gemini works.

Test screen context on a webpage or document. The blur keeps the underlying content visible enough to stay oriented without losing focus on the prompt.

## Conclusion

The new Gemini overlay design is available on all Android devices after a short force stop of the Google app. The heavy blur, translucent pill, and color accents create a cleaner floating experience that matches recent Pixel 11 hardware.

Most users see the change within minutes. If your phone still shows the older solid bar, repeat the force stop or check for app updates. The update requires no extra downloads and works across major Android brands.
