Voice typing often turns your thoughts into a mess of ums, ahs, and mid-sentence corrections. Google fixed that for Pixel 10 users with Rambler. The feature arrived with the November 2026 Pixel Drop as part of Gemini Intelligence. It cleans spoken input in real time so the text that lands in your message looks polished.

This guide shows exactly how to turn it on, what devices support it, and how it fits with other Gemini Intelligence tools. You stay in control the whole time.

## What Rambler Does on Pixel 10

Rambler is Gemini-powered voice typing inside Gboard. You speak naturally. It removes filler words, fixes self-corrections, adds punctuation, and formats the result into clear text. A glowing animation shows while it listens. When you finish, the cleaned draft appears in the field.

Google first launched it on Pixel 11 devices. The November Pixel Drop expanded it to Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold. It also reaches the new motorola signature 27. Availability depends on region and language, and some usage limits apply.

The feature needs an internet connection for full capability, though limited offline mode exists. Gboard must be your default keyboard with microphone permission.

## Confirm Your Device and Update

Check three things first.

Your phone must be a supported Pixel 10 model (not the Pixel 10a). The November 2026 Pixel Drop must be installed. Android AI Core should be current.

Open Settings, tap your Google profile at the top, then All services > System services. Look for Android AI Core and install any pending update. Google rolls this out through Play Services, so a manual check speeds it up if the feature is missing after the system update.

Restart if needed. Then open Gboard in any text field.

## Enable and Use Rambler Step by Step

Follow these steps once the update is in place.

1. Open an app with a text field, such as Messages or Gmail. Tap the field so Gboard appears.
2. Tap the gear icon on the keyboard toolbar to open Settings.
3. Select Voice typing.
4. Switch the dictation mode from Standard to Rambler.
5. Return to your text field and tap the microphone icon. Speak naturally. You will see the listening animation.
6. Tap Done when finished. The cleaned text drops into the draft.

You can also trigger the intro dialog the first time. Tap Start if it appears. Change the preferred mode later in the same Voice typing menu.

Test with a short message that includes an um or a correction, such as “Meet at three, wait no, two pm.” Rambler should output the polished version.

## Tips for Better Results

Speak at a normal pace. Rambler handles pauses and self-corrections, but clear speech still produces better drafts.

Grant microphone permission to Gboard and keep the app updated from the Play Store. Set Gboard as the default keyboard if it is not already.

Check language support. The feature works for select countries and languages. Switch Gboard languages if needed for mixed input.

Review the output before sending. Results can vary, and Google notes that you should always check responses.

If Rambler does not appear, confirm the Pixel Drop, update Android AI Core, and restart the phone. Some users report a short delay while the update reaches all devices.
