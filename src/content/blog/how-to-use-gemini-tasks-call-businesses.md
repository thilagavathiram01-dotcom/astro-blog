Google expanded Tasks in the Gemini app on 17 November 2026. Eligible users can now hand off multi-step jobs, including asking Gemini to call businesses on their behalf. The feature builds on Google Duplex technology and runs on the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model for free users in supported countries.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="813" />

This guide shows how to start a calling task, what Gemini discloses, and the current limits. It draws only from official Google announcements and support pages.

## Who can use business calling in Gemini Tasks

Tasks themselves are free for adults in the United States, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. Business calling is more restricted. Google’s help documentation states that Gemini can make phone calls to US phone numbers for users who are 18 or older and located in the United States.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="848" />

Calling is not currently available in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska. Gemini calls from a Google virtual number, so standard carrier rates do not apply. Businesses can opt out through their Google Business Profile or by saying so during a call.

If you are outside the United States or in one of the excluded states, Tasks still works for other jobs such as calendar updates, bill analysis, and web research. See our earlier guide on [how to use free Gemini Tasks](/blog/free-gemini-tasks-personal-assistance-guide/) for the basic toggle and Hydrogen setup.

## Prerequisites before you start a call

Confirm these points first:

- You are signed in with a personal Google Account (work and school accounts are not supported).
- Gemini Apps Activity is turned on.
- You are 18 or older and in the United States (for calling).
- The Gemini app is updated on Android or iOS, or you are on gemini.google.com.

Google recommends giving clear, complete instructions the first time so Gemini can finish without extra steps from you. Include preferred times, alternatives, and any details the business will need.

## Step-by-step: ask Gemini to call a business

Open the Gemini app or go to gemini.google.com. On the home screen, switch the toggle from Chat to Task. The prompt box changes to “Describe a task.”

Type or speak a specific request. Examples that match Google’s guidance include:

- “Call the two nearest Honda dealerships and book a service appointment for my 2019 Odyssey early Friday morning next month. Check my calendar for free slots.”
- “Ask the nearest dry cleaner how much they charge to hem pants and whether they can finish them by Thursday.”
- “Call [restaurant name] and check the wait time for a party of two tonight. Put our name on the list if it is under 45 minutes.”

Gemini shows a summary of the task. Review the phone number it will dial and any personal details it plans to share, such as your name. You can ask it to revise the plan before proceeding.

When you are ready, select Call now or Call when open. You can also schedule the call for a later time. Gemini places the call from a Google number, identifies itself as an automated system calling from Google, handles hold music and menus, and reports back with a summary and transcript.

If the business does not answer, Gemini can retry. You stay in control and can interrupt or cancel at any point.

## What happens during and after the call

Google Research explained that Duplex technology now powers these calls inside Tasks. Every call begins by stating that it is an automated system from Google. Gemini does not share payment or private account details unless you explicitly approve it.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="808" />

After the call ends, Gemini updates your Google Calendar if an appointment was booked and places any receipts or tracking information in Google Wallet where relevant. You receive a transcript so you can verify what was said.

In one published example, Gemini called two dealerships in parallel, found an open Friday morning slot that matched the user’s calendar, and completed the booking in under five minutes.

## Tips for better results

Be precise about the outcome you want. “Book the earliest available service appointment next week that fits my calendar” works better than “call the mechanic.”

Provide alternatives. Mention a second preferred day or a maximum wait time so Gemini can finish without calling you back for clarification.

Review the summary every time. Confirm the business name, phone number, and any personal information before you approve the call.

Start with low-stakes requests such as checking hours or product availability. Once you trust the results, move to bookings.

If a business opts out, Gemini will note that and stop attempting calls to that number.

## Limits and what to expect

Calling is limited to verified US businesses and US phone numbers. It is unavailable in the five states listed above. Tasks themselves remain free in the six countries, but advanced actions such as purchases through the Agent Payments Protocol also start in the United States.

Gemini runs each task in a sandboxed environment. It asks for approval before purchases or other sensitive steps. Usage limits still apply and are higher on paid Google AI plans.

Outside the free countries, Tasks requires a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription and is not available in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or Nigeria.

## Conclusion

Gemini Tasks turns a simple toggle into a practical way to offload phone calls and follow-up work. Eligible US users can hand Gemini a clear goal, review the plan, and let it handle the call while they stay in the loop through transcripts and calendar updates.

Start with one specific request today. Check the official Gemini Apps Help page for the latest eligibility details, then try the Task toggle on a non-urgent job.
