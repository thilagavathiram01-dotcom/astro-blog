Google made Tasks in the Gemini app free for adults in six countries on 17 November 2026. The feature runs on the new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model and lets you hand multi-step work to the assistant while you do other things.

This guide shows how to turn it on, what it can do, practical examples, safety notes, and how to get reliable results on Android and iOS.

## What Free Gemini Tasks Includes

Tasks replaces the older paid Spark agent. You toggle from Chat to Task in the app, describe the job, and Gemini works in the background. It uses your Google account data for calendar, email, and other connected apps when you allow it.

Free access covers the United States, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. You need a personal Google Account, to be 18 or older, and to have History turned on. Work and school accounts are not supported.

Advanced options such as calling businesses, Google Pay checkout, and text or email channels start in the United States only. Calling is unavailable in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska. Purchase tasks carry a maximum budget of $1,000 and expire within 14 days.

The system runs inside a hardware-isolated virtual machine on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine. Each session is sandboxed and not shared across users. Prompt-injection filters and Safe Browsing limit the links it opens.

## Step-by-Step Setup

1. Update the Gemini app from the Play Store or App Store to the latest version.
2. Open Gemini and sign in with your personal Google Account.
3. Confirm History is on. Go to Settings > Gemini Apps Activity if the toggle is off. You can keep history while opting out of model training in the new controls.
4. On the home screen, tap the Task toggle above or below the prompt box. It switches from Chat.
5. Type or speak a clear task. Examples: “Book a table for two at a nearby Italian restaurant Friday at 7 pm” or “Summarise my unread emails from this week and add key dates to Calendar.”
6. Review the plan Gemini shows. Approve steps that need confirmation, such as purchases or calls.
7. Let it run. You can leave the app. Progress appears in the task thread with status labels such as Ready, In progress, or Needs attention.

The same toggle works on gemini.google.com. Select Hydrogen if the model dropdown appears. You can also start tasks by forwarding emails to gemini@google.com once Channels are set up.

## What You Can Ask It to Do

Gemini Tasks connects to more than 60 apps. Common uses include:

- Calendar and email organisation, including conflict detection
- Shopping research and checkout with Google Pay (US) while still earning card points or cashback
- Restaurant or appointment booking, with Gemini disclosing it is an AI
- Bill analysis from utility portals via Google Password Manager integration
- Grocery list additions through Instacart or similar partners, including photo follow-ups
- Comparing options across multiple sites and compiling a shortlist

You set spending caps for purchases. Receipts and tracking info land in Google Wallet. For routine trusted actions you can set “Always allow” so it does not pause every time.

## Practical Examples That Work Well

Start with one of these proven patterns:

- Service appointment: “Our 2019 Honda Odyssey is overdue for service. Call the two nearest dealerships and book the earliest Friday morning slot that fits my Calendar.”
- Utility bill review: “Access our utility portal and analyse the last three bills. Identify the main driver of the recent spike.”
- Trip shopping: “Find the best USB-C charger options under $40, compare wattage and colour, then help me check out with the discount code you find.”
- School email batch: “Forward these school emails to you. Extract every date, add them to Calendar, invite my spouse, and flag any conflicts.”

Each example keeps the request specific and ties into existing Google data so the model needs fewer clarifying questions.

## Tips for Better Results

Give specific instructions. Include dates, budgets, preferred outcomes, and any constraints. Start with simple tasks and expand once you see the pattern of success.

Monitor the work panel for completed, current, and planned steps. Jump in early if the direction drifts. You can add more instructions at any point.

Turn on Channels in the US to text or email tasks. Open Settings > Channels > Set up under Text with Gemini, then verify your phone number. You can also forward emails to gemini@google.com. Reply STOP in the text thread if you want to pause messaging only.

Free usage has compute-based limits that Google does not publish in detail. Complex or frequent tasks consume more of your daily allowance. Confirm sensitive actions yourself until you trust the pattern.

Keep the subject line descriptive when emailing tasks so the model understands intent quickly.

## Limitations to Know

The feature is not available in the European Economic Area, UK, Switzerland, or Nigeria even with a paid plan. Outside the six free countries it requires Google AI Pro or Ultra.

Some websites still challenge automated agents. Gemini may hand the final click back to you. Always review purchases and calls before they complete.

Calling is restricted in five US states at launch. Work and school accounts cannot use free Tasks. History must stay on for the feature to operate.

## Safety and Privacy Notes

Each task runs in a dedicated, hardware-isolated virtual machine that is never shared. Multiple layers of defence include prompt-injection filters and Safe Browsing. Gemini pauses for confirmation on critical steps such as clicking Buy.

You can disconnect apps, delete history, or adjust task settings at any time. Google published a technical paper on the multi-layer defence approach if you want the full details.

Give feedback with a thumbs-down when a task misses the mark so the system improves.
