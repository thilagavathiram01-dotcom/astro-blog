Google made Tasks in the Gemini app free for adults in six countries on 17 November 2026. The feature runs on the new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model and lets you hand multi-step work to the assistant while you do other things.

This guide shows how to turn it on, what it can do, and how to get reliable results on Android and iOS.

## What Free Gemini Tasks Includes

Tasks replaces the older paid Spark agent. You toggle from Chat to Task in the app, describe the job, and Gemini works in the background. It uses your Google account data for calendar, email, and other connected apps when you allow it.

Free access covers the United States, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. You need a personal Google Account, to be 18 or older, and to have History turned on. Work and school accounts are not supported.

Advanced options such as calling businesses, Google Pay checkout, and text or email channels start in the United States only. Calling is unavailable in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska.

## Step-by-Step Setup

1. Update the Gemini app from the Play Store or App Store.
2. Open Gemini and sign in with your personal Google Account.
3. Confirm History is on. Go to Settings > Gemini Apps Activity if needed.
4. On the home screen, tap the Task toggle above or below the prompt box. It switches from Chat.
5. Type or speak a clear task. Examples: “Book a table for two at a nearby Italian restaurant Friday at 7 pm” or “Summarise my unread emails from this week and add key dates to Calendar.”
6. Review the plan Gemini shows. Approve steps that need confirmation, such as purchases or calls.
7. Let it run. You can leave the app. Progress appears in the task thread.

The same toggle works on gemini.google.com. Select Hydrogen if the model dropdown appears.

## What You Can Ask It to Do

Gemini Tasks connects to more than 60 apps. Common uses include:

- Calendar and email organisation
- Shopping research and checkout with Google Pay (US)
- Restaurant or appointment booking
- Bill analysis from utility portals via Password Manager
- Grocery list additions through Instacart or similar partners

You set spending caps for purchases. Receipts land in Google Wallet. Gemini discloses it is an AI when it calls a business.

## Tips for Better Results

Give specific instructions. Include dates, budgets, and preferred outcomes. Start with simple tasks and expand.

Monitor the work panel for completed, current, and planned steps. Jump in if the direction drifts.

Turn on Channels in the US to text or email tasks. Open Settings > Channels > Set up under Text with Gemini, then verify your number. Forward emails to gemini@google.com.

Free usage has compute-based limits. Complex tasks consume more of your daily allowance. Confirm sensitive actions yourself.

## Limitations to Know

The feature is not available in the European Economic Area, UK, Switzerland, or Nigeria even with a paid plan. Outside the six free countries it requires Google AI Pro or Ultra.

Some websites still challenge automated agents. Gemini may hand the final click back to you. Always review purchases and calls.
