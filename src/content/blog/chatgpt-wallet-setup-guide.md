OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet on 13 November 2026. It lets your dots AI agents complete online purchases after you review and approve each one with biometrics.

The feature stores your card once. After that, a payment token replaces the real number at checkout. Neither the agent nor the merchant sees your full card details. You keep your card rewards and dispute protections.

Wallet works only inside dots conversations. It is not available in regular Chat, Work, or standalone Codex threads. Access is rolling out gradually to ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the United States who are 18 or older, live in the US, and hold an eligible US-issued credit card.

## Who can use ChatGPT Wallet

You need a ChatGPT Pro plan. Free, Plus, and most other plans do not include dots or Wallet yet. The card must be a US-issued American Express, Chase, Mastercard, or Visa credit card. Debit cards and virtual cards are not supported at launch. For Chase cards, you must be the primary cardholder.

You also need a US billing address. Approvals require Face ID, Touch ID, or a similar biometric check on a supported device. If your computer lacks biometrics, OpenAI can send a link or QR code to a signed-in phone.

Set up a dot before you start. Wallet is not available until you have at least one active dot.

## How to add a card

You can add a card by asking your dot or through Settings.

In a dots conversation, type “Help me add a card to Wallet.” Open the secure setup link that appears. Never type the full card number in the chat.

On mobile, open the left navigation, tap Settings, then Wallet, then Add Card. The same secure form appears.

Complete these steps:

1. Enter the card number, expiration date, and security code in the secure form. American Express cards use the four-digit code on the front.
2. Review the ChatGPT Wallet terms and Privacy Notice, then tap Add card.
3. Finish any bank verification. Your bank may ask for a code or an in-app approval. Wait until the screen shows “Card added.”

If you are unsure the card saved, ask your dot to show your saved cards before trying again.

For Chase cards, install the Chase app and sign in with biometrics first. Follow the setup link or scan the QR code to approve the connection inside the Chase app, then return to Wallet.

## Approve a purchase

Once the card is saved, ask your dot for help. Examples from OpenAI include “Help me find a new sweater” or “Book me a hotel for my trip to Chicago next weekend.”

When the dot is ready to check out, it shows a purchase request. Review every detail:

- Merchant name
- Items or booking summary
- Total amount
- Selected card (last four digits)
- Any shipping or recurring-payment terms

Tap Approve and complete the biometric check. If you cannot approve on the current device, use the link or QR code on a phone signed into the same account.

After approval, the payment network generates a token with its own number, security code, and expiration. The dot finishes checkout with that token. “Ready to check out” means your approval succeeded; it does not guarantee the merchant accepted the order. Check the merchant confirmation before starting another purchase.

If you change your mind before approving, decline the request. To cancel an order that already went through, contact the merchant directly.

## Manage saved cards

Ask your dot to show your saved cards. The list displays each card’s nickname, last four digits, and expiration date. To set a default, say “Make the card ending in 1234 my default.”

To remove a card, identify it by the last four digits and confirm. Removal stops new Wallet payments with that card but does not cancel existing orders or close the card with your bank. Contact the merchant for refunds or to stop recurring charges.

Cards in Wallet are separate from the payment method used for your ChatGPT subscription and from any gift-card balance.

## Tips and limits

Review every request carefully. Recurring charges follow the terms you agreed to with the merchant. The agent cannot move money or invest on your behalf.

Not every website accepts agent checkouts yet. If a purchase fails, confirm with the merchant before trying another method. OpenAI notes that some fraud systems may still block agent traffic.

Never share your full card number, security code, or bank verification codes in chat or support messages. Enter them only in the secure forms.

For order problems, contact the merchant. For unrecognized charges or bank issues, contact your card issuer. For Wallet setup errors or suspected account access, contact OpenAI Support.

## Conclusion

ChatGPT Wallet turns a one-time card setup into controlled agent shopping. You review the details, approve with biometrics, and the network token handles the rest. The rollout is limited, so eligibility and store support will expand over time.

If you already use dots for other tasks, Wallet adds a practical next step. Start with a small purchase to learn the approval flow. For more on ChatGPT’s newer interface features, see our guide to [GPT-6 Intelligent UI](/blog/chatgpt-intelligent-ui-gpt-6/).
