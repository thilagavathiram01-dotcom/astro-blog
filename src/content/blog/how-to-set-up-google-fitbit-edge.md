The Google Fitbit Edge arrives in stores today, October 27, 2026. It sits between the screenless Fitbit Air and the full Pixel Watch 5. The $179.99 tracker brings a 1.34-inch AMOLED display, built-in GPS, ECG, and up to seven days of battery life in a slim 11.5 mm aluminum case.

You get three months of Google Health Premium included. That unlocks AI coaching and advanced insights. This guide walks through unboxing, pairing, initial setup, and the settings that matter most.

## What You Need Before You Start

Fitbit Edge works with Android 11 or later and iOS 16.4 or later. You need a Google Account and the Google Health app. Download it from the Play Store or App Store before you charge the tracker.

The device arrives partially charged. Use the included magnetic charger. A full charge takes about 90 minutes. Five minutes of charging restores a full day of battery when the level is low.

Water resistance reaches 50 meters (5 ATM) with IP68 dust protection. Avoid hot water and soaps during the first few hours while the seals settle.

## Charge and Power On the Fitbit Edge

1. Attach the magnetic charger to the back of the Edge. The pins align with the contacts.
2. Plug the USB-C cable into a power adapter or computer port.
3. Wait until the screen shows a charging icon. A full charge is recommended for the first use.
4. Press the side button for three seconds to power on if it does not start automatically.

The Hybrid OLED display shows the time and basic tiles once it boots. The physical button provides haptic feedback for workouts and menus.

## Download the Google Health App and Create or Sign In

Open the Google Health app. Sign in with your Google Account. If you previously used the Fitbit app, your account migrates automatically in most cases. Google Health replaced the standalone Fitbit app earlier in 2026.

Grant the requested permissions for Bluetooth, notifications, and location. Location helps with GPS workouts and automatic activity detection. Bluetooth is required for pairing.

## Pair the Fitbit Edge

1. In the Google Health app, tap the device icon or the “Add device” button.
2. Select Fitbit Edge from the list of available devices.
3. On the tracker, follow the on-screen pairing code or confirmation prompt.
4. Confirm the code matches on both the app and the Edge.
5. Wait for the connection to complete. The app will sync initial data.

The process usually finishes in under two minutes. Keep the tracker within a few feet of your phone during pairing. Once connected, the Edge appears in the Devices section of the app.

## Complete the Initial Setup

After pairing, the app walks you through profile details. Enter your height, weight, age, and sex. These improve calorie and heart-rate zone estimates. Set a daily step goal. The default is often 10,000 steps, but you can adjust it later.

Choose notification preferences. The Edge can show call and message alerts. Media controls appear when you play music on a connected Bluetooth device. Google Maps turn-by-turn directions are expected later this year.

Select a watch face. The library includes analog and digital options with complications for steps, heart rate, and weather. You can change faces anytime in the app or on the device.

## Configure Health and Fitness Tracking

Open the tracker settings in the Google Health app. Enable continuous heart-rate monitoring. The optical PPG sensor tracks heart rate around the clock. The ECG app is available in supported regions for on-demand atrial fibrillation checks. It requires a one-time calibration.

Turn on automatic activity recognition. The Edge detects up to 100 workout types, including runs, walks, and rides, even if you forget to start a session. Built-in GPS records outdoor routes with claimed accuracy improvements over previous Fitbit trackers.

Set sleep tracking preferences. The device monitors sleep stages, heart-rate variability, and skin temperature. A dedicated temperature sensor tracks nightly fluctuations. Review the morning recovery score in the app.

Health Guardian features track trends such as blood-pressure estimates and insulin-resistance patterns. These are wellness insights only and not medical diagnoses. They become more useful with the included Google Health Premium trial.

## Optimize Battery Life

The Edge targets up to seven days with the always-on display off and default Battery Saver settings. Enabling the always-on display drops runtime significantly, often to around two days.

Tips for longer battery:
- Keep the always-on display off unless you prefer it.
- Reduce notification frequency for non-essential apps.
- Charge with the included magnetic puck for the fastest top-ups.
- The modular design allows battery replacement in supported regions, a first for Fitbit trackers.

Fast charging restores a full day in five minutes when the battery is low, using a 30W adapter.

## Tips for Better Tracking Accuracy

Wear the Edge snugly on your non-dominant wrist, about one finger-width above the wrist bone. Clean the optical sensor regularly. Avoid wearing it over tattoos or clothing that blocks the sensors.

For GPS workouts, start the session on the device before you begin moving. The upgraded GPS chipset and antenna improve route accuracy compared with the Charge 6. Heart-rate readings can take a minute or two to stabilize during intense intervals.

Sync the device at least once a day. It stores roughly seven days of detailed motion data and 30 days of daily totals if it is offline.

## Customize Bands and Appearance

The Edge ships with an Active Band in one of four case colors: Obsidian, Fog, Olive, or Canyon. Interchangeable bands include Active Sport, Performance Loop, Magnet, and Metal Links. A Stephen Curry edition is available separately for $219.99.

Swap bands by sliding the attachment pins. The process takes seconds and does not require tools. Match the band to your activity: silicone for workouts, metal for daily wear.

## Troubleshooting Common Setup Issues

If pairing fails, restart both the phone and the Edge. Forget the device in Bluetooth settings and try again. Ensure the Google Health app has the latest update. On Android, check that Google Play services is current.

If the display stays dim, adjust brightness in the device settings. Outdoor visibility is strong, but the screen is still smaller than a full smartwatch.

For ECG or advanced health features that do not appear, confirm your region supports them. Availability varies by country.

## Conclusion

Setting up the Google Fitbit Edge takes less than 15 minutes once the Google Health app is installed. Charge the device, pair it, complete your profile, and enable continuous heart-rate and automatic activity tracking. Adjust the always-on display and notifications to match your battery needs.

The included three-month Premium trial lets you test AI coaching and deeper insights. After the trial, decide whether the subscription fits your goals. The Edge is available now at the Google Store and major retailers.
