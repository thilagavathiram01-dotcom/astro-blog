Google rolled out 66 new regional voices for the Gemini app on Android and iOS on November 16, 2026. These options cover U.S. English, U.K. English, Indian English, Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian, and Korean. Each voice matches local cadence, pitch, and rhythm so conversations feel more familiar.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="255" />

The voices apply across Gemini Live sessions and when you tap Listen on a chat response. They focus on natural everyday speech rather than exaggerated accents. For users in the UK, this includes more than ten options such as Geordie, Scouse, Brummie, Scottish, Welsh, Northern Irish, Modern Received Pronunciation, and Multicultural London English.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="260" />

## Why Regional Voices Matter

Previous Gemini Live voices sometimes shifted toward a generic American or Australian tone. The new set aims to fix that by matching the rhythms people actually hear in daily life. You can preview each one before committing. The choice sticks for the whole app until you change it again.

This update arrives as Gemini expands to more surfaces, including Apple CarPlay. Pairing a local voice with hands-free chats makes the assistant less distracting during a drive.

## How to Preview and Select a Voice

You need the latest Gemini app from the Play Store or App Store. The new voices appear gradually, so check back if the list looks the same as before.

**On Android or iOS during a Live session:**

1. Open the Gemini app and start a Gemini Live conversation.
2. Tap the three-dot menu in the Live interface.
3. Browse the voice options and preview them by tapping play on each.
4. Select the one that matches your preference. It applies immediately.

**From app settings:**

1. Open Gemini.
2. Tap your profile picture or the settings gear.
3. Look for Gemini’s voice or Voice settings.
4. Preview and choose. Confirm if the app asks.

Google notes that the picker does not always label voices by city or region. Listen to a few samples that mention local food, sports, or culture to hear the difference clearly. Gemini will not name the accent if you ask it directly.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="255" />

## Tips for Getting the Most From Regional Voices

Start with a short Live session so you can interrupt and test how well the voice handles quick topic changes. The same voice works when Gemini reads long chat replies aloud, which is useful for accessibility or multitasking.

If you use Gemini for language practice, a regional voice can make the listening side more realistic. Pair it with skills that include reference files for consistent output. See our guide on [adding files and sharing Gemini Skills](/blog/how-to-add-files-and-share-gemini-skills/).

Keep the app updated. Google continues to roll out the voices over the coming weeks, so new options may appear after the initial launch.

## What the Voices Do Not Change

The selection only affects spoken output. Text responses stay the same. Gemini still follows the same safety and privacy rules regardless of voice. If you manage chat history and AI training separately, those settings remain independent of the voice choice. Check our recent post on [controlling Gemini history and AI training settings](/blog/control-gemini-history-and-ai-training-settings/).

Some features such as Daily Brief may have age or regional limits, but voice selection itself is available to all Gemini users once the rollout reaches your account.

## Conclusion

The 66 regional voices make Gemini Live and spoken replies sound closer to everyday speech in your area. Preview them in a Live session or through settings, pick one that fits, and it applies app-wide. Update the Gemini app, try a few samples, and keep the conversation going while you focus on the road or other tasks.
