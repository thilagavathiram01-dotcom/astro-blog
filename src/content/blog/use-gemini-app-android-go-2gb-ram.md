Google expanded the Gemini app to eligible Android (Go edition) phones on 18 November 2026. These devices, used by hundreds of millions for daily internet access, education, and business, previously offered only basic voice commands. The re-architected app now delivers the core Gemini experience on hardware with less memory.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="50" />

This guide shows how to check eligibility, install the app, start conversations, and use key features while noting the limits.

![Person holding a black Android smartphone](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1587573088942-300aeee98aa8?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Check Device Eligibility

Your Android (Go edition) phone needs at least 2 GB of RAM. Sign in with a personal Google Account, or a work or school account that has Gemini Apps access. Availability follows the countries and languages where Gemini already works.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="110" />

Family Link managers can disable access for users under 13, or the applicable age in their country. On Android Go, Gemini is available as part of the Google Search app and also as a standalone download from the Play Store. Some newer devices from partners such as Transsion may ship with it preloaded.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="50" />

## Install or Update the App

Open the Google Play Store on your phone. Search for “Google” or “Google Search.” If an Update button appears, tap it. If the button says Open, you already have the latest version. You can also search for the Gemini app and install it directly.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="110" />

After installation, you can start a chat by touching and holding the Home button. On supported devices, press and hold the Power button. “Hey Google” voice activation and “Ask about this screen” are not supported on Android (Go edition) devices.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="110" />

![Hand holding smartphone showing app icons](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1606191104230-eb69171eaf15?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What You Can Do with Gemini

The app supports practical tasks without requiring high-end hardware.

- **Stay connected**: Ask Gemini to call or send text messages to contacts.
- **Camera help**: Point the camera at notes, forms, or signs to translate text or receive step-by-step guidance.
- **Gemini Live**: Hold free-flowing spoken conversations in more than 45 languages. Use it to brainstorm ideas or practice for an interview out loud.
- **Image creation**: Generate custom images with Nano Banana, such as a flyer for a weekend bakery sale.
- **Everyday assistance**: Set alarms, create calendar events, find local information (for example, drive time to work or a restaurant with an EV charger), upload files for extra context, and play music by mood or activity.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="110" />

These features run on the free Gemini plan by default. Paid Google AI plans add higher limits and access to more models on eligible accounts.

## Practical Tips for Smooth Use

Close background apps before starting a long Live session or camera task so the phone has free memory. Keep storage space available because the app is designed to be economical even on devices with limited storage. For multi-step work such as comparing products or handling routines, check our guide on [using Gemini Tasks to automate daily chores](/blog/gemini-tasks-free-guide-automate-daily-chores/). Tasks is free in several countries, including India, and runs on the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="47" />

If you rely on hands-free activation, note that you must use the Home or Power button instead of “Hey Google.” Test camera translation on a printed form or notes to confirm it works well on your specific device.

## Limitations to Know

“Hey Google” and “Ask about this screen” are unavailable. The app replaces the previous Assistant for Android (Go edition) and focuses on the core conversational experience. Performance depends on the individual device; Google re-architected the app for lower memory but does not publish device-specific benchmarks. Availability can vary by account type, region, and language.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="50" />

## Conclusion

The Gemini app now puts Google’s personal AI assistant on eligible Android Go phones with 2 GB of RAM or more. Download or update it from the Play Store, start conversations with the Home or Power button, and use Live, camera help, and image creation for everyday tasks. Check your device’s RAM and account access first, then explore the features that fit your needs.
