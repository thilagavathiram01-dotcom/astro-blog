Google expanded the Gemini app to eligible Android (Go edition) phones with as little as 2GB of RAM on November 18, 2026. Hundreds of millions of people who previously had only basic voice commands can now run the core Gemini experience.

This guide shows exactly who qualifies, how to get started, what works, and what does not.

## What Changed for Android Go Devices

Android Go is the lightweight version of Android designed for entry-level hardware. Until mid-November 2026 these devices offered only limited Assistant features. Google re-architected the Gemini app so the core conversational experience runs smoothly on lower-memory hardware.

The update replaces Assistant for Android (Go edition). It arrives either as part of the Google Search app or as a standalone Gemini app downloadable from the Play Store. Some newer devices from partners such as Transsion ship with it preloaded.

## Eligibility Requirements

Confirm these points before you start:

- Your device must run Android (Go edition) and have 2GB of RAM or more.
- You need a personal Google Account, or a work or school account that includes Gemini Apps access.
- Gemini must be available in your country and language.
- Family Link managers can restrict access for users under the applicable age.

On Android Go the experience lives inside the Google Search app by default. Regular Android phones already supported the full Gemini app at the 2GB RAM threshold; this change specifically targets Go edition hardware.

## How to Install and Start Gemini

Follow these steps on your eligible Android Go phone:

1. Open the Google Play Store.
2. Search for “Google” or “Google Search”.
3. Tap Update if the button is available. If it says Open, you already have the latest version.
4. Alternatively, search for and install the Gemini app directly from Play Store.
5. Sign in with your eligible Google Account.

To open a conversation:

- Touch and hold the Home button, or
- Press and hold the Power button on supported devices.

“Hey Google” voice activation and “Ask about this screen” are not supported on Android Go edition devices. Use the button methods instead.

## Key Features Available on Lower-Memory Phones

Google lists these capabilities that work on eligible 2GB Android Go devices:

- **Gemini Live**: Hold natural voice conversations in more than 45 languages. Practice interviews or brainstorm out loud.
- **Camera help**: Point the camera at notes, forms, or signs for translation or step-by-step guidance.
- **Image creation**: Generate images with Nano Banana (example: a weekend bakery sale flyer).
- **Calls and messages**: Ask Gemini to call or text contacts.
- **Alarms and calendar**: Set alarms or create calendar events.
- **Music**: Play songs by mood or activity.
- **File uploads**: Attach docs, photos, and other files for more context.

These features run by sending requests to Google’s cloud; the phone itself does not need to store the full model.

## Limitations to Know

Keep these constraints in mind:

- No “Hey Google” wake word.
- No “Ask about this screen” capability.
- Performance depends on available free RAM, storage, and network quality.
- Availability still rolls out gradually; not every eligible device receives it on the same day.

If the app feels slow, free storage, clear the Google app cache, and close background apps before retrying.

## Tips for Smooth Performance

- Keep the Google Search or Gemini app updated.
- Free at least 500MB of storage.
- Use Wi-Fi when possible for camera and image features.
- Restart the phone after the first install if the long-press shortcuts do not appear.
- For deeper Android customization ideas, see our guide on [Circle to Search on Android](/blog/circle-to-search-android/).

## Conclusion

The November 2026 expansion puts practical Gemini features on the budget phones that serve as the primary internet device for hundreds of millions of people. If your Android Go phone has 2GB of RAM and an eligible Google Account, update the Google app or install Gemini from the Play Store, then long-press Home or Power to start.

You now have voice conversations, camera translation, image creation, and everyday actions without needing a higher-end handset.
