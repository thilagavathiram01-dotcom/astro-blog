Android 17 makes multitasking easier with App Bubbles. You can now launch any app as a floating bubble straight from the Recents screen. This works on Pixel devices running the latest Android 17 builds, including QPR3 Beta 3 and later.

The feature lets you keep apps open in compact windows while you use your main screen. You stay in control and switch between them without leaving your current task.

## What Are App Bubbles?

App Bubbles turn any app into a small floating window. You can resize, move, or minimize it to a bubble on the edge of your screen. Up to five bubbles can stay active at once.

Previously you started bubbles by long-pressing an app icon on the home screen or in the app drawer. The new Recents option adds another fast path.

![Person holding a smartphone showing multiple app screens](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1781606424389-df9697a46b1c?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## How to Launch a Bubble from Recents

Follow these steps on a device running Android 17:

1. Open the app you want to bubble so it appears in your recent apps list.
2. Swipe up from the bottom of the screen and hold briefly to open the Recents overview.
3. Find the app card you want.
4. Tap the app name or the three-dot menu at the top of the card.
5. Select **Bubble** from the menu that appears.

The app opens as a floating window right away. You can drag the bubble to any edge and tap it to expand or collapse the window.

You can repeat the process for other apps. The system groups them into a bubble stack when you add more than one.

## Tips for Better Use

- Open the app first so it shows in Recents. You cannot bubble an app that is not already running.
- Close a bubble by dragging it to the bottom of the screen or tapping the close button inside the expanded window.
- On larger screens the bubble bar appears at the bottom for quicker access.
- Bubbles work alongside split-screen and picture-in-picture for flexible workflows.

If you do not see the Bubble option, make sure your device is on the latest Android 17 update. The Recents shortcut arrived in QPR3 Beta 3 and is rolling out more widely.
