Google expanded Tasks in Gemini on November 17, 2026. The feature lets you hand off multi-step jobs so the assistant works in the background while you focus on other things. It rolls out free for users 18 and older in the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India, powered by the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model.

This replaces the earlier paid Gemini Spark experience for many people. You switch from Chat mode to Task mode, describe what you need, and Gemini plans and executes the steps under your direction.

## What Tasks in Gemini Can Do

Tasks handle longer projects that go beyond a quick answer. Google notes that Gemini can make calls, pay bills, and finish tasks using connected apps and services.

Examples from the official announcement include booking service appointments by calling dealerships, analyzing utility bills to find savings, shopping for products with Google Pay, adding items to grocery orders via connected apps like Instacart, and turning school emails into calendar events. Advanced options such as business calling, purchases, password manager access, email forwarding, and texting start in the U.S.

Gemini discloses that it is an AI when it calls a business. Businesses can opt out. It does not share payment or private account details unless you approve. You set a spending cap for purchases, and receipts go to Google Wallet. The system uses isolated virtual machines on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine for security.

## How to Start a Task

Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS, or visit gemini.google.com. Sign in with a personal Google Account. Work or school accounts are not supported. Make sure Gemini Activity History is on.

Flip the toggle above the input box from Chat to Task. Describe the job in plain language. Include details such as your budget, preferred time, or constraints. Gemini starts working and keeps going after you leave the conversation. You can run up to 15 tasks at once and stop any with the Stop button.

For U.S. users, additional ways exist. Forward emails to gemini@google.com. Or set up texting:

1. Open Gemini and tap Settings.
2. Tap Channels.
3. Tap Set up under Text with Gemini.
4. Verify your phone number. You need a U.S. number with an eligible carrier.

Gemini pauses for confirmation before critical actions like completing a purchase. You can set “Always allow” for routine steps.

## Using Connected Apps and Payments

Tasks connect with more than 60 popular apps. You can ask Gemini to add items to an Instacart order by text or photo, then hand off for checkout. Shopping uses Google’s Shopping Graph and the Agent Payments Protocol with support from Mastercard, Visa, and American Express coming soon. You earn card rewards and can set a hard spending limit.

Personal Intelligence helps when enabled. Gemini can use your Calendar, Maps, and other Google data with permission to find free times or nearby options.

For developers building similar experiences, check the [Gemini 4 Hydrogen guide for Android](/blog/add-gemini-4-hydrogen-android-firebase-ai-logic/).

## Tips for Better Results

Start with clear, specific prompts. Mention deadlines, budgets, and any actions to avoid. Review the plan if Gemini shows steps before acting.

Monitor progress and jump in with more instructions if needed. Provide feedback with a thumbs down when a task misses the mark so the system improves.

Check availability. Calling is not available in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska. Features expand over time.

Keep permissions minimal. Disconnect apps or delete history in settings when finished.

## Security and Limits

Google runs tasks in sandboxed virtual machines dedicated to your account. Prompt-injection filters and Safe Browsing limit links. You control approvals for sensitive steps.

Tasks may not complete every request perfectly. Websites continue adapting to agent use. Gemini sometimes hands control back for the final click.

The free rollout covers the core Task mode in the six countries. Advanced calling and payment features begin in the U.S.

Tasks in Gemini turn the app from a chat partner into an assistant that finishes multi-step work. Free access in six countries on Gemini 4 Hydrogen makes it available to many more people. Switch to Task mode, describe the job, and let it handle the busywork while you stay in control.
