Pixel Watch owners can now design their own watch faces with text prompts. Google rolled the AI watch face generator from Pixel Watch 5 to Pixel Watch 4 in early October 2026. The feature uses Gemini Intelligence to turn a short description into a custom dial you can set in minutes.

You open the Pixel Watch app, pick Create, type or speak what you want, and review the results. The process works the same on both watches once the update arrives. No extra apps or coding required.

![Smartwatch showing a colorful digital face](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-z-sIt0N_YlY?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What the AI Watch Face Generator Does

The generator creates watch faces from your description instead of limiting you to preset templates. It draws on the same Gemini Intelligence that powers other Pixel features. Core layouts stay consistent: vertical digital, vertical with complication arcs, or horizontal with one complication. You control the digits, colors, and theme.

Reports from users who received the Pixel Watch 4 update (version 4.2.12.989940186) confirm the option appears when adding a new face. Pixel Watch 5 has had the tool since launch. Generation typically takes 10–30 seconds and needs an internet connection.

If the first result misses the mark, a Try again button regenerates from the same prompt. You can also start over with a new description.

## Check for the Update on Pixel Watch 4

Pixel Watch 5 users already have the feature. Pixel Watch 4 owners need the firmware that packages the AI generator plus several Watch 5 faces (Weave, Modular, Reveal, Chronograph, and the Steph Curry dial).

1. Open the Pixel Watch app on your phone.
2. Tap your watch, then System or About.
3. Check the software version. Look for 4.2.12.989940186 or later.
4. If an update is available, download and install it. Keep the watch on the charger and connected to Wi-Fi.

Rollout is gradual. If the Create option is missing after the update, wait a day and restart the watch.

## Create an AI Watch Face Step by Step

Use the Pixel Watch app for the best experience. The watch itself can switch faces, but prompt entry happens on the phone.

1. Open the Google Pixel Watch app.
2. Tap Watch faces, then Add new (or the Create button).
3. Choose Create your own or the AI prompt option.
4. Type a clear description or use voice. Examples: “blue and white marbled pattern with thin digits” or “yellow and purple dotted lava look.”
5. Submit the prompt. Wait for the results (usually under 30 seconds).
6. Swipe through the options. Pick the one you like or tap Try again.
7. Tap Use on watch or Save to add it to your gallery.

On the watch, touch and hold the current face, swipe, and select the new one. You can still edit complications and colors after generation, within the limits of the chosen layout.

## Prompt Tips That Produce Better Faces

Short, specific prompts work best. Focus on color, pattern, and digit style. Avoid overly complex scenes that the system cannot map to a watch layout.

- Name two or three colors and a texture (marble, dots, gradient).
- Specify digit thickness or shape if it matters (“thin white digits,” “bold black numerals”).
- Match the face to a band or outfit (“forest green and gold with leaf motif”).
- Retry the same prompt if the first set feels off; small variations appear.

The tool rejects some prompts that are hard to parse or need a stronger connection. Keep language simple and concrete.

## Limitations to Expect

Layouts are fixed to a few base styles. You cannot freely place every element. Complication support follows the chosen template. Generation quality varies; some faces look polished, others need a second try. The feature requires an active internet connection on the phone during creation.

Google has not published a dedicated support page for the AI generator yet. Behavior comes from hands-on reports on Pixel Watch 5 and the recent Watch 4 rollout. Features can change with future updates.
