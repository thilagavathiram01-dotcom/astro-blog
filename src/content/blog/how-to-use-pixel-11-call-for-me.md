Gemini on Pixel 11 can now place routine business calls for you. The feature is called Call for Me. It dials from your number, introduces itself as a Google AI assistant, and talks through the task while you watch a live transcript.

Google is rolling this out as an early preview. Availability is limited. You need a Pixel 11-series phone, a paid Google AI plan, English as the device language, and the Public Beta of Phone by Google.

This guide walks through official requirements, the exact prompt flow, how to take over a live call, and what Gemini is not allowed to do.

## What Call for Me actually does

Call for Me is not the same as Hold for Me or Direct My Call. Those tools still help after you start a call yourself. Call for Me starts the call from the Gemini app and stays on the line.

Google’s help page lists three job types Gemini can handle:

- Get business hours, product availability, service quotes, and other information.
- Book, confirm, and manage appointments and reservations.
- Navigate phone menus and wait on hold.

Examples from reporting and Google’s own copy include reserving patio seating, asking a hardware store if a part is in stock, moving a haircut, or adding an item to an existing order.

Gemini uses your device’s mobile network and your phone number. Standard carrier rates apply. It starts every call by saying it is an AI assistant from Google calling on a recorded line on your behalf, and it states your name.

## Check that you qualify

Google lists a hard checklist. If any item is missing, the Call for me button will not appear.

You must:

- Be 18 or over and in the United States.
- Use a Pixel 11 phone with a US SIM card. Coverage in early reports also includes Pixel 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL, and 11 Pro Fold.
- Sign in to the Gemini mobile app.
- Have a Google AI subscription.
- Install the Public Beta of Phone by Google from the Play testing page.
- Update the Gemini app from Play Store.
- Set the device language to English.

Google notes that the feature is rolling out gradually. Eligible users may still wait a day or two after meeting every requirement.

For now, Gemini can only call US phone numbers.

## Join the Phone by Google Public Beta

Call for Me depends on the beta dialer, not the stable Phone app alone.

1. Open Google Play and search for Phone by Google, or go to the testing link Google publishes in the Gemini help article.
2. Join the Public Beta and wait for Play to deliver the update.
3. Open Phone by Google and confirm it is your default phone app.
4. Update Gemini from Play Store and force-stop both apps once if the feature does not appear.

Keep English as the system language while you test. Mixed language settings are a common reason the option stays hidden.

## Ask Gemini to place the call

Google’s official steps are short. The quality of the prompt matters more than the tap count.

1. Open the Gemini mobile app on your Pixel.
2. Ask Gemini to make a call. You can also type `@Call for me` in the prompt.
3. Be specific. Include the business name, date, time, party size, product name, and a backup option.
4. Review Gemini’s summary. Check the number it will dial and the details it will share, such as your name and contact info.
5. Ask Gemini to edit the task if anything is wrong.
6. Tap **Call for me** to start.

Good prompt examples from Google:

- Call [Restaurant Name] and book a table for two at 7 PM tomorrow.
- Ask the dry cleaner on Main Street how much they charge to hem pants.

Write the fallback in the same prompt. If Friday at 7 is full, tell Gemini to try 7:30 or Saturday. That saves a second call after the first one ends.

## Watch the call and take over

Your microphone stays muted by default. You can still follow the conversation.

- Cancel before the call connects with **Cancel**.
- Follow the live transcript in Gemini, or listen to live audio.
- Tap **Audio** to turn call audio on or off.
- Tap **Take over** when you want to speak. Gemini announces that you are taking over, then leaves the call.

After the call ends, you get a notification. Tap **View results** to read Gemini’s outcome summary in the app. The Phone app also keeps the full transcript and the recorded call in your history.

Daily limits apply. If you hit the cap, wait until the next day before asking Gemini to dial again.

## What Gemini will not do on a call

Google blocks several call types. Do not try to work around them.

- Emergency numbers such as 911.
- Payments, financial transactions, or sharing credit card numbers.
- Sensitive personal data such as Social Security numbers, passwords, or health information.
- Telemarketing or selling products and services.

Businesses can also refuse automated calls. Recipients who do not want Gemini-originated calls can opt out at g.co/gemini/opt-out and opt back in later on the same page.

By using the feature you accept Google’s Terms of Service, the Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy, and the Gemini Apps Agentic Calling Additional Terms.

## Practical tips that keep calls short

Give Gemini one job per call. Mixing a reservation and a product question on the same line raises the chance the summary is wrong.

Confirm the phone number in the pre-call card. Gemini may pick a second location of the same chain.

Stay nearby for the first minute. Menus and hold music are the easy part. The human pickup is when you may need to take over.

Review the recording in Phone by Google before you treat the summary as final. Names, times, and confirmation codes are the details most worth checking.

Send thumbs up or thumbs down on the Gemini summary. The Phone app also shows periodic surveys. That feedback is how Google tunes an early preview.
