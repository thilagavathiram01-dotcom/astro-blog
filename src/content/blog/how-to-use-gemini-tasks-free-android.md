Google made Tasks in the Gemini app free on November 17, 2026, for users in the United States, India, Japan, Korea, Canada, and Australia. The feature, previously called Spark and limited to Google AI Pro subscribers, now runs on the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model for free accounts in those countries.

Tasks let you hand off multi-step jobs. Gemini works in the background, connects to apps like Gmail and Calendar, and reports results when done. You stay in control and approve critical actions.

This guide shows how to start using it on Android if you meet the requirements.

## Requirements for free Tasks

You need a personal Google Account. Work and school accounts do not support Tasks yet. You must be 18 or older. Gemini Apps Activity (History) must be turned on. The feature appears in the Gemini mobile app, the Mac app, and at gemini.google.com.

Outside the six countries, Tasks still require a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription, and the feature is unavailable in the European Economic Area, Nigeria, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom.

Free access uses the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model. Paid subscribers can access newer models for Tasks. Usage counts against compute-based limits that are lower on the free tier. You can run up to 15 tasks at once.

## How to start a Task on Android

Open the Gemini app on your Android phone. Sign in with your personal Google Account if needed. On the home screen, look for the Chat and Task toggle near the prompt box. On mobile it sits above the text field.

Tap Task. The prompt changes to ask what you want done. Type a clear description of the job. Include any deadlines, budgets, or specific instructions. For example: "Check my unread emails from today and summarize the ones that need a reply by 5 PM."

Submit the request. Gemini creates a task thread and begins working. You can leave the app. It runs in a hardware-isolated virtual machine dedicated to your account.

Check progress in the sidebar under All or by opening the task thread. Status icons show whether a task is in progress, needs attention, or is ready. You can add more instructions while it runs.

## Advanced options starting in the US

US users get extra channels. Text Gemini after a one-time setup: open Settings, tap Channels, then Set up under Text with Gemini, and verify your phone number. You can also forward emails to gemini@google.com to start a task.

Gemini can call businesses on your behalf in most US states after you approve the plan. It identifies itself as an AI and does not share payment details unless you allow it. Calling is not available in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska.

Google Pay checkout is rolling out for eligible US users 18 and older. Set a spending cap. Gemini asks for confirmation before completing a purchase. Receipts appear in Google Wallet.

These advanced features start in the US and expand later. Free Tasks in other countries cover research, scheduling, and app-connected work without the calling or shopping pieces.

## Practical examples

Ask Gemini to keep a family calendar updated. Describe the sources (school emails, shared calendars) and the schedule. It can run recurring tasks.

Request a comparison of products with a budget limit. Gemini uses shopping data and asks clarifying questions before suggesting options.

Forward a batch of emails and ask it to extract dates and add them to Calendar with invitees.

For bill analysis, grant temporary access to a utility portal via Google Password Manager. Gemini reviews past statements and highlights patterns.

Always review the plan Gemini shows and approve key steps. Do not enter passwords or payment details directly in the task thread.

## Tips and limits

Write specific prompts. Mention connected apps you want it to use. Type @ to select a skill if you have created one.

Set spending caps for any shopping tasks. Use Always allow only for low-risk routine actions.

Monitor compute usage. Free limits are lower than paid plans. Delete remote browser data in Settings if you want to clear saved site sign-ins.

If a task fails or produces incorrect results, give thumbs-down feedback. You can stop, edit, or delete tasks from the sidebar.

Tasks require History on, so chats are saved. You can still control training separately in Gemini settings.

## Conclusion

Gemini Tasks is now free for eligible users in six countries and runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen. On Android, switch the toggle from Chat to Task, describe the job, and let it work in the background under your direction. Advanced calling, texting, email, and checkout features start in the US.

Confirm you meet the age, account, and History requirements. Start with a simple recurring task such as a daily email and calendar summary, then expand as you learn the limits.

Check the official Google announcement and Gemini Apps Help pages for the latest availability and settings.
