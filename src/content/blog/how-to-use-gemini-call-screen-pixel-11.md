Pixel 11 owners in the US now get a more natural Gemini-powered Call Screen. When an unknown number rings, Gemini asks why the person is calling in a conversational style, shows a live transcript, and lets you jump in or hang up without answering first.

This upgrade arrived with the November 2026 Pixel Drop. It builds on the existing Call Screen tool that already filters spam and first-time callers. The change is limited to Pixel 11 series devices with a US SIM and English language.

## What the upgraded Call Screen does

Call Screen still runs on-device and does not use Wi-Fi or mobile data for the screening itself. Contacts are skipped. Spam and suspicious numbers can be handled automatically according to the protection level you choose.

On Pixel 11 the Gemini layer makes the exchange feel less robotic. Gemini handles the back-and-forth until you decide to pick up or end the call. A live transcript stays visible so you can follow every reply in real time.

Google’s Pixel Phone Help page confirms the Pixel 11-specific reply options appear only with a US SIM and English set as the device language. Older Pixels keep the previous set of suggested replies.

## Requirements and availability

You need:

- A Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, or Pixel 11 Pro Fold
- A US SIM card
- Device language set to English
- The latest Phone by Google app from Play Store

Automatic screening is available in Australia, Canada, Ireland, the UK, and the US. Manual screening is available in more countries, including India on select newer Pixel models. The conversational Gemini upgrade itself is listed for the US on Pixel 11 series devices.

Call Screen does not work with third-party call-recording or screen-recording apps. Turn those off before you rely on it. It also skips automatic screening while you are roaming or, on some models, when headphones or Bluetooth are connected.

## Turn on automatic screening

Open the Phone app and follow these steps:

1. Tap More, then Settings.
2. Tap Spam and Call Screen or Call Screen.
3. Tap Call Screen.
4. Choose Maximum protection, Medium protection, or Basic protection.

On Pixel 7 and newer devices you also see a Protection level section. Turn on Automatically screen calls and pick the categories: Spam, Possibly faked numbers, and First-time callers.

Maximum protection intercepts more unknown numbers before the phone even rings. Medium focuses on suspicious calls. Basic mainly declines known spam. Test the setting with a friend so you know what level matches your tolerance for interruptions.

Update the Phone app first if the Call Screen menu is missing. Google’s support page says the absence of the menu usually means the device or region does not support the feature.

## Screen a call manually

Automatic screening is optional. You can still screen any incoming call on demand:

1. When the phone rings, tap Screen call.
2. Call Assist answers and asks who is calling and why.
3. Read the live transcript that appears on screen.
4. Choose a reply, answer the call, or hang up.

On Pixel 11 with a US SIM and English, the available actions include Report as spam (“Please remove this number from your mailing and contact lists. Thanks, and goodbye.”) and I’ll call you back. Pixel 10 and earlier (and Pixel 11 outside the US or not set to English) still show the older set that includes “Is it urgent?” and “I can’t understand.”

You remain in control. Tap Answer at any moment to take the line. Screening and the transcript stop as soon as you pick up.

## View transcripts and recordings

Every screened call saves a transcript on the device. To keep the audio as well:

1. In Call Screen settings, turn on Save Call Screen audio.
2. After the call, open the Phone app and tap the screened entry.
3. Choose Transcript or Transcript and audio.

You can delete any transcript and recording by long-pressing the entry and selecting Delete. Google states that screened-call information is not saved to your Google Account, Call Assist Activity, or Web & App Activity.

## Tips for reliable results

Save important numbers as contacts so they are never screened. Call Screen checks the contacts stored on the phone.

If you use call forwarding, turn automatic screening off. Screened calls are not forwarded.

Media playback may pause while a call is being screened. Expect that interruption on videos or music.

Transcripts are useful but not perfect. Google notes that Call Screen will not always fully understand or transcribe every word.

For outgoing business calls that you want Gemini to place and handle entirely, see the separate [Pixel 11 Call for Me guide](/blog/pixel-11-call-for-me-gemini/). Call Screen is for incoming unknown callers; Call for Me is the outgoing preview that dials from your number.

## Troubleshooting common issues

No Call Screen menu: Update Phone by Google and confirm your region and device model. If the menu is still missing, the feature is not supported on that phone.

Screen button never appears: Check that third-party recording apps are disabled and that the Phone app is current.

No live transcript: Tap the screening notification. On Pixel 11 the conversational mode and transcript require the US SIM + English combination.

Automatic screening stopped working: Confirm you are not roaming and that Bluetooth or headphones are not forcing a skip on your model.

## Conclusion

The November 2026 Call Screen upgrade makes unknown-caller screening more conversational on Pixel 11. Set your protection level, keep the Phone app updated, and use the live transcript to decide whether to answer. The feature stays on-device, skips your contacts, and gives you an immediate way to take over or hang up.

Pair it with careful contact management and the protection level that matches how many unknown calls you actually receive. When you need Gemini to place a call instead of screening one, the Call for Me preview is the related tool.
