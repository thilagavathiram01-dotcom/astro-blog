Google Wallet now supports Traveler QR payments for travelers heading to Singapore. The feature rolled out with Google Play services version 26.44 in early November 2026. It lets you pay at participating Alipay+ merchants using a Visa card already saved in Wallet, without downloading extra apps or opening local bank accounts.

This guide walks through requirements, automatic activation, manual setup, and the two payment methods. Everything comes from Google’s official support page and system release notes.

## What Traveler QR Does

Traveler QR connects Google Wallet to the Alipay+ network. You scan a merchant’s QR code or show your own code for the cashier to scan. The transaction uses your tokenized Visa card. Google does not process or hold the funds. Exchange rates and fees come from Alipay+ partners and your card issuer.

The feature currently works only at Alipay+ partner networks in Singapore. It is available to Google Wallet users traveling from countries where tap-to-pay is supported, including much of APAC, Australia, Canada, Europe, the UK, and the US.

## Requirements Before You Travel

You need an Android device with Google Wallet installed and updated. Add an active, tokenized Visa credit or debit card that supports contactless payments. Confirm your bank allows international transactions on that card. Enable location and camera permissions for the Wallet app.

If your default payment method is a domestic-only card or an unsupported network, Wallet will prompt you to add a supported Visa card.

Check that Google Play services is up to date. The Traveler QR option arrives through the v26.44 update. Open Settings > Google > All services or search for Google Play services and update if available.

## Enable Traveler QR

The feature activates automatically when your phone detects you are in a supported destination. Location permission must be on.

If it does not appear after you arrive in Singapore:

1. Open the Google Wallet app.
2. Tap your profile photo in the top corner.
3. Select Overseas QR payments.
4. Tap Turn on locations.
5. On the system prompt, choose Approximate and While using the app.

Once enabled, the option stays available for the trip.

## How to Pay at a Store

Tell the cashier you are paying with Alipay+. You have two options.

### Scan the Merchant’s QR Code

1. Open Google Wallet.
2. Tap Scan to pay.
3. Point the camera at the store’s QR code.
4. Enter the purchase amount in local currency.
5. Review the exchange rate and total in your home currency.
6. Authorize the payment.

### Show Your Own QR Code

1. Open Google Wallet.
2. Select Show Code.
3. Choose your payment card.
4. Present the QR code for the merchant to scan.
5. Review the details and tap Continue.

In both cases, review the total before confirming. Google Wallet does not charge its own fees, but your card issuer may apply foreign transaction fees.

## Check Your Transactions

After a payment, open Google Wallet, select the card you used, and scroll to recent activity. Tap the overseas transaction to see amount, merchant name, card used, any Alipay+ discounts, order details, and transaction ID.

## Tips and Troubleshooting

Grant camera permission if the scanner screen stays black. Go to device settings for Google Wallet and set camera access to Allow while using the app.

If the scanner reports an unsupported QR code, make sure the code shows an accepted Alipay+ partner logo. Personal transfer codes will not work.

A pending charge after a failed payment is usually a temporary pre-authorization hold from your bank. Contact the card issuer for the release timeline. Google Wallet cannot reverse transactions because it does not hold the funds.

For refunds, show the in-app receipt to the merchant. Returns follow the store’s policy. Once processed, check with your bank for the refund timing.

Keep location services accurate so the feature can auto-enable. Approximate location is sufficient.

## Security Notes

Google Wallet does not share your actual card number with Alipay+ or the merchant. Payments use tokenized credentials protected by Google Pay. This matches the security model used for regular tap-to-pay.

For broader Android payment protections, see our guide on [Android scam protections](/blog/android-new-scam-protections-setup-guide/).

## Conclusion

Traveler QR removes the need for a separate local wallet app when you visit Singapore. Update Play services, confirm your Visa card and permissions, and the option should activate on arrival. Scan or show a code at Alipay+ partners and review the rate before you pay.

The feature is rolling out now. Coverage may expand later, but Singapore is the only confirmed market today. Check the official Google Wallet support page for the latest supported destinations.
