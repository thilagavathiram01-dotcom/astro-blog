Google Play Fest runs from October 13 to 19, 2026. Play Points members can turn existing points into sweepstakes entries, compete in game leagues for up to 50,000 points, and stack purchase boosters on apps, games, and books.

The event is not available in every market. Open the Play Store on your phone and look for the Play Fest banner before you spend points or make a purchase.

## Confirm you are a Play Points member

Play Fest rewards sit on top of Google Play Points. If you have never joined, open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, and select Play Points. Sign up is free. Complete the starter quest to earn an immediate points bonus.

Check your current balance in the same screen. Points you already hold can be redeemed for sweepstakes entries starting today. Higher Play Points levels unlock better prize tiers later in the event.

## Enter the Play Fest sweepstakes

Visit the Play Fest hub at play.google.com/play-fest-2026 from your Android phone or tablet. Exchange points for entries. Larger bundles give bonus entries. You can enter as many times as your balance allows.

Prizes include a 1:1 scale 2026 Oscar Piastri racing helmet made by Bell, a Razer Boomslang 20th Anniversary Edition mouse, XREAL AURA glasses, a Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, and a 55-inch Samsung S90H OLED TV. Thousands of smaller prizes are also available.

The sweepstakes is open to select U.S. Play Points members aged 18 and older. No purchase is necessary. Official rules list a free entry method and a November 2, 2026 end date. Read the rules linked from the Google announcement before you redeem points.

## Pre-register and play the game leagues

Three leagues run during the week: Angry Birds 2, Free Fire, and 8 Ball Pool. Pre-registration is already open. Lock in your spot now so you can start matches on October 13.

Each league offers a chance to win up to 50,000 Play Points. Scoring rules and match requirements appear on the individual league pages. Play only the matches the league page lists. Extra matches outside the rules do not count.

Check eligibility for your country and account age on the league page before you register. Not every region can join every league.

## Stack the daily deals and points multipliers

Offers end October 19 or when supplies run out. Expect discounts on selected apps, games, books, and digital gift cards. Partners such as Venmo may add checkout bonuses. In-game rewards appear in MONOPOLY GO! and Pokémon GO. Limited savings apply to Crunchyroll and Calm annual subscriptions.

Every purchase earns a daily points booster. Play Pass subscribers receive double points and 6x boosters, with some benefits starting as early as October 8. Book purchases carry a 15x points multiplier from October 16 through October 18. Top-tier books and audiobooks are also priced under $6 during the week.

Buy only titles you already planned to purchase. The booster multiplies points on the transaction; it does not create free value if you would not have spent the money.

## Practical tips for the week

Open the Play Store each morning and check the Play Fest section. New limited-time gift-card deals can disappear quickly. Screenshot the offer details before you confirm a purchase so you have a record if a discount fails to apply.

If you play in a league, finish the required matches early in the day. Leaderboards update throughout the event. A late start leaves less time to climb.

Keep your Google account signed in on the device you use for both points redemptions and league play. Switching accounts mid-event can split your balance and registration.

For broader Android privacy settings that stay useful after the event ends, see our guide on [how to enable Private DNS on Android](/blog/android-private-dns-setup-guide/).

## Conclusion

Play Fest concentrates points redemptions, competitive play, and purchase boosters into one week. Join Play Points if you have not already, pre-register for any league available in your market, and review the sweepstakes rules before you exchange points. Check the Play Store daily for deals that match titles you already intend to buy.
