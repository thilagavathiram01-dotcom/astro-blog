Google AI Ultra subscribers just received a meaningful upgrade. The top-tier AI plan now includes the full YouTube Premium Family membership instead of the individual plan. You can share ad-free YouTube and YouTube Music with up to five other people in your household at no extra cost.

Shimrit Ben-Yair, who leads Google Photos, Google One, and AI subscriptions, announced the change on November 18, 2026. She noted it was one of the features subscribers had requested most often. Google confirmed the details the same day on the Google One Community forum. The upgrade applies automatically to both the AI Ultra 5x plan at $99.99 per month and the AI Ultra 20x plan at $199.99 per month in eligible countries.

## What Changed with Google AI Ultra

Previously, Google AI Ultra bundled the individual YouTube Premium plan. That covered only the primary subscriber. The Family plan replaces it. Household members living at the same address can now use their own Google accounts for ad-free playback, offline downloads, and background play on both YouTube and YouTube Music.

The change does not affect Google AI Pro. That plan continues to include YouTube Premium Lite, which omits YouTube Music benefits. AI Ultra remains the only tier that unlocks the full Family experience.

Eligible countries number more than 100, matching the markets where YouTube Premium Family is already offered. Check Google’s support pages for the current list if you are unsure about your region.

## Savings and Value

Standalone YouTube Premium Family costs $26.99 per month in the United States after recent price increases. The individual plan costs $15.99. Ultra subscribers who previously paid for a separate Family plan can save roughly $324 per year. Even if you only used the individual plan before, the upgrade adds about $11 in monthly list value without raising the Ultra price.

Other AI Ultra benefits stay the same. These include higher Gemini usage limits (5x or 20x those of AI Pro), extended access to models such as Gemini 4 Neon and Gemini 4 Carbon, 20 TB or 30 TB of Google One storage depending on the tier, Google Home Premium Advanced, and Google Health Premium.

## How to Verify the Upgrade

Existing subscribers receive the Family plan automatically. No manual plan switch is required.

1. Open the YouTube app or visit youtube.com on a computer.
2. Sign in with the Google account that holds the AI Ultra subscription.
3. Go to Purchases and memberships (or Settings > Purchases and memberships on mobile).
4. Look under YouTube Premium. It should now show the Family plan rather than Individual.

You may also receive an email from Google confirming the change. If the status still shows Individual after a day or two, contact Google One support through the one.google.com account page.

## How to Share with Your Household

Sharing works through your existing Google family group. The AI Ultra subscriber must be the family group manager. If someone else currently manages the group, that person cannot share this specific benefit. In that case, create a new family group under the Ultra account.

![YouTube app displayed on a smartphone screen](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1541877944-ac82a091518a?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

### Steps to invite family members

1. Make sure you are signed in as the family manager on the AI Ultra account.
2. Visit g.co/yourfamily or open the Google Account page, then select People & sharing > Family group.
3. If no group exists, create one. You become the manager.
4. Invite up to five other members by email or phone number. They must live at the same residential address and be at least 13 years old (or the local equivalent).
5. Members accept the invitation from their own Google accounts.
6. Return to YouTube > Purchases and memberships > Manage membership > Edit next to Family sharing settings to confirm everyone appears.

Each member keeps a separate watch history, recommendations, and subscriptions. Only the membership benefits are shared. Google notes that you can switch family plans only once every 12 months, so plan membership changes carefully.

## Tips for Smooth Sharing

Confirm every member uses a personal Google account, not a Google Workspace account. Workspace accounts cannot join family plans.

If a household member already has their own YouTube Premium subscription, they may need to cancel it after joining to avoid double billing. Google has not published detailed conflict rules for the Ultra upgrade, so check membership status after acceptance.

Family members can manage their own offline downloads and playlists. Background playback works on mobile apps as usual.

Keep the family group location set to your actual household address. YouTube enforces the same-address rule and may remove members who appear to live elsewhere.

## Conclusion

The YouTube Premium Family upgrade removes a long-standing limitation of Google AI Ultra. Households that already pay for the top AI tier can now cover ad-free video and music for up to six people without an additional subscription. Verify the automatic upgrade in your YouTube account, confirm you are the family manager, and invite eligible members through the Google family group tools. The process takes only a few minutes once the group is ready.
