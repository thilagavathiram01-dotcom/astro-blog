Google added GIF creation to the Gemini app on 20 November 2026. Powered by Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, the feature turns text prompts or your own photos into short looping animations about three seconds long. You can copy them into chats or download the .gif file.

This guide covers eligibility, the exact steps on Android, prompt tips, and practical limits. Details come from Google’s Gemini help documentation and the official announcement.

![Person holding a smartphone showing code next to a laptop](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1551650975-87deedd944c3?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Who can create GIFs

GIF generation requires a Google AI plan on a personal account. You must be 18 or older. The feature is rolling out in Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Japan, Korea, the United Kingdom, and the United States. Availability can vary even inside those countries while the rollout continues.

Open the Gemini app on Android and check for the video or image tools. If the GIF option is missing, force-stop the Google app and reopen it, or wait for the next update.

## Create a GIF step by step

1. Open the Gemini app on your Android phone and sign in with the account that has your Google AI plan.
2. Start a new chat. Tap the plus button or open the image/video tools from the menu.
3. Select Create video or the equivalent tool, then choose GIF in the format picker. You can also type a prompt that begins with “Create a GIF of…”.
4. Describe the scene clearly. Example: “A dog riding a surfboard on a sunny beach, looping.” You can add up to five images or one video as reference.
5. Wait for the generation. Gemini returns a roughly three-second loop.
6. Copy the GIF into a message or email, or download it as a .gif file.

The process uses the same Omni 1.2 Flash model that powers longer video clips in Gemini.

## Tips for better results

- Keep prompts specific about the action and style. “Looping photo effect of a coffee cup steaming on a wooden table” works better than a vague request.
- Use reference images when you want a particular look or character. Omni 1.2 Flash supports them for consistency.
- GIFs are short by design. For longer clips, switch to the video tool and pick a length between 3 and 15 seconds.
- Check the format picker carefully. Selecting GIF instead of video keeps the output as a loop.
- Download the file if you need to share it outside Google apps. Copy-paste works inside many Google surfaces.

## Limits and what to expect

Each GIF lasts about three seconds. Google has not published exact resolution or frame-rate numbers for the GIF output. Generation requires an active Google AI plan and may count against your quota the same way other video features do. The feature is still rolling out, so some eligible users may not see it immediately.

## Conclusion

Gemini’s GIF tool gives Android users a fast way to turn ideas or photos into short looping animations without leaving the app. Confirm your plan and country eligibility, open the video tools, choose GIF, and write a clear prompt. Download or copy the result once it finishes. The same Omni 1.2 Flash model handles both these short loops and longer video work.
