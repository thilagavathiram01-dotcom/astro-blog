Gmail Live lets you ask your inbox a question out loud instead of hunting for the right keyword. Google launched the feature on September 3, 2026, after previewing Gemini Audio models for Workspace at I/O.

You talk. Gmail scans messages and answers in the same conversation. Follow-ups stay in context. You can interrupt mid-sentence if the answer is already enough.

This guide covers who gets Gmail Live, how to start it on Android and iPhone, and the kinds of questions that work.

## Who can use Gmail Live

Google lists Gmail Live for Google AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra subscribers. Docs Live is limited to Pro and Ultra. Keep Live shares the Plus, Pro, and Ultra plans but is Android only.

Gmail Live is available on Android and iOS. Google says the same voice features are coming later for Workspace business customers.

You need:

- A personal Google account on a supported Google AI plan
- The latest Gmail app from Google Play or the App Store
- Microphone permission for Gmail
- English, which is the language Google named for the first mobile rollout

If you already use [Gemini in Gmail](/blog/gemini-in-gmail/) on the web, treat Gmail Live as the spoken version of inbox search, not a replacement for Help me write.

## How Gmail Live differs from typed search

Typed Gmail search still wants operators, labels, and exact phrases. Gmail Live accepts a spoken question and reads across threads.

Google’s own examples include “What’s my flight's gate number?” and “What’s going on at my kid’s school this week?” The assistant pulls the detail from mail you already received and can point you back to the source message in the on-screen transcript.

You do not start a new session for every follow-up. Ask “What time does that land?” after a flight question and the same conversation should hold the booking context.

## Start Gmail Live on Android

1. Update Gmail from Google Play.
2. Confirm you are signed in with the Google account that holds the AI Plus, Pro, or Ultra plan.
3. Open Gmail and grant microphone access if Android asks.
4. Look for the Live or Gemini voice control in the Gmail app. Google ships this as a conversational layer inside Gmail, not as a separate app.
5. Tap to start listening, then ask a full question. Speak the way you would ask a colleague, not as a search string.
6. Watch the transcript. Confirm the cited thread before you act on dates, gates, or money.

If Live does not appear, check the plan on [Google AI plans](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/) and wait for the mobile rollout to reach your account. Google described the launch as a same-week rollout, not an instant global flip.

## Start Gmail Live on iPhone

1. Update Gmail from the App Store.
2. Sign in with the same subscribed Google account.
3. Allow microphone access in iOS Settings > Gmail if you previously denied it.
4. Open Gmail and start Live from the in-app voice control.
5. Ask the question, then interrupt or follow up without leaving the session.

Keep Live is not on iOS in the first wave. If you want spoken notes on iPhone, use Keep’s existing dictation or wait for Google’s later platform list. Docs Live is on both Android and iPhone for Pro and Ultra. See [How to Use Docs Live With Gemini 3.8 Extended Thinking](/blog/docs-live-gemini-3-8/) for the document workflow.

## Questions that work well

Use Gmail Live for facts already sitting in mail, not for work Gmail cannot see.

Try:

- “What is my flight gate?” after an airline sent a day-of update.
- “When is the next school event for my kid?” when the school uses email blasts.
- “Did the online order ship?” when tracking mail is buried under promotions.
- “What did the landlord say about the deposit?” when the answer spans several replies.

Then follow up:

- “Read the last sentence of that email.”
- “What time is that in my timezone?”
- “Who else was copied?”

Skip questions that need a calendar you never mailed, a Slack thread, or a document that lives only in Drive. Docs Live can pull Drive and Chat when you grant permission. Gmail Live is built around the inbox.

## Privacy and control

Gmail Live reads mail that is already in your account so it can answer. Treat it like any Gemini feature that uses Workspace content.

- Review the transcript before you forward a date or amount.
- Do not speak passwords, one-time codes, or card numbers into the mic.
- Stop the session if you switch from a private room to a public one.
- Use a work account only after your admin enables the later Workspace rollout.

Google’s product post does not claim on-device-only processing for Gmail Live. Assume cloud Gemini Audio models handle the conversation, same as the I/O preview.

## Tips that save time

Ask for the outcome, not the folder. “What did the airline change?” beats “label:travel after:2026/09/01.”

Name people the way they appear in From fields. “What did Priya send about the invoice?” works better than a nickname Gmail never stored.

Interrupt when the answer is good enough. Google explicitly supports cutting in mid-sentence.

Confirm the source thread in the transcript. Voice search can stitch two similar trips or two school emails.

Pair the three Live tools instead of forcing one app to do everything. Search mail in Gmail Live, dump a grocery list in Keep Live on Android, and talk a first draft in Docs Live.

## Limits to expect

English is the first-wave language Google named. Other languages may follow, but do not assume they are live.

Plus subscribers get Gmail and Keep Live, not Docs Live.

Keep Live stays Android-only in this launch.

Workspace business accounts are not in the first consumer wave.

Gmail Live cannot invent a gate number that no airline mailed. If the answer is missing, open the thread and check the latest message.

## Conclusion

Gmail Live is inbox search you can speak. Eligible Plus, Pro, and Ultra users on Android and iPhone can ask for a gate, a school date, or a shipping update without building a query.

Update the Gmail app, confirm the plan, grant the mic, and start with one real question from mail you already have. Keep the transcript visible and confirm the source thread before you act.

When you need a written draft instead of an inbox answer, switch to Docs Live. When you need a list from a brain dump on Android, switch to Keep Live.
