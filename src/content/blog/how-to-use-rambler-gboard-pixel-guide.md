Rambler is a Gemini Intelligence feature in Gboard that turns natural speech into clean, polished text. It removes filler words like “um” and “uh,” fixes grammar and punctuation, and formats your thoughts into readable messages. Google expanded it to Pixel 10 series devices in the November 2026 Pixel Drop, alongside other Gemini Intelligence updates.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="5" />

Previously available on Pixel 11, Rambler now helps more Pixel users dictate messages, notes, and emails without manual cleanup. It works anywhere Gboard is active.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="94" />

## Requirements for Rambler

- Pixel 10 series or Pixel 11 series device with the latest software.
- Latest version of Gboard installed and set as the default keyboard.
- Microphone permission granted to Gboard (allow only while using the app).
- Gemini Intelligence features enabled on supported devices.

Availability rolls out with Pixel Drops and system updates. Confirm your device has the November 2026 updates or later.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="5" />

## How to Enable Rambler in Gboard

1. Open any app where you can type, such as Gmail, Google Messages, or Notes.
2. Tap a text field to bring up Gboard.
3. Tap the **Microphone** icon.
4. If an introduction appears, tap **Start** to enable Rambler.
5. If no introduction appears: Tap the three-dot menu → **Settings** → **Voice typing** → select **Rambler**.
6. Return to your text field and tap the Microphone again to begin.

You can switch voice input methods later in the same Voice typing settings.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="94" />

## How to Use Rambler

1. Tap a text area to open Gboard.
2. Tap the Microphone and start speaking naturally.
3. Talk at your own pace. You do not need perfect pauses for punctuation or formal sentence structure.
4. When finished, tap **Done**, or hold the Microphone while speaking and release when done.
5. Review the cleaned-up text. Edit if needed before sending.

Rambler distills the main points, removes repetitions and fillers, and produces a coherent message. It supports multilingual input in a single dictation on compatible setups.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="94" />

## Practical Tips

- Speak as you would in conversation. The feature is designed for natural rambling rather than scripted speech.
- Use it for quick messages, email drafts, or note-taking where polish saves time.
- Check the output on longer dictations before relying on it for important communications.
- Combine with other Gemini features such as proactive suggestions or task automations on Pixel 10 and 11 devices.
- Keep Gboard updated through the Play Store for the latest improvements.
