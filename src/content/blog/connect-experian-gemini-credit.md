Google added Experian to Gemini Connected Apps on 23 September 2026. It is the first personal-finance app in that list. U.S. users can link an Experian membership and ask Gemini about their credit rating inside a normal chat.

You stay in control. Gemini only sees Experian data after you authorize the connection. You can turn the app off later from Settings.

This walkthrough uses Google’s Connected Apps help pages and Experian’s own launch note. It does not invent score formulas or claim Gemini replaces the Experian website.

## What the Experian connection actually does

Experian said U.S. consumers can connect through a consumer-authorized flow and ask questions about their credit. Existing members link the account they already use. People who find Experian inside Gemini can join at no cost.

A typical prompt is `@Experian, what's my credit rating?` Gemini then asks you to connect if the app is off. After you approve access, the reply can include your rating inside the Gemini thread.

Experian also said users can ask about key factors that influence that rating. Coverager reported that people who want FICO Score 8 still open Experian’s website or app for that product. Treat Gemini as an explanation layer, not a full bureau dashboard.

Google’s 23 September product post grouped Experian with other lifestyle tools: apartments.com, Peloton, and SeatGeek. Those partners sit beside productivity and creative apps in the same rollout.

## Who can connect Experian today

Experian’s announcement limits the Gemini experience to consumers in the United States. Google’s generic Connected Apps help page still applies for how you switch an app on and off.

Plan on these conditions:

- A personal Google Account. Work and school logins often cannot use third-party Connected Apps the same way.
- Keep Activity on when Gemini needs to complete a connected-app request.
- An Experian membership, either an existing one or a free account created during the link flow.
- English prompts while the partner integration is new.

Availability can lag by account. If Experian is missing from the Apps list, wait for the rollout rather than forcing a second Google login.

For the rest of the September partner list, see our [guide to Gemini’s new connected apps](/blog/connect-gemini-new-apps-sept-2026/).

## Connect Experian from Settings

Experian’s how-it-works section points at Personal Intelligence and Connected Apps. Google’s help article for managing apps uses the Apps page under Settings.

On the web:

1. Open [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) and sign in.
2. Open **Settings and help**, then **Apps** (or **Personal Intelligence → Connected Apps** if that menu is what your account shows).
3. Find **Experian**.
4. Turn the app on and complete Experian’s authorization screens.
5. Confirm the toggle stays on when you return to Gemini.

On Android or iOS, open the Gemini app, go to Settings, then the same Apps or Personal Intelligence list. The labels match the web flow even if the icons differ.

Link the Experian account that already holds your credit file when you have one. Creating a second free membership can split history across two logins.

## Connect from a chat with @Experian

You do not have to visit Settings first. Google lets you name an app with `@` in the prompt box.

1. Type `@` and choose **Experian**, or type `@Experian`.
2. Ask a concrete question. Experian’s example is a credit-rating request.
3. If the app is disconnected, accept the connect prompt and finish authorization.
4. Stay in the same thread for follow-up questions about factors that affect the rating.

Keep Activity must stay on for Gemini to finish the connected-app call. If the model only returns generic credit education and never asks to link, the partner flag is not on your account yet.

Do not paste Social Security numbers, full account numbers, or one-time passcodes into the chat. Authorization belongs on Experian’s screens.

## Prompts that stay inside the published use cases

Use short asks that match what Experian listed:

- `@Experian what's my credit rating?`
- `@Experian which factors are affecting my rating the most?`
- `@Experian explain this rating in plain language for a first-time borrower.`

Then add context Gemini already has permission to use, such as a savings goal you typed in the same chat. Do not ask Gemini to invent a FICO Score 8 if Experian sends you to its own app for that number.

Experian and Coverager both noted more features may arrive later, including help with canceling subscriptions. Those extras are not in the day-one flow. Skip them until Experian documents the steps.

If you also use Wallet inside Gemini, keep the two connections separate. Wallet can surface passes and spending insights. Experian answers bureau questions. Mixing them in one prompt makes it harder to see which app supplied the data.

## Disconnect Experian and review permissions

Google’s Connected Apps help page says you can disconnect any app from the same Apps list.

1. Return to **Settings → Apps**.
2. Find Experian and turn it off.
3. On Experian’s site or app, review connected apps and revoke Gemini if a second switch exists there.

Disconnecting stops new pulls from Gemini. It does not delete the Experian membership you created during signup.

Personalization help for Connected Apps explains that linked data can personalize Gemini and, depending on your settings, help improve Google services. Read that page before you leave the toggle on for credit data.

## Tips that keep the link useful

- Ask one credit question per turn so you can see whether Experian or a general model reply answered you.
- Recheck the Apps toggle after a Gemini app update. Partner flags sometimes reset during rollouts.
- Use the official Experian app when you need disputes, freezes, or FICO Score 8.
- Do not treat a Gemini summary as a lender decision. Lenders pull their own files.
- If you travel, remember the Experian Gemini path is documented for the United States only.

## Conclusion

Experian is now a first-party Connected App in Gemini for U.S. personal accounts. Turn it on from Settings or call `@Experian` in chat, authorize the membership, and ask about your rating and the factors behind it.

Leave the connection off when you only need generic credit education. When you need a full file, open Experian directly. Gemini is the conversational front door, not a replacement for the bureau’s own tools.
