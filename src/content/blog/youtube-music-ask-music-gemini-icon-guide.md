YouTube Music just made its AI music assistant easier to reach. A new Gemini spark icon now sits in the top-right corner of most screens in the Android and iOS apps. Tap it and Ask Music opens instantly.

This server-side rollout started around November 18, 2026. It replaces the older Home-feed card for Premium and Music Premium subscribers. Non-subscribers still see no access.

The feature itself is not brand new. Ask Music launched in 2024 as a conversational way to turn a description into a custom radio or playlist. The icon simply puts it one tap away from Home, Library, Now Playing, albums, and playlists.

![Person listening to music with headphones](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1758600434152-904cb58e51eb?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What Ask Music can do

Ask Music acts as a generative AI companion inside YouTube Music. You describe a mood, activity, genre, or artist. It builds a mix that blends tracks you already like with new discoveries.

You can also ask it to find podcasts, review your listening history, or dig into artist details. Voice input works alongside typing. Suggested prompts appear so you do not always need to invent a query from scratch.

Official help pages note that creating a mix does not interrupt whatever is currently playing. You can save results to your library for later.

Eligibility is clear: YouTube Premium or YouTube Music Premium membership is required. Availability can vary by region and account during the gradual rollout.

## How to open Ask Music with the Gemini icon

1. Update the YouTube Music app on Android or iOS if it is not already current.
2. Open the app and sign in with a Premium account.
3. Look in the top-right corner of the Home, Library, Now Playing, album, or playlist screen for the four-pointed Gemini spark icon.
4. Tap the icon. Ask Music opens as a full-screen sheet.
5. Type or speak a request, or tap one of the suggested options such as Create a mix or Discover artists.

If the icon is missing, the rollout has not reached your account yet. There is no manual switch. Wait and check again later. The Samples tab has also been removed from navigation on both platforms as part of recent UI cleanup.

## Step-by-step: create a custom mix

Once Ask Music is open, follow these steps:

1. Describe what you want. Try “upbeat indie for a morning commute” or “podcasts about space exploration.”
2. Tap to play the generated mix. YouTube Music starts it right away.
3. If the result misses the mark, send a new prompt. The mix updates without restarting the app.
4. Tap Add when you want to keep the station or playlist in your library.

You can also open Ask Music from context. It surfaces while you edit a playlist or build a mix, and more agentic controls for the queue are rolling out this year.

## Tips for better results

Be specific. “Chill lo-fi for studying with no vocals” works better than “something calm.” Include an activity, energy level, or one or two reference artists.

Use voice when your hands are busy. The mic icon sits next to the text field.

Scroll up inside Ask Music to revisit recent conversations and earlier recommendations.

For podcasts, describe a topic or style. The tool can suggest shows and individual episodes you can save.

If you also use the main Gemini app, you can connect YouTube Music there for separate playback commands, but the in-app Ask Music experience stays inside YouTube Music.
