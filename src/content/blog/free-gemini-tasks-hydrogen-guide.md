Google rolled out free access to Gemini Tasks on November 17, 2026. Eligible users in the United States, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India can now hand off multi-step work without a paid plan. Tasks run on Gemini 4 Hydrogen, Google's fast everyday model for agentic workflows.

Chat handles quick questions. Tasks handle longer projects, schedules, and actions across apps. You open Gemini, switch the toggle from Chat to Task, and describe what you need. Gemini works in the background in a sandboxed virtual machine dedicated to your account.

This guide shows how to start, manage, and stay safe with the free version. Requirements include being 18 or older, signing in with a personal Google Account, and having Keep Activity on. Work and school accounts are not supported yet.

## Check eligibility and open Tasks

Update the Gemini app on Android or iOS, or visit gemini.google.com. Sign in with your personal account. Look for the Chat and Task toggle near the prompt box. On mobile it sits above the box; on web it appears below.

If you do not see Task, confirm your location and that Keep Activity is enabled in settings. The free rollout covers hundreds of millions of users in the six listed countries. Advanced features continue rolling out starting in the US.

For developers building with the same model, see our guide on [adding Gemini 4 Hydrogen to Android with Firebase AI Logic](/blog/add-gemini-4-hydrogen-android-firebase-ai-logic/).

## Create your first Task

Tap or click Task. Describe the goal in plain language. Include any timing, connected apps, or limits. Examples that match Google's own use cases:

- Analyze recent utility bills from email and identify why the cost spiked.
- Find nearby service centers, call them, and book the earliest Friday morning slot that fits your calendar.
- Compare USB-C chargers, apply a discount if available, and complete checkout within a spending cap.

You can attach files, pull from Drive or a Notebook, or reference a Skill with @. Submit the task. Gemini starts working. You can open the thread later from the sidebar under All.

Progress indicators show Ready, Needs attention, or In progress. Tap the progress chip inside a thread to review the steps Gemini already completed.

## Schedule recurring work

Tasks support time-based schedules that run once, hourly, daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly. You can also trigger on events such as a new email from a specific person.

On the web at gemini.google.com you can create schedules manually. On mobile, describe the schedule in the Task prompt: "Every weekday at 7 a.m., summarize my calendar, unread emails, and Drive files that need attention today."

Gemini asks clarifying questions when instructions are ambiguous. For purchases it pauses for confirmation or pre-approval before any Buy action. You can set a hard spending cap. Receipts land in Google Wallet.

Calling is available in most regions but currently excluded in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska. Gemini discloses it is an AI at the start of the call. Businesses can opt out.

## Text and email channels

Starting in the US, you can text Gemini or forward emails to gemini@google.com. To enable text:

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Tap Settings.
3. Tap Channels.
4. Tap Set up under Text with Gemini.
5. Verify your phone number.

A US number with an eligible carrier is required for the text channel. Forwarded emails let Gemini extract dates and add them to your calendar automatically.

## Safety controls and monitoring

Tasks run inside Google Cloud's Agent Compute Engine. Each session uses a hardware-isolated virtual machine that is never shared. Prompt-injection filters and Google Safe Browsing limit which links Gemini can open.

You can jump into an active task at any time and add instructions. For routine actions you trust, set "Always allow" so Gemini does not pause every time. Review and delete history anytime in settings. Disconnect Google or third-party apps you no longer want used.

If a task fails or produces a poor result, give it a thumbs-down. Google notes that some websites still treat automated browsers strictly; Gemini may hand the final click back to you.

## Tips for better free Tasks

Start with read-only or low-risk work such as summaries and comparisons. Add connected apps only for the specific task. Review the progress chip before approving any payment or booking.

Use Personal Intelligence so Gemini already knows your location, vehicle, or preferences and does not ask for the same details repeatedly. Pair Tasks with Gemini Intelligence features on supported Android devices for on-device speed where available.

Monitor the sidebar for threads that need attention. Pin important ones. Rename threads so recurring schedules stay easy to find.

## Conclusion

Free Gemini Tasks powered by Hydrogen let eligible users in six countries offload real work today. Open the app, switch to Task, and describe the outcome. Gemini handles the steps in an isolated environment, pauses for critical actions, and keeps receipts and calendar entries inside your Google account.

Update the app, confirm your region and personal account, then try one low-stakes task. Check the official Gemini help pages for the latest availability notes and Connected Apps list.
