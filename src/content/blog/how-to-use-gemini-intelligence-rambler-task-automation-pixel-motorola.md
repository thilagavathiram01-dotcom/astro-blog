Google expanded Gemini Intelligence to the Pixel 10 series and the Motorola Signature 27 in November 2026. The update adds Rambler for polished voice typing and expands multi-step task automation to more regions.

Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold receive these tools through the November Pixel Drop. The Motorola Signature 27 ships with them as Motorola’s first Gemini Intelligence device, with US pre-orders opening 19 November.

This guide shows how to turn on Rambler, use task automation, and create custom widgets on supported devices.

## Enable Rambler on Pixel 10

Rambler cleans filler words such as “um,” fixes mid-sentence corrections, and formats spoken text. It works in any app that uses Gboard and is available in all regions and languages on supported Pixel 10 models.

First confirm you have the November 2026 Pixel Drop installed. Open Settings, tap your Google account at the top, then All services > System services. Check that Android AI Core is updated.

Open any text field so Gboard appears. Tap the settings gear on the keyboard toolbar. Select Voice typing and choose Rambler. Return to the text field and tap the microphone icon (it shows a sparkle). Speak naturally, then tap Done. The cleaned text appears in the field.

![Person holding a smartphone ready for voice input](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1721988348117-01a350266d26?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

You can also hold the microphone button while speaking and release when finished. Rambler does not show live text; it processes the full utterance before displaying the result.

## Use Task Automation on Pixel 10 and Motorola Signature 27

Task automation lets Gemini complete multi-step jobs such as ordering groceries, booking a ride, or making a reservation. It runs in a background virtual window so you can watch or stop it. The feature expanded to the UK, India, and Japan alongside the US and other supported regions.

Open the Gemini app or long-press the power button. State a clear request: “Order my usual coffee from the Starbucks app” or “Book a ride to the airport at 3 p.m.” Confirm any details Gemini requests. It navigates the allowlisted app and stops before final payment so you can review.

Supported categories include rideshare, food delivery, grocery ordering, and reservations. Availability depends on the app and your region. Always check the final confirmation screen before approving charges.

![Hand holding smartphone showing AI apps](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1744900647430-965ccc7a6078?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

On the Motorola Signature 27 the same workflow applies. The device meets the hardware requirements for Gemini Intelligence and includes Rambler and task automation out of the box.

## Create Custom Widgets

Create My Widget lets you describe a home-screen widget in plain language. Gemini builds it for you. Examples include a monthly spending tracker, a game-day widget in your team colors, or a trip countdown that pulls flight and reservation details.

Open Gemini and describe the widget you want. Follow the prompts to place it on your home screen. The feature is available on Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 devices and on the Motorola Signature 27. It does not appear on the Pixel 10a.

## Tips for Best Results

Speak at a normal pace for Rambler. You do not need perfect sentences. Review every result before sending, because the model can occasionally miss context.

For task automation, name the specific app when possible and confirm addresses or times. Keep an eye on the progress indicator so you can intervene if needed.

Update Gboard and Android AI Core through the Play Store if features do not appear immediately after the Pixel Drop.
