Google Maps Ask Maps now handles more than questions and directions. Its new agentic capabilities let you search for open appointment slots and even call local businesses on your behalf.

This update rolled out on November 16, 2026, on Android and iOS. It covers beauty services, pet care, auto repair, and home repair. Ask Maps checks online booking systems first and can place calls when needed.

The feature starts in the United States, with more regions to follow. Travel itineraries and saved-list search work more widely, but appointment finding is currently US-only.

## Open Ask Maps and request an appointment

Update Google Maps to the latest version. Open the app and tap the Ask Maps button below the search bar.

Type or speak a clear request. Google’s example is “Find me a 4 p.m. nail appointment near me.” You can adapt it: “Find a 2 p.m. haircut slot near me this Friday” or “Look for pet grooming availability tomorrow afternoon.”

Ask Maps may ask for extra details such as preferred time range, service type, or location constraints. Answer briefly so it can refine the search.

It then shows open slots across nearby businesses. Each option includes the place name, available times, and distance. You review the list and choose the one that fits.

## How the agentic calling works

Ask Maps does not automatically book without your confirmation. It first checks online availability. When a business lacks an online booking link or the slot needs confirmation, it can call on your behalf.

You stay in control. After reviewing the slots, select the preferred option. Ask Maps proceeds with the call or online booking step and reports back.

Supported categories match the announcement: beauty (nail salons, haircuts), pet care (grooming), auto repair, and home repair. These align with earlier agentic calling experiments in Google Search and AI Mode.

The process saves time on routine errands. Instead of calling multiple shops yourself, you describe the need once and pick from verified openings.

## Search saved lists for available spots

Ask Maps also searches your existing saved lists. This helps when guests arrive or you need a last-minute table from places you already trust.

Try: “Which spots saved in my lists have availability for five people in the next 30 minutes?” Ask Maps scans the lists, checks live availability, and surfaces matching options.

This works for restaurants and other bookable places stored in your Maps lists. It complements the appointment feature by focusing on your personal collection rather than a broad local search.

## Tips for better appointment results

Be specific with time and service. “4 p.m. nail appointment near me” works better than “find a salon.” Add constraints like “within 5 miles” or “open after 5 p.m.” if needed.

Confirm details before committing. Hours and availability change. Open the place card from the results to verify reviews, photos, and exact times.

Use follow-up questions in the same chat. Ask Maps keeps context, so you can say “Show me later slots at the second option” without restarting.

Check your location permissions. Ask Maps uses your current position or saved home/work for “near me” queries. Set a custom location if you are planning for elsewhere.

Availability is limited at launch. Appointment finding and “Know before you go” route tips launch first in the US. Ask Maps itself is expanding to more than 30 European countries, including Spain, Germany, and the UK, bringing the total above 200 countries and territories.

## What to expect after booking

Once you select a slot, Ask Maps completes the online booking or call. You receive confirmation in the chat. Save the place or add it to a list for easy navigation later.

Pull up directions when it is time to leave. Look for “Know before you go” tips on parking, charging, weather, delays, and tolls (US first).

The feature fits everyday tasks more than complex travel. Use it for haircuts, pet grooming, oil changes, or plumber visits. For multi-day road trips with coastal hikes or shopping stops, the itinerary tools remain the better fit.

## Conclusion

Ask Maps turns appointment hunting into a short conversation. You state the time and service, review open slots, and let the agent handle online checks or calls. Combined with list search, it reduces the calls and tabs that usually fill holiday prep or weekend errands.

Update the Google Maps app, open Ask Maps, and try a specific request. Results appear with real place data so you can decide quickly and head out.
