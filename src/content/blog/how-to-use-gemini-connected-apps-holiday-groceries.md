Google rolled out new Connected Apps on November 20, 2026, so US users can build grocery carts inside Search and the Gemini app. Target, Albertsons, Safeway and Shaw’s join the list, powered by the Universal Commerce Protocol. You describe what you need for a holiday meal or party, and Gemini helps assemble the order without switching apps.

This guide shows the exact steps to connect the apps, start a request, review the cart, and finish checkout. It also covers related finance tools and what to expect during the rollout.

## What the New Connected Apps Cover

In the United States, Gemini and Search now connect to Albertsons, Safeway, Shaw’s and Target for grocery shopping. You can ask for items, see real-time inventory where available, link loyalty accounts, and move toward checkout. Experian expands from the Gemini app into Search for credit checks and loan shopping. H&R Block helps calculate taxes and estimate refunds right from Search. Angi and Thumbtack for home services arrive in the coming weeks.

These features sit on top of the existing Connected Apps platform that already links more than 60 services. Pair them with Gemini Tasks if you want the assistant to work in the background. ## How to Connect the Grocery Apps

You need a personal Google account, signed in, with Gemini activity history turned on. Connected Apps stay unavailable if history is off.

1. Open the Gemini app or go to gemini.google.com.
2. Tap or click Settings (or Settings and help on the web).
3. Choose Connected Apps or Apps. On some mobile layouts it appears under Personal Intelligence or Customize > Connectors.
4. Find Albertsons, Safeway, Shaw’s or Target and turn the toggle on.
5. Follow any sign-in or permission prompts. Link your loyalty account if the store offers one.
6. Confirm the app shows as connected.

Repeat for any other services you want, such as Experian or H&R Block. You can disconnect an app later from the same page.

On Search, the same apps appear when you use AI Mode or the regular interface after the rollout reaches your account. Availability is gradual, so check back if the option is missing.

## Start a Grocery Order in Gemini

Once the app is connected, you can request items in plain language.

Open Gemini and type a clear prompt. Examples:

- “Add ingredients for a Thanksgiving dinner for six to my Target cart: turkey, stuffing mix, cranberry sauce, potatoes and green beans.”
- “Build a party appetizer list at Safeway under $50: cheese platter items, crackers, dips and sparkling water.”
- “@Albertsons find milk, bread, eggs and bananas that are in stock near me.”

Use the @ symbol followed by the store name to force a specific app. Gemini then pulls inventory, suggests quantities, and shows a draft cart. Review every item and price before you approve any change. The assistant pauses for confirmation on purchases.

If you prefer background work, switch the toggle from Chat to Task and give the same instruction. Gemini continues after you close the app and notifies you when it needs input or finishes.

## Review and Check Out

Gemini displays the proposed cart with prices and availability. Adjust quantities, remove items, or add more by typing follow-up instructions. When the list looks right, follow the on-screen checkout flow. You may finish inside the Gemini or Search interface or be handed to the store’s site or app to complete payment.

Payment methods stay in the retailer’s system or Google Wallet where supported. Always confirm the final total and delivery or pickup details yourself. Gemini does not override store policies on returns, substitutions or fees.

For tax or credit tasks, open Search or Gemini and ask: “Estimate my 2026 tax refund with H&R Block” or “Check my Experian credit score.” You still complete any sensitive steps on the partner site after the initial summary.

## Tips for Reliable Results

- Name the exact store with @ so Gemini does not pick a different retailer.
- Include budget, quantity and dietary limits in the first prompt.
- Check stock and prices on the store site if the cart looks off. Inventory can change.
- Keep purchase confirmation turned on so Gemini asks before it buys.
- Link loyalty accounts early to apply discounts automatically where the integration supports it.
- Test a small order first to learn the confirmation flow.

US-only for these grocery and finance Connected Apps at launch. Users outside the United States will not see them until Google expands the rollout. Free and paid Gemini users can access the feature once it reaches their account.
