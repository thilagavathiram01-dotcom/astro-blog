Google Play Store version 54.2 in the November 2026 Google System Updates added Play Achievements. These track your gaming milestones and skills across titles, separate from developer-created in-game achievements.

With a Play Games profile, you unlock them by hitting playtime milestones, collecting rare in-game achievements, or joining events such as Play Games Sprints. Past gameplay counts automatically. Both types award XP that levels up your profile. Play Achievements also show rarity badges based on how many players unlock them.

You can view and share them from your Play Games profile, the You tab in Google Play, a game’s details page, or in-game Sidekick. Visibility is controlled in profile settings.

## What Are Play Achievements?

Play Achievements are platform-level rewards from Google Play itself. They sit alongside the achievements game developers create for specific titles.

They recognize broader activity: hours played across games, collecting hard-to-get in-game unlocks, or participating in limited-time events. Your history is included, so long-time players may see progress right away.

When you unlock one, a pop-up overlay appears. Harder achievements give more XP. Enough XP levels up your Play Games profile. Rarity badges show how exclusive each one is.

This is part of the broader gaming profile system that surfaces in the Play Store’s You tab and game listings.

## How to Earn Play Achievements

You need a Play Games profile. If you do not have one, open the Play Store, go to the You tab, and follow the prompts to create or enable it. The system then tracks eligible activity.

Earn them by:

- Reaching playtime milestones across your installed games.
- Unlocking rare in-game achievements that Play recognizes.
- Joining events such as Play Games Sprints.

No special action is required beyond playing. Past sessions count. When conditions are met, the overlay notifies you and XP is added.

Difficulty affects the XP reward. More demanding unlocks give larger amounts. Leveling your profile happens automatically once the XP threshold is crossed.

Keep the Google Play Store and Play services updated. Features roll out gradually, so the You tab or game progress sections may appear on some devices before others.

## How to View Your Play Achievements

Open the Play Store app on your Android phone or tablet.

To see progress for a specific game:

1. Search for or open the game you have installed.
2. Look under “Your game progress.” Recent achievements for that title appear there.
3. Select Your game progress to view the full list for that game.

For a broader view:

- Open the You tab at the bottom of the Play Store. Gaming stats, recently played titles, and achievement progress appear in the feed.
- Open the Google Play Games app, go to your Profile, and check the level (shown on your profile picture) along with achievements.
- On a game’s details page in the Play Store, recent progress is highlighted.
- Inside supported games, use Sidekick if available.

Share options exist from the profile or You tab. Control who sees your profile and achievements in the visibility settings.

Leaderboards remain available in the Play Games app for games that support them. Select a game, open Leaderboards, and pick one to compare scores if your profile is public.

## Tips for Maximizing Progress

Update Google Play Store and Google Play services first. The November 2026 system notes list the feature under Play Store v54.2.

Play regularly across multiple titles. Milestones and rare unlocks both contribute. Events like Sprints can accelerate progress during active periods.

Check the You tab periodically. It surfaces gaming stats, recent play, and next achievement suggestions in one place.

Make your profile public only if you want others to see levels and progress. You can still earn achievements while keeping the profile private.

If progress does not appear, confirm you are signed in with the same Google Account used for Play Games, then force a sync by opening the Play Games app and visiting your profile.

For app discovery while you play, the Ask Play overlay in the store can help you find new titles. See [How to Use Ask Play for App Discovery in Play Store](/blog/ask-play-google-play-store/) for details.

## Conclusion

Play Achievements turn your existing playtime and unlocks into profile-level rewards that appear in the Play Store’s You tab and on game pages. Create or confirm your Play Games profile, keep the store updated, and check progress under Your game progress or the You tab.

XP and rarity badges give clear feedback. Visibility settings keep the details as public or private as you prefer. The feature is rolling out with the November 2026 Google System Updates, so check for the latest Play Store version if the sections are not visible yet.
