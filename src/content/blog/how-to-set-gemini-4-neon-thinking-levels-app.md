Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers can now pick Gemini 4 Neon in the Gemini app. Neon balances speed and depth better than Hydrogen for complex work. Its thinking levels let you control how much reasoning the model applies before answering.

This guide shows exactly how to switch models and set Low, Medium or High on Android and the web. You stay in control of cost and latency for every chat.

## Who Gets Gemini 4 Neon

Neon rolled out on 18 November 2026. It appears in the Gemini app for personal Google AI Pro and Ultra accounts. Free users and Google AI Plus stay on Hydrogen or Auto. Work and school accounts depend on your Workspace licence.

The model also appears in Google AI Studio (paid API tier), Google Antigravity (Pro/Ultra), and the Gemini API. In the consumer app the three thinking levels are Low, Medium and High. Extra High exists only in the API.

If you do not see Neon yet, update the Gemini app from the Play Store and wait a day. Rollouts take time.

## Switch to Neon and Set a Thinking Level

Follow these steps on Android or the web:

1. Open the Gemini app or go to gemini.google.com. Sign in with your Pro or Ultra account.
2. Start a new chat or open an existing one.
3. Tap or click the model name at the bottom of the prompt box (it often shows Auto or Hydrogen).
4. Select Gemini 4 Neon from the list. A lock icon means you need a higher plan.
5. After selecting the model, choose a thinking level: Low, Medium or High.
6. Type your prompt and send it. The choice sticks for that conversation.

If you select Neon without picking a level, the app defaults to Low. That gives fast, concise answers. For reports, code reviews or multi-step analysis, switch to High before you ask.

The same controls work in Task mode. Neon can handle multi-step jobs once you flip the toggle from Chat to Task.

## What Each Thinking Level Does

Google’s help pages describe the three levels clearly:

- **Low**: Fast and concise. Best for straightforward questions, short rewrites and quick lookups.
- **Medium**: Balances speed and reasoning. Useful for everyday coding, document Q&A and moderate analysis.
- **High**: Deeper reasoning. Use it for long reports, tricky comparisons, debugging or multi-step tasks.

Higher levels increase output tokens and latency because the model thinks longer. Start with Medium for most work. Move to High only when the first answer feels shallow. Drop to Low when you need speed.

In the Gemini API the default is High and an Extra High option exists. The app keeps the simpler three-level control.

## Practical Tips for Daily Use

Keep Hydrogen or Auto for casual chat and simple drafting. Switch to Neon + Medium when you upload a PDF or spreadsheet and need a structured summary.

For code, start at Medium. If the fix is incomplete, stay in the same chat and raise the level to High, then ask it to revise.

Long conversations re-read the full history each turn, so token use grows. Start a fresh chat when you change topics. This keeps costs predictable on Pro plans.

You can also set thinking levels in Google AI Studio for testing before you use the same prompts in the app. See our earlier guide on [how to use Gemini 4 Neon](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-4-neon/) for the full access overview.

## Limits and Controls

Neon is not available on free accounts. Usage still counts against your Pro or Ultra quota. Complex High-level tasks consume more of that quota.

The model has a 1 million-token context window in the app for Pro and Ultra users. Knowledge cutoff is March 2026. Use Google Search grounding when you need newer facts.

You can switch models mid-conversation or start a new one. History settings still apply: you can keep chat history while controlling whether it trains models.

Gemini 4 Neon gives Pro and Ultra subscribers a stronger middle option in the app. Setting the thinking level takes two taps and lets you match reasoning depth to the task. Low keeps answers fast, Medium covers most daily work, and High handles the hard problems.

Update the app, open the model menu, and try Neon on your next multi-step prompt. You will see the difference immediately.
