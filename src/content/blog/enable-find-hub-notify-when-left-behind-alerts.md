Google has rolled out “Notify when left behind” alerts in the Find Hub app. The feature warns you when a tracker tag separates from your phone, helping prevent lost keys, wallets, or bags before you walk too far.

This matches a capability Apple’s Find My and Samsung’s SmartThings have offered for years. It works with compatible trackers such as the Moto Tag and Chipolo devices, and reports show it appearing for users in the US and parts of Europe on Find Hub version 3.1.787-06 or later.


![Keys and wallet ready for tracking](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1742318592061-15c5f19e1e47?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)


## What the Alert Does

Once enabled for a specific tag, Find Hub sends a notification if the tracker moves out of Bluetooth range from your phone. The app automatically skips alerts at the Home address saved in your Google Account (via Maps), so you do not get constant warnings when leaving the house without a tagged item.

The toggle is per tracker. You can protect a wallet tag without alerts for spare keys that stay home. Early reports confirm it currently focuses on item trackers rather than headphones or other devices.

Google previewed the feature when announcing the Pixel Tag and included related notes in Play services updates. The full rollout gives Android users closer feature parity with competing networks.

## How to Enable Notify When Left Behind

Open the Find Hub app on your Android phone. Select the tracker tag from your device list. Tap the settings gear icon in the top-right corner of the bottom sheet.

Look for the “Notify when left behind” section. Switch the toggle on. The app describes it as receiving a notification when the tag is separated from your phone.

If the option does not appear, update Find Hub from the Play Store and confirm your tracker is supported. The setting can sometimes be configured during initial tag registration in newer flows.

After enabling, test by walking a short distance away from the tagged item (outside your home). You should receive a notification. Return to the item to clear the alert.

## Tips for Best Results

Keep Bluetooth and location services enabled on your phone for reliable detection. Attach the tracker firmly to the item you care about most. Name tags clearly in Find Hub so alerts are easy to identify.

Review the Home exception if you share devices or frequently leave items in other locations. For multiple trackers, enable the alert only on high-value items to avoid notification fatigue.

Combine this with Find Hub’s locate, ring, and secure options. If an item goes missing farther away, the crowdsourced network can still help show its last known location.

Pixel Tag buyers should find the feature ready when the hardware launches. Third-party trackers already on the network benefit immediately where the update has rolled out.

## Conclusion

Enabling Notify when left behind takes under a minute and closes a long-standing gap in Find Hub. Check for the toggle on your supported trackers today, especially if you travel or leave items in cafes, taxis, or offices.

Update the app, flip the switch on the tags that matter, and rely on the Home exception to keep alerts useful. Pair it with existing Find Hub tools for complete coverage of phones, accessories, and tagged items.
