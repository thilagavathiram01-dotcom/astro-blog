Google confirmed it will retire the Nest app on January 25, 2027. All Nest device management moves to the Google Home app. Later in 2027, Google Assistant on speakers and smart displays will give way to Gemini for Home.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="38" />

If you still use the Nest app for cameras, thermostats, doorbells, Nest Protect alarms, or the Nest x Yale lock, act before the deadline. Devices keep working, but remote control and notifications require the Google Home app after that date. Manually saved video clips from home.nest.com do not transfer automatically.

## Key deadlines for Nest users

Mark these dates:

- **January 25, 2027**: Nest app stops working on Android, iOS, TV, and the web at home.nest.com. Compatible devices and video history begin automatic transfer to the Google Home app. Nest Aware (1st gen) subscriptions switch to Google Home Premium Advanced at no extra cost.
- **March 25, 2027**: Final deadline to migrate an old Nest account to a Google Account. After this, Google begins deleting unlinked Nest accounts, data, and subscriptions.
- **August 2, 2027**: Google Assistant retires on speakers and smart displays. Gemini for Home replaces it. You must accept the switch in the Google Home app or devices stop responding.

These changes complete Google’s multi-year move to a single app and voice experience.

## What you need before the Nest app ends

Download the Google Home app from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Sign in with a Google Account. If you created your Nest account after August 19, 2019, you already use a Google Account. Older accounts need a one-time migration.

Download any saved video clips from home.nest.com before January 25. Use the “Download All” button. Recent event history and 24/7 recordings transfer automatically once devices move.

Supported devices include Nest Learning Thermostats from 2015 onward, Nest Thermostat E, Nest Thermostat (2020), Nest Temperature Sensors, Nest Cameras and Doorbells from 2015 onward (including IQ models), Nest Protect (2nd gen), and Nest x Yale Lock. No hardware is discontinued.

## How to migrate your Nest account and devices

1. Open the Nest app and go to Settings. Look for the option to migrate to a Google Account and complete it if available. Or visit the migration steps in Google’s support documentation.
2. Download the Google Home app and sign in with the same Google Account.
3. Open the Google Home app. Your compatible thermostats, locks, and alarms should appear automatically once the account is linked.
4. For cameras and doorbells, you can transfer them manually now or wait for the automatic transfer that starts January 25. In the Nest app, look for transfer prompts on individual cameras. In Google Home, enroll in Public Preview if needed for early access to full features.
5. Verify live view, history, schedules, and settings in the Google Home app. Test notifications.

You do not need to rescan QR codes or climb ladders. Google handles the transfer once accounts are linked.

For detailed camera transfer steps, many users follow walkthroughs that show the in-app prompts and resulting history view in Google Home.

## Switching speakers and displays to Gemini for Home

Gemini for Home is already available in early access for many users. It replaces the scripted commands of Google Assistant with more natural conversation, context memory, and multi-intent requests. Basic control stays free. Gemini Live, advanced camera search, and Home Brief require Google Home Premium.

To prepare:

1. Open the Google Home app.
2. Tap your profile picture, then Home settings, then Early access (if not already enrolled).
3. When the “Introducing Gemini for Home” or setup banner appears, tap it and follow the prompts. Choose a voice and response settings.
4. Confirm the switch. All compatible speakers and displays in the home update. You cannot revert to Assistant after this.

If you miss the notification, check Home settings > Gemini for Home voice assistant. Update firmware on older devices if needed. See our earlier guide on [how to set up Gemini for Home on Nest speakers](/blog/how-to-set-up-gemini-for-home-nest-speakers/) for full early-access steps and compatible models.

By August 2, 2027, you must complete the switch or speakers will stop responding to voice commands.

## What changes in the Google Home app

The unified app brings Nest devices together with Google Home speakers, displays, Matter devices, and TV streamers. You gain:

- Customizable Favorites for quick camera feeds and controls.
- Ask Home for natural-language commands and automation creation.
- Access from more surfaces: Android/iOS widgets, Home Panel, smart displays, speakers, and home.google.com.
- Improved camera event descriptions (audio detection like barking or glass breaking on Premium Advanced).
- Faster everyday voice responses—up to 20% quicker in recent updates.

Nest Aware (1st gen) moves to Google Home Premium Advanced automatically. AI Pro and Ultra subscribers receive Premium plans as part of their existing subscription.

## Tips to avoid problems during the transition

- Migrate the account early if you still use a Nest password. Test the Google Home app for a week before January.
- Download clips now; do not wait until the last day.
- Update the Google Home app and device firmware.
- Enable notifications in Google Home so you do not miss transfer or Gemini prompts.
- For Workspace accounts, note that some features may be limited; a personal Gmail account gives full access.
- If devices do not appear, confirm the same Google Account is used and wait for the automatic transfer window.

After March 25, 2027, unlinked Nest data is deleted. Act before then if you need exports.

## Conclusion

The Nest app ends on January 25, 2027, and Google Assistant on home devices follows in August. Download the Google Home app, link your account, save any custom clips, and opt into Gemini for Home when ready. Your hardware continues to work; only the app and voice layer change.

Complete the steps now so remote access and notifications remain available without interruption. Check Google’s official Nest app transition support page for the latest device list and any regional notes.
