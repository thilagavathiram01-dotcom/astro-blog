Google Search AI Mode now lets you opt in to connect more personal apps for responses that draw on your own context. Starting November 23, 2026, Wallet is rolling out first, with Drive and the Gemini app following in the coming weeks. These join existing options like Gmail, Calendar, and Photos.

This guide shows how to turn on the connections, what each app adds, and how to stay in control. All steps come from Google’s official announcement and support pages.

## What Personal Intelligence Adds in AI Mode

Personal Intelligence brings your data into AI Mode answers only after you opt in. Google says the goal is more helpful responses that combine the web with insights relevant to you.

With Wallet connected, AI Mode can map financial milestones, suggest ways to trim everyday spending, and help optimize credit card benefits such as cash-back categories, travel perks, and reward points based on cards linked in Wallet. Access is read-only: AI Mode can read account information but cannot move, manage, or invest money.

Drive will let AI Mode pull from your Docs, Sheets, and PDFs. One example Google gives is building a personalized plan for tax season from files you already store.

The Gemini app connection will share context both ways. An idea you explore in the Gemini app can inform a later AI Mode response, and vice versa. You could already connect Search history to Personal Intelligence in the Gemini app; the reverse link is new.

You also start seeing more personally relevant responses in AI Overviews and AI Mode based on recent search context. If you researched a trip, a new thread can pick up where you left off without repeating details.

## How to Connect Apps to Personal Intelligence

Connections are off by default. You choose what to link and can turn any of them off later.

1. Sign in to a personal Google Account (not a Workspace business, school, or enterprise account). You must be 18 or older.
2. Open Search Personalization. You can reach it from Search Settings or directly at the Connected Apps page Google links in its announcement: myactivity.google.com/search-services/apps.
3. Select Connected Apps.
4. Choose the apps you want AI Mode to use. Wallet appears first as it rolls out. Drive and Gemini will show when available.
5. Confirm the connection. Nothing links until you opt in.

To turn personalization off entirely, visit google.com/search-personalization or adjust the setting in Search Settings. Google notes that Personalized Recommendations must be on for some features.

A Personalization icon will appear under tailored responses soon. Tap it to see which context AI Mode used. If something is wrong, correct it in the thread. For example, reply “I’m no longer a vegetarian.”

## What You Can Ask After Connecting

With Wallet active, try prompts like:

- Show my spending trends over the last three months and suggest cuts.
- Which of my linked cards gives the best cash-back on groceries right now?
- Map a plan to save for a specific purchase based on my recent activity.

When Drive arrives, you can ask AI Mode to pull from a specific document or spreadsheet for planning. With the Gemini link, start a project in the Gemini app, then continue the same thread in Search AI Mode without re-explaining the background.

Google emphasizes that you stay in control. You can disconnect any app at any time from the same Connected Apps screen.

## Privacy and Limits

Google states that connecting Wallet is read-only. AI Mode cannot transfer funds or change accounts. The same principle applies to other connected apps: they supply context for answers, not actions that move your data outside the read permission.

Availability is limited. Wallet is rolling out, while Drive and Gemini follow later. Some features, including certain Drive and Calendar uses, are listed as US English only in help pages. Check your account settings if an app does not appear.

These connections are separate from Gemini app’s own Wallet link, which already lets the assistant review spending insights inside chats. The AI Mode version brings that context into Search.

For more on how Gemini Intelligence works across Android devices and features like Rambler or task automation, see our guide to [using Gemini Intelligence features on Android](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-intelligence-features-on-android/).

## Tips for Useful Results

- Start with a clear goal. “Review last quarter’s Wallet spending and list the top three categories to reduce” works better than a vague request.
- Connect only the apps you need. You can add Drive later when the option appears without linking everything at once.
- Use the Personalization icon once it shows to verify which data shaped an answer.
- Correct facts in the conversation. AI Mode updates based on what you tell it.
- Review your Search personalization settings periodically if you want to limit recent-search context in new threads.

## Conclusion

Connecting Wallet, Drive, and Gemini to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode gives Search answers that can reference your spending, documents, and chats after a deliberate opt-in. Wallet is available to connect now for many users. Drive and the Gemini app arrive in the coming weeks.

Follow the steps in Search Personalization > Connected Apps to turn on only what you want. You can disconnect at any time. The feature is designed so you control the data that informs responses.
