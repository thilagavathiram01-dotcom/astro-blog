Samsung and Google’s Intelligent Eyewear audio glasses open for pre-order on November 12, 2026. Warby Parker announced the date during its third-quarter earnings call. The glasses run Android XR with Gemini, ship in mid-December, and start in the US and South Korea.

These are audio-first glasses without an in-lens display. They include a camera, microphones, and private speakers so you can talk to Gemini, take photos, and receive spoken help without pulling out your phone.

## Key Specs and Partners

Samsung manufactures the hardware. It uses the Qualcomm Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1 chipset. Frames come from Warby Parker and Gentle Monster. Google supplies Android XR and Gemini.

Confirmed details include up to 9 hours of battery life plus additional charges from a case. Controls use “Hey Google” voice commands or a tap on the side of the frame. The glasses pair with Android phones and iPhones. No display appears in the first models; display versions arrive later.

Warby Parker will reveal the full collection, colors, lens options, styles, and pricing on November 12. Demos start in 65 US stores the week before and expand to more than 360 locations afterward.

## Gemini Features on Intelligent Eyewear

Gemini answers questions about what is in front of you using the camera. You can get directions, send texts, hear summaries of missed messages, play music, and take photos. Real-time translation of speech and writing is supported.

Samsung adds Galaxy-specific options such as capturing a whiteboard or meeting notes directly into Samsung Notes. Gesture controls appear only with a Galaxy Watch9 according to partner reports.

The experience stays hands-free. You ask Gemini for help and receive spoken responses through the private speakers. Photos can be edited with Nano Banana-style commands in later demos, such as taking a picture and transforming it.

## How to Pre-Order

1. Visit the Warby Parker website on November 12, 2026. The full collection and pricing appear that day.
2. Choose your frame style from the Warby Parker or Gentle Monster designs. Options include different shapes, colors, and prescription lenses through Warby Parker’s labs.
3. Place your pre-order. Initial shipments begin mid-December 2026 for the holiday season.
4. If you prefer to try them first, schedule a demo at a Warby Parker store. Availability starts the week of November 12 in select locations and expands quickly.

Pre-order and delivery dates for other countries will likely follow the same announcement. Initial sales focus on the US and South Korea before wider expansion in 2027.

## Setup and Compatibility Tips

Pair the glasses with your phone via Bluetooth after unboxing. Android XR handles the connection. Sign in with your Google account so Gemini can access your data where permitted. For Galaxy users, link to Samsung Notes for automatic photo organization.

Test voice commands in a quiet environment first. The touch area on the frame provides a backup if voice is not practical. Charge the glasses and the case fully before first use to confirm the reported 9-hour runtime.

These glasses differ from the wired XREAL AURA display glasses already covered in our [XREAL AURA: First Android XR Glasses Guide](/blog/xreal-aura-android-xr-glasses-guide/). Intelligent Eyewear prioritizes everyday audio and camera use over spatial windows.

## Practical Tips Before Pre-Ordering

Confirm prescription needs if you require corrective lenses. Warby Parker handles optical labs for its frames. Check battery expectations against your typical daily wear. Nine hours covers most workdays, and the case provides extra charges.

Compare the form factor to existing audio glasses such as Ray-Ban Meta. Intelligent Eyewear adds Gemini and Android XR rather than Meta AI. Pricing will determine the final comparison once Warby Parker publishes it on November 12.

Watch the official Android and Google demos for feature examples. The Android Show XR Edition and Google’s Intelligent Eyewear sneak peek show voice queries, photo capture, and translation in action.

## Conclusion

Samsung Intelligent Eyewear brings Gemini to everyday glasses without a display. Pre-orders open November 12, 2026 through Warby Parker, with mid-December shipping in the US and South Korea. Choose frames, place your order, and pair with your phone to start using camera, translation, and hands-free help.

Follow Warby Parker and Samsung channels on November 12 for the complete collection and final pricing.
