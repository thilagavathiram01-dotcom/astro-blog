Google’s November 2026 Pixel Drop brings Gemini Intelligence to the Pixel 10 series. One of the most useful additions is multi-step task automation. You can now ask Gemini to order groceries, book a ride, or place a food order without switching between apps yourself.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="0" />

This feature already worked on the Pixel 11 series. It now reaches Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold. Availability has also expanded to India, the UK, and Japan in addition to the US.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="10" />

## What Gemini Task Automation Does

Task automation lets Gemini complete multi-step jobs inside supported apps. It runs the app in a secure virtual window on your phone. You stay in control and can watch progress or stop the task at any time.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="99" />

Common examples include:

- Ordering groceries
- Booking a ride
- Placing a takeout order
- Finding a restaurant reservation

Gemini works in the background so you can continue using other apps. The first time you use it with an app, you grant screen automation permission.

## Requirements Before You Start

You need a supported device and account:

- Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, or Pixel 10 Pro Fold
- Android version that includes the November 2026 Pixel Drop
- Personal Google Account (work or school accounts are not supported)
- Age 18 or older
- Location in the US, UK, India, or Japan (Pixel 10 is not supported in South Korea for this feature)

Check for the update under Settings > System > Software updates. The Drop rolls out in stages, so it may take a few days to reach your phone.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="4" />

## Step-by-Step: Enable and Use Task Automation

1. Open the Gemini app on your Pixel 10. You can also long-press the power button or say “Hey Google.”
2. Tell Gemini what you want. Example: “Order my usual coffee from Starbucks” or “Book a ride to the airport.”
3. On first use, follow the on-screen prompts to allow screen automation. This gives the Google app permission to interact with other apps.
4. Review the action plan if Gemini asks for confirmation. It may ask follow-up questions about size, store, or address.
5. Gemini starts the task. A notification shows progress. Tap it to view details or stop the task.
6. Complete the final purchase or booking yourself when Gemini hands control back to you.

You can monitor the task live and end it early if something looks wrong. The virtual environment keeps Gemini from accessing the rest of your phone.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="105" />

## Supported Apps and Limits

Google starts with apps in food delivery, grocery, rideshare, and reservations. Exact partners vary by region. Free Gemini users typically get a limited number of automation requests per day. Higher tiers (AI Plus, Pro, Ultra) allow more.

If you also want cleaner voice input, pair this with Rambler on the same Pixel Drop. Rambler removes filler words and formats your speech into polished text inside Gboard. See our guide on [Gemini Intelligence features for Pixel](/blog/gemini-intelligence-on-pixel-10-rambler-and-tasks-guide/).

## Tips for Better Results

- Speak or type clearly. Include details such as item, size, and delivery address.
- Keep the relevant apps installed and signed in.
- Watch the progress notification. You can stop the task if Gemini chooses the wrong option.
- Confirm payment and final details yourself. Gemini prepares the order but does not complete sensitive steps without you.

## Conclusion

Gemini task automation on Pixel 10 turns repetitive errands into simple voice or text requests. The November Drop makes the feature available on last year’s flagship and expands it to more countries. Update your phone, grant the one-time permission, and try a low-stakes task such as reordering a recent meal.
