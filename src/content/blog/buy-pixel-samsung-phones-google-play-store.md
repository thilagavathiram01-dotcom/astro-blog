Google added a Shop devices option to the Play Store in mid-October 2026. You can now discover Pixel phones, Samsung Galaxy models, watches, and other hardware listed on the Google Store without leaving the Play app.

The Play Store acts only as the discovery layer. Checkout, payment, delivery, and support run through Google Store. Availability varies by region and account. Some markets show only Pixel devices. Others include Galaxy S26 series phones and select watches.

## What Google Confirmed

Google’s Play Help page states: “You can now discover and shop for hardware devices, such as Pixel or Samsung smartphones listed for sale on Google Store, on Google Play.” When you buy hardware featured on Play, “your purchase is processed and managed on the Google Store.”

Play does not handle payment or delivery. Google Play billing policies do not apply. You use payment methods available on Google Store. Promotional codes from Play banners apply automatically at Google Store checkout.

Reports from 9to5Google, Android Authority, and others show the entry point as a “Shop devices” option in the account menu (profile icon, top right). Tapping it opens a Google Store view inside Play.

## How to Access Shop Devices

1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android phone.
2. Tap your profile picture or account icon in the top-right corner.
3. Look for “Shop devices” in the menu that appears.
4. Tap it. A page of devices loads inside the app.
5. Browse the listed Pixel and, where available, Samsung Galaxy phones, watches, and accessories.
6. Select a device. The Google Store product page opens so you can choose color, storage, and options.
7. Complete checkout on Google Store.

If you do not see the option, the feature is not yet available in your region or on your account. Update the Play Store app and try again later. In India, early reports showed Pixel devices only. Other regions listed Galaxy S26 and S26 Ultra alongside Pixel models.

## Devices That Appear

Reported listings include:

- Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro Fold, Pixel 10a
- Pixel Watch 4 or later models
- Pixel Buds and Google TV Streamer
- Galaxy S26, Galaxy S26 Ultra, Galaxy Z Flip 8
- Galaxy Watch models

The exact catalog changes by market and over time. Prices match regular Google Store or Samsung retail pricing. No special discounts were widely reported at launch. Eligible Google One subscribers can still receive the usual Google Store credit benefits on qualifying purchases.

## What Happens After You Tap a Device

Selecting a product takes you to the Google Store experience. You configure the device, apply any available trade-in or offers, and pay with Google Store payment methods. Order tracking, returns, and support go through Google Store Help, not Play Support.

Google notes that Play serves as the entry point and discovery provider. Promotional banners can deep-link into the specialized Store view. Your purchase remains governed by Google Store Terms of Sale for Devices.

## Tips Before You Buy

Check the device list on your own account rather than relying on screenshots. Samsung devices appear in some markets and not others. Compare the price against the official Google Store and Samsung site in case regional offers differ.

If you already hold Google Store credit, it works on both Pixel and listed Samsung devices through this path. Confirm trade-in eligibility and shipping options on the Google Store page before you finish the order.

## Limitations and Next Steps

The Shop devices section is a convenience layer, not a full marketplace. Only devices listed for sale on the Google Store appear. Other Android brands are not included at launch. Google has not published a full country list; the rollout is gradual.

If the option is missing, continue using the Google Store website or Samsung’s own channels. Update Play Store, clear cache if needed, and recheck after a day or two as the feature expands.
