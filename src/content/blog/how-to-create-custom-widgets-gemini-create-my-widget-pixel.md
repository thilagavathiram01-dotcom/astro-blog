Google’s November 2026 Pixel Drop brings Create My Widget to eligible Pixel phones. This feature lets you describe a home screen widget in plain language and have Gemini build it for you.

No coding is required. You get at-a-glance information tailored to your needs, such as a trip countdown or monthly spending tracker.

The tool is available on Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 series devices (excluding the Pixel 10a) for users aged 18 or older. It works in English in Australia, Canada, India, the United States, and the United Kingdom.

## Check eligibility and prerequisites

Before you start, confirm your phone qualifies. The feature rolls out with the November Pixel Drop system update. Update your phone and apps if needed.

Create My Widget can pull data from Search, Gmail, and Calendar when you connect Google Workspace. Some higher usage may require a subscription. Always review generated results for accuracy.

Note that Gemini Connected Apps integration is not available in the UK. Manage connected apps in the Create My Widget settings after setup.

## Create a widget from a prompt

Follow these steps to build a widget from scratch:

1. On your Home screen, touch and hold an empty space.
2. Tap **Widgets**, then tap **Create**.
3. In the text box, tap **Describe your widget**.
4. Type what you want. Examples include “Show me the next soccer game” or “Countdown to my vacation on July 15.”
5. Tap **Send**.
6. Review the preview. Tap **+ Add** to place it on your Home screen.

If the widget needs access to device or app data, tap **Allow** when prompted.

You can also start from a template. After tapping Create, select a category such as Important dates, Sports & fitness, or Finance. Customize the details if you want, then tap Add.

## Edit an existing custom widget

You do not need to start over if the first result is not perfect.

During creation, on the preview screen tap **Edit**. Type the changes you want and tap **Send**. Review the updated preview and tap **+ Add** when satisfied.

After the widget is on your Home screen, touch and hold it. Tap **Edit**. Enter updates in the text box and tap **Send**.

This keeps your home screen organized without cluttering it with multiple versions.

## Tips for better results

Be specific in your prompt. Include details such as dates, team colors, or the exact metric you want to track. Supplying an event date yourself helps the tool focus on the display.

Test simple widgets first, such as a countdown with a known date. Compare the result against your calendar or another source before relying on it.

Widgets that show personal data require you to connect Google Workspace during setup. You can manage these connections later in the app settings.

Create My Widget is also coming to Pixel Watch 4 and Pixel Watch 5 in select regions with English support.
