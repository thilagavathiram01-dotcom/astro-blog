Google began rolling out Personal Intelligence for Gemini on Android Auto on October 20, 2026. The feature lets the assistant pull relevant details from your connected Google apps so you can speak naturally instead of reciting addresses or flight numbers.

Drivers already using Gemini in the car gain more useful replies without extra steps. You stay in control of which apps are connected.

## What Personal Intelligence adds in the car

With permission, Gemini connects to Google Workspace (Gmail and Calendar), Photos, Maps, YouTube, and Search. It can retrieve a confirmation email, locate a place from a photo, or pull an appointment time, then combine that information into a single answer.

Examples from Google include asking for your flight gate and status on the way to the airport, requesting navigation to a doctor's appointment, or finding a car wash from a photo taken last week. The assistant handles the lookup so you keep your hands on the wheel.

Cars with Google built-in receive the same capability in the coming months. Personal responses appear only when your phone is connected.

## Requirements and availability

You need an Android phone running Android 11 or newer with at least 2 GB of RAM, Gemini set as the default assistant, and a car that supports Android Auto. The feature is available to personal Google accounts for users aged 18 or older.

It is not available on Workspace business, enterprise, or education accounts, nor in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, or Nigeria. If you already enabled Personal Intelligence in the Gemini app, the setting carries over automatically.

## How to turn it on

1. Open the Gemini app on the phone you connect to the car.
2. Tap your profile picture, then Settings.
3. Select Personal Intelligence (or Connected Apps).
4. Choose which services to enable: Workspace, Photos, Maps, YouTube, or Search.
5. Connect the phone to Android Auto and say “Hey Google” or tap the microphone.

You can disable the feature or individual apps at any time in the same settings screen. Navigation, music, calls, and general questions continue to work even when Personal Intelligence is off; answers simply will not be tailored.

## Useful voice requests to try

Once enabled, try natural phrases:

- “What’s my flight gate and is the flight on time?”
- “Take me to my doctor’s appointment.”
- “Find the car wash from the photo I took last week.”
- “Find me an indoor restaurant along my route that matches my usual vibe.”

Gemini uses the connected data to fill in the missing details. Keep Activity and the relevant history settings enabled for the fullest results.

## Privacy controls

Personal Intelligence is off by default. Apps are not connected until you turn the feature on. Cars with Google built-in share personal responses only while your phone is connected.

Google states it does not use your personal data to train Gemini models. You can review or change connected apps anytime. For more on the broader feature, see our guide on [how to use Gemini Personal Intelligence on Android Auto](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-personal-intelligence-android-auto/).

## Tips for the best experience

Enable only the apps you need. Calendar and Gmail cover most appointment and travel queries. Photos is useful for location reminders but requires Face Groups and location estimation in some regions.

Speak clearly and keep requests focused. Gemini can handle multi-part questions, but shorter phrases reduce the chance of mishearing while driving.

Check that “Hey Google” and Voice Match are active on the phone so the wake word works reliably in the car.

## Conclusion

Personal Intelligence makes Gemini in Android Auto more practical for everyday driving. A short setup in the Gemini app gives you tailored answers from the services you already use, while leaving the core driving features intact if you prefer not to connect them.

Try one or two connected apps on your next trip and adjust as needed. The rollout continues, with full support for cars with Google built-in arriving soon.
