Google now lets eligible users create and manage Gemini Tasks through text messages in Google Messages. This setup turns your phone's messaging app into a direct line for handing off work to Gemini.

You no longer need to open the Gemini app every time you want to start a task, check progress, or add details. Once configured, Gemini can send updates and you can reply in the same thread.

This guide follows the official steps from Google's support documentation for setting up text messaging with Gemini.

## Requirements Before You Start

You must meet every condition listed by Google. Missing one blocks the setup.

- Personal Google Account based in the US
- English set as your Google Account language
- Access to Tasks in Gemini Apps (available free in the US and several other countries)
- US phone number on an eligible mobile carrier
- Google Messages installed from the Play Store
- RCS chats turned on in Google Messages
- Same phone number used for both Gemini and Google Messages
- Same Google Account signed in to Gemini Apps and Google Messages

Texts with Gemini are not end-to-end encrypted. Your carrier may access them under its own privacy policy. Deleting Gemini Apps Activity does not remove the messages from your phone or carrier records.

## Enable Tasks in the Gemini App First

Text messaging only works if Tasks is already available on your account. In supported countries, free users see a Chat/Task toggle in the Gemini app or at gemini.google.com.

Open the Gemini app. Look above or below the prompt box for the Task option. Switch to Task mode and confirm you can describe a job. If the option is missing, check that your account meets the age and region rules (18+ and in a supported country).

## Set Up Text Messaging with Gemini

Follow these steps exactly as documented by Google:

1. On your phone or tablet, open the Gemini mobile app or go to gemini.google.com.
2. At the top, tap Menu.
3. At the bottom, tap Settings, then Channels.
4. Next to "Text with Gemini," tap Set up.
5. Select the phone number you want to use or tap Add a phone number.
6. Verify your phone number if prompted.
7. Follow any additional on-screen instructions.

After setup finishes, Gemini sends you a text message. That thread becomes the place to create new tasks, request status updates, or discuss an existing task.

The conversation appears in Google Messages as an RCS Business Messaging thread. In recent beta versions of Google Messages, the floating Gemini button may also route to the Channels setup page or open the chat directly once linked.

## What You Can Do in the Text Thread

Once the connection is active you can:

- Start a new task by describing what you need
- Ask for the current status of a running task
- Add more details or change direction mid-task
- Attach photos, files, or location data (each attachment is limited to 15 MB)

Tasks created through text show up in the Gemini app's side panel as a dedicated task thread. You stay in control: Gemini is designed to pause and request confirmation before major actions such as sending messages, making bookings, or completing purchases.

## Proactive Updates in Google Messages

Users who have completed the setup report that Gemini can send unsolicited but useful messages. These include morning briefings based on your calendar, price-drop alerts, subscription reminders, and package delivery updates.

This behavior appears in the latest Google Messages beta (version 20261120_01_RC00 and similar builds) for US users who have Tasks enabled. The older Gemini chat button in Messages remains available for regular conversations and is separate from the Tasks channel.

## Remove Your Number or Stop Messages

To stop receiving texts from Gemini:

1. Open the Gemini app or gemini.google.com.
2. Go to Menu > Settings > Channels.
3. On the Text with Gemini card, tap Remove next to the phone number.

You can also type STOP in the text chat to opt out, or START to opt back in.

Deleting the chat thread in Messages does not erase the activity record in Gemini Apps. Clearing Gemini Apps Activity does not delete the messages stored by your carrier.

## Common Issues and Fixes

**Carrier not supported**  
Some US carriers are not eligible. Google Voice numbers are not supported at launch. If you see an error about unsupported numbers, try a different US carrier number linked to the same Google Account.

**No verification text arrives**  
Confirm RCS is enabled in Google Messages settings. Wait a few minutes and check spam or blocked-sender folders. Restart the Messages app and try the setup again.

**Button still opens the old chat**  
If you are on a stable (non-beta) build of Google Messages, the floating button may continue to open the classic Gemini conversation. Complete the Channels setup in the Gemini app first; the Tasks thread will still appear once Gemini texts you.

**Tasks option missing**  
Verify your account is personal (not work or school), set to English, based in a supported country, and that you are 18 or older. Tasks is free in the US, Canada, Australia, India, Japan, and South Korea; elsewhere it requires a Google AI Pro or Ultra plan.

## Tips for Better Results

Write clear instructions that include limits. For example: "Find the best price for wireless earbuds under $80 and add the top option to my cart, but do not buy anything."

Keep the same phone number and Google Account across both apps. Switching accounts mid-setup forces you to start over.

Check the Channels page periodically if you add or change phone numbers.

Because the channel is not end-to-end encrypted, avoid sending highly sensitive personal information through text.

## Conclusion

Setting up text messaging with Gemini gives you a low-friction way to hand off work and receive updates without opening a separate app. The process is straightforward once you meet the US account, carrier, RCS, and Tasks requirements.

Start by confirming Tasks is available in your Gemini app, then follow the Channels setup steps. After Gemini sends the first text, you can create tasks, track them, and reply all from Google Messages.

For the latest official instructions, always check the Google Messages support page on creating tasks with Gemini.
