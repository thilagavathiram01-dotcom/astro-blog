Google lets eligible US users text Gemini directly to create, track, and discuss Tasks. The feature uses RCS Business Messaging inside Google Messages and appears as a dedicated chat once you verify your number.

This setup turns your messaging app into a channel for background work. Tasks you start by text show up as new threads in the Gemini app side panel. You stay in control because Gemini asks for confirmation before major actions.

## Requirements Before You Start

You need a personal Google Account based in the US with English set as the account language. Tasks must be available in the Gemini app (free in the US and five other countries as of November 2026). Use a US phone number on an eligible carrier. Install Google Messages, turn on RCS chats, and sign in with the same Google Account and phone number in both apps.

Texts with Gemini are not end-to-end encrypted. Your carrier may access them under its own policies. Deleting Gemini Apps Activity does not remove the messages from Google Messages or your carrier.

## Set Up Text Messaging with Gemini

1. Open the Gemini mobile app or go to gemini.google.com on your phone or tablet and sign in.
2. Tap the menu at the top.
3. Tap Settings at the bottom, then Channels.
4. Next to Text with Gemini, tap Set up.
5. Select the phone number you want to use or tap Add a phone number.
6. Verify the number if prompted.
7. Follow any remaining on-screen steps.

After setup, Gemini sends you a text. From that chat you can create a new task, check status on an existing one, or discuss details. Attachments such as photos, files, or location are allowed up to 15 MB each.

For the base Channels setup that powers both text and email, see our earlier guide: [How to Text and Email Gemini Tasks Free in the US](/blog/how-to-text-email-gemini-tasks-channels/).

## What You Can Do in the Chat

Once the conversation is open in Google Messages, treat it like any other RCS chat. Send a request such as “Track package deliveries this week and text me when they arrive” or “Compare prices on wireless earbuds under $80 and suggest options.” Gemini can also send proactive updates once Tasks are enabled, including morning briefings, price-drop alerts, subscription reminders, and delivery notices (currently observed in the latest Google Messages beta for US users).

You can reply mid-task to change direction. Gemini pauses for a one-tap confirmation before sending messages, booking, or taking other major steps.

## Tips for Reliable Use

Enable only the Connected Apps the task needs. Set a spending cap before any shopping-related request. Provide thumbs-down feedback when a task misses the mark so Google can improve reliability. Review privacy controls for chat history and model training separately in Gemini Settings; those controls continue to roll out independently.

If you no longer want texts, open Channels settings and remove the phone number. You can also text STOP to opt out or START to opt back in from inside the chat.

## Remove Your Number or Fix Issues

To stop communications: open Gemini, go to Settings > Channels, and tap Remove next to the phone number. Deleting the text chat in Google Messages does not delete the corresponding activity from Gemini Apps Activity. Deleting activity from Gemini does not remove the messages from your messaging app or carrier.

Common issues include verification texts that do not arrive (check carrier support) or an error that your carrier is not eligible. In those cases stay with the Gemini app Tasks toggle for the same background work.

## Conclusion

Setting up text messaging with Gemini gives US users a lightweight way to hand off multi-step work without opening the full app every time. Verify the requirements, complete the short Channels flow, and start with a simple task to confirm everything works. Check the official Google Messages help page for the latest eligibility details, as the feature remains limited to the US and eligible carriers.
