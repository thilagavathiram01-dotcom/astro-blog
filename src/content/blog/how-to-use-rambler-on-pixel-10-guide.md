Rambler arrived on Pixel 10 series phones with the November 2026 Pixel Drop. It turns natural speech into clean, formatted text by removing filler words and handling mid-sentence corrections.

This guide shows how to turn it on, use it in any app, and get better results on supported Pixel 10 devices.

![Person holding a modern smartphone](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1511707171634-5f897ff02aa9?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What Rambler Does on Pixel 10

Rambler is an upgraded voice typing mode in Gboard. You speak the way you normally talk. It removes words like “um” and “uh,” fixes grammar and punctuation, and lets you edit with voice commands.

Google introduced it first on the Pixel 11 series. The November Pixel Drop brings it to Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold. It also reaches the Motorola Signature 27.

You need the latest system update and a recent version of Gboard. Availability depends on your region and language. Check Google’s Pixel Help pages for the current list of supported languages.

For the full set of Gemini Intelligence tools on these phones, see our [Gemini Intelligence guide for Pixel 10](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-intelligence-on-pixel-10/).

## Confirm Your Phone Supports It

Follow these checks before you start:

1. Open Settings > System > System update and install any available November Pixel Drop update.
2. Make sure Gboard is your default keyboard. Go to Settings > System > Languages & input > On-screen keyboard.
3. Update Gboard from the Play Store.
4. Confirm your device is a Pixel 10, 10 Pro, 10 Pro XL, or 10 Pro Fold. The Pixel 10a is not included.

Rambler works in any text field where Gboard appears, including Messages, Gmail, Docs, and third-party apps.

## Enable Rambler in Gboard

Turn on the feature with these steps:

1. Open any app and tap a text field so Gboard appears.
2. Tap the microphone icon in the top-right corner of the keyboard.
3. If a dialog appears, tap Start to enable Rambler.
4. If no dialog shows, open Gboard settings (gear icon), go to Voice typing, and select Rambler.

Grant microphone permission to Gboard if prompted. Choose “Allow only while using the app.”

Once active, the microphone icon shows a sparkle. Speak and tap Done when finished. The cleaned text appears in the field.

## Use Voice Commands While Dictating

Rambler accepts natural editing commands while you speak:

- Say “make it sound friendlier” or “add a smiley” to adjust tone.
- Correct yourself mid-sentence. Rambler keeps the final version.
- Mix languages in one message if your supported languages allow it.

Speak at a normal pace. You do not need perfect pauses for punctuation. Review the result before you send, because the model can still make mistakes.

## Tips for Better Results

- Use it for longer messages first. Short replies may not need the cleanup.
- Keep background noise low for clearer recognition.
- If the output looks wrong, tap the text and edit manually or try the command again.
- Pair it with task automation on the same Pixel Drop update for hands-free daily work.

Google notes that usage limits can apply depending on your account.

## Troubleshooting

**Rambler option missing**  
Update Gboard and your system. Restart the phone and check Voice typing settings again.

**Microphone icon looks normal**  
Open Gboard settings and confirm Rambler is selected under Voice typing.

**Text does not clean up**  
Make sure you tap Done after speaking. The processing happens after you stop.

**Not available in my country**  
Feature rollouts vary. Check Settings or Google’s support page for regional status.

## Conclusion

Rambler on Pixel 10 removes the extra editing step from voice messages. Speak naturally, review the polished text, and send. Start with a message to a friend or a note in Keep to see the difference.
