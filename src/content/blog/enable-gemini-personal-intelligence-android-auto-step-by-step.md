Google rolled out Personal Intelligence for Gemini in Android Auto on October 20, 2026. The feature lets the assistant pull details from your Gmail, Calendar, Photos, Maps, YouTube, and Search so you can ask natural questions while driving. You stay in control because it is opt-in and you choose which apps to connect.

![Driver using navigation on a smartphone mounted in the car](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1600320254374-ce2d293c324e?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

Personal Intelligence works on Android Auto first. Cars with Google built-in receive it in the coming months. It is available to personal Google accounts for users aged 18 and older. Workspace, business, enterprise, and education accounts are excluded. The feature is not yet available in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, or Nigeria.

## What Personal Intelligence does in the car

Gemini can retrieve specific details and reason across sources without you spelling everything out. On the way to the airport you can ask “What’s my flight gate and is my flight on time?” Gemini checks the confirmation email and the current status in one response. You can say “Take me to my doctor’s appointment” and it pulls the address from Calendar. You can also ask it to find the car wash from a photo you took last week and start navigation.

If you already turned Personal Intelligence on in the Gemini app, the same connected apps come with you into the car. If you have not, you set it up in Gemini settings and the preferences apply on Android Auto.

## How to enable Personal Intelligence

Follow these steps on your phone before you connect to the car.

1. Open the Gemini app or go to gemini.google.com.
2. Tap your profile picture, then Settings.
3. Select Personal Intelligence.
4. Choose Connected Apps and toggle on the services you want to share (Gmail, Calendar under Workspace, Photos, Maps, YouTube, Search).
5. Confirm any permission prompts. You can change or turn off access later from the same menu.

Once connected, start Android Auto as usual. Ask Gemini a question that needs personal context. If the feature is active you will see tailored answers. If not, Gemini still handles navigation, music, calls, and general questions.

## Privacy controls

Google states that apps stay disconnected until you enable Personal Intelligence. You can turn the feature off at any time in Gemini settings. For cars with Google built-in, personal responses are shared only when your phone is connected in the vehicle. Sensitive data remains under your control, and Google’s privacy documentation covers the processing details.

## Tips for better results

- Connect only the apps you actually need at first so you can judge accuracy.
- Use clear spoken requests that reference a recent email, photo, or calendar item.
- Double-check important details such as flight times or addresses, because AI responses can contain errors.
- If you drive in a restricted region, the feature will not appear until Google expands availability.

## Conclusion

Personal Intelligence makes Gemini more useful behind the wheel by removing the need to dig through emails or photos while you drive. Enable it once in the Gemini app, select the apps you trust, and the same settings travel with you into Android Auto. The feature remains optional, so you can keep the standard experience or add personal context when it helps.
