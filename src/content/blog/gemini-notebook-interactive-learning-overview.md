Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM) now includes Interactive Learning Overviews under the Reports section. These overviews turn your uploaded sources into structured walkthroughs that mix written summaries with suggested Studio outputs such as mind maps, flashcards, quizzes, and slides.

Google highlighted the feature in its October 16, 2026 update on study tools. It is available on the web for users 18 and older. The content stays grounded in the sources you add, so the overview draws only from your materials.

## Prepare Your Notebook

1. Open notebook.google.com and sign in.
2. Create a new notebook or open an existing one you can edit.
3. Add sources: upload PDFs, Google Docs, Slides, paste text, or add YouTube and web links.
4. Aim for focused sources that cover the topic you want to study. Quality matters more than quantity.

Once sources are ready, the Studio panel on the right becomes the starting point for reports.

## Generate the Interactive Learning Overview

1. In the Studio panel, select Reports.
2. Choose Interactive (not Document).
3. Under Template, select Learning Overview.
4. Select Generate now, or choose Generate later if you want to wait for usage limits to reset.

Generation can take a few minutes depending on the number and size of your sources. The finished overview appears in the Studio panel. Open it to see a table of contents on the left and sectioned content in the main area.

Each section includes a summary plus suggested Studio artifacts. Select Add under any suggestion to generate that artifact (for example, flashcards or a mind map) and embed it. Generated artifacts also show up separately in the Studio panel for later use.

## Customize Before or After Generation

Next to the template name, select the edit icon. Adjust the prompt to change structure, language level, focus areas, or where certain artifacts appear. Then choose Generate now or Generate later.

After generation, you can still refine individual artifacts. For quizzes, new formats include short-answer, multi-select, and fill-in-the-blank questions. After you complete a quiz or review flashcards, ask the notebook chat for feedback on weak areas.

## Tips for Better Results

- Start with 3–8 strong sources that cover the core concepts without heavy overlap.
- Use the customize option to request a beginner-friendly tone or to emphasize specific chapters.
- Generate the overview first, then selectively add only the most useful Studio pieces (mind map for overview, flashcards for key terms, quiz at the end).
- Review every generated section for accuracy. AI outputs can contain mistakes even when grounded in sources.
- Export useful pieces: reports can go to Google Docs, and data tables can go to Sheets.

## Limitations to Know

Interactive reports are currently web-only. Some advanced content requires an age of 18 or older. Free accounts have usage limits that reset periodically; paid Google AI plans raise those ceilings. Generation time varies, and complex source sets can take longer.

The feature works best when sources are clean and relevant. Irrelevant or contradictory documents can reduce the quality of the overview and suggested artifacts.

## Conclusion

Interactive Learning Overviews give you a ready-made study path from the materials you already have. Create or open a notebook, add sources, generate the Learning Overview under Reports > Interactive, then embed the Studio pieces that help most. Review the output, customize as needed, and use the quiz feedback to focus your next session.

Start with one course or topic today at notebook.google.com. The combination of structured summaries and on-demand artifacts turns scattered notes into a single interactive study hub.
