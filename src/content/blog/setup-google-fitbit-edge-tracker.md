Google Fitbit Edge fills the gap between a basic band and a full smartwatch. It arrived for pre-order on October 12, 2026, and ships starting October 27. The tracker pairs with the Google Health app on Android or iPhone and brings a larger display, built-in GPS, and Health Coach features to a slim design.

If you just unboxed one, this guide walks through charging, pairing, and daily use so you can start tracking right away.

![Person checking a smartwatch during a workout](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-tT_V3qBCbak?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What you need before setup

Confirm your phone meets the requirements. Fitbit Edge works with Android 11 or later and iOS 16.4 or later. You also need a Google Account and the Google Health app.

Charge the tracker first. Connect the included magnetic cable. A full charge takes about 90 minutes. Five minutes of charging adds roughly a day of battery life when the level is low.

Wear it correctly for accurate readings. Slide the band through the loop, press the peg into a hole, and tuck the end. Push the band firmly until you hear a click so it stays secure.

## Pair the tracker with the Google Health app

Download the Google Health app from the Play Store on Android or the App Store on iPhone. Open it and sign in with your Google Account. If you do not have one, create it during the process.

Go to Connections in the app. Tap Add a device or Add more devices. Select Fitbit Edge from the list and follow the on-screen prompts. Keep the tracker nearby and charged. The phone will show a code to confirm on the tracker screen if needed.

Once paired, data starts syncing automatically. You can view steps, heart rate, sleep stages, and more in the app. The tracker stores up to seven days of full data if it is away from your phone.

## Start tracking workouts and daily activity

Swipe left or right on the watch face to move between tiles. These show steps, heart rate, and other metrics. Press the physical side button once to wake the screen or open the app list. A long press restarts the device.

To log a workout, open the exercise app on the tracker. Choose from more than 50 modes such as outdoor run, cycling, or strength training. The display shows real-time pace, heart rate zones, and splits. Built-in GPS tracks routes without your phone and improves accuracy by up to 40 percent over earlier Fitbit trackers.

Automatic activity detection recognizes up to 100 workout types in the background. It logs walks, rides, and other efforts even if you forget to start a session. Elevation from the altimeter appears for stairs and hills.

After a workout, check the summary on the tracker or open the Google Health app for more detail.

## Review health metrics and use Health Coach

The Edge tracks continuous heart rate, skin temperature overnight, and sleep stages. It includes an ECG app for heart rhythm assessments in supported countries. Health Guardian features watch for shifts in blood pressure trends, insulin resistance trends, and high or low heart rate alerts.

These insights appear in the Google Health app. Google Health Premium unlocks the full Health Coach experience. Coach uses Gemini to explain patterns, suggest adjustments to your plan, and give context for doctor conversations. Purchases of the Edge include a three-month Premium trial.

Swipe down on the tracker for Quick Settings. You can enable Do Not Disturb, Water Lock, or brightness controls. Manage notifications, watch faces, and tiles in the app under your device settings.

## Battery, bands, and daily care

Battery life reaches up to seven days with typical use and Battery Saver mode. Always-On Display drops this to about two days. Fast magnetic charging helps when you need a quick top-up.

The tracker is the first Fitbit with a user-replaceable battery. Options vary by region. It is water-resistant to 50 meters (5 ATM) and rated IP68 for dust.

Swap bands as needed. The box includes small and large Active Bands. Additional options such as Performance Loop, Magnet, and Metal Links are sold separately. Colors include Obsidian, Fog, Olive, and Canyon. A Stephen Curry special edition is available at a higher price.

## Tips for better results

- Keep the tracker snug but comfortable on the top of your wrist for consistent heart rate and temperature readings.
- Update the Google Health app regularly so new features and fixes arrive.
- Sync manually if data looks delayed by opening the app and pulling down on the Today screen.
- Use Water Lock before swimming to prevent accidental touches.
- Check region availability for ECG, blood oxygen, and certain Health Guardian features.

If syncing fails, restart the tracker with a long press on the side button and try pairing again. Official help pages cover charging issues and notification problems.

## Conclusion

The Google Fitbit Edge combines a lightweight design with strong tracking tools and Google Health integration. Setup takes a few minutes once the app is installed and the tracker is charged. After that, daily tiles, automatic detection, and Premium Coach features help you stay consistent with activity and recovery goals.
