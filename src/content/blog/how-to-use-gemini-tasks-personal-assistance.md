Tasks in Gemini turn the app from a chat partner into a working assistant that finishes jobs while you do other things. Google rolled out free access on November 17, 2026, for users 18 and older in the United States, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. The feature runs on the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model and works in the mobile app, the Mac app, and on gemini.google.com with a personal Google Account and chat history turned on.

You flip a toggle, describe the job, and Gemini plans the steps, connects to your apps, and keeps going in the background. It pauses for confirmation on purchases or unclear instructions. You can add more details mid-task or stop it at any time.

## What Tasks Can Handle for Personal Assistance

Gemini Tasks focuses on multi-step work that would otherwise eat your day. Official examples include calling dealerships to book a service appointment and adding it to your Google Calendar, analyzing utility bills in your email to find why a bill spiked, and comparing USB-C chargers then checking out with Google Pay after finding a discount code.

It can also sort school emails into calendar events and flag conflicts, or add items to a grocery order from a text or photo. In the US, eligible users can forward emails to gemini@google.com or text Gemini after verifying a phone number under Settings > Channels. Calls and Google Pay features are currently limited to the US for users 18 and over with a payment method in Google Wallet.

The system runs each task in a dedicated, hardware-isolated virtual machine on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine. It asks before critical actions such as clicking “Buy” and can be set to Always Allow for routine steps you trust.

## How to Start a Task

Open the Gemini app or go to gemini.google.com and sign in with a personal Google Account. Make sure Gemini Apps Activity (chat history) is on. Look for the Chat / Task toggle on the home screen. On mobile it sits above the prompt box; on the web it is below the text box.

1. Tap or click Task.
2. Describe the full job in plain language. Include any budget, deadline, or limits. Example: “Call the two nearest Honda dealerships and book the earliest Friday morning oil change for our 2019 Odyssey in the next month. Add it to my calendar.”
3. Submit. Gemini begins working in the background and shows progress steps.
4. Answer any clarifying questions. You can type additional instructions at any point and Gemini updates without losing progress.
5. Confirm key actions when it pauses. Use the Stop button if you need to halt it.

You can run up to 15 tasks at once. For recurring work, ask Gemini to schedule it (“do this every Monday morning”) or use the Scheduled page once available.

In the US you can also set up texting: open Gemini, go to Settings > Channels > Text with Gemini > Set up, and verify your phone number with an eligible carrier. Forward emails to gemini@google.com and Gemini replies with a link to the new task.

## Safety and Control Settings

Gemini is designed to check with you before critical steps. Purchase confirmation is on by default. Leave it enabled unless you have a specific reason to change it. You can set a hard spending cap for shopping tasks.

You control connected apps, can disconnect Google or third-party services, and can delete chat history at any time. The sandbox and prompt-injection filters add layers of protection. If a task misses the mark, give it a thumbs-down so Google can improve the system.

Work and school accounts are not supported for Tasks at this time. The feature is not available in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, or Nigeria.

## Practical Tips for Better Results

Write the job, not a question. “Find three USB-C chargers under $30 with good reviews and help me buy the best one” works better than “best USB-C charger?”

Include relevant context or files. You can upload documents, pull from Drive, or type @ to invoke a saved skill. For calendar-related tasks, Personal Intelligence helps Gemini use what it already knows about your schedule and preferences.

Start small. Hand off one clear chore first so you can see the confirmation flow and progress panel. Then expand to multi-app workflows such as reading emails and creating calendar events.

Monitor the work panel for completed, current, and planned steps. Jump in early if the direction drifts. For shopping, keep confirmation on and review the receipt in Google Wallet afterward.

## Conclusion

Tasks in Gemini gives you a practical way to offload the repetitive and multi-step parts of personal life. The free rollout in six countries on Gemini 4 Hydrogen makes it available without a paid plan for eligible users. Start by flipping the toggle, describe one clear job, and confirm the steps it needs you for. Over time you can build schedules and connect more of your Google apps so the assistant stays useful without constant supervision.
