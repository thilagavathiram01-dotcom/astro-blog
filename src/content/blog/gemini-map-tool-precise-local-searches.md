Google added a Map tool to the Gemini app on Android and iOS in early October 2026. It lets you pick a specific area on a live map instead of describing a location in text. You attach a Map Area chip to your prompt and ask for recommendations that stay inside that circle.

This guide shows the exact steps on a phone. It works the same on Android and iPhone. The tool is not available on the web or desktop apps yet.

## Open the Map tool

Open the Gemini app. Tap the plus (+) button next to the chat box. A carousel of tools appears. Scroll to the end past Photos, Camera, Avatar, Files, Drive, and Notebooks. Tap Map.

A live Google Maps view opens. A circle marks your current location. You can pan the map, pinch to zoom, or tap the search icon to jump to another place. Zoom in for a few blocks or out for a whole neighborhood.

![Person using smartphone with map app on screen](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1516321497487-e288fb19713f?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

When the circle covers the area you want, tap Explore this area at the bottom. Gemini adds a Map Area chip to the prompt box. Type your question after the chip and send.

## Ask for local recommendations

The Map Area limits the results to the circle you selected. Ask for restaurants, coffee shops, parks, or services inside that zone. Gemini returns cards with photos, ratings, price range, and hours when available.

Example prompts:

- Find quiet coffee shops open late in this area.
- List family-friendly parks with restrooms nearby.
- Show budget dinner options under 20 dollars that are open now.

Tap a card for review snippets and a link to open the place in Google Maps. Many answers also include a map preview at the top. Tap it to see pins, then export the list to Google Maps if you want to save it.

![City map with streets and landmarks](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1477959858617-67f85cf4f1df?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

The circle controls the search most of the time. Results stay inside the selected area unless the place is just outside and highly relevant. Test a few prompts to see the boundary.

## Tips for better results

Keep the circle tight when you want specific blocks. Widen it for neighborhood ideas. Combine the Map Area with other tools if needed, such as Photos for visual references.

The tool pairs well with existing Gemini location features. For navigation help after you pick a place, see the guide on [asking Maps questions from Gemini](/blog/gemini-ask-maps-immersive-navigation/).

Update the Gemini app if Map does not appear. The rollout reached most Android and iOS users by early October 2026.

## Conclusion

The Gemini Map tool removes the need to type partial addresses or street names. Select the area, add your prompt, and get grounded local answers. Try it on your next search for a nearby spot. The feature is free with the Gemini app on Android and iOS.
