Google Health version 5.11 arrived this week with practical upgrades for Fitbit Air, Fitbit Edge, and Pixel Watch owners. The update expands automatic activity detection, brings Health Guardian features to the Fitbit Air for Premium subscribers, and improves sleep tracking accuracy.

These changes address common complaints about missed workouts and incomplete sleep data. You do not need new hardware. Most improvements appear after updating the Google Health app on your phone.

This guide walks through what changed, how to enable the new options, and tips to get reliable results.

## What Changed in Google Health 5.11

Automatic activity tracking now recognizes up to 100 activities instead of the previous 10. Strength training, swimming, racket sports, and team sports are included. The system uses contextual reasoning from your routines, historical data, and manual edits. Google states this delivers nearly twice the accuracy and cuts missed workouts by more than half.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="95" />

Health Guardian blood pressure trends and insulin resistance trends are now available on Fitbit Air and Fitbit Edge if you have Google Health Premium. Pixel Watch 3, 4, and 5 owners already had these features. First monthly reports appear at the beginning of November if you meet the wear requirements.

Sleep tracking uses step data to better detect short awakenings. You can also delete incorrect naps directly from the nap card.

Glucose tracking receives smaller improvements: custom time-in-range goals and faster syncing with Abbott Lingo devices in the US.

## Set Up Improved Automatic Activity Tracking

The expanded activity detection is available now for Fitbit Air and Pixel Watch 3, 4, and 5. Fitbit Edge receives it at launch on October 27.

1. Update the Google Health app from the Play Store or App Store.
2. Open the app and sync your device.
3. Wear the tracker during workouts that last at least 15 minutes with an elevated heart rate.
4. After a session, check the Fitness tab. The app typically shows a summary within 30 minutes.
5. Confirm or edit the detected activity. Each correction helps the system learn your patterns.

Location data is optional. Grant it if you want the model to use place context for better detection of outdoor activities.

If a workout is missed, start it manually once and edit the details. The system improves over subsequent sessions of the same type.

For more on the earlier activity detection changes, see our guide on [upgraded automatic activity detection](/blog/how-to-use-upgraded-automatic-activity-detection-fitbit-pixel/).

## Enable Health Guardian on Fitbit Air

Health Guardian tracks longer-term patterns rather than single readings. On Fitbit Air it requires a Google Health Premium subscription.

1. Open Google Health and tap your profile icon.
2. Go to Settings or look for the Health Guardian section under the Health tab.
3. Turn on blood pressure trends and insulin resistance trends.
4. Wear the Fitbit Air consistently. Blood pressure trends need five or more consecutive days in the month. Insulin resistance trends need seven or more days and nights.
5. Check the Health tab at the start of the next month for your first report.

The reports give context you can share with a doctor. They are wellness tools, not medical diagnoses. Pixel Watch owners get the same features without the Premium requirement.

Sleep breathing quality is coming to Fitbit Air soon but is not part of the 5.11 rollout for that device yet.

## Improve Sleep Tracking Accuracy

The sleep changes focus on short awakenings and nap management.

Wear the device snugly overnight. The updated algorithm now factors step data so brief movements, such as getting up briefly, are less likely to split a sleep session incorrectly.

To remove a wrong nap:

1. Open the Sleep tab.
2. Find the nap card for that day.
3. Tap the option to remove or edit the nap.

This keeps your sleep score and stages cleaner. Review the data each morning after syncing.

## Tips for Reliable Data

Consistency matters more than occasional long sessions. Wear the device for the required consecutive days if you want Health Guardian reports.

Charge the tracker before bed so overnight tracking is not interrupted. The Fitbit Air’s long battery life helps here.

If activity detection still misses sessions after several corrections, check wrist placement and that the band is snug. Loose fit reduces heart rate accuracy, which the system uses as one signal.

Glucose users can now set personal time-in-range goals under the Metabolic section of the Health tab.

## Conclusion

Google Health 5.11 makes automatic tracking more useful without requiring new devices. Expanded activity detection reduces the need to start workouts manually. Health Guardian on Fitbit Air gives Premium subscribers monthly pattern reports. Sleep tracking handles short awakenings and nap edits more cleanly.

Update the app, wear the device regularly, and correct detections when needed. The system improves with your input.
