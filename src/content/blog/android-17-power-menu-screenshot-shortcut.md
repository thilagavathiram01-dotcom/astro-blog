Android 17 QPR3 Beta 3 adds a Screenshot button to the power menu on Pixel phones. Press Power and Volume Up together to open the menu, then tap Screenshot. The capture includes the status bar and navigation bar.

This small change helps when you press the wrong volume button while trying to take a screenshot. The classic Power + Volume Down combo still works. The new option simply gives you a second path from the menu you already reach by accident.

The feature arrived with build DP11.261016.003 (or .004 on Pixel 11 models) on October 29, 2026. It is currently in the Android Beta Program for Pixel 6a and newer. See our [Android 17 QPR3 Beta 3 features guide](/blog/android-17-qpr3-beta-3-features-guide/) for the full list of changes in this build.

## Why the power menu now has a screenshot option

Most Android phones take a screenshot with Power + Volume Down. On Pixel devices, Power + Volume Up opens the power menu. That menu has long offered Emergency, Lockdown, Power off, and Restart.

People often hit Volume Up by mistake when they mean Volume Down. The phone then shows the power menu instead of capturing the screen. You have to dismiss the menu and try the button combo again.

Android 17 QPR3 Beta 3 places a Screenshot button in that same menu. Tap it and the phone captures the full display. The button sits below the existing options so the layout stays familiar.

## How to take a screenshot from the power menu

You need a Pixel on Android 17 QPR3 Beta 3 or later. The power-menu screenshot option is not present on earlier builds.

1. Open the screen you want to capture.
2. Press and hold the Power button and the Volume Up button together until the power menu appears.
3. Tap the Screenshot button at the bottom of the menu.
4. The phone takes the screenshot immediately. A preview appears in the corner with edit and share options, the same as a normal screenshot.

The capture includes the status bar at the top and the navigation bar at the bottom. This makes it useful for bug reports or tutorials that need the complete interface.

You can still take a screenshot the usual way. Press Power and Volume Down at the same time and release quickly. Both methods save the image to the Screenshots album in Google Photos or the Files app.

If your Power button long-press is set to open Gemini or Google Assistant instead of the power menu, change the gesture first. Go to Settings > System > Gestures > Press & hold power button. Select Power menu. You can still open the menu with Power + Volume Up even when the long-press is set to the assistant.

## Other screenshot methods that still work

The power-menu option is an extra choice. It does not replace existing methods.

Power + Volume Down remains the fastest one-handed or two-handed option on every Android phone. Hold both buttons for about one second, then release.

On Pixel phones you can also double-tap the back of the device if Quick Tap is enabled and set to Take screenshot. Open Settings > System > Gestures > Quick Tap to start actions and choose Take screenshot.

From the Recents overview, some builds show a Screenshot button under an app card. Swipe up and hold, then look for the button on the focused app. This method captures the app window rather than the full system UI.

Quick Settings can include a Screenshot tile on some devices. Swipe down twice, tap the edit icon, and drag the tile into place if it is available.

## Tips for using the new shortcut

Practice the Power + Volume Up combo once so muscle memory does not fight you. The menu appears after a short hold. Tap Screenshot right away.

Use this method when you need the status bar and navigation bar included. Normal Power + Volume Down screenshots also include them on stock Android, but the power-menu path guarantees the full capture even if a system overlay is present.

If the Screenshot button does not appear, confirm you are on Android 17 QPR3 Beta 3 or newer. Check the build number in Settings > About phone. It should start with DP11.261016.

The feature is currently Pixel-specific in the beta. Other manufacturers may add a similar option when they ship Android 17. Samsung and some other brands already use different side-key shortcuts, so the power menu behaves differently on those phones.

Screenshots land in the same location as before. Open the Photos app or Files > Images > Screenshots to find them. You can edit, share, or delete from the preview that appears after capture.

## Conclusion

The Screenshot button in the Android 17 QPR3 power menu gives Pixel users a reliable backup when the classic button combo misses. Press Power + Volume Up, tap Screenshot, and the full screen is captured, including status and navigation bars. The usual Power + Volume Down method continues to work. This small addition reduces the frustration of accidental power menus during everyday screenshot attempts.
