Google expanded Tasks in the Gemini app on 17 November 2026. The feature is now free for eligible personal accounts in the United States, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia and India. It runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen and handles longer projects in the background while you continue with other work.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="30" />

Chat mode answers questions in a back-and-forth exchange. Tasks mode plans and completes multi-step jobs. You describe the outcome, Gemini works through the steps, and it checks with you before sensitive actions such as purchases or calls.

## Requirements before you start

You need a personal Google account (not a work or school account). You must be 18 or older. History must be turned on in Gemini settings. The feature is available in the Gemini mobile app (Android and iOS), the web app at gemini.google.com, and the Mac app.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="35" />

In the six countries listed above, Tasks works without a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription. In other supported regions it requires a paid plan. It is not available in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or Nigeria at the time of writing.

You can run up to 15 tasks at the same time. Each task runs in an isolated environment tied to your account.

## How to start a task

1. Open the Gemini app or go to gemini.google.com and sign in.
2. On the home screen, switch the toggle from Chat to Task.
3. Describe the job clearly. Include deadlines, budgets, constraints and what you do not want it to do.
4. Submit the task. Gemini begins working in the background.
5. You can add more instructions at any time while it runs. Tap Stop in the text box to cancel.

Example prompts that work well:

- Research three local plumbers who can visit this week and compare their prices and availability against my Google Calendar.
- Review my unpaid subscriptions from the last three months and list ones I have not used.
- Plan a weekend family itinerary in the city with activities suitable for children under ten and under a set budget.

Gemini will ask clarifying questions if details are missing. It pauses and requests confirmation before critical steps such as completing a purchase or placing a call.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="30" />

## What Tasks can do today

Tasks can research information, organise calendars, draft messages, compare options and coordinate across connected Google services such as Calendar, Gmail and Drive. It can also work with uploaded files or notebooks.

In the United States it has additional capabilities rolling out first:

- Call businesses on your behalf (with transcripts and approval before dialing).
- Complete supported purchases through Google Pay (you set a spending cap and it asks before buying).
- Accept tasks via text message or by forwarding emails to gemini@google.com.
- Sign into supported sites using credentials stored in Google Password Manager.

These advanced features are not yet available in the other five free countries. Google has not published a timeline for wider rollout.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="34" />

## How to set up texting (US only)

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Go to Settings > Channels.
3. Tap Set up under Text with Gemini.
4. Verify your phone number with an eligible US carrier.

Once verified, you can text tasks without opening the app. Email forwarding to gemini@google.com works for eligible US personal accounts.

## Tips for better results

Be specific about the end result. “Find the cheapest flight to Tokyo next month” works better than “help me plan a trip.”

Set clear limits. Tell it your maximum budget, preferred times, or brands to avoid.

Review connected apps in Gemini settings. Tasks can only use services you have authorised.

Leave purchase confirmation on unless you have a very specific recurring need and trust the process.

Check progress periodically. You can jump in with extra instructions without restarting the task.

## Privacy and controls

Tasks respects the same history and training controls as regular Gemini chats. You can turn history off, but Tasks requires history to be on to run. Sensitive actions still require your explicit approval. Google states that each task runs in a dedicated, isolated environment for your account.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="30" />

## Conclusion

Tasks turns Gemini from a chat assistant into a background worker for everyday admin. The free rollout in six countries makes it immediately useful for research, scheduling and organisation. Start with a simple research or calendar task, review the results, and expand from there. Advanced calling and purchasing features remain US-first for now, but the core experience is already available to hundreds of millions of people.
