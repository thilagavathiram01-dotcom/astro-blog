The September 2026 Pixel Drop is a small update with two changes that actually show up every day: a rebuilt Pixel VIPs widget and scam warnings that sit above the keyboard while you type.

Google published the drop on 15 September 2026. On phones it rides with Android 17 QPR1. Pixel Watch 3 and newer get gesture tweaks in the same wave. This guide covers the phone features you can set up in a few minutes.

You need a Pixel 6 or newer, current system updates, Google Contacts 4.77 or later, and Pixel VIPs app version 2.0 for the widget work. Scam chips in Gboard start in the United States on Pixel 6 and newer and expand to more countries in the same drop.

## What the September Pixel Drop changes

Google’s official drop post lists three phone items that matter here.

**Pixel VIPs.** Home screen widgets now let you call or text a person with one tap. A floating overlay at the bottom of the screen switches between VIPs. Notification badges sit on the widget so unread messages are visible without opening an app.

**Scam Detection.** Warnings now appear in the Gboard suggestion strip in supported chat apps in the United States. The same drop expands Scam Detection for chat-app notifications to Australia, Canada, France, Germany, India, Japan, Mexico, Singapore, and the United Kingdom, plus extra languages.

**Pause Point.** The optional Digital Wellbeing pause before a distracting app, first shown on Pixel 11, now rolls to Pixel 6 and newer. It is separate from VIPs and scam chips. Turn it on only if you want a delay, not a shortcut.

Pixel Drops are not the same as the broader September Android Drop. Motion Assist, Find Hub remembered items, and Guided vision shipped in the Android drop. For Motion Assist setup, see [How to Use Motion Assist on Android 17](/blog/android-17-motion-assist/).

## Add people as Pixel VIPs

Google Help still limits the list to **eight** people. VIPs also join Favorites, so they can bypass Do Not Disturb unless you turn that off.

1. Open the **Contacts** app.
2. Tap **Organize**, then **Pixel VIPs**.
3. Complete onboarding if this is the first time.
4. Tap **Add** and pick contacts. Repeat until the people you actually call sit on the list.
5. Grant the permissions you want: SMS for last messages, WhatsApp if you use it, location only if those people already share location with you.

Add birthdays and short notes during setup. The widget surfaces last Messages or WhatsApp contact, upcoming dates, and memos. Location only appears when the other person shares it through Google Maps or Find Hub.

You can add or remove VIPs later from **Organize → Pixel VIPs**, or by pressing and holding the widget and choosing **Edit**.

## Put the widget on the home screen

If onboarding did not pin it, add it yourself.

1. Press and hold an empty spot on the home screen.
2. Tap **Widgets**.
3. Open **Contacts** and choose the **VIPs** widget.
4. Place it, then resize if the launcher allows it.

On the updated widget you should see faces or names plus badges when a VIP has an unread message. Tap the call or text action on the widget instead of opening the old bottom sheet first. That one-tap path is the September change.

When a VIP card is open, use the **bottom floating overlay** to swipe between people without leaving the home screen. If the overlay is missing, update Contacts and the Pixel VIPs package from Play Store, then reboot once.

## Use the widget without turning it into noise

Keep the list short. Eight slots fill fast if you star every coworker.

Put partners, kids, or a caregiver in the first two positions. Those faces should be readable at a glance. Use memos for gift ideas and shared plans, not a second calendar.

If Do Not Disturb bypass is too loud at night, open the VIP or Favorites settings and turn the exception off. The widget still works. Only the interruption rule changes.

Shared location is optional. Ask the other person before you expect a live pin. If they decline, the widget still shows calls, chats, and dates.

## Turn on Gboard scam warnings

Scam Detection on Pixel started in Messages. The September drop puts a **Likely scam** chip in the Gboard strip while you type in supported chat apps in the United States, on Pixel 6 and newer.

Google also says the notification-side detector now covers more regions and languages: Arabic, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, and Spanish, plus the country list above.

Check that the feature is on:

1. Open **Settings → Safety and emergency** or search Settings for **Scam Detection**.
2. Confirm it is enabled for Messages and, where shown, other chat apps.
3. Open **Settings → System → Languages & input → On-screen keyboard → Gboard** and install any pending Gboard update.
4. In a chat, look at the suggestion strip above the keys. A warning chip appears only when the detector flags the thread or the text you are about to send.

Treat the chip as a pause, not a verdict. Google labels it as a likelihood warning. If a family member writes in a rush, you can still send. If an unknown number asks for codes, gift cards, or remote access, stop and verify out of band.

Pair this with lock-screen and account hardening in [Android Advanced Protection setup](/blog/android-advanced-protection-setup/) if the phone holds work mail or payment apps.

## Pause Point, Watch extras, and what can wait

**Pause Point** now reaches Pixel 6 and newer after debuting on Pixel 11. Open **Digital Wellbeing**, pick the apps that steal evenings, and turn the pause on. It does not block the app. It inserts a short delay so you can decide.

**Pixel Watch 3 and 4** can scroll the notification tray with the one-handed pinch gesture. **Pixel Watch 4** also gets an updated Raise to Talk model for Gemini. Update the watch from the Pixel Watch app after the phone drop lands.

Google also shipped a short Harry Potter themed pack and an Audible trial tied to the full-cast audio editions. Those are cosmetic. Skip them if you want a clean home screen.

Firmware for Dynamic ANC, Pixel Watch sleep sync, and Live Translate taps on Pixel Buds is a separate September hardware track. Do not expect those switches inside the VIPs widget.

## Troubleshooting

**No Pixel VIPs entry in Contacts.** Update Google Contacts and search Play Store for Pixel VIPs. The drop needs Contacts 4.77+ and VIPs 2.0.

**Widget still opens the old sheet only.** Wait for the server-side flag. 9to5Google tied the redesign to Pixel 6 and newer in all regions once those app versions are installed.

**No Gboard chip.** Confirm you are in a supported country and app. The US keyboard chip and the wider notification expansion are not the same rollout.

**VIP badges never appear.** Grant notification access if Android asks. Badges need the messaging app’s notifications, not only Contacts permission.

**Someone should not bypass DND.** Remove them from Favorites or disable the VIP interruption exception. Removing them from the widget list is cleaner if they were added by mistake.

## What to do this week

Add four VIPs, pin the widget, and send one test text from the new tap target. Then open a spam thread and confirm the Gboard strip can show a warning.

Leave Pause Point off until that routine is boring. The drop is useful because the two defaults—people you call and messages you should not trust—now sit where you already look.
