Small business owners spend hours each week on branding, marketing visuals, and sorting through financial records. Google just added tools inside the Gemini app to handle those tasks from one place. A new business landing page, brand style memory, and finance skills rolled out starting October 20, 2026.

These features are available to all Gemini app users worldwide in the coming days. You do not need a paid plan to start. The goal is simple: free up time so you can focus on the work that started your business.

## What the New Gemini Business Tools Include

Google announced the updates on its Keyword blog. The central piece is a business landing page inside the Gemini app. From it you can build brand assets, review finances, and pull customer insights.

Gemini now connects more easily to Google Business Profile, Google Ads, and Google Workspace. Third-party apps include Adobe, Canva, Dropbox, Shopify, Wix, Zoho, and Xero. You control which connections stay active.

Brand style is another addition. Upload your website, a brand kit, fonts, or color palettes once. Gemini remembers the style. Pair it with Nano Banana 2.1, Google’s latest image model, and generate matching visuals for product launches or campaigns without re-uploading guidelines each time.

Finance tools turn spreadsheets, receipts, and purchase records into charts and interactive models in seconds. You get clearer views of performance instead of raw numbers.

Tens of millions of small business owners already use Gemini for product ideas, customer feedback, and performance checks. These updates extend that use.

## How to Access the Business Landing Page

Open the Gemini app on your phone or the web version at gemini.google.com. The business landing page appears as a dedicated hub once the rollout reaches your account. Google is rolling it out globally over the coming days, so it may not show for everyone at the same moment.

If it is available, look for a business or hub section in the main menu or after you open a new chat. You can also start a conversation and ask Gemini to open the business tools or show the landing page. Once you land there, the hub organizes brand, finance, and insights sections in one view.

Preferences carry across devices. If you set connections on the web, they appear on mobile. For larger teams that need admin controls and enterprise security, Google points to Gemini for business on Google Cloud instead of the consumer app.

## Create On-Brand Visuals with Nano Banana 2.1

Start by defining your brand style. In the Gemini app, upload your website URL, a brand kit file, font files, or color codes. Gemini stores the style so later image requests stay consistent.

When you need visuals, describe the asset. Ask for social posts, product shots, or campaign graphics that match the saved style. Nano Banana 2.1 handles the generation. Google says the model delivers end-to-end results for launches and ongoing marketing without repeated style uploads.

Test a few outputs and refine the prompt. Mention specific elements like logo placement or color emphasis if the first results need adjustment. You can also connect Canva or Adobe for further editing once the image is ready.

Owners who already run shops on Shopify or Wix can pull product context into the same chat and generate matching promotional images.

## Turn Records into Financial Insights

Gather a spreadsheet export, scanned receipts, or purchase records. Upload them in a Gemini chat or from the business hub. Ask for a summary chart, cash-flow view, or interactive model of the last quarter.

Gemini processes the files and returns graphics in seconds. You can follow up with questions about trends, cost areas, or comparisons. Keep sensitive live accounting files offline and work from copies when possible.

Connect Xero or Zoho if you use those tools. The link lets Gemini pull context without manual exports each time. Review the outputs against your own records before making decisions. The tools speed up review; they do not replace your accountant.

## Connect Your Existing Tools

Open Gemini settings and look for Connected Apps or the business section. Toggle Google Business Profile, Google Ads, and Workspace. Then add third-party options such as Shopify, Canva, Dropbox, Wix, Zoho, or Xero.

Each connection requires your permission. You can turn any of them off later. Once linked, Gemini can reference review data from your Business Profile, draft responses, or pull product details from Shopify into marketing requests.

## Practical Tips for Daily Use

Start with one task. Upload a brand kit and generate five social graphics before connecting every account. Check the results against your real brand guidelines.

For finance, use a recent but non-sensitive export. Compare the chart totals with your bookkeeping software line by line on the first run.

Keep responses short when you are on the go. Add phrases like “keep it brief” or “one chart only” if the output grows long.

Review privacy settings in the Gemini app. Connected apps share data only while the toggle is on. Delete conversation history if you no longer need a specific chat.

Check the business landing page every few days during the rollout window. Features appear gradually.
