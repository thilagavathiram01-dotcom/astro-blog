Year-end to-do lists grow fast. Groceries for parties, credit checks, tax estimates, and last-minute home fixes pile up. Gemini Tasks plus new Connected Apps handle the multi-step work while you stay focused.

Google rolled out free Tasks in the Gemini app on 17 November 2026 for users in the US, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. It runs on the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model. On 20 November, Connected Apps expanded in the US for grocery chains and finance tools.

This guide shows how to enable Tasks, connect the right apps, and run practical year-end jobs. Check our earlier post on [free Gemini Tasks with Hydrogen](/blog/free-gemini-tasks-hydrogen-guide/) for the basic toggle and model details.

## Enable Tasks and Connected Apps

Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS, or go to gemini.google.com. Sign in with your personal Google Account.

Switch the toggle from Chat to Task. On mobile it sits above the prompt box. On the web it appears below.

Keep Activity must be on for most Connected Apps to work. Go to Settings > Personal Intelligence > Connected Apps and turn on the services you need. Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Drive) connects as a group. Third-party apps require individual authentication.

For US grocery and finance features, confirm you are in an eligible region. Availability expands over days, so refresh if a specific app is missing.

## Order Groceries with Connected Apps

In the US, Gemini now links to Albertsons, Safeway, Shaw’s, and Target for grocery runs. These use the Universal Commerce Protocol so Gemini can build a cart and hand off checkout.

In Task mode, type a clear request: “Add lasagna ingredients, strawberries, cucumbers, and vanilla ice cream to my Instacart order for delivery tomorrow evening. Stay under $80.”

Gemini checks availability, suggests substitutes if needed, and pauses before final purchase. You can set a hard spending cap in advance. Receipts land in Google Wallet.

If you prefer a specific store, name it. Gemini uses Google’s Shopping Graph for comparisons and can apply available discount codes.

For parties, ask it to generate a list from a recipe photo or text, then push the items to the connected grocery app. It works in the background so you can continue other work.

## Handle Finances and Taxes

Experian is available in the Gemini app and now in Search. US users can check a credit rating or explore personal loan options without leaving the chat.

H&R Block integration in Search lets you calculate taxes and estimate a refund. Connect the app under Connected Apps, then describe the job: “Review my uploaded W-2 and 1099 forms and estimate my federal refund.”

Gemini reads the documents you provide (or pulls from Gmail if permitted), runs the calculation, and returns a summary. Always verify the numbers against official tools before filing.

Google Wallet connection (US only) surfaces spending insights, boarding passes, and loyalty details. Tag @Google Wallet in a prompt for charts of last quarter’s cash flow or subscription alerts. The connection is read-only; Gemini cannot move money.

## Book Local Help and Other Errands

Coming in the following weeks for US Search users: Angi and Thumbtack for finding local pros. Ask Gemini to locate someone for a leaky faucet or holiday decoration setup, then compare ratings and availability.

Tasks also support calls to businesses (except in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska). Example: “Call the two nearest auto shops and book the earliest Friday morning oil change that fits my calendar.” Gemini discloses it is an AI, finds open slots, and adds the appointment.

You can text Gemini (US phone number required) or forward emails to gemini@google.com for school schedules or medical bills. It extracts dates, adds them to Calendar, and flags conflicts.

## Practical Tips for Reliable Results

Write specific prompts. Include dates, budgets, preferred stores, and constraints. Vague requests lead to more follow-up questions.

Review every step that involves money or personal data. Gemini pauses for confirmation on purchases and critical actions. Set “Always allow” only for routine steps you trust.

Monitor the live progress in the chat. You can interrupt or add instructions at any time. The work runs in an isolated environment tied to your account.

If a task fails or produces incomplete output, use the thumbs-down feedback. Sites are still adapting to agent traffic, so some flows hand control back to you for the final click.

Start small. Order a short grocery list or schedule one appointment before chaining larger projects. Check official Gemini Apps Help for the latest eligibility and regional limits.

## Conclusion

Gemini Tasks and the expanded Connected Apps turn year-end busywork into background jobs. Groceries from Albertsons or Target, credit insights from Experian, tax estimates from H&R Block, and local service searches become single prompts.

Enable the Task toggle, connect only the apps you need, and keep spending caps active. The free Hydrogen-powered experience is available in six countries today, with US-specific commerce features rolling out now.

Test one errand this week. The time saved compounds across the remaining holiday weeks.
