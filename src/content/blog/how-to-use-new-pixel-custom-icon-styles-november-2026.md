Google’s November 2026 Pixel Drop adds 10 new custom icon styles powered by the Nano Banana image model. These styles give Pixel 6a and newer phones a coordinated home-screen look without third-party launchers.

The feature expands the original Custom Icons tool that arrived earlier in 2026. Total available styles now reach 16. Higher-quality generation comes from the Nano Banana upgrade. The update is available in all regions and languages on supported devices.

## What the New Styles Offer

Custom icon styles apply a consistent visual treatment across home-screen app icons. You select a style, the system generates matching icons, and you apply the set. Icons change only on the home screen. The app drawer keeps original icons.

Nano Banana produces clearer, more polished results than the earlier model. Styles range from playful to minimal. Some styles let you tweak colors or shades after generation. Others stay fixed for a uniform look.

Availability starts with the Pixel 6a and includes all later Pixel phones. Pixel 6 and Pixel 6 Pro do not receive the expanded styles. Check for the system update under Settings > System > Software updates if the new options do not appear yet.

## How to Apply a New Custom Icon Style

Follow these steps on a supported Pixel phone after the November Drop rolls out:

1. Long-press an empty area on the home screen.
2. Tap Wallpaper & style.
3. Select Icons.
4. Tap Style or Create (label may vary by build).
5. Choose one of the new styles from the list.
6. Adjust any available color or shade options for that style.
7. Tap Create icons or the equivalent generate button.
8. Wait for the system to generate and download the icon set.
9. Open the finished style under Your styles.
10. Tap the checkmark or Apply button to set it as the active home-screen style.

Generation requires an internet connection and a signed-in Google Account. Download happens in the background. You can switch styles later from the same Icons menu. Revert to Default or Minimal at any time.

If Create is missing, update the Pixel Launcher and system software, restart the phone, and confirm you are online and signed in.

## Tips for Best Results

Pair a custom icon style with a matching wallpaper for stronger visual cohesion. The system already supports dynamic color theming. Explore color intensity options under Wallpaper & style > Colors for Neutral, Soft, Bright, or Bold looks. See our guide on [Android 17 QPR2 custom colors](/blog/android-17-qpr2-custom-colors-guide/) for details on the expanded palette controls.

Test a style on a secondary home-screen page first if you keep many icons. Some styles work better with darker wallpapers; others stand out on light backgrounds. You can keep multiple generated styles saved and switch between them without regenerating.

Third-party icon packs still require a launcher such as Nova or Lawnchair. The built-in styles stay inside the Pixel Launcher and need no extra apps.

## Limitations to Know

Styles affect only home-screen icons. App drawer, settings menus, and system UI stay unchanged. Not every app supplies themed assets, so the AI generates replacements for missing ones. Results can vary slightly by app.

The feature is not available on Pixel 6 or Pixel 6 Pro. Tablets and watches follow separate schedules. Generation quality depends on the Nano Banana model and your device’s processing resources.

## Conclusion

The November 2026 Pixel Drop makes home-screen customization easier on Pixel 6a and newer phones. Ten additional styles plus the Nano Banana upgrade give clearer, more consistent icons. Apply a style in a few taps from Wallpaper & style, then refine the look with color options if available.

Update your phone, try a couple of styles, and keep the ones that match your wallpaper. The process is reversible and requires no extra apps.
