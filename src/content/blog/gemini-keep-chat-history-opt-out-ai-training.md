Google recently updated Gemini privacy controls so you can keep your chat history without feeding conversations into AI model training. The change splits the old Keep Activity switch into separate options for History and training. This guide shows the exact steps to apply the settings on web and mobile.

Until November 2026, one switch controlled both saving chats and using them for training. Google announced the split on November 16. The new controls started rolling out widely by November 24. Users over 18 often see the training option on by default, so you need to check it after the update reaches your account.

## What the New Settings Change

The History setting decides whether past chats appear in your Gemini list and stay available for reference. The separate training control decides whether the content of future chats, including text and attachments, helps train generative AI models.

With History on and training off, your chats remain saved for your use. Google does not use those future chats for model training unless you send feedback. Audio and Gemini Live video or screenshare data stay under their own opt-in controls. This separation lets you retain useful context for ongoing projects while limiting how your data contributes to model improvements.

If you turn History off, future chats do not show in your visible history. Google still retains them for around two months to respond to you, process feedback, and support safety reviews. This is longer than the previous 72-hour window. The longer retention supports more thorough abuse detection as AI systems grow more capable.

Temporary chats follow similar retention rules. They do not appear in regular history and are not used for training, yet Google keeps them for about two months. Use them when you want a clean session that will not linger in your list.

## How to Keep History and Turn Off Training

These steps work for personal Google accounts. Work and school accounts follow admin controls and may not show the same options. Confirm you are signed into the correct account before you start. The rollout continues, so if you do not see the new labels yet, check again after an app update.

### On the web

1. Open gemini.google.com and sign in.
2. Click Settings at the bottom of the page.
3. Select Gemini History. This opens myactivity.google.com/product/gemini.
4. Keep the History dropdown set to On if you want saved chats.
5. Under Help train generative AI models with, uncheck Chat content including text & attachments.
6. Confirm Turn off if prompted.
7. Check the separate audio and Live option if you use voice features, and leave it off unless you want that data used.

### In the Gemini app

1. Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS.
2. Tap the menu at the top, then Settings.
3. Under Data & privacy, tap History.
4. Ensure History stays on.
5. Uncheck the chat content training box.
6. Tap Turn off to confirm.

Wording can vary slightly between “attachments” and “uploads.” Look for the option that controls chat content used for generative AI models. After you save the change, reopen the page to verify both settings stayed as you intended.

## What Stays the Same

You can still review and delete individual chats or ranges from the same History page. Auto-delete options remain: 3 months, 18 months (default), 36 months, or never. Choose a shorter window if you want less long-term storage. Deleting history does not erase data that human reviewers already examined; those can stay for up to three years for safety purposes.

Feedback you submit can still draw on recent chat context to improve the product, even with training off. Connected apps and Personal Intelligence features have their own permission controls. Review those separately if you do not want Gemini reading data from Gmail, Drive, or other linked services.

## Tips for Stronger Control

Check the settings again after the next Gemini app update, because labels can shift during rollout. Export important chats before bulk deletes if you need a local copy. Use Temporary Chat for one-off sensitive questions. Review Connected Apps permissions under Personal Intelligence if you do not want Gemini reading Gmail, Drive, or other data. Turn off Web & App Activity and YouTube History separately if you want broader limits on Google data collection.

Google states that retained data with History off is used strictly for safety and security, not for model training or product improvement beyond feedback handling. Human reviewers may still examine a small sample of content for quality and safety.

## Conclusion

The new Gemini settings give clearer control. Keep History on so you can return to past conversations, and turn off the chat content training option so those chats do not train models. The change is rolling out globally. Visit the History page today, confirm both switches match your preference, and review auto-delete timing while you are there. These small steps help you use Gemini with more confidence about what happens to your chats.
