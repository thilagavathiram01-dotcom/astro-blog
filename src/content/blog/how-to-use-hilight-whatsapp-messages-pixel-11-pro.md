Pixel 11 Pro owners can now receive visual alerts for WhatsApp messages from favorite contacts without flipping the phone over. HiLight, the rear LED system built into the camera bar, glows in a custom color when your device rests face down.

This guide walks through official setup steps so you can turn on the feature, assign colors, and use it with WhatsApp alongside Google Messages and calls.

## What HiLight Does on Pixel 11 Pro

HiLight is available on the Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold. It provides subtle rear lighting cues for three main activities:

- Speaking with Gemini (soft pulses while the assistant thinks and responds).
- Incoming calls from favorite contacts (customizable color; works with Phone by Google and WhatsApp).
- Incoming messages from favorite contacts (customizable color).

Official Google support documents message support for Google Messages. The November 2026 Pixel Drop, paired with Android 17 QPR2, expanded capabilities. Google’s product team confirmed WhatsApp message support shortly after the drop began rolling out, so favorite contacts who message you on WhatsApp can now trigger the same colored glow previously limited to calls or Google Messages.

The light only activates when the phone is face down on a surface. Update your Contacts and Messages apps to the latest versions for full functionality. Enabling flash notifications disables HiLight for calls and messages.

## Turn HiLight On and Choose What It Alerts

Start in Settings so the system knows which events should produce a glow.

1. Open the Settings app on your Pixel 11 Pro.
2. Tap System, then HiLight.
3. Turn Use HiLight on.
4. Enable the specific toggles you want: Speaking with Gemini, Calls from favorites, and Messages from favorites.

Once Messages from favorites is active, favorite contacts can trigger the light for both Google Messages and (with the November update) WhatsApp. Calls from favorites already covered WhatsApp at launch.

If you use modes such as Flip to Shhh, Bedtime, or Do Not Disturb, HiLight continues to glow by default. To change this:

1. Go to Settings > Modes.
2. Select the mode.
3. Tap People, then Messages or Calls.
4. Choose None and turn off Allow repeat callers if you want the light silenced in that mode.

## Set Favorite Contacts and Assign Colors

HiLight only responds to contacts you mark as favorites. A default color applies until you customize each one.

1. Open the Contacts app and make sure your important people are starred as favorites.
2. Select a favorite contact.
3. Under Contact Settings, tap HiLight.
4. Pick a color from the available options.
5. Tap Preview and turn the phone over to see the glow, then tap Done.

Repeat for each favorite. You can choose different colors so a quick glance at the camera bar tells you who is reaching out. The same color assignment works for both calls and messages once the relevant toggles are on.

For WhatsApp specifically, ensure the contact is a favorite and that Messages from favorites is enabled. After the November Pixel Drop update reaches your device, a WhatsApp message from that contact produces the assigned color glow while the phone is face down.

## Practical Tips for Daily Use

Keep the phone on a flat surface so the rear light is visible from across a table or desk. Combine HiLight with your existing call tools. If you already use Gemini Call Screen on the Pixel 11 series, favorite-contact colors give an extra layer of awareness before you decide to screen or answer. See our guide on [how to use Gemini Call Screen on Pixel 11](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-call-screen-pixel-11/) for the conversational screening steps.

Test with one or two contacts first. Send yourself a WhatsApp message from another device while the Pixel rests face down to confirm the color and timing. The light blinks or pulses briefly rather than staying on continuously.

Battery impact is minimal because the LEDs only activate for the listed events. If the glow seems dim in bright rooms, a darker surface or slight angle can help visibility.

Update your system software if the Messages from favorites toggle is missing. The feature arrives with the November Pixel Drop rollout, which continues over several weeks.

## Conclusion

HiLight turns the Pixel 11 Pro’s camera bar into a quiet notification system for the people who matter most. With the November update, WhatsApp messages join Google Messages and calls, so you can leave the phone face down and still know when a favorite contact reaches out.

Follow the Settings > System > HiLight path, star your contacts, assign colors, and enable the message toggle. The result is a simple visual cue that keeps you informed without constant screen checks.
