OpenAI Academy now groups free courses by role, not by a single beginner track. On 21 September 2026, OpenAI added paths for developers, leaders, educators, and college students next to Apply AI at Work. Two days later it also outlined a trainer program so partners can teach the same material locally.

You do not need a paid ChatGPT plan or a workspace membership. You do need a ChatGPT account if you want progress saved and a badge issued to the right email. This guide walks through enrollment, path choice, the 80% assessment rule, and what to do when a badge does not appear.

## What the September expansion actually added

Academy courses are free and self-paced. OpenAI hosts them at [academy.openai.com](https://academy.openai.com/). Enrollment and progress sit on Gradual. Shareable badges are issued by Accredible.

The 21 September announcement splits the catalog into four audiences:

- **Apply AI at Work** for knowledge workers who want clearer prompts, reusable workflows, and supervised agent tasks.
- **Build with AI** for developers using Codex or the OpenAI API, including evaluations, retrieval, and production checks.
- **Lead AI Adoption**, centered on the AI Leadership course, for people who own strategy and change management.
- **Teach and Learn with AI**, with separate courses for educators and college students.

OpenAI's framing is practical: use AI to learn AI. Courses expect a real task, not a toy prompt. An educator might adapt a lesson from materials they have permission to use. A student might turn readings and deadlines into a study plan. A developer might scope a code change and keep review in their own hands.

Badges and pathway certificates of completion are not certifications. OpenAI says they do not guarantee eligibility for a future certification program. The Help Center also separates Academy courses from the OpenAI Certified app inside ChatGPT.

## Pick a path before you enroll

Sign in with the ChatGPT account you want tied to progress and badges. Academy accounts cannot be linked or merged after you start a course. If you use the wrong email, the badge follows that email.

Match the course to the work, not to a job title on a slide.

**Apply AI at Work** has three courses, all focused on ChatGPT except Agents and Workflows, which focuses on ChatGPT Work:

1. AI Foundations (about 60–75 minutes) covers basics, prompting, context, and responsible use.
2. Applied AI Foundations (about 75–90 minutes) turns a repeated task into a workflow with defined inputs, outputs, and review points.
3. Agents and Workflows (about 75–90 minutes) covers delegation, boundaries, and checkpoints so a person still owns the result.

**Build with AI** is the technical track. Scope AI Solutions is about 30 minutes. Evaluate AI Applications is about 70 minutes. Design and Build Agentic Systems is about 110 minutes. Build with Retrieval-Augmented Generation is about 100 minutes. Optimize AI Application Performance is about 30 minutes. Codex has three courses: Get Started with Codex (about 80 minutes), Extend Codex Workflows (about 90 minutes), and Scale Codex Across Governed Teams and Systems (about 100 minutes).

**AI Leadership** is a single course of about 180 minutes. Learners connect one initiative to business priorities, name owners, and draft a roadmap. **AI for Educators** and **AI for College Students** are about 45 minutes each. Those three are standalone. They do not form a multi-course pathway certificate.

Times vary if you follow every exercise. You can leave and return. Progress saves when you are signed in.

If you already reuse ChatGPT instructions across chats, the habits in [reusable ChatGPT skills](/blog/chatgpt-skills-reusable-workflows/) pair well with Applied AI Foundations. Academy is the course. Skills are the product feature you practice on afterward.

## Enroll and finish the matching learning path

You can browse courses without signing in. Saving progress requires a ChatGPT login.

1. Open [academy.openai.com](https://academy.openai.com/) and sign in with ChatGPT.
2. Select **Courses** in the sidebar.
3. Find the course and select **Go to course**. That opens the learning path page, not only the lesson player.
4. Select **Enroll now** if it appears. The page should show a Learning Path label under the title.
5. Open the course and work through it on a real task you are allowed to share with the model.

A learning path here means the page that pairs one course with its matching assessment. Finish both. When a **Complete now** prompt appears, select it. If you chose **Maybe later**, reopen the activity and mark it complete. A path can show 100% progress and still need that last action.

Organizations can mix paths. OpenAI's example is Apply AI at Work in onboarding, Build with AI for technical teams, and AI Leadership in a champion or executive program. Schools can combine AI Foundations with the educator and student courses. Reporting for organizations is available through an OpenAI account team, not from the free individual signup.

## Pass the assessment and collect the badge

Every course has an assessment. You can finish a course without taking it. A badge requires both: course completion and a score of 80% or higher.

Assessments have 10–20 questions drawn from a bank of 50. A miss is not final. You can retry on a new random set. Open the learning path and scroll to the course and assessment activities to find the matching test.

Accredible issues the badge when the learning path is marked complete. The credential is tied to the Academy signup email, or to the ChatGPT email if you used Sign in with ChatGPT.

A pathway certificate of completion is a separate credential. You earn it by finishing every course in an eligible pathway and passing each assessment at 80% or higher. AI Leadership, AI for Educators, and AI for College Students stay outside that pathway rule.

## Fix a missing badge before you email support

OpenAI lists three common gaps.

You passed the assessment but did not complete the course. Both are required.

You reached the end, or saw a score, but never selected **Complete now**. Reopen the activity and confirm the prompt is gone.

You finished the course and assessment outside the learning path. Go back to Courses, open **Go to course**, enroll if needed, and mark the path complete. Work you already finished should carry over.

From a course or assessment page, the Learning Path card under the banner is another route to the same page. On an assessment overview, the Enroll button above that card is for the assessment only. Use the card to reach the path.

If both activities are complete and the path is marked done, email support@accredible.com with the Academy email and the course name. For missing certificates you can also contact OpenAI Support. Login, enrollment, and progress bugs go to support@gradual.com, not Accredible.

## Tips that keep the credential useful

Bring one task you already own. Foundations asks you to practice instructions, context, and review on that task. A generic “write an email” demo does not show the review habit the assessment is checking.

Keep sensitive material out of the practice chat. Use a redacted snippet when the real file has account numbers or personal data. The badge does not change ChatGPT's data controls.

Do not treat the badge as a hiring certification. OpenAI is explicit that it only records course completion plus the assessment score.

Developers should not stop at Get Started with Codex. The later Codex courses cover team workflows and parallel workstreams. API builders should pair Scope AI Solutions with Evaluate AI Applications so a demo does not ship without a failure check.

Leaders can draft the AI Leadership roadmap in the course, then assign Apply AI at Work to the people who will run the workflow. A strategy doc without a practice path is how pilots stall.

## Where to go next

Start at [academy.openai.com](https://academy.openai.com/), sign in with the ChatGPT account you want on the badge, and enroll in one learning path. Mark both the course and the assessment complete, then confirm the path itself is complete. If the badge is still missing after those checks, Accredible is the right desk, with your email and course name in the first message.
