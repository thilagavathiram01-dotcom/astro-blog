Gemini can now handle multi-step work while you focus on other things. The Tasks feature lets you describe a goal, then the app plans and completes the steps in the background.

It rolled out free for eligible users in the United States, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India on November 17, 2026. The feature runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen.

This guide shows how to start a task, what it can do, and how to stay in control of your data.

![Person using a smartphone for daily productivity](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1512941937669-90a1b58e7e9c?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What Tasks in Gemini can do

Tasks go beyond quick chat answers. You flip a toggle and hand off longer projects. Gemini uses connected Google apps and selected third-party services to finish the work.

Examples from the official launch include booking service appointments by calling dealerships, analyzing utility bills to find savings, shopping for products with Google Pay, and adding items to grocery orders. It can also turn forwarded school emails into calendar events and spot conflicts.

Calling and payment features started in the United States. Grocery and calendar actions work more broadly where the feature is available. Gemini discloses that it is an AI when it calls a business.

## How to start a task

Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS. Look for the toggle under the prompt box. Switch it from Chat to Task.

Describe the outcome you want. Be specific about constraints. For example: “Call the two nearest Honda dealerships and book the earliest Friday morning oil change next month that fits my calendar.”

Gemini creates a plan, runs the steps in a dedicated environment, and notifies you when it finishes or needs a decision. You can add more instructions while it works.

Starting in the United States you can also forward emails to gemini@google.com or text Gemini. To enable texting:

1. Open Gemini and tap Settings.
2. Tap Channels.
3. Tap Set up under Text with Gemini.
4. Verify your phone number.

## Security and control

Gemini runs each task inside a hardware-isolated virtual machine on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine. The environment is dedicated to your account and not shared with other users.

The system includes prompt-injection filters and Google Safe Browsing checks. Gemini is designed to pause and ask before critical actions such as clicking Buy. You can set “Always allow” for routine steps you trust.

You control which apps stay connected. Disconnect services or delete history at any time in settings. Review the official technical paper on the multi-layer defenses if you want more detail.

## Practical examples

One user asked Gemini to schedule a minivan service. It used location and vehicle details already known through Personal Intelligence, called two shops, checked the calendar for free slots, and added the appointment. The whole process took under five minutes.

Another request involved a high electric bill. Gemini accessed the utility portal through Google Password Manager, reviewed past statements from email, and identified that a heat pump was running during peak pricing.

For shopping, Gemini can compare options via the Shopping Graph, ask clarifying questions, apply discount codes, and complete checkout with Google Pay while staying inside a spending limit you set. Receipts go to Google Wallet.

Grocery tasks work with connected apps such as Instacart. You can text or send a photo of an item and Gemini adds it to the cart before handing control back for final confirmation.

## Tips for better results

Write the goal clearly and include any limits. “Find three USB-C chargers under 60 watts in black and buy the cheapest one that ships by Friday” works better than a vague request.

Confirm important details before Gemini acts. If a step looks wrong, give feedback with a thumbs-down so the system improves.

Start with smaller tasks until you trust the flow. Check the list of connected apps and remove any you do not need.

## Limitations to know

Calling is not available in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska. Texting requires a U.S. phone number with an eligible carrier.

Gemini can still make mistakes. Some websites require a final human click. Provide feedback when results miss the mark.

Advanced features continue to expand. Check the Gemini app for the latest availability in your region.

## Conclusion

Tasks in Gemini turn the app into a background assistant that books appointments, manages emails, shops, and handles other multi-step work. The feature is free for eligible users in six countries and runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen.

Flip the toggle from Chat to Task, describe the outcome, and stay in control through confirmations and settings. Start with a clear request today and see what Gemini can finish for you.
