Google Play Fest 2026 runs from October 13 to October 19. It combines sweepstakes prizes, competitive game leagues, and discounts across the Play Store. Play Points members can turn existing points into chances at hardware and merch. Everyone gets access to limited-time deals on apps, games, books, and select gift cards.

The event is available in select markets. Check the Play Store on your Android phone or tablet to confirm what appears for your account. Offers end October 19 or while supplies last. The US sweepstakes has a separate entry deadline of November 2.

## What you get during Play Fest

The sweepstakes lets eligible Play Points members exchange points for entries. Grand prizes include a 1:1 scale 2026 replica season racing helmet of Oscar Piastri made by Bell, a Razer Boomslang 20th Anniversary Edition mouse, XREAL AURA spatial computing glasses, a Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, and a Samsung 55-inch S90H OLED TV. Thousands of additional prizes are available.

No purchase is necessary for the sweepstakes. It is open to select US Play Points members age 18 and older. Official rules apply.

Game leagues run in Angry Birds 2, Free Fire, and 8 Ball Pool. Pre-registration is already open. Top players on the global leaderboards can win up to 50,000 Play Points per league. Matches start October 13.

Deals cover apps, games, books, and gift cards. Members receive daily points boosters on purchases. Books under $6 are highlighted, with a 15x points multiplier on book purchases from October 16 to 18. Play Pass subscribers get early access, double points, and 6x boosters. Special in-game rewards appear in titles such as MONOPOLY GO! and Pokémon GO. Limited discounts apply to annual Crunchyroll and Calm subscriptions. Checkout bonuses with partners such as Venmo are also available.

## Join Google Play Points first

Most rewards require Play Points. Join for free if you are not already a member.

1. Open the Google Play Store on your Android device.
2. Tap your profile picture in the top right.
3. Select Play Points.
4. Follow the prompts to join and complete any starter quest for bonus points.

You earn points on qualifying purchases. During Play Fest, daily boosters increase what you collect. Higher membership levels unlock better redemption options later.

## Enter the Play Fest sweepstakes

Visit the Play Fest hub at play.google.com/play-fest-2026 on your Android phone or tablet. Sign in with the Google account linked to your Play Points membership.

Exchange points for entries. Larger bundles give bonus entries. Enter as many times as you want while the window is open. More points mean more chances. The sweepstakes ends November 2 for eligible US members.

Review the official rules before you redeem any points. Free entry methods are listed there.

## Pre-register for the game leagues

Head to the league pages now so you are ready on October 13:

- Angry Birds 2: leagues.withgoogle.com/angry-birds-2/
- Free Fire: leagues.withgoogle.com/free-fire/
- 8 Ball Pool: leagues.withgoogle.com/8-ball-pool/

Complete registration, then play the featured modes once the event starts. Climb the leaderboards to compete for the Play Points prizes. These leagues turn regular sessions into structured competition.

## Claim the deals

Open the Play Store and look for the Play Fest banner or hub. Browse the discounted titles, books, and gift cards. Confirm the final price and any points multiplier before you buy.

Book purchases between October 16 and 18 earn 15 times the usual points. Play Pass members see extra boosts earlier. Some gift card offers are while supplies last, so check availability daily.

If you use Venmo or other partner payment methods, look for the listed checkout bonuses.

## Tips for getting the most value

Join Play Points and finish the starter quest before October 13. Pre-register for the leagues you play. Compare sale prices against your normal spending rather than buying just for the multiplier. Read the eligibility notes on each offer, since not every promotion appears in every country.

The event does not replace regular Play Protect or account security. Keep Play Protect on, as covered in our guide to [Android security state checks](/blog/androidx-security-state-libraries-guide/).
