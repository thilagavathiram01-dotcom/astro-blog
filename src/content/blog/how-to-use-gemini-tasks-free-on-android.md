Google rolled out free Tasks in the Gemini app on November 17, 2026. Eligible users in the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India can now hand off multi-step work to Gemini 4 Hydrogen without a paid plan.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="0" />

Tasks run in the background. You describe the job once. Gemini plans the steps, browses the web, uses connected apps, and checks with you before sensitive actions.

![Person holding smartphone showing AI chat interface](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1746608943132-065d1d4b3c5d?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## Check eligibility and update the app

Tasks require a personal Google Account and the latest Gemini app. You must be 18 or older. The feature uses the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model.

1. Open the Google Play Store on your Android phone.
2. Search for Gemini.
3. Tap Update if available, or Install if needed.
4. Open the app and sign in with your Google Account.

Availability rolls out gradually. If the Chat/Task toggle does not appear, wait a few hours and check again.

## Switch from Chat to Task

The home screen now shows a toggle under the prompt box.

1. Open Gemini.
2. Look for the Chat and Task buttons below the input field.
3. Tap Task.
4. The prompt changes to “Describe a task” and shows the Hydrogen model selector.

Type a clear instruction. Example: “Compare three nearby auto repair shops and book the earliest Friday morning appointment that fits my calendar.”

Gemini confirms the plan, runs the steps in a cloud virtual machine, and updates you with progress.

![Close-up of smartphone screen with AI assistant options](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1757310998309-87a97e562ee5?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What Tasks can do right now

Core capabilities include web research, connected Google apps, and file handling. In the U.S., additional features cover phone calls and payments with spending limits.

- Research and summarize information from the web.
- Add events to Google Calendar after checking conflicts.
- Draft emails or organize files in Drive.
- Shop with Google Pay while staying inside a budget you set.
- Forward school or work emails to gemini@google.com for calendar extraction (U.S. rollout).

Calling is limited in some U.S. states. Gemini discloses it is an AI when it places a call. Businesses can opt out.

You can also set up Text with Gemini under Settings > Channels if you have a supported U.S. carrier number.

## Safety controls and tips

Each task runs in an isolated virtual machine that is never shared. Prompt-injection filters and Safe Browsing limit risky links.

- Gemini asks before clicking Buy or other irreversible actions unless you pre-approve routine steps.
- Review the plan before it starts.
- Disconnect apps or delete history anytime in Settings.
- Never paste passwords or full card numbers into a task description.

Give feedback with thumbs down when a result misses the mark. This helps improve the system.

## Common limits and next steps

Free users can run a limited number of concurrent tasks. Limits reset on a schedule. Paid plans raise the caps and unlock more frequent schedules.

Start with a small task today. Describe what you need, review the plan, and let Gemini finish while you move on. Check the official Gemini Apps Help page for any regional updates.
