Google Pixel Tag gives Android users a dedicated tracker for everyday items. Attach it to keys, a bag, or luggage and locate it through the Find Hub app with Bluetooth, optional Ultra Wideband, and a built-in speaker.

The tag works with any Android phone running version 9 or later. Newer phones unlock tighter distance estimates and directional arrows. Setup takes under two minutes once the battery tab is removed.

![Keys and wallet ready for tracking](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1742318592061-15c5f19e1e47?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What You Need Before Pairing

Confirm your phone meets the basic requirements. Android 9 or higher is enough for standard tracking. Bluetooth must be on and the phone needs an internet connection for the initial pair. For the best experience, use a Pixel or other phone with Ultra Wideband if you want directional arrows when the item is nearby.

Remove the plastic battery tab completely. Place the tag next to your unlocked phone on the home screen. A Fast Pair prompt appears within seconds. If it does not, wait five minutes or toggle Bluetooth off and on.

## Step-by-Step Setup in Find Hub

1. Turn on Bluetooth and ensure the phone has internet access.
2. Hold the Pixel Tag right next to the phone and pull the battery tab.
3. Tap Connect on the popup that appears.
4. Review the Acceptable Use Policy and tap Continue.
5. Accept the Terms and Conditions.
6. Choose When using this app for location permission.
7. The tag appears in the Find Hub app.

After pairing, open Find Hub and select the new device. Choose a category such as Keys, Backpack, or Other. The app suggests a name based on your Google account. Edit it if needed. You can also adjust the ring volume and enable “Notify when left behind.”

If the popup never appears, go to Settings > Connected devices or open Find Hub and tap Add Device, then follow the prompts.

## Locate an Item Near or Far

Open Find Hub and select the tagged item. The map shows its last known location. When you are close enough for Bluetooth, tap Find nearby. Phones that support Bluetooth Channel Sounding or Ultra Wideband display a more precise distance and an arrow pointing toward the tag.

Tap Play sound to make the tag chime. The speaker reaches about 80 decibels at maximum volume. You can also trigger the chime from a Pixel Watch or by asking Gemini through Pixel Buds if those devices are signed into the same account.

If you have the tag but lost the phone, double-press the flush button on the back of the Pixel Tag. The phone rings. Press once to stop the sound, or unlock the phone and press the power button.

## Useful Features and Limits

The Pixel Tag includes NFC. Tap it against the back of a compatible phone to open the device details page in Find Hub. When the tag is marked Lost, a finder can see a short message, your email, or phone number so they can contact you.

Battery life is rated for over a year with a standard CR2032 coin cell. Replace it by pressing and twisting the cover counterclockwise, swapping the cell, and twisting clockwise. No tools are required. Find Hub warns you when the battery runs low.

Share the tag with up to 10 people so family members can help locate shared items. Carbon-fiber cases, aluminum luggage shells, or RFID-blocking wallets can weaken the signal. Place the tag in an outer fabric pocket of checked bags.

The tag is Android-only through Find Hub. It does not appear in Apple’s Find My network. Precision finding requires a phone with the right radio hardware; older or budget phones fall back to basic Bluetooth proximity.

## Tips for Reliable Tracking

- Test the ring sound and left-behind alert the same day you pair the tag.
- Name items clearly so multiple tags stay easy to distinguish.
- Check firmware and serial number inside Find Hub if troubleshooting is needed.
- Keep the tag outside metal or thick cases for stronger signal.
- Pair only items you own; unauthorized tracking of people is prohibited.

If you already use other Google device features, the Pixel Tag fits into the same Find Hub experience. For more on keeping Android devices secure, see the guide on [Android 17 Advanced Protection](/blog/android-17-advanced-protection-six-features/).

## Conclusion

Google Pixel Tag turns everyday objects into trackable items through Find Hub. Pairing is quick with Fast Pair, location updates appear on a map, and the built-in speaker plus optional Ultra Wideband help you recover items faster. Start with keys or a bag, confirm the alerts work, and the tracker is ready for daily use.
