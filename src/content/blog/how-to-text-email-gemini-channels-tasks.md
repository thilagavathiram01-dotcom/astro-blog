Google rolled out free Tasks in the Gemini app on 17 November 2026 for users in the United States, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. The feature runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen. In the US, you can also start tasks by texting Gemini or forwarding emails to gemini@google.com.

This guide shows how to enable the text channel, use email, and keep control of what Gemini does. It follows the official steps published on the Google Blog.

## What Channels add

Channels let you reach Gemini outside the app. After you verify a US phone number, a dedicated RCS thread appears in Google Messages or iMessage. You send a request the same way you text a contact. Gemini replies with progress, asks for confirmation before purchases or calls, and updates you when the task finishes.

Email works differently. Forward messages to gemini@google.com. Gemini extracts dates, creates calendar events, or drafts replies. Both options stay linked to your existing Gemini account and chat history.

Availability starts in the US. Text requires an eligible carrier and RCS support. Calls remain unavailable in certain states. Always review confirmations before Gemini acts on payments or bookings.

## Set up Text with Gemini

Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS. Tap your profile picture, then Settings. Choose Channels. Under Text with Gemini, tap Set up. Enter your US phone number and confirm the verification code. The setup completes in under a minute on supported carriers.

Once active, open your Messages app. A Gemini contact appears. Text a clear request such as “Add lasagna and strawberries to my Instacart order” or “Schedule a service appointment for my minivan next Friday morning.” Attach a photo if it helps, like a school flyer. Gemini processes the request in the background and texts updates.

You can reply in the same thread to adjust instructions. Gemini pauses before final actions that spend money or share personal details. For routine steps you trust, set “Always allow” in the task settings.

## Use email for tasks

Forward any email to gemini@google.com. In the example from Google’s announcement, a parent forwarded school notices. Gemini extracted dates, added them to Google Calendar, invited a spouse, and flagged a carpool conflict. The process takes less than a minute for simple batches.

Keep the subject line descriptive so Gemini understands the intent. You can also reply to Gemini’s confirmation emails if it sends any. All activity stays inside your Google account. Disconnect the channel anytime from Settings > Channels.

For more on background task automation, see our guide to [free Gemini Tasks for calls and shopping](/blog/how-to-use-free-gemini-tasks-calls-shopping/).

## Tips for reliable results

Start with low-stakes requests such as calendar updates or grocery lists. Specify preferred times, locations, and spending limits. Gemini uses Personal Intelligence when enabled, so it already knows your vehicles, home address, and connected apps.

Review every confirmation notification. If a site blocks automated steps, Gemini hands the final click back to you. Provide feedback with a thumbs-down when results miss the mark. This improves future runs.

Tasks run in an isolated virtual machine on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine. The environment is dedicated to your account and tested by red teams. You control app connections and can delete history at any time.

## Conclusion

Texting and emailing Gemini turns the assistant into a channel you already use. Free users in six countries can hand off multi-step work without opening the app every time. Set up the text channel in Settings, forward emails to gemini@google.com, and keep the confirmation prompts active.

The feature expands over time. Check Settings > Channels for new options as they roll out. Pair it with existing Gemini tools for calendar, shopping, and connected apps to reduce daily friction.
