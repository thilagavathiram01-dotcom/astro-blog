Google is giving Gemini users clearer control over their chats. On November 16, 2026, the company announced it is splitting the old Keep Activity switch into two separate settings: one for chat History and one for generative AI model training.

You can now keep a searchable record of conversations while deciding independently whether future chat content trains Google’s models. The change is rolling out globally in the Gemini app over the coming weeks. Google is also emailing users about the update.

This guide explains exactly what each setting does, how the new two-month retention works when History is off, and the steps to review or change the controls.

## What Changed from Keep Activity

Previously, the single Keep Activity (also called Gemini Apps Activity) setting controlled both how long chats were saved and whether their content could train generative AI models. Turning it off meant chats disappeared from your visible history and were not used for training, though Google still retained them briefly for service and safety reasons.

The new setup separates those choices. History controls whether chats appear in your Gemini History and how long they stay. A second setting controls whether chat content (text and attachments) is used to train generative AI models. Training settings are off by default. History is on by default for users 18 and older.

If your Keep Activity was already off, both new settings start off.

## How the Two Settings Work

**History setting**

- **On**: Chats are saved to your Gemini History. By default they auto-delete after 18 months. You can change this to 3 months, 36 months, or never auto-delete. You can also delete individual items or ranges manually at any time.
- **Off**: New chats do not appear in your visible History. Google still saves them with your account for around two months. They are used to respond to you, process feedback, and protect Google, its users, and the public (including with help from human reviewers). They are not shown in your History list.

This two-month period is longer than the previous temporary retention (previously described as 24 hours for context and 72 hours for safety when activity was off). Temporary chats follow the same roughly two-month retention.

**AI model training setting (Chat content including text & uploads)**

- **On**: Future chat content can be used to train generative AI models. This includes prompts, transcripts, photos, files, responses, tasks, connected-app data, and related usage info. Reviewed chats and related data can be retained for up to 3 years even if you later delete them or turn the setting off.
- **Off** (default): Future chats are not used to train generative AI models, unless you send feedback. Chats may still be reviewed to improve Google services in other ways and for safety.

A separate checkbox exists for audio and Gemini Live videos & screenshares. Training controls require History to be on before you can turn them on.

Even with training off, Google notes that settings do not control all processing (for example, anonymized data used to improve services, or the temporary retention described above).

## Step-by-Step: Check and Change the Settings

The controls live in Gemini History (myactivity.google.com/product/gemini or inside the Gemini app/web settings).

**On the web**

1. Go to gemini.google.com or directly to myactivity.google.com/product/gemini.
2. Click Settings → History (or the equivalent menu).
3. Near the top you can turn History on or off, or choose “Turn off and delete history.”
4. Look for the training options: “Chat content including text & uploads” and the audio/Gemini Live checkbox.
5. Select or deselect them, then confirm.

**In the Gemini mobile app**

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Tap your profile picture or initial → Gemini History.
3. If History is off, tap Off, review the disclosures, and turn it on if needed.
4. Choose the training checkboxes you want and tap Turn on (or turn them off).

You can also change the auto-delete period while you are in History settings. Work or school accounts have History settings controlled by the Google Workspace administrator.

## Practical Tips

- If you want a full history but no model training, leave History on and keep the training checkboxes unchecked.
- Turning History off hides chats from the list but does not immediately erase everything—expect roughly two months of retention for service and safety.
- Feedback you submit can still cause related chats to be reviewed even when training is off.
- Reviewed chats (disconnected from your account) can be kept up to three years.
- For personalization features that rely on past chats (such as Memory), History generally needs to be on. See our guide on [viewing and managing your Gemini Memory Summary](/blog/view-manage-gemini-memory-summary/) for related controls.
- Regularly review and delete specific chats if you prefer a smaller history footprint.
