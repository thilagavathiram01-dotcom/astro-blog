You open the same food delivery app every evening, scroll through past orders, and tap through the same screens. Gemini on Android can now handle that multi-step sequence for you after a single voice or text command.

Google rolled out task automation as a beta feature in the Gemini app for Pixel 10 series and Samsung Galaxy S26 devices, starting in the US and Korea. It runs selected apps in a secure virtual window so Gemini can navigate menus, fill details, and stop before any final payment or confirmation.

This guide covers what the feature does, which devices support it, and the exact steps to start a task.

## What Gemini Task Automation Does

Task automation lets Gemini complete multi-step actions inside supported apps without you opening them manually. You long-press the power button or open the Gemini app, describe the goal, and Gemini works in the background.

Examples Google shared include reordering a previous meal, booking a rideshare, or building a grocery cart from a list. The system shows a live notification so you can watch progress, pause, or stop at any time. Gemini never completes sensitive steps like payment or final order placement without your confirmation.

The feature is opt-in and limited to specific apps in food, grocery, and rideshare categories at launch. It uses a secure virtual window so Gemini only interacts with the target app, not the rest of your device.

## Devices and Requirements

You need a compatible phone and the latest Gemini app:

- Google Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, or Pixel 10 Pro XL
- Samsung Galaxy S26 series
- Gemini app updated from the Play Store
- Google Account signed in
- Region support (initially US and Korea)

Long-press the power button to confirm Gemini is your default assistant. If you still see Google Assistant options, update Play Services and the Gemini app, then restart the phone.

Google has indicated broader expansion to more apps and devices later in 2026 as part of Gemini Intelligence. Check Settings > Apps > Gemini for any automation or agent toggles once the feature appears on your device.

## Step-by-Step: Start a Multi-Step Task

1. Unlock your phone and long-press the power button to open Gemini. You can also open the Gemini app directly.

2. Speak or type a clear request. Examples:
   - “Reorder my last meal from DoorDash.”
   - “Book a ride home from my current location.”
   - “Add items from my grocery list in Keep to the cart.”

3. Gemini confirms the request and begins working. A notification appears with progress updates.

4. Tap the notification to watch the virtual window if you want. You can intervene or stop the task at any point.

5. When Gemini finishes the available steps, review the result and complete any final confirmation yourself.

The process keeps you in control. Gemini stops at payment screens or order confirmation buttons.

## Tips for Better Results

Use specific prompts. “Reorder my usual large pizza with extra cheese from the place I used last Tuesday” gives Gemini clearer context than a vague “order dinner.”

Keep the target apps updated. Automation works only with the apps Google has enabled for the beta. If a request fails, try rephrasing or check whether that app is supported.

Monitor the notification. Background tasks can take a minute or two while Gemini navigates screens. You can continue using other apps while it works.

Review before confirming. Because Gemini stops before final actions, always double-check quantities, addresses, and totals.

If you use Personal Intelligence or connected apps, ensure the relevant Google services (Gmail, Keep, Calendar) are linked in Gemini settings so context is available when needed.

## Limitations to Know

The beta supports a limited set of apps and is region-restricted at launch. Not every food or rideshare service works yet. Expansion is planned, but timing depends on Google and partner apps.

Gemini cannot access data outside the virtual window it creates for the task. Sensitive information stays protected, which is why final confirmations remain with you.

Battery and data use are modest for short tasks, but long navigation sessions will consume normal app resources.

## Conclusion

Gemini task automation removes the repetitive taps required for common daily actions on supported Pixel and Galaxy phones. Start with a clear voice command, watch the notification, and confirm only the final step. As Google expands the supported apps under Gemini Intelligence, the same workflow will cover more of the multi-step chores that currently require manual app switching.

Keep the Gemini app and your device software updated so new automation capabilities appear as they roll out.
