Google made Tasks in the Gemini app free on 17 November 2026 for users in the United States, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. The feature runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen and turns Gemini from a chat partner into an agent that keeps working after you walk away.

This guide shows exactly how to start a task, what it can do today, how confirmation works, and how to avoid common limits. All details come from Google’s official blog post and support documentation.

## What changed on 17 November

Until mid-November, Tasks (previously tested as Spark) required a Google AI Pro or Ultra plan. Google now offers the core experience at no extra cost in the six countries listed above. Advanced actions such as placing phone calls to businesses, completing purchases with Google Pay, texting Gemini, and forwarding email to gemini@google.com start in the United States first.

You need a personal Google Account, must be 18 or older, and must have Gemini activity history turned on. Work and school accounts are not supported yet. Availability outside the six countries still follows the earlier paid-plan rules.

## Switch from Chat to Task

1. Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS, or go to gemini.google.com on the web.
2. Sign in with your personal Google Account.
3. Look for the toggle labeled Chat and Task (above the prompt box on mobile, below it on the web).
4. Tap or click Task.
5. Type a clear description of the job. Include any timing, such as “this Friday morning” or “every weekday at 8 a.m.”
6. Optionally add files from Drive or Notebook, or type @ to invoke a skill.
7. Submit. Gemini shows its plan and begins work in the background.

You can return to the task thread at any time to add instructions or answer questions. Gemini is designed to pause and ask before critical steps such as clicking Buy or placing a call.

## Practical examples that work today

Google’s announcement highlighted several real uses:

- Analyze past utility bills from email and identify why a bill spiked.
- Research product options, compare prices via Google’s Shopping Graph, and prepare a checkout (with a spending cap you set).
- Add items to a grocery order after you send a photo or text description.
- Turn a batch of school or family emails into calendar events and flag conflicts.

In the United States, Gemini can also call businesses (it discloses that it is an AI), book appointments that match your calendar, and use the Agent Payments Protocol for tokenized card payments on supported Mastercard and Visa cards. Receipts land in Google Wallet.

For everyday free users outside the United States, the strongest results currently come from email and calendar work, file summarization, research, and multi-step drafting inside Google apps.

## Set up texting and email (United States)

Texting and email forwarding expand how you hand off tasks without opening the app:

1. Open Gemini and tap Settings.
2. Tap Channels.
3. Under Text with Gemini, tap Set up.
4. Verify your phone number (US number on an eligible carrier required).

You can also forward messages to gemini@google.com. Gemini extracts dates, creates calendar entries, and can suggest follow-up actions such as coordinating with other people.

## Stay in control and protect your data

Each task runs inside a hardware-isolated virtual machine on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine that is dedicated to your account. Google applies prompt-injection filters and Safe Browsing checks. You can:

- Jump into a running task and give new instructions.
- Pre-approve routine actions so Gemini stops asking every time.
- Set a hard spending cap for any purchase task.
- Disconnect apps or delete history at any time in settings.

Google notes that mistakes can still occur. Use the thumbs-down feedback when a task misses the mark. Businesses can opt out of receiving AI calls.

## Tips for better results

- Be specific about the outcome and any constraints (“find an appointment before 9 a.m. that fits my calendar”).
- Start with a simple research or calendar task before trying purchases or calls.
- Check the progress chip at the top of the task thread to see completed and remaining steps.
- If Gemini hands the final screen back to you, finish the last click yourself—this is expected on some sites while the web adapts.
- Review existing scheduled or recurring tasks if you already used the paid version; the free rollout does not remove them.

For more on background work and skills, see our earlier guide on [how to use Gemini Tasks mode for background work](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-tasks-mode-background-work/).

## Limits to expect

Free access still uses Gemini’s compute-based usage limits. Complex or frequent tasks consume more of your daily allowance. Calling is unavailable in certain US states (Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska) at launch. Checkout and texting features expand beyond the United States later. Always confirm sensitive actions yourself until you trust the pattern.

## Conclusion

Flipping the toggle from Chat to Task and describing the job is now enough for hundreds of millions of people to hand routine multi-step work to Gemini 4 Hydrogen. Start with calendar and email tasks today, set spending and confirmation preferences, and expand to calls or purchases only where they are available. The feature is designed to check with you before it spends money or books something irreversible—use that checkpoint.
