Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers now claim their monthly Google Cloud credits for Gemini API usage right inside Google AI Studio. The update, announced November 13, 2026, removes the need to jump between multiple Google sites for profile creation, billing setup, and redemption.

This guide walks through the exact flow using official steps from Google. You get clear amounts, the $5 prepay rule, and how credits roll over for 12 months. If you already use AI Studio for prototyping, this turns your subscription into practical API budget without extra setup friction.

## What Credits Your Plan Includes

Google AI Pro and Ultra plans include recurring monthly Google Cloud credits through the free Google Developer Program. These apply to Gemini API usage beyond your subscription quota in AI Studio.

Here are the official monthly amounts:

| Plan | Monthly Credits |
|------|-----------------|
| Google AI Plus | None |
| Google AI Pro | $10 |
| Google AI Ultra 5x | $40 |
| Google AI Ultra 20x | $100 |

Each monthly grant lasts 12 months from the issue date. Credits stack if you have not claimed earlier ones. The earliest-expiring credits are used first. Only the primary plan subscriber can redeem them. Family members cannot share the credits.

Upgrading your plan increases the monthly grant automatically. The credits cover paid-tier models and overages so you can keep building after free quotas run out.

## Prerequisites Before You Claim

You need an active Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription on a personal Google Account. Sign in to AI Studio with that same account. Google AI Plus does not include these credits.

If you do not yet have a Google Developer Program profile, the new AI Studio flow creates one for you when you redeem. No separate signup is required beforehand.

Using the Gemini API with Cloud billing requires a minimum $5 prepayment and balance. Promotional credits only apply after you complete this minimum transaction. Your prepaid balance is used only after the Cloud credits are exhausted. The $5 stays yours to spend on the API.

## Step-by-Step: Claim Credits Inside AI Studio

Follow these steps. The entire process stays inside AI Studio.

1. Open [aistudio.google.com](https://aistudio.google.com) and sign in with the Google Account linked to your AI Pro or Ultra plan.

2. Look at the left navigation. You should see an alert or card for unclaimed credits or your current balance. Click it to start the redemption dialog.

3. In the dialog, confirm or create your Google Developer Program profile. Select or set up a Cloud billing account in the same flow. Choose prepaid billing if prompted.

4. Make a deposit of at least $5. This unlocks the ability to draw down the promotional credits. The dialog may show options to auto-apply future monthly grants.

5. Complete redemption. Paid-tier API keys attach automatically. Track your balance and usage at [aistudio.google.com/billing](https://aistudio.google.com/billing).

The one-dialog flow handles profile creation, billing link, and credit claim together. Earliest-expiring credits apply first, and future grants can auto-apply to the same account.

## How Credits Work After Redemption

Credits cover Gemini API calls with your paid key for models in the Playground or in your own apps. They are consumed before your prepaid balance. When credits run out, requests continue against the prepaid amount you added (and any later top-ups). You can set a project spend cap so paid requests stop at a chosen limit.

Check your balance in the bottom-left account card or on the billing page. The page also shows Cloud Billing details and issued credits.

If you have been a Pro or Ultra subscriber for several months and already had a Developer Program profile, unclaimed credits may already be waiting. The left nav alert surfaces them on sign-in.

## Tips for Maximizing Your Credits

Claim early so the 12-month clock starts and you capture stacked grants. Enable auto-apply for future months to avoid missing new credits. Monitor the billing page regularly so you know when credits are low and can top up the prepaid balance before work stops.

Use credits for testing paid-tier models or longer agentic sessions once free quotas are exhausted. Pair this with tools like the Gemini API in your own projects or explore related workflows such as [building with Gemini 4 Hydrogen in AI Studio](/blog/gemini-4-hydrogen-ai-studio-guide/).

The $5 prepay is a one-time unlock for the credit system, not a recurring fee. Keep a small prepaid balance active so credits continue to apply.
