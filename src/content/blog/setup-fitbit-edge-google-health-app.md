Google launched the Fitbit Edge on October 12, 2026. The $179.99 tracker sits between the screenless Fitbit Air and the Pixel Watch 5. It includes a 1.34-inch AMOLED display, built-in GPS, and up to seven days of battery life. Pre-orders are open now, with devices shipping October 27.

Buyers receive three months of Google Health Premium. That unlocks the Gemini-powered Health Coach and, later this month, Health Guardian features. This guide shows how to pair the Edge with the Google Health app on Android or iPhone and start using its tracking tools.

## What You Need Before Setup

The Fitbit Edge works with most phones running Android 11 or higher or iOS 16.4 or higher. You need a personal Google Account. Workspace accounts from work or school do not link to Google Health. Install or update the Google Health app from the Play Store or App Store. The app replaced the Fitbit app in May 2026.

Charge the Edge with the included magnetic USB-C cable. A five-minute charge provides about one day of battery when low. A full charge takes roughly 90 minutes. Keep Bluetooth on and stay near your phone during pairing.

## Step-by-Step Setup in the Google Health App

1. Open the Google Health app and sign in with your Google Account. If this is your first time, follow the prompts to enter height, weight, and sex. The app uses these details for calorie and distance estimates.

2. Tap to add a device. A notification may appear automatically when the Edge is nearby and powered on. If not, open the app menu and select the option to pair a new tracker.

3. Confirm the pairing request on both the phone and the Edge screen. The Edge uses Bluetooth 5.4. Keep the devices within a few feet until setup finishes.

4. Allow the required permissions. The app needs Nearby devices and Location access on Android for setup and sync. On iPhone, grant Bluetooth access. Review any additional prompts for notifications or calendar access if you want those features.

5. Complete the on-screen profile steps if prompted. The Edge then syncs data automatically when nearby. Open the Today tab and pull down to force a manual sync if needed.

Once paired, the Edge appears under Devices in the app. You can check battery level and last sync time there. Existing Fitbit or Pixel Watch users can add the Edge to the same Google Account. Data from multiple devices stays in one place.

## Using Core Features After Pairing

The Edge tracks steps, heart rate, sleep stages, and elevation with its altimeter. Built-in GPS records outdoor routes without your phone. Google states the GPS is up to 40% more accurate than previous Fitbit trackers. Start workouts directly on the wrist from more than 50 exercise modes. Real-time guidance shows pace, heart rate zones, or splits.

Automatic activity detection recognizes up to 100 workout types later this month, including strength training and swimming. It logs sessions even if you forget to start one. The skin temperature sensor and SpO2 tracking run overnight. ECG readings for heart rhythm checks are available on-device.

Google Health Premium adds the Coach. Chat with it to set goals, adjust plans around travel or injuries, and receive weekly fitness routines. Health Guardian rolls out later in October for Premium users. It tracks blood pressure trends and insulin resistance trends in the background. Monthly reports appear if you meet wear requirements. These tools provide general wellness information, not medical diagnoses.

## Tips for Better Results and Battery Life

Wear the Edge snugly above your wrist bone for accurate heart rate and SpO2 readings. Clean the sensors regularly. Update the Google Health app whenever a new version appears. Newer app versions often improve sync and add features like expanded automatic detection.

Disable always-on display if you want the full seven-day battery. Google rates that mode at about two days. Use the five-minute fast charge when low. The Edge is water-resistant to 50 meters (5 ATM and IP68), so swimming is supported.

Connect third-party apps through Health Connect on Android or Apple Health on iPhone for a fuller picture. See our earlier post on [Fitbit Edge launch details](/blog/fitbit-edge-launch-october-12-what-to-know/) for the confirmed specs before purchase.

## Conclusion

Setup takes a few minutes once the Google Health app is ready and the Edge is charged. Pair it over Bluetooth, grant permissions, and data begins syncing. Premium subscribers gain the Coach immediately and Health Guardian later this month. The Edge delivers glanceable stats, GPS routes, and multi-day battery in a slim aluminum design that works with both Android and iPhone. Check the Google Store for pre-order and the app for the latest tracking tools.
