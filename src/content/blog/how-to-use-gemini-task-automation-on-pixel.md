Google’s Gemini can now handle multi-step tasks inside select apps on supported Pixel phones. Instead of opening DoorDash or Uber yourself, you describe the job and Gemini works in a secure virtual window while you continue using your phone.

This feature, part of Gemini Intelligence, expanded with the November 2026 Pixel Drop to Pixel 10 series phones (except the Pixel 10a) and remains available on the Pixel 11 series. It is currently limited to users 18 and older in the US, UK, India, Japan, and South Korea (with some device restrictions).

## What Gemini Task Automation Can Do

Gemini uses screen automation to interact with allowlisted apps. Supported actions include:

- Ordering groceries or takeout
- Booking or scheduling a ride
- Reordering a previous meal
- Making restaurant reservations (where supported)
- Adding items to a shopping cart

You stay in control. A notification shows progress. You can view the live session or stop the task at any time. Gemini cannot access the rest of your device outside the virtual window for the target app.

For related features on Pixel 10, see our [Gemini Intelligence guide for Pixel 10](/blog/gemini-intelligence-pixel-10-rambler-tasks-guide/).

## Requirements Before You Start

Confirm these conditions:

- Supported device: Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, Pixel 10 Pro Fold, or any Pixel 11 series model.
- Age 18 or older.
- Signed in with a personal Google Account (not work, school, or supervised).
- Located in a supported country: US, UK, India, Japan, or South Korea (Pixel 10 models are not supported in South Korea).
- Gemini app updated and screen automation enabled (check Settings in the Gemini app).

Update your phone through Settings > System > System update if the feature does not appear.

## How to Start a Task

1. Open the Gemini app or long-press the power button and say “Hey Google.”
2. Describe the task clearly. Examples:
   - “Book a ride to the airport for 6 pm.”
   - “Reorder my usual from Starbucks.”
   - “Add milk, eggs, and bread to my grocery cart.”
3. Confirm any details Gemini requests.
4. Gemini opens the required app in a secure virtual environment and proceeds.

A “Working on this task” card and system notification appear. Tap to view progress or stop the task.

## Tips for Better Results

Be specific with times, quantities, and preferences. “Order pizza for delivery” works better than a vague request. Include delivery address if it differs from your default.

Monitor the first few tasks. Gemini may ask for confirmation on payment or final details. You can interrupt and take over if needed on supported devices.

Check the Gemini app settings for the current list of supported apps in your region. Availability expands over time.

## Limits and Privacy

Tasks run only in allowlisted apps. Gemini cannot place calls, send messages outside the task, or access unrelated data. The virtual window isolates the session.

Usage limits may apply. The feature remains in beta on many devices. Availability and exact supported apps can vary by country and account.

If a task fails, retry with more details or complete it manually. Google notes that you should supervise closely and interrupt when needed.

## Conclusion

Gemini task automation removes several routine steps from your day on supported Pixel phones. Start with simple orders or rides, review the progress notification, and expand as you get comfortable with the controls.

The November Pixel Drop made this available on more devices and in more countries. Check your Gemini app after updating to see what is active for your account.
