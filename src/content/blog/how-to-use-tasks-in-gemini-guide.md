Gemini Tasks turns the app into a background agent that handles multi-step work while you do other things. Google made the feature free of charge for adults in the United States, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India starting 17 November 2026. It runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="89" />

Chat mode answers questions. Task mode plans steps, uses connected apps, and continues after you close the phone. You stay in control and can pause or edit at any time.

![Person using a smartphone for daily productivity](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1551650975-87deedd944c3?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What Tasks Can Do

Tasks handle research, scheduling, shopping comparisons, and document work. In supported regions they can call businesses, book appointments, and complete purchases through Google Pay with spending caps. Receipts land in Google Wallet.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="87" />

Examples from Google include calling dealerships for a service appointment, analysing utility bills via Password Manager, comparing chargers and applying a discount code, adding items to an Instacart order from a photo, and extracting school event dates from forwarded emails into Calendar.

The feature connects natively to Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Maps. It also links to more than 60 third-party apps such as Instacart. Each task runs in an isolated virtual machine on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine.

## How to Start a Task

1. Open the Gemini app on Android, iOS, or the web.
2. Flip the toggle from Chat to Task on the home screen.
3. Describe the outcome you want. Be specific about constraints, deadlines, and preferred apps.
4. Confirm permissions when Gemini asks to access Calendar, Gmail, or a third-party service.
5. Let it run. You receive a notification when it finishes or needs input.

You can also forward emails to gemini@google.com (US) or text Gemini after setting up Channels in Settings. Verify your phone number once.

For developers or advanced users, the same capability appears in Google AI Studio and Antigravity when you hold a Pro or Ultra plan, though the free rollout covers the consumer app in the listed countries.

## Permissions and Safety Controls

Gemini asks before critical actions such as clicking Buy or placing a call. You can set “Always allow” for routine steps like adding a grocery item. It discloses it is an AI when contacting businesses, and businesses can opt out.

Tasks use a sandboxed environment that is not shared across users. Prompt-injection filters and Safe Browsing limit links it opens. You can disconnect apps, delete history, or adjust task settings at any time. Google published a technical paper on the multi-layer defence approach.

Calling is unavailable in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska. Texting requires a US number with an eligible carrier.

## Tips for Better Results

- Give concrete success criteria: “Book the earliest Friday morning slot that appears on my Calendar.”
- Connect Personal Intelligence if you want context from past chats or photos. See our guide on [connecting Wallet and Drive to AI Mode](/blog/connect-wallet-drive-gemini-ai-mode-personal-intelligence/).
- Schedule recurring tasks for daily briefings or subscription reviews.
- Provide feedback with a thumbs-down when a result misses the mark so the system improves.
- Stay online for the first few minutes if the task needs to hand control back to you on a sign-in page.

Free users receive the core capability. Paid plans expand the number of concurrent or hourly scheduled tasks.

## Limits to Expect

Tasks can fail on sites that block automated traffic. In those cases Gemini reaches the final button and returns the screen to you. Some complex purchases still require manual confirmation. The feature is rolling out gradually, so availability can vary by account even inside supported countries.

It does not train directly on your full inbox or photo library. Limited prompt and response data may be used to improve the service under existing Gemini privacy controls.

## Conclusion

Tasks in Gemini moves the assistant from answering questions to completing jobs. Start with a small, well-defined request—such as sorting upcoming school dates or comparing two products—then expand to calls and purchases once you trust the permission flow. The free access in six countries makes it practical for everyday use without a subscription.

Update the Gemini app, flip the toggle, and hand off the next item on your list.
