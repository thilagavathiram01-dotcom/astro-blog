Tasks in Gemini turns the assistant from a chatbot into a personal helper that finishes multi-step jobs while you do other things. Google made the feature free for adults in the United States, India, Japan, Korea, Canada and Australia on 17 November 2026. It runs on the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model.

This guide shows exactly how to start a task, what it can handle, and how Google protects your data.

## What Tasks in Gemini Does

Chat mode answers questions. Task mode keeps working after you close the app. You describe the outcome you want. Gemini plans the steps, uses connected apps and the web, then reports back when it finishes or needs your input.

Examples from Google include booking a vehicle service appointment by calling dealerships, analysing a utility bill to find the cause of a high charge, adding items to a grocery order, and turning school emails into calendar events. Advanced actions such as phone calls and Google Pay purchases start in the United States.

The feature was previously called Gemini Spark. Existing tasks and schedules carried over when Google renamed it.

## Who Can Use It

You need a personal Google Account, to be 18 or older, and to have Gemini Activity History turned on. Work and school accounts are not supported.

The free rollout covers the United States, India, Japan, Korea, Canada and Australia. In most other countries it requires a Google AI Pro or Ultra plan. It is not available in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or Nigeria.

In the United States you can also forward emails to gemini@google.com or text Gemini after verifying your phone number. Calling businesses is unavailable in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana and Nebraska. Texting requires a US phone number on an eligible carrier.

## How to Start a Task

1. Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS, or go to gemini.google.com. On a Mac you can also use the Gemini desktop app.
2. Find the Chat / Task toggle. On mobile it sits above the prompt box. On the web and Mac app it sits below the prompt box.
3. Switch to Task.
4. Describe the finished result, not a list of clicks. Include dates, budget, preferred stores or any limits. Example: “Find the three cheapest USB-C chargers under 65 W that work with my phone, show total price with shipping, and prepare a cart. Do not buy anything.”
5. Add context if needed. You can upload files, add items from Google Drive, or reference a Notebook. Type @ to use a saved skill.
6. Submit. The task runs in the background. You can add more instructions later without losing progress. Use the Stop button if you want to halt it.

For the first try, keep the scope small so you can check the result quickly.

## Practical Examples

**Appointment booking.** “Our 2019 Honda Odyssey is overdue for service. Call the two nearest dealerships and book the earliest Friday morning slot that fits my Google Calendar in the next month.” Gemini uses Personal Intelligence for your vehicle and location, places the calls (where available), and adds the appointment.

**Bill analysis.** Give Gemini access to your utility portal through Password Manager and ask it to review recent statements in Gmail. It can identify the main cause of a surprise charge.

**Shopping.** “Compare USB-C chargers, ask me about wattage and colour, then help me check out with Google Pay. Set a $40 spending cap.” Receipts go to Google Wallet. You still earn card rewards. Gemini asks before final purchase unless you set a routine action to Always allow.

**Email processing.** Forward school or work emails to gemini@google.com. Gemini can extract dates, add events to your calendar, invite others, and flag conflicts.

Gemini currently connects with more than 60 popular apps. Developers can apply to the Connected Apps Platform.

## Safety and Controls

Each task runs inside a hardware-isolated virtual machine on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine. The environment is dedicated to your account and sandboxed. Prompt-injection filters and Google Safe Browsing limit which links Gemini can open.

Gemini pauses for confirmation before critical steps such as clicking Buy. You can set a hard spending cap. It discloses that it is an AI when it calls a business. Businesses can opt out of receiving AI calls.

You can adjust task settings, disconnect apps, or delete history at any time. If a result is wrong, use the thumbs-down feedback. Google notes that some websites still treat automated assistants as bots, so Gemini may hand the final click back to you.

## Tips for Better Results

- Write the outcome, constraints and preferred format. “Put the three options in a Google Sheet with total price, baggage rules and source links” works better than “flights to Tokyo”.
- Start with low-risk tasks until you trust the flow.
- Keep Activity History on; the feature requires it.
- Review the work panel to see completed, current and planned steps.
- Set Always allow only for routine actions you already trust.
- Provide feedback on misses so the system improves.

## Conclusion

Tasks in Gemini gives you a practical way to hand off everyday admin. The free rollout in six countries on Gemini 4 Hydrogen removes the previous subscription barrier for millions of people. Start with a clear, limited request, stay available for approvals, and let the assistant handle the hold music, the PDF digging or the price comparison.

Open Gemini, flip the toggle to Task, and try one job today.
