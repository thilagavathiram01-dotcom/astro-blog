Google rolled out Universal Cart to United States users on October 15, 2026. It collects products from multiple stores into one cart that works across Google Search and the Gemini app.

The cart uses the Shopping Graph of over 60 billion listings to watch prices and stock. It also pulls card perks and promo codes from Google Wallet. Checkout stays with the merchant as seller of record.

Follow these steps to start using it today.

## What You Can Do with Universal Cart

You save products while browsing Search (including AI Mode) or chatting in Gemini. Those items appear together later, organized by merchant. Progress stays available across sessions and devices on the same account.

Notifications arrive when a saved item drops in price or returns to stock. The cart surfaces relevant promo codes and compares your saved cards so you can pick the better rewards option. For participating stores you check out with Google Pay inside the cart. For others you transfer the list to the merchant site and finish there.

Participating merchants include Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta Beauty, Walmart, Wayfair, and many Shopify sellers. YouTube and Gmail support were previewed earlier but are not live yet. The feature is US-only at launch.

## Step 1: Save Products from Google Search

Sign in to your Google account. Open Search and go to AI Mode or ordinary Shopping results. Enter a normal query such as “noise-cancelling headphones under 200 dollars.”

On the product cards that appear, look for the save or add-to-cart control. Select it. The item enters Universal Cart immediately. Repeat for additional products from any retailers shown.

Google’s own example shows a handbag saved in AI Mode, then a sweater and lip gloss added later from the Gemini app. All three land in the same cart. You can leave and return later; the list remains.

## Step 2: Save Products from the Gemini App

Open the Gemini app. Ask for products in plain language. Try “recommend wireless earbuds with long battery life under 100 dollars.”

When results appear, use the save control on the ones you want. Gemini and AI Mode can place items in the cart, though they have limits on listing the full cart or showing live order status inside the chat itself. Switch to the dedicated cart view to see everything.

Items added in Gemini appear alongside those saved in Search. If you already connect other Google services to Gemini, this fits the same workflow. See our related guide on [connecting Google Wallet to Gemini](/blog/connect-google-wallet-gemini/).

## Step 3: Review and Manage the Cart

Open the cart from Search or Gemini. Products group by merchant. Each entry shows current price, return window where available, and stock status.

Remove any item you no longer want. After you buy, a “My Orders” tab may appear for merchants that share data. You can see status updates such as shipped or delivered. Not every merchant sends this information back to Google.

Cart data lives in Google Wallet. Manage privacy and associated information through Wallet settings.

## Step 4: Use Price Alerts and Savings Tools

The cart checks the Shopping Graph continuously. When a price falls or stock returns, you get a notification. This removes the need to revisit each retailer site yourself.

Promo codes that match items in the cart appear directly. Wallet integration highlights which of your stored cards offers stronger perks or cash-back for the stores involved. Review these before you decide to buy.

## Step 5: Check Out

Two paths exist:

- Google Pay inside the cart for participating merchants. Confirm the item, address, shipping method, and total, then pay with a saved card. Google processes the payment; the merchant owns the order and support.
- Transfer to the merchant site. Google fills the retailer’s cart with your selected items. You complete payment on their website.

You handle each merchant separately. Multi-store checkout in one transaction is not available.

## Extra Tips

Stay signed in to the same account on all devices. Use specific requests in AI Mode or Gemini so the suggestions stay relevant. Check the cart ahead of sales events so price alerts can surface deals. Note return windows before you pay. If a just-saved item is missing, refresh the cart view or switch between Search and Gemini.

## Current Limitations

Availability is limited to the United States. Gemini and AI Mode cannot always display the complete cart list or order updates inside the conversation. YouTube and Gmail integration remain on the roadmap with no announced date. Order tracking and in-cart Google Pay depend on merchant participation.

## Conclusion

Universal Cart gives US shoppers one persistent list across Search and Gemini. Save products from many retailers, receive automatic price and stock alerts, see promo codes and card perks, then choose Google Pay or a merchant-site transfer. The merchant stays the seller of record throughout.

Start with a Search or Gemini request, save the items you like, and return to the cart when you are ready. The list stays there.
