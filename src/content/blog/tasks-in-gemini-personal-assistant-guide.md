Tasks in Gemini now let you hand off multi-step work while you stay focused on other things. Google made the feature free for adults in the United States, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia, and India on November 17, 2026. It runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen for free users.

Unlike regular chat, Tasks continue in the background. Gemini plans steps, checks with you before critical actions, and finishes jobs such as booking appointments or adding items to a cart. You keep control and can stop any task at any time.

This guide shows how to start using Tasks today on the Gemini mobile app or at gemini.google.com.

## What Tasks can do

Tasks handle longer projects that used to require constant attention. Examples from Google include calling dealerships to book a service appointment, analyzing utility bills to find peak-hour costs, comparing products and checking out with a discount code, or forwarding school emails so every date lands on your calendar.

In the United States, additional capabilities are rolling out. Gemini can call businesses (it identifies itself as an AI), make purchases through Google Pay with spending caps, use Password Manager credentials on supported sites, and accept tasks via text message or email to gemini@google.com. Calling is not available in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska.

You can run up to 15 tasks at once. Gemini pauses and asks for confirmation before actions like completing a purchase. For trusted routine steps you can set “Always allow.”

## Requirements and availability

You must be 18 or older and signed in with a personal Google Account. Work and school accounts are not supported. Keep Gemini Activity on and opt in to Personal Intelligence where available.

Free access covers the six countries listed above. In other regions where Gemini is available, Tasks require a Google AI Pro or Ultra plan, except in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and Nigeria where the feature is not offered.

Tasks work in the Gemini app on Android and iOS, the Gemini app on Mac, and gemini.google.com. For developers interested in related models, see our earlier guide on [adding Gemini 4 Hydrogen to Android with Firebase AI Logic](/blog/add-gemini-4-hydrogen-android-firebase-ai-logic/).

## How to start a task

Open the Gemini app or go to gemini.google.com. On the home screen look for the toggle under or beside the prompt box. Switch it from Chat to Task.

Describe the job in clear language. Include the goal, any constraints, budget limits, or preferred timing. You can attach files from your device or Google Drive, or type @ to invoke a saved skill.

Tap or click Submit. Gemini begins working in the background and shows progress. You can add more instructions at any point without losing progress. To stop, use the Stop button in the interface.

For recurring work, tell Gemini to run the task on a schedule (for example every Monday morning) or manage it from the Scheduled page.

## Set up text and email channels (US)

Eligible US users with a supported carrier can assign tasks without opening the app.

For text messaging:
1. Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS.
2. Tap Settings.
3. Tap Channels.
4. Tap Set up under Text with Gemini.
5. Verify your phone number.

Once verified, you can text Gemini through Google Messages or iMessage. For email, forward messages to gemini@google.com. Gemini replies with a link to the new task.

## Shopping and payments

When you ask Gemini to buy something, it uses the Agent Payments Protocol. You still earn card points, cashback, or purchase protections. Set a hard spending cap in advance. Receipts and tracking go to Google Wallet (now expanding to iOS in the US).

Gemini asks for confirmation or pre-approval before clicking Buy. Review the cart and limits before approving. Connected Apps let Gemini work with services such as grocery platforms; more than 60 apps are already supported, and developers can apply to the Connected Apps Platform.

## Privacy and security controls

Google runs each task inside a hardware-isolated virtual machine dedicated to your account on Agent Compute Engine. The environment is sandboxed and tested by security red teams. Prompt-injection filters and Google Safe Browsing limit which links Gemini can open.

You can adjust task settings, disconnect Google or third-party apps, or delete chat history at any time. Gemini does not share payment or private account details unless you explicitly allow it. Businesses can opt out of receiving AI calls.

If a task misses the mark, give the response a thumbs down so Google can improve the system.

## Tips for better results

Write specific prompts that state the desired outcome and any limits. “Find the three cheapest USB-C chargers under 65 W, compare reviews, and show me options with a discount” works better than “find a charger.”

Start with simple tasks while you learn how Gemini checks in. Use Personal Intelligence so it already knows relevant details such as your location or vehicle. Review permissions before granting access to Password Manager or payment methods.

Check progress periodically on longer tasks. You can intervene or stop at any moment. For developers building agentic experiences, Hydrogen’s speed and the broader Gemini ecosystem make Tasks a useful reference for what users expect from background agents.

## Conclusion

Tasks in Gemini turn the app from a chat partner into a background assistant that finishes real work. The free rollout on Gemini 4 Hydrogen covers hundreds of millions of people in six countries, with advanced calling, shopping, and messaging features starting in the United States.

Open Gemini, switch to Task, and hand off your next item. Keep confirmation settings on for purchases and review the official blog post for the latest availability notes.
