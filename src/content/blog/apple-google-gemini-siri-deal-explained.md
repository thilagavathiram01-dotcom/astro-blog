Apple and Google announced a multi-year collaboration in January 2026. The next generation of Apple Foundation Models will be based on Google's Gemini models and cloud technology. These models will help power future Apple Intelligence features, including a more personalized Siri.

This partnership addresses Apple's need for stronger foundation models after delays in its own AI roadmap. Apple Intelligence continues to run on Apple devices and Private Cloud Compute to maintain privacy standards.

## What the Joint Statement Confirms

Google published the joint statement on its blog. Apple and Google entered a multi-year collaboration. The next generation of Apple Foundation Models is based on Gemini models and cloud technology.

These models power future Apple Intelligence features. A more personalized Siri was expected in 2026. After evaluation, Apple determined Google's AI technology provides the most capable foundation for its models.

Apple Intelligence stays on Apple devices and Private Cloud Compute. This keeps Apple's privacy approach intact. The companies did not disclose financial terms or exact model sizes in the official statement.

## How This Changes Siri for Users

The more personalized Siri draws on the new foundation models. It aims to understand context better and complete multi-step tasks across apps. Users should see improved natural language handling and better integration with personal data stored on the device.

On-device processing handles many requests. Heavier tasks use Private Cloud Compute. This hybrid setup balances speed and capability. Apple has clarified that it does not use the Gemini models exactly as Google deploys them to customers. Instead, it builds custom versions adapted for Apple Silicon and its privacy architecture.

Later statements from Apple executives reinforced that Google Assistant infrastructure and Google Search are not part of the system. The collaboration focuses on the underlying model technology.

## Privacy and Data Handling

Privacy remains central. Apple Intelligence runs on-device where possible. Private Cloud Compute handles sensitive requests with Apple's established controls. The joint statement explicitly states that Apple maintains its industry-leading privacy standards.

Users do not automatically share data with Google. Any cloud processing stays within Apple's Private Cloud Compute environment or approved infrastructure. This differs from simply calling an external Gemini API. The design keeps personal context on Apple-controlled systems.

## Timeline and Rollout Expectations

The announcement pointed to a more personalized Siri arriving in 2026. Apple showed progress at its developer events later that year. Features rolled out gradually, starting with supported devices and expanding over time.

Initial availability focused on regions where Apple Intelligence was already active. Some markets faced delays due to regulatory requirements. Users on older devices may need hardware upgrades for the full on-device experience.

Check Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri for status on your device. Updates arrive through regular iOS releases rather than a single download.

## Tips for Getting the Most from the Updated Siri

1. Enable Apple Intelligence if your device supports it. Go to Settings and look for the Apple Intelligence section.
2. Grant necessary app permissions carefully. Review what Siri can access for tasks like calendar or messages.
3. Use natural language. The improved models handle conversational requests better than older versions.
4. Monitor privacy settings. Review Private Cloud Compute usage in the same settings menu.
5. Test multi-step requests. Ask Siri to chain actions across apps once the personalized features are active.

These steps help you prepare as the models roll out more widely.

## Conclusion

The Apple-Google Gemini collaboration gives Apple access to capable foundation models while preserving its on-device and Private Cloud Compute model. Users gain a more personalized Siri without shifting data control to Google. The partnership builds on years of existing search and technology ties between the two companies.

As Apple continues rolling out features, the focus stays on practical improvements to daily tasks. Follow official Apple and Google channels for the latest device support lists.
