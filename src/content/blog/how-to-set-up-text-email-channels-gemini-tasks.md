Gemini Tasks now lets you hand off longer projects for free in several countries. You can also reach Gemini by text or by forwarding emails, starting in the United States.

This guide shows exactly how to enable the text channel, forward emails, and assign a task. All steps come from Google’s official announcement on November 17, 2026.

## What Gemini Tasks Offers

Tasks in Gemini run on the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model. The feature is free for eligible users in the United States, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India.

You flip a toggle from Chat to Task in the Gemini app. Gemini then handles multi-step work in the background under your direction. It can call businesses, shop with Google Pay, connect to apps such as Instacart, and update your Calendar.

Google designed the system so Gemini discloses it is an AI when it calls a business. You can set spending limits. Receipts go to Google Wallet. The service uses isolated virtual machines on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine.

For more on the free rollout, see our guide to [free Gemini Tasks](/blog/how-to-use-tasks-in-gemini-free-guide-for-daily-assistance/).

## Set Up Text with Gemini

Starting in the United States, you can text Gemini to assign tasks. The option uses RCS on supported carriers.

Follow these steps on Android or iOS:

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Tap Settings.
3. Tap Channels.
4. Tap Set up under Text with Gemini.
5. Verify your phone number.

Once verified, you can text Gemini the same way you message a contact. Describe the task. Gemini processes it in the background and replies with updates or results.

Google notes that you must have a US phone number with an eligible mobile carrier for the text channel.

## Forward Emails to Gemini

You can also forward emails to gemini@google.com. This is useful for school notices, bills, or appointment confirmations.

Forward the message from Gmail or another client. Gemini extracts dates, creates Calendar events, and can flag conflicts. In one example from Google, forwarding school emails added every date to the calendar and suggested a carpool swap.

The email address works for users who have enabled Tasks. Results appear in the Gemini app chat history.

## Assign Your First Task

Open the Gemini app on web, Android, or iOS. Switch the toggle from Chat to Task. Type a clear instruction.

Examples that match Google’s documented use cases:

- Call the two nearest dealerships and book the earliest Friday morning service appointment next month.
- Analyze past utility bills in my email and find why the electric bill jumped.
- Add lasagna, strawberries, cucumbers, and vanilla ice cream to my Instacart order, then include this photo of an empty cereal box.

Gemini asks clarifying questions when needed. It pauses before high-impact actions such as clicking Buy. You can set “Always allow” for routine steps.

## Security and Controls

Each task runs in a hardware-isolated virtual machine dedicated to your account. Prompt-injection filters scan webpages and emails. An Action Safety Agent requires confirmation before critical steps.

You can disconnect apps, delete history, or adjust task settings at any time. Google published a technical paper on the multi-layer defenses.

If a task misses the mark, give the response a thumbs down. This feedback helps improve the system.

## Tips for Better Results

Keep instructions specific. Include dates, locations, and preferred times. Connect the Google apps Gemini already knows, such as Gmail, Calendar, and Password Manager.

Start with one task. Review the live transcript or progress updates. Confirm the final action yourself when Gemini hands the screen back.

Availability and features vary by country and language. Some calling features are limited in certain US states. Check the Gemini app for the latest status in your region.

## Conclusion

Text and email channels extend Gemini Tasks beyond the app window. You can assign work from your inbox or messaging app and continue with your day.

The feature is rolling out free of charge on Gemini 4 Hydrogen in the listed countries. Try the toggle today and forward one email to see how Gemini handles the next step.
