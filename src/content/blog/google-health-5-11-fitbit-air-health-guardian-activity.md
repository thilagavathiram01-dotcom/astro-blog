Google Health 5.11 brings two practical upgrades for Fitbit Air and Pixel Watch owners. Improved automatic activity tracking now detects up to 100 workout types. Health Guardian blood pressure and insulin resistance trends also reach Fitbit Air users who subscribe to Google Health Premium.

These features run in the background once set up. You wear the device, meet simple wear requirements, and receive monthly reports plus coaching insights.

## What Google Health 5.11 Changes

Automatic activity tracking expands from roughly 10 detected activities to more than 100. Strength training, swimming, racket sports, and team sports join the list. The system uses more context from your routines, history, and edits. Google states this delivers nearly twice the accuracy and cuts missed workouts by more than half.

Health Guardian adds blood pressure trends and insulin resistance trends for Fitbit Air (and Fitbit Edge) under a Google Health Premium subscription. Pixel Watch 3, 4, and 5 already receive the main Health Guardian tools without an extra fee. First monthly reports for eligible Fitbit Air users arrive in early November if wear requirements are met in October.

Sleep tracking improvements and expanded continuous glucose monitor options also appear in the update. Sleep breathing quality for Fitbit Air is expected soon.

## Prerequisites Before You Start

Confirm the Google Health app is updated to version 5.11 on your Android or iOS phone. Pair a compatible device: Fitbit Air, Pixel Watch 3/4/5, or the upcoming Fitbit Edge.

For Health Guardian trends on Fitbit Air you need an active Google Health Premium subscription. Pixel Watch users get the core features without it. Wear the device consistently. Blood pressure trends require at least five consecutive days in the month. Insulin resistance trends need seven or more consecutive days and nights.

If you previously used the Fitbit app, your history transfers when you sign in with the same Google or Fitbit account.

## Set Up Health Guardian Trends

Open the Google Health app and go to the Health tab. Look for the Health Guardian section.

1. Tap into Health Guardian.
2. Review the available trends: blood pressure and insulin resistance for Fitbit Air Premium users.
3. Follow any on-screen prompts to enable tracking.
4. Keep the tracker or watch worn for the required consecutive days.

The features work continuously in the background. No manual measurements are needed for the trends themselves. At the start of the next month you receive a summary. Google Health Coach (available with Premium) can translate the patterns into lifestyle suggestions and notes you can share with a doctor.

Pixel Watch 4 and 5 users in select European countries also gain Breathing Emergency Detection, which can prompt an emergency call on severe blood-oxygen drops.

## Use the Improved Automatic Activity Tracking

Once Google Health 5.11 is installed and your device is paired, the upgraded detection activates automatically.

Start a workout or simply move as usual. The tracker or watch looks for patterns matching more than 100 activities. When it detects one that meets the duration threshold (15 minutes by default), it logs the session.

Review logged activities in the app. Confirm or edit the type and details. Each confirmation helps the system learn your patterns and improves future detections.

You can still start activities manually from the Google Health app when you want precise control, especially for structured sessions.

## Tips for Better Results

Wear the Fitbit Air snug but comfortable, just above the wrist bone. Consistent placement improves sensor accuracy for heart rate and other signals that feed the trends.

Charge regularly so the device stays active during the multi-day wear windows required for Health Guardian reports.

If a detected activity looks wrong, edit it promptly. The model incorporates your corrections.

For Fitbit Air users wanting Health Guardian, confirm Premium is active before the start of the month so data collection counts toward the first report.

Check the Health tab after the first few days of consistent wear to verify trends are appearing.

## Conclusion

Google Health 5.11 makes daily tracking more hands-off and adds longer-term trend insights for Fitbit Air owners who subscribe to Premium. Update the app, enable the features in the Health tab, and meet the wear requirements. Monthly summaries and more accurate workout logs follow.
