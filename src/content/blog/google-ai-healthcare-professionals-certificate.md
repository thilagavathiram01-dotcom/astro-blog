Healthcare professionals spend hours on emails, schedules, and paperwork instead of patient care. Google just released a practical certificate designed to change that.

On October 29, 2026, Google announced the **Google AI for Healthcare Professionals Certificate**. It is a self-paced program of four short courses that takes under five hours total. The focus is strictly non-clinical: prompting skills, patient communication materials, administrative tasks, and professional learning. It does not cover diagnosis or treatment decisions.

The program was built by clinicians at Google and is approved for up to 5.0 AMA PRA Category 1 Credits™, jointly provided by iiCME and Google LLC. Institutions including NYU Grossman School of Medicine, University of Alabama at Birmingham, Touro University, and Purdue University School of Nursing are already offering it.

## What the Certificate Covers

The four courses build on each other and stay focused on daily work:

1. **AI Fundamentals for Healthcare Professionals** (about 2 hours) — Identify safe tasks for AI, evaluate outputs with clinical judgment, and protect patient privacy.
2. **Strengthen Patient Communication with AI** (1 hour) — Turn medical jargon into plain-language summaries and visual or audio guides patients can understand.
3. **Optimize Healthcare Administrative Tasks with AI** (1 hour) — Extract action items from emails, solve scheduling problems, and turn documents into audio briefings.
4. **Advance Healthcare Professional Development** (1 hour) — Summarize research papers, build presentations, and prepare for exams or continuing education.

Tools used in the curriculum include Gemini, Gemini Notebook, and Gemini for Work. The skills apply to any AI tools approved by your institution.

## How to Enroll and Complete It

Follow these steps to start:

1. Visit [grow.google/healthcare-professionals](https://grow.google/healthcare-professionals/) or go directly to the Coursera page for the Google AI for Healthcare Professionals Specialization.
2. Click Enroll. In the U.S. and Canada it is available through a Coursera monthly subscription of $49. Prices vary by country. You can finish at your own pace, so most people complete it within one billing cycle.
3. Work through the four courses in order. Each includes videos, activities, and assessments. No prior AI experience is required.
4. After finishing, claim the certificate and, if eligible, the CME credits. Physicians can claim up to 5.0 AMA PRA Category 1 Credits™. Nurses, physician assistants, and pharmacists should check their respective credentialing bodies (ANCC, NCCPA, or state pharmacy boards) for acceptance.

The activity is valid from October 29, 2026 through October 29, 2029.

## Practical Tips for Healthcare Teams

- Treat every AI output as a draft. Always apply your clinical judgment before sharing anything with patients or colleagues.
- Never enter Protected Health Information (PHI) into consumer AI tools. Use only institution-approved, HIPAA-compliant environments.
- Start with low-risk tasks: summarizing long email threads, creating plain-language handouts, or drafting journal club slides.
- Share the certificate with your department. Several medical and nursing schools are already integrating it into training.
- Pair it with other Google tools you already use. If you work in Gmail or Docs, the prompting skills transfer directly. See our guide on [using Gemini in Gmail](/blog/gemini-in-gmail/) for everyday examples.

## Who Should Take It

The certificate is open to anyone in healthcare who wants practical AI skills: physicians, nurses, residents, pharmacists, therapists, administrators, and students. It is especially useful for people who feel they spend too much time on documentation and coordination.

Google’s Chief Health Officer Michael Howell noted that clinicians want training so they can focus on patients rather than screens. The curriculum was informed by draft AAMC AI competencies and reviewed by healthcare organizations.
