Gemini Intelligence brings the strongest Gemini capabilities directly to supported Android phones so your device can handle more of the daily work for you. On 10 November 2026 Google expanded it to additional devices, including the Pixel 10 series and the new Motorola signature 27.

This guide shows what the feature does, which phones support it, and how to use the main tools: Rambler voice typing, task automation, custom widgets, and Device Assistance.

![Person holding a black Android smartphone](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1592890288564-76628a30a657?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What Gemini Intelligence does

Gemini Intelligence is Google’s on-device intelligence layer for its most advanced Android phones. It surfaces helpful actions, cleans up voice input, and can complete multi-step tasks across apps when you ask.

It first arrived on Pixel 11 and selected Samsung Galaxy devices earlier in 2026. The November update brings the same core experience to more phones and adds a few new capabilities.

Feature availability still depends on your device, country, and language. Some tools require you to be 18 or older.

## Supported devices right now

These phones currently receive Gemini Intelligence or the latest feature set:

- Pixel 11 series (original launch devices)
- Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold (via the November 2026 Pixel Drop)
- Samsung Galaxy S26 series, Z Fold8, Z Fold8 Ultra, and Z Flip8
- Motorola signature 27 (pre-order in the US starting 19 November 2026)

Check Android Settings or the Google Store device list if you are unsure whether your phone is included. Updates roll out gradually.

## Use Rambler for cleaner voice typing

Rambler upgrades Gboard voice input. Instead of a raw transcript full of filler words, pauses, and self-corrections, it produces clean, organized text in real time.

**How to try it:**

1. Open any app that uses Gboard (Messages, Gmail, Notes, etc.).
2. Tap the microphone icon.
3. Speak naturally. You can say “um,” change your mind mid-sentence, or add details out of order.
4. Rambler removes the filler, fixes grammar, and formats the result.

Example: “Let’s meet Wednesday at Mario’s no actually Amy’s pizza and I’ll order the pineapple” becomes a clear sentence ready to send. It is available on Pixel 10-series devices and the Motorola signature 27, subject to language and country support.

## Hand off multi-step tasks

Task automation lets you ask Gemini to handle logistics such as ordering food, booking a ride, or making a reservation across supported apps.

On supported devices you simply describe the job in the Gemini app or via voice. Gemini plans the steps, works in the background, and pauses for your confirmation on important actions (payments, final submissions).

The feature is expanding to the UK, India, and Japan in addition to earlier markets. You remain in control—Gemini asks before completing critical steps.

## Create custom widgets with a prompt

Create My Widget (rolling out over the following weeks) lets you describe a widget in plain language. Gemini builds it and can pull data from Gmail, Calendar, and Search.

Examples:

- Trip dashboard showing flights and hotel details
- Monthly spending tracker
- Countdown to a race or event

No coding is required. The widget appears on your home screen so the information is visible at a glance. It is also coming to Pixel Watch in the weeks after the November announcement.

## Get help with Device Assistance

Device Assistance turns Gemini into a personal expert for your phone. You can ask it to:

- Free up storage space
- Show which apps have location access
- Mute distractions while still allowing important calls

It works on select devices running Android 17 or later. Speak or type the request and Gemini performs the change or guides you through the settings.

## Tips for better results

- Speak naturally with Rambler—corrections and filler are expected.
- Be specific with task automation (include time, budget, preferred apps).
- Review any action that involves money or personal data before confirming.
- Check Settings > Gemini or your device’s AI settings if a feature is missing; some require a software update.
- Feature availability varies by region. Confirm support for your country before relying on a new tool.

## Conclusion

Gemini Intelligence moves more of the busywork onto the phone itself. With the November 2026 expansion, Pixel 10 owners and early Motorola signature 27 buyers gain Rambler, stronger task automation, custom widgets, and Device Assistance. Start with voice typing or a simple task and expand from there as the features settle on your device.
