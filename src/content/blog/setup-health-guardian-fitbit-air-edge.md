Google is rolling out Health Guardian features to Fitbit Air and the new Fitbit Edge later this month for Google Health Premium subscribers. These tools track subtle shifts in blood pressure and insulin resistance trends in the background and deliver monthly summaries in the Google Health app.

Pixel Watch 3, 4, and 5 owners already have access free of charge. Fitbit users need the Premium subscription, which costs $9.99 per month or $99 per year and is included with Google AI Pro and Ultra. The features provide general wellness insights only and are not medical diagnoses or substitutes for professional care.

## What Health Guardian Tracks

Health Guardian runs quietly on supported devices. It looks for patterns in heart rate, motion, and other sensors over multiple days. Blood pressure trends require at least five continuous days of wear in a week, with overnight wear recommended for better signal quality. Insulin resistance trends need seven days and seven sleep sessions in a month.

The first reports appear at the start of the following month if you meet the wear minimums. The Health Coach can then explain any shifts, suggest lifestyle adjustments, and help prepare notes for a conversation with your doctor. Google states the tools are backed by clinical research and trained on large volumes of sensor data.

## Requirements and Preparation

You need the Google Health app version 5.09 or higher on Android or iOS. Update it from the Play Store or App Store before starting. Pair your Fitbit Air or Edge first. The setup steps for the Edge are covered in our guide on [How to Set Up Fitbit Edge with Google Health App](/blog/setup-fitbit-edge-google-health-app/).

Confirm your Google Health Premium subscription is active. Edge buyers receive three free months. The device must stay charged and sync regularly. Availability can vary by country and region. Some markets use different names such as Vascular Stress Trends or Metabolic Stress Trends.

## Step-by-Step: Enable Blood Pressure and Insulin Trends

1. Open the Google Health app and sign in with the Google Account linked to your device.

2. Tap your profile picture or initial in the top right corner.

3. Select Google Health settings, then Health Guardian.

4. Under Blood pressure trends, tap Turn on and confirm. Repeat for Insulin resistance trends if the option appears.

5. Review any consent screens about data use and wellness-only status. Accept to continue.

6. Wear the tracker consistently. Aim for the required consecutive days and overnight coverage so the first monthly report can generate.

You can turn either feature off later from the same Health Guardian menu. High or low heart rate alerts and other safety tools may appear in related sections depending on your device.

## Reading Your Monthly Reports and Using the Coach

At the beginning of each month the app places a summary in the Health tab. Blood pressure trends appear under the Heart section. Insulin resistance trends sit in the Metabolic section. Sleep breathing quality, available on Pixel Watches, shows in Sleep and Respiratory views.

Open the Coach chat and ask it to explain any flagged patterns. It can suggest adjustments to activity, sleep, or nutrition based on your tracked data. Share the summary with your doctor if you want professional interpretation. The app does not diagnose conditions or recommend medication.

## Expanded Automatic Activity Tracking

Later this month Google is also improving automatic activity detection on Fitbit Air, Fitbit Edge, and Pixel Watch 3, 4, and 5. The system now recognizes more than 100 workout types, up from 10, including strength training, swimming, and racket sports. Contextual reasoning uses your history, manual edits, and optional location data to raise accuracy nearly twofold and cut missed sessions by more than half.

You do not need to start every workout manually. The tracker logs sessions in the background and the app surfaces them for review. This pairs well with Health Guardian because more complete activity data improves the context the Coach uses for recommendations.

## Tips for Reliable Readings

Wear the tracker snugly above the wrist bone. Clean the sensors if readings seem inconsistent. Charge overnight if needed so the device stays on during sleep. Force a sync in the app if the last update is more than a day old.

Check the Google Health settings after any app update. New versions may surface additional Health Guardian options or refine the monthly summary layout. Connect other apps via Health Connect on Android if you want a broader view of activity and nutrition.

## Conclusion

Health Guardian adds proactive monthly trend tracking to Fitbit Air and Edge for Premium subscribers. Enable the features in the Google Health app settings, meet the wear requirements, and review the reports that arrive at the start of each month. The Coach helps translate the numbers into practical steps. Combined with the expanded automatic workout detection rolling out later in October, the tools give a clearer picture of longer-term patterns without constant manual logging.
