Google expanded Gemini in Chrome to all Android users in India on 7 October 2026. At the same time, the agentic Auto Browse feature arrived for Google AI Pro and AI Ultra subscribers on both desktop and Android.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="29" />

Auto Browse lets Gemini navigate sites, click, scroll, and fill forms for multi-step tasks while you stay in control. This guide shows exactly how to start, what it can do, the limits, and how to use it safely.

## Who Can Use Auto Browse in India

Gemini in Chrome itself is free for Android users in India. You can summarise long articles, ask questions about the current page, connect Keep or Calendar, and edit images with Nano Banana without leaving the browser.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="29" />

Auto Browse is different. You need:

- A personal Google account with an active Google AI Pro or Google AI Ultra plan.
- Age 18 or older.
- Latest Chrome, signed in, with the device language set to English.
- Opted in to Gemini in Chrome.
- Safe Browsing set to Standard or Enhanced.

It does not work in Incognito mode or with most school accounts. Work accounts need admin approval. The feature is rolling out gradually, so a paid account may not see it immediately.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="157" />

Daily caps are 20 multi-step requests for AI Pro and 200 for AI Ultra. Chrome notifies you when you hit a limit or the concurrent-task cap.

## How to Start Auto Browse

Open Chrome on desktop or Android.

1. Click or tap the Ask Gemini icon (sparkle) at the top of the window or the bottom action bar on Android.
2. Describe one clear multi-step task in the side panel or chat.
3. Review the plan Gemini shows. It lists the sites it intends to visit and any personal details it may share.
4. If the plan matches what you want, click or tap Start Task.

Gemini opens a dedicated tab marked with an Auto Browse icon. You can switch to it, watch the progress, or leave the browser. On Android you can switch apps and return when it needs you.

## Practical Tasks That Work Well

Google lists everyday examples such as booking parking for an event, filling a long form, or ordering groceries for the week.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="29" />

Other reliable uses include:

- Comparing hotel or flight options across dates and shortlisting those that meet your budget and cancellation rules.
- Researching product prices across sites and adding suitable items to a cart.
- Collecting information for a trip or event and organising it.
- Filling repetitive forms after you review the data.

Keep prompts specific. State the outcome, constraints (budget, dates, sites), and boundaries (“do not book or pay without asking me”). Vague requests produce weaker plans.

## Safety Controls and Confirmation Steps

Auto Browse is experimental. Google trains models to detect prompt injection and designs the feature to keep you in the loop.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="29" />

Before sensitive actions it pauses for confirmation. These include finalising a financial transaction, accepting terms, creating an account, sending messages, or submitting certain forms. You can also take over the task at any time by clicking Take over task on the active tab, complete the step yourself, then hand it back.

With permission, Gemini can use Google Password Manager to sign in to sites without seeing the actual passwords. You can review and revoke those site permissions later in Chrome Settings → AI innovations → Gemini in Chrome.

You remain responsible for what the agent does during a task, including unintended purchases or mistakes. Always review the plan and stay available for confirmation prompts.

## Tips for Better Results

- Start with one clear task instead of chaining many goals.
- Name preferred sites when you know them (“check Flipkart and Amazon”).
- Review the plan carefully; edit or cancel if the sites or data look wrong.
- Keep the device language English for Auto Browse while the rest of Gemini in Chrome supports more Indian languages.
- Monitor the first few runs closely so you learn how it behaves.
- For enterprise or work profiles, check your organisation’s AI policies. Related controls appear in [Android Enterprise updates for 2026](/blog/android-enterprise-2026-gemini-xr-controls/).

## Conclusion

Gemini Auto Browse in Chrome is now available to AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in India on desktop and Android. Free users still gain the summarisation, page Q&A, Connected Apps, and Nano Banana features on Android. Start with small, well-scoped tasks, always review the plan, and treat confirmation prompts as the safety boundary they are designed to be.

As the rollout continues and language support expands, more everyday web chores can move into the background while you keep final control.
