You leave the house and realise your keys are still on the kitchen counter. Google Find Hub now lets you set Left-Behind Reminders so your phone alerts you when a tagged item or accessory moves out of range.

The feature arrived through October 2026 Google System Updates in Play services. It works with supported Find Hub tags, including the upcoming Pixel Tag, and can cover headsets or other compatible items. Pixel Tag itself launches on 11 November 2026.

This guide shows how to check for the feature, turn on reminders, and set exclusion zones so you are not notified every time you leave items at home.

## What Left-Behind Reminders Do

Left-Behind Reminders notify you when your phone no longer detects a paired tag, item, or headset nearby. The alert includes the last known location so you can go back and collect it.

Google listed the capability in Play services v26.41 release notes. A later Find Hub app update adds more controls for tags, items, and headsets. You can enable the reminder per device and create places where alerts stay silent.

The system relies on Bluetooth proximity and the Find Hub network. Alerts depend on connectivity and device settings. Google notes that timing and reliability vary.

## Check for the Update First

The feature reaches users through Play services and the Find Hub app. Update both before you look for the new options.

1. Open the Play Store.
2. Tap your profile picture, then Manage apps & device.
3. Check for updates to Google Play services and Find Hub.
4. Restart your phone after the updates finish.

Play services v26.39 added a better registration flow that lets you configure Left-Behind Reminders during initial tag setup. Version 26.41 made the reminders available in Find Hub.

If the option is missing, the rollout may still be in progress for your account or region. Check again after a few days.

## Enable Reminders on a Tag or Accessory

Open the Find Hub app and select the device you want to protect.

1. Launch Find Hub.
2. Tap the tag, headset, or item under Devices.
3. Look for “Notify when left behind” or a similar Left-Behind section.
4. Toggle the switch on.

For a new Pixel Tag or compatible tracker, the setup flow may ask about reminders right after pairing. You can name the item, choose a category such as keys or bag, and enable the alert in the same steps.

Once enabled, the phone monitors Bluetooth range. When you walk away, a notification appears with the last location.

## Set Exclusion Zones

Constant alerts at home become annoying. Find Hub lets you mark places where you intentionally leave items.

1. In the device details, find the Left-Behind or notifications section.
2. Look for an option to add an exclusion zone or “Don’t notify at this location.”
3. Choose whether the zone applies to all devices or only the current one.
4. Confirm the place (home is the common choice).

The phone will skip the reminder when you leave the item inside that zone. You can add more zones later if needed.

Strings in recent Find Hub versions confirm support for both global and per-device exclusion zones.

## Tips for Reliable Alerts

Keep Bluetooth on and location services enabled for Find Hub. The feature uses proximity data from nearby Android devices in the network.

Test the reminder in a low-stakes setting. Leave the tagged keys in another room and walk away. Confirm the notification arrives and shows the correct location.

For Pixel Tag, wait until 11 November 2026 when it goes on sale. Pricing starts at $29 for a single tag. It requires Android 9 or newer and Bluetooth 6.0 or later on the phone. You can share the tag with up to 10 people.

Existing third-party tags that already work with Find Hub may gain the reminder after the Play services update. Check both the Find Hub app and the manufacturer’s app.

## Conclusion

Left-Behind Reminders turn Find Hub into a practical daily tool for keys, bags, and headphones. Update Play services and Find Hub, enable the toggle on each important item, and add an exclusion zone for home.

The feature is rolling out now ahead of the Pixel Tag launch. Test it once it appears so you know the alerts match your routine.
