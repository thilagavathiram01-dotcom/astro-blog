Google announced new Android protections against mobile scams on October 14, 2026. The headline feature is Enhanced Protection Mode in Google Messages. It adds cloud Gemini analysis via Private AI Compute for complex conversations, plus smarter on-device link checks with Gemini Nano.

These updates address rising sophisticated fraud. Scammers use spoofed numbers, fake bank alerts, and slow-burn conversations. Android already flags more than 5 million suspected scam calls and texts monthly. The new mode strengthens that defense for users who opt in.

Enhanced Protection rolls out in the coming weeks as an optional setting. It works alongside existing on-device Scam Detection. Privacy stays intact because cloud processing happens in an encrypted dedicated space inaccessible to Google.

## What Enhanced Protection Adds

Enhanced Protection Mode unlocks three main improvements in Google Messages.

First, Scam Detection gains Private AI Compute. On-device models already catch traditional patterns. The cloud Gemini models now evaluate complex context and intent faster in chats with unknown senders. This catches novel tactics that try to hide over multiple messages.

Second, link safety improves with Gemini Nano on supported devices. Existing URL checks flag known bad sites. Gemini Nano adds real-time web signals for brand-new domains that scammers register quickly. Available on Pixel 10 and newer, Samsung Galaxy S26 series, Galaxy Z Flip 8 and Fold 8, and select other phones.

Third, redesigned warnings for unfamiliar links give extra time and clearer context before you tap. These checkpoints apply to links from numbers not saved in your contacts. The feature expands beyond previous limited availability.

Google Messages already moves suspected spam to a dedicated folder and shows real-time warnings. Enhanced Protection layers stronger analysis on top without requiring you to share message content unless you choose to report a conversation.

## How to Enable Enhanced Protection Mode

The setting arrives in the coming weeks. Once available, enable it from the Protection and safety section.

Open Google Messages. Tap your profile picture or initials in the top right. Select Messages settings. Scroll to Protection and safety. Look for the Enhanced protection toggle and turn it on.

Confirm any prompts about privacy or data processing. The mode is opt-in. You can turn it off anytime in the same menu.

Supported devices include Pixel 6 and newer for the Private AI Compute portion, plus Samsung Galaxy S24 series and other AICore-compatible phones. Gemini Nano link checks require newer hardware as noted above. Update Google Messages from the Play Store first.

If the option does not appear yet, check back after the next Play system or app update. Google System Release Notes track related Play services changes.

## Expanded Chat Scam Alerts and Other Safeguards

Google is expanding on-device Scam Detection for chat notifications beyond Pixel phones. Partners include HONOR, Infinix, iQOO, itel, JOVI, Motorola, OPPO, OnePlus, realme, TECNO, vivo, and Xiaomi. Hundreds of millions more users gain “likely scam” warnings on notification shade for supported chat apps later this year.

The alerts appear before you open the app. Tapping the warning shows tips such as avoiding personal information or links. Content stays private unless you share it to improve detection.

Additional Android protections address phone scams. Automatic screen-share privacy blanks login fields and payment screens in Chrome and Android WebView during live sessions. This extends existing native app protections. The feature rolls out this month to Android 15+ devices.

Proactive call-forwarding protection blocks apps from enabling forwarding in the background. It also warns if someone tricks you into entering an MMI forwarding code on the dial pad. Availability starts early December on Pixel and select devices. This helps stop OTP theft and account takeovers.

Verified financial calls already block millions of spoofed bank numbers in partnership with institutions like Banco do Brasil and Itaú Unibanco. Real-time checks hang up fake inbound calls.

## Tips for Stronger Protection

Enable Advanced Protection if available on your device for broader security hardening. See our guide on [Android Advanced Protection setup](/blog/android-advanced-protection-setup/) for details.

Keep Google Messages as the default SMS app and signed in with your Google account. Turn on spam protection if it is not already active under the same settings menu.

Never reply to suspicious messages with “STOP” or personal details. Forward confirmed spam to 7726 for carrier reporting. Use Circle to Search on suspicious texts for extra analysis.

Update your phone regularly. The October 2026 security patch and Play system updates include related fixes. Check Settings > Security & privacy > System & updates.

For chat apps outside Google Messages, the expanding notification alerts provide an extra layer. Review any “likely scam” banners carefully.

## Conclusion

Enhanced Protection Mode in Google Messages adds meaningful AI defenses against evolving mobile scams without compromising the privacy of on-device detection. Combined with expanded notification alerts, screen-share safeguards, and call-forwarding blocks, Android strengthens its built-in tools.

Enable the mode when it appears in your settings. Stay cautious with unknown senders and keep software current. These features reduce the burden of spotting every sophisticated attempt yourself.
