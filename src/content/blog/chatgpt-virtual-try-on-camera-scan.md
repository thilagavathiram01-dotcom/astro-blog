OpenAI added two phone-friendly ChatGPT updates on October 1, 2026. Product listings for clothes and accessories can show a Try on button, and the phone camera can capture several pages in a row and combine them into one PDF.

Both features are listed in the [ChatGPT release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes). Try-on is available in ChatGPT on mobile and web. The multi-page scan is a phone camera flow. Rollout can still vary by app version and account, so update the app if a control is missing.

## What the October 1 shopping update includes

The shopping note has two parts. First, a Try on button can appear on product listings for clothes and accessories. Select it, then take or upload a selfie. ChatGPT generates a virtual try-on with ChatGPT Images. Your reference photo is saved for later try-ons. You can change or delete it in Settings, then Personalization, then Reference photos.

Second, you can save a product to Favorites or create a folder and organize finds in your ChatGPT Library. OpenAI does not describe this update as a checkout change. Instant Checkout, announced earlier for some merchants, is a separate feature.

Treat the result as a preview, not a fit guarantee. Fabric, size, lighting, and how the photo was taken all change what you see.

## Try on a listed product

1. Open ChatGPT on the web or in the mobile app and sign in.
2. Ask for clothes or accessories you want to compare. Include the item type, a budget, and any must-haves, such as a waterproof jacket under a set price.
3. When a clothing or accessory listing shows Try on, select it.
4. Take a selfie or upload one. OpenAI's notes say the reference photo is stored for future try-ons.
5. Review the generated image. Ask a follow-up if you want a different color or a closer look at the item, and check the listing for size and material details before you buy.
6. Save the product to Favorites, or create a folder in Library so you can return to it later.

If Try on does not appear, the listing may not be eligible, or the control may not have reached your account yet. Update ChatGPT and try another clothing or accessory result.

## Change or delete the reference photo

OpenAI documents a direct path for the saved photo: Settings, then Personalization, then Reference photos. Use that screen to replace the photo or remove it.

A clear, recent photo is more useful than an old group shot. Face the camera, use even light, and avoid heavy filters. If you only uploaded a tight selfie, do not expect the preview to show how a full outfit hangs. The release notes specify a selfie or uploaded photo for the try-on flow. They do not claim body measurements or a size recommendation.

Delete the reference photo if you share the device or you no longer want ChatGPT to reuse it. Saving a product to Library does not, by itself, publish that product to other people.

## Save finds without losing the thread

Favorites is the fast path when you only need a short list. A folder is better when you are comparing several options, such as interview outfits or travel layers.

Name the folder after the job, not the store. "October client visit" is easier to scan later than a pile of unsaved listings in one chat. Library is also where ChatGPT keeps other generated items, including flashcards. If you already use that habit, product folders sit in the same place you return to for study tools. See [how flashcards work on web and mobile](/blog/chatgpt-flashcards-web-mobile/) for that separate Library flow.

Image generation quality is covered in [ChatGPT Images 2.5 sketch and edit tips](/blog/chatgpt-images-2-5-sketch/). Try-on uses ChatGPT Images, so the same care about source photos applies: a sharp input gives the model less to invent.

## Scan several pages into one PDF

The same release notes say it is now easier to bring notes and documents into ChatGPT on your phone. Capture multiple pages in a row, and ChatGPT combines them into a single PDF, ready to upload to your chat.

That is the useful part for class notes, a printed receipt packet, or a form you cannot download. You stay in the camera flow instead of photographing each page, exporting images, and merging them in another app.

![Printed notes and a pen on a desk](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1455390582262-044cdead277a?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

1. Update the ChatGPT app on your phone.
2. Start a new chat and open the camera from the composer. The control sits with the other attachment options.
3. Frame the first page so the edges are visible and the text is in focus.
4. Capture the next pages in order. OpenAI says ChatGPT combines those captures into one PDF and prepares it for the chat.
5. Send the PDF with a specific request. Examples: "Transcribe these notes and list action items," or "Turn this handout into flashcards."
6. Check names, dates, and figures against the page images before you rely on the summary.

The release notes do not publish a page limit. If a long packet fails, split it into two scans and tell ChatGPT the order.

## Capture tips that keep the PDF readable

Good light matters more than a long prompt. Shoot under a lamp or near a window, and keep your shadow off the page. A contrasting background, such as a dark table under white paper, helps the edges stay visible.

Hold the phone parallel to the page. A steep angle bends lines and makes small type harder to read after the PDF is built. If a page has a stamp or a handwritten margin note, include the full sheet instead of cropping early.

Skip glossy pages in direct sun. Glare can wipe out a line that ChatGPT then has to guess. For sensitive documents, decide what should enter the chat at all. A PDF uploaded to a conversation is context for that chat, so remove account numbers you do not need summarized.

## Practical pairings

The two updates work well together when a shopping decision starts on paper. Scan a size chart or a return policy, then ask ChatGPT to compare it with a listing you are considering. Use try-on only for the clothes and accessories that show the button.

Students can scan a lecture handout and ask for a one-page recap plus flashcards. Shoppers can save three jackets to a Library folder, try on the ones that offer the button, and drop the rest. Neither flow replaces a measuring tape or a store mirror. They cut the time spent switching apps.

## What to do if a control is missing

Confirm you are signed in and on a current app build. Try-on is documented for mobile and web. The multi-page camera scan is documented for the phone. Web ChatGPT can still accept a PDF you already have, but the one-tap multi-page capture is the phone update.

If Reference photos is missing under Personalization, the try-on control may not be on your account yet. OpenAI's note describes the path. It does not say every listing or every region shows the button on day one.

## Bottom line

Use Try on when a clothing or accessory listing offers it, then manage the saved selfie under Settings, Personalization, Reference photos. Park products in Favorites or a Library folder so the shortlist survives the chat. On the phone, capture multi-page notes in one camera pass and let ChatGPT build the PDF before you ask for a summary or flashcards.
