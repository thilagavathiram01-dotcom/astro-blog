Staring at a blank note when you need a packing list or grocery list wastes time. Google Keep now uses Gemini to turn a short prompt into a full checklist you can edit and check off.

The feature, called Help me create a list, lives inside the Keep app on Android. You describe what you need. Gemini drafts the items. You insert the list and tweak it. Pixel owners get it without an extra plan. Everyone else needs a Google AI plan.

This guide shows the exact steps, prompt examples that work, and ways to refine the results.

## Who can use Help me create a list

Google’s support page states two clear eligibility rules.

You need a Google AI plan (Google AI Pro or Ultra, or the former Google One AI Premium) to use the feature on most Android phones. Pixel devices include Help me create a list. Usage on Pixels still counts toward Gemini limits, so a plan extends access.

The button appears only in the Android Keep app. It is not listed for iOS in the current help documentation.

Update Keep from the Play Store before you start. Older versions may hide the option.

## Step-by-step: create a checklist with Gemini

Follow these steps on a supported Android device.

1. Open the Google Keep app.
2. Tap Create (the + button) to start a new note.
3. At the bottom right, tap Help me create a list.
4. Type a prompt that describes the list. Be specific about people, location, duration, or constraints.
5. Tap Create.
6. Review the generated checklist. You can tap Good suggestion or Bad suggestion for feedback, or edit the prompt and tap Refresh.
7. Tap Insert to add the checklist to your note.

Once inserted, the note becomes a standard Keep checklist. Check items off, reorder them, or add more by typing.

## Prompt examples that produce usable lists

Vague prompts return generic results. Specific ones save editing time.

Try these patterns:

- Packing list for a 3-day camping trip with two kids in summer, including snacks and rain gear.
- Groceries for a week of high-protein vegetarian meals for a family of three, under a $150 budget.
- Spring cleaning checklist for a 2-bedroom apartment, room by room.
- Classic movies to watch on Halloween, mix of family-friendly and horror.
- Weekly meal prep tasks for Sunday afternoon, including shopping and cooking order.

Google’s own examples match this style: “Packing list for a camping trip with 2 kids in summer” and “Groceries for a week for a vegetarian family of 3.”

After generation, edit the prompt at the top of the preview window and refresh if items are missing or wrong.

## Tips for better results

Add constraints. Mention number of people, dietary needs, budget, climate, or duration. Gemini uses those details.

Review before you insert. The preview lets you reject a bad list and regenerate. Once inserted, you edit manually.

Give feedback. Tap Good or Bad suggestion under the list. This helps Google improve the feature. Avoid including personal or sensitive information in feedback.

Combine with existing Keep habits. After insertion, pin the note, add a color, or set a reminder. If you previously used Keep reminders, those have moved to Google Tasks. See our guide on [finding migrated Google Keep reminders in Tasks](/blog/find-google-keep-reminders-in-tasks/).

Use it for recurring lists. Save a strong prompt in a note so you can copy it next time.

## Create lists from the Gemini app too

You can also reach Keep from the Gemini chat. Connect the Google Keep extension or use @Google Keep.

Ask Gemini to create a list and save it to Keep, or add items to an existing note. This works on web and mobile when the extension is enabled.

Voice works in some flows. Describe the list out loud and let Gemini format the checkboxes.

## Limitations to know

Gemini can miss items or invent ones that do not fit. Always review.

The feature does not mark lists as AI-generated after insertion.

It requires an internet connection for generation.

Pixel users may hit usage caps without a paid plan.

## Conclusion

Help me create a list turns the slowest part of checklist creation into a one-sentence prompt. Open Keep, tap the button, describe the list, and insert. Specific prompts produce the best starting drafts.

Try a packing or grocery list today. Then refine the prompt until the output needs almost no edits. That is when the feature pays for itself.
