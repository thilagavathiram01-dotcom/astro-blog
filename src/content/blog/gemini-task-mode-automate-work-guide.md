Google moved its Spark agent into the main Gemini chat as Task mode in mid-October 2026. Paid subscribers can now toggle between Chat and Task directly above the prompt box and hand off multi-step jobs that continue while they step away.

This guide covers the requirements, how to start a task, how to add schedules, and how to keep control of what Gemini does. All steps come from Google’s Gemini Apps Help Center and the October 13 product announcement.

![Person working on laptop with AI tools](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1486312338219-ce68d2c6f44d?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What You Need Before You Start

Task mode is not available on free or Google AI Plus accounts. You need:

- A personal Google Account (not a work or school account)
- Age 18 or older
- Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription
- Keep Activity turned on in your Gemini settings
- Opt-in to Personal Intelligence

The feature is unavailable in the European Economic Area, Nigeria, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom. It works in the Gemini mobile apps, the Gemini app on Mac, and gemini.google.com.

Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers also gained access to Gemini 4 Argon in the same October 13 update. You can select Argon from the model picker in the app for deeper reasoning on complex tasks.

## Switch to Task Mode and Create Your First Task

Open the Gemini app or go to gemini.google.com. Look for the Chat and Task buttons above the text box. Tap or click **Task**. The prompt area changes to accept a goal rather than a regular question.

Describe what you want done. Google’s help pages give examples such as:

- “Every day at 7:00 pm, look through my emails, calendar and Drive, and let me know what to prioritise for the day.”
- “Whenever I receive an email from my manager with an action item, help me address it and draft a reply.”

You can attach files from your device, Google Drive, or a Notebook. Gemini may ask for confirmation before it sends messages, edits files, or accesses external sites. Review those prompts before approving.

After you submit, the task appears in your history. Filter by status (Ready, Needs attention, In progress) to track progress. Open the task thread and tap the progress chip at the top to see completed, current, and planned steps.

## Add Schedules for Recurring Work

Schedules turn one-off tasks into repeating or event-driven workflows. Google supports three types:

- **Time-based**: once, hourly, daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly
- **Gmail monitors**: runs when an email matches a filter
- **Topic monitors**: runs in response to news, finance, sports, or local events

To create one, switch to Task mode and describe the timing in the same prompt, or use the blank template option for time-based schedules. You can also ask Gemini inside an existing task thread to add or edit a schedule.

Manage everything on the Scheduled page. There you can pause, resume, edit, or delete schedules. If you turn off Task mode, existing schedules pause automatically.

For time-zone changes, Google’s help notes that schedules follow the time zone set in your Google Account.

## Use Connected Apps and Skills

Gemini can act across more than 40 connected apps, including Instacart, OpenTable, Adobe, and Shopify, plus Google Workspace tools. When you start a task, specify the apps or documents involved so Gemini knows where to work.

You can also call skills with the @ menu. Skills provide reusable instructions for writing style, presentation prep, or other repeatable workflows. Create or manage them under Task settings or the skills section in help.

Google stresses that safeguards are not perfect. Watch important tasks and intervene if Gemini needs a password, payment details, or human decision.

## Tips for Reliable Results

Keep prompts specific. State the goal, the sources to use, the output format, and any limits (for example, “do not send emails without my approval”).

Check the Scheduled page daily for the first week so you catch any unexpected behavior early.

If a task needs local files on your computer, keep the Gemini desktop app open and enable the option to run tasks remotely from other devices in Task settings.

For coding or long research jobs, switch to Gemini 4 Argon and raise the thinking level if available. The model shares progress more often on longer tasks.

## Conclusion

Gemini Task mode folds the former Spark agent into the main chat interface. With a Pro or Ultra plan, Keep Activity, and Personal Intelligence enabled, you can hand off multi-step work that runs while you are away and schedule it to repeat or trigger on events.

Start with a simple daily priority summary, confirm the permissions it requests, and expand from there. Always review outputs that affect email, files, or purchases.
