Google's November 2026 Pixel Drop brings Gemini Intelligence to the Pixel 10 series. Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, and Pixel 10 Pro Fold now get features that first appeared on the Pixel 11 line.

This includes Rambler for polished voice typing, Create My Widget for custom home screen tools, and expanded task automation. Updates roll out starting 10 November 2026 and continue over the following weeks. Availability depends on region, language, and device. Some features require users to be 18 or older.

If you are still on an earlier Android 17 build, check the [Android 17 QPR2 Beta install guide](/blog/android-17-qpr2-beta-6-1-pixel/) for related Pixel update steps.

## What is Gemini Intelligence on Pixel 10?

Gemini Intelligence packages Gemini capabilities directly into Pixel devices. It helps with multi-step tasks across apps, generates personalized widgets, and improves voice input.

The November drop expands it from Pixel 11 models to the full Pixel 10 family (excluding the Pixel 10a). Motorola's first Gemini Intelligence device, the motorola signature 27, is also joining, with U.S. pre-orders starting 19 November.

Key additions on Pixel 10:

- Rambler in Gboard cleans spoken text in real time.
- Task automation handles logistics such as ordering food, booking rides, or making reservations.
- Create My Widget builds custom home screen widgets from a text description.
- Proactive suggestions surface relevant actions, such as sharing an ETA.

These features use Gemini models and may require an internet connection for full capability. Google notes that some higher-usage features may need a subscription.

## How to enable and use Rambler

Rambler turns natural speech into clean, formatted text. It removes filler words like “um” and “uh,” fixes grammar and punctuation, and handles mid-sentence corrections. You can switch languages mid-sentence in supported cases.

It runs inside Gboard and works in any app where the keyboard appears.

**Prerequisites (from Google support):**

- Pixel 10 series or Pixel 11 series device.
- Latest Gboard set as the default keyboard.
- Microphone permission granted.
- Internet connection for full features (limited offline mode available).

**Setup steps:**

1. Open any app with a text field, such as Messages or Gmail.
2. Tap a text area to bring up Gboard.
3. Tap the microphone icon.
4. If an introduction dialog appears, tap Start.
5. If no dialog appears, open the Gboard menu (three dots or keyboard icon), go to Settings → Voice typing, and select Rambler.

Once active, speak naturally. Rambler processes the audio and inserts polished text. Review the result before sending. A waveform or indicator shows when it is listening.

Google states that ramblings are not stored. Always review output, as results can vary.

## Create custom widgets with a prompt

Create My Widget lets you describe a widget in plain language. Gemini generates Material-style home screen widgets. It can pull data from Gmail, Calendar, and Search when you grant permission.

Examples include a monthly spending tracker, a trip countdown that includes flight details, or a game-day widget in your team colors.

**Availability:**

- Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 series (not Pixel 10a).
- Select countries and languages (including English in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and India).
- Users 18+.
- Rolling out over the next few weeks from 10 November 2026.

**How to create one:**

1. Long-press an empty area on your home screen.
2. Tap Widgets.
3. Look for a Create button (or open the Create My Widget experience).
4. Choose a category or tap Describe your widget.
5. Enter a prompt such as “Show me my monthly shopping spend” or “Countdown to my Berlin trip with flight details.”
6. Review the generated options.
7. Tap Add to place it on the home screen, or Edit to refine.

You can adjust the widget later. Support is also coming to Pixel Watch 4 and Pixel Watch 5.

## Use Gemini for multi-step tasks

Task automation lets you ask Gemini to complete logistics across supported apps. Examples include ordering groceries, hailing a ride, booking a restaurant reservation, or reordering takeout.

This expansion reaches the UK, India, and Japan in addition to earlier markets. Proactive suggestions can appear in context, such as offering to share an address from a confirmation email when a friend asks where you are meeting.

Open the Gemini app or use the system assistant. Describe the task clearly. Gemini shows its progress and asks for confirmation before final actions such as payment.

Device Assistance is another related capability. Ask Gemini to free storage space, check location permissions, or mute notifications while keeping important calls active. It is available on select devices running Android 17 or later.

## Tips for better results

- Speak in complete thoughts for Rambler rather than single words. It handles corrections better when you keep talking.
- For widgets, start with a short prompt and then edit. Specific details such as “include hotel address” improve the output.
- Grant only the permissions you need for Gmail or Calendar access in widgets.
- Check responses before acting. Google notes that results may vary and features are not perfect.
- Keep Gboard and the system updated so new features appear.

Rollout is staggered. If a feature is missing, wait a few days and check for system updates under Settings → System → Software update.

## Conclusion

The November 2026 Pixel Drop extends Gemini Intelligence to Pixel 10 devices. Rambler cleans up voice input so messages sound polished without extra editing. Create My Widget turns a short description into a useful home screen tool. Task automation offloads routine logistics to Gemini.

Start with Rambler in Gboard for daily messaging, then try a simple widget for information you check often. Confirm availability in your region and review every result before you send or confirm an action.
