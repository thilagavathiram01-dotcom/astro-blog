A stack of notes does not tell you what to study next. Gemini study notebooks do. Google built them as a goal-based space inside the Gemini app: you name a topic, add sources, sit a diagnostic quiz, then work through short lessons that track strengths and gaps.

The feature launched for personal accounts in June 2026. On 22 September 2026, Google Workspace confirmed it for school and work accounts when an admin has both the Gemini app and Gemini Notebook turned on. Personal accounts still get it in every language the Gemini app supports, outside limits listed for the EEA on some Workspace plans.

This guide follows official steps from Google’s Gemini blog and Workspace updates. It does not invent extra quiz scores or availability claims.

## What a study notebook is (and is not)

A study notebook is not the same thing as a regular Gemini chat, and it is not a full replacement for [Gemini Notebook](https://notebooklm.google/) (the research product formerly called NotebookLM).

Google describes study notebooks as an adaptive learning space. You set a goal. Gemini splits that goal into more than 100 learning objectives, groups them into topics, and labels them Strengths, Focus areas, or Not started after you take quizzes.

A regular notebook in Gemini is a source-grounded workspace for projects. A study notebook adds the diagnostic quiz, bite-sized lessons, and the skill dashboard. You can still open the same materials in Gemini Notebook for flashcards, Video Overviews, and other studio outputs.

If you already use Gemini for morning planning, pair this with the setup in [How to Set Up Gemini Daily Brief for Free in the US](/blog/gemini-daily-brief-free-us-setup/). Daily Brief organizes the day. A study notebook organizes one subject.

## Who can use it right now

Google’s June launch notes said study notebooks roll out globally for personal accounts on the web, in languages the Gemini app supports. Mobile support was listed as coming later that summer. School-issued accounts, including users under 18, were scheduled after the personal-account launch.

The 22 September Workspace post adds this admin rule: study notebooks appear for users in a group or organizational unit where **Gemini** and **Gemini Notebook** are both On. There is no separate end-user toggle. Rapid Release domains started a gradual rollout on 17 September 2026.

Workspace availability in that post covers Workspace customers and Workspace Individual subscribers outside the European Economic Area, plus personal Google accounts globally. Confirm your domain with your admin if the Study tile is missing.

## Create a study notebook step by step

Google’s Gemini account and Workspace posts agree on the same starting path.

1. Open the Gemini app on the web at [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com).
2. In the left sidebar, choose **New notebook**.
3. Select the **Study and learn** tile (Google’s June posts also call this the **Study** option).
4. Type the subject and a clear goal. Example: “Prepare for SAT Reading using my uploaded practice set.”
5. Upload class materials: syllabus, notes, readings, PDFs, sites, or Drive files.
6. Answer any follow-up questions Gemini asks so the goal stays narrow.
7. Take the diagnostic quiz when it appears. Treat it as a baseline, not a grade.

Be specific in the goal. “Help me with chemistry” is weaker than “Master stoichiometry and gas laws for my midterm using these lecture PDFs.”

Google says the diagnostic quiz maps strengths and weaknesses so the plan is not a guess. After that, the notebook builds short lessons aimed at the gaps.

## Work through lessons and quizzes

Each lesson is short on purpose. Official copy says lessons include practice quizzes based on the materials you uploaded. You can pause and ask a question inside the notebook without leaving the lesson.

Google also noted that later lessons would add more visuals such as diagrams. Do not wait for those extras before you start. The quiz loop is the core product.

After follow-up quizzes, labels on the dashboard update. Focus areas rise when you miss related objectives. Strengths appear when you show consistent correct answers. The dashboard then ranks recommended lessons so you have a next step.

You can still filter the list yourself. Official notes say you can browse by Strengths, Focus areas, and related views instead of only following the top recommendation.

## Prep for a standardized exam

Study notebooks are not only for a single class. Google listed SAT prep grounded in authoritative questions from The Princeton Review inside study notebooks. The same diagnostic-plus-lesson loop applies.

Google said it would add more exams during that summer window, naming JEE, NEET, ENEM, ACT, and GRE. Check the exam list in your own notebook before you plan a full course around a test that has not appeared in your account yet.

Keep exam prep in its own notebook. Mixing SAT Reading with a chemistry syllabus muddies the objective list and the dashboard ranking.

## Connect the same sources to Gemini Notebook

Google states that notebook sources integrate across the Gemini app and Gemini Notebook. That means you can generate interactive flashcards, Video Overviews, and similar study objects from the same files you used for lessons.

A practical split:

- Use the **study notebook** for the diagnostic, lessons, and skill dashboard.
- Use **Gemini Notebook** when you want a cited recap, an audio pass, or flashcards from the identical source set.

Do not treat either product as a substitute for the official exam board or your instructor. Ground answers in files you uploaded, then check them against the syllabus.

## Tips that keep the plan honest

Upload the real course pack first. A notebook with no sources still runs, but Google’s design is built around materials you provide.

One notebook, one goal. If you add a second course, create a second study notebook so objectives stay countable.

Retake progress-check quizzes when the dashboard stalls. Workspace copy says you can request those quizzes to refresh the baseline.

Ask questions in the lesson, not in a separate chat that has no sources. The notebook already has the files.

If you are on a school or work account and the Study tile is missing, ask the admin to enable both Gemini and Gemini Notebook for your OU. End users cannot flip a hidden study switch.

Review any generated practice item against your textbook. Adaptive lessons still need a human check before an exam.

## Conclusion

Study notebooks turn Gemini into a course-aware tutor with a measurable map: diagnostic quiz, short lessons, then a dashboard that ranks what to do next. Personal accounts can start on the web today. Workspace users can start when Gemini and Gemini Notebook are on for their group.

Set one goal, add the files you already have, and sit the first quiz before you write another outline by hand.
