Google’s November 2026 Pixel Feature Drop introduced Create My Widget. This tool uses Gemini to build custom home screen widgets from a short description. Eligible Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 owners can generate widgets for spending trackers, countdowns, weather alerts, or personal data from Gmail and Calendar.

The feature stays limited to specific phones and regions. It works in English for users aged 18 or older in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and India. The Pixel 10a is excluded.

## Who can use Create My Widget

Google lists four requirements:

- Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL, Pixel 10 Pro Fold, or any Pixel 11 series model.
- Location in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, or India.
- English as the device language.
- Age 18 or older.

UK users lack Gemini Connected Apps integration. Widgets that pull personal data from Gmail or Calendar may not work the same way there. The feature is rolling out gradually with the November Pixel Drop. Some phones still need a beta update to access it.

## Enable Create My Widget if it is not visible

If your phone has not received the update yet, join the beta:

1. Open the Google Play Store and search for Create My Widget.
2. Scroll to the beta section and tap Join.
3. Wait a few minutes, then tap Update to install the beta version.
4. Long-press an empty area on your home screen, tap Widgets, and look for the Create button.

You need the default Pixel Launcher. Third-party launchers hide the option until you switch back.

## Create a widget from a description

Follow these steps once the Create button appears:

1. Long-press an empty space on the home screen.
2. Tap Widgets, then tap Create in the top-right corner.
3. Choose Describe your widget or pick a suggestion such as Holidays countdown or Weather alerts.
4. Type a clear prompt. Examples include “Show my monthly shopping spend from Gmail” or “Countdown to Diwali with days remaining.”
5. Tap Send. Gemini generates a preview in Material You style.
6. Review the preview. Tap Edit to refine the prompt, or tap Add to place the widget.
7. Resize and move the widget like any other home screen element.

The preview screen also offers Create new if you want to start over. Widgets can pull public data such as weather or stocks, or personal data after you connect Google apps.

## Example prompts that work well

Specific prompts produce better results:

- “Monthly spending tracker showing total from Gmail receipts in November.”
- “Countdown to next cricket match for India with team colors.”
- “Daily weather with UV index and wind speed for my location.”
- “Upcoming calendar events from my work calendar only.”
- “Stock price for one company with percentage change.”

Avoid vague requests such as “make something useful.” Gemini may ask follow-up questions if details are missing. You can edit any placed widget later by long-pressing it and choosing Edit.

## Tips for better widgets

Connect Google apps during setup if you want personal data. You can manage connections later in the Create My Widget app settings. Keep the language set to English. The feature supports a limited number of widgets per account in the current beta. Material You styling adapts to your wallpaper colors automatically.

If the widget fails to load data, check that the required Google services are linked and that you have an active internet connection. Public data widgets work without extra setup.
