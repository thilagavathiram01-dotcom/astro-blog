Google expanded Tasks in the Gemini app on 17 November 2026. The feature is now free for adults in the United States, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. It runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen. Tasks let Gemini handle longer jobs in the background while you stay in control.

This guide shows how to start a task, what it can do, the limits, and the safety controls. All details come from Google’s official blog post and Gemini Apps Help pages.

## What Tasks in Gemini does

Chat mode answers questions and stops. Task mode takes a goal and works through the steps. Gemini can check Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, and the web. It pauses for your approval on important actions such as purchases or sending messages.

You can run multiple tasks at once. Official help pages note a practical limit around 15 concurrent tasks. You can stop any task from the text box.

In the United States, additional options are rolling out: texting Gemini, forwarding emails to gemini@google.com, calling businesses (except in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska), and completing purchases with Google Pay up to a set limit with confirmation.

Tasks require a personal Google Account and the Gemini History setting turned on. They are not available on work or school accounts.

## Switch from Chat to Task

Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS, or go to gemini.google.com on the web.

Look for the toggle near the text box. On mobile it sits above the box. On the web and Mac app it sits below the box.

Tap or click **Task**. The first time, follow the short setup prompts. Confirm that History is enabled in Settings if the option does not appear.

You are ready to describe a job.

## Start your first task

1. Select **Task**.
2. Describe the finished result in plain language. Include dates, budget, preferred stores or services, and anything Gemini should avoid.
3. Add context if needed. Type @ to choose a skill. Use Upload & tools to attach files, add from Drive, or add a Notebook.
4. Tap Submit.

Gemini creates a dedicated thread. You can add more instructions while it runs. The thread appears in the sidebar (or under the menu on mobile). Progress shows as Ready, Needs attention, or In progress.

Example prompts that match Google’s descriptions:

- “Find three local plumbers who can come this week and compare their prices. Put the results in a short table.”
- “Check my Gmail for school event dates this month and add them to my Calendar with my partner invited.”
- “Compare USB-C chargers under $40 that support 65W. List the top three with links.”

You can schedule a task to run once, at a set time, or on a recurring basis (for example, every weekday morning).

## Manage running tasks

Open the menu and look under All or the Tasks section. Tap a thread to see the steps Gemini completed.

To stop a task, open the thread and use the Stop control in the text box.

You can pin, rename, or add a finished task thread to a Notebook for later reference.

If Gemini needs you to complete a final step on a website, it hands the screen back. Approve or decline as needed.

## Safety and control settings

Google designed Tasks to pause before critical actions. Purchase confirmation is on by default. Leave it enabled unless you have a specific reason to change it.

You can set “Always allow” for routine actions you trust, such as adding a regular grocery item.

Review connected apps in Settings. Disconnect any you no longer want Gemini to use. Delete history at any time.

When Gemini calls a business, it discloses that it is an AI. Businesses can opt out. Gemini does not share payment or private account details unless you explicitly approve.

Tasks run in an isolated environment. Google describes multiple layers of protection, including prompt-injection filters and Safe Browsing checks.

If a result is wrong or incomplete, give it a thumbs-down so the team can improve the system.

## Practical tips for better results

Write the goal, the limits, and the desired output format. “Research X and put the findings in a Google Doc” produces cleaner results than a vague request.

Start with one clear job. Once you see how Gemini handles it, you can chain follow-ups in the same thread.

Check Calendar and Gmail permissions before you assign tasks that touch those services.

For shopping tasks in the US, set a spending cap and keep purchase confirmation on.

If you already use Gemini Canvas for interactive tools, you can still hand longer research or multi-step jobs to Tasks. See our guide on [how to use Gemini Canvas for four productivity tasks](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-canvas-four-productivity-ways/) for the complementary workspace features.

## Current limits and availability

Availability is rolling out. If you do not see the Task toggle, wait a few days or check that you are signed into a personal account in a supported country.

Calling, texting, email forwarding, and Google Pay checkout are currently limited to the United States and have further restrictions by state or carrier.

Tasks are not available in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, or Nigeria even with a paid plan.

The feature uses Gemini 4 Hydrogen on the free tier in the listed countries. Paid Google AI Pro or Ultra plans may offer additional capacity or models in other regions.

## Conclusion

Tasks in Gemini turns the app from a chat partner into a background helper for multi-step work. Flip the toggle from Chat to Task, describe the finished result with clear limits, and review the key steps before you approve them.

The 17 November 2026 update made this free for hundreds of millions of people in six countries. Open the Gemini app today, switch to Task, and try one small job from your current list.
