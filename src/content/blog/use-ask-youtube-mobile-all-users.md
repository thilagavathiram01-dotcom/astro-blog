YouTube search on your phone just got conversational. Ask YouTube, the AI tool that answers complex questions with structured results and relevant videos, is rolling out to all signed-in users on Android and iOS in the United States.

Previously limited or tested mainly with Premium subscribers and on web/TV, the mobile version now opens the same natural-language search to everyone in the US. You tap Search, select Ask, type or speak a full question, and refine with follow-ups.

This guide covers how to open it, what it returns, practical examples, and limits based on YouTube’s official blog and support pages.

## What Ask YouTube does on mobile

Ask YouTube replaces keyword lists with a conversation. You describe what you need in plain language. The response combines written guidance with videos, Shorts, and clips. Follow-up questions keep context so you can narrow results without starting over.

YouTube announced the mobile expansion on October 12, 2026. Emily Moxley, VP of Product Management for Viewer AI, noted that viewers already use it for DIY Halloween costumes, video essays, and practical debates. The same experience is now available on phones and tablets for US accounts.

It remains separate from the Ask button that appears under some video players. That watch-page tool answers questions about the specific video you are watching. The search version helps you discover videos across the platform.

## How to open Ask YouTube on your phone

1. Update the YouTube app from the Play Store or App Store if you have not done so recently.
2. Sign in with a Google account that is 13 or older and located in the United States.
3. Tap the magnifying glass Search icon at the top of the home or browse screen.
4. Look for the Ask option next to or inside the search field and tap it.
5. Type your question or use the microphone if voice input is available.
6. Review the structured answer and any embedded videos.
7. Tap a suggested follow-up or type a new question to continue.

Rollout is gradual, so the Ask control may not appear on every device the same day. Restart the app or check again later if it is missing. Supervised kid accounts and the YouTube Kids app do not include the feature.

## Practical examples that work well

A simple keyword search for “road trip California” returns a list of vlogs. Ask YouTube handles the full request: “Plan a three-day road trip from San Francisco to Santa Barbara with stops for hiking and local food.” It returns an itinerary-style summary plus matching videos.

Other useful prompts include:

- “Show me beginner-friendly videos on replacing a laptop battery without specialized tools.”
- “Compare wireless earbuds under $100 that work with Android and have strong noise cancellation.”
- “Find video essays that explain the history of the internet in under 20 minutes each.”
- “What are the steps to clean a clogged printer head, and which videos demonstrate it clearly?”

After the first answer, ask “Which of these options is easiest for someone with no prior experience?” The conversation retains the earlier context.

## Tips for better results

Be specific about constraints. Mention budget, time available, skill level, or device type. Vague prompts produce broader lists; precise ones surface tighter recommendations.

Check the source videos. Ask YouTube surfaces content, but you still verify claims, especially for technical, medical, or financial topics. Sponsored segments can appear in results.

Use it alongside regular search. If you already know a channel or exact title, standard search remains faster. Switch to Ask when the query is open-ended or multi-part.

For product comparisons, pair it with the dedicated shopping flow covered in our earlier guide on [Ask YouTube product reviews](/blog/ask-youtube-product-reviews-shopping/). That workflow focuses on review videos and tagged products.

## Current limits and availability

Availability is currently limited to signed-in US viewers. YouTube has not announced a date for other countries or languages. Apple TV is not supported for this search experience.

The watch-page Ask tool that works during Shorts and live streams is expanding separately and is not yet live for everyone. Stick to the Search > Ask path for the mobile conversational search described here.

Results depend on public YouTube content. Private, unlisted, or age-restricted videos may not appear. Answers can be incomplete or incorrect, so cross-check important details.

## What comes next

YouTube plans to expand Ask YouTube gradually. The same conversational approach already works on web and TV for US users. Mobile closes the gap for people who primarily watch on phones.

If you create videos, clearer titles, chapters, and on-screen text help the system match your content to questions. Viewers benefit when creators structure material so AI tools can extract steps and timestamps accurately.
