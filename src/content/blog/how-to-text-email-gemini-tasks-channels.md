Google rolled out free Gemini Tasks on November 17, 2026, to users in the US, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. The feature runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen. In the US, you can also start tasks by text message or email without opening the Gemini app.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="255" />

This guide shows how to enable the new Channels for text and email. You stay in control because Gemini asks for confirmation before major actions like booking or paying.

## What Channels Do

Channels let you hand off jobs from the apps you already use. Text Gemini through Google Messages or iMessage. Or forward emails and attachments to gemini@google.com.

Gemini replies only to you. It never uses Reply-All. You get a link to review results in the Gemini app. Tasks created this way appear as new threads in the side panel.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="266" />

The feature is currently US-only and requires a personal Google Account based in the US with English as the account language. Tasks themselves are free in six countries, but advanced Channels start in the US.

## Set Up Text with Gemini

You need a US phone number on an eligible carrier. Texts are not end-to-end encrypted and may be accessible by your carrier. Deleting Gemini Apps Activity does not remove messages from your carrier or messaging app.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="270" />

On Android, install Google Messages and turn on RCS chats. Use the same phone number for Gemini and Messages.

Follow these steps:

1. Open the Gemini app on Android or iOS, or go to gemini.google.com.
2. Tap Settings, then Channels.
3. Under Text with Gemini, tap Set up.
4. Select your phone number or add a new one.
5. Verify the number if prompted.
6. Follow any remaining on-screen instructions.

After setup, Gemini sends you a text via RCS Business Messaging. You can then start new tasks, check status, or reply to steer an ongoing job. Snap a photo of a school flyer and text it to add dates to your calendar.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="256" />

The new text channel coexists with the older Gemini integration in Google Messages. You can tell them apart by the icon.

## Set Up Email with Gemini

Email works from Gmail, Apple Mail, or Outlook when signed in with your personal Gmail account. It also supports apps that use the official Gmail API.

Requirements include a US-based personal Google Account, Tasks access, and the same account signed in across Gemini and Gmail.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="266" />

Enable it:

1. Open Gemini (app or gemini.google.com).
2. Go to Settings > Channels.
3. Next to Email with Gemini, tap Turn on.
4. Follow the prompts.

Forward an email, thread, or PDF to gemini@google.com. Gemini replies only to you with a link to review and confirm. It accepts up to 10 attachments. Inline images count toward the limit, but Google Drive links do not.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="284" />

One practical example: forward school emails so Gemini adds every date to your Google Calendar and invites family members. It can also flag conflicts, such as a basketball practice overlapping another event, and suggest texting another parent about carpool.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="264" />

## Practical Examples

- Text a grocery list and a photo of an empty box. Gemini adds items to an Instacart order and hands you off to finish checkout.
- Forward utility or medical bill PDFs. Gemini analyzes them against past statements and highlights patterns, such as a heat pump running during peak pricing.
- Email a shopping request. Gemini compares options using Google’s Shopping Graph, asks clarifying questions, and prepares Google Pay checkout with a spending cap you set in advance.

Gemini pauses for one-tap confirmation before sending messages, booking, or buying. You can set “Always allow” for routine actions you trust. Receipts land in Google Wallet.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="264" />

## Limits and Privacy

Text requires a US number with an eligible carrier. Calling features (separate from text) are unavailable in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska. Email and text Channels are rolling out in the US first.

Tasks run in a hardware-isolated virtual machine dedicated to your account. Gemini uses prompt-injection filters and Google Safe Browsing. You can disconnect apps, adjust settings, or delete history at any time.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="264" />

If a task misses the mark, give it a thumbs-down so Google can improve the system.
