Meetings end, but the follow-up work often starts immediately. On October 12, 2026, Google added next steps to the post-meeting screen in Google Meet. Powered by Gemini and Workspace Studio, the feature turns discussion notes into ready actions such as a draft follow-up email in Gmail or action items sent to Google Tasks.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="172" />

The routines appear only when “Take notes for me” was enabled during the meeting. They work for both one-off and recurring meetings, and you can turn a specific step on for all future meetings that use notes.

## Requirements Before You Start

You need an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan that includes Gemini features in Meet. Common editions are Business Standard and Plus, Enterprise Standard and Plus, and certain education or AI Pro add-ons. “Take notes for me” and Ask Gemini must be available and enabled for your account or domain.

Administrators control Gemini note-taking through the Google Admin console under Apps > Google Workspace > Google Meet > Gemini settings. End users can start notes during a call if the admin has allowed it.

Smart features should also be turned on in your Google Account settings so Gemini can process the meeting content.

## Enable Take Notes for Me During the Meeting

1. Join or start a Google Meet call from your browser or the Meet app.
2. Look for the notes or Gemini pencil icon near the top of the meeting window.
3. Select “Take notes for me” or “Start taking notes.” All participants see an indicator that notes are being captured.
4. Optionally enable the transcript if it is available for your plan.

Gemini listens to the conversation, creates a summary, identifies action items, and saves the notes to a Google Doc. After the meeting the Doc is emailed to the organizer and the person who started notes, and it is attached to the Calendar event when one exists.

Without this step, the post-meeting next-steps section does not appear.

## Use Next Steps on the Post-Meeting Screen

When the meeting ends, stay on the post-meeting screen. Look for the section titled “Routines for this meeting.”

Two routines launch at the start:

- Draft a follow-up email in Gmail that includes the summary and key points.
- Add the meeting’s action items to Google Tasks so they appear in your task list.

Tap the toggle or button next to the routine you want. The action runs immediately. You can review and edit the draft email before sending, or adjust the tasks after they land in Tasks.

For a recurring series, turning a routine on applies to the whole series when notes are enabled. You can also set a routine to run for every future meeting that uses “Take notes for me.”

After you enable a routine the first time, you can manage it later from the Meet post-call screen or in the Workspace Studio Flows library.

## Manage Routines and Review Results

Open Gmail to find the draft follow-up. Gemini uses the notes it generated, so the draft usually includes a short summary and the main decisions or requests from the call. Edit names, dates, or extra context before you send.

In Google Tasks, the action items appear as individual tasks. Assign due dates or move them into a specific list if needed. The feature does not automatically assign owners unless the discussion clearly named them; you still review ownership.

If the routines do not appear, confirm that notes were started during the meeting, that your account has the required Gemini access, and that the feature has finished rolling out to your domain. Rollout can take several days after the October 12 announcement.

## Tips for Better Results

- Start notes at the beginning of the meeting so the full discussion is captured.
- Speak clearly and name owners and deadlines when you discuss tasks. Gemini extracts what is said.
- Review every draft and task list. The routines accelerate the work; they do not replace a final check.
- For recurring meetings, turn the routines on once so they run automatically when notes are enabled.
- Combine this with existing Gemini features such as Ask Gemini during the call if you need a quick recap before the meeting ends.

If you already use Gemini for other Workspace tasks, this fits the same pattern of turning conversation into structured output. See our guide on [building Gemini Workspace skills](/blog/build-gemini-workspace-skills-google-docs/) for related automation ideas.

## Limitations

Only two routines are available at launch. Google has said more will arrive later. The feature requires “Take notes for me” to have run during that specific meeting. It is not available on free consumer accounts without an eligible AI plan. Transcripts and notes remain subject to your organization’s data controls and retention settings.

Post-quantum cryptography is also rolling out for Meet calls on supported clients, but that change is separate and requires no user action.

## Conclusion

Google Meet next steps remove the usual gap between “we should follow up” and the actual email or task list. Enable notes during the call, end the meeting, and tap the routine that matches what you need. The draft appears in Gmail or the items land in Tasks, ready for a quick edit and send.

Eligible Workspace and AI Pro users can try it on the next meeting that uses “Take notes for me.” Check the post-meeting screen for the Routines section and turn on the steps that save you time.
