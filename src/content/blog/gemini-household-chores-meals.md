Household work rarely looks like a clean project plan. You stack bills, guess at leftovers, and stare at a blinking modem light while dinner waits. Google’s 15 September 2026 Gemini App post walks through four jobs the app can take off that list: repairs, meals, spending, and large purchases.

This guide turns that official list into steps you can run today. It uses Gemini Live camera sharing, Connected Apps such as Instacart, and file uploads for receipts. It does not invent features Google has not published.

Google’s AI & Economy ATLAS v1.0 study found that more than 86% of conversational AI use happens outside formal work. That is the point of this how-to: treat Gemini as a kitchen and garage tool, not only a work chat.

## What you need before you start

- A personal Google account signed into the Gemini app or gemini.google.com
- The latest Gemini app on Android or iOS, or the desktop app if you already use it
- Camera and microphone permission for Live sessions
- Keep Activity on if you want Connected Apps on web and iOS (Google Help documents this requirement)
- Instacart connected only if you want grocery checkout from a meal plan

Live camera sharing is documented for Android phones and tablets. For spoken camera cues and framing help, use the same Live path described in our [Gemini Live Guided Vision guide](/blog/gemini-live-guided-vision/).

Do not point the camera at passwords, medical records, or other people without consent.

## 1. Troubleshoot a repair with Gemini Live

Google’s household post uses a simple example: the internet is down, so you show Gemini the modem.

1. Open the Gemini app.
2. Tap **Live** (or swipe left), or say “Hey Google, let’s talk Live.”
3. Tap the **camera** control and grant access if Android asks.
4. Point the rear camera at the device, the error sticker, or the blinking LED.
5. Ask a narrow question: “What does this blinking light on the modem mean?”

Follow-up prompts that stay inside the same official pattern:

- “Read the model number on this sticker, then give the first three checks for no WAN light.”
- “This dishwasher is making a grinding noise. What should I check before I call a technician?”
- “Walk me through assembling this toy using the parts in frame.”

Keep the subject still. Fast pans waste the session. Treat spoken steps as a first pass. Unplug power before you follow any electrical advice, and stop if a repair needs a licensed technician.

## 2. Plan meals from the fridge, then build a list

Google documents two meal paths: a typed plan with diet rules, and a Live camera sweep of the fridge.

**Typed plan**

In a normal Gemini chat, state constraints in one prompt:

“Build a 5-day dinner plan for two adults and one child. No peanuts. Under 30 minutes of cook time. Add a matching grocery list grouped by store aisle.”

Ask for substitutions if someone in the house is a picky eater. Ask Gemini to drop items you already have.

**Fridge scan**

1. Start Gemini Live and turn the camera on.
2. Pan slowly across shelves. Name the section if the frame is crowded: “This is the door. Now the bottom drawer.”
3. Ask: “What can I make with these leftovers?”

Google says Gemini will identify ingredients and suggest recipes on the spot, which can cut food waste. Confirm what it named. A wilted herb and a fresh one look alike on camera.

**Instacart checkout**

Google’s post states that connecting Instacart lets you add ingredients to a cart and check out in a few taps. In Gemini settings, open **Connected Apps**, turn Instacart on, and finish the partner sign-in. Then return to the meal chat and ask Gemini to add the missing items. Review the cart on Instacart before you pay. Gemini does not replace your payment confirmation.

## 3. Extract household spending from receipts

Google’s third chore is budget work without manual typing. The documented example: after a trip, upload receipt photos and ask, “Summarize my Mexico expenses in USD.”

Steps that match that flow:

1. Photograph receipts on a flat surface with even light. One receipt per shot is easier to check.
2. In Gemini, attach the images or drop them into the chat.
3. Ask for a table: date, merchant, category, original amount, currency, USD total.
4. Ask a second pass: “Flag duplicates and any tip line you cannot read.”

Upload only what you would store in your own files. Do not include full card numbers. If a receipt is faded, type the total yourself instead of trusting a guessed digit.

Use the summary as a draft for your spreadsheet or banking app. Gemini is not your ledger of record.

## 4. Compare a large household purchase

The official fourth job is research for cars and appliances. Google’s example prompt is to compare top-rated hybrid SUVs for a family of four and get a side-by-side table. At a dealership, you can also ask for negotiation talking points.

A prompt that stays faithful to that example:

“Compare current hybrid SUVs suitable for a family of four. Table columns: seating, cargo, official fuel economy source if you have it, known safety-feature packages, and typical ownership notes. Mark anything you cannot verify.”

Then narrow:

- “Drop rows that cannot fit two child seats and a double stroller.”
- “List questions I should ask the dealer about the warranty on the hybrid battery.”

Check every number against the manufacturer page or the window sticker. Gemini can assemble a table faster than a browser tab stack. It cannot see the car in front of you unless you start Live and point the camera at the placard.

## Extra home prompt: a chore chart

Google’s own short product video shows a roommate prompt that fits the same household theme: build a weekly chore chart, then send reminders into Calendar. That needs Calendar connected under Connected Apps.

Try: “Build a weekly chore chart for four roommates. Put reminders on my Google Calendar at the start of each week.”

Review the events before they fire. Connected Apps in Live still roll out by account type. Work and school accounts have a shorter Live connector list in Gemini Apps Help.

## Habits that keep the help useful

- Ask one job per session. Mix a modem repair and a week of meals in the same Live chat and the answers get sloppy.
- Verify labels, prices, and allergens on the printed source.
- Disconnect Instacart or other shopping apps when you are not planning groceries.
- End Live when you set the phone down so the camera is not left running.
- Keep Activity off if you do not want Connected Apps on web or iOS. Expect most third-party connectors to disappear until you turn it back on.

Google’s household post estimates that saving U.S. households an average of 30 minutes a week with everyday AI could add up to roughly $100 billion in annual economic value. That figure comes from the same ATLAS research note. Use it as context, not as a promise that your Sunday will shrink by half an hour.

## Quick checklist

- Live + camera for blinking lights, appliance panels, and fridge shelves
- Typed chat for multi-day meal plans and diet rules
- Instacart only after you review the cart
- Receipt photos for a first-pass spending table
- Side-by-side tables for cars and appliances, then check official specs
- Calendar reminders only with the Calendar connector on

Household AI is useful when the question is sitting in front of you. Show Gemini the modem, the leftover shelf, or the receipt pile, then keep the last confirmation in your own hands.
