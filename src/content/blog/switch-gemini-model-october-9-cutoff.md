Google is changing which Gemini models a personal account can open in Gemini Apps. If you do not pay for a Google AI plan, those changes start on October 9, 2026. AI Plus subscribers get a separate email with their own start date.

That is not a vague roadmap note. It is on the [Gemini Apps Help page for model access and limits](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17004136). The same page says you can pick a low, medium, or high effort level for each model you still have, and that AI Pro and AI Ultra keep a Deep Think option for maximum parallel reasoning.

This guide shows how to switch models on the web and in the Android app, how effort affects your limit, and what to do before October 9.

## What changes on October 9

Google says model availability for Gemini Apps on personal accounts changes in October 2026. Users without an AI subscription are first. Their change starts October 9. AI Plus subscribers should wait for email rather than assume the same day.

The help page lays out access by plan across three model families: Flash-Lite, Flash, and Pro. Reporting on the updated document, including [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/03/gemini-model-limits-oct-26/), says accounts with no plan keep Flash-Lite and lose Flash and Pro. AI Plus keeps Flash-Lite and Flash, then loses Pro on the date in the email. AI Pro and AI Ultra keep all three.

Deep Think is the extra control. Google describes it as maximum parallel reasoning and assigns it to AI Pro and AI Ultra. It had been limited to the higher Ultra prices, so the $19.99 AI Pro plan is the tier that gains it.

Usage limits stay separate from model access. Gemini uses compute-based limits that refresh every five hours until you hit a weekly cap. Prompt complexity, features, and chat length all count. Paid plans raise the ceiling: AI Plus is 2x standard, AI Pro is 4x, and AI Ultra is 5x or 20x higher than AI Pro depending on the subscription.

## Switch the model on the web

You must be signed in to change models. Open [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) in a desktop browser.

1. Start a chat or open an existing one.
2. Look under the text box for the current model name.
3. Click that name to open the model menu.
4. Select the model you want for the next prompt.

Google’s help article on [managing your Google AI plan](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14517446) says the picker sits at the bottom of the prompt box, and that you can choose a specific model from that menu without leaving the chat. If a model is missing, it is either not on your plan, still rolling out, or blocked for your account age. The older help text notes that Gemini 3 Pro is not available if you are under 18.

Labels in the menu may say Fast or Thinking rather than the full model name. Fast is the speed-oriented option for summaries and everyday prompts. Thinking is the heavier option for coding, files, and multi-step work, and it spends your limit faster.

## Switch the model in the Android Gemini app

Update the Gemini app before you check access. Google’s October notice says the latest app version gives the best match to the new limits.

1. Open the Gemini app and confirm you are on the Google account you use for chat.
2. Tap the model name in the prompt bar. On current builds that control sits near the message field, not only in the top bar.
3. Pick Flash-Lite, Flash, or Pro if your plan lists it.
4. Send a short test prompt and confirm the reply header still shows the model you chose.

If Pro disappears after October 9 and you are on the free tier, that matches the published schedule. Do not clear app data first. Check the plan, then the picker. A stale app build can hide a model that your account still has.

For plan changes, use Settings and help, then View subscriptions, on the web. The same flow covers upgrades and cancellations. Step-by-step billing notes are in our guide to [managing a Google AI plan from Gemini Apps](/blog/manage-google-ai-plan-gemini-apps/).

## Set effort level without burning the five-hour window

Google’s October help page adds a second control: for each available model you can select low, medium, or high effort. Higher effort improves the chance of a thorough answer on hard tasks. It also uses more of your limit.

Use low effort for rewrites, short translations, and list cleanup. Use medium when the answer needs a few steps but not a long plan. Save high effort, extended thinking, and Deep Think for prompts you would otherwise retry three times.

Features that drain the pool faster, per the same help page, include image, video, and music generation, Deep Research, the Pro model, extended thinking, and Deep Think. A single Deep Research run can matter more than a dozen short Flash-Lite chats.

If you hit the limit, wait for the five-hour refresh. That refresh does not reset the weekly cap. When the weekly cap is gone, a higher plan is the official way to raise it, not a new chat.

## A short checklist before October 9

- Sign in on the web and note which models the picker shows today.
- On Android, update Gemini, then repeat the check on the same account.
- If you rely on Pro and you are on the free tier, finish those chats before October 9 or move them to a plan that keeps Pro.
- If you are on AI Plus, watch email for the Pro cutoff. Do not treat October 9 as your date.
- Turn effort down on routine prompts so Flash or Flash-Lite lasts through the five-hour window.
- Compare plans only if a missing model blocks real work. AI Plus is listed at $4.99 a month and AI Pro at $19.99 in US reporting of the current tiers. Prices vary by country.

Google has not said where Gemini 4 Argon sits in this table. Argon was announced for trusted cyber defenders first, with a public rollout still pending. Do not assume the October 9 picker will include it.

## What to do if the model you need is gone

Open a new chat and check the picker again. Some sessions keep the model you started with. If the name is missing from the menu, the account no longer has it.

Next, confirm the Google account. A work or school account follows admin rules, not the personal-plan table. Personal Gmail accounts follow the October schedule.

Then review subscriptions. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from Settings and help, then View subscriptions. After a plan change, return to the prompt box and select the model again. The menu does not always refresh mid-chat.

If Flash-Lite is the only option left, keep prompts short, attach fewer files, and avoid media generation until the five-hour window resets. For a wider look at plan rows and Deep Think, see our [October 9 Gemini model access guide](/blog/gemini-model-access-october-9-plan-guide/).

## Bottom line

October 9 is the date free Gemini Apps users lose broader model choice, starting with Flash and Pro according to the updated help document. AI Plus gets an emailed date for the Pro cut. AI Pro and AI Ultra keep Pro and gain Deep Think.

Switch the model from the prompt box, set effort to match the task, and treat media generation and Deep Research as expensive features. A two-minute check this week is enough to see what your account will still open after the cutoff.
