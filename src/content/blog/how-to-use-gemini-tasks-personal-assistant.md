Google rolled out Gemini Tasks free on November 17, 2026, for users in the United States, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. Powered by the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model, it lets you hand off multi-step jobs instead of chatting for answers.

You open the Gemini app, flip the home-screen toggle from Chat to Task, and describe what you need done. Gemini works in the background, asks for confirmation on sensitive steps, and notifies you when finished.

![Person working at a laptop desk with coffee and notebooks](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1768219565623-501f93ae384b?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

## What Gemini Tasks Does Differently

Chat mode answers questions. Task mode plans and executes multi-step work. Google describes it as handing off jobs so you can move on with your day.

Examples from the official announcement include calling dealerships to book a service appointment, analyzing past utility bills to find peak-pricing issues, shopping for a charger with a discount code, adding items to a grocery order from a photo, and extracting dates from school emails into Calendar.

Tasks run in a hardware-isolated virtual machine on Google Cloud that is sandboxed for your account. Gemini pauses before critical actions like pressing Buy and can be set to Always allow for routine steps you trust.

## How to Start a Task

Open the Gemini app on Android, iOS, or the web. On the home screen, switch the toggle from Chat to Task. The prompt area updates to accept task descriptions.

Type a clear goal. For example: “Call the two nearest Honda dealerships and book the earliest Friday morning service appointment in the next month for our 2019 Odyssey.” Gemini uses Personal Intelligence if enabled to know vehicle and location details.

You can also forward emails to gemini@google.com (U.S. rollout) or set up texting via Settings > Channels > Text with Gemini after verifying your phone number.

## Practical Examples You Can Try

**Analyze a surprise bill.** Give Gemini access to your utility portal via Password Manager and past statements in email. Ask it to identify why usage spiked. In one reported case it traced a heat pump to peak-pricing hours.

**Shop with a spending cap.** Ask for the best USB-C charger options matching wattage and color. Set a hard spending limit. Gemini uses Google’s Shopping Graph, finds codes, and checks out via Google Pay while you keep card points and protections. Receipts land in Wallet.

**Handle school or work emails.** Forward a batch of messages. Gemini extracts dates, adds them to Calendar, invites family members, and flags conflicts for carpool swaps.

**Grocery from a photo.** Text or message a photo of an empty box. Gemini adds the item to an Instacart order and hands off for final checkout.

These features connect to more than 60 apps. Advanced calling and payment tools start in the U.S.; availability varies by country and carrier.

## Safety and Control Tips

Set a hard spending cap before any shopping task. Gemini is designed to stay inside it and still earn rewards.

Review the plan Gemini shows before it starts calling or browsing. You can jump in, add instructions, or stop at any point.

Businesses can opt out of AI calls. Gemini discloses it is an AI at the start of the call.

Adjust settings anytime: disconnect apps, delete history, or turn Task mode off. Remote browser data (cookies for signed-in sites) can be deleted in Settings.

If a task misses the mark, give thumbs-down feedback. Google notes that site owners are still adapting, so some flows hand the screen back for the final click.

For related browser help, see our guide on [using Gemini in Chrome on Android](/blog/gemini-in-chrome-android/).

## Conclusion

Gemini Tasks turns the app into a background assistant that finishes jobs across calls, email, shopping, and Calendar. The free rollout covers six countries on the Hydrogen model. Start with a clear, low-risk request, confirm the plan, and expand from there.

Check official settings for the latest regional limits, as calling and payments expand over time.
