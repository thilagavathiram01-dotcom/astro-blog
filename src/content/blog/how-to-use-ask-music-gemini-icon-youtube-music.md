YouTube Music added a Gemini icon in the top-right corner of the app on Android and iOS. Premium members can now open Ask Music from almost any screen instead of hunting for a card on the Home tab.

The icon matches the Gemini spark used in Gmail and other Google apps. Tap it to open a full-screen conversational interface. Describe a mood, activity, or question and receive a custom mix, artist suggestions, or podcast recommendations grounded in YouTube Music’s catalog.

This guide shows exactly how to find the icon, what Ask Music can do, and how to get useful results. It follows the official YouTube Music Help page and the recent server-side rollout reported in November 2026.

## What changed with the Gemini icon

Previously, Ask Music lived mainly as a card on the Home feed. You had to scroll to the right section or remember where the prompt lived. The new icon sits permanently in the upper-right corner on Home, Library, Now Playing, album pages, and playlist pages.

The change is a server-side update. You do not need a new app store version. Some users saw the icon appear in mid-November 2026; others will see it later. Non-Premium users do not get the icon or the feature.

Ask Music itself is not new. It launched in 2024 and already let Premium members generate mixes and explore content with natural language. The icon simply makes the tool easier to reach while you browse or play music.

## Requirements before you start

- Active YouTube Premium or YouTube Music Premium membership.
- YouTube Music app on Android or iOS (mobile only for now).
- Account not supervised; in the EU and some other markets you must be 18 or older.
- Availability in regions where YouTube Music Premium is offered (Hungary is excluded at the time of writing).

If the icon is missing, wait for the gradual rollout. Restart the app or check for updates, but there is no manual toggle.

## How to open Ask Music with the Gemini icon

1. Open the YouTube Music app and sign in with your Premium account.
2. Look at the top-right corner on the Home tab. The four-pointed Gemini spark should appear.
3. Tap the icon. Ask Music opens as a full-screen sheet.
4. Choose a suggested action such as “Create a mix” or “Discover artists,” or type/speak your own request.
5. Review the results and tap play. Creating a mix does not interrupt the track currently playing.

You can also reach the same interface from the Home tab’s “Ask any way you like” prompt if it is still visible. Scroll up inside Ask Music to revisit recent conversations.

## Create a custom mix step by step

Ask Music builds a personalized radio or playlist from your description. It blends tracks you already like with new discoveries from the official catalog. It does not generate synthetic music or unofficial remixes.

1. Tap the Gemini icon.
2. Select “Create a mix” or type a prompt that names a mood, activity, or style.
3. Examples that work well: “upbeat electronic for a 30-minute run,” “chill acoustic for rainy afternoon,” or “songs similar to my top tracks from last month.”
4. Tap the play button on the result. The mix starts without stopping whatever is already playing.
5. To keep it, add the mix or individual tracks to your Library.

You can start a fresh experience at any time by tapping the new-experience control. Quality varies; the system uses a large language model to interpret the request and may block sensitive or policy-violating prompts.

## Discover artists, podcasts, and listening insights

Beyond mixes, Ask Music supports discovery and reflection.

- Discover artists: ask for recommendations based on a genre, era, or artist you already follow.
- Find podcasts: describe a topic or mood (“intellectual podcasts about technology” or “pop-culture recaps”). Refine with suggestion chips that appear at the bottom.
- About your listening: ask for your top songs, favorite BPM, or tracks you only play on weekends. Results stay grounded in your actual history.
- Build a playlist or customize the queue: describe what you want added or removed while a mix is playing.

For podcasts, tap a recommendation to explore episodes or save a show to your Library. Follow-up prompts let you narrow the results further.

## Practical tips for better results

Be specific. “Workout playlist” produces a generic result. “High-energy hip-hop for strength training with no slow intros” gives the model clearer constraints.

Use voice input when your hands are busy. The microphone button sits next to the text field.

Check recent conversations by scrolling up. This is useful if you want to revisit a mix you generated earlier in the day.

Save what you like. Mixes and individual tracks can be added to Library so they appear in your normal playlists later.

Provide feedback if a result misses the mark. YouTube uses this to improve the feature. You can also report responses for legal reasons through the normal YouTube feedback channels.

## Privacy and data notes

Ask Music is ad-free because it is limited to Premium members. Conversations are not used to serve ads.

The tool may use your listening history, top tracks, and the queries you submit to personalize suggestions. Human reviewers can examine conversations after personal information is removed. Conversation history follows your YouTube Search History settings. You can review or delete past Ask Music activity from My Activity. Deleting Ask Music activity does not erase your regular watch or search history.

## Limitations to know

The feature is mobile-only right now. It is not available on the web or desktop app in the same form.

Rollout is gradual and server-side, so the icon may appear on one device before another.

Accuracy varies. Treat factual answers about artists or releases as starting points and verify important details.

Supervised accounts and users under 18 in certain regions cannot access Ask Music.
