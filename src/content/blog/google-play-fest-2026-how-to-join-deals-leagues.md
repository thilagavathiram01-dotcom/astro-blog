**Google Play Fest 2026: How to Join Deals, Sweepstakes, and Gaming Leagues**

Google Play Fest runs October 13–19, 2026. It combines daily deals, Play Points multipliers, competitive game leagues, and a US sweepstakes with prizes that include a 1:1 scale Oscar Piastri racing helmet, Razer Boomslang mouse, XREAL AURA glasses, Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, and a Samsung 55-inch S90H OLED TV.

The event is available in select markets. Sweepstakes entry is limited to eligible US Play Points members aged 18 or older (no purchase necessary; free entry methods exist). Offers end October 19 or while supplies last. Check eligibility on your device before redeeming points or making purchases.

## What Play Fest Includes

Play Points members can exchange points for sweepstakes entries. Larger point bundles unlock bonuses. The more points you redeem, the more entries you receive. The official announcement lists high-profile prizes such as the Oscar Piastri helmet replica made by Bell, the Razer Boomslang 20th Anniversary Edition mouse, XREAL AURA standalone Android XR glasses, the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, and the Samsung 55-inch S90H OLED TV with 4K AI display and 165 Hz refresh rate.

Competitive leagues run in Angry Birds 2, Free Fire, and 8 Ball Pool. Pre-registration is open. Top players can win up to 50,000 Play Points per league. These leagues turn ordinary play into structured global competition during the event window.

Deals cover apps, games, books, and select digital gift cards. Play Pass subscribers receive double points and 6x boosters. Book purchases earn a 15x points multiplier from October 16–18. Daily purchase boosters apply for members. New users can join Play Points free and complete a quest for bonus points. Special in-game rewards appear in titles such as MONOPOLY GO! and Pokémon GO, and limited savings are available on subscriptions including Crunchyroll and Calm.

## Step-by-Step: Join Play Fest

Open the Google Play Store app on your Android phone or tablet. Look for the Play Fest banner or section. Confirm you are signed into the Google account linked to your Play Points membership. The event page is also reachable via the link referenced in Google’s announcement (play.google.com/play-fest-2026).

### Join or Confirm Play Points

If you are not a member, tap to join free. Complete the introductory quest. Points accumulate from purchases and activities. Visit the Play Points section in the Play Store to view your balance and available offers. Membership is free and provides the foundation for both the sweepstakes redemptions and the daily boosters.

### Redeem Points for Sweepstakes Entries (US Eligible Accounts)

Open the Play Fest hub in the Play Store. Select the sweepstakes option. Choose the number of points to exchange. Larger redemptions add bonus entries. Review the official rules linked from the event page before confirming. Entries close November 2, 2026. The rules include a free method of entry, so purchasing is not required to participate. Confirm your account meets the age and location requirements shown on the page.

### Pre-Register for Game Leagues

From the Play Fest area or via the league links in the Google announcement, pre-register for Angry Birds 2, Free Fire, or 8 Ball Pool. Registration locks in your spot before matches start on October 13. Compete on global leaderboards during the event window. Pre-registration gives you time to review the specific rules for each league and prepare any in-game setup you may need.

### Browse and Use Deals

Scroll the apps, games, books, and gift card sections. Check for limited-time discounts, in-game rewards (such as in MONOPOLY GO! or Pokémon GO), and checkout bonuses with partners like Venmo. Book deals under $6 appear alongside the 15x points multiplier window. Play Pass members see early access and enhanced multipliers. Gift card deals from brands such as Disney are available while supplies last.

Confirm each offer’s dates and eligibility on the product page before purchasing. Some items are while supplies last. Read the fine print on any points multiplier so you know exactly which purchases qualify.

## Tips for Maximizing Rewards

Check the Play Fest page daily. New boosters and limited gift card deals appear throughout the week. Redeem points earlier rather than later if you want more entries before the November 2 cutoff. Track your points balance in the Play Store so you know exactly how many entries you can claim.

Play Pass subscribers should activate their subscription before the event for the double points and 6x boosters. The enhanced multipliers apply during the main event window.

For leagues, pre-register on the day the links appear so you are ready when matches open. Review leaderboard rules in each game. Playing consistently during the competition window improves your ranking chances.

If you use Android security features such as Private Space or app lock, keep Play Store and related accounts accessible during the event. See our guide on [setting up Android Private Space](/blog/android-private-space/) for related device controls. Make sure any parental controls or account restrictions do not block Play Points activity or league participation.

Monitor the event for any last-minute offer changes. Google notes that individual promotions have different eligibility rules, so what appears for one account may differ for another.

## What to Expect During the Week

The main promotion runs October 13–19. A dedicated books multiplier operates October 16–18. Sweepstakes entries remain open until November 2 for eligible accounts. League matches begin once the event starts and run through the competition period.

Deals refresh or rotate, and limited stock items can sell out. Checking the hub each day helps you catch new boosters or gift card promotions. Play Pass early access may surface certain titles or discounts before they appear for other users.

## Conclusion

Play Fest gives Android users a concentrated week of discounts, point multipliers, and competitive play. Start by opening the Play Store, confirming your Play Points status, pre-registering for any leagues that interest you, and reviewing the sweepstakes rules if you are eligible. Offers and availability vary by account and market, so verify details on your device. Follow the official event page and rules for the most accurate, up-to-date information.
