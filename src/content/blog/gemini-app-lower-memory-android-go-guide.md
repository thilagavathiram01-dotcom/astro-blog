Google expanded the Gemini app to lower-memory Android devices on November 18, 2026. Hundreds of millions of people who rely on budget phones for daily internet access can now use the personal AI assistant.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="2" />

Previously, these devices only supported basic voice commands through Assistant Go. The re-architected Gemini experience runs the core features smoothly on hardware with limited RAM. This guide covers eligibility, setup, daily use, and practical tips for Android (Go edition) phones.

## What Changed for Lower-Memory Devices

Google rebuilt the Gemini app so the main conversational experience works on phones with less memory. Eligible Android (Go edition) devices now get the app through the Google Search app instead of a separate download in many cases. It replaces Assistant Go with a more conversational interface.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="20" />

The rollout targets devices that serve as the primary internet connection for education, work, and daily tasks in many regions. Core capabilities stay available without requiring high-end processors or large amounts of RAM.

## Check Device Eligibility

Your phone qualifies if it runs Android (Go edition) and has 2 GB of RAM or more. You need a personal Google Account, or a work or school account that includes Gemini access. Family Link managers can restrict access for younger users.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="38" />

Gemini works in all languages and countries where the full app is available. The feature rolls out gradually, so it may not appear on your device immediately. On these phones, Gemini lives inside the Google Search app. Some newer devices include it preloaded.

To confirm the app version:

1. Open the Google Play Store on your device.
2. Search for "Google" or "Google Search".
3. Tap Update if the button appears.
4. If it shows Open, you already have the latest version.

You can also download the standalone Gemini app from the Play Store on supported devices.

## Start a Conversation

Launch Gemini by touching and holding the Home button. On supported devices, press and hold the Power button. These gestures replace the old Assistant Go trigger.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="38" />

Note that "Hey Google" and "Ask about this screen" are not supported on Android (Go edition) devices. Once open, type or speak your request. Conversations stay available in your chat history across devices when signed in with the same account.

## Key Tasks Gemini Handles

Gemini on these devices covers everyday actions without needing high-end hardware. You can:

- Ask it to call or send text messages to contacts.
- Check drive time to work or find nearby places, such as a restaurant open for lunch with an EV charger.
- Set alarms or create calendar events.
- Upload documents, photos, or other files for more context.
- Play music by mood or activity, for example "Play pop party jams".
- Take a photo to translate text or get step-by-step help.
- Brainstorm ideas or practice for an interview with Gemini Live.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="38" />

These features keep the experience practical for users who previously only had basic voice commands. File uploads and local queries work the same way as on higher-end phones.

## Tips for Smooth Performance

Keep the Google Search app updated through the Play Store. Close unused apps before long conversations to free RAM. Use short, clear prompts for faster responses on limited hardware.

Upload only necessary files to avoid storage pressure. For image tasks, take clear photos in good lighting. If the feature has not appeared yet, wait a few days and check for app updates, as the rollout is gradual.

Sign in with a Google Account that has Gemini access. Work or school accounts may need admin approval. Test basic actions first, such as setting a timer or finding local information, before trying multi-step requests.

## Conclusion

The Gemini app now reaches lower-memory Android devices, including eligible Android (Go edition) phones with 2 GB of RAM or more. Update the Google Search app, hold the Home or Power button, and start using conversational AI for calls, local searches, calendar tasks, and more.

This change puts practical AI tools in the hands of more people without requiring expensive hardware. Check your device today and explore the features that fit your daily routine.
