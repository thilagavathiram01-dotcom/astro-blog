Google expanded Gemini Tasks free of charge to hundreds of millions of people in the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India on November 17, 2026. Powered by the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model, Tasks lets you assign multi-step work and let the AI handle it in the background while you focus on other things.

Unlike regular Chat mode, which answers questions and stops, Task mode plans steps, uses connected apps and websites, and continues working until the job is done or it needs your input. You stay in control and can pause, add instructions, or stop at any time.

## What Gemini Tasks Does

Tasks handles longer projects that would normally require you to stay in a conversation. Examples include scheduling appointments, comparing products, organizing dates from emails into your calendar, or researching options across multiple sites.

It runs on Google's Gemini 4 Hydrogen model for free users in the supported countries. The system works in a dedicated, hardware-isolated virtual machine sandboxed for your account. Gemini pauses before critical actions like purchases and asks clarifying questions when instructions are ambiguous.

You can run multiple tasks at once. Google designed the feature so Gemini saves you time on repetitive or multi-step errands without requiring constant supervision.

## Who Can Use Free Gemini Tasks

Free access is rolling out gradually to eligible users 18 and older in these countries:

- United States
- Japan
- South Korea
- Canada
- Australia
- India

You need a personal Google Account (work and school accounts are not supported) and Gemini History turned on. If you do not see the Chat/Task toggle, the feature has not reached your account yet. Check for app updates and try again later.

Advanced features such as business calling, Google Pay checkout, texting Gemini, and email forwarding are currently limited to the U.S. for eligible users. Business calling is not available in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska.

## How to Start a Task on Android

Follow these steps to hand off work:

1. Update the Gemini app from the Google Play Store if needed.
2. Open the Gemini app and sign in with your personal Google Account.
3. Look for the Chat / Task toggle near the prompt box (above it on mobile).
4. Switch the toggle to Task.
5. Describe the job clearly. Include the goal, any constraints (budget, dates, preferred stores), and what Gemini should not do without asking.
6. Submit the task. Gemini begins working in the background and notifies you when it needs input or finishes.
7. Monitor progress in the task thread. You can add more instructions or tap Stop at any time.

On the web at gemini.google.com the toggle appears below the prompt box. The process is the same.

## Example Tasks You Can Try

Start with low-risk jobs to learn the flow:

- "Compare the three cheapest USB-C chargers under 65W available for delivery this week and list the best options with prices."
- "Review the school emails I forwarded and add all important dates to my Google Calendar. Invite my spouse to the events."
- "Find available appointment slots at nearby car dealerships for an oil change next Friday morning and add the best one to my calendar."

Gemini can connect to Google apps like Gmail, Calendar, Drive, and Maps when you grant permission. It also works with more than 60 connected apps for tasks such as grocery orders.

In the U.S., eligible users can set up texting or email forwarding. Go to Settings > Channels > Text with Gemini > Set up and verify your phone number. You can also forward emails to gemini@google.com.

## Tips for Better Results

Write clear, outcome-focused prompts. State the end goal rather than every click. Mention deadlines, budgets, and any hard limits.

Approve or pre-set "Always allow" only for routine actions you trust. Gemini is designed to ask before buying or submitting forms.

Provide feedback with the thumbs-down option when a task misses the mark. This helps improve the system.

Set spending caps for shopping tasks when available. Receipts land in Google Wallet.

## Safety and Limits

Each task runs in an isolated environment with prompt-injection filters and Google Safe Browsing protections. You can disconnect apps, adjust settings, or delete history at any time.

Gemini discloses that it is an AI when calling businesses. Businesses can opt out. It does not share payment or private account details unless you explicitly approve.

Tasks is still evolving. Some sites may require you to complete the final step. Availability and specific capabilities can change as Google expands the feature.
