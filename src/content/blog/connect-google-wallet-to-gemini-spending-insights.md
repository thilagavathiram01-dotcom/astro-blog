Google added Google Wallet to Gemini's connected apps on November 13, 2026. U.S. users can now ask Gemini questions about their own spending habits and receive charts, subscription lists, and budget suggestions drawn from their transactions.

The feature sits inside Personal Intelligence. It stays off until you turn it on. Gemini receives read-only access, so it cannot move money, complete payments, or manage accounts.

## What the Wallet connection provides

Once connected, tag @Google Wallet in a chat and ask a clear question. Gemini can generate dynamic charts of cash flow, asset allocation, and budgets. It can surface recurring charges and flag any that increased in price this year. It can also review last year's gift and travel spending to help set a holiday budget.

Google lists three example prompts:

- Review spending from the last quarter and suggest a plan to save a set amount over the next three months.
- List subscriptions still being paid and note any price increases.
- Compare last year's gift and travel costs to propose a holiday budget for this year.

You can optionally link bank and credit card accounts to Wallet through Plaid for deeper data. Plaid supports additional account types such as loans and investments. Linking remains optional.

The connection works in the Gemini app on Android and iOS and on the web at gemini.google.com. It is available to all Gemini app users in the United States, not limited to a paid tier.

## How to turn on the Google Wallet connection

Update the Gemini app from the Play Store or App Store if needed. Then follow these steps:

1. Open the Gemini app or go to gemini.google.com and sign in.
2. Tap your profile picture or initials. On the web, open Settings.
3. Select Personal Intelligence, then Connected Apps.
4. Find Google Wallet and connect it.
5. Optionally link accounts through Plaid if you want more transaction detail.
6. Start a new chat, type @Google Wallet, and ask a specific money question.

Standard protection remains the default until you change the setting. You can disconnect at any time from the same Connected Apps menu.

If the option does not appear, the rollout may not have reached your account yet. Check again after a day or two. Availability is currently limited to the U.S.

## Privacy and control details

Google states that Gemini can only read information from connected accounts. It cannot transfer funds, make purchases, or invest on your behalf. Data is handled with the same safeguards used for other Personal Intelligence sources such as Gmail, Photos, YouTube, and Search.

You control the connection. Turn it off for individual chats if desired, or use a temporary chat that is not saved. On the web and iPhone, connected apps require Gemini Apps Activity to be enabled.

Google notes that it does not train models directly on Wallet data, though reviewers may see summaries of chats that use the feature. Review your activity settings if you want tighter control over history and training. Related Gemini privacy options are covered in existing guides on the site.

## Tips for useful results

Ask specific questions rather than broad ones. "Show cash flow for Q3 and suggest cuts to free $800 over 90 days" produces clearer charts than "help me budget."

Cross-check numbers against your bank statements or Wallet app before acting on suggestions. Gemini summarizes; it does not replace official records.

Combine the feature with other Gemini tools. For device-specific automation on Pixel phones, see the guide on [Gemini Intelligence features for Pixel 10](/blog/how-to-use-gemini-intelligence-on-pixel-10/).

If you prefer to keep financial data separate, leave the connection off and use a dedicated budgeting app instead.

## Conclusion

Connecting Google Wallet to Gemini gives U.S. users a quick way to visualize spending, spot forgotten subscriptions, and draft budgets inside the chat they already use. The setup takes a few taps, remains opt-in, and keeps control with the user. Start with one clear question after you enable the connection, then decide whether the added context is worth keeping active.
