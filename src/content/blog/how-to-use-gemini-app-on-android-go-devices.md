Google expanded the Gemini app to eligible Android (Go edition) devices on November 18, 2026. This brings core AI features to phones that previously relied on basic voice commands.

The re-architected app runs the main Gemini experience on hardware with less memory. It reaches hundreds of millions more people who use entry-level Android devices for daily internet, education, and business tasks.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="325" />

## Check Eligibility for Gemini on Android Go

Your device must meet these requirements from Google’s support page:

- Android (Go edition) with 2GB of RAM or more.
- Signed in to a personal Google Account, or a work or school account that has Gemini Apps access.
- Available in the same languages and countries as standard Gemini.

On these devices, Gemini arrives as part of the Google Search app and replaces Assistant for Android (Go edition). You can also download the standalone Gemini app from Google Play. Some newer partner devices from brands like Transsion may ship with it preinstalled.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="341" />

Family Link managers can disable Gemini Apps for users under the applicable age in their country.

## Update Google Search or Install the Gemini App

Follow these steps to get the latest version:

1. Open the Google Play Store on your Android Go device.
2. Search for “Google” or “Google Search”.
3. Tap Update if available, or confirm it already shows Open.
4. Alternatively, search for the Gemini app and install it directly.

After installation, sign in with your eligible Google Account if prompted.

## Start a Conversation with Gemini

On supported Android Go devices, launch Gemini in one of two ways:

- Touch and hold the Home button.
- Press and hold the Power button (on models that support it).

“Hey Google” voice activation and “Ask about this screen” queries are not supported on Android Go. These limitations keep the experience lightweight.

Once open, type or speak your request. Gemini handles multi-turn conversations and remembers context within the chat.

## Key Features Available on Android Go

The optimized app includes practical tools that work on lower-memory hardware:

- **Gemini Live**: Hold natural spoken conversations in more than 45 languages. Practice interview answers or brainstorm ideas out loud.
- **Camera help**: Point the camera at notes, forms, or documents. Gemini translates text or walks you through step-by-step help.
- **Image creation**: Use Nano Banana to generate images, such as a simple flyer for a local sale.
- **Calls and texts**: Ask Gemini to call or message contacts.
- **Planning**: Set alarms or create calendar events.
- **Local information**: Ask complex questions like finding a restaurant open for lunch with nearby charging.
- **Media and files**: Play music by mood or activity, and upload photos or documents for more context.

These features run through the re-engineered app so they stay responsive even with limited RAM.<grok type="render_inline_citation" citation_id="326" />

## Practical Tips for Daily Use

Keep the Google Search app updated through Play Store for the best performance. Clear unused apps if storage feels tight—the Gemini experience is designed to stay efficient.

Try Gemini Live for tasks that benefit from spoken back-and-forth. For documents, open the camera tool and frame the page clearly under good lighting.

If you also use higher-end Android features on other devices, note that full Gemini Intelligence (on-device advanced tools) requires more RAM and specific hardware. Android Go focuses on the core conversational and camera experiences.

## Limitations to Expect

The Android Go version prioritizes reliability over every advanced option. Screen queries and always-listening “Hey Google” are excluded. Some deeper integrations available on flagship phones may not appear here.

Performance depends on your exact device and remaining storage. Google has not published a full device-by-device compatibility list beyond the 2GB RAM minimum and Android Go requirement.

## Conclusion

The Gemini app on Android Go devices puts useful AI tools in the hands of people who rely on entry-level phones. Update the Google Search app or install Gemini from Play Store, then activate it with a long-press on Home or Power. Start with Live conversations, camera translation, and simple image creation to see the value immediately.

Check eligibility on your device today and explore the features that fit your daily needs.
