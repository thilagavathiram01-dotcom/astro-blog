Google Messages beta now routes its floating Gemini button to the new Tasks chat. Once set up, Gemini can text you first with package delivery updates, price-drop alerts, subscription reminders, and morning briefings. The change appeared in Google Messages version 20261120_01_RC00 and is currently limited to US users who have Tasks enabled.

This replaces the older conversational Gemini chat that has lived in Messages since 2024. The new experience uses the Channels feature rolled out on November 17, 2026, so Gemini acts more like an assistant that reaches out than a chatbot you must open.

## What Changed in the Messages Beta

Tapping the Gemini floating action button (or the top-right icon on the redesigned homepage) now opens gemini.google.com/channels on a fresh install. After you complete setup, the same button launches an RCS Business Messaging thread with Gemini inside Google Messages.

Gemini still answers general questions and accepts image or file attachments. The standout difference is that it initiates contact. Early testers report daily messages covering:

- Upcoming Black Friday or holiday price drops on items they track
- Expiring subscriptions that may need renewal
- Travel dates pulled from calendar or email
- Package delivery reminders, plus a follow-up once the package arrives

The integration requires Tasks to be turned on in the Gemini app. It is available only in the United States at launch.

## Prerequisites

Confirm these items before you start:

- Personal Google Account based in the US with English as the account language
- Tasks enabled in the Gemini app (toggle from Chat to Task on the home screen)
- Google Messages installed with RCS chats turned on
- US phone number on an eligible carrier
- Access to the latest Google Messages beta (version 20261120_01_RC00 or newer)

For the base Channels setup that powers text and email, see our earlier guide: [How to Text and Email Gemini Tasks Free in the US](/blog/how-to-text-email-gemini-tasks-channels/).

## Step-by-Step Setup in Google Messages

1. Update Google Messages to the latest beta from the Play Store. Opt into the beta program if you have not already.
2. Open Google Messages and tap the Gemini floating button (or the Gemini icon in the top-right corner).
3. The app redirects you to gemini.google.com/channels. Sign in with the same Google Account.
4. Under Text with Gemini, tap Set up and verify your US phone number.
5. Return to Google Messages. The Gemini button now opens a dedicated conversation thread with Gemini.
6. Confirm Tasks is active in the Gemini app by switching the home-screen toggle to Task and sending a simple test request.

Once the RCS thread is active, Gemini can message you without you opening the app. You can reply in the same thread to steer a task, ask a follow-up, or upload a photo of a school flyer or receipt.

## Practical Ways to Use the Proactive Chat

Text Gemini a product link or a short description of something you want to track. It can watch for price drops and text you when the price falls. The same thread works for delivery tracking: share an order confirmation or tracking number and Gemini sends status updates plus an arrival notice.

For daily use, ask Gemini to send a morning briefing that pulls from your Calendar and Gmail. Examples include travel plans, subscription renewals, and package ETAs. You can also ask ordinary questions such as weather or simple research; the chat is not limited to formal tasks.

Gemini pauses for one-tap confirmation before major actions such as booking or paying. Receipts land in Google Wallet when purchases complete.

## Tips for Better Results

- Keep the Gemini app and Google Messages signed into the same account.
- Enable RCS and keep the phone number verified on the Channels page.
- Set a spending cap inside Gemini Tasks settings if you plan to let it handle purchases.
- Reply promptly to confirmation prompts so tasks do not stall.
- If proactive messages feel too frequent, adjust preferences or pause specific monitors inside the Gemini app.

The older Gemini chat in Messages is no longer the default path in this beta. The new Tasks-oriented thread is the one that receives proactive alerts.

## Limits and Availability

The Messages integration is US-only for now. Tasks themselves are free in the US, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India on Gemini 4 Hydrogen, but the proactive texting experience inside Google Messages has not expanded beyond the United States. Texts are not end-to-end encrypted. Deleting Gemini activity does not remove messages stored by your carrier or in the Messages app.

Google has not published an official timeline for wider rollout. The feature remains in beta and can change with future Messages updates.
