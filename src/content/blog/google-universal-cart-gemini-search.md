Google now keeps one shopping list across Search, AI Mode, and the Gemini app. Universal Cart holds items from different merchants in a single place, then offers checkout on Google or on the store site.

The feature is live for eligible accounts in the United States. YouTube and Gmail were promised later. This guide follows Google’s own help article and the I/O 2026 product post, not rumor.

## What Universal Cart actually is

Universal Cart is a persistent cart stored with Google Wallet. You add products while you search or chat. The cart groups those products by merchant.

Google says the cart runs on Gemini models. It can surface price history, stock alerts, and deal hints after you save an item. It can also flag incompatibilities, such as PC parts that do not fit together.

The brand stays the merchant of record. Google Pay checkout is only for participating stores. Everyone else gets a hand-off that fills the merchant’s own cart.

## Who can use it right now

Google’s Gemini Apps Help page states the feature is currently available only in the United States. You can add items from:

- Google Search and Shopping
- AI Mode in Search
- Gemini Apps

YouTube and Gmail were on the I/O roadmap. Treat those surfaces as pending unless they already appear on your account.

You need a signed-in Google Account and, for Pay checkout, payment and shipping details already saved in Google Wallet. Participating checkout brands named at I/O included Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta Beauty, Walmart, Wayfair, and selected Shopify merchants such as Fenty and Steve Madden.

## Add an item from Search or AI Mode

1. Sign in to Google in the United States.
2. Search for a product, or ask AI Mode a shopping question with a brand or model.
3. Open a listing that shows an add-to-cart control.
4. Add the item. Confirm it lands in Universal Cart, not only a merchant pop-up.

Repeat for a second retailer if you are comparing. The cart is meant to hold mixed merchants in one list.

If you shop in Gemini, ask for product options, then use the listing’s save or cart control when it appears. Do not expect the chat to recite your full cart. Help text says Gemini Apps and AI Mode have limited access to cart data.

## Open and clean the cart

1. Open Universal Cart from Search shopping tools or from the Gemini app shopping entry your account shows.
2. Review items grouped by merchant.
3. Open a product to check size, color, or price.
4. Remove anything you no longer want.

Do this before checkout. A leftover SKU from last week will travel into Pay or into the merchant site fill.

## Check out two ways

Google documents two paths.

**Check out with Google Pay.** Use this when the merchant participates. Complete the purchase on Google with saved Wallet methods. Confirm shipping, tax, and the final total before you submit.

**Check out on the merchant site.** Google helps populate that store’s cart. Finish the order on the retailer page so you keep their return policy, loyalty price, or gift options.

Contact the merchant if something goes wrong after payment. Help text is explicit: purchase problems go to the store you bought from, not to a generic Google shopping desk.

## Track orders after you buy

Some merchants share order data back to Google. For those orders, open the **My Orders** tab inside the cart.

You may see shipped or delivered status for supported stores, plus a history of recent cart purchases. If a store does not send data, the order will not appear there even when the charge succeeded.

Cart and order records live in Google Wallet. Use Wallet’s data and privacy controls if you want to review or limit what is stored.

## Limits Google actually lists

Gemini Apps and AI Mode may not:

- List cart items or status updates inside a chat reply
- Add items with a spoken command alone, if no listing control is present
- Give live order updates in conversation

So treat chat as discovery. Treat the cart UI as the source of truth.

Universal Cart is not a price-match guarantee. Deal and stock alerts depend on merchant feeds. It is also not a replacement for the store’s own app if you need gift cards, store credit, or in-store pickup rules that Google does not show.

If you already use Gemini for research on the phone, keep shopping separate from browser agents. [Gemini in Chrome on Android](/blog/gemini-in-chrome-android/) can summarize pages. It does not replace Universal Cart checkout.

## Practical tips

Save Wallet cards and a current address before the first Pay checkout. Missing shipping data is the usual stall.

Add one item per merchant first, then open the cart. Confirm grouping looks right before you pile in a full list.

For a multi-part build, add every component, then read any incompatibility note Google shows. Do not skip that screen.

If India or another market shows Flipkart or local listings inside Gemini, that is a separate commerce test. Universal Cart help still lists the United States only.

Review My Orders a day after a purchase. If the row is missing, use the merchant confirmation email instead of waiting on Google.

## Conclusion

Universal Cart is a U.S. shopping list that follows you from Search and AI Mode into Gemini, then splits checkout between Google Pay and the store site. Open the cart UI to edit, pay, and track. Use chat only to find products. Keep Wallet tidy, and send purchase disputes to the merchant of record.
