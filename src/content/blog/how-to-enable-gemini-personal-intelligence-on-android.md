Gemini Personal Intelligence lets the assistant use context from your connected Google apps and past chats to give more relevant answers. It stays off until you opt in.

This guide shows how to turn it on for your Android phone, choose which apps to connect, and manage the settings that control what Gemini can see. If you also use Android Auto, the same phone settings carry over to the car. See our [guide to Gemini Personal Intelligence on Android Auto](/blog/enable-gemini-personal-intelligence-android-auto/) for in-car details.

## Check eligibility first

Personal Intelligence requires a personal Google account and age 18 or older. Workspace, school, and supervised accounts are excluded. Availability varies by region. Google has restricted it in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and Nigeria.

Open the Gemini app. If you do not see Personal Intelligence in settings, the feature has not reached your account yet. Rollouts are gradual.

## Enable Personal Intelligence in the Gemini app

1. Open the Gemini app on your Android phone.
2. Tap your profile icon or the menu in the top-right corner.
3. Select Settings, then Personal Intelligence.
4. Turn on the main toggle if it is off.
5. Review the Memory option. Enabling it lets Gemini remember details from previous conversations.

After you enable it, Gemini may prompt you to connect apps. You can skip or choose specific ones.

## Connect apps for better context

Under Personal Intelligence, open Connected Apps. Toggle the services you want Gemini to use. Common options include:

- Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Drive)
- Google Photos
- YouTube
- Google Maps and Search
- Other apps such as Keep or third-party services where available

Start with one or two apps, such as Calendar and Gmail. Test a prompt like “What meetings do I have tomorrow?” before connecting more.

You can disconnect any app later from the same menu. Disconnecting stops future access but does not automatically delete previously stored context.

## Manage activity and privacy settings

Personal Intelligence relies on several activity controls. Check these in your Google Account settings:

- Keep Activity must be on.
- Web & App Activity enables Search and Maps context.
- YouTube History enables video-related answers.
- Smart features in other Google products must be on for Workspace connections.

In Gemini settings you can also review or delete stored Personal Intelligence data. Google states the feature is opt-in and that you control which apps are connected.

## Practical prompts to try

Once apps are connected, try these on your phone:

- “Summarize unread emails from this week that mention travel.”
- “Add a reminder to my Calendar based on the confirmation in my inbox.”
- “Find photos of the restaurant I visited last month.”

These work better when the relevant app is connected and activity settings are enabled.

## Tips for daily use

Turn on only the apps you actively want Gemini to reference. Review connected apps every few weeks. If answers feel too personal or inaccurate, disconnect the source app and test again.

Use the Instructions option under Personal Intelligence to set response preferences, such as shorter answers or bullet points. Changes apply across Gemini surfaces that support the feature.

## Conclusion

Enabling Personal Intelligence on Android takes a few taps in the Gemini app and deliberate choices about which data sources to share. The same settings prepare your phone for more context-aware help and for Android Auto if you use it. Start small, test prompts, and adjust connections as needed.
